{nd} Bright orb hovering in same spot for about 30 minutes then disappeared.

This is the fourth strange object I've seen in the sky in about a week period. On July 4th I was outside talking to my neighbor when I noticed at about 8:30 P.M. a strange bright object in the north west sky. The Sun was setting, but it was not yet dark. I thought it might be a bright star or planet, or possibly a satellite reflecting the Sun's light. It appeared to hover in the same spot for about thirty minutes, but seemed to have moved a little north (but this may be due to earths movement). Then the object grew dim and faded, which I thinks debunks the idea it was a star or planet. It hovered in the same spot for about thirty to forty fives minutes, and then disappeared. What does that? I do have some photos and video footage, but it would take special software to zoom in to see the object with clarity.
