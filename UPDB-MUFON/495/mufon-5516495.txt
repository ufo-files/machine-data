(GS) I saw 2 triangle-shaped, VERY BRIGHT, objects at an unknown distance, but over the roof-level of a full-sized barn. Were gone when I came back w/ a camera.

I was house-sitting for some relatives in a rural vicinity of Henry County, Ohio. When I went outside to star-gaze, I noted two, extremely bright triangle shaped objects in the sky. I could not tell how far they were, but they were above the roof-level of a full-sized barn. They appeared to be maybe 1/2 mile or farther, but out there, I have no way of telling how far. At first, I was not sure what I was looking at, but I did know there were no fixed lights in that direction. After several minutes (maybe 10?) I decided to see if I could get a picture of them with my Kodak Easy Share, but I was not able to as it was too poor a quality. The pictures turned out like dim blobs [I need to save for a decent telescope and low-light-capable video]! I have no idea how long they were there, but I had to go inside for a moment (nature called). When I got back outside, they were gone. I checked for them again the following night but nothing else was observed.
