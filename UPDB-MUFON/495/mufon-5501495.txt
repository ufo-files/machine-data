Jun 11th 2250.Went out side to my verhicale to get my cellphone, noticed the haloe around the moon, very bright. On getting my phone I turned left away from my verhicale and looked up and noticed a very very small red dot appear about 40 degs above the imagined horizon, I watched for about 20 secs and realized that the light was now getting larger, ran inside to phone friend, came back out side and now the object was a thump tack size and went behind some trees and vanished.I went back inside my house and got my binoculars came outside and went back to where I was standing the first time I saw the light and realised I had not used my cell phone camara. Then to my amazement another light appeared from the same direct this time I took 4 pictures, 2 of them with flash, each time the flash went off, the light hesitated and proceeded on its course going behind the trees, I ran to my back yard gate and got the light in my binoculars, the light had now veered away to the north, I counted to my self 1 one thousand to 4 one thousand and to light was gone following the earths curvature. 

The following morning I went to one of my neighbours to tell him what I had observed, unknown to me he too had witnessed the same event when he let his dog out and he too has taken several photos of the first object light, he went inside when his dog was finished.

During this whole time I was very calm yet excited that what I was witnessing was not of this planet, I severed 10 years in the Royal Navy,as a boy during WW2 we were trained to observe and report enemy aircraft.
