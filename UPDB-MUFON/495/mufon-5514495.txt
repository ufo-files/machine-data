{dr}Strange lights over the horizon heading ne from south of Downtown Disney

I was sitting on the balcony at the Buena vista palace on the 20th floor looking south over downtown Disney in Kissimmee FL. It was dusk and from the horizon I noticed a group of lights ascending heading north west. No sound or discernible visual indication, except that I counted 14 of them in a mass group. At first it looked like a squadron of fighters taking off, but did not follow normal operational procedures. The spacing was varied and as they broke in distance from one another they slowly dissappeared in a westerly direction.
