{dr}At least 75 or More fast moving orbs flying in front if the sun

Was sitting on my porch with full sun and cloudless sky. A hanging lantern on my porch strategically blocked out just the sun but not the surrounding sky. I started to notice many white star like objects zooming in and around the sun. There were mosquitos and insects out, however I could tell the difference immediately between those and the objects that were much much further hop in the sky and illuminated. Some came in fast inn a direct path and directly cruised the sun. Some made corkscrew like movements. Some started in one trajectory... Stopped midair and then zoomed off on a complete different direction. I called 2 friend who both also witnessed the same event from 2 different cities nearby. I know I've never ever seen anything like this before in my life.
