{tjd} Object hovered over Newburyport bridge for about 15 minutes and disappeared

Around 9am, a coworker brought my attention to a stationary object that we observed hovering, not moving, for between 15 and 20 minutes in the morning of May 24th, 2019. 

We were in the Cashman Park region of Newburyport and we were looking almost directly east. We both observed a white/reflective glowing, yet solid & glinting object sit idle, unmoving against the morning sun. I'd estimate the object was between 1-3 miles away from us and we both watched it, moved around, looked at it from several vantage points and still it did not move.

Eventually it either disappeared or moved away from the area - we did not see it move away. Looking at the object was difficult because of the bright morning Sun and the direction we were facing to observe it.

I'm an amateur pilot and naked-eye astronomer and spend a lot of time looking up. I generally know what I am looking at, and why something is there. I can not explain this, I can not explain what we saw. I could see distinct edges and glints from the sun. I describe it as spherical or orb-like but I recognize it could have been another shape. It was not a balloon and not a plane or a bird/bat/bug. If it was a drone it was huge. At the distance we observed it, I'd estimate it was as big as a meter across.

I don't know what I saw. It is unidentified and now unidentifiable (because it is gone). I'll be watching the sky in that direction from now, especially in the morning and when the sun is particularly bright. I sense it was attempting to "hide" by situating itself in the direction of the Sun. Very interesting, very cool thing to observe.
