{rjl} Approx. 50-100 lights in a chain/line at low elevation.

At approx. 10:05 pm on 4/24/2020, a line of lights were observed by three individuals. The lights were moving in a line at a slow rate of speed. The lights were observed for about 2 minutes by all three witnesses. The lights were seen in the north and moved towards the east as the line of lights increased in elevation. The line of lights appeared close together at first but then appeared to move further apart. The lights then disappeared while headed east.
