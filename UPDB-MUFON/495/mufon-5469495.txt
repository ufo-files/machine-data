Commercial air traffic being followed by something.

Sitting in a traffic jam. Heavy traffic so engine switched off. Nice sunny day (everyone goes to the recycling centre when the sun comes out). Bored, so looking around i looked up.... saw a commercial aircraft leaving a vapour trail (and another one heading the opposite way in what i would describe as an acceptable distance - leaving a vapour trail). The first aircraft was being followed by something that i would say was not much smaller, was white in reflection and was matching the speed of the aircraft it was following. What was strange was that the following object was leaving no vapour trail like the aircraft and, while i could see the outline of the wings on the aircraft i could not make out wings on the following object. Tried to get my phone out to shoot some video but, the plane (and the object disappeared behind a large cloud). I was stuck in solid traffic so sat, with my phone ready to catch it coming out of the other side but it (they didnt) so must have turned behind the cloud. I would hazzard a guess that the distance between the aircraft and the object was approximately no more than a couple of hundred feet. The object was tailing the aircraft to its left and back a little. Ive never seen anything follow any aircraft that close before.

I did not see it after that. If it was something that was of conventional technology then it, would have to leave a vapour trail also would it not?
