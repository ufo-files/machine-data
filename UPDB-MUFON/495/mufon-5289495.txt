Glowing Verticle OVAL/TICTAC seen 5.29pm 2/22/21 Near Crown Point

We were returning to Crown Point from West Beach traveling South towards the 109th Street exit for crown point. Looking up at the sun setting behind the clouds I noticed what appeared to be a large object in the sky reflecting the sun light coming from behind the clouds.
When I first witnessed the object it was just under the clouds and as I had my phone in my lap I managed to snap various photo's from the passenger seat of the car I was in. I could not see the object moving it appeared to be static hovering in the sky , this appeared to be confirmed when the cloud cover started to wrap around the object as the clouds came down, i have detailed this in one of the photographs.  Looking at the photo's i can confirm the time of my event as 5.29pm and I witnessed it for approximately 1-2 minutes before it disappeared behind the descending cloud cover.  I have included all the photo's that you could see the object in and were not motion blurred. It appeared to be very large, high up and quite far off. If the sun had not been reflecting off of the side i probably would not have seen this so easily. I would say this did not look like a plane or helicopter due to the size but also because the shape was elongated vertically rather than round reflection or horizontal plus it did not appear to move the entire time perched up in the sky near the clouds.
