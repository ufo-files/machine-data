{dr}Bright object near sun for over an hour not moving, then gone

Trimming shrubs at a customers house with my wife and another couple. I was looking up and the shrub blocked the sun so I could see the object very near it. It was a clear sky, no clouds . As long as we held something up to block the sun the object was very bright and easy to see. The object was not moving noticeably but did stay about the same distance from the sun for over an hour. I was wondering if it could be a planet or star . Ive seen the moon during the day but never a star or planet. We went for a quick bite at close to noon and the object was gone when we came out. The object was bigger than stars you would see at night and was very shiny, almost chrome like.
