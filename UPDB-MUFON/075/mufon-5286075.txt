First thought it was a pearl shaped object within the cloud.

A little after 8:55pm on Wednesday August 11th I was sitting at a Chic-Fil-A drive through in La Vale, MD. and was taking in the sunset while waiting in line. I noticed what at first, I thought to be an oval cloud out ahead of some other cloud cover. As I observed it, I noticed that it seemed much lower than the high streaming clouds of the sunset. While it was somewhat dark on the bottom, just like the typical cloud at dusk it appears bright on the upper portion (which should have not been possible considering that the setting sun was already below the NW horizon. I took a quick photo with my Android phone. When I took a moment to look at the picture, I noticed that it was fuzzy, so I took another with image stabilization. This photo, once zoomed in, shows a definite outline of a pearl-like object. As I observed it at first it did not move but then began to move slightly from NE to SW against the movement of the typical clouds. After proceeding through the drive-thru line I stopped and tried to make further observations, but the object had disappeared. This seemed highly unlikely to me since the last time I observed it the movement was not fast at all. It was also not nearly long enough for an anomalous cloud to dissipate.
