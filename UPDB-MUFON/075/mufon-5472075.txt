Green disc

This sighting occurred about 12 years ago in late summer about 6pm. My wife and I were walking down the street outside our house and we both looked up to the sky which was overcast wondering if it would rain. Through a break in the clouds we saw a dull green disc with a darker center fly by- the cloud cover seemed to be fairly low. The sighting lasted only a second. There was no sound, in fact the evening was very quiet. I can't say if the sighting was a craft of some sort or a meteorite with a disc shaped gaseous cloud around it but it moved with the disc shape staying intact. It is one of the only two strange aerial sightings I have seen, both in Sudbury, Ontario.
