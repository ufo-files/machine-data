{cak} Shocking and mysterious

We had family visiting and it got late so I walked them out to their car. I have a habit of always looking up to the sky whenever I step out so as I always do, I looked up to the sky as I walked them out. Suddenly a light and movement coming from the North caught my attention. I observed the object and it's movement and realized it was not a plane nor helicopter. I could not tell the distance of the object but it was very high in the sky but big enough to make out what it was. It was a cloudy night but the object was still visible amongst the clouds. In my excitement I shouted out to my sister, her husband and their son (they were inside the house) to rush outside to see what I had spotted. We all agreed that what we saw something not familiar to us. My sister tried to record it/snap a picture with her cell phone but we couldn't get a clear picture. The object was round in shape, with a large white hazy sphere surrounding it. Between the sphere was another round shaped object with a green still light in the middle. Surrounding the green light was like a projection of some sort displaying odd green shapes and designs in sequence (it reminded me of Tetris). It moved along the sky from North to South until it stopped above us, hovered for a few seconds and suddenly ascended. It ascended for about a minute or so then it was gone.
