Irregular string with apparent formations

There was a string of red-orange lights appearing from behind a nearby hill in the approximate area of 14th street at an indeterminate distance and altitude traveling from east to west before turning south. There were at least 20 but, not more than 40, pulsating lights, that changed color from red-orange to white upon turning south and fading out. They made no sound and were in 2 and 3 ship formations as others were singular. I made a police report and the dispatcher (Victoria) said there was at least one other reported sighting. I checked ADS-B exchange and Flightradar24 and neither had any visible data for any objects and the SpaceX Starlink tracker stated there were no visible satellites for the next 5 days, and a live satellite tracer showed 3 satellites in the vicinity, but not traveling in line. I have 8&1/2 years of military service and spent the last 4 doing satellite communications configuration, control, monitoring, and maintenance. These were not of any known satellite constellation or natural phenomena. I apologize for the higher quality photo being out of contrast, the flash was on.
