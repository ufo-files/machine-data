{rjl} Bright light like a planet but moving fast and changed direction and speed

Walking home from dinner I noticed a bright light like a planet moving quickly north to south. I pointed this out to my wife and she agreed the speed was too fast to be a plne , and living here for 20 years we know what planes and helecopters look like. As we observed the object it seemed to slow down and change direction and elevation to the east and climbed in altitude. The direction seemed to cross behind the path of an obvious small plane that was flying e/ne.It didn't seem the plane had any affect on the object. It stayed at a steady brightness throughout the flight and once again decended and quickly went beyond out field of vision/ horizon.
