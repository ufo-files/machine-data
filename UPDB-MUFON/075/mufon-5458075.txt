{cak} Mysterious Object

I have never seen this kind of movement in the sky before. At first I though it could have been an airplane but I did not see any blinking lights. It was an orb shape extremely bright light, brighter than anything else in the sky. The night sky was very clear I could see a lot of the stars and the moon. The object traveled at a high rate of speed compared to aircraft that move in the sky. I then thought it could be a meteor but there was no burning tale. The object was traveling from the west heading in a easterly direction. It only last for about two minutes until the light got smaller and faded into the distance.
