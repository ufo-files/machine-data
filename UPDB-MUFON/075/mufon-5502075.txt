{cak} transparent or invisible object that intermittently reflected lights from the ground

I was on the south side of Colombia Missouri doing a night road paving job at or around 11:15pm I saw a flash of light in the sky to the north I continued watching the spot when i saw it again this time it had moved south closer to me in the area of the sky of the big dipper it didnt appear to emit light but reflect the lights from the ground kind of flareing appearing to be round or spherical. I continued to watch the sky and follow its course south but it did not appear again.As far as my feelings i was amazed even felt energized it was not something you expect to see in the sky
