Airplane-like objects but slower, flying to the left and right. Not straight line. Zigzag line.

It was a summer night, black sky. I was standing on my balcony, no alcohol, no drugs involved, I am a mother... Looked at the sky and saw a yellow light coming towards me (in my direction) but slower than airplanes do and at lower height. They showed triangle shapes. One came first in zigzag movements from south to the north. Slowly, comfortably. Not in hovering kind of moving but definitely, Soon, in a second, a second object followed it in the same manner. They showed rhythmic movements... There was a two-three second timespan when I could see both things in the sky. I could not see where they went because of the roof. I felt nothing but curiosity. When the second object appeared I thought e.t. vehicles. No noise was heard, no further light emitted. The objects had sharp edges. I tried to keep it in secret, here people say you are crazy if you think of a UFO. I heard or saw no news of "weather balloon" or military activity around here. I am used to seeing lights in the sky at night since an airport is near (Budapest, 70 km) but these were very different from them. In colour, shape, movement pattern and speed. And the direction. The airplanes usually go to the west-north-west to land. But these came from the south.
