Disc like Object

I was driving from Cookeville near mile marker 226 heading towards Nashville. The day was sunny and the sky was clear with some clouds. I noticed something in the sky off to my left and it was just hovering there. I pointed it out to my wife as then a tree briefly obscured my vision. I figured if it was an airplane, the object would still be there. As we passed the tree the object was gone. I looked around to see where it was, and it was gone. Nowhere to be found. It just disappeared. The object was disc shaped with a dome on top. Its color was a glowing white color. My wife did not witness it because it was gone before i could point it out again.
