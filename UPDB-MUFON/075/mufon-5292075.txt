Light like a star, moved, saw multiple

saw a light through my bedroom window, looked like a star stretched vertically? But it had two beams of light coming from its top that went out and down at 45°, I looked back 5 minutes later it had visibly moved in my field of vision to the other side of a tree on the hill. So I went outside to look, and notice many more in the sky in various locations. At least 6 prominently. Where the original one was was over a hillside so I thought it was maybe a light pole, but i went out in the morning and there are no light poles on that ridgeline. What was really freaky, was that I looked back out of the window about 20 minutes later and the light was gone.
