Unknown bright light

While driving home after photographing the Milky Way all night, I happened to see an extremely bright light that looked very unusual around 5:30am. The sun was already lighting up the sky in the East but this light was extremely bright and had a strange triangle shape almost. I could not see any object whatsoever, only the light. I captured the object with my phone and tried to turn down a road to get better footage but it eventually got too far away to video. To be very clear, I am not saying this was anything “extraterrestrial” or anything that cannot be explained by someone with more experience than me. It’s just something that was very strange in person so I wanted someone else to take a look and get their opinion. It was likely something that is easily explained but after zooming all the way in I simply cannot see any type of object whatsoever. And the light was unbelievably bright to show up that prominent in the sky at 5:30am since it was nearly daylight. The sky wasn’t dark at all. With one look at the video someone can probably tell immediately what it was!
