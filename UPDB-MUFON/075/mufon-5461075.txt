{jpg} Military Jet enroute to intersect 2 dots

On April 26, 2019 at 111pm, bright sunny day I was at the Black Mail box location photographing jet aircraft doing air combat drills over Area 51 test range. I always review my images when I get home and I spotted a particular aircraft was in direct flight path two intercept these two bright objects in one frame. No more than a second goes by I shoot another frame. The objects had move to the East and that plane never saw the move and kept going up. I never saw these object as well. See for your self. My camera was a Canon T6, at 300mm, f5.6, 1/1000, and iso 100. I had dirt on my photographs via the lens thats what the big smug marks are..It was a windy day and I didnt see it at first.
Also at about 123pm I wanted to increase my shutter speed to 1/3200 to prove that although my human eye may not be able to see objects faster than MK4, THe camera might be able to stop some thing. I will put 3 frames up, less than one second apart. Look at the middle right side. That blurred dot is some thing. My settings were F5.6 110mm 1/3200 and iso 800. The dot is only on 1 frame.
