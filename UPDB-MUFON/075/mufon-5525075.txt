Was resting on a park bench after walking, looked up and saw very bright glowing object high in the Northeastern sky.

I was walking at Rock Mountain Lake Park in Denver. I had walked a couple of miles and decided to rest on a bench for a minute, which is not something I usually do. I looked up at the sky and saw a very bright sphere shaped object high in the sky. It seemed to be going higher instead in a typical direction. I saw two planes in the air, one headed east and the other west. This was not a plane or helicopter. I observed it for a few minutes and it vanished. When I first saw it I did not believe what I was seeing, because this was my second sighting. I also know for a fact, I was meant to see it, just like the first one I saw. It felt normal to see it.
