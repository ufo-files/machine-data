Large black triangle

I live in Manassas Park, VA and was witness to the large black triangle spotted over Centreville but closer to the weekend of November 6th-7th. At first I simply dismissed it as a plane since I live close to Dulles airport but after a minute of observing I realized it wasn't. I admit I'm not a very good judge of distance and size but I could tell after watching it was far too big and slow moving to be an aircraft. It had bright white lights in what would be the middle rear of the craft in the direction it was moving. It also had blue lights at each tip. The blue lights are what made me stop and think a second that running lights are red (port) and green (starboard).
