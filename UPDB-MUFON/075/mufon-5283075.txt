We seen a lot of lights in the night sky

we was sitting on a quiet night at the front porch on my RIP Brother house in Puerto Rico. When I seen all the lights. I thought they were fireworks and waited for their display but there was none. I kept on looking while having a conversation with my wife and my sister in law. for more than two or three minutes and still there was no display of flairs or explosions or any smoke trails. Then I ask my sister in law what was the lights in the sky and if there any celebration in town. She said no today is Sunday and at this time lots of people are home except the guys that like to hang out. So i ask them do you see the lights as they told me that I'm seeing things; i said come look.then I ask them do you see what I see they said yes so I reply "then you are seeing things too" So I grave my camera t hat I had purchase about three day or so and started to record the lights then as I recorded my camera died and never work again after a big bright light was caught on the lens I disposed the camera the next day because it just stop working even after a new charge.
