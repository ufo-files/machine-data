{tjd} Triangle

Wife and I were out to hopefully see the forecasted northern lights. We seen a few meteors and at first thought we were seeing another. It was a perfect triangle of red orange lights. It was definately one solid triangle with a light on each corner. As it went straight over our heads from west to east it passed over big dipper eclipsing one star. It also passed a commercial airline in same direction like it was standing still. It move at speed similar to the few meteors that we seen but this was some type of craft for sure. It appeared to be 10 times as wide as the commercial jet in comparison. It also seemed to be almost fuzzy or faint in appearance but was definately there. I would expect a sonic boom at this speed but there was not a sound. I hope to someday understand what we saw.
