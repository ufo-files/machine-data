Unusual sighting

In short, clear night, thought I saw a satelight high in the sky at about 11 o'clock, watched for a few moments, saw another smaller one behind it on same path but moving faster. The second caught up with the first appearing as one then both (or the one) a couple of second later, just disappeared. Sky's we're clear, no clouds, they never reappeared.

I've seen many satelights and that's what they looked liked. But never seen them just disappear.

I'm sure this is nothing unless it happens to connect with something else. That's why I'm telling you . Have told no one, not even my wife. Just been thinking about it for a year and decided to report it to you in case it helps connect some dots.
