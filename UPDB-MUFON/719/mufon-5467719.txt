{td} Two different objects passed in front of the moon. Each going a different direction

At approximately 9:30pm on June 24th I was observing the moon through my new telescope when I saw a black round object pass in front of my view of the moon. I grabbed my phone and recorded it passing from right to left. I was only able to catch 3 seconds of video. Being super excited at what I just saw, I held my phone at the ready and continued to watch. Approximately 20 minutes later I saw another black round object pass in front of the moon, this time flying the opposite direction. I was able to catch it for a longer duration with my phone this time. I caught the video using a Nextstar 130SLT and my IPhone 6s.
