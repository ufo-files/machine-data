Showed up in photo

My husband and I went to the dock where the Mayflower II is tied up this morning at dawn. I was facing east taking sunrise photos then turned to the west to get a shot of the buildings lining the shore and the harbor from our place at the end of the dock.

When I downloaded my pictures just now, I noticed in that one shot what looks like a vertical contrail and, below it, a round shape with two rectangular lights. (Neither of us witnessed this prior to seeing it in the photograph.) Of course, the shot becomes pixilated when enlarged but its clear enough that we can both say weve not seen anything like it before. And, its not at all similar to UFOs weve seen pictures of in the past. 

Please note: I checked the other photos I took to see if there was something on my lens that could have caused that image but on no other pictures did that appear.
