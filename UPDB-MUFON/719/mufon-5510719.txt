{dr}Very Bright Teal Green

Was observing sunset to view the planetary alignment of Jupiter, Venus and Mercury due WNW and a light of unusual color Teal-Green caught my attention a few degrees to the North of my viewing area approximately 1/2 hour after I began viewing. I watched curiously as it came from above distant horizon line approximately 1/2 mile to 3/4 mile distance, no sound emanating. I watched it descend into a valley, it was still twilight and could still make out features in the valley. I watched as it descended and ascended three times until I gave up as it was still in the valley and I was getting cold due to a cold snap in the climate, and some strange vocalizations coming from below in the valley in the direction of the object I was viewing. I observed it for approximately 20 minutes. I am an avid stunt kite flier and am sure this was no kite or remote controlled airplane/helicopter. Also a regular backyard astronomer for 38 years, an avid ufologist as well. I was not drinking or under the influence of any drugs at the time of my viewing. Sky was clearing with thin high level stratus clouds above and in the distance that were quickly dissipating due to lack of daylight heating/convection. No wind at all.
