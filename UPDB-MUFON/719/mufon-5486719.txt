Many orbs in northern sky

On Dec. 6,2015 at about 7PM while looking out a 10th floor balcony window I noticed at least 10 or more bright white round orb shape objects appear in the northern sky over about a 30 minute time period. Most appeared as single objects but one time two appeared at once side by side then moved above each at a very close distance. All these orbs dimed and moved in different directions to start but all moved to the west eventually and out of sight.
