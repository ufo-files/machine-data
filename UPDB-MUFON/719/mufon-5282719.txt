Transparent Horseshoe Shaped Craft with 3 Bright White Lights Across Middle

Was standing in driveway placing a dog food order on phone when I looked up and noticed what I 1st believed was 3 stars in a straight line. After a few seconds I looked again because that seemed strange & at that time observed what I could best describe as a Horseshoe Shaped Craft with a Flat Nose...Rounded in Back ...Transparent like Glass with 3 Bright White Lights across the Middle....
Approx 30' to 40 Ft in Diameter... 400 to 500 AGL...moving at about 80 Knots...Completely silent... Less than a 1/4 Mile out from me...Traveling from N.W to S.E. Lost site of it when it went behind Roof Top. Went and got wife and when came back out believed to have reacquired and watched it hover about 3 mile out and up to about 1,200 to 1.500 AGL for about 40 mins. went back in house to get Night Vision Camera and when came back it was gone. While hovering the lights seamed to Pulsate where the were solid when flying over.
I Skydive &n used to work at an Airport so been around Planes & Airfields for over 40 years. Not saying it was Alien...Might have been Military ...but I have never seen anything even remotely like that.
