Two bright silver metallic orb shaped objects vertical to each other moving then swapped axis and seperated

Two bright reflective orbs I noticed vertical to each other on a clear sky against the setting sun I thought it was the sun reflecting on to dissipating clouds but was far too bright and a siver metallic colour the way they were moving was too fast to be a cloud we were driving so it was hard to tell whilst moving and i couldnt believe what i was looking at so i got my partner to pull the car over stop and let me look at them while we were stopped i thought it was clouds then strangely noticed no other clouds in the sky and it was very bright and metallic silver colour not white like a cloud they started to separate and changed axis from top to bottom then split up and went different directions into the sunset months later I was showing someone the photos I took and zoomed in to my amazement two other dark shaped cone like orbs hovering in the horizon as you can see in the photos they move closer towards the sun
