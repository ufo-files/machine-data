{jpg} Cluster of approx 15 spheres rotating oval, translusant red, white, All disappear then reappear

Perfectly clear cloudless afternoon went out to the front porch on a home I was remodeling. Looked up to the north of Cedar Falls and saw approx 15 translucent white/blue spheres rotating moderately in unison to each other. Surmised to myself some local event had occurred and I was seeing small balloons. Noticed it was pretty breezy and wondered how balloons could keep together and rotate equally to each other. Continued watching the spheres rotate in unison and then completely disappear then reappear a few seconds later. Watched this for approx two minutes and then decided to video the event. Once I had my cell phone video ready the spheres fell away from my line of sight behind local neighborhood trees. Googled the local Waterloo Airport tower and phoned them if they had any other reports of anomalies, they had not, other than mine at that time. My entire life, I have been looking skyward and had never witnessed anything couldn1t explain naturally, this incident baffles me. 

I originally posted this info to another site that same day and since have revisited my post and have seen others witnessed/posted this in my local city at the same time, same day, same city..
