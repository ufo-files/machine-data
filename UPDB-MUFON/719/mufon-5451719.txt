Saw some lights in the woods. Drove up the country road to see if we could get better view.

As soon as lights stopped or became not visible I did a 3 point turn. Started back heading west when the next thing I remember is having the gas to the mat tearing out of a field back onto the road. As I got onto the road the engine was revving high tires were spinning but no screeching and not moving. I remember yelling and looking over my shoulder down the tree line where three orbs came flying behind the car about 20 ft up and 20ft behind. They swooped around the trees hovered for a second and I was screaming I was terrified then the lights headed south amd thats when the car finally moved. Looked at the clock and about 2 hours had passed. I didn’t see any entities only I was driving west and next thing I know is tearing out of the field 2 hours later.
