Hovering Lights

We were leaving my Nieces first birthday party. My boyfriend and I always look up into the sky just to see we may spot something out of the ordinary. We approach our car and notice 3-4 lights in close proximity to each other like in a line. We start walking towards the end of the block as the view opens up more and can see more open sky. It stayed there. The 3-4 seemed to be rotating as of around a disk. We follow a light it would trail to the right and disappear, but a new light on the left appeared. We stayed and watched in Awe. Not wanting to be the only person witnessing my boyfriend quickly calls over someone who just so happened to be out in their garage. We point out the lights, he sees them but within seconds the lights simply dim out. We attempted to record but the lighting wouldn’t focus.
