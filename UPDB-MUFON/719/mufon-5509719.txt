{nd} Orange orb that would light up then fade away traveling fairly fast

This UFO was seen by several people at a 4th of July party, plus probably many others around Whitefish, MT. It was suggested that it could have been a hot air balloon that was lit on fire for the 4th, but it seemed to go too fast for that and showed up three different times in the course of two hours. It looked like a flaming or glowing orange ball that rapidly flew across the sky, sometimes becoming very bright. The last time it flew straight over the top of us, and didn't appear to be very high. There was at least ten of us that saw it at the party.
