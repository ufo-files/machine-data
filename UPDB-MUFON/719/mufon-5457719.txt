{rjl} Bright light appeared extremely high up, tic tac shaped craft appeared, then light again

While stopped at a stoplight, my attention was drawn by an impossibly bright light extremely high up in the sky. This lasted two to three seconds, then the light vanished and was replaced by a black, tic tac shaped craft that remained stationary for approximately ten seconds. The light reappeared where the craft had been and lasted for another three seconds before it disappeared altogether on the spot. As soon as it disappeared, the tire pressure light came on in my car. I reset the tire pressure warning before continuing and it didn't come back on again.
