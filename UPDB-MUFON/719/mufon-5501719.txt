{rjl} Disk flying then changed direction near airplane, after flew from one side of the horizon to the other.

I was on break near the airport of san jose at work around noon time in 2009. I was looking up with a coworker at an airplane coming in perpendicular to the airprort runways. We thought it was awkward that it would be flying that way. And behind it we saw a metallic very shiny frisbee shaped that was flying behind, above and against the direction of the plane. the plane was pretty close and the disk was a bit smaller than it. We both said what the heck is that thing, it made a U turn after passing the plane. It was going faster than the plane that was closer and about 2-3 seconds after it made the turn except it vertical U turn like a U rotated 90 degrees, and after it finished the loop it paused for a second or two. After it did that it shot across the sky north towards san fransisco. It moved from one side of the sky to the other in less than a blink of an eye. it was not a plane there is nothing that can move that fast, and I worked at that job for a few months and was outside everyday, I know its not anything that I have ever seen before or since.
