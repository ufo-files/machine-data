4 Orange Orbs Near Santa Clarita

My fiancee, a friend and I were sitting on top of a hill at Valencia Blvd and McBean Pkwy waiting for 4th of July fireworks to start. At 9:05pm were scanning the sky looking for fireworks when I noticed 4 orange orbs in the sky flying over a mountain range belonging to the west end of the Angeles National Forest. At first I thought they were planes because of their speed, but then realized they were an odd color and had no flashing lights at all. They were flying north in a lopsided "V" formation with one in the front center, one on the left, and 2 on the right. After about a minute they slowly changed to a trapezoid formation, similar to the shape of the "Big Dipper" constellation but without the handle. As they continued flying north towards us, we noticed that the bottom-right orb in the formation started to dim, until it was so dim that it was not visible at all anymore. A few seconds later, the lower right orb began to fade until it was not longer visible as well. The last 2 orbs faded out one at a time as well, until nothing could be seen anymore. We were all very curious and interested, and baffled by what they could be. I can confidently rule out them being any form of commercial aircraft due to the lack of appropriate lights (and the fact that the lights went completely out). I cannot completely rule out the possibility that they were Chinese Lanterns, though the speed and seemingly deliberate formations give some evidence against the Chinese Lantern theory.
