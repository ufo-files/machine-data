{rjl} ONE NIGHT IN 1967 WHILE HIKING NORTH OF PASADENA WE SAW A CRAFT HOVERING OVER THE CITY.

FILLED OUT AND FILED AT MUFON HQ FROM A PAPER REPORT BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR 188009 ON 12/05/2019
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One night in around 1967 my friends (about four of them and I) were hiking up to Hennniger Flats campground to spend the night, as we often did. It's about three miles up to the flats and from there you can go all the way up to Mount Wilson Observatory, about twelve miles total. Well about half way up to Henninger Flats we came to the outside of the leg of the mountain giving us a great view over the city of Pasadena. We saw this craft moving over the city lights below from our left (from the east traveling westward) and it stopped in front of us, just hovering.
It had the shape of a disc with a cylinder shape on the top of it.
Kind of like a top hat. Inside the cylinder were large windows, tall square shaped that emitted different colored lights, flashing one after the other in a counter clockwise direction when looking down on top of the vehicle. After 15 to 20 minutes my friend wanted to get back to hiking so we started right back up the hills right after we left. I started thinking that I could hike anytime I wanted to, but I would never see a craft like that again. So I went right back again to see more of the craft. and it had vanished. No warning, no evidence, no sound. I was sad it had left.I have always felt blessed from this experience! Seeing is believing.It frustrates me to hear people say "what if" on documents on television because I know we are not alone. I also believe they watch over us, especially now that we have figured out how to make nuclear bombs. I have also had a lot of time to think of the validity of this craft being one of our own. But if we had this technology in 1967 why would we have to send astronauts to the moon in a much lesser craft 2 years later?
