UFO changes direction then disappears in TN

I was driving down Interstate 24 East and around exit 11 at 0345 an object in the sky caught my eye. At first I thought it was a shooting star, but as I kept watching it, I noticed it was going slower than a normal shooting star. It was also larger than one, and the white color appeared to be pulsating in its brightness. The car in front of me hit their brakes, and I assumed it was because they saw it too. It was moving towards the northeast for approximately 10 seconds and then completely disappeared. Afterwards, I knew I had seen something definitely out of the norm, and almost even stopped the car in front of me to see if they saw it too. I had very good feelings and wasn't fearful.
