just after midnight on new years eve I looked out an upstairs bathroom window to see if I could see some fireworks. Looking north east over the hills I saw a bright orange light heading toward my location. I thought it may have been an airliner. But the light was flickering and not strobing. I went outside to get a better look and saw two more lights moving along with it traveling in the same direction. Then I saw a 4th light. I grabbed some binoculars to get a better look but could not identify any mechanical features. the lights moved rapidly crossing high over head and then faded out as if ascending out of sight.
