Strange Waveform with Distortion Behind it in Picture

I was at Burning Man, it was around 2 am, I was taking long-exposure images with my camera, around 7secs each exposure. I had captured several shots with my camera on a tripod, so no shake, the image is stable in all the shots, and can be confirmed by the stationary items in the shots. I didn't see the light until I was reviewing the images the next day, it is in there, a strange blue waveform, moving what looks like the right to left across the horizon. the moon and some light poles are in the shot and are in the same place in each of the images so it's not a camera shake. I have included 3 images, 1 before, the one with the anomaly in it, and the one after.. these were on auto so i didn't touch the settings or tripod during the entire shoot..
