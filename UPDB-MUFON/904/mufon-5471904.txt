{cak} Star-like bright light, no sound, no movement, watched for several minutes and then it just winked out. No object in sky where it was while shining.

My wife and I were driving to her place of employment in Hazleton around 5 pm. It was still light enough to see aircraft in the sky. As we approached our destination I observed a bright white light in the sky to the south. At first I thought it was just an aircraft light, but it did not move, it just hung there in the sky stationary. I looked for the red & green navigation lights of an aircraft but there were none. After a few minutes, my wife also made a comment about the light and asked me if I saw it. I replied that I did and had been watching it for a few minutes. As we watched and discussed what we were watching the light suddenly blinked out leaving nothing in the sky. I could still see other airplanes in different areas of the sky because it was not yet that dark. I must stress that the light did not fade away, it just instantly went off. Very weird.
