Waking up of something very strange then looking out window to see lights going in circle.

I was living outside of military base in a home In Iraklion Greece. My husband was in the Air Force with a top secret clearance. Something woke me up and there was a presence in the hallway that I could not see. I looked out the window and I seen lights turning in a circle off in a distance. I screamed husbands name several times to wake up. He never woke up. I am not sure what woke me up but there was something in my room that woke me up for sure. The presence was very real and intense feeling. That is what made me look outside to then see the lights going round and round.
