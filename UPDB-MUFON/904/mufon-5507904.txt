{rjl} My nephew saw it first, I observed it heading South, no sound, disappeared

My nephew called to me from outside my Mother in Laws home in Thompson Settlement, a small community SW of Jonesville, Va. I went outside to see what he had observed. A shiny domed craft was observed in 100 percent visibility skies flying South at an undetermined altitude. No sound, no vapor trail. The craft proceeded in a South Direction, then disappeared completely and instantly. There was no where for it to go, and there were no other craft visible in the sky at that time. The sky was blue with no clouds visible. At first I thought it might be an aircraft that had already gone by, but there was no vapor trail and no noise, there also were no wings visible. Speed was undetermined, but not real fast.
