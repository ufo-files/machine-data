{rjl} Spotted a silver, circular object with colored lights in the dusk sky over the NY Thruway in Sept 1982

Twice while i was riding with my family on the NYS Thruway near Nanuet NY in the fall of 82/83 when i was 11-12, i looked off into the sky and saw the a circular, silver craft with strips of colored lights rotating lit around it. The object was moving seemingly parallel in the same direction as the car but i couldn't at what seemed the same speed but maybe was a faster rate. I looked away for a few seconds and when i looked back it was gone. I didn't mention it to my parents or brother but i've remembered it all these years and finally got validation through all the others who've seen things in the Hudson Valley during that time. It was solid in color as i could see it against the dusk sky and the lights seemed to be almost built into the craft all the way around.
