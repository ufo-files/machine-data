Exact scenario as reported on July 14, 1952 over Chesapeake

I was watching the "Hangar 1" program on Netflix when a story of 2 commercial pilots flying over the Chesapeake Bay on July 14, 1952 observed 6-10 red lights arranged in a straight line. I witnessed the exact same phenomena on Halloween 2020. There were 6-10 red lights in the Eastern sky arranged at a 45 degree angle. They moved slowly, in unison, towards the NorthEast. After about 5 minutes, they disappeared one by one as they reached a certain position. Two to three days prior, I observed a spotlight in the same vicinity. It didn't appear to come from the ground, rather appeared to be moving in a 360 degree horizontal pattern. I would see about 180 degrees of rotation, then it would disappear momentarily before repeating the cycle. I've seen this light a few times since then, but not regularly. Each time it has been around 8:00 p.m. I've also seen other lights moving in the same flight pattern, but it was hard to discern if it was native aircraft or not.
