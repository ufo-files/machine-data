Bright White light travelling across Cardiff

I was walking through a park that at night has no light sources so I always look up at the stars. This time I immediately spotted a bright white light. Certainly twice as bright as the brightest star in the sky (which had no clouds). It was moving at around the same speed as a conventional aircraft would at around 40,000 feet although it did not possess the usual pulsating blue/red lights. There was a plane in the sky at the time and so I could see in comparison that the UFO was neither a plane or helicopter. The object travelled the whole way across the sky, in more or less a straight line but was wobbly and "clumsy". About half way it stopped briefly then continued into the distance until it was no longer observable
