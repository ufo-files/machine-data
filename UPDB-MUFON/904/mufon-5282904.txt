Close distance UFO encounter.

This encounter happened in 2008, or 14 years ago at 00:45. When I had this encounter, I was 6 years old. One night, I woke up in the middle of the night. I didn't know what to do or how to make myself sleepy and go back to sleep. So I decided to look through the window to see some stars. When I was gazing for stars, I happened to notice that something was blocking my view to my right, something black in color. So I tried to find out who it was. Maybe my seepy eyes played tricks on me or something. But then, when I looked at it more closely, I noticed some of its details. Since the encounter was in our block of flats' little car parking lot, there was a staircase outdoor lamp that covered the whole parking lot. That light helped me to see that metallic reflection on the object and spot that that thing was moving in a place hovering in the air near my window. When I absorbed all of this knowledge, I was terrified of the encounter, which serves as a reminder to me today that that day actually occurred. Then, out of curiosity, I attempted stretching to see what was below, and I immediately noticed the blue lights. I was really scared that day. I tried to wake up my mother, but she was still sleeping, so I decided to take a picture of it. However, when I returned with the phone and attempted to take a picture, it flew straight ahead with a slight shift to the right. I believe it used warp drive to escape.
