2 orange like orbs that vanished

4 friends were outside visiting on a Friday night. We had sat there for atleast 4 hours and had the porch light on. We had turned it off probably hours before Because the moon was out as well as the stars and it was a nice light. I believe someone had brought up the stars and i remember looking up and trying to find the brightest star in the sky. At which point I notice 2 orangish glowing lights one above the other unlike anything I had seen before. We all have seen planes as well as the light they have on the front when they are landing. This was nothing like that. I jumped out of my seat and started yelling at them to look and asking what that was. They all also witnessed with me and then the top one just vanished and almost immediately after the other one just vanished.
