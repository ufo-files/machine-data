non blinking white object

my husband and i were on my deck and he said gina look and this white round object was coming towards us.i got my camera which took about 10 seconds,my 11 year old daughter then came out with me,the object stayed lit,was now over us and i video taped it as it went away from us for about 17 seconds.there were 3 planes around us probably in a five mile radius,not commercial planes small ones they all blinked this one didn't ant the object was about the same altitude as the planes.we could here the planes in the distance but this made no noise as it came over us and the planes were going in a different direction as the object.me have a small municipal airport in Plymouth so i see many planes day and night and this was not a plane or helicopter or even a glider,it had no blinking lights at all.it traveled towards the direction of an almost full moon and were not sure if it faded out or if we just couldnt see it past the moonlight.we knew it wasnt a plane and too close to be a star of satellite.my daughter got nervous and i think i was too but i was trying to focus on getting the video and my husband was kinda freakin out.i happened to get a phone call while i was trying to video and was trying not to say anything to this person while it was going on,i ended the call while taping and was trying to whisper to my husband that i was taping it.its still saved to my memory card in my camera just because the image seems better when you see it thru the LCD screen.its on the video im sending but it def seems clearer from my camera.
