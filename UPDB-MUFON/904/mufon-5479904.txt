{dr}Strange object flying slowly in the sky.

We recorded this strange object over our house in Rochester NY. It was moving very slowly and looked almost star like. Once I zoomed in with my camcorder some unusual details emerged. The first clip shows the object at high altitudes and at various focal ranges. The trees serve as good reference points to how slow this appeared to be moving. The second clip shows the anomaly much closer just above the tree line. It looked like there was a ring of orbs or some mass circling within the object and pulsating. It was such a big mass in the sky we expected it would be covered on the news that night and speculated it was a new type of drone or weather balloon experiment. But nothing was ever reported and we couldn't find a local explanation to account for it. 

From 80178 - same incident reported again /cms/dr

My brother and I witnessed this strange object moving slowly through the atmosphere over house in Rochester NY on August 17th, 2016 from 6:30pm to 7:15pm.

The object was coming in from the North East and about 55 degrees above the horizon. 

The first clip shows the object moving at a high altitude and a almost star like speed. We thought at first it might be a planet or celestial body moving in low orbit.

The second clip shows the object at much closer range, just above the tree-line, with what appears to be a pulsating ring of lights circulating around the structure.

I was able to record the anomaly at 1200mm with my Sony telephoto lens and camcorder.

It was such a big mass in the sky we expected it would be covered on the local news that night and speculated it was a weather balloon or some new drone experiment. 

But nothing was ever reported and we couldn't find a local explanation for the sighting.
