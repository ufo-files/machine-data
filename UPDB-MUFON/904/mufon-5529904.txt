Our 11 year old daughter saw same UFO the morning our house burned down

On January 28th our daughter reported seeing a cigar shaped object hovering over the treeline at her school, we reported it to MUFON. One week later on the same day, Friday, our son saw the same object hovering over our house. We also reported that sighting. One week later, on Saturday morning Feb 9th our daughter woke up and saw the same UFO hovering outside the front of our house at approx 5 AM. She also noticed smoke coming from her floor and woke us up. The front of our house was completely engulfed in flames. We all escaped and were completely unharmed with the exception of one of our dogs. The house was completely destroyed. The UFO she saw was the same one she reported seeing at her school two weeks before. It was cigar shaped and large, approx. the size of a two story house. Both our daughters description and our sons one week prior to the fire were the same.
