{cak} UFO appearance with 3 cylindrical light circles and a trail line

My family and I had just finished eating at a restaurant. As we were heading to our car, my wife remarked on the "alien looking cloud" in the sky. We all had never seen a cloud like this so we all took out our phones and snapped a few photos.

As we sat in the car to drive home, we reviewed our photos before setting off and were amazed that my camera seemed to have capture a UFO on the far left.

At first I thought it was glare from the lamp post but at closer inspection there appear to be 3 circular lights coming from it and a trail line beneath it. 

The UFO was not visible to our naked eyes and only showed up on camera. Camera glare or UFO? Only you can decide but I am convinced this is extraterrestrial.
