Object with 5 lights on the vertex of a pentagon. One is brighter. Seen almost on the zenith. Very far (h>1000m)

This happened during the CANSAT (CAN Satellite) contest on may 25 of 2019 at 12:53 hrs in Cd. Universitaria, México City. While taking photos of the drone and the satellite, I noticed a very small light near the field of view of the camera moving in one direction and changing direction after a while. It was hardly seen with the naked eye, so most of the people in the event didn't noticed it. The photos are my best and are taken with a Canon 6D equipped with a 300 mm telephoto. I don't know what this is this but I would like to know if you have received material of similar objects. Perhaps it is a spy drone or something like that (I'm a scientist and personally, I don't believe that tripulated interstellar ships are visiting us). I'm sending the original pictures for you to process them as you like. Even on the pictures it is hard to recognize the small dot near the drone and the CANSAT. Once amplified, the pictures show a very strange shape. I've never seen anything like that before.
