Bright lights unnaturally hovering in sky.

I was walking home from work, same route as I always take, and as I turned the corner and could see the sky in front of me, there was anywhere from 10 to 15 glowing lights, similar to stars, but moving.

There was a very dark storm cloud further in the background, which really helped to contrast the glowing lights, and in my opinion, they hovered in a way that would appear to be similar to someone investigating or researching the storm cloud. They floated around, and at one point almost began to join into a formation, before slowly dispersing one by one into the storm cloud.
