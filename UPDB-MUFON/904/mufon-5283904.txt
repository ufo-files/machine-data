Multiple objects of various sizes in lighted skies at night

On November 4, 2021 at 9:00 pm, while driving on interstate I-40 East right at the I-140 exit near Castle Hayne NC (New Hanover county), the skies were lighting up with beautiful orange, white and yellowish colors for no explainable reason. Sunset that day was at 6:15:41 pm. The skies went from being normally dark to having all these beautiful sunset brilliant colors. I started taking photos with my iPhone and it was not until later that evening while viewing my photos did I see all the unexplainable objects in the sky. Some were oblong, some were bright white in formation flight, and some appeared saucer or rectangular in shape. There was no noise, as I did roll my window down to take some of the photos. I also noticed the traffic abruptly slowing down as it was evident others were observing the colors of the skies.
