light makes a z pattern and approaches my car

Me and my girlfriend were on an oil lease road in the country within 3 miles of town of barnsdall on the road from barnsdall to bartlesville. We were parked and witnessed a light make a sharp z pattern in the sky and it was proceeding towards us directly. We were both extremely frightened. I started the car and drove towards town and put the light behind us. We went as fast as we could until we got to town and we were very relieved that we were not captured, which was the impression that I had. It was trying to get us. Sometimes I wonder what would have happened if I would have stopped. It made a very strong impression on me.
