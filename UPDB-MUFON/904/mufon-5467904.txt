{cak} I saw a flash of light fall from sky. Then 33 airplanes fly over area.

I was sitting on my porch and at 12:15 I saw a yellow ball about the size of a tennis ball to the eye in the horizon. It fell straight down for what looked like ten to fifteen feet. It was really bright. I first thought i saw a meteor drop through the sky. I knew right away that I just saw something amazing. Within one to two minutes i saw a plane (I think) flying over the area and thought that was pretty strange. This is where it gets weird and I knew I was seeing something amazing. Two more planes flew over pretty close to each other. Then one more, then three more together. In ten minutes ten planes had flew through the sky in roughly the same area. I was amazed. I called 911 to report it. My phone line was completely full of static but the operator said I was "clear as a bell." I don't think they took me serious but a deputy did show up and we watched two more planes fly over at different times. By four a m. I counted thirty three planes and the last three turned around and flew back in the direction they came from. I know witnessed something that was once in a lifetime and now i cant stop looking at the sky. I have not seen one plane fly over this area since. But in four hours I saw thirty-three???!!!
