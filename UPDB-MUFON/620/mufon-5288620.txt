Heading west along Highway 91 in Richmond BC after attending a birth in new Westminster. Driving west in clear conditions. Not tired. Glowing lime green (pulsing) orb came out of the west from the sky and came towards me at the level of car roof height and then follow the outline of the car hood and slowly passed the windscreen about 1 foot off the car surface. There was a buzzing feeling or sound - electrical feeling - and then it was gone. The experience is still so clear. I reported it the next morning to our local university astronomers. They did not know what it could be. It was definitely something different and special and I have never experienced anything like it since
