{cak} Two UFOS during rush hour

Saw two flying orbs a few weeks before this sighting at night but couldn’t tell what it was. This sighting was during rush hour in Houston. They look like meteors and they could be but they moved fluidly and one flew straight, not like it was falling. I looked for flashing lights like on a plane but there were none. And I looked for contrails too. They both looked gray and reflective like from the sun. I guess what really caught my eye was the way they flew and the fact that there were two. I recorded it on my phone and a few seconds after I stopped, I saw a jet that had the contrails behind it and it looked like nothing i had seen. I was just wondering opinions on what it could be. Sorry for the poor description but I hope you’re able to zoom in. There are two, one goes behind the power lines for a moment. Thank you for your time.
