{jpg} Floating City

Traveling south on Route 17 in Ramsey, New Jersey. Both my wife and I observed a city directly ahead of us en-route to Olive Garden restaurant, floating in the clouds and gleaming with a pinkish, sunset-type hue. The buildings were all in neat rows, and all buildings were rectangular in shape. There were a few that resembled skyscrapers, and there was a very prominent skyscraper in the front row of buildings, approximately in the middle of the row. There are no cities of this resemblance in northern New Jersey anywhere relatively south of our observed location.
