White lights (brighter than the stars) traveling in a straight line from the NW to the SE towards greater Cincinnati, OH area. At one time in the sky I counted 13 lights moving at the same speed, but there were more appearing after counting. You could not have used a ruler to mark a straighter path than they were following. The lights never got any closer or further apart from each other. It was approximately 6:15 a.m. (Dec. 11, 2019). I was totally stunned by what I was seeing and unable to explain.
