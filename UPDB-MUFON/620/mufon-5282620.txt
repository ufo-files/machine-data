A bright colored object over the Hudson River

It was 5 15am when I woke up. I looked outside my window and saw a bright light in the sky over the trees, very far. I thought it looked bigger than a planet and looked like a headlight of an airplane but unlike an airplane it did not move. it just stood there and sometimes the light would go off completely but other times it would be brightly lit. I would say it was blinking. The light would go on and off. I watched it for about 1 hour 15 minutes then had to leave for work
