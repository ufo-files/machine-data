(GS) Very bright orange red glowing lights or spheres.

My son and I were driving south into Fulton, Mo on business 54 (north bluff street) and I noticed 5 very bright lights hovering over downtown Fulton. After about a minute 3 of the 5 disappeared and the 4th a minute later. We pulled over and observed the 5th hover for over 3 minutes before it rose into the clouds.  I was moving north very slowly and looked like it was rising into the clouds. There was no noise from the source of the lights.  I have iPhone video but not sure of the quality of the video until I load it onto my computer.
