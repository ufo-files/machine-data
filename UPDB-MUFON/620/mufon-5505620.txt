{rjl} While vacationing in Florida my daughter and I both witnessed a craft hovering above the pool late one night,from separate balchonies.

Vacationing in Florida one summer I woke late one night and stepped out on the balchony. There I observed a large disk shaped object with windows along the rim. Inside were several people dressed in lab coats. Many others had come out on their balchonies to watch/ We all went back to bed and forgot. Twenty-four years later my daughter, who was in a different suite on a different floor at the time of the incident, brought it up. She remembered the same thing as I. Then while watching a program showing various crafts she and I both recognized what we had seen. The individuals within the craft did not appear to be of alien origin but rather quite human. I have no recollection of the craft departing. What I recall is seeing other observers simultaneously turning as if in a daze, myself included, and returning to bed as though it never happened. Certainly the potential number of witnesses could be in the hundreds.
