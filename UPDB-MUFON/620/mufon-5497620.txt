{dr} saw a bright white light that seemed round with a vertical shape under the light. A red light flashed at times. When it got light out it withdrew.

It was @5:30am I was looking out a 2nd floor window of my house (which I often do when I have awakened early)facing west when I noticed a bright white light in the almost starless night. I could see it slightly moving so I thought it was a helicopter at first, but it remained hovering for at least 45 minutes. There was an oblong shape underneath it (it was somewhat far away, but not as far as a real star)and it flashed a red light intermittently. At one point an airplane (? it flew like a plane and had a light) flew from the south in front of the bright light and disappeared to the north. As it began to get light out (I watched it approx. 45 mins) I saw the light withdraw to the west as if at a very high speed. Did not look or act like a helicopter, airplane, drone or anything else I've ever seen. It was too far away to see any details, other than the lights and a vertical shape underneath. I felt when I was watching this object at first that maybe it was some sort of military helicopter (live near a base) but when it continued to hover in the same place for a long period of time it seemed rather odd. I actually went downstairs and opened the front door to see if I could hear anything out of the ordinary (no). The bright light never dimmed until it disappeared in the western sky. It looked like it was almost being pulled to the west and the light was bright until it was a tiny speck, then gone. I woke my husband to come look at this light, which he did and agreed it seemed strange.
