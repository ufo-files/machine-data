Taking photos of Nashville, Tn skyline including AT&T building

I took pictures of Nashville, Tn skyline. When I cropped the picture I noticed an object in upper right corner in the picture. I immediately took a video because I could not see the object without looking thru the camera/phone. Initially I thought it was a reflective distortion from my windshield. After further review I noticed the clouds passing in front of the object and lights traversing on the object in the beginning of the video. I also noticed, from additional still photos, that the 2 whitish wing like structures changed positions from above the object to below the object before it vanished. Please note that If the clouds were not moving in front of the object, partially obscuring it I would have dismissed this as a distortion/reflection from my windshield. It also seemed to be projecting something or that something was being projected.
