{cak} Strange flashing lights moving across the sky.

I was walking, with my dog, the short distance between my mailbox and my apartment. I usually look up at the stars while walking outside at night. At first I thought it might just be a plane until I noticed the strange behaviour of its lights. I considered using my phone to film it but I figured that my phone camera was not good enough to see anything. I came back in my apartment to look up aircraft lights before I reported it as a UFO. I lost sight of it by coming back indoors to ask my family about it.
