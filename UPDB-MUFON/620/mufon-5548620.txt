Silver shiny flying object

I was walking home along my street from my Spanish class at the community college, when I happened looked to my right (north) and saw a silver, disk shaped object flying across the sky at a great distance, I first though it must have been a jet, but there was no jet trail, and the object was moving faster than any jet I had ever seen. I then realized it wasn't even shaped like a jet, more like the classic disk shape. It was moving in a linear fashion from east to west across the northern sky, also, there were no clouds in the sky. I felt a feeling of wonder as I watched it move, I had recently began researching UFOs in my spare time, to see if there was anything to the phenomenon, a subject I now consider to be a realty. I eventually lost sight of it when it disappeared behind the roof of the house I was walking past, when I got past the house, it was gone.
