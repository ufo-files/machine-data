{tjd} Captured red orb of light on video, with unbelievable changes in movement and trajectory. Also emitting what appears to be a bright flash of some sort

On August 25th 2019, a friend and I were camping at Buck Creek/Ranger Creek campgrounds. I was sitting by the fire I had going, waiting for the fire to burn out. It was about 1:00am, I had music on my phone going silently. All the other campers in my range of site/area appeared to be sleeping, no other fires were going that I could see. As I sat in my chair I was star gazing the few stars I could see through spotted openings in the cloud cover. I looked off in the distance to my left, at what appeared to be a fire. While I was staring at it, I realized fire does not float. I will leave my video recording, from my Samsung S8 zoomed in at 5x, to speak for itself, about what happened next.
