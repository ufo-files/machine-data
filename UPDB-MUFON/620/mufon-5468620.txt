{td} Suddenly appeared out of nowhere and was very bright

It was about 10:30pm when I gathered the trash to take out when I opened the front door and took barely a step outside when I saw the object. It immediately caught my eye as it was eye level and very very bright. I called for my mom to come to see it as she had talked about seeing it before but in a different direction. We both stood in front of the door and could clearly see the object in the sky. It was as if it knew we saw it because it began to pulsate, its brightness growing for a few seconds before fading. It did this the entire time we watched it. Thinking I could see a clearer image of it, I came back inside and grabbed our binoculars before returning to our front porch. I fiddled with the sight for a bit but every time I thought I got a them focused, the object would move out of sight. It would go behind the trees and then reappear when I looked at it without binoculars. This happened for about 6 minutes off and on before, when the last time, I got focused on the object. When I did, it completely disappeared behind the tree and I could no longer see it so my mom and I went back inside. That's when I looked out the kitchen window and it had came back but for only a few seconds or however long I looked away to tell my mom it had disappeared and came back. But when I looked again, it was gone. I could no longer see it barely peeking through the trees. There were no planes in our view, nothing that appeared to be a satellite, and it was alone so it was not a star constellation. The way it moved was also odd and nothing a random airline plane could do and it made no noise that we could tell.
