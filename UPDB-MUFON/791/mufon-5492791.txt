{dr}3 Red/Orange lights in a tiangle formation,like a pyramid shape,one at the top two at bottom

Me and my best friends where walking down the Long Island Railroad heading to my friends house,i just happen to look up in front of us and I see these 3 red/orange glowing circles in a tiangle formation,i was like look!holy crap what is that!We all stood there in amazement not having any idea what we are looking at,just that it doesent look like anything we have ever seen in the sky before.Well,all my life I have allways wondered what that was we saw that night,well last night I was watching Hanger 1 and one of the stories was about some kids that saw a triangle of lights on Long Island NY back in 1977,i was blown away!That is exactly what me and my friends saw!It has been so long I just cant remember what the lights did,all I can remember is the vision of seeing them in a triangle.I'am about to try and get a hold of one of the guys that was with me that night and see what he remembers about it.I just cant believe I saw the same thing that i saw on the show,i almost dident sleep one bit last night thinking about it,lol.Well it feels pretty neat knowing that we were not the only people that saw that,that night.
