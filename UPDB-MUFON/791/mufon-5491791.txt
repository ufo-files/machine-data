{dr}During fireworks a reboot orb appeared, hovered, glided me then quickly dimmed out to nothing.

At around 9:30 pm my neighbors the street were doing small ground fireworks in their driveway(nothing big that would pop in the sky). While sitting on my couch I caught sight of a red orb that appeared in sky above the tree line. My first thought was it was a firework, but it didn't explode or drift downward it instead glided NW. I ran out of the house to get a better look and it was an orb gliding slowly in a NW direction pulsating red/orange. I ran back in to grab phone/camera and dragged my husband out. When I got back it was a much smaller orb still only red orange it hadn't traveled a long distance and it then just faded out. I was unable to get photo. Approx 30 min later a plane traveled the same path but remained visible, with the flickering lights expected from a plane. Ft. Bragg and Pope Airforce base are both nearby I am used to seeing their aircraft during training. This was different, it did not move as quickly it did not have flickering lights it remained a red orange orb. It did not drop it glided slowly and then fadeded quickly.
