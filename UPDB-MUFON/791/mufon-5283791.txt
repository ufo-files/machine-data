cylindrical blimp like object moving extremely slowly

I was driving home at night from choir practice and almost home, I happened to look up out my window and saw what I thought was a blimp but it was cylindrical in shape with no hanging structure where the blimp pilots usually would sit. It was almost like a plane without any wings. There was a row of windows on the "blimp" itself. I stopped at a light and continued to watch it. It was fascinating. I originally thought maybe it was a new version blimp or new kind of vehicles that was being tested. It was dark but the moon gave some light to observe the vehicle. I have never seen anything move so slowly. We had watched blimps at Sea World but this was really different. I went home and told my husband about what a cool vehicles it was. The next night, we were out and we both observed the same type of vehicle. It even just kind of hung in the air. After we saw it, I thought how dumb that we didn't at least take a picture of it. It could easily be some new plane someone is testing or maybe Disney is looking at new options to move the guests at the new Star Wars Hotel. We are also about an hour from Kennedy Space Center. It was not terrifying at all but really cool. Wonder if anyone else observed something like this.
