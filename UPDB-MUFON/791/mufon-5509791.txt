{dr}4-5 lights appear on eastern horizon and then slowly vanish

While watching local fireworks display from the farm, I noted 4-5 dim lights appear on the eastern horizon...the lights slowly increased in intensity and formed a large triangular shaped, which moved very slowly southward. The top two objects then assumed a matching altitude, merged into one light and then disappeared. The remaining lights slowly dimmed and then disappeared. The lights were very low on the eastern horizon from my viewing position. A few minutes later, a single light would appear further to the north of the original incident, and then very slowly dim out of view.
