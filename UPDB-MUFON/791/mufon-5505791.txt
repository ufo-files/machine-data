{rjl} My wife and i were traveling by car to visit her parents when as we topped the top of the mtn. it crossed in front of us. It appeared round in shape with lights circling on the bottom. Decended down into a hollow at about 35 or 40 degrees.

Traveling to visit my wifes parents around Xmas in 1975. As reached the summit of the mtn. directly in front of us at about 100ft above us we saw a disc shaped craft with blinking lights on the bottom going in a circular motion. I first thought it was a helicopter flying to low so I quickly rolled my window down to hear it but there was hardly any sound, sort of a hum. I was traveling in about a 30 or 40 degree decent. There was a pretty heavy fog and it was quickly out of our sight. Concerned it might crash I stopped the car to listen but heard no sounds. The object appeared to be traveling at about 30 mph. We were young at the time with one baby and we decided not to tell anyone about it in case they would make lite of us. I have now seen another 35yrs. later and I know it is about time to tell of what we have seen.
