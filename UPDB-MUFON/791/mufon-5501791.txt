{dr}I was driving and south and turned left at an inter section, and I looked back and saw a pulsating changing fireball.

I was driving to Raymond, which is south, then I took a left at an inter section and headed east. I looked in my rear view mirror, and saw the object. Approximately 1/2 mile behind me to the west. I sped up and got to the place I was going to. I was stirred up a little bit. When I first noticed the object, it appeared to be a flame. It was flickering like a flame, or pulsating, and changing from one color to another. It stayed in one place. I turned north on a road, and didn't see it anymore.
