(GS) It hovered over I-70 and took off in lightening speed straight up

I was turning onto the I-70 W exit when the cars in front of me began to slow down, to around 40 mph. Then I immediately noticed the bright lights hovering so close that it was seen easily from my front car window. I thought I was seeing a strange reflection from the window and just when I got a good look at it and could see it had two very large lights coming off its ends, it then took off at lightening speed and that is when I realized that I just seen my first UFO and looked at my car clock to get the time. My adrenaline was pumping so much that I still cant seem to settle down.
