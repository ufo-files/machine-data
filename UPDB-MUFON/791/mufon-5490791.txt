{jpg} A triangular UFO with 3 white light points and a circle of bright flashing colors on belly

A friend and I were traveling at night (~2130 hrs) on a rural country highway in Godwin, Sampson County, NC, when we observed three bright white lights, in the general shape of a triangle, enter our field of vision. We stopped at a crossroads as this happened and continued to observe. It became apparent this object was definitely a dark triangular object, about the size of a nickel from our vantage point, with three bright white lights - one at each vertex - with a ring of flashing, multi-colored lights on its underside. We rolled down the windows and found the object was noiseless, at least from our location. We approximated our distance from the object to be roughly one to five miles judging by its size and speed against the night sky. It moved at a relatively modest and consistent rate of speed from the south-southeast in a generally northerly direction until it disappeared from our view due to the tree line. The event lasted approximately one to two minutes. We also noted there was a strong electrical storm further southwest in the Fayetteville, NC, area.
