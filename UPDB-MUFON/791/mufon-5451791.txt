Lights in tight circular format

Was looking at full moon to the south of my home. Turned to go inside and noticed bright light on the sky. Further noted it was numerous lights on a tight circular formation. Estimate height lower than an airliner but was stationary. Watched for about 1-2 minutes Then thought of taking a photo and video but phone was inside. Ran into house and back out in less than ten seconds but object was gone. Retired military so very well trained in aircraft identification. I have never witnessed anything like this so I cannot say what it was.
