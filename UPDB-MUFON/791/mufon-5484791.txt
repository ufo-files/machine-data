{nd} small white flash in west then dark orange object losing color to black going east. No FAA lights that are usually very apparent.

We were watching the skies towards the South in Ahwatukee. Aircraft frequently depart and are readily identifiable. At approximately 7:30pm, in dark skies a small white flash occurred at about 80 degrees elevation in the western sky over the Estrella Mountains. The flash diminished to a low orange glowing object that appeared triangular. The color appeared to further diminish to black as it proceeded silently and rapidly in a straight line exiting to the east. I could not determine altitude but the orange surface approximated an aspirin. The surface was not a solid dull orange but had lines of glowing orange on a black surface.
