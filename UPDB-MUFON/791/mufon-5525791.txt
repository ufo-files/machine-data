Massive Fleet in SouthEastern Sky

I and two other whitnesses were scanning the sky and watching for fireworks and as we were doing so, I saw a group of what looked like 3 flares that just shot up. They were coming up over the distant tree line that was about three miles away. They were well above the tree line, then a group of them appeared, a sort of very bright red orange lights, about twenty or twenty-five of them. They were moving southwest. Lasted about five to ten minutes, before appearing to disappear within the clouds or just fade out.
