I was flying my drone on a set GPS course as im watching it in the sky I see a plane. I see a white orb by the right wing. Then the orb follows the plane and changes direction. My drone malfunctioned and I took my eye off the orb and was not able to find it in the sky again. It was white and when it was over my head it flickered black and white like a football spiraling in the air.
