{rjl} Starlike UFO spotted above that slowly moved east with short directional changes

My boyfriend and I just got home from a friend's house. I wanted to do some star gazing but needed to clean up the kitchen first. The TV was on while we were putting stuff away. It turned off on its own out of nowhere. We were a little freaked out and then saw our cat sitting below the TV staring at nothing. I called his name and offered food but he was being creepy and wouldn't even look at me. I turned the light on and called his name again. He still wouldn't look at me! So I went over and picked him up and turned the tv back on. I put him down and decided to get some fresh air so I went to my driveway to look at some stars. I look directly up and see a starlike object that's moving. No blinking lights or anything. I got 3 videos of it and pieced it together. The first video I pointed my camera phone straight up and didn't move at all. This way you can see the direction/ movements it was making without any camera shaking. I then stopped the video and started another one once it was over my neighbors house. They have a big tree in their front yard so you can see that I wasn't moving the camera at all. It's a good reference point! I then took one more video of the same but not as zoomed in. I then replay all 3 videos but in fast forward so you can see the weird path a little easier. Enjoy!
