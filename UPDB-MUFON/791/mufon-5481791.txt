A friend of mine and I went night fishing at a lake called Emma. Its just north of Topeka Indiana. We were sitting in the float tubes on a calm summer night. You could hear a pin drop. There was no wind either. At about 3 am I was facing the shoreline and my friend was facing the other direction fishing deep water. When I went to cast I saw a blue fireball appear about 30ft above the trees right in front of me, then another and another until it formed a giant circle. The second the first "afterburner" connected to the last one the whole circle ignited. I looked towards my friend because the light was so bright. Then I realized I could see for miles beyond the lake. It was like night turned into day. I looked back at the circle and right when i did...Boom the exact sound you hear when a jet breaks the sound barrier. I know because I have seen it a few times at air shows. In that second it just vanished. In other words it went from a dead stop to breaking the sound barrier in a split second. No human made craft can do that. The scary part was before the lights. We never heard nor saw anything. It hovered over our heads to get to its take off point and we had no clue. I know it sounds crazy. At first I thought I dozed off and dreamed it, but after the light and the bang my friend starting freaking the hell out. I knew then what I saw was real. People can deny all they want. I know now they are out there...It all happened it about 4 seconds. I was too stupified to even try to say "hey look" to my friend. Take what you will from this. There is no exaggeration. It is all 100% truth.
