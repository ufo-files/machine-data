White cylinder, black

Saw a broad daylight UFO this morning! Was driving north on Channing St.. Brilliant clear blue sky. Noticed a white flying object headed Northeast. Since something seemed off about it l looked several times. It had no wings or tail that were visible, and had a black "nosecone" on the front. Although it was difficult to judge the distance, it was clearly the wrong size. If it was a high up jetliner, it was moving too fast (only visible for maybe 5 seconds, traversing maybe 60 degrees of sky from noticing it till disappearance), and no contrail or smoke. If it was a small Lear jet, that was closer, it would be too small for passengers, judging by the apparent speed and objects length. It looked like a cartoonish rocket without wings or fins.
