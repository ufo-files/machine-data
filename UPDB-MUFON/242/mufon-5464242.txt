Observed object hovering then it flew over our heads.

I was I think 9 or 10 years old. Some of my brothers and friends were playing football outside our house when we observed an object hovering over a block of flats approx 100ft tall? We commented that it looked strange and questioned if it was a helicopter. The amount of flashing lights and the noise we said no. We kept watching it and it moved rapidly to the left approx 100metres within a split second then moved back to its original position and stopped again. Then suddenly it flew over our heads and we ran I to our houses screaming. I have since spoke to one of my brothers in 2018 and he told me the same story without any promting. The noise and the lights where fantastic. I can remember it as if it was yesterday. It still gives me goosebumps today.
