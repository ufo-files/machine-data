I witnessed a pulsating all red object in my backyard

When I lived in Fowler,Ca in 2014 I was out in my backyard we lived in the country surrounded by grapevines & this was around 7:30 PM on a very clear night on January 15 on a Tuesday. I suddenly saw an all red pulsating squarish rectangular object with rounded edges gliding over my yard coming from the East. It glided silently right over my yard & over the vineyards at a distance it was about as high as a two story house or so. It kept the same speed the entire time. It came across from my left going West then it made S turns then headed South in a straight path. I watched it for a good 30 minutes or so I remember this because, I looked at the time before I went outside and also during as I ran inside to check the time I wanted my husband to see it but he didn't want to come out then i ran back out to watch it again. I watched it until I couldn't see it anymore. It was heading towards Selma the next town after Fowler. It was something I never saw before. It must've been the size of an adult bike? I did not see anybody around. Back then drones were not the topic. It did not act like anything I am familiar with no windows just a solid all red pulsating slow moving object. It seemed Intelligent and knew where it wanted to go. Just strange for sure. I submitted a hand drawing of the event. Thank you.
