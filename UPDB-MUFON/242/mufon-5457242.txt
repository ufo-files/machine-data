{jpg} Two blue beings walked into house through windows

As reported to ASD in person: "My husband and I were asleep when a humming noise, vibration, and light woke me up. I saw a stationary bright light outside our bedroom windows shining a beam through the pulled shades into the room. I woke up my husband and he and I sat up in bed. Then we both saw two human-like blue beings walk through the windows and wall into the room. They were about 4 1/2 feet tall with one slightly taller than the other. They had no hair and they were wearing some kind of silvery jumpsuit. They also glowed. We were in shock, and sat there, paralyzed while one walked over to my side of the bed and the other walked through our bedroom into the hallway. I sat there, staring into the eyes of the being for what seemed like an eternity, but the whole thing lasted about 10 minutes or less. The next thing we knew it was morning and we got up and got ready for work as usual, when my husband said, what happened last night? And then we talked about it and we both recalled the events exactly the same way. We looked outside on the ground and saw no indentations. We just want to know what is going on." Witnesses wish to remain anonymous.
