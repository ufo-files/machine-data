Bright orb light flashing off on dimming..5 occasions..once saw 2

I first saw the object Sept.6 2018 just before dark. Was sitting on my couch and this bright light across the bay in front of the mountain caught my eye. It was round and bright white. I took pictures. It blinked off and on but stayed in the same spot. It remained there for approximately 1 hour. I also took videos. At times it would blink off completely and reappear minutes later. It finally went out completely about 30 minutes after dark. I spoke to a few friends and family members showing them the pictures and videos. I also posted them on Facebook. I was excited and very hyped up..however this has been my 5th UFO sighting. I then rewitnessed the same object on 4 more occasions the last being Oct.12 2018 but this last time there were 2 of them both blinking off and on and going bright to dim.At one point they became further apart. I was encouraged to report this to Mufon especially since I have pictures and videos.
