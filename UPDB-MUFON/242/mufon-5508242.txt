{nd} Glowing red light flying in a direct path due southeast. Fuzzy outline.

While watching TV at 8:30 I saw a red light in the sky that was glowing. I went out to my porch and stared at it for a minute. I then got my phone to see if I could video it, but it seemed too high and too dark for me to video it on my iPhone. When it reached directly above I went into the house and came out to see it on the south side of my apartment. I watched as the light faded into the atmosphere and disappeared. Pretty sure it wasnt a plane because I am always looking at the night sky and know what most of them look like. I have very little emotional reaction other than some excitement to see what I saw.
