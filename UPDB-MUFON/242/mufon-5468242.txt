{td}Very high cloud cover object came out diag. Right to left, stopped went back up like a V

I was 13 in 1966 and had a paper route to earn money for a Boy Scout trip to Philmont. It was a sat. am. And I had finished delivering my papers about 6am and was standing in the street outside my home talking to another paper boy who had finished his route, I was facing south. There were extremely high smooth cloud cover, I1m now a pilot and I would estimate from memory it was overcast at about 10-12,000 ft. A bronze copper object came out of the cloud, stopped and went back into the cloud in a V shape leaving a vapor like trail. From where I was standing it was very distinct and about the size of an aspirin. I looked at my friend, he looked at me, speechless and I went in my home.

I am now 66 and I can still see it as if it was yesterday. I have a BS in Comp. Sci. And am retired from IBM after 38 years. I have told maybe 5-8 people about this over my lifetime, but feel it important that it be documented before my time is up. I have long since lost track of my friend, but we never spoke about it to each other after that moment.
