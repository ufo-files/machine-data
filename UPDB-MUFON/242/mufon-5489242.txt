White glowing ball light. Observed moving in a staright line path

Me and my friend were doing exercise in Bear creek park Surrey B.C Canada, early morning around 5:40 or 5:45 AM. I saw a constant bright star like light it was very high in sky, we observe the incoming light path was from west to east south, it was having constant elevation and was moving with a tremendous speed. There was no exhaust trail. With in few seconds it was away from our eyes. It was on constant flight path. Looking at the speed looks like there was no gravitational pull. We made sure it was not aircraft or rocket or meteoroid. There was no sound at all.
