{cak} TRIANGLE SHAPED OBJECT GLIDING

Husband had to be at work by 4:00 am. Took dogs outside . Love to star gaze. Waiting for dogs I observed an object coming towards me but off to the right. Gliding with no noise. If I hadn't been looking up I would have never seen this. Two glowing oblong sections were visible on either side of the craft at the very back of the wings which is how I determined it was triangular shaped. The object itself was black. The oblong shapes on the wings appeared to be recessed with three circles filling the spaces. The oblong areas were glowing orange as if they were hot. Not bright but, enough that you could see what they were. With the glowing oblong structures being recessed into the craft it was undetectable unless it was almost directly over head. I kept watching and noticed it looked as though the two glowing sections merged. ran in to get my husband and ran throught the house to get another glimps. I never saw it after that. I dont consider the glowing oblong shapes to be lights. They looked as though they were radiating heat. Just a soft glow. The craft made no noise and was quietly gliding overhead.
