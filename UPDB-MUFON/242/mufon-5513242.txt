 On Christmas morning I was at home watching the sunrise and talking to a relative in Mo. describing to him the gorgeous sunrise over the ocean. When the sun was about 2 solar diameters above the horizon line in the middle of a circular opening in the clouds just to the left of the sun about 2 fingers width from the solar disc I saw what looked like a perfect spherical pearl. It had that pearlescent look to its surface. I watched it for about 10 seconds when the clouds obscured it. Later I found this video on youtube. At the very beginning and from 33 to 46 seconds in the video the size and position of the sphere in the video is exactly what I saw except mine looked more like white pearl. The rest of the video does not look like what I saw. Way too big and the shadow is wrong (in reference to the sun). Looks fake. I don't know what I saw just that I saw it. Did not have my camera but described what I saw to my brother in law as I was seeing it. Any ideas?  Na mahalo ~ [name removed/cms/dr]
