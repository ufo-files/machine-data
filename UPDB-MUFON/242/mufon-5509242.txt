{nd} What appeared to be the full moon rising,had a line of blinking red-orange lights that outlined the top of a triangle shape which hovered and completely evaporated above the city lights

I was unloading groceries from a late nite shopping trip and paused,as I usually do,to look at the night sky and observe whatever stars I can see beyond the city lights of the L.A.basin.
There was the usual landing lights from planes approaching a nearby airport and a couple helicopters on the horizon. I was raised with an aeronautical background so I am familiar with all the air traffic that crowds this sky,especially since I am within a couple mile from a military base to the west of me. A glowing round white yellowish shape hovered in the eastern black sky and I at first thought it was the moon behind some clouds. Upon closer observation I noticed a straight line of blinking/pulsating red-orange lights above the shape. I have been witness to multiple UFO sightings over the span of decades and I stood still and watched it,noticing that there was a darker outline of a triangle. At that point,the lower whitish light disappeared and one by one,without moving,changing altitude, or direction,the red-orange lights in a row disappeared...and the sky reflected once again the city glow and stars where the object was. Since it was a distance away, theres nothing else I can add except it was an unusual object in the clear night sky.
