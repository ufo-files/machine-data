2 bright white lights like stars but brighter. Not moving. Very distant (like stars)

I was out walking after dark on Stanton Road, Sandiacre, Nottingham NG10 5DE (approximately 19:50 on 25th February 2019). The weather was clear and there were many stars visible. I often look at the stars and I am familiar with most constellations. Ursa Major was rotated so that Alkaid was closest to the horizon and Dubhe was the high point. About 5-10 degrees east of this I saw two bright white lights. (Looking North-East from where I was walking) At first I thought they were stars but then I realised that there are no stars in that position usually so they struck me as being odd. I looked at them very closely. They were round, white, star-like points of light, about the same distance apart as Mizar and Alioth and at a similar elevation (almost parallel to those stars) but further East. They did not move at all. As I continued to walk, I passed a streetlight that was illuminated. I thought to myself that the light pollution from the streetlight would mean the lights would become obscured as I passed, and I continued to look closely at them to see if this was the case. Indeed it was: the lights, having originally been constantly bright, began to fade in the light pollution (as did Ursa Major) until I could no longer see them. Once I had passed the light and the sky was dark enough to see the stars again, the two points of light had disappeared and did not return.
