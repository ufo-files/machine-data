{dr}observed three lights in triangle shape in sw sky

I was looking for ufos, I always look for them, every night and day. Tonight around 8 pm I saw 3 star like lights in the sw sky that I had never seen before. I felt immediately it was a ufo. They formed a triangle shape and the two at the top appeared white (like a star) in color and the bottom light appeared to be orange in color and flashed or pulsated. The triangle appeared to be slightly longer than wide. Somewhere around 9:30 pm one of the lights went out (kind of like when a bulb blows out) then another went out and the one at the upper right stayed on. Storm cloud cover started coming in and I went inside. Also,during my observation an airplane was on the landing pattern into Fort Myers Regional airport. The plane was heading south and was west of my location and would soon bank east to the airport. Im sorry but I cant remember if it was earlier or later in the sighting. The triangle never seemed to change its location. In the intrim I had to do a errand which took me south on Del Prado Blvd to Veterans Pkwy and I followed it all the way there and back. Also, I looked for a solid form but could not tell in the darkness.
