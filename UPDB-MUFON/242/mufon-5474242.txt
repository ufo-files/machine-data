Bright star flies at low altitude over town

The night of October 20, 2014.

While on my way home from the mall a star brighter than the planet Venus appeared in the northern sky, heading east. Unlike stars and planets this thing was flying faster than anything celestial. 
Difficult to say how high it was but it definitely flew at lower altitude than commercial airliners. There were no blinking lights, no rumbling sounds of jet engines and no contrails. By the time I had turned off Sister Green Road then walked the length of my street about eight minutes had passed. Yet this object was barely creeping across the sky.

October 28, 2014 at 7:12pm.

The following week at roughly the same time at night I observed yet another bright object flying south. I stared at it for three minutes until it vanished beyond the mountain range.
