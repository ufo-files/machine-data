5-6 orange Lights In Diamond formation.......

I saw something hovering approx 300 ft above the tree lines. at least 6 lights. one flash after another as to distinguish the size of object. A semi Truck was behind me as I was filming it, he beeped at me at least 4 times and I waved to him and pointed into the direction of the object,...I don't think he saw it, or he would have got out of his truck. The only reason I didn't get out of my car was bc I had a 3 yr old in back seat. I assumed the video was proof enough of what I saw.

Object was at least 3-400 yards in total size. report was made to local police department.

I filmed the object for 1.5-2 minutes 
(before truck behind me approached).....when I went home to show my family the video, it was gone....The crazy part is, When i was filming it, in the back of my mind I felt that there was no way this video would work just bc how unbelievable it was, and i was absolutely right. when I went to show my family the video was not there.
