Silent Low Amber Colored Starlike Orb

Well I got a call from my dad at 10 PM last night July 1st, saying "Your not going to believe this, but me and the neighbors just saw a UFO." This struck me as odd because my dad is the biggest skeptic ever and for him to call me out of the blue and tell me he saw something was crazy. Anyways I talked to him and his friend both who witnessed it (middle aged men, who again denied anything like that was possible), And they both described an Amber colored orb floating through the sky coming east over Hermitage flying right over the house that zig zagged direction, stopped hovered, zig zaged again, hovered once more, and then took off straight into the air completely out of sight. They watched this for a little over a minute, but agree its very possible it could of been in the sky longer without them noticing (considering they were outside talking). It made no sound and flew much lower then a plane, and remained a bright amber color without any blinking or pulsating lights. It was a very clear night too so I know they saw something, and it turned middle aged skeptics into believing they saw something they could not explain..Decided to post this today after my dad called curious if other people saw it or reported it too.
