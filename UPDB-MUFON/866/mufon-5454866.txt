{tjd} Consistent flight path of well over twenty star like objects moving west to east at a very high altitude following one another at equidistant space.

I watched over twenty star like objects at a very high altitude move west to east over Albuquerque at equidistant increments in a perfectly straight line or path. At their altitude, they seemed to come out of the dark in the west and disappear in the east as quietly as they came. Shortly after the lights past, a single light just like the previous cane from the SW dark and headed to the point of dismissal of the lights that followed one another. After this I did not see any others.
