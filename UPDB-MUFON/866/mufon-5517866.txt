{nd} Strange movement of fast-moving lights in the sky, constantly changing direction. Second light appeared minutes after noticing the first.

Having a cigarette on the porch of my home, I had noticed what I thought was a shooting star while I had looked south. Upon investigating, I had noticed that the moving star-like entity was rapidly changing its flight path. It had maneuvered several times making an elongated figure-eight pattern. Shocked at seeing how this vessel had maneuvered, I then saw a different and brighter faster-moving light, travelling on a straighter path. The maneuvering entity in question was of a faint whitish and radiating orange color. The second was a solid brighter white color. 
The maneuvering UFO had moved and changed direction too fast so as to rule out a satellite, falling debris, or any sort of human air/spacecraft. The way that the first strange light was maneuvering, along with its speed, had frightened me because it gave me the feeling that the vessels technology is very much so beyond human means. I eventually lost track of the vessel its path was so erratic.
