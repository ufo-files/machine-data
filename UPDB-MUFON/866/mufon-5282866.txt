Short Description: On porch, circular glow not particularly bright hovered and dropped out of sight. Too bizarre:submitted hoping other witnesses did the same.

Grants Pass, OR 1/28/22@approx.7:30pm-8:00pm

To the SW/W it was a huge glow which caught my peripheral as it went from a hover to dropping near vertical and dissipated instantaneously. 
Duration was no more than 3 seconds and though it began at a hover, it seemed to be in motion the whole time. No wobble or disappearing and reappearing. It seemed large and at a distance of a mile to maybe 3 miles though I really couldn't be sure. It did have the resemblance of a spotlight into a dense fog. But that simply isn't what I saw.

I personally have never witnessed anything paranormal or the unexplained in the sky. That said this really caught my attention. So I'm submitting this in hopes someone else reported the same here in Southern OR. Or perhaps it's been reported here before already or again in future. 

It certainly got my adrenaline going. It was that weird.
