{rjl} Saw object when driving on I-85. Object vanished into thin air.

I was driving to work on I-85 travelling north. Just south of exit 38, I looked up ahead and saw a silver disc-shaped object. It was stationary. I thought at first it may have been a plane banking to land at GSP but it did not move. As I looked at it, it simply vanished. The road was clear so I scanned the sky for a few seconds but there was no sign of it. 

However, last night at about 23:30, I noticed some high-flying jets (4 in total) in formation circling the area around Greer in wide arcs. I thought nothing of this at the time.
