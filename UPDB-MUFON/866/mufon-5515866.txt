{dr}Two objects observed over Golden, CO

While driving I noticed a star-like object very high in the sky. It was moving slowly from East to West. I was at a traffic light. When I took my eyes off the object to check the light, and returned to view the object, it was gone. However, I then witnessed another object much closer to me. This one was red and moving in the same direction. It looked more angular than the first object, as if it had 90 degree angles. It moved swiftly and rapidly across the sky. I turned my head again to check the traffic light. When I turned back, it was gone. 

These sightings follow another sighting which occurred yesterday in the same area. I managed to film the previous object with my cell phone. The link is here: [Link moved to FI section/cms/tg]

This sighting was of a star-like object way up in the atmosphere. I was able to see it clearly, but my camera did not. However, I thought it disappeared, but my camera actually caught it reversing direction and accelerating at an amazing speed.
