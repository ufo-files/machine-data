{dr}light in night sky

It was about 8:58 p.m. when I saw something moving among the stars in the eastern sky. It looked like a white or bluish-white light like a star or satellite, but it was moving in a north direction with altitude increasing. It disappeared at about 45 degrees altitude in the eastern sky only about 5, 10, or 20 seconds after I first saw it. The light did not seem like a hallucination, though. It seemed to actually be there, like anything else in the night sky.

It doesnt seem to have been a satellite. I checked on Heavens-above.com for objects above 5 magnitude and there was no satellite that disappears in the eastern sky at the altitude and at the time sighted.
