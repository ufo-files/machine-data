Stealth helicopter with smart skin in PDX

I was sitting at my computer when I heard a loud helicopter pass outside. Thehelicopter forward white light was visible as was the red tail light. This isthe second time this summer that I have seen it. It is at least 70ft in lengthwith multiple rotors. I have never been able to distinguish ANY features otherthan its length. The aircrafts smart skin is the exact color as the sky. I havegood eye sight and was less than 1500 feet away from it when it passed over. Iwent outside to get a better look at it but lost sight of it after a minute. Itwas heading from the south to the North PDX military base at 10:18pm PST.
