four rectangular moving objects...glowing....all moving at the same speed but not in formation

My partner was letting our dog out and yelled at me to come outside quickly. I have no idea at all what I saw, but they were four tiny rectangles moving at a fair speed, roughly north west. They were not in a formation as such but two were noticably ahead of the the other two. The front two were fairly far apart. The back two were closer together.
They were very small and very high but they appeared to be rectangular, not round. They all glowed but did not give off any light in any direction that I could see. They were fast but not zipping about. They were very direct and not varying their path at all.
My feelings were of delight! My partner had seen a group of similar objects a few nights before and is reporting to you now. I didn't see them then but was so pleased to see them last night.
Cloud cover was initially light but after about four minutes very heavy cloud came over and obliterated the whole sky. We looked out a few more times but the cloud was too thick to see even stars.
One thing of note....my partner described the objects as round. I can't possibly agree with that...to my eyes they were more than square...definitely tiny but rectangular. They were very high and I couldn't distinguish any features on the four rectangles other than the l
light.
