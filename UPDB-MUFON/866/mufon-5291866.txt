Abrupt change in direction

At around 2300 on a clear night I observed what I presumed was a satellite travelling rapidly from the horizon in a straight South Easterly direction. At a distance behind it I saw a plane following in the same direction - I heard the jet engine. As the "satellite" approached overhead it suddenly changed direction without virtually any turn - it was travelling south east one second and the next it was travelling west. It accelerated and disappeared towards the western holiday.
