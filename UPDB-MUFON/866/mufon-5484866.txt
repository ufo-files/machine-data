{jpg} LEAVING THE HOUSE AND AT THE END OF THE DRIVEWAY WAS A HUGH, BRIGHT YELLOW/WHITE GLOWING ORB

FILLED OUT AT MUFON H.Q., WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER ON MARCH 2ND,2016
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON JULY 17TH, AT 10PM, WHILE LEAVING MY HOME WITH MY MOTHER AND BROTHER AND HIS DAUGHTER, WE SAW A YELLOWISH/WHITE/ ORANGE ORB WITH DISTINCT EDGES, OVER THE END OF THE DRIVEWAY. WHILE WE WATCHED IT MOVING BY, IT WAS CLOSE ENOUGH TO HIT WITH A SHOT GUN, IT SPLIT INTO PIECES AND CONTINUED TO MOVE TOWARD WAYNESBORO. IT MADE NO SOUND.
WAYNESBORO IS SOUTH OF US AND HAS A NUCLEAR PLANT. WE COULD ONLY FOLLOW ONE OF THE ORBS AND LOST IT BEHIND TREES. IT MOVED FAST AND RANDOMLY FLASHED. WE COULDN'T TELL IF IT WAS FOLLOWING HIGHWAY 25 OR NOT, AND WE COULDN'T TELL WHERE IT CAME FROM. MY MOTHER AND BROTHER ALSO SAW THE ORB AND WILL DISCUSS IT WITH THE INVESTIGATOR.
