I was coming out of my house with my son and 2 dogs to go for a walk...I walked to the end of my drive waiting for my son to follow...while I was waiting I heard a bird on my house roof...I looked the bird for a while then I looked up more and noticed 7 lights in the sky hovering over my house...They were hovering for about 5mins when my son came out of the house I said look can you see lights there in the sky?? he looked up and saw them...I said what do you think they are...He said erm...I dunno mum..I said are them Chinese lanterns..Then thought no they are not as they were just hovering and not floating away..so was puzzled..they were still there 10mins later as we both kept looking at them..then 5 lights broke off the 2 lights and moved across the sky to the left then stayed there..the remaining 2 lights were still over our house as I pointed at them the 2 lights went over to the 5 lights..There was no sound whatsoever and all 7 lights shot upwards at a great speed and punched a hole in the clouds :O Again no sound whatsoever... Another night about 2 weeks later I was in my garden alone it was around 11pm and a very warm night as was thinking what a beautiful night and stayed outside for a while..as I was standing there..I looked over across my garden and noticed a bright orange light in the distance...I looked at it and thought a plane was in trouble as it looked like flames and we do have a flight path (Luton) that goes around...but this orange glow was not on the flight path as such...it was coming towards me and getting larger..I still thought it was a plane in trouble but as it was getting closer there was no sound whatsoever..so thought well thats strange having no sound the size of it should of had some sort of sound coming from it..but there was silence..and as it was getting closer it was sphere like in shape and a bright orange/yellow shape...it got closer and closer...still no sound emanating from it...I actually put my hands out as was scared...and then it went...just disappeared right before my eyes...No moving off or shooting upwards it was there then it wasn't...Very strange.
