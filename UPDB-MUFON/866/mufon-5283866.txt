linear object spotted moving west to east

Dora was out looking at the stars when she saw a line in white lights moving west to east. She called out for me (Dan) to hurry and come see what she is observing. Me and my brother Jeff came out and saw a line of white lights moving slowly across the sky. i first though it was a comet but the closer I looked it didn't look like any picture or video i have ever saw of a comet. it had 1 larger light followed by 2 smaller lights and repeated the same pattern for about 10 to 12 lights all in a straight line and looked like they were attached. They looked to be 4 to 5 inches wide looking up at them, we see jets and other military planes all the time and this was a lot bigger in the sky than anything we have ever saw. i've seen many objects over my 60 years of live that i couldn't explain but nothing like this. i've watched meteor showers in the past and it was not a meteor. this was a manufactured object.
