Ball of fire slowly decending lost sight as if landed appeared again changed to egg shape took off

I was driving in a northerly direction when I spotted a large ball of fire which just suddenly appeared between 30 to 45 degrees in front. My first thought was a firework which I quickly dismissed as it was too large and coming down slowly. The view of this object was offset by 5 mins from my straight line of vision, it carried on coming down until I lost sight which makes me believe it landed somewhere but within 5 seconds it reappeared going upwards slowly but started to change into an egg shape slowly this time though with droplets of fire coming from its base, this happened whilst it was still moving slowly upwards for 2 or 3 seconds then shot off at such a high speed that it just vanished, no trail or smoke. Since watching your programmes I felt I needed to get this off my chest as it has bothered me all this time. Only in writing to you made me work out the possible location of where this ufo was coming down and points to Fleet Command at Northwood. Sorry about the date and time it was a long time ago bit what I do know is that a Capital Radio DJ saw something very similar in his description on a Sunday I believe whilst he was in his kitchen, his was going horizontally, his sighting was near mine.
