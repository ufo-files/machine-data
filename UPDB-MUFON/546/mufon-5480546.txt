{jpg} Approached from SE to NW. Hovered, wiggled then Ascended into cloud cover.

Object approached from SE to NW at 6:15am. I was feeding the horses before work. It was luminous and was three spheres stacked on top of each other. This caught my eye because of the darker sky due to cloud cover. The center sphere was larger, but I got the impression they were attached to each other. Sunrise was behind and below object from my point of view, yet it was brightly luminous. It moved slowly towards me then hovered and ascended into low cloud cover. I captured it on video, but my IPhone 6SPlus ran out of storage midway. The object had greater detail with the naked eye than the video. I have included the video and a sketch. I checked my Plane View Live app and no aircraft was in the vicinity at that time. I do not know what it was but I am curious if anyone else saw it?
