Thought it was a star in the sky but it slow moved NE

I am always looking at the sky when I take the dog out for its last time at night. I noticed the stars, but this one was almost above me and I quickly realised it was moving. I watched to make sure for a few seconds and realised there were no aviation lights. It was like a bright star slowly moving. I ran in grabbed my camera from upstairs and ran back down and tried to get my setting right. Filming on a Nikon D5000 is a pain! I filmed it. It wasn't very good as it was only a white blip on the screen. I checked if it was the ISS and it wasn't. There was no noise, it just glided across the sky. I could not guess what height it was. I do have the movie, but it doesn't show anything much except the white light. I know we have quite a few aircraft here in Lincolnshire, but there was no noise of them at this time. And as I said there were NO flashing aviation lights.

Time was 9pm and it was in the sky for at least 10 mins and faded in the direction of Woodhall Spa
