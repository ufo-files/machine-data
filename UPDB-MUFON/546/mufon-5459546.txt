{jpg} Sphere/oval near Strands beach hovers, pulsates, then appears to morphe into airplane with navigation lights.

I walked outside of my house and glowing oval/sphere was hovering probably less than a mile away. about 1:31 into video another sphere drifts closely by object in upper right hand corner of screen. The intitial sphere (especially when slowed down frame by frame) seems to pulsate and develop red and green navigation lights on its sides like it developed wings. Object then appeared to be an airplane and flew right over me. My camera shut down. I have seen this on numerous occassions and have over 50 videos on my phone since October 2018. The sightings are over Dana Point harbor and over ocean out towards Catalina Island. Objects dart in 45-90 degree angles, pulse, often fade out suddenly and are often accompanied by other spheres of various size and brightness that create what appears to be deliberate formations.
