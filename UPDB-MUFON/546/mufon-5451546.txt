Bright noiseless object observed

Myself and my family (wife and two kids) observerd a shiny/bright object on a clear evening moving silently across the sky. The shape of the object was not possible to see as it was to high and far away. I recorded a video of the object using an iPhone 6 but it was hard to follow the object with the phone as it was too small to see. The observation lasted less than 2 minutes when near the end of the video the object seemed to disappear or it was just far enough away as to be not observable.
