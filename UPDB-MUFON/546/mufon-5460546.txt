{tjd} Four bright triangular crafts appeared to hover over Hershey Park after descending from the clouds.

I was with a group on the observatory tower at Hershey Park (a rotating elevator called The Kissing tower) and took a rapid series of photographs with my phone of the landscape. I. Noticed in the photos afterward that in the direction above the Hershey headquarters, there were four bright objects in close formation that appeared to be in an upside-down triangle shape, each around the size of a plane. In the four photos I took in that direction, the first showed one of them descending from the clouds, then the other three photos showed all four objects in close formation. Other photos I took in other directions as the elevator ride rotated didnt show any objects. The attached files are zoomed in screenshots of the original photos.
