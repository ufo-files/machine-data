{nd} 4 separate red/white star-like objects, maximum 2 in the sky at a time might have had octahedron shape

Was drinking coffee in kitchen while looking out window. 11:20-11:40 am MT. Spotted four star-like and mostly stationary objects that rapidly changed white and red and were able to disappear and appear. Individually they stayed in the sky for about 5 to 7 minutes each. There was a devaition of about 10-20 degrees from when I spotted them in the West and Northwest about 45 degrees above the horizon until they disappeared at bout 20-35 degrees. They moved in erratic directions, several degrees at a time, not in a common direction as you would suspect from the prevailing winds. They were at the altitude of the faint cirrostratus clouds.I took 8 pictures that show nothing more than a faint pixelated grey dot. They may have been mylar baloons, but I do not know for sure. Question: Do they pop at high altitudes which would explain why I saw them suddenly disappear? One appeared at its closest approach to have an octahedron shape (which rules out a common mylar baloon AFAIK).
