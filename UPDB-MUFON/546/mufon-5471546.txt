{rjl} spouse and self observed dark line moving/growing across visible surface of full moon then disappear

1) Driving SE on Staples rd toward our gym to work out
2) Husband said: "look at that moon" as he often does when it is full. I looked and noticed a dark line moving/growing across it and asked him "do you see that?!"
3) I asked him what he thought it was and he said he had no idea. I suggested either a shadow of a craft or something to do with the "lunar wave" as filmed and described on youtube videos
4) A dark grayish black line became visible on the bottom right visible quadrant of the full moon and began to either grow or move upward in a diagonal direction across the surface of the moon. It was straight as an arrow, and looked as if someone had taken a dark sharpie marker and was drawing a precisely straight dark line across the surface of the moon. When it reached the other side of the moon perimeter (about 1/3-1/2 way up from the bottom), it began to shorten on the side that it had started on, and as it got smaller and smaller, it eventually disappeared.
5) I was relieved because I have known for a year that there is more to our world than most know about and I have been expressing this belief to my husband which had been falling on deaf ears until this night. He had told me previously that if any of this is true, "why doesn't he see anything", and then we witnessed this together. I felt elated and excited to have witnessed something so unusual and unexplainable. We discussed later that we did not feel fear or anxiety after having witnessed the event. We felt a changed world view that we could share together moving forward. 
6) The object, pulse, or whatever it was seemed to disappear into thin air after it shortened on the other side of the moon from where it first appeared. It did not move out of sight before it dissipated before our eyes, leaving the full moon visible as it had always been before.
