Saw large chrome ball following path or river, on my way to work in the dark.

I was on my way to work at the lumber mill, in Oregon. It was December, about 5AM in the morning. Coming up the road towards the mill, I looked up at the clear night sky and saw what appeared to be a large chrome ball. I could see it was slowly following the river across the highway, near the mill. I slowed to a stop to watch it. It moved awfully slow and I was taking in the detail. It really looked like chrome, and I saw no windows. It seemed to be about the size of a pickup truck. It kept going until some tree obscured my vision, and I started to drive up the driveway to work. The mill is a large complex and has many tall lights. It seems the object may have been inspecting the mill, perhaps trying to decide what it was. I discussed this with my uncle who was a Green Beret in Vietnam, and who did 27 years in special Forces. He told me he has seen the chrome ball 3 times in Mexico, and one flying saucer about 30 feet above his head. He also claims that some “men in black” showed up to his house in Mexico, while he was away, and the neighbors told him about it. When he retired, my uncle lived in a house on the beach in central Mexico. He already reported his sighting on this site and submitted pictures he says.
