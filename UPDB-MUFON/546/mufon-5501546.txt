My family an I, joined a big family reunion, with a lot of members of it.
The hole day was of joy, laughin, and a beautiful picnic day in my uncle1s farm.

So, as I said, the hole day, was quite, nice and peaceful...
but the night, ... was another story...

There was one of the worst & scariest moments of my hole life and my family members till nowdays!
At the end of the day, and in the very long hours of the night finally all the people said farewell, and good bye1s. And we start, to climb the old Mercedez truck.
We started the way back.
At first, my aunt Olga,my mother1s sister, said: Hey! look everybody!, it1s an UFO!!!....

Hey! look, everybody! it1s an UFO ! ; and god knows, we all wished she was wrong again!...
