Shiny oval shaped UFO hovering over Yarmouth

I was driving south to work this morning on I-295 and noticed way above the town of Yarmouth a shiny silver object. At first I thought it was a bird since there was a flock nearby, but then I noticed that the object appeared to stay very still, it was too high in the air to be a bird and it was shiny, reflecting light from the sun. i looked away for a second and then back and it had moved its location slightly. I had to keep driving but I'm positive it was a UFO.
