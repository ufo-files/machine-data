My son was watching a plane flying. He then saw an object that was moving quickly on a direct path towards the North. The object came to a complete stop in the sky. It just hovered their for a little bit (minute)!. He felt like he was being watched. He felt a pumping in his chest when he saw dark colored beam or arura from the bottom of the craft. The craft had 3 red lights that appeared to wrap around the object but he could not see the other side of it. It also had one white light as well. The white light was under the middle of one of the red lights.  He turned to tell his mom about it and looked back and it was gone.
