I woke about 2 am and my room was full of 7 to 8 foot entities huddled around me

I have been visited many times since I was a young boy by entities in my room. The last time was last Saturday night about 2 am. They were tall, about 7 to 8 feet high, dark skinned with fuzzy dark hair. They seemed concerned about as I had recently separated from my wife. When I sat up and woke, because the room light was on I heard them say "he's awake, we can't be seen here. Go ! Go ! And in an instant they were gone. It was so real that I rang the local Police Station at Glebe and they said no officers had been sent to my home. So why was my room packed with 7-8 foot high entities who vanished in an instant. This has happened since I was a kid and I have to talk to someone. I am a 51 year old retired Bank Manager. I have no reason to make these stories up. Why is it happening. ???
