ORBS THAT GLOWED

I WAS TRAVING IN A CONVERTABLE WITH THE TOP DOWN LATE AT NIGHT AND SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A BRIGHT STAR WITH COLORED LIGHTS THAT REVOLDED AROUND CENTER PULLED OF ROAD AND GOT OUT TO STARE AT THIS STAR WITH LIGHTS AND THEN TWO ORBS CAME FROM THE DIRECTION OF THIS BRIGHT STAR, THESE ORBES THAT SEEMED TO BE ABOUT 30 FEET IN DIA. EACH CAME RIGHT OVER US MAYBE 200 FEET ABOVE US THEY MADE NO NOISE THE ORBS GLOWED FROM WITH IN BUT DID NOT CAST ANY LIGHT OUT SIDE THE SKIN OF THE ORBS WHEN THEY PAST OVER HEAD AND TRAVLED WEST THEN SEEMED TO DISAPPEAR, WHEN I TOOK MY DATE HOME I FOUND MYSELF IN A TOWN THE WRONG WAY FROM WHERE I WAS GOING AND WHEN I GOT HOME 4 HOURS HAD PASSED AND GOT HOME INTIME TO LEAVE TO GO TO WORK WITHOUT GETING ANY SLEEP, WHEN I WENT TO WORK ANOTHER WORKER HAD A SIMULAR EXPERENCEABOUT A WEEK EARLYER. ABOUT 2 YEARS LATER I WAS LISTING TO A LOCAL RADIO TALK SHOW ABOYT UFOS AND A PERSON CALLED THE SHOW FROM WICHITA KS. THAT WORKED AT NIGHT ON A CONSTRUCTION CREW THAT BUILT GRAIN STORGE SILOS AND HAD THE EXACT SAME STORY WORD FOR WORD, 4 OR 5 YEARS LATER I WENT TO A DOCTOR AND THE WANTED ME TO HAVE A CHEST EXRAY FOR A CHECK UP AND AFTER I HAD THE EXRAY THEY SAID THAT I NEEDED TO GO TO A SPECIALEST BECAUSE THERE WAS SOMETHING THAT SHOWED UP IN THE EXRAY THAT THEY DIDN'T NOW WHAT IT WAS, THE SPECIALEST ASKED IF I HAD AN SPORT INJURY, I SAID NO HE SHOWED ME THE EXRAY AND THERE WAS A SPOT THAT LOOKED LIKE A CAPSULE RIGHT IN THE MIDDLE OF MY CHEST HE SAID THAT IT MUST BE SCARE TISSUE, SEVEREL YEARS LATER IWOKE UP TO GO TO WORK AND WHEN I SET UP ON THE EDGE OF THE BED I HAD A SEVERE PAIN IN MY CHEST SO I LAID BACK DOWN AND THE NEXT THING I KNOW WAS HAVING AN OUT OF BODY EXPERENCE I COULD SEE MY SELF ON THE BED WITH THESE HUMAN LIKE BEINGS WORKING ON ME THEN THE NEXT THING I KNOW I AM BACK IN BED WITH SOMETING ON ME WITH A BONEY HAND OVER MY FACE, WHEN THE HAND WAS REMOVED I GASP AND SET UP AND THER WAS NO ONE THERE AND THE PAIN WAS GONE AND HAS NEVER BE BACK MORE YEARS PAST AND HAD ANOTHER CHEST EXRAY I ASKED THE TEC ABOUT TH SCARE TISSURE IN MY CHEST AND SAID THAT THERE WAS NONE I HAVE HAD SOME OTHER SIGHTING OF WHAT I THOUGHT MIGHT BE OTHER SIGHTINGS
