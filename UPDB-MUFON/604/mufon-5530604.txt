Deep blue triad of lights spotted hovering over field by Route 9 in Freehold.

At approx/ 9:55pm EST, whilst leaving the freehold mall movie theater on the night of july 31st, 2010, me and my significant other were turning onto county road 537 to get to route 9 south. To the Immedeate right on 537 before merging right to route 9, there is a very large open area. We both noticed a floating close-knit triad of deep blue lights, remaning solid and hovering approx a half-mile or less away over this field at a fairly low altitude. To the best of my knowledge, no aircraft in the USA uses a solid and dark/deep color, especially dark blue, not to mention it remained stationary and silent. We viewed this craft for approx 1 minute. We temporarily lost site for a minute as we merged onto Rt 9, where trees obscured our view. As we came to another clearing some dinstance down the road, to our disappointment, the object was no longer there. It was trully strange indeed.
