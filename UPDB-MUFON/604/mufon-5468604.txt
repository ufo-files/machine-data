Object noted in the sky at dusk as it was seemingly not moving, or if it was, only at a extremely slow speed. An airplane passed by in a different direction during the same period which highlighted the unusual activity of the object I am writing about. Myself and my friend observed the object for approximately 10 minutes before turning and heading back home. Shortly after changing our direction to leave I turned around and the object had disappeared. Granted it was a cloudy evening, but for the object to have disappeared within seconds, it clearly had to change its speed by a considerable rate. For that reason I believe it was aware of our presence and feel it departed after we did.
