Hundreds of UFOs flying in a tight grid pattern flying in one direction

My son and I were driving up the street from where we were living and saw out the car window hundreds of ufos flying in a tight grid formation. We stopped and I ask my son if he saw what I saw and he did. They were way up and probably over a mile up because they looked small. They were oval shape and white in color. They were not birds or geese. They were up to high and they did not have a typical v geese pattern. They were in a grid pattern side by side but hundreds that filled the sky. I called the state police but they had no reports.
I have always wanted to tel our story.
