{jpg} Orange orb that morphed into two separate orange orbs.

I took my dog outside so that she could potty for the night and while she was relieving herself, I was looking up at the night sky. I noticed a bright orange orb travelling in a flat, straight line due South from my location. When the orb was over a wooded area south of my home it suddenly morphed into two separate orange orbs. Those two orbs traveled 180 degrees from each other with one headed directly East and the other one headed directly West. Those two orbs flew in straight lines until they disappeared from sight.
