{rjl} Cigar Shaped UFO with a tilt

I went out for a smoke around 6 pm on 1/4/14 and walked down the sidewalk to observe the South West direction. Since I had my sighting on 12/23/13 I watch the sky in this direction like a hawk when I am outside. It was just starting to turn dusk and the Western Sky was orange from the setting sun with a few clouds. I then looked further to the South and seen a unusual object in the sky heading in my direction. I did not have my camera with me so I made a dash into the apartment for it. Once I got back I started filming what looked like a Cigar shaped craft with the front facing down towards the ground. It was a dark grey in color and no lights or noise was observed. I watched and filmed the object until it headed into the clouds and was gone. I later reviewed the recording and the quality of this video is horrible. At about 15 seconds right under the highest cloud seen you will see a blurry gray object move from left to right slowly.
