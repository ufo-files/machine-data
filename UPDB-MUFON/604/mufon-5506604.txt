{kt}Blue disc of light moved from above my house then changed direction quickly.

I was sitting on my patio looking South over my back lawn. The object was immediately noticeable and drew the attention of myself and two of my friends that were with me. The three of us immediately said it was a UFO. The object was a blue disc that emitted a small cone of light from the bottom, though the cone was only a few feet lower than the object. It came from above my house, moving in an exact South-East alignment before abruptly darting South and disappearing. The whole encounter only lasted about 3 seconds. As soon as I saw it I couldnt take my eyes off it, and immediately after the hair stood up on my arms and neck and the three of us couldnt believe what we just saw. At first I was confused and panicking, then became somewhat fearful and felt as if something else was going to happen, though nothing ever did.
