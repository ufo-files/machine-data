explosion of strobe like lights in cloud

My wife and I were headed to Sioux Falls traveling south on I-29 headed south we where just past the Dell Rapids exit and all of a sudden in front of us in the clouds there was a explosion of extremely bright lights going of in the cloud. I turned my head towards my wife and said did u see that! She said yes what was that! I continued to drive and watched the area where the lights appeared and a long object was moving slowly east in the clouds. I watched it for maybe 30 secs and it disappeared. Wasn't shaped like airplane it was as long as a airplane but had dome shapes on each end.
