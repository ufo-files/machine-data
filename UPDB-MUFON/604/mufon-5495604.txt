{nd} My son and I were taking scenic pictures of the mountains, there were no clouds, then there seemed to be.

My son and I were driving leaving Sanders County into Lincoln County, Montana. The sky was clear blue and the area very scenic. I decided to pull over to take a couple of photos. My son took pictures with his iPhone. (The pictures I am submitting are from his iPhone I had him email to me.) I had him save all photos prior to and after these submitted, to show that were no clouds before this photo, as they seemed to suddenly appear. We did not experience anything other than taking pictures and noticing something odd when we looked at them. Upon reviewing them and enlarging them, I noticed a few odd shapes and "cloud" formations. there is a long rectangular object, streaks of clouds that appear to have been sliced through in a downward motion, and another disk shaped object with a shiny top in the center that you can see more clearly on his phone. What's VERY odd is underneath that object there appears to be either numbers or letters made from "cloud" shapes. We both see the number 3 or the letter E a couple of times. There are also a couple of other "shapes" to the left and right of the two major objects I have written about.
