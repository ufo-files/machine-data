Large boomerang shaped craft hovering above the road.

Driving home around 10:30 pm, my wife and daughter saw a huge boomerang shaped craft hovering above the road. They passed under it and still saw it through the rear window. It had one giant white light at each end of the "v" and a glowing yellow light at the front of the "v".  They were 1/4 mile from home. They picked me up to go back and see it but as we drove back about 5 minutes later it was gone. 

They said it was large, as if two 747's were hovering side by side. My daughter got a very clear view of the shape of the craft. My wife was driving so she only saw the three lights and not the actual shape.
