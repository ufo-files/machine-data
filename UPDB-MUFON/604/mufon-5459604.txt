Hovering Balloon type object floating in straight line rotating and moving slowly.

Hovering Balloon type object floating in a straight line. Could easily see that it was a solid object with parallel sides although still Cylindrical rotating and moving slowly in full daylight . Moved in straight line bearing South West. Sighting lasted about 3- 4 minutes. There was another person walking in the parking lot and I asked her to look at the object and tell me what she saw. Her reaction was mixed and she said, "I've never seen anything like that before." Her pace quickened and she left quickly in her car.  

I watched it continue until it disappeared out of sight behind the building and the trees. It made no sound at all. 

When I first saw it from a distance of about 800 - 1000 meters I thought is was a balloon stuck to a distant tree branch. As I got close I knew it was not a balloon. Was not concerned about my safety nor concerned of any harm from it as it passed by between myself and the main building of the Commonwealth Pool in Saanich near Victoria, BC Canada. After the sighting I tried to come to terms with with what exactly I couldn't identify conclusively.
