Disc shaped bright white light moved horizontally above the tree line incredibly fast

I was taking my dog for a walk at night and the dog got very nervous/afraid and would not walk further and tried to jump up on me 5 times to get my attention. At the same time I heard a swishing sound up in front of me in the woods to the left and when I looked up a very bright white lit disc moved incredibly fast from left to the right horizontally over the tree tops and was gone. The object was probably 100 feet in length and from my distance was about the size of a football/small basketball. The sky was lit up a little during this period and then returned to normal darkness. The dog was then back to normal and we continued with our walk.
