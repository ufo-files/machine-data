Two

Two bright white lights that appeared to be spherical in shape hovered and zig zagged above the North Eastern part of the Canoga Park district of Los Angeles. These objects were observed from approximately the De Soto Avenue and Community Street intersection area and at about a 60 Degrees angle up looking up. One object was stationary and the second one was not. The second object that came into view came from the South West and met with the first one which was stationary. At some point the second object moved away so fast that I did not even realize that it was gone for a couple of minutes. The main object which was the first and stationary object was observed for a full 10 minutes before as some point disappeared instantly. These were NOT stars, drones, balloons, or white plastic bags that were swept up into the atmosphere. Also very important regarding the second object which was the moving object is that the it moved in that unconventional way of going one direction and then turning in difficult to make angles and fast stops. The approximate time of observation was between 2:45PM and 2:54PM on April 15, 2021, Thursday. The approximate altitude of these objects appeared to between 1,500 to 3,000 FEET ABOVE GROUND LEVEL. Although most likely not related, however, there was one single engine Cessna type aircraft that flew directly over during the middle of this event.
