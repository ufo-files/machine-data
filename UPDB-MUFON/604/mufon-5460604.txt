{tjd} Washoe Valley NV, Facing South, Night, Looking out window at night saw the craft hovering very near by

!977, Washoe Valley< NV . Late at night, I was the only person awake, I was 16 years old. I looked out the window of the home, I was looking South. At that time and to this day, from that home there is nothing behind the house for about 3 miles, and then hills beyond that. 
I woke up and walked to the window and looked out. In the darkness about 40 years from the home I saw a craft, the craft was hovering between 20 and 30 feet off the ground. I could tell that it was thin from bottom to top in my view from side on, and about 70 feet long. I could see white lights at different points that game me the idea of the size. Though there was no back lighting at all, I could see its size. I dont know how.
I dont remember what happened after that. I just remember looking at it, and then nothing.
