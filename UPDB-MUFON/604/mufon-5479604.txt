Three almost bubble like objects are moving radically through the air and they then become solid black triangles

It was a normal spring evening. I was looking out my balcony when I noticed three objects moving around near the hill to my house. They were transparent, however, I saw nothing in them. I kept watching them in awe when they came above the hill and became solid black triangles. Two moved south towards one of the radio/cell towers on the hill. While the other moved north towards me and just hovered there. I realised I needed to get a camera and take a photo when I came back all objects were gone. 

The weirdest thing about this event was the fact that there were birds that were flying all over my district in Tbilisi that never seemed to land. Thew was small and brown and had a weird structure to them. When I saw the sighting, they clustered together and flew higher than I had ever seen them go. They flew up between me and the objects however I could still see it. 

Another person saw it but shrugged it off as if it was nothing.
