Me and my girlfriend work midnights and had just got up to go to work, I walked outside to check the weather and saw a bright red light in the sky, as it got closer it had an orange middle it it. I yelled for my girlfriend to come see it, she came out and saw it as well. the object had no sound was flying north to south, flew right over our apartment into the clouds, then disappeared. then as i was looking in the direction of its disappearing, i turned my head to see a second one coming in the same flight pattern. i could see and air plane behind it flying from west to east with strobe lights, so i knew it was not a plane. I have never seen anything like it in my life. it was amazing to see something like that.
