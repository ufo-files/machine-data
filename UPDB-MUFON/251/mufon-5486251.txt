{dr}Object appeared to be watching us as we watched it once it was noticed traveling parallel to us.

We were traveling home after some shopping and I noticed something out of the corner of my eye and brought it to my husband's attention. It appeared to be mimicking our actions as we slowed and stopped at traffic signals.It looked to be a block over from the road we were on so we tried to get closer by pulling in behind a bar.The object stopped over a house and a tree directly in front of us, like it was following us, where we pulled in to try to get closer and we both seemed to sit and stare at each other. The object had multiple colors of lights that seem to rotate and flash around the object. We both knew we were looking at a UFO, as nothing could do this, the body of the craft seemed to almost blend into the dusk/dark sky and especially as there was absolutely no sound coming from the object. We wondered why no one else seemed to notice the object. I don't know how long we sat and examined each other but eventually the lights stopped flashing and rotating and went completely black they came back on and remained solid,after what seemed to be only a minute. I was afraid of what was going to happen next, then the object elevated above the house and tree even further and began moving back to the west in the direction we had originally come from. We followed for probably about a half mile before it shot off,at a speed that no one could follow, into the sky.
