{dr}Saw lights in the sky moving in odd ways

I was on the phone with a relative in my neighborhood, when they mention seeing lights in the sky. The phone disconnects so I went outside to see if I could see what they saw and I saw about 5-10 lights in the sky becoming brighter and all together in a circle, then break apart into a line. I called to everyone in the house to come out and see the lights. As they came out I went in to get my camera, when I turned on the camera I found the batteries dead. I went back in to get batteries and came out and started recording the video. The video is grainy at some points but when I zoom out it does look clearer. I took another video after with some lights before they completely stopped. These lights seemed to be coming from behind the clouds and lit up the clouds they were near. They moved quickly at times and hovered at other times. I heard no noise and just kept thinking what could this be! My friend took video with his phone at the same time, from the corner of my street. I would really love to know what we saw.
