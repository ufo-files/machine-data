Big bright object hovering above me among the stars, black hole sucks it in.

I live in an extremely dark rural area. I like to stargaze on my free time.

On a clear dark night, relatively new moon, I noticed a peculiar looking star-like object directly above me. It was much bigger than all other stars; but it couldn't have been a star as it was at least 3x larger and brighter than seeing a planet at its brightest. It appeared closer than a star too. It strangely intrigued me, so I fixated on it for at least 15 minutes; I felt the strangest feeling looking at it. 

All a sudden, it looked as if a perfectly circular and prominent black hole having the size of a dime formed around the object; it sucked the object in within a second, and then vanished. Following that, was perfectly circular grey cloud that appeared and slowly faded within two seconds.

I've always been skeptical of any sort of extra-terrestrial life, I've never seen anything paranormal or as strange in my whole life, and I've stargazed all my life growing up... but I'm now convinced UFOs are among us. However if I told anyone I would sound crazy. I know it's not possible to see black holes in the sky. The following weeks after, our municipality would have strange unexplained power outages, and I was always afraid to sleep at night. 

I've thought about it for months, and finally decided to submit a report, hoping maybe someone else in the area has seen something similar.
