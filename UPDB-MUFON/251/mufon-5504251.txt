{cak} A large blue orb over the gray clouds right before the beginning of sunrise

It was around 6:20 am while walking my little dog Bingo I looked up and saw this beautiful blue orb that was rather large. First I thought it was the blue sky appearing before sunrise but the clouds were real gray and overcast. The orb seem to be in front of them. I was transfixed with the sighting. I looked down at my dog and back up and it was gone. I viewed it for a good 4-5 minutes. When I saw it I was excited thinking this is a sighting and at the same time I'm thinking what is this. It was really beautiful and I first thought is this some weird Northern Light.
