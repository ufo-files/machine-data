{dr}5 Orange fire-like orbs came from the south then turned west.

Standing on our balcony on the Outer Banks looking West toward the mainland we saw five orange globe-like objects flying up the Sound. We often see military aircraft in the area and these did not fit any observed description of one of them. They flew north then in front of us arched to the west. There was no observable sound. All of the aircraft we see in the area make some type of noise. The almost looked like hot air balloons that were on fire but the all followed the same path and turned west.
