I was in the backseat of a car and saw 4 red orbs sitting still in the sky

I was with my two friends on the night of Sep 18th, 2021. We were driving on wantagh parkway to go to Jones beach. I was in the backseat of the car and all of a sudden I noticed 4 red lights sitting in the sky. I didn’t have time to point it out to my friends as I was too busy trying to record it. Once we drove past the orbs I looked back only to see that they were gone. I don’t think these were flares because they usually flicker and drop down from the sky. These lights I saw were standing still in the sky for a good minute. I was the only one to witness these lights out of my friends. I got video of the event that’s a little less than 20 seconds long.
