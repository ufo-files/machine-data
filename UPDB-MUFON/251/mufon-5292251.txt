Observed large object in photo

At approximately 6:30pm yesterday, I was taking multiple photos of this particular scene with the soybean fields and the windmill (approx 60 feet high) as I approached it on my evening walk. I looked through each of my photos of the same scene and zoomed into each one to see what I liked the best for my instagram account. When I zoomed into this photo, I saw the round black object which seems to be very large given the distance and size relative to other things in the picture. During this evening while I was multiple photos of the same photo, I did not hear or see this object. It only appeared later when looking closer in the photo. There were no objects in any of the photos I took before or after this one. I included the original photo and one where I zoomed in and circled the object. It looks to be either round or possibly triangular shaped when I zoom in. My first reaction is that it is too large to be a bird. I would think that if it was a standard airplane, the wings would be more pronounced. Not really sure what it could be - but it had to be fast so not to end up in any of my other photos.
