Small grey disc shaped craft going across sky

I was standing in the back yard watching the satellites. A small meteorite came down to my left. About 15 minutes later I watched a small grey disc-shaped craft passed directly overhead from east to west. There were no lights. It was completely silent. There was a very thin mistlike cloud last night and this travelled either just above or in the clouds. It was out of line of sight within 10-15 seconds. It was not travelling superfast, but looked like it had a purpose or destination.
