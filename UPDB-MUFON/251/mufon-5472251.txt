{rjl} Just above treetops I saw what I thought was a huge helicopter with a long red pulsing light on one side. No sound at all and then it disappeared.

My husband and I were inside of our vehicle sitting under our carport and I saw what I thought was an unusually large helicopter with a long flashing red line on one side that went along the outline of the aircraft on one side. the sighting was after dark. It looked kind of bright inside like maybe there was a window on the side of it which was on same side as the direction it was traveling. It was barely moving, almost hovering. What was weird was there was no light on the tail that you normally see on a helicopter. I just assumed it was a helicopter. There was no sound at all. When I pointed to it to show my husband, it disappeared.
