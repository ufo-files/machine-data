During the time my sister and I were heading to her apartment. When we turned out of our parents house I saw something extremely bright hovering in the distance over a neighborhood. I thought it was a helicopter, but it wasn't moving, just staying completely still. Heading down Pflumm road, I realized that it was mad of three lights. You could see a triangle shaped out of the three lights, it was darker than the night sky. I quickly told my sister to turn around when we reached the next light. Once we turned, the bright lights were gone. We parked the car, looked around in the sky but saw no trace.
