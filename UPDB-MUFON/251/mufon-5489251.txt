{nd} Simply appeared then disappeared, fish like black body, orange tail. Near clouds, no wind.

I was sitting on the porch smoking a cigarette and talking on the phone to a friend in California. About 5 minutes into the conversation I caught movement above me. When I looked up I saw an object that reminded me of a decorative Chinese fish. It was black bodied with an orange tail. At first I thought it was a kite. Then I realized it was far too high, and there was no wind, not even the clouds were moving. I watched it for an hour thinking it might have been an unmanned hover craft. It slowly turned from side to side. I wanted to get a closer look with the camera on my phone. When I turned the camera on and aimed it to the sky the object was gone. It had simply disappeared the same way it appeared. I spent 10 minutes looking for before I came to this web site and there is still no sign of it. It didn't come near the ground, make any sound that I could hear, there were no lights that I could see, and no trail of any kind. It's the strangest thing I've ever seen.
