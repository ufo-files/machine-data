Mysterious Lights (Orbs) and then an Ghostly Entity Caught on Ring security camera.

On May 14th, 2021, at approximately between 2209hrs (10:09 pm) to 0026hrs, my Ring security camera caught 19 - 90 second clips of Orbs moving swiftly and slowly around my garage. In one of these video clips, several vehicles pass by the garage, which debunks any notion that these Orbs are a reflection of anything light source. The video which captures these Orbs at 2329hrs shows them in a linear formation and then swiftly disappearing out of range of the camera. The video clip at 0026hrs shows a possible entity, coming into the camera and slowly drifting past and underneath the mounted camera.
