Red Light followed I-5 North, Hovered, then Zipped Upwards & Vanished

I was standing in the Harbor Freight parking lot about to get into my truck when I looked up and noticed what at first I thought was a small plane, several miles off below hilltop level, about to make an emergency landing on I-5 during a busy traffic period. Thinking this was an imminent disaster I was powerless to resist rubbernecking...

Slowly as it approached it descended well below the surrounding hills to what appeared to be maybe 150ft altitude and moved northward along the freeway corridor. I realized I could not see wings or fuselage glinting in the bright setting sun, then it also occurred to me that the brilliant red light was glowing steadily, not a plane's strobe lights. 

It moved steadily north, slowly growing larger and drifting slightly left, seeming to follow the freeway, then it stopped moving and held its position. Nearer in my field of vision was the traffic signal on Hwy 199 and the stoplight turned red, but was significantly less bright (50%?) than the strange red light seeming to hover just above and to the left, but at least a mile further in the distance.

The red light stayed for perhaps 30-45 seconds as I glanced around to see if anyone else noticed what I was seeing, but I was alone in the parking lot. I glanced back, puzzled by this brilliant red light, seemingly without source. I stared at it expectantly for several seconds when it suddenly zipped upwards and either faded into the distance, or faded out & disappeared so fast I couldn't follow it with my eyes, it simply streaked into the blue sky and vanished.
