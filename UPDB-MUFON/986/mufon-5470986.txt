{cak} Silver disk flies by me at eye level while sitting at intersection

I jumped out of my Jeep on Bristol Road and took five photographs on my I Pad. The I Pad logs several nanoseconds of movement in every picture for some reason I didn't even know that. I was focused on a brightly lit light hovering by the power lines and street lamp. I saw in before in 2014, and saw the same thing again now. A couple days later I remembered the photos, and looked at them while I was in a dark room. Then I noticed that besides the silver disc with white lights, there was a large gray craft in the photo. There was also a triangle of white lights I didn't notice before, near the street lamp. The silver disc flew between two houses past the larger craft. I didn't see the red light reflecting off the large gray craft. At the time I only saw the thing with the bright lights. After I looked at it it began to move away. It passed in front of me at 40-50 mph. It made a strange noise when it accelerated. After 15 seconds I jumped back in my Jeep and took off.
