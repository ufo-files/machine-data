{nd} I watched two pink orbs hover, then come together, then disappear

I was out camping and one of my friends came to pick me up to hangout since they live close to the campground. On the way back once we were back on the campground he told me to look at the horizon. There were two pink orbs floating right above the trees, across the bay. I watched them move farther apart, come together, and then disappear once I got out of the car. A first I thought it may have been a flare, being on the water it isn't implausible for someone to be in distress. Afterwards I felt really afraid. I don't know if it is related, but today 01/08/16 there was a big boom across the whole county and no one can figure out what it was.
