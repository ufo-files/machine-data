{tjd} 2 hovering stars then accelerated at extremely high rate of speed

Both educated professional and competent witnesses. Visiting the wine country by Seneca lake and stargazing late at night. All stars in the sky were completely static. Then two of the "stars" which were static and close to each other in proximity immediately accelerated from a dead stop in directions approximately 80 degrees apart from each other. One heading North and the other East. No blinking lights on either object and extremely fast. Faster than any plane I have seen or know of and at apparently a higher altitude. We observed planes flying that night both before and after the event and could clearly differentiate planes from the UFOs. Neither of us has ever seen an object travelling at such a high rate of speed.
