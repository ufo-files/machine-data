{jpg} Reflective orbs seen at what appeared to be a very high altitude flying in a linear path, disappearing and then reappearing in the daytime sky.

I saw the object going westbound on I 20 near the Douglasville exit. I was in the passenger seat watching planes pass overhead when I noticed something glint in the bright sunlight. At first I thought it was another plane, but I could see no vapor trail, and it was way too high up to be a helicopter. It was higher up than any airplane in the sky at that time by a good twenty degrees from my field of vision. As I continued to look, I noticed the outline of a spherical objects which was otherwise transparent with a slight glow flying in a linear fashion from north to south. After flying for 1-2 seconds, the object seemed to disappear. It reappeared a second or so later in a spot that was not continuous with a linear flight path and then flew behind a nearby cloud.
