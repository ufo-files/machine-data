Meteor like object headed for house, then became a diamond like object that cruised/hovered over the house at low altitude and made no noise.

I was sitting with my friend in his hot tub when all of the sudden a meteor like object appeared to be heading for us from far out extremly fast. Once it got to us it was flying very low with no noise and was a diamond shape. It had 4 white/natural lights on the corners that didn't blink. It was cruising while over the house, then stopped for about 1 second then continued to cruise and we lost sight of it in the trees. We concluded that it had to be a ufo based on the fact that it cruised over the house at an altitude well under a mile and made no noise at all. The entire event happened in about 15 to 20 seconds.
