{jpg} very bright object hovered in the same area for about 5-7 minutes disappeared then reappeared.

I was getting off work and I was getting carts, I started getting carts at the far left corral and there were no stars out yet. Once I had gotten all the carts to middle corral I had looked NW and I saw this very bright object in the sky. It was too big to be a star and it wasn't moving and had no flashing lights like a helicopter or airplane would have. I continued getting carts and the object was still in the same position, it looked like it was pulsating a little I even stopped to look at it for about a minute. Then once I had gotten the rest of the carts to the last corral I had noticed the objected had disappeared and reappeared to the left of it's original position and was a little bit lower. When I had gotten all the carts I looked back in the direction of where I'd seen the object was gone. When the objected disappeared it looked like it was moving in slow motion, but it also looked like it was moving very fast at the same time. The best way I can describe that last sentence is the light looked like it was extended until it stopped moving.
