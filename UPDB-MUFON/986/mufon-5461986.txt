{cak} Like a harvest moon.

Date: 1965, Spring/summer. (Your program would not accept the date.). We ( my Parents, three brothers and myself) were going to my Grandparents house on the north side of the airport. We were driving around the airports NE side to the N side coming from Crestwood to Inglenook. My Dad was driving and I was seated behind him at the window. I had been paying attention to the airport runway lights. I remember looking up and seeing the moon: it was a rich golden color and was growing. It was beautiful. We all looked at it. Thats all I remember of the incident.
About 10 years later, in Pinedale, Al, I took out the garbage (can was outside back door) after dark. Strong wind started up; it seemed to be coming from above our house so I looked up expecting to see a helicopter. For some reason I put my hand & arm up as if to block the Sun....nothing was there. I became afraid and went back into the house.
(Pinedale, Al is just outside Montgomery, and not too far from Maxwell AFB.).
Within next 2-3 yrs. my sister-in-law witnessed helicopter over her ranch near Ramer, Al. Copter shone bright light over her trailer, her barn, and grounds in-between. Next morning her favorite quarter horse was found dead on the edge of a pond on her property, with branches of tree next to it broken as though the horse was dropped through it. I talked her into reporting it to Maxwell AFB while I was there.
