{td}Bright object under 1,000 ft moving overhead then shot of at 90 degree angle very fast

As reported to ASD today in person:"Last night I arrived home after taking my wife to work and I had the feeling to look at the sky to the south. I looked up and saw an very bright white object coming towards me at slow speed, then shoot off at a 90 degree angle very quickly and much faster than I've ever seen a plane move. It was out of sight in less than a second. The object did not turn, it simply moved suddenly to the west, unlike normal aircraft. I believe it was at about 1,000 feet or less. There was no sound.
