{cak} Standing on balcony saw an extremely bright light coming from the northwest flying east watched for about 1 min before taping

I live in the Hollywood area of Los Angeles. My apartment is within 100 feet of the Walk of Fame. On the night I witnessed this UFO I was in my apartment with some other friends of mine. I went outside onto my balcony to smoke a cigarette when at aprox. 8:25pm I noticed a starlike, bright, white light in the NW sky. Using my cell phone compass, I've approximated it to be at 310 degrees NW. It steadily headed in my direction with no sound and a constant to brightening white light. It continued in a curved path when I lost it over my own apartment roof when it was then heading 90 degrees east. The object was to bright to determine any structural details , just an orb shaped light. I started my recording about half way through my sighting, in total I viewed the object for no more than three minutes. My video is 1:21 minutes long. My feelings during and after the sighting were relatively calm. I've seen several UFO's on this same balcony, but this is the first I captured on my phone. But yet I was still slow to the punch in recording, I guess there is always that initial shock factor when you see something you know isn't quite normal.
