Glowing orb seen above road.

Hi Im submitting this picture on behalf of my friend who actually saw the object together with his dauther and took the photo with his phone early morning on 4th June 2018. They described it as a small glowing orange orb just above the road and on right to the sun as seen in the pic. It was stationary and they observed it for a few minutes before they left the area. Estimated high of orb was 50 meters or less. Unfortunately they did not stay long enough to see where it went or how it came to be there as both were commuting to work and school respectively. Also both were positive that it wasn't a sundog, star or similar object and were quite excited to see something extraordinary just feet from their house, something that should not be actually there.
