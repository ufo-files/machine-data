On July 6th around 1AM walked off porch towards street. We looked up and in southwest horizon noticed an object that should not have been there. It was low in the sky and brighter than anything else in the sky. Watched for about 10 minutes and noticed it was slowly drifting lower on the horizon. Went and got the binoculars and decided to drive and follow it. It was oval shaped with colored lights rotating in the middle section. Even without the binoculars you could see the colored lights. It started drifting a little faster. Drove from Binghamton NY to Johnson City NY (apx 4 miles)to get a better view. Now it changed direction going north towards the Broome County airport. Watched it for about 2 1/2 hours. During this time it had smaller bright white lights that where orbiting around main craft. Sometimes they disappeared then would reappear sporadically. Further off,I could see a second craft that also had colored lights. But it was definitely farther north. Since it drifted right by the airport I wondered if the air traffic controller was seeing this. It was right there at the airport. Also another craft came from the south and shoot across the the sky northbound. It was very fast moving and could not tell where it went. The other colored light crafts were still there when I went home.
