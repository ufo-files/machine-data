{cak} 2 hovering objects, then dozens & dozens of strobe flashing lights

First I saw 2 stationery lights. When I looked through binoculars they looked starlike. After many minutes one went straight up and the other went to the northeast. I followed the second with my binoculars and saw both white and green flashing lights. 
Afterwards I saw dozens of white lights in the west blinking on and off. I got one in my binoculars and tracked it as it moved. Then I could see alternating red and white lights in an arrow pattern. 

I have a photo of the 2 stationary lights, but could not submit report on my phone.
