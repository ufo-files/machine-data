Very bright orb in the sky

My friend was over, I had walked my friend out as she was leaving and locked up the door downstairs. I started heading upstairs passed by the large windows in the corridor of my house and I noticed from my peripheral view a big ball of light or a huge white orb you could say. While I turned to look at it I saw a lot of colors (like the colors of the rainbow) and by the time I passed to the other window to take a better look it had already disappeared. 

What made me notice the object was it's bright lights, since they were really bright I don't think it would've gone unnoticed. I didn't know what to think when I first saw the object, I was just really curious to what it was. It didn't seem to be moving, just standing still; and judging by the location, it seemed like it was close to the airport (since there's an airport not too far away from my house). A few seconds after I noticed the bright orb, it moved a bit and I caught a glimpse of a short, colorful (rainbow) streak that followed, but it didn't leave a trail. All that happened in the span of 3-4 seconds I would say.

After the sighting, I'm not sure if this is connected, but I felt really weird. I felt a lot of mixed emotions like being confused, sad, and upset for no apparent reason.
