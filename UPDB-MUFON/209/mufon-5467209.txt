Saw HUGE

My husband and I were having a campfire in our yard (country). It was around 12:30. I saw a bright light in the west, and thought airplane... but it was so huge, and was not moving. I pointed it out to my husband,and he said satellite. But I mentioned that it wasn't moving, and was HUGE and BRIGHT. It was the clearest white color, and completely circular. About 40-45 degrees up. Almost instantly after we both were watching it, it started to move north-bound. Fast. And it instantly dimmed to the brightness of a star, and then eventually disappeared. The entire encounter was 30 seconds. The encounter after it started moving was about 20 seconds. Immediately after it was gone, we discussed, and we both concluded it was not "normal." Too bright, too circular, too fast...
