Start size pulsing rhythmic light

While watching the stars we noticed that one of them was beginning to 'pulse'. It started off very slowly and then over a 10 minutes became a quick flashing light. Similar to a hazard light. This wasn't a constant flash, but seemed to have a rhythm. This moved a short distance and then was stationary again. It then started to slow right down, until there were almost a second between pulses. Then it just seemed to be gone.
