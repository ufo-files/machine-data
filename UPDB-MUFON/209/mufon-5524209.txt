Cigar shaped UFO infesting the skys around Waller and Hockley, Texas

We were riding fence at dusk and saw thirty or more cigar shaped UFO's infesting the skies. We tried to take pictures of them but our cell phone cameras would not work on them. They buzzed us with humming sounds and there were window like lights up and down the sides of them. We were drinking a bit that night but were not that drunk and could still ride a horse. This was the damnest thing we'd ever seen.
