{cak} Black disc moving fast

I was taking photos of the sunset and snapped several shots in the same direction (without moving my camera at all). I did not see the UFO while I was taking photos. When I got home and looked at my photos, the very first photo has this object in it, but the very next photo did not. There were no planes at all in the sky, and no birds. This object is black, and was moving extremely fast since it doesn’t appear in the very next photo, that I snapped, probably 2 seconds later. If it had been a plane, the object would have appeared in the very next photo. It doesn’t look like a plane, is large ( and is definitely not a bird). You will need to zoom in to see object.
