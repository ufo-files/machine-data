Three objects moving in funny way from left to right, at steady speed before vanishing and leaving black trail behind

I was taking my nephew home, the time was 20:43 on Sunday the 17th July 2016 in a town in England called Grimsby. my nephew got out my car and was just about to enter his house and I looked up in sky and above the field where we live next to I was looking at 3x black or grey kind of type of aircraft but all three were at least three times bigger and a lot wider than any aircraft I've seen before, they was so close to each other and they were a funny shape slightly curved at the top of objects, I shouted to my nephew and as soon as he looked they disappeared, they did leave three black smoke trials in the sky that's lasted for 6 to 7 seconds before they disappeared that he saw. They was nowhere in sky to be seen, like they vanished out nowhere but when I saw them they was moving funny and not fast at all. My nephew saw the trails in the sky and he said he thought he heard a shotgun noise but I never as I was in car. The field that it happend over on is a football field and no hunting or shooting can happen or take place on it as there's no cover for trees or anything just a open field where children play football on that's for public
