We thought it was a star until it started moving rapidly

My wife and I had been seeing several odd flashes of lights prior to this day. Watching a meteor shower, and seeing a blue teardrop come down. Not like falling but like something dropping. Also had witnessed a green one over my neighbor’s house, who just so happens to be my wife’s grandmother, the same night. There are more as well that we believe had led up to this night. We had just set up our telescope, to watch the Great Conjuction and I kept seeing a star in front of me, that kept flashing and pulsating. I felt at the time it was trying to get my attention and I kept telling my wife it didn’t look like a star at times. The more we watched the more I was convinced that it was not. It started getting cold so we started packing the telescope up and that’s when I saw a bright flash of white light. It was like someone turning on a flashlight at me from the sky. As we watched it,it started to pulsate faster and started to rise up. Once it got to a certain height it appeared to have changed shapes. It moved across the horizon and looked to just fade out. I was able to shine my flashlight at it and the object appeared to reflect the light but I couldn’t make out the shape or details of the ship. That is what we are certain we captured.
