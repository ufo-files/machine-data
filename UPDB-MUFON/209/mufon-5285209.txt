Round UFO pulsating different colours.

Around 5am me and my partner were looking out the window when something caught our eye. We immediately grabbed our phone and started to record. My hand was very shaky... It looks like it's moving all over the place but it was actually stationary. I zoomed in and out so you could see the distance and by zooming out you can see its not moving. It was flickering changing colours for over an hour. It was still there at 6.10. When we looked around 6.45 it was almost daylight and you couldn't see it. We assumed it was gone but can say for sure. It's almost like the circle expands bigger and then smaller changing colours whilst doing this.
