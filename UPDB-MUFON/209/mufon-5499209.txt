{cak} very bright light ascended straight up and disappeared

I went out into the garden between 9 and 10 pm. I looked up to see if the stars were visible or if it was going to be a cloudy night. I immediately saw a bright light. There is a small craft airport a few miles north of me. There are always planes in the sky. When they are coming straight at you they look very bright until they get a little closer and you aren't just seeing the front light anymore, you can see the blinking colored lights on the side. I thought that was what i was seeing but it was almost directly above me and that was strange. It didn't move. It grew brighter for a couple of seconds then faded out or ascended within a few more seconds until it completely disappeared. I stayed out for another 10 minutes and saw nothing else out of the ordinary.
