{rjl} Triangle like flying object.

It looked like a triangle flying object but the bottom of the triangle was curved. It had a red constant light at the tip of the triangle and multicolored lights along the curve. I tried looking out other windows but I couldn’t see it. Then I pulled into a gas station and there was nothing in sight. Definitely not a plane moving across the sky.
