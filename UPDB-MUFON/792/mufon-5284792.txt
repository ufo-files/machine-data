UFO flame and changed into orb

At 7:54 pm last night (Oct 14th) my wife and I observed a UFO in the shape of a flame south to south-southeast of Gooseberry Island in Westport, MA. 

When we first observed it, it was the shape of a flame moving left to right, stopped and then morphed before our eyes into a glowing orange/red orb and started moving vertically down the horizon. It then began to hover and blink. After blinking a few times, it disappeared.

I have added a video, 4 photos and the screenshot of a flight app around the time we saw the object.
