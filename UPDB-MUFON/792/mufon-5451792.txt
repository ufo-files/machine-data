Green and Red in Eastern sky to the right of the moon traveling Eastward moving to East view of moon at high rate of speed

At about 8:55 PM on July 3rd, I stepped outside to sit and smoke a cigarette when I saw a blinking light approaching the proximity to the moon. It was blinking red and green. ( I'm familiar with aircraft lights and they don't flash like that). The object was traveling Northward and made several directional moves and continued North. I called my wife and she came out and saw it too. She said that looked like what she saw 3 years ago. We continued to watch the light and she handed me my flashlight and I pointed it that direction and turned it on, first regular then strobe. I did this a couple of times. The object stayed in one place and the lights went out a couple of times. Then it headed in a Southernly direction. When it got about to the moon it suddenly blinked bright white about 3 times then it looked like it was heading downward to or possibly E/SE and disappeared. Helicopters and planes don't move like that.
