Observed star/satellite point of light move across dark sky at high rate of speed

At 8:25pm CDST, 8/28/2021 while star gazing on my courtyard, just after watching a conventional jet standard night lighting flying north to south at altitude, I noticed a point of light at a much higher altitude appearing like a distant star moving very fast from south to north, I assumed it was a satellite. But it traveled some distance very fast and stopped dead stop for a second or two and reversed it’s course back north to south on same path but only stopped half way, stopped, made two circles very quickly, then went in a south-west direction a second or two, stopped again, did another circle, continuing SW when I lost sight of it.
