{dr}Saw at the corner of my eye, really bright. Then sped off really fast

I was standing outside taking my dog out for the night, and (as I always do) I was star-gazing. I was looking at the big dipper, just to the north of my house, and a bright light caught my eye... so I turned to the left to see this bright white ball of light speed off slightly toward the horizon and to the left. I sat there for a little bit kind of confused because a satellite doesnt go or blink out that fast (you can see them all the time) and it wasnt a plane because I saw one earlier before this (also seeing them fly by to land in Detroit, or just passing high up above).
