it was a hot night in june of 1994 my friend andi were bass fishing on lake mccoy in apopka florida i stretched my neck and inadvertantly looked to the right. thats when iobserved the brightest light i had ever seen in the night sky.i commented to my friend hey do you that xxxx and at that exact moment it was hovering directly over our heads.imean 20 to 30 feet above.it had around circle of super bright light directly in the center of the bottom of itwith slightly duller rectangular lights surrounding the circle it emitted a strange low pitched whirring pulsating sound and the rectangles seemed to get brighter in sequence.at some point it shot off and up to the southeast paused and disappeared. and when we got back to the truck one and one half hours had elapsed.my friend and i discussed the event briefly and agreed to tell no one.not long after this we quit speaking to eachother and have not seen or spoke to eachother since and we were best friends. inever believed in ufos or much of anything else up to that point but this event was truly alife changing experience.thanks for the chance to finally tell this story
