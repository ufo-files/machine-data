Large black triangle shaped UFO hovering over HWY 580

My husband and I were traveling north on HWY 580 towards HWY 13 when we saw a 3 brilliant bright lights (changing colors - green, red, white)in the sky over the freeway. As we continued on the freeway, I opened up our sunroof in the car and as we traveled directly underneath I could see the black massive triangular shaped object hovering above with no sound, the lights all turned white. I watched it slowly travel south through the rear window. No longer was the triangle in view only 3 tail white lights. We exited the freeway to get back on heading south (same direction as object)but could not see it any longer. I lived near an SFO for over 20 years and worked for the airlines for a short period of time - this object was NOT A PLANE.
