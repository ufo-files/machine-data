{rjl} Observed three

Came home from a late night cruise in my car, and my eyes were im mediately drawn up to the sky. It was then I saw the first "UFO". Bright randomly flashing lights that seemed to swirl and flash all over the "craft". Moved much slower than the planes I'm used to seeing. And silently at that. There was no cloud cover at all and I had an unobstructed view of its whole pass overhead. Just as soon as it was out of sight, a second, and then a third followed the exact same path with even spacing and speed. I see alot of plane traffic around my area, but these weren't plane lights that I saw. The intensity and frequency of the flashes was much higher than anything I've seen before and over a greater area of the "craft". They also seemed to be much lower than planes normally fly yet, were completely silent. The whole event was very strange. Like I was supposed to see them. Its hard to explain, I mean, I wasn't looking up and then noticed it. I was looking at the ground and its like something made me look right at it.
