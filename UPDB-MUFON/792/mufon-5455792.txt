{rjl} Flickering Yellow, Very Bright

This object was a uniquely bright object glowing a distinct flickering yellow. The Sky was somewhat overcast, and planes were not visible. 

The flickering of the object gave the inkling of a star, but was in fact much brighter. The only thing i can compare it to is the light given off from a rocket launch, but more Yellow and utterly stationary. I was so dumbstruck by the thing, I slowed my vehicle to view it better.

After driving and viewing the thing for approx five minutes, i looked away to avoid an oncoming vehicle. Looking for the object after that, I could not find it. It had seemingly blinked away, after having been stationary for so long.
