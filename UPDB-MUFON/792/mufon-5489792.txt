{jpg} Rectangular object in sky over Marlton, NJ

At around 6:15 or so on August 26, 2015, I had just gotten off of 295 and was driving on Rt. 70 east when I noticed what appeared to be a rectangular object in the sky a little above the tree lines in the distance. It was a dull (unreflective) solid gray color, and was moving north in a slow descent. The speed of this object was unremarkable--about the speed that planes appear to be moving when observed from the ground. It did not have the shape of a plane, however. It disappered behind the trees moments after I first saw it.
