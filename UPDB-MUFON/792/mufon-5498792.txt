{nd} Viewing on NETFLIX Oceana Air Show 2010. View video at about 12min and 30 sec into show. F14 traveling right to left. Object is behind jet and turns and rapidly leaves view from left to right when viewing on screen.

While viewing the Oceans Air Show on NETFLIX (2010) By the way it is the only air show from Oceana on NETFLIX. Viewing about 12 minutes into the show you will see a F14 Traveling from right to left and an object will be seen behind and above the jet. The object then rapidly descends to the right when viewing on screen. Paused this viewing SEVERAL times and can see a bright top on object before leaving camera view. No assumptions are made as to what it MIGHT Be! I'm a Aviation Mechanic and I did work on the F14 at NARF or NADEP in Norfolk. Thank You.
