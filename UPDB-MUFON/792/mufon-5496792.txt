{cak} I was looking through my telescope at Betelgeuse by Orion, it flew right into view, I saw it clearly, All black and amber lights, It was like slow motion watching it.

It was Jan. 10, 2015 about 6:15PM. I set my telescope up to look for the comet Lovejoy, I was looking at the star Betelgeuse by Orion's belt, then it happened a flying object came right in front of the star it was like slow motion it looked something like a ship from Star wars (Millenium Falcon), it was all black with Dim amber lights, it turned slightly and stayed in my view for about 45 seconds. My telescope is 60X I had it set to the highest setting. I am still in shock at what I saw. Something was flying it. I have seen Shooting stars, satellites, airplanes, but nothing matches this, it was out of our solar system.
