{jpg} witnessed u f o in sky zip in triangle formation and then zip away at incredible speed

My brother,myself, and three friends were walking to my house, when one of the friends said "Hey whats that",pointing straight up.We all looked up and observed a blueish silver object with a number of red lights on it in the sky.At first I thought it was a plane until I realized it was not moving.After a second or two it zipped in a straight line, stopped for a second or two and zipped in another direction and stopped,then zipped in another direction and stopped.This was in a triangular pattern.The object repeated these movements several times,then shot up at about a 70 degree angle at an incredible speed and was gone in a spit second.We all looked at each other and took off running for the house and told my parents what we saw, and of course they told us it was probably a plane or helicopter.The next morning,the news reported that others had seen it and it was also in the paper.I realize that this happened over 50 years ago but I NEVER forgot about it .I also realize,looking back,that when this object would zip then stop,I estimate it would travel approximately ten(10) to fifteen(15) miles in just a split second.
