{kt}I walked out into the backyard, looked toward the foothills, and saw it.

It was a little after 8:00 p.m. I walked out on the back patio to have a smoke like I always do.i just randomly looked over at the foothills to my right, and saw this red,round object with what looked like orange fire( like you see from a rocket) coming out of the back. The thing was, that it made no sound, yet it was moving toward the stationary red lights on the foothill. I remembered that I had my phone in my pocket. So, without taking my eyes off of it, I pulled out my phone. While I was doing this, the orange fire that was coming out of the back went out, and the object stopped moving. It was still red. When I turned my camera on, it turned from red to just a white round light. It then started to move away from the lights on the hil, still not making any sound. I managed to get three still pics. I watched it move away and disappear. The color remained white as it left. At first, I thought that it was a shooting star or something. But, shooting stars don't stop, hover, change direction and color. I truly have no idea what it was. I wish I had thought to get my phone out sooner, I was just so blown away by what I saw. The first pic is of the object moving toward the foothill lights. The next two are of it moving away.
