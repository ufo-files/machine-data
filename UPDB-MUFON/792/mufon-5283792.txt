Looked like a star only for or five times as bright moving across the Blue sky then suddenly disappearing

Driving on interstate 90 in the Berkshires in Massachusetts, me in the passenger seat looked up at the sky in a Northwest direction and saw an object that looked like a star only was so so much brighter and highly visible in of the blue sky even though it was in the afternoon. I saw it moving across the sky in the westward direction and, confused as to what it was, leaned forward to get a better view. Then it suddenly disappeared having been visible to me a total of five to seven seconds. I examined the area in the sky where it had been to see if there was any plane or helicopter that somehow would have had such a bright reflective surface but the sky was completely empty. Then I realized I could not have been just a reflection off of a plane or helicopter because it was far too big and bright.
