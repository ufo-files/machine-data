3 Rrange orbs..changed to bright white, irregular shape, with flashing lights on side

In town of Ruskin at a resort. Noticed 3 bright unusual lights over Tampa Bay that hadnt been there earlier in night or on other nights. My wife and I saw them while outside hotel so I went to room and filmed them from the balcony. Have no idea what they were. There is an airport nearby I believe but these lights stayed pretty much in one area above the bay most of the time (at least 30 minutes).

I don't know if they could have been helicopters searching the bay but they didn't seem like that to us. I have attached a picture that came from the video I took. It is of one of the objects when it is orange. I have about 10 minutes of HD video of them, although it gets pretty shaky at times.
