many lights in the sky that can not be explained

My brother, Dane, and I were both skateboarding in our driveway when suddenly we noticed a “star” that was moving. At first we thought it was an airplane, but then after some consideration we realized there was no blinking light that usually comes with an airplane. After watching it move to the left for awhile, it suddenly did a 90 degree turn upwards, and we were absolutely stunned. Right after we saw that, our outdoor light proceeded to turn off, which made Dane and I run inside afraid. After we came back out, we tried to see if it was still there. It was very cloudy last night (the date it occurred) but once it cleared up a little, we saw at least ten moving “stars”. At one point we saw four of them form a triangle. Then we ran inside to get our dad, as I’m seventeen years old and scared of absolutely everything. After awhile of watching them, we went inside as there ended up being little to no visibility. I talked with my grandma, who lives two houses down from us, and she said she has also seen them (on a separate day). One day last summer we saw a “star” in the daytime that disappeared right in front of our own eyes. I’m not a skeptic (and nor am I a believer) but what we saw can not be explained under any means. (We tried to take video evidence but our phone qualities could not capture it. All we ended up getting was a video of us screaming and laughing.)
