Large glowing disc hovering then flying

We were drivin north up Chinchilla-Tara road. We first noticed the large disc shaped object when it was slowly moving in the air because it was so different to anything else in the sky.
We thought it was a close satellite at first but it was much too large. The object moved East very slowly before it stopped and became stationery in it's tracks. After about 30 seconds of this, the object accelerated at extremely high speeds into the clouds. The only feelings felt were amazement and a little bit scared. There were no physiological changes. We lost sight of the object when it shot off East behind clouds.
