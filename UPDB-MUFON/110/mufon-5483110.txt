{nd} bright white flashes, flying object

while on the deck star gazing with my son, we began seeing bright flashes of light in the northern portion of sky. as if a headlight was being turned on and back off. much much brighter than any star in the sky. few and far between at first, however over the next hour, we seen several flashes similar in diferant areas of the sky. flashes lasting 1-2 seconds each. close to midnight, about 45 degrees above the west horizon, several flashes in quick sucession, roughly in the same area, out of which we watched, what i would describe as a falling star, fly directly overhead toward the east and out of site. seemed slower than a falling star, no flashing lighs, just a dim glow.
