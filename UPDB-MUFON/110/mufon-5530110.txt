Distant star-like moving across western sky during Perseid Meteor Shower

My children and I were watching the meteor shower between 8 and 9 pm MST, then noticed a very distant object that looked like a distant star, it was moving at a steady pace. We watched it for about 10 minutes moving across the sky on a slightly curved path, seemed like a very long distance. Before we couldn't see it anymore it seemed to be dimming a little, like it was getting further away. Then my son said it had a gold or amber glow around it for a second, that's when we saw it shoot off into the distance and couldn't see it anymore. It was so far away there was no way I could have video taped it (not that I even thought about until after), the sky was clear and filled with stars, it seemed to just blend in, except it was moving. There really isn't much more I can say about it, other than I am happy it kept it's distance.
