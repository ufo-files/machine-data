While viewing the Perseid meteor shower on August 13, 2021, at approximately 12:05am, we saw a UFO pass by Chestermere. 

We believe the UFO was dark grey/green in colour, chevron-like shape (but more like a thicker Mercedes Benz logo without the circle around it) , very low to the ground, maybe 1500 feet up, no lights, no sound, moving very quickly from Chestermere NE to Chestermere South. It flew the distance in about 7 seconds and then was out of sight.
