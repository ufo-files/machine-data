{cak} Two Orbs of light, moving incredibly fast, become one and then two again and disappear.

Tonight, at approx 9:10pm, My husband and I witnessed two bright orbs of light moving incredibly fast from east-to-west in the southern sky, it was as if they became one for a split-second and then split-apart again, this all took place within 5-6 seconds! So, I searched the internet to see if there were any additional reports and Yes, this sight was witnessed across Texas by the following reports: 

KATY, TX: Glowing orbs flying fast no sound. BURLESON, TX: Two bright lights traveling very fast with one speeding up to the other, then one disappeared and the other continued straight. KATY TX: Bright blue LED type circular shapes moving across sky to the NW in Katy, Texas. COLLEGE STATION, TX: Bright light flew thru sky, split in two and then disappeared. AUSTIN, TEXAS: Tandem lights streaked across the sky over Austin moving incredibly fast without making a sound. WIMBERLY, TEXAS: One glowing light, airplane like, but moving far too fast, divided into two orbs1. Additional Reports also from: Nacogdoches-TX, Thorndale-TX, Mesquite-TX, Cypress Mill-TX, Manor-TX, Manvel-TX.
