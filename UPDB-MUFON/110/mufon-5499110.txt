I was walking, on my way home, from my friends house at approximately 1:15 am on September 21, 2014. It was a dark sky with light cloud coverage a ball of light was apparent. It did not have the shape of the northern lights. I though the object was a meteor but it never picked up speed. I decided to snap a few pictures on my phone to research later. The object was a little difficult to estimate due to the distance but I believe it was possible the size of a care tire. It came toward my direction slowly appeared to hoover then adjust course and left gracefully with in 10 seconds. I was worried because I wasn't sure what it was and it was coming within my vicinity. I watched the object leave.
