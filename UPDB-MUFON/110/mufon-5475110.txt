Daylight object coming North to South. Silent

Wife and myself were sitting on deck late afternoon and both witnessed object coming from North to South almost directly over our home. At first glance we did not make much of this until the shape became apparent and I grabbed my phone to record so we could zoom in and play back to see what it was. After showing some friends and neighbors, they thought object was definitely odd. Wifi dropped in house. Did not notice and electrical drop as we were outside.
