{dr}Silent, slow moving fireball just above the tree line.

On the evening of July 18th approximately 8:30pm, my husband and I were out walking our dog. Coming from the North/Northwest sky I noticed what appeared to be a rather large orange light moving toward us just above the three line. As it passed overhead, it looked like a glowing fireball. It didn't seem to make any obvious noise such that an airplane would make. It was moving rather slowly and neither of us could see a clearly defined object, just a glowing fireball, but it was low enough in the sky to have been able to identify an airplane or helicopter. I watched the object move across the sky toward the South/Southeast until it went out of sight. My husband thought it may have been a helium balloon on fire being blown by the wind. I have no explanation for what it was.

I am always paying attention to the sky. I have seen many shooting stars over the South Mountain Foothills, Sun Ray park specifically, when I walk at night. There are many airplanes and small prop planes that fly over the area as well, so the object caught my eye immediately because it looked different from normal plane lights. It was larger and much more orange. As it came closer overhead I knew it wasnt an airplane but I have no explanation for what it was.
