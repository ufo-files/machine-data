{cak} saw a cigar shaped object with three windows above the tree line

I stopped next to my dads trailer to relieve myself when I looked up and seen a cigar shaped white object with three porthole windows in it. It hovered a few hundred feet adove the trees and then moved towards the south. It made no noise at all. I was shocked! I tryed getting my brothers attention but he must have been in the house. Whole sighting only lasted 1 to 2 minutes.
