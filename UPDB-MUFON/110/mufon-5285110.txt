UFO?

I was in my back garden (North East direction) watching for bats around 21:30 on Sat 4th September. I noticed what I at first thought was ISS coming over, but considered it’s East to North East direction to be wrong, checking my satellite app I could not see any satellite listed as being in the proximity that I was looking. I next checked my plane app, no planes coming over either. Then from this one light ejected two more, one either side, both of which had flight paths not possible by craft of our planet. The two objects flew in curves, erratically. They were as brightly illuminated as the initial object. The erratic flight paths make no sense in my mind. Has anyone else seen this, and have any suggestions as to what I viewed? The object then went above solid cloud cover and I could see them no more. My first ever UFO sighting/experience which was cut painfully short by clouds!
