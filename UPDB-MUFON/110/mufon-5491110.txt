{rjl} observed flaming object in sky behind house,flaming ball, no sound,father would not believe the observed face, huge eyes in window two straight nights, staring straight at me

My father and I were walking through the woods on our way home from chopping wood and it was dark, I noticed a fireball in the sky above us and mentioned it to him but he said it was a meteor,but it was strange because it was too low and silent.That's all I can remember untilI went to bed and fell asleep . I don't know what woke me up but when I looked at my window there was a strange face, huge head and those huge black eyes!we just stared at each other, I wanted to turn away but couldn't, then I guess I fell back to sleep. I didn't mention it to my father because he was really macho and didn't believe in the paranormal.The next night it happened again ,same face, same eyes, but this time I got up out of bed and walked to the window and once again we stared at each other for about two or three minutes I think. When I woke up next morning I told my brothers about it but they laughed at me, they slept in the same room with me but now I wonder why I never thought to wake them up during the episode.I did go to the spot where I estimated the"fireball" might hit if it had touched earth and found abare spot but being young I didn't know what to look for. Now I shiver when I think about the face and eyes in the window. Funny though at the time,I felt no fear at all.I am willing to submit myself to a lie detecting machine or whatever it takes to show that it really happened.I've not talked about this for45 years because I wasn't sure if it really happened or if I dreamed it and also ridicule, but I remember both "staring sessions very clearly.Once again I will submit to anything to show this actually happened.Thank you for listening..had to get it off my chest finally.Thanks again!
