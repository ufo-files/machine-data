2 Linked Orbs Flashing Blue in the Sky

I noticed that there was this flashing or brightening blue looking light or star in the sky at night. In my other videos of it, it looks a really blue but on this early, cold winter morning, it looks like two linked golden orbs that are flashing blue light.

It was there almost every night in Jan-Feb 2018. When I went outside to feed the dogs or cats or take a video or photo of it at different times of the night on different days, it was invariably in the sky. Sometimes, on different nights, this light or object was in the northern sky or in the western sky. This time, it was in the western sky.

With my own eyes, I could tell that it was moving around and the light around it was flashing or getting brighter. I could see that was kind of too big to be a star and I could see the blue. I couldn't really tell how low it was in the sky or how far away. I am shooting the video through some trees in the yard so it is not that high in the sky.

I think it was fairly close (closer than a star) because of the video I have of it. I couldn't see the detail with my own eyes but the video shows what looks like linked gold orbs that intermittently flash a very nice blue. It is very pretty.
