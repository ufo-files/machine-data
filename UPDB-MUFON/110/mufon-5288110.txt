Unidentified light raised, lowered, split, rejoined, changed colors, and pulsated before rapidly leaving

I was waiting for the school bus just after 6:00 AM during the winter of either ‘94-95 or ‘95-96. I had probably recently turned 12 or 13, and it was likely January or February. 

I should say immediately that I first noticed the light in the sky to the northwest, generally in the direction of Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, which was 15 miles away.

Over the course of probably over three minutes, I watched the light hover, pulsate get dimmer and brighter, and change colors - I remember at least red, white, and yellow. It raised and lowered, at no point low enough to be obstructed by the treeline, Once, it split into two lights. These lights at the very least traveled away from each other a fair distance horizontally before coming back together. I’m not sure I’m remembering this detail correctly, but I think they may have moved back and forth horizontally a bit before they came back together. While apart, each light was as bright as the single. Very suddenly, the single light travelled away, either northward or up and northward at a very, very high rate of speed. 

I’ve not seen anything like it before that or in the intervening 25 years. I do imagine the visual could be roughly replicated by a pair of helicopter drones now, but to the best of my knowledge, that technology wasn’t developed until long after my experience. I also did not get the impression that what I was looking at was small or close.
