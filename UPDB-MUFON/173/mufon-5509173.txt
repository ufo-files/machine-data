First and foremost I want to make this clear! I am Not looking for publicity. I don't care what anyone thinks about me. And I don't care if people think Im crazy. I just want to know the truth. My Mom, step dad and me saw a lot of strange lights in the sky almost every night in the summer of 1998 or maybe 1999. Sorry but I really cant remember the year.
A lot happened before and after that. But the night I saw the big red light come up and over the trees is a night I will never forget (or never remember). Because thats all I remember. The big red light came up over the trees and was moving toward me and I cant remember anything after that. Before that night my Mom and I both had waking dreams of someone standing over us and we couldnt move. I have a Lot more to talk about but don't want to on here. I really hope you don't think Im crazy or making this up.
