{cak} Silver disc object hovered above neighborhood then slowly came towards me.

One afternoon I decided to go outside for no apparent reason. I just stood in my driveway and looked out at the sky. Planes and helicopters fly by my house everyday but in this particular afternoon, none passed by. However, I noticed a silver disc-shaped object hovering where I was looking. It was still and did not move for what felt like a long period of time. The moment that observation clicked in my head was when I knew that it was not a plane or helicopter. The longer I looked at it, the more I felt like it was watching me. That was when it started to slowly move in my direction. I had felt like my feet were rooted to the ground until I noticed that I was heading towards me. I lost sight of it the moment I ran inside my house.
