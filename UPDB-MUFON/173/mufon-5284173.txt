Close Encounters blinking lights around the outside edge.

I saw them both on 11/15 & 11/16 around 6:10 pm. I could only see one well; it was round, it had white, red, & green lights around the outside edge and it spun slowly in a counter-clockwise direction. The lights flickered. It stood still for awhile and then moved slowly south. My son came over the first night and watched it with me. I called my daughter in Acworth (20 miles north of me) and she saw them as well from her deck. The one I could not see as well was much higher up, but you could see that it also had similar lights that flickered. There's a lot of air traffic here, as we're relatively close to Dobbins AFB, Atlanta airport and a county airport nearby. There was no mistake that these were NOT airplanes.
