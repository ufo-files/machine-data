{dr}Starlike 30 sec. zigzag movement left trail zoomed out of site

1974 or 75 Army tank Competition laying on tank waiting our turn. Looked up and say a star like UFO zigzagging in the sky above us. Put my thumb and fore finger to see how far zigzag movement was between thumb and finger about 1 inch. It was moving forward in a zigzag movement for about 30 seconds. Then it zoomed out of sight leaving a light trail, full throttle.
