{cak} Star like light moving over North Head Manly 4 nights in a row

I noticed a Star from my bedroom window at 4am Tuesday when I was up feeding my baby, I noticed it move, so watched it longer. It rapidly changed direction, made small loops, went up straight into the sky and dropped directly downwards like it was falling and continued to do this for another hour until the sun came up. I checked again the next night but didn’t see it. Thursday night I saw it again and it did the same thing which I managed to capture on my phone camera but quality isn’t great, at one stage another aircraft hit close to it and the light shot directly up and a red light came shooting out the underneath, again it left at sunrise. Then it was back again Friday night - first noticed it further north/east over the ocean at around 7pm, it went directly upwards when planes started coming in that direction to land at Sydney airport and soon left. I noticed it again at 4am and filmed it from a video camera on a tripod - it zips across the sky, disappears, reappears in a different area and then zips away out of the lense area. At one stage before I had filmed it an aircraft flew right up to it - I presume it was a regular plane but it was before 5am and in Sydney we have a flight curfew so no commercial flights can fly at that hour. After this it headed upwards and SE until it disappeared. I have noticed it out over the ocean again at dusk tonight.
