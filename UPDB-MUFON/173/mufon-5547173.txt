White Ball of Light

I walked out the front door to take the dog out about 10:20 and I always look up at the sky to look for shooting stars and constellations. I got to the street and turned around looking NE above my house I saw what I thought at first was an airplane approaching or leaving the local airport which is about 5 miles away to the north. It was moving about the same speed as a very low level jet but seem to be at a much higher altitude and as I looked closer it looked more like a star - Round. It was about 80% above the horizon if 90% is directly overhead. As I walked slowly down the street I kept my eye on it and it continued to move smoothly N to S and I watched it for about 15 seconds until it just shrank into nothing or slowly dimmed to become invisible. There was no trail as with a shooting star and moved too slow to be a shooting star. My eyes followed its trajectory thinking I would see running lights if it were a jet aircraft that had just turned off its landing lights but there were no lights whatsoever and I listened closely for any jet engine or other engine sounds but heard nothing while it was visible or after it became invisible.
