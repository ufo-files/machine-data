{dr}Directly above observer starlike object was stationary, then moved in easterly direction

Nightly I go out to observe stars and planets. The other night, around 1am I was looking directly up and looking at the stars. One started to move. It moved at a leisurely pace toward the east and then was obscured by cloud cover. I thought it might be a satellite or the space station. I have observed satellites before and this was too bright to be a satellite. Maybe a meteor or something. But it had no brilliant glow or intensity in it's appearance. I do not believe it is an airplane. We are in the flight path of MSP so I am used to seeing departing (higher altitude) planes and landing (lower altitude) planes. I did not see navigation lights or strobes. It was like a star that was stationary and started moving, literally.
