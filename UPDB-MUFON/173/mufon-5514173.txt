{cak} First noticed the higher one, 2 minutes later the lower appeared, then disappeared. Higher dimmed then disappeared.

I was on my way home from choir practice when at 8:41 PM I noticed to the left of my car a vibrating, light. It was flashing red, white, and yellow-orange. At 8:44 PM I noticed another one slightly lower and diagonally left to the original one I saw. About 2 minutes later, the lower one disappeared at 8:47 PM. Then another two minutes later the original one dimmed out and completely disappeared at 8:48 PM. They appeared very low, right over the treetops and overpass. They did not move up or down, or away. They just hovered then disappeared.
