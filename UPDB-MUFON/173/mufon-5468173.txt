{td} BIG ROUND LIGHT

AT WORK SITTING IN LAWN CHAIR DURING LUNCH ON EAST SIDE PARKING LOT. I WAS LOOKING UP TWORD'S THE N/W AT OUR BUILDING NOTICING HOW LOW THE PATCHY WHITE CLOUDS WERE. I MADE THE COMMENT TO MY HUSBAND THAT IT'S AS IF I COULD CLIMB ON TOP OF BUILDING & TOUCH THEM. AS I TURNED MY HEAD TO THE EAST STILL LOOKING UP I SAW A BIG WHITE CIRCLE OF LIGHT I THOUGHT IT WAS A HELICOPTER LIGHT. BUT THERE WAS NO SOUND COULDN'T SEE ANY OTHER SHAPE EXCEPT THE CIRCLE.I LOOKED AT MY HUSBAND ASKED HIM DID HE SEE THAT, HE SAID YES. I LOOKED BACK UP & IT WAS GONE. I JUMPED OUT OF CHAIR & STARTED LOOKING ALL AROUND UP IN THE SKY. NEVER HEARD OR SAW ANYTHING FLY AWAY. THE LIGHT WASN'T A BRIGHT LIGHT OR SHINED LIKE A FLASHLIGHT BEAM. JUST A GLOW OF A WHITE CIRCLE. 10X10
