{cak} Glowing amber orbs spotted in N Lakeland Fl

I was contacted by my brother on 1/24/15 @ 7:19 P.M ,He told me to go outside & look at some strange lights in the sky.He was only a few miles from my house when I received his call. I quickly grabbed my 20x80 binoculars & looked to the west & spotted 6 to 8 lights ascending from the horizon.Being a amateur astronomer I spend many hours viewing the night sky and familiar with planets & stars and their location. When viewed through my binoculars these objects appeared to be amber to orange in color. As they moved closer they spread apart , no sound was heard & it was a clear cold night! One By one they disappeared as if the lights were turned off. Several months ago I was contacted by a friend that had witnessed the same thing near my location!
