{rjl} Bright White/Orange Spinning Object

At approximately 20:45 on 3/22/2020, my wife noticed a large, incredibly bright light through the living room window. We live in a very rural area with little to no neighborhood lighting and little traffic making clear nights like 3/22 very clear and ideal for star gazing. Through binoculars, the object appeared round, bright, with extremely bright outer white lights. The middle of the object had a translucent square. Through the naked eye, the object was noticeably moving at times both vertically and horizontally. It was difficult to discern the size or distance of the object, however small aircraft were seen nearby the object and were much smaller than the UFO. We live about 45 miles from Denver International Airport and the object was seen in the general direction of the airport, however the object was much larger and brighter than any stars or aircraft in the area.. Pictures taken with my phone showed the object spinning rapidly a translucent haze on the edges - looked very much like energy protruding from the edges of the object. Video on phone shows the object moving in rapid directions very quickly. At approximately 22:45 on 3/22/20, the object went from white to orange and began descending toward the horizon (last photo attached was the last one taken as it disappeared to the horizon). File 082 is interesting toward the end as orange round flashes are seen around the object.
