Nearly the exact same phenomenon as we witnessed July 2nd (case #116853).

DATE: 4 July 2021; 1015pm. The objects we witnessed were NOT part of a 4th of July celebration!  This phenomenon is similar to what we witnessed on July 2nd (case #116853), but tonight, the objects appeared in one group of four and three groups of three, a few miles from the previous sighting. 

Unidentified low-flying, silent, circular-shaped, fire-emitting objects (it appeared that fire was being emitted from the top and the "wings") entered from the south, hovered for a moment, made sharp turns to the east, and then very quickly ascended into the sky, disappearing immediately. The objects in each small group moved in coordinated tandem with each other.

It was very, very eerie to see such inexplicable events twice.
