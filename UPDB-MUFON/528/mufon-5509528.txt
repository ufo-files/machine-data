Floating object in Singapore sky

On 10 July 2013 at about 6:53pm, an object was spotted "floating" in the sky of Singapore. It was at a long distance, hence appearing as a tiny "spot" in the sky. It could be seen well with naked eyes as it strongly reflected the sunlight.

I used a 70x (optical zoom) digital video camera (however, only SD resolution) in order to see the shape of the object better. One the attached video, one could see that there was a smaller object "floating" around the bigger one. This smaller object was not visible to the naked eyes nor seen while filming with the video camera.

After around 10 minutes, the objects disappeared among the cloud. It did not re-appear even after waiting for 5 minutes. However, a flying craft was seen at a great attitude, at around the same area of the sky where the object was last seen. At this time, both of my video camera batteries were totally flat and I could not capture the flying craft on video. Even though the flying craft was very small, as seen from the naked eyes, it could be seen as it left a very visible contrail. The flying craft was seen only for only about 10 seconds as it disappear among the cloud.
