I was 10 years old. In Gary IND. I was in bed. Looking out window thought I saw lighting bug. Brother in room said to early for lighting bugs. My window was straight south. By the time my brother got to window this thing was as big as a bus. He started screaming for dad and ran out of the room. As I remember this,we all ended outside watching this thing pass by going north towards lake michigan. It was large. rec. in shape. row of white lights. went slow, no noise. It passed us and as we watched it ,it waS GONE.It seemed to just disappear. Went over the lake. #5 my feelings. I remember being so excited . I saw a UFO. 1968. I still get goose bumbs and for some reason start crying every time I remember this. But then, the next day. No one said anything . I'm really trying to remember and to this day I cant remember any of us talking about this.Like it never happened. I know it did. I think now it went in THE LAKE? I now live in Vegas
