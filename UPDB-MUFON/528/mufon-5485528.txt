{dr}3 Balls of light, changing directions and speed.

Before this event occurred, me and my mother were talking about a previous UFO incident that occurred last August, that I failed to report but I will gladly explain it to the field agent assigned to this case, while on the front porch. My mother and sister also saw this event. We were all excited about this event.

- 1st object - 
I saw a bright light in the Southern sky at first thinking it was the star Sirius but quickly noticed that it was getting closer at great speed. The object was seen roughly 15 miles away moving South on a Northern trajectory, once it got to me (3 seconds later - 18,000 mph) the object turned 90 degrees to the North, speed dropped dramatically moving slowly until it turned 90 degrees back to the South ascending and out of sight completely within 10 seconds.

- 2nd object -
Also seen coming from the South on a Northern trajectory, it came in just as fast as the previous object except this object came to a complete stop and hovered where the 1st object made its first 90 degree angle and stayed there roughly 5-10 seconds before ascending Southwest, out of sight completely in 7 seconds.

- 3rd object -
Same path, speed and duration as the first object.

- Strange additional incident - 
There were 6 commercial aircraft in the area during the entire event. All commercial aircraft were diverted from their original flight path and once cleared of the area they went back to the original path.
