Faint bluish-white stationary light blinking at 3 second intervals, began moving left after 7 minutes then vanished.

Was watching a movie in my living room, boring movie so was half-watching planes fly by out the window. A few went by over an hour. I noticed one faint bluish-white dot completely stationary. I watched it for a while thinking that it was a plane flying away from me, but it didn't change in place or brightness for about 5 minutes. I used a plane tracker on my laptop and there weren't any planes anywhere nearby. Intrigued, I sat at the window watching it. It blinked on/off at about an interval of 3 seconds. There was a sort of very faint reddish tint right after the blink occurred. It was about the size of a large star but oddly much fainter. After about 7 minutes, it starting moving straight left/west at a good pace for about 6 seconds and then suddenly disappeared. Definitely not a star, and it seemed too far/faint/large to be a drone. Very odd.
