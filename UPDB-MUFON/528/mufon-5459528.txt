{tjd} 4 white orbs moving back and forth - 3 in a triangular pattern

I was driving south on Quincy Rd in south Aurora heading towards Parker Rd. I was between Smokey Hill and Parker Rds. I noticed 4 bright white orbs at an approximate 40 degree angle in fornt of me through my windshield. Three were in a triangular pattern and one was below the lowest orb. They were initially hovering and then the orbs in the triangular pattern started to rapidly exchange positions moving back and forth in a vertical line. The orb at the bottom was moving back and forth vertically below the forrmation. As i was approaching my turn to head west on Parker Rd, two orbs appeared to merge together or at least fly one in front of the other. Once I turned on Parker Rd I quckly looked back but could not see the objects any longer. Duration was approx 15 seconds.
