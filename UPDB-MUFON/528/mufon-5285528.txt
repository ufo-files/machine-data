Almost like a shooting star.

I’ve seen a shooting star before, it had a bit of a trail, was slower, and was much smaller, I could barely see that in the sky. However on Wednesday night around midnight/1am, I was smoking a cigarette outside with a friend. The night was beautifully clear and I was gazing up the stars. Out of nowhere a white circular object appeared (like a star but bigger, seemed closer). It’s like it popped out of some invisible tunnel right above me. It continued north for about a second or two before completely disappearing into what I would call an ‘invisible tunnel’. It looked like it entered and exited something, and I just happened to see the in-between, right above my house. It ‘popped out’ ans darted north into the ‘tunnel’ faster than I’ve ever seen something move- including the shooting star I saw years ago. I said to my friend, ‘oh my god did you see that!! I think it was a shooting star!!’ but he wasn’t watching and it happened in a split second.
