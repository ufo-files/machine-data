I was sitting in my room at approximately 10:48 PM.,when i heard my dad screaming for me to come outside. I ran out to our backyard , and to my amazement when i look up at the sky there were up to 15 balls of light of various sizes and colors blue,green,and white balls of light, seemed to be at various altitudes.Across the sky which seemed to spread out for unknown amount of miles.Upon first seeing these lights i was in complete shock.There were no stars out to be seen just these objects.honestly at first i got nervous ,due to the amount of lights in the sky.As i stated they were all balls of light it seemed to me like the blue and green lights were gathering up all the white lights ,almost like a herd.During that time witnessed other blue and white lights ,coming from numerous directions in which i can only describe as lagging behind the other lights .But they were making up their distances ,by reaching incredible speed. I noticed only 2 balls of light .one was blue the other white make 90 degree direction changes.they were all heading NW, towards the sea.As i stated before when first witnessed , i was in shock .But it almost looked something from the movie close encounters ,because of the amount of lights in the sky and the different directions they were coming from.Once we lost sight of the lights heading NW .I stood outside looking up at the sky to 1AM, And i did not notice one light or star in the night sky.I was so caught up in the moment that i didn't get my phone to record the event ,which i am seriously regretting now.
