A hovering purplish bluish light

I was driving home from work at 2am going eastbound on the 10 freeway going up a mountain in the city of Yucaipa. It’s dark in this specific area of the freeway because of the mountain. I noticed a hovering bluish purplish vibrant light with a subtle orangish yellow color in the center. It was a tone of bluish purple that I never seen before and it immediately coughs my attention. It was as if it hypnotized me in a way that I just found a beautiful color and the way it just floated effortlessly. I felt calmed and so relaxed looking at it then snapped back into the fact I was driving on the freeway and I immediately grabbed my iPhone to take a photo. By the time I opened my camera I was almost under the object and took the photo. I’m still upset at myself for not taking a video of it instead of the photo. Strange how the photo does not show the orangish tint of color in the center of the object. It was more oval spherically shaped but the photo shows a steak in it. If you zoom into the photo you can see the glowing around it and it doesn’t seem to have a hard surface structure to it. With that said, I’m assuming it was an orb. If anyone else seen this in Yucaipa California please reply or report. I’d like to debunk this if possible. If not, I’ll take it as a UFO I guess...
