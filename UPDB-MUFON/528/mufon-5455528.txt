{tjd} Several objects moving in a straight line from SW to NE

I went outside to have a smoke at aproximately 5:50 am. I do so every day and look the stars and cloud movements. The sky was just begginning to brighten in the east .For a second or so I felt a bit disorientated because it looked like the stars were moving and not the clouds. I then noticed 3 or 4 objects moving overhead in a straight line. I then noted several other objects to the SW in a very straight line. The objects appeared to be very high and the size of the other stars in the sky. I ran inside to get my wife who came outside and also witnessed these objects. At least 10 of these object moved, in groups of 3 or 4 passed overhead and disappeared to the NE. My wife says she saw a few of them move up and down slightly but I did not notice them.
