{nd} Hovering circular object

I was driving home on route 83 in East longmeadow. I drive this route all the time. It was about 10:20pm and super dark and all of a sudden I saw this blinking red light in the corner of my eye. I look up and I see this thing with 2 red lights hovering over high in the sky over some tree and a house. I thought that it was weird that a helicopter would be out at this time of night, but if it was that close to me, I knew I should have been hearing a really loud chopping sound. So I turned down my music and heard nothing! At this point, since I was the only one on the road, I slowed down and opened my window to see if I could hear any noise at all. All I heard was a faint whirling type sound. I watched it for a couple minutes as I was passing by and it literally just hovered there. It was hovering in kind of a circle motion. While I was staring at it, I could see that it was circular in shape and it looked like it had 2 blinking red lights on both "ends", but it also looked like there was a dull white interior light on in the "vehicle" itself. I am upset that it was so late and I needed to head home, but I watched it for as long as I could until I drove out of sight. When it left my sight, it was still hovering in the same area. I tried to get a picture, but it was so dark that you couldn't see anything.
