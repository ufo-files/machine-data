{kt}ANGULAR SHAPE METALLIC NO WINDOWS DEEP HUMMING

BUDDY AND ME STOPPED ON OLD MACON ROAD, AND BUDDY FIRST NOTICED IT AS IT BEEN BEHIND TREES OVER HEAVY POWER LINES, AND IT MADE TURN TOWARD US HEADED WEST AFTER IT GOT TO JUNCTION OF POWER POLES, THEN WE BOTH WATCHED IT GO OVER, MAKING DEEP HUMMING SOUND, NO WINDOWS, METALLIC COLOR, MISC. COLOR LIGHTS LIKE HEADLIGHTS ON VEHICLE, BUR NO BEAM EMITTED.  VERY ULTRA MODERN AS IT WAS JULY OR AUGUST OF 1968, AND I HAVE NOT SEEN ANYTHING THIS MODERN TO THIS DATE EXCEPT IN MOVIES. NO PROPELLOR. NO THRUST, SO HOW IT STAYED UP NO IDEA. MORE MODERN THAN SPACE SHUTTLE OE STEALTH PLANES THAT I HAVE SEEN. SIMILAR LOOK OF ROBOTS ON MOON OR MARS BUT MUCH BIGGER, AND WE CUT HAVE EASILY HIT IT WITH A MID SIZE ROCK REAL HARD. ABOUT 15-20FT WIDE, 10-12FT HT., 30 FT LENGTH. NO SIGNS OF ANY ON BOARD, NO PORTHOLES TO SEE FROM. NO ABDUCTION, BUT SURELY THEY KNEW WE WERE ONLY 20 FT. ON ROAD WATCHING THEM GO OVER POWER LINES AT A SPEED OF ONLY 5 OR SO MILE PER HOUR.   I HAVE TALKED WITH FOLKS THAT SUGGESTED POSSIBLY RECHARGING SOME SORT OF POWER SYSTEM, AS THEY WERE ONLY ABOUT 5 FT ABOVE POWER LINES. WE DID NOT FOLLOW IT FOR SOME REASON AS IT HEADED DOWN ROAD IN STEADY DIRECTION. WE JUST WATCHED IT IN AMAZEMENT AS IT WEST DOWN THE POWER LINES.   HERE IT IS ALMOST 46 YEARS LATER THIS SUMMER OF 2014, AND I STILL THINK ABOUT IT MOST EVERY DAY.. NEVER TO THIS DATE I HAVE NOT SEEN ANYTHING IN REAL LIFE THAT MODERN, AS IT MAKES WHAT I HAVE SEEN THEN SEEM RATHER ANTIQUATED, AND THAT WAS 1968. OBVIOUSLY VERY ADVANCED BEINGS, NO FEAR TO US, BUT WILL PROBABLY NEVER KNOW WHO OR WHERE THEY WERE FROM. MORE MODERN THAN ANY THING I HAVE EVER SEEN TILL NOW. THIS IS THE TRUTH. ADVANCED BEINGS AND MACHINE ALSO.  STILL AMAZED. TRULY  TOTALLY ADVANCE MACHINE....WHO DRIVING?  NO IDEA, AS NO WINDOWS TO SEE INTO MACHINE. I REALLY DONT THINK THERE IS A MACINE THAT MODERN THIS DAY IN 2014 THAT I HAVE SEEN ON NEWS.
