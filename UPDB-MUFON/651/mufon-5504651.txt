{kt}Very bright approx 12 yellow lights with one red formation

Was preparing for bed closing my window when I saw what looked like approx. 11 or 12 very bright lights with one red light in the center sort of in a triangle formation. No noise, flying low, barely making it over an nearby apartment building. Moving very quickly, when got to the building they all tilted together to go over building. I was very upset and could not sleep and am still very shaken over this. Was not a believer. Not sure what I saw but know 100% not an commercial aircraft. I could no longer see when it cleared a building. Was amazed that something so low had no sound coming from it.
