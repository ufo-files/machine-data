I was in my room playing XBOX One when an extremely bright light that flashed from the UFO caught my attention. I thought the object was a military aircraft or a normal jet. It then created a thick white light as the three lights merged at the front. I was felt confused at first to what was going on. When I seen the UFO move i felt threatened, however I also had another strange feeling. I felt that whatever was in the craft had seen me, or something else and was also scared, trying to make a swift escape.This could explain the various quick movements it was seen making. After seeing the craft i felt weird, like it hadn't really happened. I also felt a connection with the craft and felt like it was not evil. I lost visual contact when it managed to do 5 or 6 quick movements, seemingly defying what direction it had intended to go in, before hovering and vanishing.
