{jpg} Similar to an Iridium flare but remained stationary

I am familiar with Iridium flares and have watched many of them. Whatever this is, it is similar to Iridium flares but shows no apparent motion. My wife and I have been seeing these for several months, and they usually appear very near the same location. Like Iridium flares, the light becomes visible and grows in intensity until it is very bright and then fades out. The one on 10/0/2019 exhibited the usual pattern and then reappeared in the same location but did not reach the same brightness as it has a few seconds earlier. The light does not show any apparent motion during its appearance nor does my satellite tracking software show any visible satellites in the area during the sightings.
