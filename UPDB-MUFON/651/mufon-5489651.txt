{dr}Teardrop, hovering back and forth for about 10 mins. Then zig zaged and flew off at an amazing speed. Was 100's of witnesses

I was around 8 years old. My mother was taking me to get allergy shots. We were coming from Copiague NY heading toward West Babylon. We took Oak Street which I think turns into West Hoffman then Rail road ave. That road follows the long Island railroad. We were coming to a stop light that was red. We were about the 6th or 7th car waiting. To my right was a big flower nursery. When we were waiting I seen it first. The UFO looked like a teardrop shape, was chrome looking and hovering back and forth for around 10 mins or more. I told my mother look its a UFO! She said oh my Gosh! The crazy thing was, was that nobody honked their horn or drove away. We are in NY, come one now we honk horns for any little thing and no one honked there horn or drove away. Everyone was in Aww looking at it. It was maybe around 500 feet away and right above the tree line. Pretty much right above the rail road track. Then after awhile it zig zagged extremely fast . Way faster then any of our jets could fly. I also have many years of military experience and know we don't have anything like that is our military. Its hard to remember but I thought it might have changed shapes a little to but I'm not 100% sure. It was almost 30 years ago. Me and my mother both remember it. Me and my brother also seen other UFO's at our old house but it was out in space. It looked like stars were chasing each our doing sharp angles. We both still remember that. I don't know if this has anything to do with it but my brother said he had on several occasions has woke up floating in the air in our room and he was paralyzed and tried to scream out to me but he couldn't. We always thought he was dreaming or it was some demonic or ghost thing.
