{rjl} Domed disc 1000 ft dia X 50ft depth at apex of dome. Over Atlantic, 2 ea.

I was aboard a military C-12 enroute from NAS JAX to NAVSTA Roosy RDS @ 13hrs 3 Jan 1983. I was a Civilian employee of COMNAVAIRLANT. First craft was @ about 1000ft headed NW, speed about 300knots.White Domed disc no appendages nor any fin or wings. Second craft appeared @ 1310hrs headed NE.speed and discretion same as the first fraft. We were in the area of Andros Isl.
