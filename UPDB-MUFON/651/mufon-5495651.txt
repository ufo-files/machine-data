{dr}Explosion then a Giant Starlike object appeared and ascending

I was walking back from taking the garbage out and saw what looked like a shooting star. The shooting star/object was moving west away from Washington DC, as it was moving west it exploded into two pieces. It looked like a blue flame that split into two. Half of it went West and the other half moved East and disintegrated. At that moment I thought it was pretty cool that I saw a shooting star but then thought I never seen a shooting star explode like that. I then proceeded to look up at the sky and about a minute after I saw the explosion, I looked up and saw what looked like a very big star. I would say it was comparable to the size of the North Star. The Star got bigger and then started to ascend upward and disappeared. Now I am an educated man and have seen dozens of shooting stars but never saw a shooting star explode and then see object in the sky move closer to Earth and then ascend upward and out of our atmosphere. Pretty crazy but pretty exciting as well.
