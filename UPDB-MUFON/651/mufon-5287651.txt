Four vertical lights slowly descending in perfect straight line to water, then disappeared

Happened right when dusk becomes dark, but water/horizon line still visible. Four descending vertical lights, evenly spaced, top three lights white, bottom light red. A few minutes before sighting, all electricity was lost. Observed at an oceanfront villa, Kapalua, Maui, looking up and out to the right of Molokai and into open ocean. Silently descending from stratosphere toward horizon where sky meets water, it took about 3 minutes from initial viewing high in sky for bottom red light to reach water/horizon. Then in continued vertical unison moved to the left, remaining in this formation and disappeared in front of island of Molokai in the Pailolo Channel (deepest depth approx 850 feet). Observed by two people.
