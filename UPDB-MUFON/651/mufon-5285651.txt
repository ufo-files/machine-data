I was admitted to hospital for 3 days for tonsils removal...
I was super lonely each night and i had a few to the South sky big windows to see out and wished that someone would come and visit me i was 12 at the time......that first night i slept barely and poorly looking out the big windows....i woke the next day.....had breakfast and they did some routine tests then i got to go to the big sitting room around 11 am to build paper and cotton ball teddy bears as a project for 1 hour.....i was at the table starting and by myself when two visitors showed up (they looked like my Aunt and Cousin exact but their eyes were black and dark) the head nurse said i had visitors and she brought them to the room showing them where i was..they smiled and did not say a real word the whole time....i did not care though as i was just glad to see anyone at this time...my cousin helped me out mostly handing me things as my Aunt watched i was picking up voices in my head like they where talking to me but no words said.....it was weird but who cared as they helped me and visited i was so happy....i finished my project and the head nurse showed up and said time for them to go...they got up and were off as she led them off.....i had lunch right after and then supper and turns out my mom and dad showed up at that time finally.....i told her about the visit and she thought it odd and would look into it......i had my surgery the next day and was released soon after to go home......that night.  I talked with my mom and asked her if she talked to them and she said yes she did but it could not of been them who came as mu Aunt did not drive and she was far north half way across the city from the hospital and would not take a bus that far......my cousin was in school that day she was with me so no way she could of been visiting period......to this day i never forgot this event and it still spooks me as to who it was who visited and it made sense if they were entities as their features (eyes) and no words spoken the whole time was not right either.........i got a chance to question my Aunt my self even later and she said was not her and that was that.......I had also periods of time missing while i visited i cannot account for period.......
