Formation of long cigar shaped crafts flying over my apartment building. Triangle Formation.

I was cooking dinner that night and as I waited for the water to boil, I looked out the window in my living room area of my house and I saw one bright light, so I went over to the window and looked out and saw that light gradually get closer. Then, I looked off to the left of it and there were two more lights but the one in the middle of the 3 I could barely see the body. It was long and cigar shaped with a single light in the front. Almost as if it was leading the group. At first, I thought it was just a military formation, but I heard no sounds coming from the crafts and I don't recall any military or commercial aircraft that are shaped like a cigar. They moved in a straight line but as they got closer to my apartment building, they slowed to a stop above it, but I lost sight of it after it positioned itself above my apartment building. So, I rushed outside of my apartment to look out and I saw nothing. Not even in the air in all direction I looked. After the incident I just felt amazed that I saw this because it was my first UFO encounter in my life.
