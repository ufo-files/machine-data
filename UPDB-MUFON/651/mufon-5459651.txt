{jpg} Black Triangle, Underlit

A group of us earl teens played hide & seek in a small forest behind a friend's house on Park Drive in Westfield, Mass.
As I was hiding I became lit-up by a dark, triangular craft passing by overhead. It appeared to have a spotlinght on the nose and other lights througout.
It made but a hum as it passed.
After the incident at least one other person discussed with me the oddness of this plane (for we did not call it a UFO).
The "plane" was low flying, black and triangular shape.
In the nineties I saw the FTL airshow and saw the B2 and remarked out it looked like what I saw as a kid.
It was only recently that I learned this was an event viewed by many.
I have had other sighting of things that I shall report on, but none so clearly a craft nor with such interaction as the light shined upon me.
