{tjd} Very bright, glowing planet-like stationary object appeared for 1 min unmoving, dimmed in less than a second to a similar brightness of the stars, slowly dissolved

At 6:10 am I went outside to let my dogs out. I noticed something shining brightly in my peripheral vision, turned to look, and in the SE corner of the sky was a huge, incredibly bright, planetary-like object- square-ish shaped with rounded edges. It was far bigger and brighter than anything else in the sky and seemed to glow from within.
I stood and stared as I've never seen anything like it. About to go inside to grab my phone, within less than a second- a blink of an eye- the object went from a brilliant light to dim lighting that was similar to the glow from the stars/planets in the early morning sky. It stayed at this dimmer light for about a minute, and then seemed to slowly dissolve behind something. Beyond the dissolving, it didn't move at all.
