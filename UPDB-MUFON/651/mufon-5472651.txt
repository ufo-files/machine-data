{dr}Chopper-shaped object looking as if on fire, in storm clouds, multi-directional path

On August 10th I was on my porch. I screamed out to my wife because I thought a storm was coming, to the north of me and Grand Rapids. I am in Wyoming. Coming from a different cloud bank (where there was no lightning), an object/vehicle came out of the cloud in a straight line, looking as if it was on fire. At first I thought it was a helicopter on fire. It was moving erratically and still on fire; it kept changing shape. I have been around the world in Japan and all over, and since out of the military, I have been all over the U.S., and I have never seen anything like this in my life. This was real. It kept changing and the fire, it was like a ball with a globe over it and it started blinking lights and it went right into the storm. I've always been interested in UFO sightings fortunately, but I've never ever seen, I thought it was made up. If somebody could have seen what I've been through.
