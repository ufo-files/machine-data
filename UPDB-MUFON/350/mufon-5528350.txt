I went outside for a smoke and as I stepped out my back door I saw over the roof of the house behind mine what I thought at 1st was a plane I

I used the branches to try and judge movement and I couldn't see any and I now noticed it looked a bit odd there were two normal white lights 
that looked the same as on most commercial jets but they were in the middle very close together and there were two outer lights but all four were very close together and at the outer lights it seemed to curve some and there was no bright white blinking off of any of them which you normally see on planes and there was no small red blue or green blinking light that I could see anywhere, I was starting to panic at this point because it still wasn't moving and now I can't remember what color the two outer lights were. My brother and son never saw it, my mother did and she said it looked wierd not like a plane at all but she thinks it may have been a helicopter, but she says it definitely was not moving at all for a few minutes.I believe in UFO's, I always have as far back as I can remember and I have seen one in broad daylight back in about 1997, but I'm not crazy and I don't think every flashing light in the sky is a UFO, I'm a very rational, objective person. I live in Boston and I'm very accustomed to airplanes and helicopters day and night, I'm well aware how they can look odd sometimes especially at night and how they can look like they aren't moving. This was positvely not moving, and I'm about 98% sure it was a saucer shaped UFO. I do want to add though, air traffic seemed quite normal so I'm guessing there was nothing odd picked up on radar anywhere.
