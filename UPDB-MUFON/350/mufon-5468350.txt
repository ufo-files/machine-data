FILLED OUT AND FILED WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE BY L. FLECHTNER 18809 AT MUFON HQ. ON MAY 24TH, 2018
-------------------------------------------------------------------
I WAS DONE FOR THE DAY AND WAS GOING IN THE HOUSE FROM THE BACK PORCH WHEN
I SAW THIS REDDISH CLOAKED IMAGE LIKE A BARREL TRAVELING FAST ACROSS THE SKY. THEN IT JUST STOPPED. IT SEEMED TO SHUDDER AND VIOLENTLY SHAKE AS IT HOVERED. I FELT OR SENSED IT HAD NOTICED ME. THEN A LARGE WHITE PASSENGER AIRLINER FLEW BY ON ITS WAY TO LAND AT THE TUCSON INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT AND THE REDDISH CLOAKED BARREL FOLLOWED IT GOING THE SAME ALTITUDE AND SPEED. THE UFO PASSED THE AIRLINER BECAUSE I SAW IT GO PAST THE LANDING LIGHTS AND THE UFO WAS IN FRONT OF THE PLANE. THEN THE UFO STOPPED AND AGAIN STARTED TO VIOLENTLY SHUDDER AND SHAKE AS IT HOVERED.
I COULD SEE THE WINDOWS ON THE AIRPLANE SO I GOT THAT THE SIZE OF THE UFO WAS HALF THE SIZE OF THE AIRPLANE. THE UFO WAS DARK REDDISH AND HAD A CLOAKED LOOK TO IT, FUZZY AROUND THE EDGES, ALMOST TRANSPARENT. IT WAS ABOUT 1/2 MILE AWAY FROM ME AND ABOUT 2,500 HIGH IN ELEVATION. THE NIGHT SKY WAS PITCH BLACK.
THE AREA OF THE SIGHTING IS KNOWN AS THE SAN XAVIER SECTION.
