{jpg} A light in the sky that moved in a way we had never seen befor.

My brother and I had left the house that night with out anyone knowing. We were on are way back home at about 3:00am when we saw it, a light in the sky that moved so fast you could not even see it happen. It would then be in another part of the sky.We both started to run as it got closer to us. It was then a light blue light hit us, and lifted us both off the ground (about 5 feet in the air).We were both still trying to run, but we were just stuck in the spot.It was then that the light just went out, and we fell to the ground.We both looked at one another, and said to one another we would never talk about what had happen.
