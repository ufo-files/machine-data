Sightings in Antarctica

Back in the mid 901s I was stationed with a naval squadron at mcmurdo station Antarctica. On a routine flight to South Pole station one of the aircrew brought our attention to the port side windows where several objects were observed keeping pace with our LC-130. They seemed to follow our every move for about 5 minutes or so and as quickly as they appeared they were gone. The photographers mate on the flight managed to snap numerous photos before the disappeared. Upon arrival back to mcmurdo station we were met by two individuals and taken by van to the main building. All film was confiscated and all of us on the flight were told to speak of nothing and that we saw nothing. This is just one of many encounters I experienced while down on the ice.
