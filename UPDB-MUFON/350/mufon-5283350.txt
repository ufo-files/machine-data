UFO observed after reviewing video of two NJ State Police Helicopters Flying

While at work, I observed two NJ State Police helicopters circling the area multiple times, I decided to take some video to show my manager who is a retired NJ State Trooper. Afterward I asked my manager about the helicopters, and he said they were circling because they were waiting for a funeral to start. I wasn't until 12/29/2021, I reviewed the video I shot and saw the blue light circling one of the helicopters. The UFO "Blue Orb Light", came from behind one of the helicopters, stopped turned around and circled one of the helicopters then went out of view. I didn't notice it when I was actually filming the incident, I observed it only after I reviewed the video.
