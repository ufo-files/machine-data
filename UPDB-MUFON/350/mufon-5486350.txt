{rjl} lanterns in sky floating in a south to north path ; looked like the big dipper in orange moving across sky. I couldn't discern how high they were; no noise

My daughter came to pick up our grandson at approximately 8 pm, Saturday,December 26,2015. She had gone out the door and came back to the door and said for me to come out and look in the sky. Orange- yellow orbs were floating from the south to the north. They seemed as high as an airplane would be.I tried to make sense of what I saw. The pattern in the sky was like a constellation that was moving. The spacing between the objects remained the same. The orbs did not pulsate or flicker. The whole event took less than 5 minutes. My daughter attempted to take a photo but had no room on her cell phone. It was a very surreal event.I cannot understand what I saw!
