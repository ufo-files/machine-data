Orange fireballs with dark black orbs

I noticed the orange lights appearing in a group to the south west of my property. Among the lights which moved quickly were black orb like shapes that appeared to drift balloon like then change direction ascending and descending seeming to interact with one another meanwhile the bright lights were appearing until there were at least 7 of them. The black shapes were very odd and looked so black It was if they were just holes. they were oblong orbs and I have never seen or heard of anything like the, As soon as I could grab my camera they started to move S/W more rapidly. I took 3 pictures with my Samsung phone and started the slow video. The photos were not on the camera and only a small clip of the video that shows a light moving left to right make a 90 degree turn and shoot straight up. I have seen the orange things once before a few weeks ago. My neighbor was with me at the time of that sighting. The black orbs were very very dark and featureless. With the sun still setting they looked like the complete absence of light. So dark it's difficult to explain. I felt like if I had seen a black hole in space this is what it would look like. Nothingness.
