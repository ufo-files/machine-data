northeast coast bright light west to east

while out walking the dogs down the see font (seaton carew), i seen a large bright light moving , i thought at first it was venus , but this one was moving from west to east , i stopped next to a lamp post and watched this object come straight over head, still this bright light , then thought it must be the landing light of an aircraft or helicopter spot light ... but no noise, this light never got dull , as watched it move over my head , i could hear the lamp post start to shake, as though there was wind , but i couldn't feel any , as it moved slowly away to the east i could see a second light a lot higher up and smaller which moved in the same direction , gutted i never had my mobile and guess no one else around , this all happened in a 5 minute period
