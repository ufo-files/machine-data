{cak} Rotating lighted disc shaped object.

1. Was just waking up, went to the kitchen to make a cup of coffee. 2.It was a brightly lighted object in the sky. 3. At first I thought it was a helicopter and soon realized it was not. I observed the object thru rather powerful bincoculars. It had about 6 very bright white lights and approx. the same number of dimmer lights. It hovered for several minutes and rotated and seemed to slightly change shape at times. Also for a short while some of the lights changed color from white to yellow.The object rotated what appeared to be it's nose toward an aircraft that was approaching it. 5. I felt it was certainly not an airplane nor a helicopter as it did not have anti collision light or traditional red and blue lights. I was a fighter plane crew chief in the USAF in the 1980's, so I have a familiarity with aircraft. My feelings were that I had seen this type of craft on two other occasions, the last one I reported to Mufon. To be honest it looked beautiful, as if it were putting on a show .6. The craft gradually gained altitude until I could no longer see it even with binoculars.
