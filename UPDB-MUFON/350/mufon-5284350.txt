Trapezoid Shaped Craft that was translucent, milky white with red lights

I was in my back yard looking at the stars when I saw the craft to the east flying south and slightly west. I saw it for about 5-10 seconds and it was flying in a straight line at a steady speed - not super fast, but not very slow either. It was roughly the shape of a trapezoid with more rounded edges and was a translucent milky white color that had red lights in the middle part of the trapezoid. It resembled the way some jelly fish look as far as the translucent part goes and apparent texture. I am certain it wasn't a plane, helicopter or any other known objects. I've been an avid star gazer and have been into astronomy for 30 years and have seen hundreds if not thousands of shooting stars, satellites, planes etc and this was 100% some kind of craft that was none of those. I tried to film it on my phone, but by the time I pulled out my phone and tried to it was already too far away to capture. I grabbed my camera after this happened and took a picture of the sky where I had just seen it and did my best to draw what I saw in photoshop (I'm not an artist so it's not great). Was an amazing sighting!
