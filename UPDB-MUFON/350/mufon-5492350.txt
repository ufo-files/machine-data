{rjl} Observed an unusual glowing reddish orange light at night coming from the west and disappearing when over head.

I came out of the front door of my house around 9pm. I observed two small airplanes in the night sky, one to the north and one to the south. The night sky was cloudless. Then almost immediately I observed a bright,glowing object/light in front of me coming from the west. It seemed rectangular in shape and was reddish orange like a fireplace ember. My sense was that it was higher than a local plane but not near as high as the San Francisco jetliners that pass overhead. Within 2 seconds, I saw a small reddish orange light or fragment come off from the object/light and fall earthward. The object/light continued toward my direction, heading eastward, at a steady speed. As it came toward me my sense was that it was now much higher in the sky than when I first saw it. When it was directly overhead, it just vanished. This observation lasted about 12 seconds. There was no noise associated with this event.
