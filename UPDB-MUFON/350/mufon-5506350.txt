Bright orange light hovering over the ocean.

Its was a clear night in Feb 2012. My girlfriend and I were at a beach party around 9-11PM we went for a walk away from the party and as we got further and further away from the crowd I noticed this bright orange light hovering in the sky over the ocean, we both stopped and we saw the light move in ways that I couldnt compare to any aircraft I have seen in my 18 years of living. It was moving side to side and up and down but not as linear . At first I was thinking of things it could of been, like a Street Lamp or plane but it was just a single orange light moving over the ocean dancing in mid air. I wasnt sure what to believe. After staring at it for about a minute or two trying to figure out what it was we started to feel uneasy about what it could be. We turned around and started to rush back. To make sure that I had seen it I looked behind us to try and see it again and it was gone. 

I asked my girlfriend yesterday 16/11/2013 if she remembers seeing it and she can recall everything that I saw even in more detail. Im just glad I wasnt the only one who saw this.
