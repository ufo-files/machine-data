{dr}craft nose pointed east but traveledeast following our car and totally silent flying to our left just above the pine trees of even height

My teenage son and I were driving toward our home in Powhatan, coming from Midlothian. It was around 9 pm when we noticed a delta-shaped aircraft flying along side of us, although it was to our left above the treetops. It looked as if it were facing east but was travelling backwards with us as we drove west. As I remember it had at least 3 red lights: one toward the nose and one near the top of each "wing." It made not a sound. It seemed as it was following us; I got spooked and then my son said, "Let's get out of here!", and I floored the accelerator without looking back.

During the late 90's there were alot of UFO sightings along Huguenot Trail and around Judes Ferry Road, next to my house. There were orange balls that separated, came together, then sped into invisibility (this I saw at least 4 times). Also very high in the sky there were objects that looked like distant planets or stars that would move suddenly! 

My son had night terrors of craft landing on our property during those years, but I never took them as real.

The UFO activity in that area seemed to slow down and even stop in the early 2000's. I am reporting this now because I just heard about MUFON taking reports, and I have never forgotten these odd sightings.
