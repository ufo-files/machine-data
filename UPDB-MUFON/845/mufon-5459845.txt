We reported this before. It wasnt til i saw john kellys drawing of the object that i realized it was the same craft. Black, moving at 30 mph. Both together and heading towards the hogback. Then, after thirty minutes one seperated and headed towards Colorado springs while the other continued west. These flat, 70ft to 100ft discs moved at super slow speeds, no sound, and rolled in the air as if they werent effected by gravity, wind or perpulsion. They didnt move up or down but continued at the sam altitude and slow speed. No sound of blades cutting the air or engines. Very strange and exciting at the same time. We tried to take pictures but two devices died that were full power. We did get one pic but phone didnt have the quality to make them worth anything. They spun in random directions like something floating slowly in space... it was eerie how they tumbled. I have a basic understanding of science and their movement, speed and abilitys matched nothing im privy to. They were both flat shaped, about 70 to 100ft in length. Curved all the way till the back where they had three distinct points. When i say Kellys sketch it was identical to what we witnessed. I hope you can find the previous report and picture.
