{cak} Witnessed 2 triangle shaped crafts east of hospital at fairly low altitude

I was pulling into Columbus Regional Hospital's Emergency Department when I witnessed 2 sets of 3 lights in the shape of triangles. They appeared to be at about 500 feet in altitude and moving very slowly above the neighborhood that was Northeast of me and traveling initially in a Southeast direction, then turning Eastward. What first caught my attention was that the right set of lights were positioned vertically. If there was a craft connecting these lights together, it then turned downward to reveal the shape of a triangle. So it appeared that when I initially saw the lights, the craft would've been on its side. Once the craft positioned itself in that way, it changed its course to an Eastward direction. That's when I focused on the second set of lights to the left of the first set. These were very clearly in the shape of a triangle as well. The second craft followed the first, appearing to be turning on it's left side in a left turn. Both sets of lights were the same colors: green, orange and white at each point of the triangles. They were not blinking but solid. Upon first sight, I immediately rolled down the window to get a better look. They moved incredibly slow and I didn't hear any noise coming from their direction and I was pretty close to them. I would've stayed and filmed them or followed them but I was obligated to immediately get into the hospital.

I do call it a craft, though I did not see any craft connecting these lights together. The way in which these lights moved, lead me to believe that they were most definitely connected in some way.
