{jpg} Pulsing color changing lights

First notice a very bright white light traveling from the east. It stopped for a moment and slowly moved to the 10 o'clock position in the sky. I took pictures for about an hour and 15 minutes of this light changing colors. I recently purchased a new camera and used the software to enlarge the pictures and found them to be very interesting. I have 200 pictures that show movement, changing colors and morphing into what I call a "blue hole". The object finally just turned off or disappeared.
