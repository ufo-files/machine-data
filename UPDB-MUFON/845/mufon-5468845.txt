{jpg} ufo hovering over aurora mall

Myself and my now exwife where driving to the aurora mall. We were having a verbal disagreement. We found a parking space and parked we got out of the car. As we walked and continued our verbal disagreement I noticed something strange hovering in the air over the mall. as I pointed to what I saw my wife looked over and she didn't say a word. We watched this ufo hovering over the mall then it hovered toward our direction but stopped. it then sat in a spot in the air hovering. (to me it looked to be almost what looked like a octagone shape it was a matalic silver slash orange or yellow color which might have been the sun reflecting off of the craft). It was not flat like a disc it sat in the air for a few minutes. the look of the craft is what I would compare to a stop sign shape. I was in shock like our we really seeing this right now. The craft then shot up at an angle which left a streak of light behind it. then it stopped on a button. then shot left at an angle which left another streak of light and it was gone. I never heard a sound from it. We then walked toward the mall talking about what we both just experienced. I dont know how it seemed like we were the only 2 people to see this. We went home and looked on the news but there was never a mention of it.
