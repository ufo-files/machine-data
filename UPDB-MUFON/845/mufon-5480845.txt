{cak} lights in sky joined then ascended, hovered and vanished

We walked out of a store and looked up and commented how low the full moon was. Out of the right corner of my eye i see really bright lights. There was a star to their bottom left which seemed completely dull compared to them. I at first thought it was a plane but then realized they were coming closer together while ascending straight up, completely vertical. They got so close that it almost looked like it was one light (because the lights were so bright and they were enough away from each other that i could see that they were definitely in a triangle shape). It hovered for a few seconds then literally disappeared before our eyes. No dimming, no flash...just gone.
