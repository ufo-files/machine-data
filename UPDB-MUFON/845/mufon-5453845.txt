{cak} 10-15 lights flying in a direct straight path equal distance apart as high as the stars

Approximately 8:30 pm on 4/15/2020 my next door neighbor called for us to look at the sky and pointed in the direction to look....I immediately saw a line of bright lights equally spaced (to the eye the distance between lights was about 10-20 feet apart) at an elevation equal to the stars in the sky. The lights were moving in a straight line one after another coming from the west/southwest and moving to the east . I didn't begin counting immediately but i personally visualized at least 10 to 15 of these lights moving across the sky quickly.
