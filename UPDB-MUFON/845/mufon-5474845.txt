{rjl} A solid red light with 3 pulsating white lights next to it. No fuselage

I was looking out of my kitchen window when I observed what I thought was a helicopter. But the light pattern did not match any helicopter I'd ever seen. The object was to high and large to be a drone, and again, the light configuration did not seem to match a drone. The object just hovered for about 2 minutes. It did not wobble, yaw or rotate. A commercial airliner descended out of the cloud cover and the object began to move slowly away from it and then it just shot off at rocket speed.
