UFOs going fasts (filmed in slow motion)

I was working on a rooftop. I thought I was seeing stars from being over heated. I thought I could see things zipping back and forth. Then I decided to shoot a slow motion video, I didn’t realize what I captured till later. I didn’t believe in ufos/aliens before today... 

I captured two crafts in the top right of my video screen going inconceivable speeds darting to the center and then down to the right just above the hilltop. You will see reflections of the craft and shadows in the clouds. Clear flight paths seemed to be toying with each other.
