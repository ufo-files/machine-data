{rjl} Massive bright light above clouds. Stationary for a second, then accelerated and ascended out of sight. Light faded as it ascended.

We witnessed a massive bright light suddenly appear above the clouds in bend Oregon. Object was stationary for a second and was a singular white dot. Object then sped to the west for about 3 seconds and incredible speeds, faster than a satellite. Object began to ascended to the point where you could no see it. This left me baffled as it made no noise, and does not follow patters or similarities to other aircraft. Around 10 minutes later, a helicopter was circling the area before taking off to the east.
