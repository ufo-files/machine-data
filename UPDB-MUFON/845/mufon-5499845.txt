I was driving N on S Meridian when I saw a craft coming from the west below tree level as it was going over the street it was risen up. It got above the trees and then it was gone. On the bottom of the craft it had a black mark on it. The rest of it was silver. The wind was coming out of the south at 15 to 20 mph. The craft didn't have any problem with the wind. It look as big as a plane but no wings or nose. I looked down to see if any other cars was seen what I did and there was none. It was mothers day everyone was at lunch. I didn't know what I was seen but it had my attention till it took off. I try to understand what I saw but it just don't make since to me. As I pass where I seen it take off I looked in the field to make sure it just didn't land in the field. Their is a field in the area it was coming from but I as so dumfounded I did t check it out. It took me this long to get the nerve up to report this.
