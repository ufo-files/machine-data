{nd} Round object flashing with a triangular center

My son and I were walking down Delmar Blvd. toward Skinker Blvd. about 8:45 p.m. on May 20, 2013 in University City a part of St. Louis, Missouri. I suddenly turned towards my left because I sensed something, my left ear felt strange, as I did so, immediately I looked up into the sky and saw an object.

I told my son to look, at first, he thought it was a helicopter.

It was flashing but not like a plane. The colors were reddish gold and kept fading in and out. It was circular but seemed to have a triangular shape in the middle of it. I tried to take a picture of it on my phone but it would not show up. I asked it to come closer and then it disappeared.

I wondered as I looked at it,if it had anything to do with the weather. a tornado watch was in the forecast for our area at the time.
