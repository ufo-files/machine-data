{rjl} Bright light moving fast and changing direction, Seen multiple nights over a month

I first noticed this bright light almost exactly a month ago from the time of this writing. I was on the back patio of my house smoking a cigarette a little after 8 pm on January 24th 2017, when I saw a bright light moving around in the sky. I first noticed it because it was much brighter than any other light in the sky. Initially I wrote it off as a drone but thought it was a little weird to be flying a drone at night. But the more I watched it, I became convinced that what I was watching couldn't possibly be a drone. It moves fast, quickly changes direction at almost perfect angles, and comes to a halt without slowing down. It usually starts as an incredibly bright white light, brighter than any other star in the sky. I've seen it go from bright white to a blue white, green, and almost orange red and it seems to change colors randomly.  I've observed this now over 10 times in the past month. It's usually in roughly the same area. I've watched it fly around for well over an hour on multiple occasions. The 21st and the 22 of February I saw two of these lights in different areas of the sky. I've never had any real reactions to the object, other than frustration of not being able to figure out what it is. The couple of times that I've stood outside long enough to watch it disappear, it eventually gets further and further away until it drops off the horizon. My wife, mother in law, father in law, and brother in law have all also witnessed it.
