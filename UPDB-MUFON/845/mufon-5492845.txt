{dr}5 yr old sleeping in front yard with neighbor

in Blanchard ok 1963 sleeping in front yard with neighbor boy who lived across the street.just 2 boys laying in front yard looked up seen large object with red green yellow flashing lights with no sound just moving very slowly right above water tower in town ,watched it just move slowly across the sky till it was out of sight. it was 55 yrs ago can still show you the yard where I was sleeping ,still remember it like it was yesterday.after it was out of sight he got up went home I went into the house.have maybe told 5 people in my life about it.i know it was real have had the same memory my whole life.
