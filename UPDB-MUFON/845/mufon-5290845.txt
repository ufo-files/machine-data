Black spiky hovering objects.

I was looking out of my living room window at what I thought may be birds flying about but they were higher in the sky than usual and hovered in the same place for a long time - at least 20 minutes, possibly longer. 
I took a couple of photographs at this point.
They were jet black and seemed spiky in shape which I thought was odd.
At the end of the observation, the left one seemed to fall from the sky behind a building and the right one went East but not at too high a speed.
They are tiny dots on my photos, above the large building facing me, in the clouds.
What were they? I’d like to know.
