My family and I were outside enjoying the warm weather. It was dark out and so we were looking at the stars. My daughter asked me why the stars were moving and so I looked and was wondering the sane thing. There were 13! 13 star like ufo's in a straight line moving over my house slowly. They were in a uniform line together. They looked just like stars white in color and circle. I watched a few come out of no where do a zig zag and turn into the line and move with the rest. I counted 13 of them for an hour over my house. Then a jet came out of nowhere and flew over my house putting out a trail of smoke with no sound. The jet appeared 3 times. The last time it appeared it had a sound like a plane. The sky was clear untill the jet showed up and clouds came out of nowhere. My entire family witnessed this. 5 of us. I tried to get pictures and a video but my phone couldn't get a good shot of them at all. It actually was at half power and then it went from 60% life left to 5% with in 20mins of watching this happen.
