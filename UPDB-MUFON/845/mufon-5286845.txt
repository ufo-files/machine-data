UFO hovered and moved in quick bursts

In stallion springs CA I was sitting in the jacuzzi at around 940 pm amd saw the ufo in the western sky. It appeared to look like a star at first. I noticed many satellites prior to this and am able to differentiate between the two. I performed different optical tests on myself to make sure it was for sure. My wife and I are both professionals. I teach at a university and she is an engineer.  She observed the UFO as well. We were both in awe.
