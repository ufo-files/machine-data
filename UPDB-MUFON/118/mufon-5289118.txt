Blast of light on me when pulled over on lonely road 3am

Travelling a long lonely stretch of road at about 3am. Pulled over on other side of road to get out to take a pee. No cars for miles. A blast of white light hit me. I didn't remember this till some time later. Afterwards, I remember driving again and asking my girlfriend who was sleeping at the time, along with other band members in the van, why we lost about 3 hours. Never thought any more about it until years later when I think I realized that something may have happened???
