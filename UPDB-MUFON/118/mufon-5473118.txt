{cak} Ball of light

I was driving North on I-5 just past vista point by Dunsmuir when I looked out at the horizon and noticed a ball of light rising from Mt. Shasta. At first I thought it was a helicopter, but it hovered for about 5 minutes and pulsated an orange light. After about 5 minutes it shot straight up towards the night sky. I was in awe when I saw the doped and trajectory of the object. It was not the first time I had noticed occurrences in the area.
