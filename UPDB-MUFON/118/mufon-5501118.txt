Red/orange glowing orbs

I was coming home from work. Traveling north in hwy 26. I saw about 5 to 8 bright red glowing balls that seemed to be only about 100 feet off the ground moving at a fault rapid pace from east to west. I immediately pulled over and tried to record them on my iPhone. As I was trying to record them they started to disappear but I noticed three more in a straight line traveling almost overhead. They were so bright they cast light onto the highway overpass that was about 200 feet away. They moved at a constant pace, never deviating in direction or pace. They made absolutely no noise. They travelled east to west but the wind was barely noticeable but the wind was blowing from the south. I was amazed that none of the passing cars seemed interested at all. I tried to video the events but playing back the video it only recorded 3 seconds even though I was positive I was recording the whole time. Then as quickly as I saw then they faded out if sight. They seemed to be about the size if a beach ball and the light never flickered.
