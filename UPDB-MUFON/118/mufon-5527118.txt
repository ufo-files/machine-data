Looking up an noticed object zipping across sky at night without red blue strobes.

I was outside our apartment enjoying a cigarette at night with my girlfriend. She and I often look at the sky for objects. She noticed a spherical object flying fairly high in the sky. We both looked for strobe light which will often signify a plane at night. The object moved into our field of vision and I sent her inside to get her phone to take a picture. The photo did not come out, but we did see a plane about 2 or 3 minutes later about the same altitude with strobe lights. It gave us an estimate to how fast the other object was moving, which was considerably faster between parts of the big dipper. The object disappeared by continuing its flight in the same direction. The object did not move in any lateral fashion. I did not really feel anything, as I do believe in alien life. I would add it was kind of exciting to watch.
