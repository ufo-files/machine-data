White lights in a triangle pattern with red blinking light

Slow moving triangle of white lights with a red blinking light. It was making NO sound which is what peaked my interest. We are very close to March Air Force Base as well as a popular sky diving location and see and hear lots of jets and planes. U can always hear them. That is what made the absence of sound even more puzzling.
