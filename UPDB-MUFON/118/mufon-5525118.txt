Small basketball sized object moving quickly 40 or so ft from the ground.

I was outside on my deck (2 stories up), and noticed an object moving perfectly straight (West), rather quickly. Upon observation I realized it clearly wasnt a bird. I ran inside and grabbed my camera just to zoom in on it for a closer look. In less than 15 seconds (Im an amateur photographer so my camera was close by), I came back out and got 1 pic before running out onto my front lawn to try to get another one. I watched it disappear into a cloud before I could do so. Within 5 mins the sky was extremely darkened by very odd and dark clouds. I noticed 2 small flashes of light which I took to be the coming of a thunderstorm, though it never materialized. I thought perhaps the object was a balloon, but it wasnt very windy out, and in my experience balloons generally move rather erratically, following the direction of the wind,not perfectly straight at the same speed. I think perhaps it was some sort of environmental monitor (I live within a mile of an EPA Superfund site), but I thought I would share regardless since Ive never seen anything even remotely resembling it. After the sun set there were several helicopters in the general area which may be unrelated, I see those often near the EPA site, but usually just one.
