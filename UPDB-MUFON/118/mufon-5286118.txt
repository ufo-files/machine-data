Large Hoovering Lights and Time Lapse

It was late, 9 or 10 pm. I cant remember even the year, as this was a suppressed memory for a long time. I was home with only the other witness. The sky went dark except the bright lights that came down. They came down so fast. We just watched it. I don't know how to descibe how it felt. Scary but not. We went outside. And the next thing we knew, it was dawn and I was in my house.
The memory came back to me a few years ago, but i sort of just chalked it up to an active imagination, until I asked the other witness about it last night and she described exactly what I remember. We have never talked about it, even the day after it happened, until last night,
