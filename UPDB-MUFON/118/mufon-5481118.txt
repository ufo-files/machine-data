Glowing objects flying eastward over Crossfield

My son saw some glowing objects flying eastward, from where we were they were probably over Crossfield. He came to get me from the house and we then saw about 10 more objects traveling in the same direction, glowing and twinkling colours. Their direction was eastward and then upward and finally blinking out.
