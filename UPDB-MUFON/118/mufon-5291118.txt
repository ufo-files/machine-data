My room needs to be very dark for me to sleep. My husband at that time was a continual heavy snorer and I barely got any rest. One night I woke in a paralyzed state. I felt a presence and fear. I could move my eyes and saw that my ex was lying on his side and was not snoring. Then I noticed the light absorbing shape at his foot of the bed. It was looking at me and not moving. It had squarish shape for a head. I feared, closed my eyes and prayed for it to go away. Then I felt better, opened my eyes and it was gone. When I got up in morning I went into my bathroom which is attached to my room and I noticed a small drop of blood on my forehead just to the left of dead center above my eyebrows. This spot has never gone away and on some nights when I feel that I haven't slept or feel like I was beat up, I will find a sliver of black or gray material coming out of it, protruding. This hole has slightly raised edges. I have not seen this again in my room.
