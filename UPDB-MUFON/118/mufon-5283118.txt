Saw a silver oval shaped object pass over head.

While photographing clouds in my back yard, I noticed a silver oval shaped object approaching from the west. As the object passed over head, it was zig zagging left to right with very sharp turns. I found the object in my camera's view finder and took nine photos of the object. It was heading east when I last saw it, the duration of the sighting was about 2 minutes.
