{rjl} Ball of light; orb

I was outside taking pictures on my camera of several (8-10) parallel chemtrails. When I saw a moving jet begin to emit a chemtrail, I began filming it across the sky. I didn't notice anything until I uploaded the film onto my computer. About 30 seconds into the film, I saw a ball of light seemingly come away from the jet on the north east side, slightly change angles, then shoot up out of range of my camera. It is easy to miss...hard to see but I thought with your technology, you might want to take a look at it.
