{jpg} white orb and smaller reddish-orange orb beneath moon

My boyfriend and I were walking back to our apartment after parking our truck up the street, he was walking behind me and told me to stop, and pointed to the moon and said "do you see that?" I didn't notice it right away, but for some reason said, "to the bottom left?". I then noticed the larger white orb, and told him to take a picture. At this point the smaller reddish orb was there the whole time, but I thought it was an airplane and didn't pay much attention to it. He took to pictures and started to record a video and I realized the reddish circle wasn't moving and that it was also and orb. We then continued to walk home; the orbs were still visible when we went inside.

Please note, the moon looks like it's bouncing around in the video, but that's just because he was walking while recording it.
