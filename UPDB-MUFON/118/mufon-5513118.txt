{cak} strange object showed up on pictures

I went to the beach for the Galactic Alignment on the Morning of December 21 2012. Since my google research said the true galactic alignment occurred at 6:11 est I woke and walked to the beach about an hour before sunrise. There was a rainbowish colors on the horizon and I decided to take some pictures. Because it was so dark I didn't expect them to come out good and didn't really look them for over a week. When I did I found unusual light on my series of 4 pictures. Stars remain fixed and unblurred, while something else is obviously moving in a very unusual pattern.
