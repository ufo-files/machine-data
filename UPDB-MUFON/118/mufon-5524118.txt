white spherical object observed around 9:40am in clear sky, no surrounding craft

While vacuuming car at a car wash in Pleasanton, I looked up (why I don't know)to the sky and observed a white spherical object (aspirin size, maybe 1000 ft +/- (not good at aerial distance). The object was absolutely stationery for about 2 minutes in one position then moved laterally, somewhat slowly about 3 inches left if you held up a ruler to the sky (again, not good with distances). It stayed stationary again for about 1-2 minutes but I had to get going but when I completed my car vacuuming (about 5 mins later) the object was gone. The object was somewhat overhead, to the west.
