{cak} multicolored hovering orbs that took off after observing for a few minutes

I was cookin up some bacon, gettin ready for Sunday mornin possum roast, when I looked out the kitchen winder and saw a bunch of lights in the sky. They were different colors and they kinda hovered at first over the paint creek area. Then one by one they came toard town. At the end a few of em came up all at once and took of in formation together. I turned to ma and said, "what the f is going on? I mean what the f is going on?" Then they was gone.
