{kt}At 8:10pm My Husband saw in the sky 4 orange lights in a straight line formation gliding one after the other.

At About 8:05 My Husband went outside to walk our dog & at about 8:10 he saw these 4 orange lights low in the sky, he called for me to come outside I saw these orange in color bright lights gliding in a straight line formation one after the other traveling east. They were silent. They were low in the sky as an low airplane would be. My Husband went back inside to get my cell phone, then my Mother came outside at that point and we all just stood there in awe and amazement really. They were really bright, traveling one after the other, in small groups, and 3 of them disappeared as soon as an airplane was heading toward them, the Airplane was traveling from south to north. I called wink newsroom at 8:21, I told who answered the phone, there were orange lights in the sky to go quickly outside she would see them, the woman who answered the phone was terse in her response she said I was the only caller and that they were probably Chinese lanterns. I told her these didnt look like Chinese lanterns not at all. She hung up the phone while I was still talking. I counted at least 15 lights from the time I came outside. I took 2 photos one came out orange with a light on it. My Husband has a video on his phone, showing 2 traveling one after the other and one really good pic of a orange light. The other pics showed up a white light. After they stopped coming a Military Helicopter or it could of been a Police Helicopter, it wasn't muffled, it went over our house traveling east too.
