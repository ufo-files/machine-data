As a young man, I observed this disc form over my head while I was in an alfalfa field on our farm.

As I was involved in track and field events in high school, I was running around the aforementioned alfalfa field to build up my endurance and I was running toward the south end of the field and I was near where the north end of our orchard was. I became aware of a whirring noise directly above me which was in the sky and I looked up and saw this large dark object, it was huge, and it was hovering right above where I was standing and it looked like it was in three adjoining layers. The reason why I say it was in adjoining layers because each layer had a row of lights going around. On the lower level the lights were going in a counter clockwise fashion; on the second layer it was going in a clockwise fashion and on the top layer it was going in a counter clockwise fashion. This lasted for 8 minutes. After this time had passed, abruptly it went straight south over our orchard and disappeared. It made a whirring sound as it left like a loud wind.
