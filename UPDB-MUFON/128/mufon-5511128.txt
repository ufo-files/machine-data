{cak} Bright saucer like object!!!

I was sitting in my yard at approximately 10pm with my family enjoying the beautiful weather when a large lighted object caught my eye. I was facing west and this thing was coming from the south heading north. My son, daughter, neighbor and husband saw it and we were able to take pics and video. The object was so slow and LOW made no noise and lasted long enough thay we all had enough time to get our phones on it...It definitely moved in a very controlled manner. It was in our view for approximately 1 minute!!! It seemed to be slowly ascending then speeded up and then it was gone as if it was never there.
