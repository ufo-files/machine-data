Like a light orb, very bright at first. Bright as plane light from great distance.

I was taking a nightly walk around the area that I live during the week for my job. The first time I noticed it I was walking through a large empty cinema parking lot. I looked and saw a large bright round light, the type of light you see as planes fly overhead on their decent to DFW airport in Dallas. I remember thinking it was sad not seeing many jets flying any longer due to COVID. I walked more heading North and looked up again and saw this object fly very fast to the North East of town, not the direction of planes flying to DFW airport. Then it falls a great distance from up high in a vertical direction toward the ground, with still a very bright light.   I thought I would hear a boom. Like a plane crash. But honestly it came from very high to ground very fast also. No boom. 1 or 2 seconds later it ascends straight up but from the Southwest. I would say from Little Elm, Texas area. It actually hovers for quite some time. I was walking without my phone, so I was getting close to my residence and I began to run back. I was able to get my phone. Samsung S20. And as I was running back to the street corner for better view, It flew from Little Elm, TX area back to Denton and crossed Interstate 35E. At this point I was able to video the footage. Also, when it flew off very fast it was flying back across Interstate 35E. Traveling East.
