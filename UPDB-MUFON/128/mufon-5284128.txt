Noticed something in the air at the back side of a farm while driving by.

It was making a sort of pattern in the very far back of the farm we were driving by. It was maybe a couple or there hundred feet above the ground at first and not a sound but it moved in several and seemingly purposeful directions almost like it was looking for something in a pattern. We pulled over and I tried to record on my cheep Wal Mart phone but gave up and just started snapping pictures. It was almost like it noticed us, it elevated but straight up not anything like a plane and faster than a helicopter. At times it looked like it was either spinning or making itself blurry. It got several times higher than it had been and sat there for a few seconds and started to head in a south west direction. It seemed like it may have went a mile or so and then just poof it took off at an incomprehensible speed. It was just a streak. Never a sound.
