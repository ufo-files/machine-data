Pulsating light that seemed to move quickly in different directions, before fading out

I was standing in my room looking out the window when I saw a bright star seem to appear in the sky. The object got bright and then started moving. At first it hovered, then began to ascend. After this it quickly descended at a fast speed, before moving side to side. It often erratically changed direction, slowing down and speeding up. It sometimes pulsated in brightness, with it going from very dim to very bright. It finally stopped and began to slowly ascend north. At this point it gave out one last extremely bright pulsate, before twinkling and fading out.
