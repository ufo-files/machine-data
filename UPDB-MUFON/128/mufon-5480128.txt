{rjl} White orb hovering in single location

Coming home from work and was getting on the off ramp to exit the interstate and I witnessed a white dot randomly appear. I kept track of it while driving so as to be sure to not lose sight of it. It is only about a mile from the off ramp to my house. While watching it as I was driving, the object appeared to possibly, i cannot say for sure but possibly, be following me from a far distance until I made it to my house and was able to get a better glimpse of the object with some binoculars. It could be seen with the naked eye, the binoculars were just to get a better look and I could only make out 2 white lights on the bottom right but the left side was too bright to be able to make out anything more. I sat there watching it for a good 15-20 minutes hoping to catch it disappear on video with no luck. I then left to get some food, it was still there when I left, I went less then 0.5 miles to a fast food chain and was gone for only 10 minutes, when I came back and looked to see if it was still there, it had gone. The video that is uploaded is what could be seen with the naked eye. The video shows both a zoom view and the standard view of the object. The other 2 are still photos. One photo is a zoomed (zoomed in on with the phone capabilities) in unaltered photo of the object, the second picture with my finger in it is a picture that is also unaltered. If you manually zoom into the picture with my finger in it, you will see the object right above my finger. Each picture and photo is taken using a Samsung Galaxy S7.
