Starlike brightened as laser pointed towards object

My uncle and eye were looking at the stars. I spotted a starlike moving object which resembles a satellite moving NNW. He pointed a laser towards the object and trailed it as he would usually do. The difference between this time and every other time the object actually shown brighter as if reacting towards the laser. As my uncle stopped following, it began to dim.
