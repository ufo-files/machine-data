two triangle-shaped objects flying north to south, then changed direction to west

We were outside looking at the Perseid meteor showers when two triangular objects appeared in the North approx 60 degrees above the horizon, just before 11pm. They were glowing slightly as if reflecting the sunset. They flew directly overhead and just before they would have disappeared behind a tree at the South end of our property, they immediately changed direction (the second one doing a very quick loop) and flew off to the West. The entire thing only took a minute and there was absolutely no sound.
