I first noticed a black dot in the sky,it was only when I looked through binoculars it became more interesting!

I was sitting at my desk by the window in my office, when I leaned back into my chair, and noticed Black dot in the sky. It was traveling in my direction, so I ran into the front room and grabbed my binoculars. My wife and daughters were in there at the time, and asked what I was doing. I then looked thru the binoculars out of the front room window and got a good visual on the object. This is where I swore out loud...the object was flipping over and over, tumbling horizontally??? It was Black, disk shaped with a proud centre. It was very odd! We all ran out to the garden to observe it, and that is when I shot video footage on my mobile phone. The object descended then changed direction and started emitting white lights. flash, then a red glow. The object would stop flipping, then start, it even flipped back on itself whilst maintaining a flight path. This object did not act like a balloon or look like one. We all saw a it through the binoculars. We watched as it disappeared in the distance.
