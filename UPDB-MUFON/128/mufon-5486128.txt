{nd} 5 red/orange pulsating lights disappeared one by one in the sky

At approximately 7:04pm my father walked outside for just a few seconds when he saw in the distance, 5 unfamiliar lights pulsating in the pitch black sky. He ran inside to grab my mother; they saw 5 red/orange lights arranged similar to the dippers, one white blinking light hovered between them all. These red/orange lights, from our perspective, were round and large compared to all other star in the sky, but not as large as the moon. The bottom red/orange light faded away within seconds. The second light shortly followed. My mother ran inside to grab me. At 7:05pm I grabbed my phone and I walked outside to start taking pictures and videos. I saw three red/orange lights in the sky at different heights. The lights moved from North West to North East. The three lights pulsated a red/orange glow. The light closest to West pulsated and descended while fading out until it was no longer visible. Then the next light closest to West raised, and lowered itself to be at the same height as the light closest to East. When the two remaining lights were lined up, the light farthest West faded and descended until it disappeared seconds later. The last remaining light shortly after also faded and descended until it disappeared. The white blinking light remained underneath all of the pulsating red/orange lights during the entire time of the event, while moving back and forth from West to East. My father, mother and I were all very shocked. We were freaking out, what we saw was unbelievable. My mother kept asking "did that just happen?". My father said that in all his life, out of the 15 years he has been going into our back yard every night, he has never seen anything like this. We are still astonished. These lights were not airplanes, helicopters or any other type of familiar aircrafts. They were Unidentified Flying Objects. They disappeared in the sky one after another. These were no aircrafts; they were UFOs.
