{rjl} Jet like object hovering then disappearing

Driving north on Telegraph Rd between Walton and Dixie, but traffic was slowed for some reason. While idling I saw something to my right side and turned to look. I said aloud to myself, "Now why is that plane flying so low and so close to the road?" There were some trees, but I was at an opening where I could see the object. So after I considered why a plane would be right there and so low, I looked again and said to myself, "That thing isn't flying, it's hovering!" But there was no sound (I had opened my passenger window by then). I had to turn back toward traffic as it has started moving a little and when I turned my head back to look at the object again, it was gone. Just disappeared in seconds! I looked on the internet to see what kind of plane or jet might look similar. The object was high enough that I was actually looking at the bottom. The only thing I could find to compare it to was a Harrier Jet. The object I saw was very large. I do not know how many degrees off the ground it was but I took a guess to fill in that question. This area is just a field as far as I know as there are no businesses on that side of the road.
