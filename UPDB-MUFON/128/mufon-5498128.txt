{dr}Saw an unusually bright neon blue round light in SW sky. It grabbed our attention because of the color. It moved in directions that are not possible for a plane, helicopter or flare. It was moving when we saw it and then stopped. Then moved west.

My friend and I were returning home when she noticed a bright neon blue light in the SW sky. It was 11:26 pm on Oct. 30, 2014. I looked and it was lower and brighter than a star. It appeared about 1-2 miles away. It moved toward us - moving NE. It then made an erratic movement moving sharply to the left then down, lower in the sky, then to the right. It was at this time we knew it was not a plane, helicopter, flare, firework or anything else we have seen in the sky before. I grabbed my cell phone and took 3 pictures. The first did not capture it. The second captured it as we saw it and the 3rd image is blurred. Not sure if it the camera moving on that one, but probably as I did not see any blur when watching the object. We watched it and when it moved to the right (NW) it stood still for about 30-45 seconds. It then moved in a straight direction away from us going SW and disappearing under clouds.
