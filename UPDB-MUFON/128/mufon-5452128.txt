3 bright Lights at different times of the evening.

I was laying down on my patio love seat while watching the night sky. Lately, I've seen quite a few strange anomalies at night. I see at least one a night! Last night, 06/15/2020, I saw 3 different lights in the sky that looked like a star was being turned up and then all of a sudden turned off. These lights appeared right in my plain of vision. I think I may have also seen a satellite as well last night. It looked like a low lit, small star that was moving way slower than a shooting star and no light trail behind it. It was all very fascinating!
