Entities creeping around my front yard

Honestly sounds ridiculous but I had an overwhelming feeling of being "watched", and peered out my front door blinds and saw these strange glowing lights in the field just past my front yard. One was vivid green, one whitish, then three were smaller and red, and all seemed to be floating, or hovering around, but it was like they were there wanting me to see them yet at safe enough distance to really identify them, then at the 2:20 mark of the video a grey 4 ft entity shows up even closer and just stands there staring at me! He stood at the fence dividing my front yard from the field where the others were. I filmed it for a while, got creeped out, and alerted a family member to come see, but they had already left. Also I'd like to include the grey seemed to be interdimensional, like he was glitching. Video isn't the greatest but it was pretty profound to witness.
