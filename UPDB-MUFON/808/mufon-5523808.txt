bright white light and three smaller multi-colored lights around 6:30pm

first main object was very bright white light almost star like, but it was too early to be a star of that size and brightness. it did move from the southeast in a straight line to the northeast and then just stopped. a few moments later, a smaller object also moving up from the southwest was heading directly for the larger white object. this smaller one had all the colors and was flashing and seemed like it was changing shape. then it ejected two small objects that moved slowly away from its parent object. those two small objects also had colored lights but not as bright. clouds moved in and i could no longer see the colored lights but the whole time this was going on, the larger bright light was in the same position in the sky and did not move, nor change in its brightness. then more clouds moved overhead and i could no longer see the white light.
