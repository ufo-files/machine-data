{tjd} 4 bright white lights falling fast

I was sitting outside looking west at sky, dusk nearly dark. Bright flash made me think for a micro second shooting star. I was looking almost straight at it, my eyes focused on 2 side by side bright white lights that flashed on and off very quickly. Followed immediately by 2 lights on and off right below. This happened at shooting star speed, total time maybe half a second. Whatever the lights were they were falling very fast and seamed pretty close, (half to one mile). Lights were too powerful to be a drone. Falling too fast to be a crashing aircraft. Not hovering, could see no craft around lights shadowed against dusk sky.
