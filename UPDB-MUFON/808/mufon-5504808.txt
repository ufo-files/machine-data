{cak} heard a low humming realized there was a shape blocking out cloud cover

I had walked my girlfriend at my house at about 2:20~ after she had left i went inside then after a few minutes thought to grab something from my car before heading to bed. When i got outside i went to the car grabbed what i needed and turned to return back inside when i heard a buzz/humming sound that sounded a lot like the sound a industrial transformer emits like an electrical whine. The sound had a property similar to the drone a helicopter emits as it seemed to radiate from multiple directions thinking it was just a helicopter out of sight or some other vehicle i turned back to go inside when the sound peaked it became significantly louder and it was at this time i realized i could see an oval shape blocking out the reflected light the moon was casting on the light cloud coverage watched it as it passed over noting that as it passed over head it seemed the air got much warmer and also that my eyes and head began to ache after about 15 minutes it had moved beyond where i could/hear it and there was no other activity. The way the object moved it made me think of a balloon except for its size and the fact it paused for approximately 2-3 minutes when it first crested the roof of my house and into my sight. It emitted no light of any sort.
