Unique explosive flash of swirling light cascading down and changing color

I woke up early because I had forgotten to turn off the garden sprinklers and the water pipes were making their usual sounds when the pump starts and stops. After turning the water off outside I was headed back in and happened to look up at the early morning sky. I paused for a minute, just to look around when a sudden flash of light exploded into view well above the tree line, high in the sky…something I have never seen before. It was like a firework going off except the light swirled downward. 
The display began with a large reddish burst that quickly morphed to yellow, and then into white more so as the stream of light swirled down. After making one revolution, the stream morphed from a white light into a long downward tail of bluish light dissipating as it finished.
The flash only lasted a second, perhaps two. There was no noise associated with it. Just a sudden swirling stream of light, and then it was gone. I waited around for a few minutes to see if something would follow, but nothing. It was very cool to see it, but wonder what it was.
