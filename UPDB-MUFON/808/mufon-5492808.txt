{dr}Red object stopped, then took off incredibly fast parallel to highway.

In 1973, not sure of the month, my girlfriend and I were on our way back to Hurtsboro, AL from having been at the drive in movies in Auburn. Around 10:00 probably, I noticed what I believed to be a shooting star in the distance. It came down very fast and stopped momentarily at treetop level, about 30', then it took off at an incredible speed directly in the same direction we were heading. It disappeared in the matter of a second or so. It was a disc shape and was a red glowing light. My girlfriend looked at me and asked if I had just seen that, and I told her I did. We were speachless after seeing this. Neither of us had ever seen anything like this before. It most certainly was not a helicoptor or an airplane. During the three or four weeks before this event, several UFO sightings had been reported in the Auburn area.
