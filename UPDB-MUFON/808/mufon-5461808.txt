My wife and I were attending our daughter’s Ultimate Frisbee game in Charlotte, NC in February 2003 at 3:30 pm, while she was attending UNCW, when we noticed a very large cigar (oblong) shaped, non-bright silver colored flying craft overhead and without making any noise or wings & tail section. It appeared to be approximately at an altitude of 45k feet or higher, flying extremely fast and not emitting any cold air vapors. I would guess the object was moving an estimated speed of between 4,500 to 6,000 mph plus and a size estimating between the length of 4 to 6 USAF C-5s, moving faster than any known man-made aircraft has been recorded this close to the Earth. The craft traveled across the horizon from my right to left in a very short time frame of less than 45- 90 second period, again faster than normal aircraft can fly. My estimates are based on my seven (7) years in the US Air Force dealing with military aircraft, and I presently was working as a Federal Security Manager with the TSA/DHS at the time out of Greenville, SC location and held an appropriate security clearance during both tenures.
