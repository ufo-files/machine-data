Object hovered and pulsates for about 15 min then disappeared

I was driving south on egg harbor rd and I noticed a bright white light . At first I thought it was a plane making a descent into PHL., but the object did not move. Then the object got brighter and more white and changed from white to pink and started to pulsate . When I stopped at a traffic light I stated to observe the object more , I looked for a point of reference like a tree and a rooftop to see if the object moved. It did not . Then as I was watching it it vanished
