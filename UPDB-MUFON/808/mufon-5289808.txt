Metallic craft stationary then zoom into storm

Driving home after work in Granbury Tx headed to Tolar Tx on Hwy 377 . I was watching a storm front moving east enjoying the lightning displays. When I observed the Metallic egg shaped object hovering just outside the storm. The object stayed stationary for just a few moments long enough to think what is that and am I actually seeing this.... Then the object zoomed directly into the storm heading west ... As I continued on driving, maybe 3 or 4 minutes later 2 military jets flying low and incredibly fast zoomed bye in the same direction the object had zoom away in.
