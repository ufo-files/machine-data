I was driving my work truck and saw a pyramid shaped UFO above a large cloud

filled out and filed at MUFON HQ by the Field Investigator with the witness on the phone. (Witness was not sure of the year either 2011 or 2012, it was summer, maybe July or August. He was sure of the time.)

I was driving on Harmony Road in Severance and kept seeing something reflecting light in the sky. When I stopped at the only light in town, a very large lone cloud floated over the city and above it was the thing that was reflecting the sun, a chrome colored pyramid. It hovered just above the cloud. I tried to grab my flip phone and tried to take a photo of it, but before I could find the button on the camera the pyramid had disappeared. It was too large to totally fit behind the cloud, so it must have shot up or disappeared from sight. I did zoom in with my camera around the cloud and sky looking for it but it was gone.
