{rjl} Observed two red objects in the western sky at dusk, traveling w to sw to s.

I stepped outside at dusk and and saw 2 objects in the western sky. The objects were red and looked as if they were burning, like flares. However, they were moving in roughly parallel path which varied (a non-straight path) from west to south, at what appeared to be the same altitute. I called my wife outside and we both observed the objects; thats when I took the short video. We observed them until they both "blinked out". These objects were not local aircraft; nor were they meteors. They moved too quickly and in a horizontal parallel path to be flares, and they looked fairly large when they were brightest; at 5:31pm on 12/29/13.

I have a 20 second video of these objects but am unable to upload to this site via my iPad for some reason.
