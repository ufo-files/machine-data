{dr} bright light moving slowly to North, stopped, hovered maybe 15 seconds, then shot up and East and out of sight

I was in college, and dating a new boyfriend. Just got home went to bed. Was laying in bed with the blinds open pulled up so the whole window area was open. Was staring up at the sky and noticed a bright light moving across the sky in a Northerly Direction. Sky was clear and could see stars. The Dallas-Fort Worth Airport had been open about a year or so, and I thought maybe it was an airplane going to the new airport. Then suddenly it stopped which I knew wasn't something an airplane could do. It hovered for about 10 seconds or so and then suddenly it shot up towards the east and mainly up really fast and was out of sight in maybe one and a half seconds. I jumped out of bed and went to the window and stared up hoping it would come back and it didn't. The window was about three feet from the bed. I think it was around 11 pm central US time.
