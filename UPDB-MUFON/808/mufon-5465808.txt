I was taking picures of the sunset to the west. I captured this reddish sphere shaped object with evenly placed lights.

I was standing on my porch taking pictures of the sunset to the west. It was cloudy with dark and light clouds and sun. I took several pictures and then I went back inside. I was looking at the pictures and noticed the object in the cloud and blew it up on my phone. I didn't know what to think. Possibly, a weather balloon, but I have never seen one with lights on it. It was a sphere shaped object with evenly placed lights. It appeared to be reddish in color. I did not see it move and did not see it with my eyes. I only saw it in the pictures. I did go back out to see it again, but nothing was there. The clouds and sun all changed formation.
