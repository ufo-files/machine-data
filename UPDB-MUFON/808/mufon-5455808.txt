{rjl} BRIGHT RED ORB APPEARED

WHILE STANDING ON THE BANK OF A CREEK AN EXTREMELY BRIGHT ORB APPEARED & ASCENDED UP A HILL & DISAPPEARED. IT LASTED FOR 30 SECONDS. THERE WAS NO SOUND. THERE WAS NO LOGICAL REASON. IT WAS ABOUT 4 FEET OFF THE GROUND. IT STAYED 4 FEET OFF THE GROUND WHILE IT ASCENDED A HILL & DISAPPEARED. IT WAS NOT A REFLECTION OR A LASER POINTER. THE SUN WAS SETTING BEHIND THE HILL. NO ONE WAS IN THE AREA. A PERSON WOULD HAVE MADE A NOISE WHILE WALKING THROUGH THE WOODED AREA. THE WAS NO SOUND. I WAS BRIEFLY SCARED
