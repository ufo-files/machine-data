(GS) Vibrant orange light that semi-slowly brightened and dimmed as it glided across the sky

My husband and I were out smoking a cigarette when we spotted an orange light, orb-ish craft in the north eastern sky. At first we thought it could possibly be a plane, but the way the lights were functioning and shining were completely different than any aircraft Ive ever seen. As it glided along going south, it slowly brightened and dimmed, like a very slow pulsation. There was nothing distinguishable about it other than the light. When it wasnt shining bright orange during its pulsating, it appeared to be a tiny red dot, there was no body to it. I was very excited to be seeing it and was uplifted by the experience, but my husband was a bit shocked to admit what we were looking at. 

As it started going further south, it appeared to be getting farther away either at a very fast rate, or even going outside of the Earths atmosphere... its hard to explain. It just got smaller and smaller rather quickly until it disappeared in an instant. 

We go out every night and see all different kinds of planes very often, weve never seen anything like what we saw on that night.
