My brother, his girlfriend and I were riding in a car down south from Hagerstown MD to Norforlk VA.

About halfway through the ride he looked up and screamed "WHAT THE HELL IS THAT!?" I looked up to see a bright blue light stationary until it darted out of sight. It was way to fast to be anything man made. And it couldn't have been a plane if it was stationary for a moment. It was about as high as a plane. I couldn't see any structure or craft, just the bright blue orb of light. It was bigger than any other star in the sky.
