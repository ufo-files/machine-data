I was with a friend in his garden, when I noticed a white light in the sky. At first I thought that it was a star even though it was probably about 3 times bigger than the biggest star I have ever seen. After 5 seconds of standing still it slowly started to move towards East, at the same time, losing intensity, and after 5-10 seconds it shrink into 2 bright spots, barely seen. Another 15-20 seconds it was necessary for them to fade away. It was definitely a UFO. My friend didn1t take it seriously at first, and he didn1t get a second chance to look at it and maybe think twice, since it disappeared too fast; which means I didn1t get a chance to catch it on tape because there was just not enough time.
