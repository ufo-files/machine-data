{cak} Riding from Florida to Georgia on I-75N, about 8:20pm, saw objects and filmed them.

While riding back seat on my way from Florida to Georgia, on I-75N, we ran into a quick storm. After the rain slacked up, I thought I saw a Tornado forming so I (ended game I was playing on my tablet) started to record. Then I noticed two (2) shining, star like objects moving around in the clouds. I tried to focus on them, but thought maybe it might be from the window(spots,reflections), so I moved the tablet a little just to see if they moved or stayed in one place. I also recorded while trying to look with naked eye, but they disappeared. Later on, after I watched the video, I could see how they cloaked in the clouds, and that there was an even bigger one hovering up above them (2) to the left. It is kind of hard to see, but it is the classic saucer shape. I also could see in the lower right, what appeared to be a red star. I have since made a you=tube video of said event.
