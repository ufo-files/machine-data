Multiple days, multiple witnesses, no doubt.

First day, over busy town in mid afternoon. Wife got out of car in drive-through to get video and it vanished at incredible speed. Second day, appeared much brighter and lower. Pulsing over tree tops and stationary. Next night was again watched by multiple people moving from nearby location at steep angle up. Speed was amazing. It went from large and bright to moving among the stars and out of sight in about four or five seconds. Each time the observed object was different size/brightness , or at least appeared to be. Two of the observers have reservations about speculation but cannot imagine what it was that they seen. Another has been close to one during our last wave a few years ago and was sure it was the same as before. The rest are certain it was a craft. I reported it on this sight after it happened and have not seen a mention yet, so I have tried again.
