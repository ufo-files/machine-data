Super bright orb followed by a small star like object.

I was siting down outside on my backyard deck looking at the stars with my binoculars for a while just doing my usual astronomy thing and wanted to give my eyes a break and have a sip of my water. when all of a sudden i felt compelled to look up.I saw a really bright large orb or something flying across the sky,I tracked it with my binoculars and could completely tell that it was no air craft that i know of.There was no sound of an engine nothing at all,and there was no aircraft in the sky at the time.It was also being followed by a star like object,it was kind of funny because it reminded me of close encounters the movie,the orb i saw was at least 1000 to 2000 feet in altitude and i would say about 35 to 50 feet in diameter traveling maybe 80 to 100 km/h.I was facing north and the objects were traveling from the west to the east over top of me.I will never forget this encounter ever!!!,it was the most crazy thing i have ever seen.
