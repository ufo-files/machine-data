Unusual flying object

I had just gotten home from work about 8:15pm, gotten out of my car to walk across the parking lot. I was looking north when I saw an object with many lights in the sky and seem to strobe about every 20 seconds or so. To me the object had a main cylinder body and two V shaped wings with white lights running along the sides of the V's and possibly on the main body as well. It would pulsate/strobe as well. I don't recall hearing anything but traffic noise from the road nearby. The object flew over a apartment complex just north of where I live and the area is well light. It is not a usual flight path for planes to come into the airport. I actually thought it was a plane with landing lights on but after looking at it again I noticed all the lights. I could not make out any color on the object-it was a black silhouette. The object flew west/northwest over the trees and pulsated one last time before I lost sight of it. I just felt curiosity of what I was seeing at the time.
