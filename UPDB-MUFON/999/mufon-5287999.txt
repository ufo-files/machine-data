I was on my back porch smoking while looking briefly at my phone and when I looked up I noticed this dimmed orb/round object with no flashing red or green lights. Made no sound at all whatsoever. At first I thought it could of been helicopter or such BJ things weren’t adding up at all. As I continued to watch it hover and then move very slowly across the sky until I called for my wife and son who was inside and they had a look and couldn’t understand what they were seeing either. My wife pulled out her phone to try and get video of it but it wouldn’t show up on her phone hardly. The weird thing was when she first hit record, the orb/circular object got very bright for a few seconds and started moving a bit faster. When that happened, my family went onto the front porch to continue to watch it till it vanished over the tree tops. My son was amazed of the object that brightened and made no noise as it went on it’s way. It’s just weird how bright it got when my wife went to hit record and then it dimmed down again and started moving in a bit of a hurry but not crazily fast
