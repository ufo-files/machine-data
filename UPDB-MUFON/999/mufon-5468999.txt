2 Red lights travelling together parallel then came together in an explosion of light and dissapeared

Firstly, I was eating a slice of pizza whilst walking through a field on my way home when something was brought to my attention right in the corner of my eye, there was two red lights travelling parallel to each other, I thought they must be helicopters or airplanes, they were not airplanes as they were travelling at a much faster speed than an airplane and also had so sound. These lights were just like a flying sun, they burned your eyes if you looked at them. These lights were travelling closer towards me, and instantly stopped in a instant right over my house. These two lights joined together and made a very bright explosion light, it was like looking at the sun. My eyes ached for at least 30 seconds and could not see anything. When I got my vision back, I looked up at the sky, and these 2 lights were gone. I instantly ran to my house to tell my dad about this experience, I also felt sick for about 20 minutes after the experience. I still cannot explain what this was.
