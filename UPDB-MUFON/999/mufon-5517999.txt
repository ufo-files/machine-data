walking to the convenience store, saw a bright moving light in the sky which then ascended to the point where it was barely visible

I was walking to the convenience store, and saw something in the sky last night at about 11:00pm in aurora ontario. I happened to look up, and saw a bright light (brighter and larger than a star) that was moving eastward across the sky at a steady pace. at first, I thought it was a satellite,I don't think it actually was a satellite, as they tend to look further away and travel in a straight line. the object that I saw was travelling relatively straight but would sort of 'jump' off course every 20-30 seconds. my sighting lasted for about 3 minutes, and then the object started to disappear until all I could see was a very faint flickering light which then travelled upwards in the direction of a rather strange looking "star" until it was no longer visible..... this "star" is a flickering/colour changing light in the sky that has been visible for a few months now, but I'm not exactly sure what it is. it kinda' freaked me out a little bit.
