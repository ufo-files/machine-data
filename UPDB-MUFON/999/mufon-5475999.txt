While driving home I seen a UFO and got a couple of good pics

This event took place in Markham Ontario, Canada. At approximately 11:05pm I was heading north on Donald Cousins Pkwy when I first noticed the object to the east of me heading SW. I noticed it because it long and flat and it unusually bright (like led auto lights) lights across the Front of the object. It was at the height of a helicopter and moving at slightly above average(helicopter) speed.
I kept looking at it while I was driving, when it was safe to stop I got out and snapped a couple pics. It looks like I got the bottom of the object, where you can clearly see 3 lights in a triangular formation. The object also did not make a sound and seemed to get a little faster as it continued SW. I followed it until I couldn't see if anymore. It did not appear to change altitude or direction
