UFO / Lights over South London

My cousin shot this footage of 3 lights in South London, he said came up from behind trees. He said he had seen them before so he was ready with the camera phone this time. He said he heard a weird noise that accompanied the lights and that they came up from behind the trees in a straight line formation then some blinked on and off before making a triangle formation then they blinked out randomly until one was left that shot off in various directions at high speed !
