Bright star like object

I was at home when i stepped out back and as i frequently do looked up at the night sky and saw this Very bright round object.At first i thought it was a plane,where in the path of alot of air traffic from norfolk international,but the more i stared at it i realized it was much brighter than any plane or object for that matter that i have ever seen moving in the sky.It had a twinkling star look to it.I tryed very hard to see any strobe lights as to rule it out as a plane but saw none.I motioned for my dad to step out and he also saw the object,We both observed what i would call a normal passenger plane passing lower and in a south to north track to said object.In comparison the passenger plane was much duller[it had the usual lights of any passenger plane ive seen at night]and probably 1/4 the size of object.I would guess the object was somewhere between 40k and 60k feet in alltitude.When me and my dad lost sight of object behind the trees,we watched it till you couldn't see it anymore.We both felt that it was too big,Bright, brilliantly vivid of a white glow to have any normal explanation for.
