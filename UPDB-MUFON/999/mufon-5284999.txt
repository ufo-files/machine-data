Saw 3 bright orange orbs in a triangular pattern in High Point NC

I was on my way to the store at 9 PM on October 2 2021 and as I was on Oak View Rd traveling NE I looked up and to the left and saw 3 bright orange orbs moving slowly in the North direction. I pulled my car over and watched them travel slowly north. There was a lead one and a second one to the left but behind the first one. The third one was in line with the first one. They seemed to be the same distance apart from each other. Triangular formation moving at same speed. Got my phone out and videoed what I could. They were gone within 3 seconds. No aircraft or anything in the air. About 70 degrees temperature and clear sky.
