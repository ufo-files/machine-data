Bright light coming through the roof of my house illuminating the interior of the house. I watched a dark irregular shaped approximately 90 feet over my house shine a spot light onto and through my house. The object then moved to the house across the street and illuminated it with the same spot light for about 20 seconds. The object repeated this process with 2 other houses on the street and then faded from view.
