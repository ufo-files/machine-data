small, alternating green & white lights with hummingbird-like movement

I was walking the dog one week night mid-Dec 2011 & got to the end of the driveway. I looked down the street to my right (north east direction) and noticed an odd light in the tops of a group of about 6 pines trees about 1/4 mile away. They are in the back yards of some houses. No one else was out on my street. It was dark and the night was clear, temp about 45 degrees. The light was small, alternating white and green, and it flited among the tops of the pine trees. At first I thought it was a plane, then maybe some kids out with a toy, but that wasnt it. The movements reminded me of a hummingbird or bumblebee. I couldnt see it distinctly given the distance and I was not wearing my glasses. I watched for about 2-3 minutes trying to figure out what it was. I had never seen anything like this before and I have lived in this neighborhood for 4 years. After about 3 minutes it took off behind the pine trees, up and away from me, apparently in a pretty steady line until I lost sight of it. Now every night I always glance down to the pine trees hoping to see it again, but no luck so far.
