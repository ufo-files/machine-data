{rjl} On my way from work saw multiple Orange orbs flying over the highway.

I was on my way home from work in Erlanger Ky. I was traveling east on I 275. I first noticed the lights as I was approaching a railroad bridge that spans the highway. At first I thought they were strange lights on a train . As I looked closer I realized they were to high and random to be on a train. I slowed down to get a better look and realized I was seeing multiple ufos. I took the nearest exit which was Dixie hwy. I pulled off on the nearest street which was winding way. I exited my car so I could get a better look. There had to have been over a dozen glowing orange orbs that flew directly over me. They weren't in any particular formation. It was hard to actually see a shape. They were traveling from the north heading south. They were orange to an orangish red and seemed to pulse. There were no red or green blinking lights no wings and no noise. I work close to CVG and see planes and helicopters on a daily basis. There were no clouds and no trees obstructing my view as they passed overhead. I have never seen anything like this in person and was excited and dumbfounded at the same time. I managed to get my I phone and take a few pictures and 2 short videos. They aren't the best quality because I was so excited and nervous and almost in disbelief . From the time I noticed them on the highway till I watched them pass was around 10 minutes.
