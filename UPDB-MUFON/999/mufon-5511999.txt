{dr} A bright flashing Orange/Glow light hovering over Bushwick in Brooklyn

I was walking home from a family members apartment. I walk everyday about fifteen blocks. On my way tonight at exactly 7:30, i looked up at the indigo sky which was beautiful. Here in Bushwick, Brooklyn, the planes hover a bit low to land at JFK or Laguardia. You can always tell a plane thats arriving or departing. Theres no mistaking that. Tonight I notice a rather large orange light flashing object in the sky. At first i thought it was a bright star. After five minutes the light was dimming and pulsating. Then it started changing directions. I then think well, this cant be a plane. I could usually hear the sound of the plane but there was no sound. I keep my eyes on the object which begins to change and hover in different directions for about five blocks. And suddenly as I make my way across the street to my apartment, it completely disappears without a trace. A few other people near me stopped and wondered what the bright light was but they just kept moving dismissing it. I never stop in the street to look at stars, at least not this early in the night. The sun just set and the blue was light enough to provide more than sufficient visibility to distinguish between stars and planes. It was surreal. It felt like, what could this be? It was the most fascinating orange glow, And I wish I had a camera. My phone ran out of battery, so conveniently for whatever it was, I was not able to document it.
