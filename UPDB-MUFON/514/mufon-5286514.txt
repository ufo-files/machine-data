Red blinking triangle shaped ufo

Was letting my dogs out to go potty looked up because I noticed red blinking lights flying fast  The craft was flying in a weird way like erratic movements but heading strait towards me but at the last minute turned heading west and went into clouds and kind of disappeared. I had lost it for a few seconds but then saw it totally heading west but the lights were not on. It appeared to be a black ufo and still in a triangle shape. It flew off fast then I didn’t see it anymore. I got some of it on video and this was the second craft I’d seen in a year. The first was a yr ago in a boomerang shape with 4 white pulsing lights that was heading north east very slow with no noise. There was no noise with this red craft either.
