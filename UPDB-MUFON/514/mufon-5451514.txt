WARP SPEED

I was sitting on top of Grassy Mountain the fourth highest mountain in Missouri so I can get a good clear unobstructed view of the sky. I had been sitting there for several hours already. I noticed something in the Big Dipper constellation & it was pulsating very slowly and controlled. As I was watching it did something that will be etched into my brain forever. The best way to describe it would be touche it jumped from that constellation to where we are and warp speed all you could see was a big fat white line from the Big Dipper to us. it seemed to move faster than the light which I know is supposed to be impossible according to our physics. But it's seriously looked like something you would see in a movie like maybe Star Wars or Star Trek or something of that nature when they do the warp speed thing and it looks like the spaceship stretches out. Well that's exactly what I seen and if you were standing on the ground I am 5 foot 9 and holding my arm out at full length the line would have appeared 4 inches long to me. And then it just slowly & quietly drifted through the night sky slowly pulsating and disappeared after about ten minutes. You could see no shape of anything just light on the downside of the pulse you wouldn't be able to even see it & then it would just light up and fade back out light up fade back out. By far one of the coolest things I've ever seen I thought about shining my green laser at it to see if i could get its attention but I didn't know if it had a better laser than me so I just sat there in amazement. I stayed there until the sun came up to see if I can see it or anything else again. I tried to record it but I need to upgrade my phone I guess
