{cak} Observed with Night Vision a Saucer type craft meet up with a massive Triangle shaped space ship

I was outside at 2:20 AM when I noticed a "moving white star" so I grabbed my Night Vision Scope and focused on it.To my shock and surprise,I saw a round disk shaped craft maybe 100-200 feet wide.It had three layers of central tiers making it maybe 50 feet tall.The craft also had two small round globes that were following it just behind and above the disk craft.The Saucer and its two round companions all met up with a massive Triangular shaped craft that was a 1000 meters long on each side of the triangle.This massive ship had a round subdued light at each corner.The space behind the massive ship was distorted in the shape of an egg and the stars were blurry looking through the distortion field.I estimate the altitude was 80,000 feet,well above normal commercial jets but still in the lower atmosphere.The four all seemed to merge together and then move away to the North.I could no longer see the Saucer but the two globes were visible above the rear of the giant ship.They all moved North into Canada until out of sight.Except for the first view of the saucer shaped craft they were only visible in the Night Vision Scope!I could not see the giant ship or globes with the naked eye!We are not alone!
