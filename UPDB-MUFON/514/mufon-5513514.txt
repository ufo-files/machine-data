{nd} 6 bright red lights moving towards sky and dissapearing

First 3 bright red lights appeared from horizon and moved slowly towards the sky in loose formation, looked like they arched somewhat before disappearing. Afterwards light 3, 4 and 5 followed the same path separately. I felt I was viewing them from quite a distance away. All 6 seemed to disappear into the sky. Looked like helicopters, but no sound and way too many too close. At first I thought they were missiles of some sort!
