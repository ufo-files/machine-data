(GS) I was sleeping and was awoken to what I believe was some sort of alien encounter

I was sleeping when I woke up and heard a loud buzzing noise. I attempted to open my eyes but could hardly open one, the laptop was open on the opposite side of the bed so I had some light and in front of me appeared to be a small humanoid. I then realized my mouth was completely open and I was unable to move it nor make a sound. I tried screaming for help but all that came out was some faint mumbles. Then I attempted to remove what was in my mouth with my arms but for reasons I cant explain I couldnt move them. I could move my legs but it was as if I was a fish out of water at that point. Soon after I was kicking my legs around my eyes seemed to close automatically and when I re-opened them everything was back to normal...besides my extremely dry mouth. I immediately looked at the clock, it read 4:27 A.M. I was extremely frightened to move, I was unsure what had just happened. Im not sure if this was some sort of dream I was having, and I truthfully don't remember any dream of mine, or if it was indeed some sort of alien encounter.
