Two bright lights flying in perfect formation.

So just after sunset as I was looking around I noticed two bright objects that appeared out of nowhere. They seemed to be in perfect formation with one about 65 degrees above the horizontal above the other. No sounds or noise was made, and there was a large group of people there who saw it. They made a very,very slow turn and then all of the sudden disappeared as fast as they appeared. They seemed to occupy a very small area relative to their speed. The general area was eastern towards Wyoming.
