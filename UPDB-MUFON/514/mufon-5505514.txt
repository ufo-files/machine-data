Was at traffic lights stopped, when this huge shadow went over my car

I was stopped at traffic lights on erindale road and balcatta road, when just out of the blue a shadow covering a four lane street plus buildings flew over, I saw the shape of it, before it actually made me duck my head and I looked around to the other cars they did the same thing as well. It's shape which appeared in the middle of the intersection, was a huge triangle , and it actually was quite fast but just enough to see the shape, it just went over in a few seconds maybe ten. I was taken aback that I called hubby at 12:40pm, the incident happened at 12:32pm cause I automatically looked at my car clock, we had an event like this many many years ago in duncraig where there were quite a few reports about this huge huge shadow going over the suburb. Well, as I drove through intersection I tried to look in review mirror to see if I could identify anything eg. Plane or a very huge bird, but didn't see anything and we had clear skies all around, and definitely didn't hear any sound especially on the size of the shadow it cast, I have googled to see if anybody else observed anything but haven't read anything about this incident, here's hoping some one else's saw and reported it, just so I don't think I'm going mad.
