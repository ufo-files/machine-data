{rjl} 2 spheres racing in erratic flight patterns, defying physics

It was 0430 in the morning. I was standing at my back door, observing the sky. I awoke with a strange urge to go look up at the stars. I noticed what appeared to be 2 stars, outside of the atmosphere. Suddenly, they both accelerated faster than anything I have ever witnessed. They then split-up, and proceeded to fly in what I can only describe as erratic and gravity defying. They would stop suddenly, hover, ascend, descend. I was now fully engulfed in this event. The objects would make 90 degree turns, without de-accelerating. They made circles at one point, and one of the spheres disappeared abruptly. It almost "phased out." The remaining sphere then made a series of erratic movements, and then shot directly upwards and disappeared into the darkness of space. I am open to the possibility of "others" flying in our skies. I am having great difficulty in understanding what made me jump out of bed in the dead of a New England historic winter, and stand in shorts as I stared up, anticipating something. It was as if I was being called out to see this show. It was over as fast as it began. I walked into the street hoping to find someone else who had just witnessed what I had. There was no one outside awake. The speed, and maneuverability of these objects far exceeds anything I have ever seen before or since.
