Bright object in the s/w not the usual.

Early on the morning of the 27/8/2013 3:30AM i went out onto my 2nd story flat kitchen roof to relax and watch the sky as i very often do to look at the constellations sometimes the odd bit of stardust and passing satellites. Just before the sighting i was observing the hunter in the south-east as i turned my head to find another constellation a pea green flash caught my eye in the south west.I locked on to it as i did it was pulsating from pea green to a dirty white then more of a burnt orange rather than a copper then to a dull gold more like brass in colour. When i first seen it,it was static then started to move slowly then stopped again after maybe 30Seconds. A short beam of light omitted from the object, but this beam did not touch the ground then the object moved very quick and came to a stop almost as quick. Distance traveled unknown as no reference points. Then the object droped about 30degrees,again the beam was seen then the object moved to the south-east still in plain sight,as amazing as all this was the next bit really made me think deep. What i seen next was really weird the object came to a stop still pulsating and just seemed to cloak and then nothing. Many things went through my head as to what it was. This was no airplane no chopper no hot air balloon because they are not allowed to fly in the U.K. at night and no blimp. I watch the night sky on a regular basis and can explain away many,many things after the event i went back inside and sat down for maybe an hour thinking did i see what i seen!!! This is my first sighting and hope it wont be my last. This object was under intelligent control far beyond any technological capabilities that we have at this time.
