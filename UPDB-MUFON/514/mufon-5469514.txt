{td}Strange craft with two bright lights and a blinking white light, but moved unlike an airplane

Driving home from a movie with my wife, when she noticed this light that was hovering/very slowly moving over the city. It had two bright lights a distance apart that would make one think of an airplane, but they were very bright and big. It had a flashing white light as well, which further made us feel maybe it was an airplane, but this didn't behave like one. It hovered and seemed to be quite low. Our view was blocked by a small hill as we were driving and when we got back to where it should have been in view again it was gone. 

We drove home, where we happen to have a pretty good view of the town. My wife noticed it again but flying far off along the horizon shockingly low to where it looked perfectly in line with the horizon. It then just stopped moving. And we lost sight of it as it blended in with the rest of the lights in the horizon.

Very strange. Don't know if it's some weird man made aircraft or something. But definately not anything normal.
