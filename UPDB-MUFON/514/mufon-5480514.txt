{nd} Large Glowing Orb

I was walking out of house to my car parked at the end of my driveway. I noticed there was an orange light about 30 yards down my driveway near a not longer powered dusk to dawn light: It grabbed my attention because it appeared as if the light was on but I noticed it was infact the light was not on. The orange glowing orb was the source of the light. It hovered for at least one minute and made no sound. I didn't fear what I was looking at, but I felt I personally was intentionally being observed or watched. It was about halfway up the treeline and flew sideways behind the trees and disappeared out of my sight. I thought I might observe the object again as I drove away from my home in a similiar direction, but I did not see the object again.
