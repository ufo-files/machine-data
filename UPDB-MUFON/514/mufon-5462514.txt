{cak} I saw a fire ball fall and where it fell

On the 4th of Oct. I saw a fire ball falling. At first I thought it was going to hit me but I couldn't look away. It did not hit me but it went down just N/W of me just behind some hills. I heard a thump when it hit and a I call it an energy release. There was a light but light does not have an edge it just fades off giving way to the dark. This light had a definite edge It was fully bright right up to the edge and it was dome shaped. That is why I think it was an energy release. I put it at about 1 1/2 to 2 mi. wide This was not a meteor I have seen them come down before and this fire ball was moving much to slow to be that. There are no people where it fell and it is hard to get to.
