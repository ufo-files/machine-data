derigible-like shape only no horizontal or vertical stabilizers

November 16, 2011 
Wednesday night about 7:30 PM (2130Z)

Weather - a very thin layer of cirrus clouds moving from west to east. Height could not be determined, but I could see the star Vega through the clouds.

While in my hot tub in my back yard I saw a 1/2 dozen or so aircraft fly overhead, a satelite travel from north to south and two meteors.

I also saw a cylindrical, derigible-like shape craft, at the same altitude as the thin cloud layer. The craft traveled quickly from north to south in a straight line. Its speed was about the same as the lower flying aircraft that I observed earlier (about 250 knots). The base of the cloud layer was illuminated by the city lights of Medina. As the craft headed south it passed through the cloud layer making it visible as a gray-black fuzzy silhouette. 

The derigible-like shaped craft had no lights, made no sound, and had no visible wings or stabilizers. No engine pods or nacelles could be seen. No windows or markings were visible.
