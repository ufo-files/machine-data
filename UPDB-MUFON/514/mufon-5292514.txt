Dark Circular Object spotted with 3 very dim lights in a triangle fashion moving north

My wife and I were sitting outside on our front step facing North. It was very dark around 11:30p. My wife pointed out an object moving North directly above us. At first I couldn't see it but then my eyes picked up on the movement and it became clearer. It didn't seem far away... like 500 feet maybe? My first thought was that it was some kind of drone, but it was a dead silent night and the object made no noise whatsoever -- absolutely nothing. The other thing was there was something odd about how dark it was... whatever it was it seemed like it was trying to be hide in the night sky. 

The UFO was a flat circular object with three extremely dim lights on the bottom of it. The lights were lined up like the 3 points of a triangle. But the lights were so dim they appeared more like they were a byproduct of some energy form... and not something it was purposely creating to be visible. The object was probably the size of a quarter and the height was very difficult to detect. I'm assuming the height of 500 feet just based on how fast it was moving. It's speed was extremely constant and the trajectory was a perfect straight line. It didn't appear to change elevation.

I don't know if it was an alien craft, I suspect there's another reasonable explanation, but it was striking enough that I thought I'd report it.
