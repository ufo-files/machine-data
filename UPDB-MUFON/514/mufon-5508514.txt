flashig light in sky. Never moved but had random flashes. Was not a star plane or satelite

I live across from my parents house. We were watching the Seattle Seahawks and Denver Broncos pre season game. At the end of the 3rd quarter I went to my house to get a bottle of water. When walking back I noticed this light to the west flashing randomly. I thought it was a plane at first with the red and green flashing lights. It was to bright to be a star and it wasnt moving. I watched it for about 10 minutes and it didn't move and I noticed other colors flashing. I am very familiar with aircraft lights. I use to be a Helicopter mechanic in the U.S. Marines. I showed my dad and we watched it for a while. I got a pair of binoculars and we watched it change colors over and over flashing in a random pattern. Aircraft lights are very steady. After the game was over the light was still there. The object was very far away and my phone wouldnt pick it up. I got out my Nikon D90 and put it on a tri pod and recorded it for a little bit. The object never moved after about 2 hours. I had to call it a night so I stopped watching it.
