Metallic Orb/Sphere, not very big

While waiting at a bustop on Speedway Blvd and Palo Verde, I observed what looked like a ball or circle in the NW direction. At first I thought it could have been a metallic type balloon. But the ball shaped firgure got closer and stayed at a low elevation. I figured a balloon would have floated up, but this orb or sphere stayed on a fixed elevation and headed from NW to SE toward the Rincon Mountains. The movement was not erratic, it did not change elevation, just a shiny, metallic sphere with no windows, wings, rotors, wheels, just a smooth round shiny ball moving about 50-100 feet above the power lines from when I saw it in the NW sky as it headed at a moderate speed SE toward the Rincon Mountains.
