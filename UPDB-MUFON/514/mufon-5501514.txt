6/29/14.Took my dogs out back to go the bathroom. My backyard faces north. I looked up to the east and saw a orange yellow light moving slowly to the north. Light would fade slightly but stay visible. At one point it looked as if a small red light fell from it. No fireworks were going at that time and a Roman candle wouldn't last that long. No sound quietly moved across the tree line enough for me to see. A report similar to this from grosse point. Good hour plus from my house. They said it was south of them. Right before one of my trees could block my sight it disintegrated. At first I thought maybe it was a lantern you light with a match and let go. But those move straight up and the wind was dead that night. This all happened in 20 seconds and it was too intriguing to run back in the house to get my phone to record it. The picture I posted with this is from today 7/1 and that is my backyard and tree line the light was visible from. Small Grey cloud at the 11 o'clock position was where the light disappeared.
