I have and continue to observe Strange looking flying craft

I have seen objects for years. I have never reported any of my observations.
I continue to see various types of craft, no matter where I am.
My latest was back in April I think, I watched a very large slow moving triangle fly over the top of my house. Moving very slowly at night and quite low, less than 500ft. Hardly any cloud cover.
It took nearly 3 minuets to move out of site and it banked to the right.
There was also an helicopter, most possibly military, we have 3 RAF camps near by. It came in from the NW moving in from the left of the triangle it got very close to the left rear of the triangle before moving back to the NW.
There were three large lights on the rear of the craft, and the helicopter was about the same size of one light.

For some reason through all the years that I have seen these objects, I never think of taking a photo. It1s like my mind gets blocked from thinking of taking any pictures.
I have a cousin who also has the same experiences, he also has said the same thing about not thinking about taking a picture. We find that pretty strange.

Anyway, not sure if anyone is interested in hearing all the other sightings my cousin and I have had, but you could contact me anytime.

Regards.
