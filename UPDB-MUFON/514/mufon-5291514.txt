Large number of lights, traveling single file

I went out to get the chickens this morning and there was a continuous stream of flying objects (50-75) with lights traveling NE from the SW. They appeared to be silently gliding across the early morning sky. There were so many I had time to go in and wake my wife so she could see them too. Breathtaking.
