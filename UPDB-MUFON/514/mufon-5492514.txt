{rjl} During severe weather, took picture and objects were seen in foto.

On Tuesday evening on June 23, 2015 my brother was at WalMart in Islandia, NY. He went outside around 8:30pm and started taking pictures with his cell phone at the evening sky. There was severe thunderstorm happening and the sky was changing colors. When he observed his pictures, he noticed something in one of the photos. Three orb like shaped crafts, purple in color. Also, when you zoom in on the picture, you can see a silhouette of a triangle shape around the orbs.
