Solid light traveling fast and high over NAS Whidbey

I was outside looking at the stars at 1130. I noticed several small aircraft in the area. They had the usual stobe lights an the were all flying under the clouds. I spotted a solid white light light traveling SW to NE. It had no flashing lights. It appeared to be the same size as the aircraft in the area, but it was moving very fast. It was also much higher than the aircraft. It eventually passed out of sight above the clouds. It was not a meteor. There was no tail. I don't think it was a satellite because the ones Ive seen are kind of tumbling and reflecting light. There was no aircraft from NAS Whidbey flying either.
