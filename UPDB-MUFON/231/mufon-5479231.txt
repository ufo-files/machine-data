{nd} Glowing orbs over Red Rock Texas

Sitting on my front porch Thanksgiving night, I saw a yellowish orange light through the trees in my front yard. It was moving from South to North and at first, I thought it was fireworks. Taking a closer look, this was nothing like I have ever seen. It was yellow in the center and deep red on the top. It was pulsating, moving in a steady flight path and a steady speed like an airplane. This was NO airplane. I watched this object fade into darkness in the North sky. Moments later, another identical object followed suit in the same flight path and speed. There was no sound at all. I estimate these objects were maybe a mile high and less than a mile from my location. Unfortunately, I did not have my camera that night and shared my story with friends. Saturday night, the 26th, once again I saw a string of 6 of the same objects flying at the same altitude, same speed, same flight path. It was on Saturday night the 26th when I was able to photograph and get a video of 2 of them. The video is shaky. I had to walk a little to get a better angle. As you'll see in the video, after the first one disappeared, a second one came in behind, following the same path. I saw a total of six of these objects Saturday night and no sightings since.
