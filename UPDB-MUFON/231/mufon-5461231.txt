{rjl} One white orb witnessed slight calculated movement spinning or rotating and moving also releasing smaller red and blinking objects

Both me and the other witness walked into my backyard. Ive been seeing UFOS for almost a year frequently so having a witness to this sighting was exciting for me. When we entered the backyard we were drawn to the moving lights in the sky, I pointed out the hovering, pulsating, bright, white orb that was southwest in the sky. It appeared to be rotating, and made slight directional movements in which after it released a number or other smaller red glowing objects that would blink in correlation to their source they would make formations in different shapes such as a triangle and a square then a trapezoid. I've witnessed before these same lights in different locations around Ohio for example i witnessed these lights form a star in the sky after i had asked them to show me something crazy, following making the star and calculated formation they then made the star spin a clockwise fashion and then followed the vehicle we were in for an hour and a half to two hours with two other witnesses after i asked that they follow us.
