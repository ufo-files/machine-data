{dr}Bright Object in Sky Looked like a Head light in the sky

I was looking out my window in Birch Bay,WA on August 19, 2012. I have a view of the ocean. At about 8:20 I saw a bright white light over the bay. I was talking to a friend on the phone and joked that I was looking at a UFO. I reached down to pet my dog and in a matter of seconds, there was nothing in the sky. I did not see it fly away it was just not there anymore. I thought it was kind of strange and then today my friend sent me a link showing that other people in this same area had seen things like this in past month also. It did not look like a plane because it was not moving.
