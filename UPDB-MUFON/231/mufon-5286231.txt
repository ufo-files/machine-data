Circle objects extremely bright white light seen off I-65 - and then vanishes.

I was driving back from a vacation with four other friends. We were on I-65 northbound in Scott Co. near Scottsburg, on our way to my mom's house in Columbus to spend the night before heading all the way home to Chicago. There was not a cloud in the sky - completely blue skies.

To the right of the car, high in the sky but not crazy far away, I see a white circle object that is extremely bright. It's pulsating and really, really bright. It reminded me of when the sun is going down and reflects off a plane. But staring at the object didn't hurt your eyes like that would.

I was sitting shotgun and pointed out the object to the friends in the back seat (it was to the right of the car so the driver and driver-side back seat couldn't see) to the person sitting in seat directly behind me and the person in the middle of the back seat. They both saw it instantly and we watched for about 5 seconds before it just, poofed. It kind of fissled out, but like it was mostly gone and then just gone. Just like that. We made sure we all saw the same thing and then looked for it for the next 15 mins and nothing at all. Again, it was perfectly blue skies and we should have been able to find what it was.

Relevantly a boring siting but I've been really frustrated since seeing it - as it was definitely something that wasn't a plane that just - disappeared.
