cylindrical craft spotted

Something interesting happened just now (about 15-20 minutes ago). I was walking home from work (it was about 19:35ish), when I looked up, Something I normally do while walking home (I live in a small rural town in South Africa), o see where the moon is and what planets & constellations I can spot. The lights was in a single row, it looked like it was a single craft with 15 lights at the bottom. From my point of view it was about 5 cm in length, and was about 6-7 km away from me. I tried following it's path, and was able to see it for about 5 minutes before I lost it behind a thick cloud.
