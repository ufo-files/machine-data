{td} Saw group of UFOS hovering over power plant.

In 1971 while attending school in Monroe, Louisiana I went outside of town with my girlfriend. We went to a known make out place that was on a hill overlooking a power plant. While we were doing what college kids do when in the make out place, we noticed four disk shaped objects hovering over the power plant. We sat up and started watching them and the were stationary as they hovered over the power plant.. After about 6-7 minutes we saw three more objects coming from our right in formation and they stopped near the first four. The seven of them sat there for about 10 minutes. There was no sound and the UFOs seemed to be completely stationary as we watched. We observed them as they started to move off to our left and they moved into a BV formation as they moved away. The formation was a single disk followed by two disks followed by four disks. The entire experience was very weird and a bit scary. This experience really made me think about our role in the universe because it was clear these "UFOs" were not of this planet. Pretty scary but more interesting than scary.
