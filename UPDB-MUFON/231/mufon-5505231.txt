{rjl} Craft travelled in a straight path silently overhead, lights were solid white then turned red and blinked when directly overhead.

Over the last month, I had been noticing a blinking hovering object in the sky and was trying to figure out what it was, paying more attention to the sky. Walking towards the bike path through the field by Patriot's Square, I saw a craft flying just above treeline. It was completely silent and transparent, all I could see were the lights that seemed to be on two wings with one in the center, one in the front and one on the back. The lights were solid white and as the craft then flew over my head, the lights turned red and blinked as if acknowledging my presence, then turned white again and continued flying at a quicker pace. I continued my walk home dumbfounded.
