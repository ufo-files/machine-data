{jpg} The ufos asended up through the trees about 3 km away from me and moved across the sky until gone.

On October 21 2014 at about 9:20, me and my girlfriend were sitting on my couch watching TV. I looked out my sliding door window out onto the front porch and could see a glowing object moving upward through the trees about 3 km away. I asked my girlfriend 'hey, can you see that?' I moved onto the porch and watched it move through the sky in a very straight prominent line. Like it knew where it was going, never going off course. This happened 5 different times with 5 different orbs. The objects were large and glowing red, yellow, and orange. Once again all the objects moved in the exact same direction on the exact same line. They all looked exactly the same. I've never seen anything like this.
