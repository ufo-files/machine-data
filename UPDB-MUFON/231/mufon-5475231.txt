{cak} Alien abduction/ genetic modification

It was the day I was born, I vaguely remember it, but I keep getting flashbacks to being in the incubator. I was in some sort of incubator type thing with tubes flowing to certain parts of my body. I look around to see what I assume to be doctors wearing strange breathing masks. The doctors had a grey-green tint to their skin and large eyes with a line in the middle, similar to those of a snake. I heard the 'doctors' say this ominous line, "Subject 627, our Heavy Trooper" and the next thing I know I'm back in the hospital. Ever since that day weird things have been happening to me and I've gotten a feeling that I'm meant for something more, like some sort of intergalactic warfare. I can take apart and put back together any weapon without having any education or knowledge of it whatsoever. I am abnormally strong too. I always have a feeling that there are others like me, others that are "Heavy Troopers" and we're part of something more. I have no doubt in my mind that the aliens were Orion aliens.
