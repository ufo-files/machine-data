Then the beams of light disappeared and the starlight object on the left veers to the left and up, the star like object on the right veered to the right and up and as they both did that the first starlight object produced a flash of light and then they were all gone. No sound whatsoever. The sky was clear, the sky was dark and I guess I just happen to be looking at the right spot at the right time.  It was the flash of light at the end that made me decide to report this. Wow
