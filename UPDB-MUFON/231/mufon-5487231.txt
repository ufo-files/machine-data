{nd} Myself and two friends were driving down Anderson Rd hill heading to the river. When we got towards close to the stop sign we noticed 3 bright lights in the shape of a triangle.

It was around 8:30 to 9pm,my two friends and I were driving down Anderson Rd heading towards the Little Miami River.

I noticed it first and said to my friends,"Oh my God..look at those lights!" I knew immediately that it was a UFO. It was an indescribable feeling of excitement and fear all in one.

When we got to the stop sign we could clearly see 3 bright lights over the river. The lights were at each point of the triangle. We couldn't believe what we were seeing. We sat and watched it hovering there for several minutes and then it traveled down the path of the river. We followed it up the river for several miles and then we thought we were crazy for following it and we stopped. We lost sight of it when it continued down the river. It's an event in my life that I will never forget!
