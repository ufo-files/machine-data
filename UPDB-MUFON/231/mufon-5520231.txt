Triangle object rotating 3 bright lights on each corner and center changing colors

Isaw an object in the sky which at first I thought was maybe a group of ballons but quickly discounted this. I was triagular shaped and revolving slowly with 3 bright lights. One on each corner. The center was changing color constantly and it would move to the right and left at times then just hover for a few minutes. I would drop down and stop then move back to its original position and stop. This went on for at least twenty minutes. I then took 2 picture with my cellphone and a video which didn't appear in the filming cause I was moving it around hoping it would be in the lense opening. It then shot up to the left and blinked out. I know it was not ballons or a helicopter or any other thing. It was awesome to see however.
