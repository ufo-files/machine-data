{nd} 3-4 stadium like florescent orange lights in triange pattern moving south right over lake togo

I noticed a strange light fixture and glow to the south of the grocery store in the corner of Orange Blossom Trail and Poinciana Blvd, just south of Kissimmee Fl about 930pm. Once I started driving south on Pleasant Hill Rd I noticed the lights which were in a triangular/squate pattern, hovering right over the north part of lake Toho, about 1,500-2,000 ft high. Lights were a stadium like orange fluorescent glow, not anything that seemed like a plane nor helicopter, no navigation lights or anything normally seen at night in a flying plane or helicopter. I noticed that the object was slowly heading south and slowly rotating on its axis , but once it I turned into my subdivision, I lost sight over the trees. Seems the object was starting to head SE , but then it disappeared once I got home and looked towards the lake Toho area. Never seen anything like it. Not easy to dissimulate size since it was dark, possibly about 40-60ft+ across. Kissimmee Airport is just north of here and Im used to seeing cessna's , helicopters and private jets flying in at night and this resembled nothing like a plane. Lots of people driving south on Pleasant Hill Rd saw this im sure as I was surrounded by traffic..Very interesting !!
