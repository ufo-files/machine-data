{dr}Trail of orange lights hovering and traveling diagonally then going away

I was at the batting cages in Burtonsville, Maryland standing in the parking lot. I noticed the odd color of the lights and I thought it was a plane at first, but they were traveling in like groups of three and moving not like any plane I have ever seen. There were numerous amounts of the lights. They would disappear after a while. When I first noticed them, I thought they were meteors coming right at me. But then i saw them moving awkwardly and knew it was something totally out of the ordinary. I lost sight of it when I went inside the batting cages, they were gone when I came back to my car.
