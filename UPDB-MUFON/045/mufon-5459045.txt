{rjl} Yellow, illuminated, non flashing orb travels west to east across sky.

A yellow glowing orb traversed the northern sky from west to east. It was about twice the apparent size of Jupiter, which is the southwestern sky. As a reference point, this object was two to three times the brightness of Jupiter as it was observed this evening in the southwestern sky. We observed the object for 30-40 seconds as it proceeded from West to East. It had no flashing lights and no other notable features or lights. It was a basic bright orb. It traveled silently and did not emit any jet engine sounds, nor were there any propeller sounds. As an astro-photographer I can easily identify planets, stars, planes, helicopters, and satellites. This object did not resemble any known aircraft. We have observed planes of varying sizes in the night sky and in our area they are always accompanied by a sound, this object emitted no sound what-so-ever.
