Triangular color changing

My fiance saw it first. Like a color changing light in the sky. I zoomed in and recorded it. He went and got my small telescope. I'd only used it once years ago so it took a while to figure it out. When I finally got it focused, using 22mm lens, I lined the view fi Der up and took a peak. It was beautiful. Triangular but not straight edged. At the three corners were wisps. It changed colors. Mostly red and blue but some greens and faint red orange. It was definitely not man made. A told my fiance to cone out but he was busy. By the time he got out, it was growing smaller. He could not see it. It was just a star at that point. I hovered for the most part. When it moved at the end it had to be away from us because it went from a small but clear object in the telescope to a star in under a minute. Some if the technical questions like elevation I guessed at.
