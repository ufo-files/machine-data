{rjl} Bright spot in daytime sky appeared stationary suddenly vanished.

On a bright sunny afternoon day witnessed a very strong reflection coming off an object in the sky. My first impression that it was a small aircraft such as a Cessna or similar. As I watched it over the course of 15 to 20 seconds it appeared to not move but just sit in the sky. Eventually it appeared to bank or turn and the reflection eclipsed. I watched and expected to see the silhouette of some kind of known aircraft, but saw nothing.
