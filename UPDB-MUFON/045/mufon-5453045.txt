Where I worked at we would see Apache helicopters frequently. 2-3 at a time. We never knew where they were coming from or why but they came from SE traveling N. On the day I witness the UFO I had just left work and was traveling down hwy 565 towards hwy 146 in Baytown. As I came around a small curve and could see ahead about a 1/2 mile I noticed a large upright rectangular object hovering about 120' above some power lines. The suface was a grey, dull color with no visible lights, windows or doors. It just hovered there. I took my eyes away from it for a second and when I looked back up it was gone. Within a minute or two there were 2 Apache helicopters flying over my location. I was working south of Interstate 10 and that is where we would always see the helicopters go towards.
