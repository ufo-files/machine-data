{cak} 3 Lights in triangle formation

On 10/10/12, I heard the Helicopter that tends to fly around Wilmington with its spotlight. I look up trying to see where it was and as I was watching it cross my path I saw 3 lights in formation or perhaps a triangle fly at high speeds pass over the helicopter. No sound was heard besides the helicopter but the lights or object was flying at great speed with the helicopter not noticing it. It was flying at a straight V pattern toward Carolina Beach. I lost track of it pretty fast due to the tree line. No idea what it was but if it was 3 jets I would have heard them before or after.
