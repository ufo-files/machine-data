{cak} Fireball seen over Southside Easton, PA

Last night 9/6/2013, I was walking my dog around 9:00 p.m. (I took video at 8:58 p.m.) when I saw what I thought was a hot air balloon high in the sky, because I could see flames. This was moving in a straight line and too fast to be a balloon and way too high in the sky. Then I thought that maybe it was a plane on fire but it wasnt falling and it kept moving across the sky. I took out my phone and videotaped it. I was really expecting for whatever it was to suddenly explode but it just kept moving across the sky until it the fire either went out or it just disappeared.
