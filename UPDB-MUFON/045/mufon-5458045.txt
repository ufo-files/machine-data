{rjl} Triangular shaped craft with no wings and a flat panel of lights at the apex/front of craft

This looked like the craft I reported seeing in July 2019. It was very similar, as it was flying around in circles changing direction like the one seen in July. It was triangular shape with 2 lights pulsating on the front horizontal panel, a flashing red and blue light on the sides and a white flashing light on the underside back end of the craft. It had no wings. It looked like a triangle with the apex cut off by the lights panel.
