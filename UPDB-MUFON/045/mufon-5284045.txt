Orange Lights Over Westerville

I was traveling north on route 3 heading into Genoa township from Westerville proper. An orange light traveled southbound over route 3 and directly over my vehicle. As I turned left into my neighborhood I observed another orange light and I quickly realized it wasn't alone. As I reached my residence I got out of my vehicle looked to the east. I counted at least eight of these orange lights moving in formation and changing direction. What was most striking was some were traveling horizontally. These were not balloons, lanterns, or flares.

I would have thought that these were drones but the distance these would have had to cover would have been from the Westerville area heading east out to New Albany and all the way to the south end of Westerville. This might be perhaps 8 to 10 square miles.

In the initial video sequence it looks like the lights are blinking but this is from the tree. I have a longer video available if necessary but I wanted to snip this and get this reported as quickly as possible.
