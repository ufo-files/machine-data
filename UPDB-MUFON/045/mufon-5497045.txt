{dr}a chrome like circular object in the sky

On the 30th of october in Roberts, Idaho I walked outside of my house which is a few streets from the city hall of Roberts. I looked up in the sky and saw a glow next to a cloud. I kept looking at it thinking it could be a balloon, plan, or even a star. However, the more I looked at it it gave a gleaming of chrome probably from the reflexion from the sun. I quickly grabbed my friend from inside the house, and he said "it's a star" . However, at the time the incident happen there were no other stars around the object. the object hovered in the same position for the time we saw and took pictures of it. the clouds moved but the object remain fixed and motion less. we had to go to work and we stopped looking at the object, but once we picked up our third coworker and mentioned the incident to him he pointed out he also saw the same object in the sky. we had no specific feelings towards the incident and no side effects from it.
