Watching the Dragon 9 launch

Last evening I was watching the Dragon 9 launch at 7:27 pm and to the left of it, I spotted what I thought was an aircraft. I watched it and it did not move. It had a row of blinking lights of white red and green. This remained stationary for a good half hour before I went back inside. I tried photographing it and tried binoculars but never got a good view. Several aircraft were in the sky well below it. Did anyone else spot this?
