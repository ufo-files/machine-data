{cak} Object with unusual array of lights and no sound

We were staying at a motel in Edmonds for the night and I am an avid sky watcher. I was looking west out of picture window out towards the Puget Sound and noticed a pretty bright array of lights coming in over the sound. I quickly grabbed my Sony camcorder which I take everywhere and started shooting. The object came in first looks like headed east. Then it turned towards me heading south. I zoomed in and it turned again towards the west and disappeared over the motel roof line. All the while I listened for an engine and there was none.
