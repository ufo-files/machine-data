{jpg} 3 bright objects with sequential glowing lights over gulf of mexico

My wife and I were sitting on the balcony of our condo overlooking the Gulf of Mexico and Mobile Bay at sunset. After the sun was below the horizon we observed what appeared to be 3 glowing/reflective orange-yellow objects above the horizon in the southwest sky. We could not tell the distance but the direction was towards the entrance to Mobile Bay. The lights appeared to fly slowly in a line in a southern direction and gradually descended below the horizon. It was hard to tell if they were descending or if they just appeared too be from the rotation of the earth. We observed the objects through binoculars and they each had what appeared to be a brighter glowing light of the same orange/yellow color that moved sequentially from front to rear and then started over. We took a series of photos which I am attaching. There was no other sounds or objects associated with this sighting.
