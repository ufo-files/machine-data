{cak} Mysterious Blinking Lights

On June 17 of this year, I observed and recorded two mysterious blinking lights (orbs) outside my place of residence. The exact location of the lights was right above the carport. While recording, I stood on the front porch, a short distance away. It was almost as if the orbs were "winking" at me, flaring up over the edge of the carport roof. As you can tell from the video, it was raining at the time. One orb appeared under the brightest one. This is not the lone appearance or sighting of this phenomenon. The lights have appeared in the exact same location multiple times, going back to late last year (at least that's when I first became aware of them) except originally, there was only one light. Although I am the sole witness to this particular sighting, a family member is an additional witness to the other sightings which took place last year. On those occasions, I walked under the carport roof to investigate, but was unable to find the source of the lights.
