[dr]at least 4 large glowing orange object below cloud cover witnessed by 7

On Friday, June 14, 2012 myself, father, brother, and sister were enjoying an early Fathers Day dinner out on our deck. Around 10:15pm my sister and brother came back outside on the deck and immediate my brother blurted out, "What the heck is that?!?" Sitting in my gazebo with my dad I was sure I was going to have to tell him that it was just a plane. It was a cloudy night so you couldnt see any stars or the moon. I bent down to get a better view and off to the west looking I immediately saw 3 hovering orange lights in a triangle formation. The lights didn't move and were as bright as street lights viewed a block away. I knew I wasnt seeing a plane, helicopter, or anything familiar. I quickly exited the gazebo and ran down the deck stairs toward the levy to get a better look. As the four of us ran, I called to my neighbor who was outside with 2 children. All three of them ran over and we watched what we now noticed were 4 orange lights hovering. I tried to record them with my camera phone but as anyone with a camera phone knows that even taking a clear picture of the moon is almost impossible. We watched for a minute as they seem to ascended into the clouds one by one. Im not sure how long they were present below the clouds but we saw them for about a minute and a half total.
