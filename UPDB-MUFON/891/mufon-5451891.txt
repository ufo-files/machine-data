Black object over Kansas City

There were approximately 7 of us outside our VFW. We were having a drive through free produce pickup.
My friend and I were chatting and she looked up and said "what is that". I looked up and witnessed a black object. I then hollered over at my husband who was talking with a couple men and asked him what the hell is that? He said he had no idea. (My husband is an Airforce vet).
My friend and I ran upstairs to our top porch and grabbed some binoculars. I took a few pictures and we viewed it with the binoculars.
It was moving North a bit faster then. It was solid black and roundish. Through my binoculars I could see a blueish tint around the bottom. 
It was silent. 
The weather was cloudy and a bit windy. The object did not move with the wind. It was on its own course. 
It seemed to disappear going North.
