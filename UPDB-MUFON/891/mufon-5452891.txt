Silver reflective cylinder about 1 football field long with no visible windows, lights, wings, tail or differentiation of fore and aft.

While driving home I noticed an unusually bright reflective object in the sky in a north east direction. The sun reflected along the length of the object. It was a cylinder about as long as a football field. It was in the sky at about 45 degrees with no obstruction on a clear sunny day. I clearly saw the object for about two minutes. At the time it didn’t move at all and I saw no indication of a propulsion method. It was a smooth silver narrow cylinder with no wings, tail, engines, windows or doors that I could see. The front and back were very similar with a rounded but not pointed edge. I didn’t take a picture because I was driving.
