One big fire ball.

I was up on the 5th floor of Westchester Medical center looking out the window toward 75north. All of a sudden I looked up and saw a HUGE fireball, My first reaction was maybe it was a small comet burning up. My second reaction was we were about to be bombed, then it seperated into three pieces, the middle piece remained larger than the two pieces on either ide. They all maintained equal distance from either as they spread apart. Then they dissapeared and as fast as they went two came back and then two more appeared to form a diamond, then a fifth appeared on the bottom right. the two on the left and right dissapeared and appeared lower, then bottom left light moved diagonolly toward upper right. I tried to get my video camera on my droid to work but it wouldn't penetrate the window. I ran down five flights of stairs out the back door and as I turned the corner two were left litterally zipping back and forth and then they were gone as fast as they appeared. This went on for atleast ten minutes.
