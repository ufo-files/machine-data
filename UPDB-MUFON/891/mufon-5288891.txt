Craft witnessed in backyard in July 2020

Husband and I were laying in bed, I notice a red blinking light flashing in a triangle outside to the east of the house. I go up to my window and take a quick video (second video I uploaded) couldn’t see much so I rush outside. The craft is now hovering out back but is a saucer shape but the lights are flashing like they were going in a pattern around the craft, green blue, yellow and red, it slowly moved by, no noise, it had a metallic appearance that was almost like looking through water or as if it was see through. Totally crazy, and we watched it for 10 min in awe, wish I would of got more video, husband, teenagers and little girls all saw it with us. Crazy thing is we haven’t stopped seeing them since this day. Some weeks I see two or three. I have a bunch of video footage as well. We also saw a orb fly by the next night. I have that video as well.
