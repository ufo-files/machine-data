{nd} Orange light dissapears revealing cylindrical probe like craft.

I was grilling out at home and Observed a bright orange light and knew right away it was not a plane. The sky was overcast so it was against greyish white clouds. The object was moving East to West very slowly. After about a minute or so the light faded to nothing and I could clearly see the object against the clouds. I can only describe it as cylindrical and vertical like a probe or something. I watched it for a few minutes then it faded into the clouds. This was very exciting for I have never observed a craft before.
