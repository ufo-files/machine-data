A friend & I were camping out in the woods beside my house on a summer night in around July . There was a store & small service station a mile to the west of my house . We were in our early teens ,around 12-14. Our mode of transportation was bicycles . My friend had a slow leak in his front bike tire .Around 11pm we decided to ride to the store & put air in his tire . about half a mile from my house we saw a red light like on a plane but it wasn't blinking & no noise & no other lights . We thought it wasn't moving so we stopped on our bikes . Approx. 3 seconds later it started moving north slowly . No noise or blinking . We started back moving & a few seconds later it stopped ./ We went through this routine several times . It appeared it was watching us as we were it .Finally it moved @ a faster pace to the north & disappeared . We made it to the store & pumped his tire up . It was @ a 5 road intersection . We thought we may get in trouble if the police saw us out that late . Weird we had seen no other cars out the whole time . We turned back headed east toward my house & my friend looked back when we got to where the road I lived on started & my friend looked back ,slammed on his brakes & hollered " GD LOOK (my name) !!!" I stopped fast & looked back . There was a big orangish yellow ball sitting there almost as low as we were hovering . No sound . Just like a really big full moon rising but close & just hovering still . We were looking west then . It seemed within no more than 50 feet away . Orangish/yellow like swirling inside slightly . We kept talking & asking each other what it was and making comments that it was the same thing that had watched us all the way there . Very still & quite .Not sure how long it sat there . I'd say @ least a minute or two then Fastly headed west . Again no sound . Like it was there & real quickly got smaller & gone in a few seconds . We took off to my house as fast as possible ,went in,woke my parents up & told them . My friend wanted to go home he was so scared . So we loaded his bike in the trunk of Mama's car & took him home . We knew nothing about missing time until later on TV shows . Not sure really what happened to us . I've had bad dreams of gravity stopping & people holding me down all my life since .
