{cak} three three red pulsating lights were observed orbiting around each other then forming into one light then dissapearing

Around 10 pm on Saturday September 27th, me and three other friends were in the parking lot outside of the Ridgemark golf course when I first noticed one singular red light. I told my friends that it looked weird since there were no blue lights or any other indicators of it being an aircraft. No more than five minutes later I notice that the lone red light had now multiplied into three lights. I immediately tell my friends to look at what I'm seeing. I was able to catch a 30 second video of the red lights orbiting around each other changing shape from a triangle to a straight line, then, finally, dissappearing. The whole scene lasted about two minutes.
