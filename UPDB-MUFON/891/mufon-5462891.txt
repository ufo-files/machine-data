{jpg} 1968 distant sighting which led to our chase and encounter.

In 1968 (summer) my boyfriend and I watched from my apartment balcony this object fly from one end of Las Vegas to another and back several times and into the hills. It hovered, changed direction, and flew extremely fast from one point to another several times. It was bright and we could see the activity very well. The next night, we saw the object and decided to chase it. It was probably between 7 and 9 p.m. We were not at all afraid but I think we were excited to investigate. We drove out into the desert, parked the car where we could see the object. We got out of the car and the object approached us. It was nearly over our heads and it seemed we could almost reach up and touch it. It was very quiet, just a quiet hum. It was a solid triangle shape. I remember it being a dull metallic color with lights positioned at each point of the triangle. The light colors, as I remember, were red, blue and white. It hovered for some time, long enough for us to get a good look, moved off slowly and then sped off. We went immediately out to the airport to try and get info on what we saw but no one knew anything about it. We, went home and that was all there was to it. There was no missing time and as far as I know we were not abducted.  At no time did we feel afraid as I remember. I moved away a few months later to another state so I don't know what happened after that if anything.
