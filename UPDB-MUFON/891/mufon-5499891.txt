Bright star like object streaking across the sky, then fading to nothing

We were outside on a deck when we noticed a bright light moving north across the sky. It was too big for the space station, and not blinking like a plane. I thought it might have been a satellite but it was too bright for that and seemed too slow. It eventually faded from view shortly after we spotted it. There was some haze in the air from forest fires in the interior of the Province. Otherwise, a clear night.
