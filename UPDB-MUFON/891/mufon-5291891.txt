bright WHITE ball of light fell

On Saturday night, September 12, I was taking my dogs out back for a potty break when I saw a bright WHITE ball of light falling directly downward. It was moving super fast and faded from sight before reaching the horizon and being consumed by the lights of the city. Relying on a new mini compass, I originally recorded the direction as east/northeast. However because I had my doubts with this new mini compass, I located my trusty pocket compass and verified the location the next day. The ball of light was directly to my north, in Orlando.
