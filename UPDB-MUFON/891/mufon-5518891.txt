Three reddish lights unblinking flying over house.

About 8:15 pm, my wife and I were approaching our house from our back alley. Since Ive seen UFOs all of my life, Im prone to checking the sky. I noticed toward the south three unblinking red lights, bigger than stars without a doubt. They were flying in a northerly direction. I pointed them out to my wife who also saw them. Since we heard someone shooting off fireworks a few blocks to the southwest of us, she figured it was due to that, but then changed as we stood under the stars and watched these lights. There was a few clouds in the sky. As they almost got over our house, they seemed to vanish or blink out. My wife again thought that they were embers from the fireworks, but I told her embers burn out fairly quickly and move with the wind. These did not and I perceived that they went behind the clouds. The whole incident lasted about four to five minutes.
  These was not the first red light Ive seen over our house. Last July 4th, an unblinking red light flew in a south to north direction over our house and was seen by one of the neighbor pre-teen kids. But thats another story.
  Thank you.
