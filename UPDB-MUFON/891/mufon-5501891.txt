{dr}SUBJECT AND SON WERE WALKING AND SAW A MULTICOLORED ORB

SUNDAY NIGHT ABOUT 10:30 PM JUNE 15 SUBJECT AND HIS SON WERE WALKING NEAR THEIR HOME WHEN THEY SPOTTED A MULTICOLORED ORB IN THE SKY. THE ORB CHANGED COLORS BY FADING INTO ONE COLOR AND THEN ANOTHER. LIGHTS WERE GREEN, YELLOW, WHITE,AND BLUE. THEY WATCHED FOR 20 MINUTES UNTIL IT SLOWLY CROSSED THE SKY. IT SEEMED AS BIG AS A TWO BEDROOM HOUSE AND CROSSED OVER THEM MOVING WEST TO EAST. THEY LATER SAW A BRIGHT WHITE LIGHT HOVERING IN THE SKY. THE MOON WAS JUST COMING UP AND THE CLEAVLAND AIRPORT IS NEAR BY. THE ORBS MADE NO SOUND AS THEY WENT OVER. THEY SEEMED A LITTLE HIGHER THAN TREETOP. SUBJECT AND HIS SON WOULD LIKE TO KNOW WHAT THEY SAW AND IF ANYONE ELSE SAW IT TOO.
