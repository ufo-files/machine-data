Took dogs out around midnight on Thursday January 16, 2020 and was observing stars west of my house. Suddenly a very bright oval light appeared (like a light bulb being turned on) either over Crystal River Florida or west over the gulf. It traveled north in a straight line for close to a minute and it ejected an object from the rear that was approximately one tenth the size of the oval light but the light was just as intense. Within 3 or 4 seconds the small light started descending and both disappeared. The next morning I realized my security camera's may have captured it but for some reason I haven't been able to access the DVR.
