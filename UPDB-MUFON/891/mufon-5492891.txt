{cak} A floating rocket shaped object, came by me when I was young. As it went by I touched it and it just kept going in a straight line out of site.

When I was appropriately six years old I was outside playing with my friend by our barn. I don't know why I looked up and across our pasture, but I did. This cigar shaped object came slowly our direction. It could not have been 5 ft off the ground. It traveled through our pasture coming right towards us, down our driveway and right past us by the barn. It kept going in a straight line through our other pasture until I lost site of it. The amazing thing is that I touched it as it went by us. I remember banging on it with my hand. It was barely moving and just seemed to float as it went by. It was white in color and it has red at the tail end. I remember thinking that it looked like a rocket or a missile. I still can see it in my memory clearly to this day. I have no idea what it was. I have told my family about it and they seem to agree it was some sort of ufo. It would be interesting to me if there have been other similar/same visuals of this. I remember just a feeling of interest, and not much more. Cause I went back to goofing off with my friend to the best of my knowledge.
