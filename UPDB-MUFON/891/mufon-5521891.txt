Large red/orange bright light with yellow/white lights behind it flew east to n.west ascending

We were leaving my parents house around 8pm and saw the object directly above my parents house(approx. 500 ft above). It was a large red/orange light about 1/4 the size of a full moon, with smaller yellow/white lights (3-4) that seemed attatched right behind. My wife and I ran inside to show my parents and they came outside too. It was flying fairly slow east to west, also gaining altitude. After approx. 2 min it started heading more north in 5 min it have dissapeared out of our sight.
