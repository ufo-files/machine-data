{dr}Rotating 3-4 lights observed for 20 miles while moving

Traveling from New Jersey on the New York State thruway, spotted 3 lights hovering changing patterns In the north sky while in a moving car. Lights shifted to the eastern sky. My dad woke me up as he spotted 3 lights moving in the sky. Lights went from 3 to 4 in minutes. We thought it was spotlights but it stayed with our car as we traveled 20 miles and rotated. Lights were still observable outside of the backseat window after the time of 8:05 pm. Started off as 3 lights, rotated and the lights would move close together then spread out about the wingspan of a LARGE plane. Lights rotated into a ball like form then stretched again, cycle repeated. During the sighting we were trying to figure out what it was, after the sighting we were shocked. We lost sight because as we traveled further north then a change of direction to east. In the video the lights appear as blue but as I watched with my eyes they were a white color.
