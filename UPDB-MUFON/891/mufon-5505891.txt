{nd} bright red light in the northeast sky about 9:15 on the 24th of december 2013. after abserving for several minutes with family smaller object detatched, slowly fell, almost like leaf, then accelerated downwards out of sightline.

My family and I were going out to the car at about 9:15 - 9:30 Christmas eve. I looked up to the Northeast over my house and saw a VERY bright red globe hovering in the distance. expecting it to be a helicopter I realized it was not flashing and was not quite the right shade of red. I pointed this out to my family and they watched it with me. it did not move but seemed to be hovering up above everything emitting no sounds (this is what was disturbing) after watching it we got in the car to go along in that direction keeping an eye on it when a secondary object dropped from the main one slowly going down leaving a small trail. as it headed down it sped up in its decent out of sight. after that the main object literally just disappeared. We told some family members and my brother in law saw it on his way home after it was split in two. this left us (3/4) with a very odd feeling as it was so unusual and hoping we get an answer.
