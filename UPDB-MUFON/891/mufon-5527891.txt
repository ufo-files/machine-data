two small fast moving high altitude objects

It was early in the morning, clear skies. I noticed in the sky ahead of me to the north at an altitude about 30 - 60 km i guess. 100000ft or something. looking at first like a regular shooting star I noticed it was moving at up to twice the speed of a regular shooting star, and had no trail. it moved in a direction across my left shoulder in a curving/zipping kind of fashion. the path it took was not linear or a simple curve, it had a sharp irregular swerving path that convinced me it was not a shooing star at all. it disappeared and another appeared at the original spot and began traversing a similar path then promptly disappeared after that. my friend did not see it clearly and only said that they had noticed only a movement in the sky.
