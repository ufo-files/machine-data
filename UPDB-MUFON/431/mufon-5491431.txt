{cak} Two lights stopping and accelerating at extremely high speeds and stopping at another point in the sky.

On July 3, 2015 a couple of friends and I went to a firework show at Mountain Meadows park in Moorpark, CA; just like we do every year. A few minutes after it started some of the stars stared to look like they were moving, but I ignored it thinking it was just my imagination. Then, a few minutes after that I noticed two lights in the north east that started flashing green and red but they were stopped in mid air. I kept watching them as they came from a hovering position to darting across the sky and then stopping at another point. I poked my friend sitting next to me and showed him these two lights, at first he didn't look but then we started to notice that not too many people were watching the fireworks anymore, but instead these dancing green and red lights. They seemed almost as if they were interested in the explosions but when the show ended they both turned yellow and took off to the south east. After it was over I asked someone who worked for the air force that was sitting next to me watching the if he knew of any aircraft that could do that and his answer appeared to be no. As we left I saw about five helicopters hovering around on the opposite side of the show as the UFOs.
