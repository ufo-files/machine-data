A bright light with no discernable features

I observed a bright light moving across the sky that I at first assumed was an airplane. But then it just stopped. I watched it for 3 or 4 minutes then went inside. I periodically came outside about a dozen times for the next 4 hours and it was still in the same place, like a bright star. I woke up my brother, who was in a helo squadron at Norfolk navy base and explained to him about it,,knowing it couldn't possibly be a helicopter, but just wanting confirmation. He agreed there was no way it was a helicopter and remarked that it was over the biggest navy base in the world and we called it a night and went to bed. He woke me up at 8am and told me to come outside. The light was Still there! In the same place from the night before! It could be seen against the blue sky. We watched it for a while and it finally disappeared around 9am.
