Camcorded event before and after boom

i observed deep amber lights that hovered and finally displaying huge deep amber to orange light masses like they wanted to let me know of their presence. They presented themselves a few minutes before the boom. I ran into the house to see if my boyfriend was ok and I was terrified after what I just observed. My first impression was that something crashed or was shot down because I could hear heavy plane traffic and heavy activity of these amber colored lights. As I was walking back to my back door, i could see off to the west of me in the middle of a field some glowing amber orange lights slowly approaching my position and while i was observing, they put out a beautiful display of sequencing light display and little balls of deep amber projecting off one of them. I was afraid but I captured on camcorde and Mufon was contacted and are analizing my tape at present.The night after I camcorde another similar display and I contacted our local Mufon rep.
