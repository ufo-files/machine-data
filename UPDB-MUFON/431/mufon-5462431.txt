{rjl} Weird, oval object with lights in the sky.

I was talking a walk on the outskirts of Chicago, Illinois. The sky above me suddenly became brighter. I thought it was just a cloud moving away from the moon so I thought nothing of it. I looked up and saw a silvery, oval shaped object just sat there in the sky. It had bright white lights and some sort of glowing pattern on its surface. It was about the size of a basketball . I wasn't scared because I'd heard of other things like this before but I was very confused. I checked my watch and it had just stopped so I pulled out my cellphone to check the time too. The time had just stopped. It felt like only a few seconds but after the event, the time skipped and 20 minutes had gone by. The U.F.O. had just disappeared.
