{tjd} Orbs

My best friend called me saying there were orbs in the sky. He gave me a direction of the objects according to his vantage (South East towards) Holladay in Salt Lake City. I looked in that direction and didn’t see anything. I looked northeast to see 3 orbs. 2 were fading in and out of visibility as if ascending or descending. There was one orb that was mostly always visible. I called my dad who is a sceptic. He brought out binoculars, we both saw them. They faded out of vision.
