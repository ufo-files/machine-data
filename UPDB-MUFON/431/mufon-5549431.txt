A octagon shaped craft hovering at the height of a ten story building that had fast, jerking movements.

me and a friend were in her car on Smoky Hill Road going to the Blockbuster down the street. There was a line of four bright lights (white) evenly spaced across one section of the sky. As I watched them I noticed that all four lights were attatched to separate crafts. The lights would pulse and get brighter and the objects would seemingly jump across the sky. There were a great deal more when we looked around, maybe around 12 and all sitting still or moving at an extreme rate. One object was low enough for us to see it almost in it's entirity. It was shaped in a perfect octagon with three white lights that were visible from our angle. It also had a red lighton the front end. The object was moving extremely fast and would occasionally shoot a very quick beam of white light across the sky. We were driving but very slow and watching the object. The person in front of us was only going about 5 mph as if they had noticed the object as well. Once we reached the Blockbuster we watched the objects and quickly went inside. Shaken, we could not stop discussing the incident as we rented our video. We left the store about 15 minutes later and all but 2 of the lights were gone.
