{rjl} Thought they were lights on Paris Mountain, but got brighter

I'm visiting Greenville SC, my sister-in-law wanted to show me the house she and her husband bought. On our way, on Pleasantburg Dr, we were noticing lights and she said, "that's Paris Mountain, you have have Brad (my son) drive you up there to see all Greenville." Then we noticed the lights were getting brighter and moving. After passing by her home, we pulled over to observe the moving objects which were NOT lanterns. They rose up and one by one dissipated. 
FYI, I am a State Section Director/Field Investigator in PA
My sister-in-law will also be a witness for the investigation, upon contact, I will supply her contact info and video of the end of the event.
