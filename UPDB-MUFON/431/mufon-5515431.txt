A large UFO hovering over Godlings Way with bright flashing lights

I was driving up a hill when I saw a bright flash in the sky, I looked up and saw a giant cupcake shaped UFO.
It was hovering stationary over housing and fields, with a giant red light on the top (similar to a cherry on a cake) it also had flashing white lights on it from the top to the bottom in strips. 
It remained there for around 4 seconds, I stopped my car to get out and have a better look. But by the time I got out it had completely disappeared.
