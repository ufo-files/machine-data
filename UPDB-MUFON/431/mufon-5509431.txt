(GS) Ship hovered outside apartment window

My handy-mans son captured this image from his iPod touch standing in his bedroom in their apartment in West Hollywood. They wish to remain anonymous and didn't even want to circulate this photo. The boy stated that he looked out his window and saw this shape. He then called down to his mother and sister to quickly come up and see it. He captured the photo quickly and as his sister ran downstairs and outside to view it from another angle, it disappeared.
