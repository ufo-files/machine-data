I was driving in my truck heading east on Interstate 8 just coming off Interstate 10 by Casa Grande and I noticed just above me to my right what I thought was a plane flying low. When I got closer I noticed that it was not moving or it was moving very slow. As I kept driving I ended up almost right underneath the object. I didn't really have a chance to stop and get a good look or listen for any sound it was making, but it had three lights that made a triangle shape on the bottom. I lost sight of the object as I kept driving so I don't know how long it stayed there.
