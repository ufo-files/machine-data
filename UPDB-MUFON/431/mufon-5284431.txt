Bright “star” plus a red orb heading towards it

Driving west on Hwy 7, towards Sharbot Lake, Ontario.  Hwy quite empty of traffic, just occasional other vehicles.  I had been observing a bright star-like body, thinking it might be the planet Venus as it was alone in the darkening sky, a solitary bright spot in the southern sky and quite low on the horizon. No other stars visible at this point.  While glancing up at it, I suddenly notice to my left a bright, reddish “star”, a red light, but it was moving in a long arc upwards, then down and disappeared into the brightness of the first star.  My first thought was it was a comet or meteorite, but it was too slow for that  As well, it had no following trail.  It moved slower than any meteorite I’ve ever seen but much, much more quickly than any aircraft.  The red light travelling in an arc lasted about 20 seconds, appeared to aim directly for the first “bright star”, and did not reappear on its trajectory.
