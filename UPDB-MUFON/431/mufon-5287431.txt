Driving on 2 lane road about 11 pm at night. No other traffic. Neon green specks burst in sky directly. in front and above me. No more than a mile away and about a 45 degree angle. I thought it might be fireworks but there was no noise and it was not a holiday.  Then the green ligjht. burst again and streamed down and disappeared again. Then the green light came down again and drew a triangle and the triangle turned on it's axis and appeared 3 dimensional and then the whole thing blinked out. I came home and called WBBQ, a local radio station that monitored police traffic and had a reporter on the street for years. I asked the DJ if anyone was reporting green lights in the sky and he said "Oh yeah, lots of people are calling in." He asked where I was and I told him South Augusta and he said the calls had started in Aiken, SC. and I was as far south as he had heard.
