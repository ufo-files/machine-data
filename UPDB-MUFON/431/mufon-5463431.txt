Giant ball green light hovered then shot vertically at great speed

This event was witnessed by myself and four others. We were returning from York to Scarborough after watching a film at the cinema. We were on the A64 road. As we approached the village of Staxton we saw an enormous ball of green that was glowing and just stood stationery over the Royal Air Force radar base that was Staxton Hill. The entire car full witnessed this enormous ball. After hovering there a few moments it shot vertically straight upwards towards space at an incredible fast speed and then it vanished way above us.
