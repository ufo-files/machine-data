I entered the courtyard of my apartment to fetch my laundry at 7:55 PM. A small bright star-sized object that was approximately five times brighter than near by stars was hanging in the north east portion of the sky when i exited my apartment. I looked skyward for no apparent reason and fixated on this light. When I saw it move I looked closely for the flashing beacon lights of an aircraft and when I saw none I surmised that it might have been a satellite or orbital object entering earth's atmosphere. I saw it traveled east to west in a slightly wobbly path slowly across the sky until it turned from white to red, then faded from view. I focused intently on the spot in the night sky where it faded and seam to shrink or burn out of view but it never reappeared. It was moving much too slow for a communication satellite or a meteor.

I was excited to see such a strange thing at first but the object left me feeling vaguely unsettled.
