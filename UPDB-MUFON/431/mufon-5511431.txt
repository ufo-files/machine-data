{nd} I went out to see if I could see meteors and saw these other things instead. Lights flashing

On April 22, 2013, I went outside to see if I could see the meteor shower. I looked up and immediately saw something that was NOT a meteor. The same thing I saw for the first time 1 1/2 years ago. Last night was the 3rd time seeing this. But, I looked around and saw 2 more. They were in a triangle, miles away from each other. The lights are what caught my eye. They were flashing lights. Red, yellow, green, white, and a blue that could have been purple. I watched them for over 45 mins, hoping to see one take off so I could tell myself I wasnt crazy. Im a skeptic...Im interested, don't get me wrong. But, still a skeptic. I watched these things move! My friends kept telling me it was meteors, weather balloons, or possibly something sent up to catch a glimpse of the meteor shower. My friend took a walk to see if she could see anything and called me freaking out and scared. She saw all 3 too! We saw them move around a little, but only very short distances. I wanted to watch until they moved, but I couldnt. I cant stop thinking about it. Im always looking to the sky in hopes of seeing something different and cool, but these scared me.
