NOT a plane or balloon. Very remote wilderness terrain. Mid day, white disk that stayed stationary at a 45 degree angle for 5 minutes or so. Saw it go through a cloud, then hover and move on until disappeard behind a mountain.

I was driving home from a day kayaking on the Lochsa river in northern Idaho. It was about 4:00 PM on a unusual(for this time of year!)beautiful day. Blue bird skys, with popcorn white clouds. I was driving [item moved/CMS/nd] after kayaking the Lochsa river. While driving on a very curvy road I spotted a white, disk like object hovering but yet moving at a very slow speed, about 500 ft in the air, one mile ish away from me. The weird thing was, it, the object remained at a 45 degree angle the entire time. Unlike a plane that were to be banking, it never changed its angle or course. Also, the object didn't emit any light really, it was just solid white with a darkish strip that seemed to move slightly. I watched it for quite awhile, at times losing it behind mountains only to see it again. Thought about it intensely, trying to rule out a plane or glider or balloon, but nothing made sense. This is such a remote location, its HIGHLY unlikely that a balloon or glider would be in the area. At first thought it was a glider, then after watching it for awhile realize,there is NO location for a glider to take off in this remote wilderness. THEN realized, this was no glider. I know this area well. Not something you see.....ever.
