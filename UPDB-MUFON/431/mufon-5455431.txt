{jpg} Short but strange sighting

As I was driving through Jasper Tennessee I saw a series of blue,white and orange lights that were lined up in a straight line across the sky. They remained stationary when I noticed them and I am unsure how long they had been there until I saw them. The lights began to fade out from right to left one by one and they did not return. They covered a large area in the sky and were close together. They were much larger than any star in the sky.
