{jpg} It was like a star with a cloud around it. Before I lost site of it, it let out 2 tails.

I was sitting on my porch and saw a star that looked like it had a cloud around it. It is a completely clear night. I could tell it was moving slowly. I went outside before I lost view of it. That's when it started to leave a bright white tail. One on top, one on the bottom. Almost like it shined a light on its own vapor trail. I could not record it though. Shortly after the tails it disappeared. Saw it for almost 10 minutes. I have never seen anything like it. Freaked me out.
