Looked like 3 partially filled large balloons moving in unison close together.

I was driving down the road on Corydon Avenue south of Assiniboine Park about 5 PM. The object was round/oval in shape coming in my direction. I stopped the car and got out to get a better look. As the object approached it was actually three objects travelling in perfect unison close together. The objects didn't touch each other. The amorphous objects looked somewhat like 3 large jelly beans. At the time I assumed it was 3 partially filled weather balloons that had escaped their tethers moving in unison with the wind.The objects moved over the city. I checked the newspapers the next day but found no mention of the object(s).
