green orb flew from east to west over the tree top

sitting at a campfire on the bank of the Muskoka river near the town of fraserburg I saw a green orb fly over the tree top about 10m in height in an east to west direction. The following summer sitting at same campfire saw the same green orb fly by,same direction same height this time multiple witnesses.  Looked like roman candle, size of softball, very fast. Direction it came from is extreme wilderness no houses or roads for 35 or 40 km . IT looked exactly like a roman candle but came out of pure wilderness .WAS NOT ANY KIND OF FIREWORKS.
