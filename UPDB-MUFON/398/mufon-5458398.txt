{rjl} Hovering, moved up, moved down, then suddenly shot towards north with utmost speed.

As I was driving home, took notice of disc shaped object, approximately 100 feet in diameter with a silver dome and white around the dome. It was hovering then moved up, hovered and then moved down to hover again. Hovered to the left, then suddenly with great speed, shot towards the north and disappeared.
