I was at work on night shift, I was showing a coworker a UFO video on YouTube. I jokingly said "I'm going alien hunting" as I was walking out of the front door. I noticed there were two bright white stars out instead of one which I had noticed before. As I was watching it I noticed one was moving horizontally at a steady pace, it suddenly stopped, turned a dark reddish color then shot straight up into the night sky, it's speed made it seem to disappear. I called my cooworker to see it but he was too late, it was also too fast for me to record it on my phone.
