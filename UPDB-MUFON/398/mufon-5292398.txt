MY FATHER, A POLICEMAN & I WITNESS 2 UFOS DOING EXERCISES IN THE NIGHT SKY FOR OVER AN HOUR. AT NO TIME DID WE SEE ANY MILITARY/AIRFORCE AIRPLANES CHECKING THE UFOS OUT. IT WAS THE MOST UNBELIEVABLE SIGHT I EVER SAW TO THIS VERY DAY.
THE INDIAN POINT NUCLEAR POWER PLANT IS OUT OF PEEKSKILL, ON HUDSON RIVER OPENED 9/16/62.
THIS TOOK PLACE IN ROCKLAND COUNTY, NEW YORK SUMMER OF SPRING OF 1966 OR 1967. ROCKLAND JOURNAL NEWS REPORTED IT WAS SWAMP GAS. I CALLED TALLMAN POLICE. POLICEMAN CAME AND WITNESSED UFO EVENT. EVENT WAS AN HOUR LONG. THEY HAD JUST BUILT A NUCLEAR PLANT NEAR HUDSON RIVER. MY DENTIST HAD AN EXPERIENCE WITH UFO THAT BURNED HIS BODY & SCORCHED HIS HAIR. HE TOLD ME TO CALL NAT'L INVESTIGATIONS COMM ON AERIAL PHENOMENA IN WASHINGTON DC. I CALLED, AND A WEEK LATER 2 MEN CAME TO MY HOME 9:30 PM WITH RECORDING DEVICE. TWO BRIGHT CIRCLES IN THE CLEAR STARLITE SKY.
2. DID I SEE WING'S OR ROTOR BLADES LIKE A HELICOPTER?
NO
3. HOW LONG WERE THESE ORBS DOING FLYING EXERCISES?
A LEAST AN HOUR
