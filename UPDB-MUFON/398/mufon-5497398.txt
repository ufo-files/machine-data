{nd} Hiding in a rainstorm

I was shooting a timelapse of the storm that was approaching in Simi Valley, CA. I got the Idea to put the Gopro (1st generation) on the roof and switched it's view from southeast to north east a few minutes after setting it to run. I went inside to not be rained on. It wasn't until i looked at the video with a frame by frame mentality after about a week of the occurrence that i noticed an anomaly, I saw nothing with my own eyes. As i was looking at each frame I noticed right after the heavy downfall that there seemed to be (something) darting through the image. Once I changed the image look (with adjustments to exposure, contrast etc...) whatever this thing was became more defined. This is a high def camera with no flaws and still works perfectly to date. I don't know what i'm looking at and it has confused everyone i have showed it to. I am putting it out there to try and get some real answers but that continues to be my desire. Everything else natural in the frames are visible.... birds, raindrops, etc. What am i looking at here?
