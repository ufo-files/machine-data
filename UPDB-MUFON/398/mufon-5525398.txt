Silver metallic disc flying south of the 101 freeway

I was driving to work , stuck in traffic. I was traveling north on the 101 freeway, and something in the sky caught my eye. I thought it might be a small white plane, but then the sun shined on it, as it must have tilted a bit, and it had a silver metallic surface. It was oval shaped, and i kept my eye on it as it traveled from south to north for about 3 to 5 seconds, then it just vanished into thin air!!! I kept looking and looking for it, but it was gone!!! This is my second sighting in three weeks, and i am stunned!!
