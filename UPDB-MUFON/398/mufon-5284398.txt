3 Orb/Diamond Near Silent crafts

Observed 1 orb like UFO outside of my house on 11/2/21, at 10:22PM. Orb was moving slow and silent while seemingly changing shape and color. As it floated behind a tree out of sightline then disappeared. 

Returned outside about 20min later to spot the same orb. It began to travel in my direction and I approached to get a better look as well. As I came out into the middle of my street i noticed 2 additional orbs - 1 hovering behind my house, to the west. The other, at a distance further to the west from the initial craft. 3 in total. 

The craft furthest west vanished before I had a chance to film it. The 1st (nearest craft) approached slowly in my direction to the west and then began veering south. At closest distance, I estimate it was approx. 1/2 mile away, at an altitude just under 1000ft or so. After traveling south of me about a mile, the craft hovered in one spot for about 1min. Meanwhile, the 2nd craft was hovering in the same spot, about 1.5-2 miles to the east, 1.5- 2kft in altitude. As i looked back to the 1st craft it had vanished. The 2nd craft began ascending slowly further east until it eventually vanished as well.

About 10 min after I returned to my house, I heard what sounded like fighter jets over my house. I managed to run outside and catch the last one as it was heading south in the same direction as the 1st craft.
