The stationary object looked pentagon/ heptagon shaped, when zoomed in, with my Sony DSC-HX50. I had no tri-pod.

Every day we observed a stationary star in the south-east above Bang Phli. While the sky is cloudy. No other stars to trace in the neighbourhood. In fact, my girlfriend attended me on this light. I get my camera and made some photos. Then I decided to record it with the camera. Pity enough I had no tripod. So, I had to control myself while zooming in, to keep the camera steady. Bit by bit I zoomed in, then I saw it more clear. Looks there is a dome one it or more domes. Looks there is a certain kind of pattern on the surface of the object. I was aware if there was something moving on it. Transfer of place on the object. The object was every day on the same spot. Once I requested a colleague to look at the sky in the certain direction and he saw the object too. Certainly, it is no imagination.
