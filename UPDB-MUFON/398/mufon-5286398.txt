I was standing on my front lawn taking out the trash. The stars are really bright outside my family’s house so I would always stand and stare out into space. I had seen a lot of shooting stars, meteors, etc. But this night I saw some kind of reddish glowing thing in the distance. I watched it for a while and then it started darting across the sky. At first it was far off, the colors changed from red to white at one point and then back, but then it shot close (it stay probably a mile or less away, but I’m bad at telling distance). Another one appeared. The same light. The same kind of humming noise. They started kind of dancing together in the sky. I was trying to convince myself that it was just a star or an aircraft but it was moving so quickly. They were jerking around in the sky in a way that was so unnatural and unlike what I had ever seen an aircraft able to do at those speeds. It lasted for about 25 to 30 minutes. I had tried to document it early on with my camera but all I had was a cheap phone camera that could barely capture a bathroom selfie.
