Triangle ufo

I was walking down a country road around 2am and in the sky I watched a huge triangle shaped object flying above me and kept flying until I could no longer see it. A few nights later I was telling my cousin about what I saw so we went out to a different country road and as I was trying to explain again what I had seen it appeared that the same object flew over us.
