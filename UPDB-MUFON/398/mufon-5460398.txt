{jpg} 4-6 spherical objects flew over neighborhood and into large cloud.

Walking through my neighborhood, I saw some 4-6 spherical objects moving at a relatively quick pace (think the speed a traffic helicopter would travel while filming) from the South heading towards the Northwest. The objects were too large to be birds but were too small and too close to me to have been planes. The objects did not appear to be drones; they were round and completely silent, did not have any propellers, and lacked lights. The objects, by my estimation, were between 4 and 10 feet around, completely spherical in shape, and were a dark metallic color, only exhibiting a slight glinting of sunlight on the topside. 
The objects were observed to be flying in a loose formation, staying approximately 20-40 feet from each other. About 3 minutes into the sighting, the objects formed a delta "V" formation and entered a large, dark cloud (the type that are loaded with rain). Once in the cloud, the delta formation did an apparent 170-degree turn and began heading in a Southeastern direction (towards where I was standing). Finally, the objects, still in a loose delta formation, began flying higher into the cloud structure, popping in and out of cover. After about 35-40 seconds of them ascending, I lost sight of them. 

I did not attempt to film the objects. First, the objects were too small and faint against the sky to be adequately picked up by my phone's camera. Second, my phone was in a difficult-to-access spot and I did not want to break visual on the objects.
