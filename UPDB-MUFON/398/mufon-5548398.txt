Distinctive Triangle Shape and Two lights

I was camping out on the Golfcourse, when I studdered upon a rock. The rock was particularly glowing in a greenish form. I looked up while holding the rock and noticed that there was a distinctive light in the sky. While I was watching the light, the light became two lights dancing around in the sky. I have 20-20 vision, so I knew that this could not have been a government aircraft from the near bye Air Force Base. As I watched the lights from about 250 feet, I noticed that they had suddenly just disappeared. This had gone on for about 2 minutes until I saw another Huge Distinctive Triangle shaped object. The Triangle had 3 lights about the size of the first two I saw. One light was on the corner of the Triangle as the other ones were to. It was about 500 feet away from the ground, and I could tell that It was watching me. It hovered around me with no sound at all for about 2 and a half minutes. It felt like there was something missing as I watched the object. After, I could see my friends were rushing towards me, so I turned around and pointed at the object, but at about that time, the object was gone.
