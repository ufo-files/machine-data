Object reflecting light hovered then moved at moderate speed for 15 miles then turned.

I was looking out at Lake superior when I felt like I needed to turn around. Upon turning, I saw a white object hovering in the sky to the west of my location. It hovered for about 5 minutes. I initially thought it was a drone. It then started moving parallel to the lake in a NNE direction. I followed it in my car along Hwy 61 for about 15 miles until it veered west and went deeper over the forest. I found a dirt road going west and followed it for several blocks until the road abruptly ended. The attached images were taken as it moved away from me at the end of that road. It was nearing the summit of the Sawtooth Mountains as I lost sight of it.
