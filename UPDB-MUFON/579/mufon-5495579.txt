Rapidly moving white sphere. No noticed glow. No sound heard. No flashing lights. Not a satellite, unless it was crashing. Lost sight due to hills.

Looked out of window for deer. Saw single round white light object travelling rapidly wsw to ene. No sound, no flashing lights, 2 to 3 second sighting. It went nearly over the house but I did not hear any noise. Hills to east so aircraft sight and sound may not be heard by the time I went outside to listen for plane, windy couldn't hear any. I'm familiar with satellites and meteorites. Too big and travelling too low for satellite. Not enough glow and no trail for meteorite. Kind of exciting cause this is nothing I've ever seen.
