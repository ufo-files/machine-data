Orb hovered and viewed for hundreds of miles

White orb first viewed in Roswell at 11:30 am. We had lunch and continued to travel towards the famous crash site near Corona for several hours. We decided to not go to the crash site after arriving at private property about 10 miles outside. We continued on into Corona.. The orb remained in our field of view for 4 hours and did not move the entire time. We could see the orb for over 100 miles. The closer we got to Corona the bigger it got. We lost view of it the closer we got to Santa Fe.
