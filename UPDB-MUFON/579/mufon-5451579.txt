Star Wars type Star Destroyer looking UFO over Auburn, WA in summer of 1992-1993ish.

My wife of 17 years passed away from breast cancer in 2013. I thought I would finally share what she had told me one day about a UFO encounter she had when she was young. I have never seen one, and she had only seen a UFO that once. It was sometime in the summer of `92 to `94 when she was around 18. She was on her way back home from a friends house late at night, maybe 1-3 am or so when she noticed some lights in her side mirror after going over a hill. I think she was somewhere near Algona Hill in Auburn, WA. She thought it odd because she didn't see any car lights in her rear view mirror and thought she was alone on the road so she says she looked out the window and saw some lights in the sky moving over her. 

She pulled over and saw this huge object with lights all over it like windows and it was moving overhead slowly about 30-40 mph. I kept asking her about the size of the object, and how she knew it was that big and how low it was and she said it was very nearly just over the treetops of the hills, so that was maybe 500 feet or so high. She said it was triangular in shape, at least a mile long, and it took several minutes for it to pass overhead. She said it made no noise. She figured it was some kind of blimp or test aircraft because she said the Boeing plant flies aircraft all the time nearby and so she got into her car, drove home and never spoke about it again until one day many years later we were talking about UFO's after I watched Star Wars with her. She'd never seen the movie before, I'd seen it a hundred times and she said it looked like those Imperial Star Destroyers, triangular, huge and with lights all over.

So that's her story. And yes I asked her if she was doing drugs or drinking that night or coming back from a party and she said no. I believe her.
