{rjl} Triangular/V shaped lights overhead going south to north with no sound and VERY large in size.

About 9:15 pm on 4/22/2015 I decided to sit out on back patio to enjoy the beautiful night. I was sitting facing the west and I could see the moon at about 90 degrees above horizon with a very bright star to the right. To my left was the roof line to the house and there was another fairly bright star to the left maybe at 100 degrees or so from horizon. I was looking up at about a 180 degrees while seated. I noticed a plane going in a southerly direction with the standard blinking aircraft lights. As it was going out of sight due to my roofline that is when my eyes caught something directly above. It was massive in size compared to the plane I had just seen. It was lights in a triangular/V shape moving south to north. The lights were all same size and shape and we're a pale white/yellow light. They did not blink at all either. They seemed a little brighter around the edges of the circles. There was one at the point and three lights on one side and 2 on the other. I could not see a ship but the lights were evenly spaced and maintained spacing as it moved. There was no sound at all. We have lots of commercial planes fly over all the time and I grew up near Westover AFB with C5A's flying over so I am very familiar with aircraft at night. I have even seen the stealth bomber flyover during air shows at Westover and THIS was no airplane. I lost sight after I turned to go to door and get my son. It flew fast but steady as it went in a northerly direction. As far as distance I could not tell but looked very high. This is my testimony to the best of my recollection of the event.
