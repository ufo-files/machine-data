Sitting at night on front porch. I was looking up at the sky when what appeared to be a huge Chevron shaped craft moved in a straight line across the sky and disappear behind the treeline out of sight. Illuminated behind by the moon. No lights, no sound. No time to go in and get camera. I knew I was seeing a UFO. I sat and watched and could not believe what I was witnessing. I sat there for a few minutes after trying to get my head around what I had just seen. I still remember it today as if it was yesterday. An amazing site. My friends laugh but I know what I saw.
