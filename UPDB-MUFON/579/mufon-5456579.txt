While driving home on highway 66east bound in evening I noticed a large bright light above the hill overlooking the foundry. I wonder when they put it up and how big and brite it was. I drove past the light and pulled in a parking lot on the right side about a block away. I turned off my truck and got out. I saw the light above the hill and then it went out. A few minutes later I looked up and above me a bluish light came on slowly and then slowly went dark. Then a pinkish red light came on then went slowly out. This went on and off as it slowly to the west. No sound and the lights were about 100ft above me.
