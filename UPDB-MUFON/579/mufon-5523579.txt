I observed an object coming from behind the tree tops that had a row of lights on the bottom and was a skinny rectangle. It disappeared after 3 seconds.

I went out my back door to smoke a cigarette and noticed something flying from behind a line of trees. I first thought it was a small aircraft as we do have an airstrip in town and often have small airplanes flying around. However, this was just like a strip of lights and then just vanished after a few seconds of flying in a straight line. At about arms length I would say it was about 2 inches. My little girl was outside with me and even asked me "what happened?". I waited about 5 minutes then went in to give my two girls a bath. After about 10 minutes my mother came downstairs and came in the bathroom..I immediately told her that I saw a UFO..she was shocked because her and her husband just came back from a walk and she had just seen what she described as something burning, falling from the sky.
