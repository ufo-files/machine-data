{cak} Late evening/early morning awoken by whisping sound, payed no attention to it. that moring found weird object in backyard.

I was sleeping at the time amd awakened by a whish/whispy noise and really thought nothing of it. I found this object several hours later in my backyard. At first, from a distance, I thought it was a mess that an animal had made . When I got closer I saw it was something different I have never seen before. It was broken open on one side with a jelly goo that was pretty solid, with a skeletal looking white object in the gel. The outer shell was leathery to the touch, yes I touched it. I thought maybe it was a pod type "puff ball" or mushroom growth, then thought that something that size could not have grown that quick( in a few hours) or also maybe it was an aborted egg/embryo from a large bird, just very strange! I slid this object onto a cardboard, took it to the high school science teacher, he had no idea of what it was. I never persued the matter. I kept it frozen for 3 or 4 weeks and it faded in color and eventually deteriorated to a glob.....I threw it out! Any ideas what this is/was? Is it something natural or from somewhere unknown?
