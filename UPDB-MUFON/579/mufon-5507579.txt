{nd} we saw a bight light comming at us from just above the trees while in our field.

while in our field doing a camp out with our 3 girls, they went to bed about 12:30 am in the tent , my wife and i sat by the fire talking and having some coffee(as we don't drink and and don't smoke anddo any drugs what so ever) we both got up from our chairs to start to clean up the camp sight and as we were talking we both notice at the same time a very bright light came from the North just over the trees and we both thought this was very strange as it just made a hum sound but not one that was very loud and it was moving very slow just over our heads and we both held each others hands and was moving back wards away from under this object and it did not slow down and was very bright and just went south just over the HWY and into the hills and went over them and shot to the left and was gone that fast. we both just stood there kinda in disbelief and shook up and we were not tired for the rest of the night till day light came then we got the place cleand up and drove to our homw which is less then 100 yards from the camp sight.
