First week in April at about 5:00AM I was out with my dogs and watching the stars, looking for satellite which always move east. I was watching what I thought was a plane, but there were no blinking lights, just a solid light moving East by Southeast. After about 10 seconds, the small light brightened to 10 times it original size, in a glowing ball,for about 3 seconds, then returned to the original light shape and continued on the same path. The light appeared to stop, glowed 10 times the original size again and then made an immediate 90* left turn and was visible for 1 to 2 seconds and the object disappeared moving several times the original speed and was gone. I don't believe in things I don't see. But I have seen strange things before in the sky and shrug them off. This object took my breath away. I am amazed at stars and satellites, but this was like nothing else I have ever seen. I watch "MUFON FILES" and that caused me to contact you and report this.
