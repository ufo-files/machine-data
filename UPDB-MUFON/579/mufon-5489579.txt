Just exit the 23 freeway turn east to head toward Simi Valley. While driving along Olsen Rd I looked up and saw a gray tube descending in a angle toward the ground. My first thought was the object was going to crash. It continued just above the hill (didn't seam like a lot of space above the hill)until I lost vision. I did notice a small fixed wing aircraft above the sighting but the airplane appeared to be somewhat far away but kinda above the object. 
A drone? It was weird so I wanted to let you hear about it.
I have never seen anything before like that in the air. Previous Air Force here, so this event led me mind searching as to what it may of been.
