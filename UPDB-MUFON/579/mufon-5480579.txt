{jpg} Ball of light moving SW to NE no blinking. Them ascended turning into a red dot.

We had just got home and was looking at Mars when a bright light appeared moving quickly and steady. We kept looking for flashing lights and listened for sound thinking it was a plane but as it got closer there was no sound while over head and no colored flashing lights like airplanes or helicopters it was on a straight path headed NE and continued as a steady very bright light and started ascending on its same trajectory. It got smaller and smaller until it turned into a red light and disappeared. The Video was the best I could get with some snap shots as well.
