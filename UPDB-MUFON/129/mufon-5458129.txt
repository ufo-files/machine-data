Fast accelerating object

I was outside trying to observe a satellite, I thought I observed it but it was too high an altitude I observed it for 30 seconds heading south. The object was travelling at the normal satellite speed i.e. 6 minutes from horizon to horizon, then it accelerated and moved from about 80 degrees to about 30 degrees in about 2-3 seconds and disappeared into the clouds. I stayed for a short while but nothing could be seen
