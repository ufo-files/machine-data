Went out onto back porch to have a cigarette. Looked up into sky due east, Lots of cloud cover, no stars visible. Saw red light, slighly larger than a star through the tree. The light pulsated but did not move in any direction or change size. It was framed by tree branches so that I was sure it was not moving.After about 2 minutes (I was halfway through my cigarette)the light just disappeared all at once. I checked the time when I came back inside, 11:21 p.m. I believe that it was below the peaks of the wasatch mountains from my point of view, but hard to say due to the darkness and cloud cover.
