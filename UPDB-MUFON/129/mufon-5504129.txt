deer hunting season ufo sighting at camp

Fortunately for me I have been blessed by 2 sightings ..1 which I just sent to you about an hour ago... 1959 in Ont. ..This second sighting was in Nova Scotia..5 of us fellows were at a friends hunting camp Nov 11 1995 ?? have to check camp log to make sure of the year 1995 is close..We had just finished supper when I had to go out to have a pee,when I noticed something hovering just above the trees at the east end of the lake about a mile or so .I called the other fellows to come and have a look ...it was cigar shape and had from what we could see at least 4 or 5 windows something similar to a big airplane , windows were dimly lit. thats all we could see. We watch it move from our right to our left in a northerly direction until it disappeared,There was not a sound ..it was deafenately some sort of airborne vessel ...in my estimation the cloud cover was at about 200 ft. AGL ..Because I was a pilot for the Dept of N.R. I had a dept. radio with me I called the radio room to verify that all of our helicopters were at the hangar. Of course they were they would not have been out anyway but I had to confirm. I had the radio room call Halifax contol tower to verify that there was nothing on the radar or any aircraft out there and there wasnt ..The UFO just disappeared without a sound ...We had not had a drink yet but we sure made up for it later
