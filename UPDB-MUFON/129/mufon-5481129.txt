Was walking my dog when a giant object flew over my head, ascending in 3 seconds, first close to the ground like a helicopter, and few minutes after like an airplane or a star (that's when it was either stuck in the sky, or going further from the earth

1.Not too far from my block, just walking my dog (away from the block). Then i see someone coming out of the corner (two women and a little girl), so as a typical antisocial and shy person i turned away and continued walking with the dog (now going towards the block).

2.It happened really fast, in few seconds. I saw a quick, large round light over my head.

3.It looked like a helicopter, or a small blimp with helicopter like handles at the bottom, and with a large light (either on the front, or down, facing the ground). 

4.A mix of a blimp shaped helicopter and a triangle. It had a really big light on it. In just few seconds it quickly raised from 30ft to now 400ft, and like 20 seconds after to 1000ft, that is looked like a star.
Then it looked like it stopped in the sky, or moved very slowly, or flew further from the earth into a single direction. After that, when coming home and taking out the trash i noticed a really small light, which looked just like the 1000ft above the ground object. It was flying towards east, very fast. I heard a noise similiar to that of a plane, coming from the object (which is wierd, even for a plane itself, as it left no smoke behind).

5.I felt pumped, since i always wanted to be a witness of such sighting. Unluckily for me i never got a camera to record stuff like that, and my phone was too bad to record in the dark. 

6.I knew running around, trying not to lose sight of the object was a silly idea, mainly because it was too fast and people around didn't seem to notice it, or even the sound at all, so i just headed to my apartament.
