{cak} clear night with low moon. saw a fireball traveling not extremely fast and not slow like drifting. pulled over to video. it changed to Star like appearance and slight direction change

I was heading home on a beautiful clear night. The moon was low so the night sky was still black with tons of bright stars out. Suddenly...I noticed off to my front Left what looked like a fireball traveling thru the sky. I didnt feel scared or in danger. I just couldn't figure out what I was looking at. It seemed to be traveling East about the same height that a helicopter would fly or slightly higher. I didn't hear any noise. And oddly...it didn't light up the sky around it. I pulled over to video. It was hard to film. In the video you can see the burst with color...then change to more white star like. As I adjusted to get a fixed point in the film (the moon) the object changed its direction to a more East path...and then I lost sight of it. I never turned down my radio...but you can hear the spring peepers (frogs) in the video. If that object made any noise..i didn't hear it. Sorry for the F Bomb at the end..but i didn't want to edit it out and make the video appear altered.
