Thought group of 3 stars binoculars lights which were 'wormlike' moving back and forwards

I like to observe sky at night. Looked out window and saw about twice height of top of pillars of Erskine Bridge over River Clyde what I thought were 3 stars very close together. Got binoculars to check, and observed three, maybe four lights which were red, blue and white. They squiggled around in a long form, almost worm like, but the object was stationary. It remained there for more than an hour without moving, and I returned to bed. I would say it was - before observation through binoculars, possibly dome like with smaller area at top and wider area at bottom. A lot of helicopters go over this bridge due to the number of people who jump from it. It did not seem like that. No movement with naked eye, only with binoculars, and no sound at all as I opened my door and checked. Time is approx as I had risen from bed to go to toilet. I was not afraid at all, and would just like to know what this was. I only lost sight of it as I became so tired, I went to bed. Got up at around 4 am, and it was not there, though the sky was lighter by that time.
