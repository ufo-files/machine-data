Saucer (disc) shaped, Grey metal outer hull, with a band of white light around bottom part of craft

The craft suddenly appeared at the right (NE) of the vehicle over a farmer's plowed corn field. At a slow rate of speed, it came across the field, towards the car, flew just above the trees that lined the street. It flew over the tree line (to the NW) and disappeared for a few seconds then appeared right over the car again and flew back in the direction it came from so quickly, it just vanished in the blink of an eye.
