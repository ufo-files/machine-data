Glowing sphere

Multiple glowing spheres have been spotted on a very regular basis by different residence over the past few months across the county. It's become a such a regular occurrence we feel the need to reach out.

Glowing spheres appear in the northwest, hover & follow residence at a distance then disappear heading due west.

Very bright piercing orange red ball of light. 

Hair stood on end, slight humming to be heard. First couple of sightings was in utter disbelief, not just very uncomfortable and worried.
