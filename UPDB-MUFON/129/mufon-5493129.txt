{nd} leaving work at 5am Spotted 3 triangle shaped objects in the sky just above the power poles near my home at the time.

Sometime around the 15 th of Nov 1967 me and my coworkers were going home after our shift it was about 5:30 am when suddenly there were 3 dark colored triangle shaped objects appear just above the power poles the moon was full so it was not hard to make out that this was not something you see every day. And they were about 3 to 4 feet in size and they came down to check us out they made a circle around our car and they hovered for a few minutes and they were gone in just a couple of seconds.
