{rjl} Took picture of helicoptor at recent Atlas launch. Zoomed pic and found unknown object in front of copter's nose

I took several pictures while awaiting the launch of the most recent Atlas rocket from Cape Canaveral. Upon review of my pictures, one I took of a helicopter has a clear disc shaped object just off the nose of the copter in the picture. Clearly this was closed airspace, and the object seems too distant and high to be a bird. I was unaware I caught the object in the picture until I reviewed it the next day.
