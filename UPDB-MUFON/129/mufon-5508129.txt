This took place in Olean, NY at about 8:30 pm. The object was a circular fireball that had a lateral trajectory. It was not a meteor or shooting star--it was large and beneath the clouds. At first, I assumed it must be an airplane or helicopters light. However, as I looked closer, it was clearly a flaming ball of some sort. Ive seen fireworks of all kinds and have never seen anything like this. The object, again, had a steady trajectory across the sky and did not lose velocity or brightness. I lost sight of the object when it went behind the treeline behind my home.
