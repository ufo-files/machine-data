{cak} Orange light,orange cone. Disrupt airport?

Driving to work about 5:30am on the I-15 south, I noticed on my right side above the hill an orange light with an orange cone slightly angled down. It looked like the orange cone flag you see at an airport only brightly lighted. My thought was ,when did they put a flag on the mountain I never noticed that before. It was only about a half inch above the hilltop so about the right height to be a flag. So I was watching it ,driving along ,and after about 10 seconds it disappeared. Now I got excited! so now I'm watching the sky as I'm driving. I'm away from the area with the hill now, and BOOM. Now the orange light appeared in a different part of the sky and seemed as if it had smaller lights underneath it or maybe kinda morphed the shape a little bit. Illuminated the sky kind of like when an airplane flies to the cloud. About the same amount of time it disappeared. I'm sooo excited I saw a UFO!!!! It wasn't done yet. Once more it appears ,now at the south end of Rancho Cucamonga, near Ontario international airport. Bright orange light slowly descending. This was at 5:40am. I finished my commute watching the sky the best I could. Then it occurred to me, no flights were landing or leaving from Ontario. This is very strange. The morning hours are busy for flights from that airport. I arrived at my office at around 6:00am and started telling the guys. Most gave it a laugh until I showed them no planes were coming or going. We saw passengers planes in the sky only far away near other airports. This all happened with a clear sky no clouds,other lights or obstructions.
