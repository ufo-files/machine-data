VA Day Celebration Fly by with UFO's Mimicking formation

On Veteran's day the Leavenworth community was conducting their annual Veterans Day Parade. While observing the AF jet flyby I recorded approximately 6 seconds and when reviewing the video I noticed several flashes looking like strobe lights.  Upon close examination I noticed orbs that were ascending and descending at an incredible speed and suddenly stopping and hovering in multiple formations. Once the jets got close to their position thee orbs assembled in formations of three by three with one orb in over watch position. After the jets passed by the orbs assumed a square formation of four. After wards the orbs disappeared ascending at high rate of speed and some proceeded at a slower speed towards the NW sky.
