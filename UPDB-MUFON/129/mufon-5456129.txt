Saw the ufo from a distance. We believed it was a plane as we were driving next to a small local airport. As we got closer we realized it was not an airplane as there was no blinking light. At first we only saw two lights, but as we drove towards it we saw three, one on each tip of the craft. We began filming the ufo when it started hovering shortly and then taking off very quickly multiple times. The craft looped around the sky above the airport and got further away. As we kept driving, it began getting closer to our car while lowering down towards us. As it flew over the car from left to right, we kept driving straight as there was no where to turn around. We were all quite scared when the craft flew over the car because of how much lower it had descended above us. At this point we were all shook up and stopped filming. By the time we got to a driveway to turn around, the ufo appeared to be landing behind the tree line. We drove around a little longer and never saw it again.
