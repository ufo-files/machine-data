Equilateral triangle formation of three greenish-white lights, red light in the centre, completely silent, about 500 feet up.

Walking home at 11:30 pm, it was a cloudless starless sky over the city. I happened to look up to see three greenish-white lights, in equilateral formation, moving eastward, with one apex leading. Thinking it was an airplane, I noticed a red light at the exact centre, which is odd since the red light is always on the left wingtip. Then I noticed the object was completely silent, travelling due east at least 500 feet up. An airplane soon followed, over 1000 feet up. I could clearly hear the engine. The object continued east in a straight path until lost behind the trees.
