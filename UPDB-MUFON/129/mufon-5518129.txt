My friend and I were in a pasture at around 11pm in summer 2006 when we both saw several small lights in the sky, seemingly in space. The lights erupted into many colors and expanding circles. This lasted for several minutes before they again erupted, this time looking like a spaceship going into hyperdrive. At first we thought they were fireworks, but they were too far away. We lost sight of the object when the lights dissipated. Thinking something big just happened, we dashed inside and turned on the news, but saw nothing. I never thought anything of it until recently, and I have poured over youtube videos of exploding stars and UFO sightings, with nothing even closely related. There had been nothing in the sky prior to or after the sighting. I hope you can help me determine what this thing was, whether natural atmospheric/space phenomena or whatever else. Unfortunately I don't have any footage of the event as by the time we went back outside with a camera, the lights were gone.
