{rjl} Triangle,3 white/yellow lights in equilateral triangle, red pulsating light in center

First sighted object nearly overhead out living room window. Quickly ran outside, observed object travel due north at about 100 - 200 mph, then appear to move north-northeast and descend towards intermodal rail yard to north, near Hoytville, Ohio. As the object moved north, first the front light, then the red center light became obscured by the structure of the craft. It appeared to hover above the freight yard, this may have been a trick of perspective, however. At this point, only the 2 aftmost lights were still visible. I observed that the distance between the aft lights appeared to be about half the distance between the lights atop light poles at the freight yard. Realizing that the craft was headed North towards Bowling Green,I ran inside to get my phone to text my friend to keep an eye out for it. Upon returning outside with my phone,I could no long see the object. I have some sketches I made Monday at work while trying to keep it fresh in my head. The most unsettling thing was the complete lack of sound. How do you keep an vehicle that size in the air without any noise? My daughter saw the object through the window,but did not accompany me outside.
