Large cigar shaped with windows all the way across the whole object that had very bright light coming from every window

After a sudden rushed urge to sit out on the porch I quickly hurried out the front door with my fiancé just behind me. As I step out the door I noticed a long bright object moving through the sky upward just above the horizon. I immediately pointed it out to my fiancé and I at the same time stumble over a few words (basically “is that a chem trail?”) them both immediately say “no that is…..” as my fiancé trailed off I continued with “What the hell am I looking at?” He immediately started (what we both witnessed) recording it on his iPhone as it ascended higher into the sky above us. At this point with the craft although very far up in the sky was very visible to the naked eye. We could both clearly see the cigar shape craft completely lit up by what appeared to be “airplane like” windows that were all very brightly lit. It continued on its weaving upward path in the clear night sky till it disappeared into the middle of a clear sky. We were amazed by what we just saw and we scrambled to rewatch it on the video from the iPhone but all that was there was a still picture of a long blurred object in the sky. I went inside and immediately sketched a picture as I didn’t want to forget a single detail!!!
