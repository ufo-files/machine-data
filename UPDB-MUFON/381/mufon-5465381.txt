{tjd} Cloud with lightning flashing inside of it, small orbs going into it

I was sitting on the porch in the afternoon.  I watched a cloud form then seen over 10 orb shaped glowing lights go around this cloud, and then into the middle of it. Once all these glowing orbs were finished going into this cloud the cloud disappeared.There was flashes of lightning in the middle of this cloud. There was no sound. It was the only cloud in the sky that day.
