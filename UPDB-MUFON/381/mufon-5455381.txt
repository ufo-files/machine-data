{cak} Low flying small object with white lights.

Mike and I saw a UFO. It was 1:30 am and we were traveling north on Highway 97 approximately milepost 50 between Goldendale and Toppenish on the Yakama Reservation. It was pitch dark. There were no other cars on the road. The UFO was traveling toward us (but not directly at us) at about the same rate of speed we were traveling. It was traveling left of the left lane over the creek. As it got closer Mike and I said at the exact same time, “What the hell is that!?” It was too high to be a car but not by much. It was flying very low. Had white lights kinda like headlights but more than two lights. It was small, too small to be manned by a human. Appeared to be saucer shaped. It passed us by. We wish we had stopped to watch it.
