On October 11 of 2018. I was working at the Calphalon Corporation doing preventive maintenance work on the HVAC units on the building. While coming off of the roof I went to my work van. I had opened the back door to my van and had heard the telephone pole wires having a noticeable humm coming from the wires. It was a sunny day, not very much cloud cover. While looking up at the pole I seen an object hovering in the sky. The shimmering of the object is what caught my eye. I was just staring at it and then it went of to the east very fast in a horizontal line. As I was watching this 2 fighter planes from the south came roaring over my head. Before I heard the planes I snapped a picture of it on my work phone. It was too small to see on my work phone I blew the picture up as much as I could and took a picture with my personal phone. I called mufon that day and to be honest felt kinda of stupid calling and just let it go. I was at my diner table going through pictures and came across the picture. So I am giving it to you guys. To be honest I have no idea what I saw. But here is the picture.
