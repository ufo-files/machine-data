{jpg} Green light moving, hovered, moved, hovered, moved out of site - all in a straight line

I was on a rooftop with a number of people. We were in downtown Omaha where light pollution is significant. My friend and I were chatting when we both looked up to the same green light as it was moving from the approximate N to S of the sky while we were facing East looking up at about 75 degrees. The green light was on a path for about 5-10 degrees, moving very quickly (faster than a commercial plane or helicopter) then stopped immediately, stayed there for about 10-15 seconds, and immediately started moving along the same path for about another 5-10 degrees and stopped as it did before. It started again in the same manner and then faded out of our site but looked like it just continued moving and probably didn't stop again. The stopping and starting were immediate. They were quickly slowing or accelerating - it was instant. We both said, did you see that? We both said yes and had no idea what we saw. It did appear that the object was headed to Offutt Air Force Base which is about 15 miles to our South.
