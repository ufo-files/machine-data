{tjd} Coworker observed over the Hamburg public library changing shapes

1000 - 1430 hours

Object appears to look like bright star magnifying in luminosity. Object first slowly moves in a figure 8 pattern then speeds up, hovers slowly, repeating the pattern in different areas. Object changes into octopus like shape with the light trails resembling tentacles with smaller orbs of light at the end of each trail. Repeats pattern several more minutes then changes shape back to large star like shape and brightened. This was bright enough to see on a perfectly sunny day and moved in relatively jerking ways changing direction abruptly between the patterns. This was observed by several others that were outside in southern Hamburg.
