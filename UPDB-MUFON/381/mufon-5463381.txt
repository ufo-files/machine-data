It was cloked in darkness. Slowly moving over my house 3 lights appear like a gate and it went through it and vanished.

There was a bright star in the West for a number of weeks. I came home one night and went inside. Came back out to clear the car and I looked up at the stars from the front of my house as I watch satellites come overhead often and I noticed a dark clocked shape maybe 100 meters above the ground directly above my house just hanging there. I grabbed my smart phone to record it and it shut down. I ran inside to grab my camera and a second cellphone to try and record. Neither came on. They were dead. I observed the object and can only describe it as aerodynamic and sleek. No noise. 3 lights appeared to the east in the form of a triangle. The object moved toward it slowly and connected and it and the 3 lights vanished. Then my camera came on and so did my cellular phones.The whole experienced lasted about 15 minutes. I don't know how long it was sitting there before. I don't want to share my details because I want to remain anonymous. I just wanted to share and make someone aware that these sightings also happen outside the United States.
