I saw three objects which hovered and may have ejected another another

I was walking along the west path along Belle Isles beach. I looked up to see something shiny fall very quickly away from three round, grayish objects. At first, I thought they were kites or helicopters but they didn't move. They seemed to slowly drift toward each other over the course of ten seconds. Cloud cover moved in and obscured my view of the objects. After the clouds cleared a bit, I could only see one and then I couldn't see them at all. It was like they faded away.
