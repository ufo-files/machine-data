{cak} Straight line path, orange red circle during fireworks displays.

I was watching fireworks over an elementary school, and was on the look out for lights in the sky that were unrelated to the fireworks. I saw an orange red circle travel across the sky in a straight line, not blinking or flashing. I saw this happen in the same path a total of four times in ten minutes. I also saw the same thing, only further away, traveling in the same direction. I only lost sight of the object when it traveled too far away to see. I had seen a couple of planes fly over before I noticed the circles, and the planes all had multiple flashing lights on them and were much bigger. I was excited to see something unusual, the thought of seeing an extraterrestrial craft was amazing!
