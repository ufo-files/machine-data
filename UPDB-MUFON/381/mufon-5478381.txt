3 orange fireballs hovering over bypass in maple ridge on xmas day

I heard my dog barking outside so I went outside to quiet him and when I turned the corner of my house, I was startled by 3 orange orbs in the sky. They were radiating an incredible light- like the plasma in neon signs, or embers in a fire (photos do not do it justice). I began filming them with my phone. Two of the orbs hovered to the left of the sky while the third one traveled to the right , out of view of my phone. My phone began malfunctioning while I was filming. The two left hand orbs became difficult to see. I noticed the third orb which was on the right side was very visible but my phone kept shutting off. I was able to catch it for a couple seconds before it became blocked by my neighbors house.
