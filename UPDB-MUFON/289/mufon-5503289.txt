{rjl} Translucent sphere, bright blue, traveling EXTREMELY fast...no sound.

Another officer and I were working an off-duty job at a game room in Jacksonville. We were out in the parking lot just talking and, all of a sudden, from the s/w heading to the n/e was this large, translucent blue 1sphere1 which streaked across the sky at incredible speed. It went from horizon to horizon in about 1 1/2 seconds and there was NO sound whatsoever. The altitude of this object appeared to be approximately 500 to 1,000 ft.
