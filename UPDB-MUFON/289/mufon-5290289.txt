Due west, red orb w/cross or tic-tac-toe design

Edmonds, Wa looking due west (per street grid & orientation of home), about 7-8pm. My older sister Jan, a neighbor Dave & me were upstairs listening to 45's. I saw something out the window & told the others to look, over the neighbors trees. The object was over the neighbors trees (evergreens about 150 yds away), half as much taller than the trees was a solid red, uniformly lit orb. This orb had a distinct tic-tac-toe pattern in black lines over red, it moved rapidly from side to side over a distance # times it's width, a steady almost blinking appearance. It did not appear to be in any other motion or moving in any direction, while looking stationary, it did not approach or move away. At the time of observation, we had no ability to determine distance to or the size of the object due to it being a dark clear night. After a 10 minute time frame, it just disappeared. On our property we had a horse pasture which took up the western half of a square, 2 acre lot; the grass in the pasture stood about 2-3 ft high because the field was currently vacant. The tall grass is how I'm estimating when this was, this incident took place 1968-1969. My father died in 1965 & I know sighting was a few years later. I know I was a snow skier at the time, because the next weekend I told someone the story on the ski slopes. The morning after the sighting I was out in our front yard & noticed a circular pattern in the grass in the pasture. A circle of grass 15'-18' was evenly smashed down flat, there were no burn marks or other indentations. This made me wonder.
Before I entered college at the age of 29, I completed a series of aptitude tests through vocational rehabilitation. My case worker said my perceptual scores were literally off the charts, I scored in the top 2%. She said I could do anything I selected as far as a program. This was 1988, I completed the AA degree program in CAD drafting in half the time & got a job at [company name moved to Investigators report section/CMS/fgc] in Tool & Production Planning. My point is I my visual skills are significant. Only after finding that circle in the grass can I estimate the actual distance of the object. That object had to be within 1 mile of our house when spotted.
