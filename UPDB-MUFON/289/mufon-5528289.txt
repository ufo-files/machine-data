3 lights at the corners of a triangle. Flying low and arcing toward the ground.

In blue bell, Pa, at 7:43 PM, i was sitting in the car with my friend outside his house, when we noticed three lights in a triangle in the sky. We watched it, and we knew it wasn't airplane because it was so close to the ground and it was arcing toward the ground, in a parabolic arc, nothing like the path of an airplane. We were shocked when we saw it, too shocked to think about taking a picture until it was too late. It went below the tree line after about 3 seconds and we lost sight of it for good.
