saw a light in the sky, like a star but much brighter and moved

It was around 4:20 AM and I was outside having a smoke, star-gazing and this one caught my eye. It was the brightest one in the sky. I was still, under a over-hang and it seemed to be going out of sight - slowly. I kept an eye on it, it went up a little then over and at one point there seemed to be like sparkles come off it! It moved faster by now and just kept going out of sight. It was different seeing something come from it, other times there is just a light. There was one time I called the wife to come see, she watched with me for 5-10 seconds this little white light bounce all across the eastern part of the sky checking out stars like a bird going to flowers. Anyway, this time was different so I even called Colleen at work and told her. I am still at awe.
