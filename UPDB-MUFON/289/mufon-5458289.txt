At about 4:30am, while at work, a colleague and I both saw a yellowish, green light slowly falling/descending. It was going straight down.

The object was round in shape, and glowed, with no trail. It appeared in size, from us, almost double the size of an aspirin.

Guessing, we both figure it landed/crashed about 10-15 miles from our location.
