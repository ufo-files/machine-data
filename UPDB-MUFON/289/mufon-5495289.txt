{rjl} Mutiple orbs, flashing, looking metallic and reflective..never seen anything like them.

I was working at my desk in my home office and at aproximately 3:15, I glanced out my window and saw several orbs pulsating up in the sky. At least over 10. At first, I though it was debris in the air but after studying it for a few minutes, I realized they weren't decending. My window faces north and they were moving north east. I took a video. Weather balloons are much larger to my knowledge and I just can't account for their flashing. They were reflective. I studied them for 15mins altogether before they went out of my view.
