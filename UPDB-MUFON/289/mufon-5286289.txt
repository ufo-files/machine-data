Observed white dot in a stationary position for 60 seconds

UAP sightings Franville Ontario

June 28 
Approximatley 19:30 hrs
Sighting duration: 60 seconds.

Witnessed by my wife and I..

At the above stated time I saw, in the north west sky, what appeared to be a distinct white dot. This dot was just sitting in the sky adjacent to a jet stream being produced by a airliner which was moving a northerly direction away from the dot.

I alerted my wife to what I noticed and asked her to verify she saw the white dot too, which she did confirm. From this point on I continued to watch the dot. After first it completely disappeared almost like a visual pop. Seconds later it reappeared and then went slightly translucent in the middle leaving it to look like a circle. Then a second later when back to being a solid white dot again. Approximately 10 seconds later it simply vanished gradually in a manner that I can only describe as "de-materializing" like in star trek. It did not move left or right, away from us or closer to us...simply just got fuzzy and gradually disappeared, remaining in the same place and size.
