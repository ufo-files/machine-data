{cak} I saw large orbs after viewing photos taken. I also noted an object that looked like a tear shaped UFO after magnifying the photo.

On 8-10-2014 my husband and I went to South Portland's "Bug Light" on the ocean front to take pictures of the Super Moon. When we arrived about 7:30 the moon was hidden behind clouds as it had started to rise. When the moon emerged from the clouds around 8PM I took pictures of it and of the lights across the water. When I got home and put the pictures on my iMAC I had 5-6 pictures of UFOs. Some were large whitish spheres and one was a small light that I magnified and saw was a tear shaped object that looked like many other reported UFOs on Filer's Files. This was the second time spheres have appeared on pictures I have taken on the Maine coast. The first I sent was 11-20-2011 at Scarborough Beach. It was reported on MUFON and I was told it was sea spray. It wasn't. There is a very faint large sphere above and to the right of the Super Moon, as well.
