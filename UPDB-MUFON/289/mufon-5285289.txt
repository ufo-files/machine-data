2 Unknown objects observed following low flying Cessna

Sitting in the backyard and observed a small Cessna flying fairly low. It caught my eye because of how low it was flying compared to other small aircraft that frequent the area. Immediately following the plane I observed an object traveling in the same direction but higher in the sky. At first I thought it was another aircraft; however, there was a single, bottom pulsating white light underneath the craft and then I observed it, on the same course, make quick and smooth angular changes in the sky. The object continued until it was out sight. While looking over the tree line I then witnessed what at first looked like a foil birthday balloon fly from East to West in front of the house. The object stayed saucer like with the front somewhat raised and then disappeared. It happened so fast that I was unable to get my phone to document. Incredible experience.
