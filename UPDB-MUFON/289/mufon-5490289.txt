I was at the waterfront in Toronto watching the Perseids, only saw about 2 dozen. I was lying on the ground had been there about an hour and a half. Then I saw what I thought was another meteor I tracked it and it seemed to move in a zigzag perhaps even changing speed. Most meteors appear, increase in brightness and then quickly fade to black. This thing didn't do that. It just kept moving towards the East or North East, didn't increase in brightness, it was exactly like a satellite, but didn't move in a steady uniform motion. I've seen satellites before this thing didn't match what i've seen. After a few seconds I knew this was not a meteor or a satellite, I'm fairly certain it was something else. Its brightness faded after 30 - 45 seconds.
