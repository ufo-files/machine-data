Two flashing lights over Derby

I was sat in my garden playing with the dog At 11:45 am when I noticed two very bright Flashing star like lights Travelling from west to east going against the wind which was going from south to north 
One light was about 3-4 seconds in front of the other at high altitude when the tailing stopped and hovered and then moved to the south where it stopped next to a cloud for 30 to 40 seconds 
I ran into the house to get my binoculars which were next to the door I was gone for no more the 4 seconds maximum but when I got back both lights had gone
