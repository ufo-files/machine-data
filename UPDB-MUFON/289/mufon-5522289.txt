Observed two lights telephone pole height size 2-3 ft dia red and white while driving to work about 1.5 from home. lights where so bright I had to sheild my eyes

I left work at about 6:10 am, November 2008 it was still dark out, the road that I drive down is always deserted at that time as I traveled about 1.5 from my home I encountered two lights red and white on my right side about telephone pole height and stationary. They were about 2-3 ft in diameter as I got closer about half a block away I slowed my truck down to about 3-5mph until I was within 20ft or so it was just off the road. I stopped for 1minute or so,the lights were so bright I had to shield my eyes they were brighter then any lights I have seen in my life time., I knew that nothing could be this low and stationary, I was nervous and scared so I proceeded to work, I have never seen any thing like that in the 16 years I have drove down this road. I could not believe what I saw that morning and it still remains with me today.
