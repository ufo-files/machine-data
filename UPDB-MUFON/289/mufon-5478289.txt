{jpg} Star gazing saw oval white light moving East very fast then turned 90 deg to SW

My wife and I like to sit out back and stargaze. We often see unexplainable objects in the sky and have started looking for these. Tonight we saw a bright oval object appear overhead very high up...if I had to guess, I'd say 15,000 ft. but it is just a guess. The object was traveling East very fast when it lit up. It literally made a hard 90 degree turn to the South-West as the light faded it sped off across the sky and out of sight...The event lasted only seconds but we both saw it. Very exciting observation!
