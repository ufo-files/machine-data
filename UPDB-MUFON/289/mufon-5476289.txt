{rjl} Shape shifting, cloaking UFO following 4 jet chem trails on 10.11.2016, around 12:00pm.

On 10.11.2016, around 12:00pm, I returned home from work and noticed a white dot changing shapes and cloaking, as it traveled along 4 Chem trails left behind by supersonic jets that flew over my neighborhood. In my pictures, the UFO is above the top line of the 4 lines that the supersonic jets left behind. Upon review of my pictures of the UFO, I noticed that the white dot initially began as 1 light, then changed to 2 lights, then a triangle shape, then 4 lights, then 6 lights, & finally back down to 2 lights, then 1 light. The UFO rotated, changed shapes, cloaked, & became stationary several times, as well as cloaking itself when a commercial plane flew by & then reappearing once the plane flew out of sight. I asked my neighbor and my wife to witness the UFO along with me. When they came outside, the UFO that I had seen traveling West was gone, however, another UFO was witnessed by all 3 of us, now traveling North, toward Chem trails left by a second set of 4 supersonic jets that flew overhead, after the initial UFO was seen following along the first set of Chem trails. This 2nd UFO seemed to be close & was also changing shapes. I managed to get a short video of it, however, the video is shaky. After confirming what she saw was a UFO, my wife was so scared of what she had seen that she ran back into the house. My neighbor also confirmed that what she had seen was a UFO.
