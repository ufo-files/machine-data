My dogs began growling in the living room at 11 p.m., Thursday, April 5, 2018, CST. I live on a farm and the cows in the pasture across the street were mooing loudly. I went to the large picture window in the living room facing west. I looked for coyotes or other animals. My dogs wer looking out the glass of the door and the window. The cows were looking up. I observed a huge round white light traveling slowly towards my direction. The light became larger and brighter, then appeared to stop, and began pulsating. This continued 15 minutes, then like flicking off a light switch, it was gone. As soon as it disappeared, the cows quieted, and my dogs stopped growling. Everything was still and quiet. I looked at the clock, and 15 minutes had passed since I began watching. I was curious and tried to reason out what it could be. It was odd that my dogs never barked. I felt very alert. This is not my first experience here. It is a rural area with an open sky.
