Three bright orange orbs flying

I was standing in my backyard having a cigarette and i saw what I thought was a lantern crossing the sky above the roof tops in my suburb gainig altitude and travelleling at some speed. I thought nothing of it. I walked to the other side of my garden and it appeared in the distance over my roof line travelling in a northwards direction gainig altitude and speed away from me and into the darkened clouds then disappearing. I wasnt sure at the time what it was. (Bright orange orb). Then I seen two more orange orbs following in a similar direction a 30 - 60 seconds between each sighting. The last was closer and seemed brighter but followed the same flight path as the first two. There was no noise I could hear.
I was fixated on them and unfortunetley didn't get any pictures.I just stared at them and was trying to work out what I could be seeing. I dismiss them being a lantern as they were really bright orange and disappeared from view within a minute. Also there was hardly any wind. Quite a freaky experience as I have never seen anything like this before. Hopefully I wont be the only person reporting this as I live in a populated area of Melbourne.
This sighting has happened at night, the only feature I could see was the bright orange orbs. The date is the 14/06/2013. Sighting ocurred at 10.15pm
