(GS) 2 orange ufos flying together out of the west, one went straight the other turned and went east and disappeared

I was driving from the north down M-97 (Groesbeck) when I looked up and saw 2 bright orange foot ball" shaped objects side by side traveling from the north,then heading west. I kept watching these objects when the one on the left veered off towards the east. I looked back and couldnt see the other one, so I kept watching the one headed east. Then it just disappeared. There was an airplane flying south, about the time this happened. It was 6:05 and I called my mom and as I was describing what was happening that was when the one heading east disappeared. No sound or trials came from these objects. They were brighter orange in the center with a more of a bright yellow edge. I hope I am describing this right! It was so crazy to see. Even though they looked close together, they were probably a mile? apart from each other.
