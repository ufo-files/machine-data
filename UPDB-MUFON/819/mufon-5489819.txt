{jpg} Black triangle craft close to me doing same speed as my car 25-35mph

I grew up in queen creek behind williams Air Force base and at one point lived on the base. It's no longer active. So I was driving to a friends house in the evening and I always look at the landing strip as I head down rittenhouse road. This happened two years before the government announced stealth fighters so reporting it this late I may be wrong about the year but should have been around 97. So I'm driving looking at the base and I see this giant black triangle with green round lights. On in front two on ends. I told people about it but a few years later they came out with stealth so I never reported but I've been told they don't fly with lights on only when landing so that's why I am reporting now. So this thing was at least 500 feet long solid black flying the same way I'm driving. It going so slow that's what caught my attention it was so big it should have fell out of the sky as slow and big it was. I rolled down the window and no sound I also attributed that to the stealth later on in life. It was flying low and slow and coming from the base I don't know if it took off from there or just went over it I caught it just a few hundred feet from the run way. We drove side by side I drove for two miles until I had to turn off to get to my friends road as I took the right turn I stopped an watched it continue east and the moon was full it passed right in front of the moon again very low I made out the entire structure a perfect solid black triangle
