Stationary Craft Observed NE of Catalina Foothills, Tucson, AZ

First saw the light holding still over the mountains to the nort-east of my location, not movement at all. WIth binoculars, saw that there were two closely spaced lights, one red, one white; the white remained steady, the red blinked on and off. It did not move at all, was not a helicopter. It stayed in place for over twenty minutes, then I got bored and cold just watching it sit there, and went inside. Checked through a window approx. 10 minutes later, and it was sitll there, same location.
