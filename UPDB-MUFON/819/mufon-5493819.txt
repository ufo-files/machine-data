Going toward to to get something to eat. My friend spotted the lights over the field next to us. It was stationary about 400 feet in the air. We turned down the next road to go see what it was. As we turned, we saw the back of it and there were 2 white lights on the outsides and 2 green lights in between randomly flashing. As we started down the road, it took off toward the east. By time we had traveled half a mile, the object was at the horizon.
