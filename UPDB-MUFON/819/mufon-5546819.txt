very bright and quiet

my girlfriend and i were sitting on a golf car, behind her parents house after a late campfire. we were watching the sky for roughly an hour. then i saw a relly bright light from the corner of my eye, so i looked at it through the trees, and figured it was the moon. a few minutes later, the bright light had moved into plain view. we watched it for maybe 10 minutes. it was flying very slow, so slow if it was anything else it would have fallen out of the sky. it was very big, as i said before i thought it was the moon. so i got very excited and ran to tell her father to come and check this out, by the time he came out it was the size of a pin head and was very far away. then the wierd thing, a few weeks later we find out we are preagnant for our first child. i have had expierences ever since i was a little boy. but i never put 2 and 2 together. all i have to say to people who think we are crazy, spend atleast an hour looking at the sky a night for a month and tell me you havnt seen anything! i hope you like the story and it is written as i remember it.
