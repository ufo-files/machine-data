V-Shaped Stealth/Cloaked Craft with Afterglow Lights

I was outside my house walking to my car and looked up at the Big Dipper and scanned across the sky to Orion and to try to identify other constellations and I saw ANOTHER craft, v-shaped in a black stealth/cloaked form. With low glowing lights in a v-shape—it kinda looked like how when you turn off your light you can see the after flow essence of the light from when it was on. It was significantly lower than planes and not on the normal plane routes. It was moving fast across the sky but waved around like it was almost floating with the wind or like a feather and then it just disappeared. It was definitely plane size, very large. 

I had an anxiety attack after and it’s hard for me to believe what I just saw. Just like the last UFO I saw (again in my own front yard), I only saw it because I was already looking at the night sky. It would not be visible to your eyes if they were not already focused to the night. 

I strongly believe this could be secret military craft. I’m in awe how I’ve seen two UFOs in the span of a month (two completely different stealth craft types too). Living in San Diego, I know we have a strong military presence here and I’m assuming that’s what this is? But I am not ruling out extraterrestrial contact either.
