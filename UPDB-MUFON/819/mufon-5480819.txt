{rjl} Transparent UFO encounter near woods

The event happened last year, the night before Halloween. It was about 11:15 at night. I had just gotten home from work. I got out of my car and went around to the passenger side to get my bags out. Right before I grabbed the handle, I heard what sounded like a big gust of wind approaching. I turned around to look at the tops of the trees on the hill across from the parking lot. Nothing was moving. I stared and waited. Then a warbling sound (like sheet metal being moved) mixed in with the wind sound. I still stared. Then, I realized that the sound was now above the trees, but yet I couldn't see a thing. Where this craft was, I could see the stars in the sky twinkling. Then, extremely quickly, it was hovering right above me, very close, about 6 to 8 feet above me. The warbling sound stopped by now. I could hear what sounded like air moving extremely fast around it. It was moving from top to bottom, too to bottom, not from side to the other side. Still, I could see nothing. It moved up, then back down to exactly where it was, then up, back, then up and....gone. Just gone. I will never forget that. It all happened pretty quickly. I was never scared. I'm not sure why. I think it might be because my mind was going a million miles a minute trying to make sense of what was going on....why I couldn't see this thing. Anyhow, I went inside and immediately checked the clock. 11:20. No time lost. So I don't believe I was taken anywhere. By the way, the spot in the trees where it was at...there are power lines that run through there. There were no lights that I could observe. Nothing but sound. It was incredible.
