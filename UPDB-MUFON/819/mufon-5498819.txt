{cak} green yellow purple and red pulsating lights from a flat round craft

We went outside at 3:20am and I noticed a really bright pulsating light so we look harder at it and notice red green and purple pulsating lights. So we grabbed the binoculars and observed flashing pulsating lights (in the order from left to right) green,yellow, purple and red. We could tell that the object was flat at the bottom and round. It was hovering but then started moving closer and erratically right to left. Then we noticed a plane from the south flying toward the object. As the place got closer the object ascended very quickly and away from the oncoming place and then started to hover completely still. The plane passed and was much lower than the object. It started to get further away and was staying pretty stationary but was moving to the right and left a lot. Another plane went straight for it about 10 minutes later and did the same thing the other plane did but the object didn't move. The plane flew by. We watched it move closer, left, right, further away. We watched for 45 minutes but it's still in the same area. It's been there for about an hour now. I don't know how long it was there before we saw it. It's a huge craft, very far away and high in the sky but we could definitely tell every time it moved or got closer or further away. Very crazy experience. Frightening, yet I couldnt look away. This is my third UFO experience in this area of Texas and I haven't gone looking for it, just happened to look up at the right moment.
