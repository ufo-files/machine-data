We were on our honeymoon and about 3 days into our week trip in Lahaina. It was a crystal clear night and just like the previous nights we sat on the 13 floor balcony star gazing. We had seen satellites small aircraft, helicopters the previous nights. We both saw a small bright satellite moving across the sky fairly fast. As we watched it turned and headed straight for us. It changed shape to a very large and bright light like a spotlight. The light at one point shined right in our eyes. It then shot off in reverse at about a 45 degree angle and disappeared. No sound whatsoever. Neither of us drink or do any drugs. We still can't explain what we saw.
