Silver saucer shaped disc with colored lights.

First saw it hovering about 100ft off the ground making no noise. My guess would be that it was about 40ft. in diameter. Had colored lights that I believe were on constantly. Was about 500ft. from me but I could tell it was shiny silver in color and the typical saucer shape with a smaller dome on top.  Remained motionless for a few moments then started moving slowly to the north. Did that for about 20 seconds than accelerated to the east very quickly and it was gone in a second or two. 
This happened about 43 years ago and I still remember it like it was yesterday. Thought I should tell my story before my memory does not allow me to do so.
