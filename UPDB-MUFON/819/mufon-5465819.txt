I was with two friends looking at the sea on the beach that night when we saw that light descending into the water and sinking in.

I was with two friends at the beach looking at the sea when we saw a blue glowing object descending into the water. We pointed at it and we asked each other what would it be, while it was descending. We three thought since the first time that it was an UFO, it was impossible to be anything more.

It appeared above us, aproximately 1 mile of distance between us and the horizon. It just descended into the water and sunk in. 

We just were shocked, we didn't believe what we just have seen, and we remained motionless, looking at the horizon without doing anything.

We lost sight of the object just because it sunk into the water.
