Pictures taken from airplanes orbs, lights, tic tac shaped, square and varied UFO's and several ships hovering beside plane.

When I Zoomed in things got really strange. I just saw the spaceship because someone else pointed it out and I didn't realize until just now that there appears to be at least three ships in the background.
