Strange atmospheric change and lights

On this night I stepped out side my house to smoke a cigarette when I noticed to my right near the center of town a mist stretched across and was multiple colors starting from green on the right to orange and red. Beside the sound of the cicadas I felt a hum. I could feel it from the ground vibrate through my body. I started to recording the strange mist(which by the way there was no smell of smoke…because I thought maybe a bonfire and the smoke just caught the town lights) the air was still with no breeze and almost felt like it was charged kind of like static. Anyways while zooming in I caught a flash of a light in the mist. I thought maybe a drone but the light flashed quickly and changed direction instantaneously and appeared at different locations of the sky almost erratic but still ascending at the same time. I didn’t see anything else after it disappeared and the mist moved out of town within maybe 30-45 minutes. While the mist was still clearing I heard aircraft approaching and flying low overhead and then maybe 2 or 3 I saw them crossover town from different directions almost grid like. I forgot about this video until I was making a TikTok the other day and found it in my archives. It was 3 years ago but I will probably never forget that night . Around 2:30 min of video is where I see the first flash in the mist and the object appears maybe 2 or 3 more times.
