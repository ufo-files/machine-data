{cak} Appeared to be 7 satellites in a row going same direction with equal distance apart

Last night at 4:31am, my dogs wanted to go out. I now live in Green Valley, Arizona facing the incredible Santa Rita Mountains – close to Mt Hopkins) I was hoping to see some of the meteors that are supposed to be visible in the next few days but what I observed has me a bit rattled.  The moon and three planets were quite visible at that time of night. I usually can spot a satellite pretty easily and wonder of wonders, I saw one almost immediately going from W/NW to E/SE.  I then saw one right after it going exactly the same direction.  Then I looked in front of those two and saw another one – each spaced about the same distance apart. Then I observed more following the first one I saw and in total I counted 7 in a row.  There could have been more where I saw the one furthest away but by then, that satellite was barely visible.  All were solid color just like a normal satellite but not as bright as the space lab.   In all of my years looking at the stars, I have never once seen two in a row and certainly not 7.   After the 7th, I did not see another following them.  They were certainly high into the atmosphere.
