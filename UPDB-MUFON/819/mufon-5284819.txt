Metallic looking tic tac … have never seen anything like it

We were on vacation in a cabin in West Virginia. We saw two of the same ufos the day before with a few others. Two people we were with spotted them first and said they circled each other then went in separate directions. I saw them going in different directions, but not the circling. The next day my wife and I were outside by ourselves most of the day. There were 4 or 5 flyovers helicopters and jets over the course of the whole morning and afternoon. Then around the same time 8:20 pm as the day before I saw one ufo coming across the sky… a metallic tic tac. I told my wife to come over and look… it lasted about a minute and it was a direct flight pattern. I managed to get 54 seconds of video.
