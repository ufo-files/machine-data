Object observed flying through clouds in photo

While driving on the highway near Berge, Germany, I took a series of photos of a huge white cloud that was very beautiful. Driving through the countryside we were passing some giant windmills that I thought would look nice to take a photo of with the giant cloud in the background. I took the photos and then then I looked over them in my photo gallery and that's when I noticed a black object in the last photo. The object does not appear in any other pictures (although possibly as a spot in the picture I took immediately previously). There are no black spots on the window so I know it is not a window spot. I did not see the object when taking the picture, only upon the review of the photo did I notice it.
