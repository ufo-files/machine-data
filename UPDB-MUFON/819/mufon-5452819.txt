UFO seen while walking my baby at night

I was walking with my baby's father and my baby in her stroller at night at exactly 9:48pm and he told me "look!" and points at a glowing white light in the sky traveling at the slow pace to the west. We both can't take our eyes off it and as were staring at it, it suddenly stops abruptly and then continues to go straight again withtin a millisecond. The UFO begins to move in a wavy path up and down. Then as we start talking about it, it seems to start moving up towards space and slowly disappears. As it is almost out of sight completely, it starts to flash red and then its gone into space.
