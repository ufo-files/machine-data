{dr}Red orbs slowly ascended formed triangle and cross shape moved then dissperated

Me my brother and dad were in the safeway parking lot when on the horizon I saw 3 red orbs rise from very low(seemed to be over water) they rose about a degree every 3 seconds. At about what I think the level the jets here fly at cruise height they stopped for about 2 minutes they either slowly moved to (from my perpective 3cm) or quickly "teleported" to there. They formed a triangle and a cross in the sky. After about 5 minutes "hovering" in a triangle or cross formation they would get very dim then go away then out of the horizon 3-6 more would come up in the same formation that the previous ones disappeared in. We went inside safeway to get the rest of the family and about 20 minutes later when we came out they were gone. Some other people around us saw them too. I watched for them the entire way home and looked it up then found this site. And thats really all that happened.
