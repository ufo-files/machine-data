{dr}Observed very large object heading east in Appleton Wisconsin.  Object had 2 lights, one on the front and back.

Was sitting in my hot tub with a friend.  I noticed around 10pm two bright lights moving across the sky in tandem. At first I thought the light was a satellite before I observed the rear light.  The object moved across the Big Dipper that gave us some perspective of the size. It appeared cigar shaped and moved at a slow rate across the sky. The lights were non pulsating. I grabbed my cell phone and attempted to record the event.  But the recording was too dark. We lost sight of the object after passing over the house.  My friend and I were in awe of the event that took place.
