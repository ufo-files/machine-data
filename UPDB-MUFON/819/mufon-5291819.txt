2 glow-stick looking objects moving slowly across the sky

We were traveling westbound on Route 30 in Shererville/Dyer Indiana when we noticed 2 bright glow-stick looking objects in the sky above us. The objects appeared to be orange/pink in color and glowing. but due to the time of day it was (almost sunset) it could have been the sun reflecting behind the objects causing that color. One of the objects was barely visible but the other was much more visible as it seemed closer. I began to record as soon as I saw them but lost track of the objects when we were turning into our destination. When we arrived at our destination I was able to spot the objects again and began recording a second video. The object(s) were to the west of us and moving very, very slowly to the south, and they eventually just sort of faded away and could not be seen anymore. I have included the 2 original videos and also the original videos using screen recording and zoomed in for more detail.
