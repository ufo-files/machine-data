{jd} no craft seen,just lights flashing across road like a train crossing,blinking right to left,and time loss

This happened back in 1994.My girlfriend at the time and I were babysitting for her uncle at his house in knightsen ca,just past Oakley.Around 1:00 am,everyone was sleeping,I got bored and decided to go to pittsburg and pick up a friend and show him this house,it was a mansion,with an equestrian center in backyard.I pick up my friend,and it's about a 30 min ride there and back,so,we get into knightsen,and it's a long straight road,about a mile long,and all of a sudden right in the middle of the road,in front of us,these red lights flashed across the road,back and forth,like it was a train crossing,but no bells or train,I'm heading straight at it and it's coming no closer,and I'm really nervous,going faster and can't catch up to it.Then it's gone,my friend said,did you see that,yes,I had to pull over he was getting car sick.So we get back in the car,and headed for the house.Long story short,when I was driving,we kept passing this catfish pond,I found this payphone and called the house,told my girlfriend I can't find the house,she asked.where I was,I said post office,she said I was right around the corner,and I look up and I see the bright lights from the equestrian center.We finally get to the house at 5:30 am,from 1:00 am.That's a 4hr time loss.
