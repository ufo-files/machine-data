{cak} thought it was daytime star, got celestial binoculars and realized it was a hovering object

Walking my dogs I thought I saw a daytime star with my naked eye. Never seen a daytime star so went and got celestial binoculars that I use to view night time stars with and started shaking when what came into view was an object just hovering in sky. I went in and got my wife and she viewed it also. It remained there for about 20 minutes and then I viewed a white circular light come on in middle of it and an orange glow around it. It then moved rather swiftly due north until I lost it. I am frequent sky watcher and avid astonmer, this was not an airplane,or meteorologic event.
