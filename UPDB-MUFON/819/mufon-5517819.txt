{nd} object hovered in sky for 5+ minutes then accelerated straight up

I was at the Eifel Tower Restuarant with my new Wife and our wedding party on June 22, 2012 at roughly 7:45pm. The sky was perfectly clear with no clouds, and the sun was beginning to set. 

I noticed an object in the sky that didn't look right. It appeared to be stationary and showed little movement for over 5 minutes. When I first saw it I assumed it was a rocket in the distance but as time lapsed and it didn't move I began to think it may be something other than military.

My party continued to watch until after a short period the object began to rise in the sky slowly at first and then accelerate. We watched it until it disappeared.
