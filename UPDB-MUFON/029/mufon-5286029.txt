Bright circular object.

I went outside an observed a beautiful sunset. I called in my house for my wife to come outside. Looking west I saw an object dart right and then elevated slightly and shot through the clouds at incredible speed. It lasted about two seconds. Was very far away. Object must have been massive. Did not reappear.
