For the past tweo weeks my grandson and I have seen a bright light. The first time it was just south of where we where, where it hovered over a farm house light, but about two hundred feet above it. Then it went west about half a mile, stopped, after about a minute or so, it went back to the first spot. It then went east wnd the northtill it pasted route 9 it then went west to the Central Illinois Regional Airport(CIRA) where it landed without running lights.
Thsi past week, we saw the same light, but North of our position, which was the same place as the first one. So we got in my truck and headed toward it, where my grandson used my phone to get pictures, I also saw a jet fly over the light, it was a lot higher up in the air though. As we got close to it we came into the small town there, the light was stationary until we tried to get real close to its position, at which point it moved out of our vision, until I saw it light up the runway at CIRA.
