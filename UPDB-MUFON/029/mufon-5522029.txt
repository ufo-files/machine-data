in marion nc in 1989 i was with my parents going to church ,we just got on the interstate maybe traveled an eighth of a mile and it was starting to get dark and i saw in tha woods a blue orb floating above the creek bed traveling as fast as we were on the interstate.going up and down in that creek and below the tree tops doing this for about 1mile.i told my parents about it when i saw it and they were so amazed at this object and when we got to the restarea it came up into the sky and floated above a pond behind the restarea and toke off like a streak of lightning into tha sky.by tha way the leaves where off tha trees at this time of year
