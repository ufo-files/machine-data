Large Glowing Round(ish) Orb Seen in SW Ohio/Tri-State Region

The following sighting occurred while driving south on the 1500 block of OH-28 in Goshen, OH at 5pm on Friday, October 1, 2021: 
While driving in my Mother’s car as her passenger, the two of us and my pet Bull Terrier were sitting in the car slowly moving in Friday evening traffic. When, all of a sudden in our front view facing south from our position (Goshen, OH), we saw a Round “Sun-Like” Orb glowing bright with a reflective light hovering in our view. We tried to convince each other it was a plane. But, as the object remained what appeared to be stationery for over a minute, we concluded it could not possibly be a plane. Also, we commented how bright it was shining, no plane would reflect like that even on a sunny day. It struck me it could be a weather balloon made out of reflective material similar to the shiny plastic “emergency blankets”... But, after looking away for a second to look at my dog in the rear seat. I turned around, and was shocked as the object had completely disappeared from our horizon of sight... I wish I was quicker and got a photograph, but sadly I did not. I do not believe it was a plane or a weather balloon, it did fit the characteristics of ether. I hope this can help someone. Thanks for your time.
