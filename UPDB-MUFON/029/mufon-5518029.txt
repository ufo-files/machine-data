{dr}Possible abduction

Well back in 1991/92 I lived in Boones Creek Tn. It was early summer and around 2 or 3 am.
I was in bed and I was suddenly wide awake...I jumped out of bed and coming threw my window was the purest white blue light I have ever seen. It was pulling away from the yard. I ran to the back door and went to the fence to see what it was but it was on the other side of the large tree that was in the yard..it was like everything was bathed in a soft yellow light.
The dog was just laying there unresponsive..I hollered at it and it didn't budge..I went into the house and everyone sleeping wouldnt wake up..I then returned to my room and sat there till morning, I asked everyone about it and they thought I was crazy. After that for three years I hard horrible headaches. I saw several doctors and none could explain it..then they just stopped. I have tried to talk to people about it but I am often looked at as if I lost my mind. I figure that this is my last chance to tell this and hope that no one laughs at me.
