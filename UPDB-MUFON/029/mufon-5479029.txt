For reason I looked up and saw a triangular shape moving at a high rate of speed, it was highlighted by thin dim lines of the triangle with lights on the corners of the triangle with halos around them. The triangle went in a straight line and I lost sight of it behind my plum tree, I tried to follow it but it disappeared. I wasn't scared, I stayed out there for another ten minutes or so so I could see if I could see anything else. I didn't see anything else. When I went back inside I started freaking out, because of what I saw and how close it was to my house and family.
