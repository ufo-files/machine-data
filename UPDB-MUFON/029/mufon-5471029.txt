{cak} Witnessed a flash of light that remained in the same area of the sky for about 45 minutes

I live in Sedona AZ. My wife,brother in-law and myself were sitting in my backyard stargazing. I was just telling my B.I.L about this light that I've seen on several occasions over the past few years. No sooner did I say it and there it was roughly in the same area of the sky.. I got my night vision and laser pointer and began to record.. I recorded for 8 minutes or so... but the actual time the light remained in view was 45 minutes .. it moved very slowly thru the constellation ophiuchus. It moved in a staggered path and seemed to remain still for several minutes at a time while traveling. It didn't just disappear it turned away and just became fainter until we lost sight if it..
