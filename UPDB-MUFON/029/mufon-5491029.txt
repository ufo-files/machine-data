spotted high in sky lights on two sides coming to a point. Cone shaped with 7 to 8 lights on each side

I went outside on approx.June 9,2015 at about 11;30 pm to have a cigarette and sky watch,which i commonly do. I Noticed coming from the north a assortment of white lights coming in my direction. The shape was close to a triangle shape except there was back section,it was basically a v with 7 to 8 lights per side. I'm not sure if it was many crafts flying in formation or just one craft.I'm sensing it as being one craft since they stayed tight for the whole sighting. the main reason i;m reporting this sighting is there was something strange with the way it flew over head ,it seem to be loosely hidden by some kind of cloud or fog cover. The mist stayed with the craft through the entire sighting. It appeared to be some sort of coverage for the craft since it was difficult to spot at first but once i had it in my sights i never took my eyes off it. It was also flying at a high altitude so you really had to look hard to spot it specially with the fog substance around it. I've had many sighting with photos of them but i've never seen or heard of this kind of action by a ufo. The ufo flew directly over head and continued to travel in a southern direction till i lost site of it. It flew at a steady speed without waver.I'm curious if anyone else spotted this type of ufo and also thought it important to report this since it had this slight quirk to it .
