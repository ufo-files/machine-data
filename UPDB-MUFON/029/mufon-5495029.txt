{dr}I was taking photos with my iPhone 6 plus of the beautiful sunset and cloud formations and when I looked at my photos I saw the orb.

I had just finished doing a little grocery shopping and was waiting at the bus stop in front of the Family Dollar on East Market Street in Greensboro, NC. I saw the sun was setting and it looked beautiful so I pulled out my iPhone and just started snapping pictures of the spectacular clouds and the setting sun so I could enjoy them and add them to my collection of nature photos. When I scrolled in my phones photo folder and noticed that there was a bright greenish yellow circular Orb like thing in my shots. At first I figured it was just lens glare but then when I enlarged the photo the orb had depth and a part of it was actually BEHIND a portion of the cloud and that made it strange to me. So I decided to report it.
