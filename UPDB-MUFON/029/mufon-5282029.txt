Red lights

We were fishing and looked up at the consolations then noticed a line of lights. It looked like they were coming out of something because we just seen the beginning, not an end. Looked like hundreds after watching for 30 minutes. They were random, fast and slow. Mostly fast. We were freaking out, wll the sudden really low to the ground a red flare like light just BOOM flew past fast! And we saw regular planes(with the flashing lights) and these were VERY different and how they flew in the sky. They were also higher than regular planes. After watching we noticed some of them hovering!!! We thought we were getting bombed or something. We were so freaked out and could see so many of these lights I was going to call my mom and tell her I loved her.
