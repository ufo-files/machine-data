spherical UFO flies along side plane.

On the 31st May 2009 at around 1.30am, I was filming with a Sony CCD TRV87E camcorder. Upon zooming onto a plane that was heading for Bournemouth airport I instantly noticed a spherical ball of light flying along side it. The object (that I would say was approximately a few feet across) was flying quite close to the plane and traveling at the same speed. The object which flew with a steady flight path and no obvious flashing navigation lights slowly pulled to the left and out of sight. [Link moved to FI section/cms/tg]
