wormhole or inteligent alien vortex

My German Shepard dog left me behind that day after she saw something in the bush that scared her.
Viewed an intelligent alien vortex or some type of wormhole while hunting in Hay River, NWT, Canada 1979 August after taking a trail never taken before ending at a small round clearing with tall grass.

Entity or object appeared at far end of field to be a circle with smoke being sucked into the center as the object spun clockwise or counter-clockwise, about a meter across in size or bigger.

Once I realized what is was after seeing into it which was a vast realm of another world, it moved toward me quickly then I jumped out of the way to find it had disappeared behind me into the bush.
