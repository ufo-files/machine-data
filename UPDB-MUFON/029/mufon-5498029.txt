Non terrestrial aerial craft seen hovering for several minutes before rapid acceleration

During a routine patrol in RC South under command of cENTCOm space command, my unit and I observed a small pill shaped cylindrical craft hovering utilizing some unknown form of reaction less drive system. After hovering above a small ridge it generated a form of light lending effect and disappeared from sight. A report was filed to military HQ. a word of mouth directive from an unknown Air Force O6 was issued to my men and myself not to disclose this info. I hold a Ph.D in physics and my knowledge of terrestrial technology is beyond question. This was a man shaped object that emitted no noise and was capable of controlled flight.
