Almost like it was searching for something

I had been up to Avebury circle to do a ritual for my birthday. I have done this since I got my 1st scooter aged 16. I left the village of Avebury in my camper van & drove up into the hills to find somewhere that I could park up for the night. I had parked up made a cup of tea & made the bed up inside, then sat drinking my tea outside the camper. I had my radio on low & was enjoying the night sky. I noticed a bizarre star formation as it was a perfect triangle, when it slowly dawned on me that it was moving. It then stopped & emitteded a beam from a reddish amber light in its center that I hadn't seen until then. After a few mins it would shift its position & repeat it. It was then after a few more mins my radio just screeched with white noise or static & it was virtually on top of me, just sitting there. The air made my skin feel like it was pulsing with heavy static electrical pulses. I couldn't move although I wasn't paralyzed. I was shaking with total fear & terror. After it sat above me for roughly 17 mins I was in disbelief, fear & confusion. It sat there, it was absolutely massive but totally silent. It was roughly 100 - 150 ft above me & to see the whole of 1 of its sides you had to pan your head. I think you could easily fit a football stadium within it. It then rotated on its axis & shot off at impossible speed to the north west. It was then my radio returned to normal, roughly 5 mins after it had sped off 3 helicopters appeared from the east & followed its general direction. I can not say they were related to my sighted object however I saw no helicopters or planes before hand & none after the 3 mentioned. I hope in my heart that this is 1 of our military's projects because if its not ours, we need to be extremely afraid. I know that this is as brief an account as I can place here but I have given all the facts as I can recall.
