Saw falling, flickering light just stop then head south.

Thought I was witnessing a real bright falling star. Flickered as such but quite a bit brighter than usual. Stopped to watch. I seen it at about 1230 above me and occasionally flickering in and out. Followed it to probably 2 o'clock. There it just stopped on a dime. Hovered about a second then went straight south at a high speed. Not blinking fast but faster than a airplane fast. It started at that speed. Did not accelerate. Looked as if it was as big as a passenger van.
