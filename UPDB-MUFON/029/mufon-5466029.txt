{jpg} I stepped out onto my balcony to smoke a cigarette look up into the night sky and saw only one star. It looked strange.

On Saturday 9/1/18 at 11:00pm I stepped out onto my balcony of the second floor of my house in Clearwater Florida to smoke a cigarette before I went to bed. The sky was dark there were no stars except for one that appeared overhead approximately 2 miles to the south and at an altitude of approximately 7000 ft. I thought it was strange but there were no other stars and that this one seem to have some kind of a geometric shape like a V shape. At first I thought it was an airplane because of its brightness and unusual appearance but as I continued to observe it it never changed its location it just appeared to be hovering there. It was definitely not a helicopter as I would have heard the noise of chopper blades. It also was not a weather balloon as there was no ascent no movement in position. My night vision isn't good I can't focus very well so I went back inside to get my cell phone. I saw no point in taking a video since the object wasn't moving so I took stills. When I viewed the pictures that I had taken I could barely see a tiny point of light in the dark screen. When I expanded the picture I was surprised not only to see that it actually was v-shaped like a flying wing but also had lights along the wings and on the tips and there was a larger light in the center. The pictures showed that it was rotating and in two of the pictures it looked like a dumbbell with a much larger light on the tip of the right wing.
