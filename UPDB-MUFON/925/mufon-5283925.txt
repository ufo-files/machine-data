Yellowish red orb dropped in front of window changed direction then disappeared.

I was laying in bed (center of bedroom) on the phone with my long distance boyfriend. I have two windows that are positioned higher on the wall. Out of the corner a bright light caught my eye even though my blinds on both windows were closed. As i turned my head an orb was falling into view, appeared to stop very briefly, turn in a half circle, then disappeared into the bottom plane of view. During this time i was able to stand but once i reached the window and moved the blinds nothing was there. Back porch light was on as well.
