Flashes of light

I was outside putting out Christmas lights and I tend to look in the sky to find satellites. While I was staring a very large flash of light appeared. It caught my attention and I focused on that area and I saw it happen a few more times. I got the attention of my husband and he saw the same thing. The flash of light did not stay in one spot or move in a straight line. It popped up in different areas and then it just stopped. It was just a white flash of a light. We thought maybe it was from an airplane but there were no aviation lights. It appeared to be pretty high up because it was Directly above us.
