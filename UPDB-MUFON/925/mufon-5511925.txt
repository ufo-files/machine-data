{nd} 30+ objects in a straight line then some moved up

I was driving my son to school just coming off 208 and getting onto 287 just before 8 am when my son commented on a strange long straight black line in the sky. As we got closer we noticed many black objects that looked like balls. At first I thought they were geese but they werent in a v formation. There were far too many to be geese. We then noticed many small lights within each black orb. They were coming from the south heading northwest. About 6 of them on the right side started going higher just a little. We lost track of them when we took the 202 exit.
