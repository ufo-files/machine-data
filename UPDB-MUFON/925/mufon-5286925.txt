Noticed ufo in a picture later on after exploring a trail near our cabin we stayed for vacation

Took a walk through a wooded path next to this pond of the cabin we were staying at in Hocking Hills, OH. It was a pretty day so I took some pictures and didn’t actually physically see the UFO until scrolling through my photos later that evening. The entire walking experience felt strange though. After I got my scenic pictures and began to walk back to our cabin, heard rustling in the bushes with no one there. Then as I was walking by this abandoned dock that sat in the woods near our cabin I heard this strange “womping” or jet-like sound. Started filming it and then heard more rustling in the bushes. Before I ventured into the woods I noticed this large, hovering cloud that was somewhat shaped like a flying saucer.
