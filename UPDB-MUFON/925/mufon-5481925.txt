{jpg} Disappearing and reappearing. Moving sporadically and quickly in a rather small area in the sky.

I was at work, taking a smoke break in my car at around 10pm EST in Columbus, Ohio. I reclined my seat and was staring at the night sky and was staring at what I thought was a star, until it started moving left to right, up and down, making very random turns at a really high speed. It stayed in the same general area in the sky and kept disappearing and reappearing. This went on for approximately 40 minutes. I ran inside to round up my coworkers to have a look, and they both saw it as well.
