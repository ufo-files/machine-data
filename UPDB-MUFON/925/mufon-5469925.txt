Three objects silently passing overhead flying in triangle formation

Three object were observed on 29 January 2018 at 14:51 passing overhead in the direction E to W.
There was an 12 mph WNW wind gusting at 23 mph.
The objects were filmed with a full spectrum converted SLR camera fitted with a 760 nm infrared filter.
No sound was noticed and the objects appeared to fly in strict formation for the four minutes I was able to observe them.
They appeared to be dumbbell shaped or two spheres one in front of the other.
All three objects appeared to be identical in shape and size and pointing in the same forward direction. 
They did not appear to rotate in any way.
I was able to follow them until they became so low on the horizon that they were lost behind trees and buildings.
Just before I lost sight of them they appeared to turn to a SW direction.
The white appearance of the objects may be down to the fact that I was recording in infrared and in black and white.
I did not see these objects with the naked eye myself so I don't know if they were visible in normal visible light or not.
I am familiar with many aircraft that fly in my area, gliders, helicopters, airships and planes but these did not resemble any I have seen before.
The police helicopter that is based near me was seen flying a few minutes later but this is seen flying about a lot of the time.
