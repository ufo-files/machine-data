{cak} I SAW WHAT LOOKED LIKE A RAINBOW AND TOOK PICTURES

My Wife and I were driving to the store when we saw what appeared to be the beginnings of a rainbow which had gold and amber tones. So, I started taking pictures thinking that I'd get a picture of a beautiful rainbow. Then, the brightness next to the rainbow began to shine more brilliantly and seemed to have wisps of clouds coming off of this shining object in the sky. We looked in awe trying to figure out how we were seeing the sun to our right and what appeared to be a smaller sun next to it. Today,I was showing an employee the pictures and he pointed out the triangle. I thought this was odd and it appears that the triangle came out of the burst of light.
