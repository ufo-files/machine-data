Seen an object from the south, continued north than stopped and turned a bright light toward us.

My partner and I where outside getting some air and enjoying the night sky and started noticing objects very high in the sky going in different directions, I looked toward the south and a light caught my eye because it was moving so slow. I asked my partner what is that. He replied I don't know. while we were watching it, it stopped and turned toward us, my partner said be still, him and I both were thinking if it come this way we were going to run, it turned the light back and just disappeared. This thing what ever it was did not make a sound. Scared the crap out of me.

I have debated reporting it because of fear of being ridiculed. Is there a way of finding out if there where any additional sightings? The actual size is hard to describe because we only observed a light.
