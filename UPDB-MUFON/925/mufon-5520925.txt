A disc like shape with 4 orange lights flying up, in a zig-zag motion

I was at the lake, camping with my family, I couldn't sleep that night, my head was hurting. I few hours after just looking through the window, I saw 3 yellow/orange lights hovering, I first thought it was a plane, but there was no plane noise, only a slight hum. Then a slight outline appeared around the lights, I looked closer and took my camera out but then it started flying away, in a zig-zag type motion, first up, then backwards and down, then up again a few times, I couldn't get the camera started, so I just looked at it, and it started dissolving away, or faded.
