Two identical large gray discs each with one circular spot in its center.

Looked as if they were attached or very closely following one behind the other, trailing a large bank of gray clouds which was smoothly moving south. The discs were the same gray color as the clouds but their outlines were clearly visible against the blue sky around them. The Hudson Valley (my location) has been known for many years as an area for frequent sightings of UFOs. I believe that the discs were trying to blend in with the clouds so that they would not be noticed, and it is possible that if they were not alien, they may have been created by our own government perhaps by a secret intelligence group of some kind for security or other purposes.
