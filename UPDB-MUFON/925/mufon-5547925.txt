Sighting of an object 2 feet off the ground (mirror like reflective) gliding in and out of the forest & fields.

I saw in a field and broken forest (about a mile southwest of my home) an object. It moved in and out of the forest cover just a couple of feet above ground. Very reflective it appeared to be made of mirror like plates. It seemed to glide in and out of the forest cover and back under cover again. It had mirror like panels, cigar shaped overall. It's apparent size about ona and a half to 2 inches wide at 1 mile distance.
