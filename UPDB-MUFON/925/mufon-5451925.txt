4 or 5 waves of 3 massive triangles flying abreast.

On a clear summer night in Calgary. My cousin and I witnessed wave after wave of massive triangle "ships" flying in the night sky. 

They were traveling at a great velocity, likely over 300mph

The triangles were 3 abreast, each over 100'. There was about 6 waves of vessels. 

They made absolutely no noise. All we could hear was the air displacement, or whooshing, as they flew over head. No more than 100' in there sky. 

As soon as they left, the police helicopter was in there area, and police cruisers were driving up and down the alleyways.
