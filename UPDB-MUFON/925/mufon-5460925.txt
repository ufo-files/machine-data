{cak} Shooting-star like object

A shooting star like object was observed except it’s apparent luminosity was not consistent with shooting stars prevously experiences. Unlike the usual fade-in, climax, then fade-out in an arc this object appeared altogether at once above the Little Dipper, 3-5 times brighter than any star and moved extremely quickly about 2 inches across the night sky to the north and the light went out like the flick of a switch. No fade-in/fade out or arc.
