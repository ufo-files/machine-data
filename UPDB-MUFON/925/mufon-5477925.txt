{rjl} 3 ligts hovering then 2 moving very close to smoke/clouds formed by forest fire.

It was Thursday morning February 16th 2017 at 7:20 a.m. I was driving east down landside Drive in Valrico Fl. when I noticed the Cyclone style clouds that were a result of forest fires east of where I was located. I pulled partially off the road to get pictures of this rarely-seen phenomenon. I noticed three lights in the sky close to the clouds I was photographing, two together and one by itself. I took two pictures using my cell phone that included these lights. On the second picture the lights to the left seem to have changed positions. I had heard that there were drones in the air getting information on the fire, so at first I discounted the lights as drones. However when I viewed the pics later and expanded the picture it seems like there are specific shapes to these lights. As you can see there are telephone lines and homes with cars passing by and I have included the screenshots with the details of each photograph. I was only able to stay in this position for about a minute,there was a truck behind me honking trying to leave the subdivision. When I got out on the main road and stopped again the lights were gone. Please let me know if there's any other information I can provide. Looking forward to seeing if there are any answers out there for these unusual objects.
