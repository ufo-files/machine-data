{tjd} Saw 6 lights moving diagonally from around behind Jupiter then moved North while 2 or more Hovered until the last one passed then they too left

At 7:35 pm went out side noticed Jupiter straight to the west. Then from behind Jupiter saw a moving “star” moving North & up thought was a satellite but then it was followed by five more as 2 sat stationary then I yelled for my husband who came out & saw at least 8 or 9 white star like moving objects. I grabbed my phone and recorded what we saw not thinking I could pick up photos but I do have some photos and it shows the diagonal moving objects as well as the other stars that were in the system next to Jupiter. This happened not even a mile North above Interstate 80 & Middle Road Exit Bettendorf IA. Size of objects was about pencil tip & there was a plane flying lower near same area Flying North to South & plane lights were bigger & flashing normal. Also 2 other normal planes heading East from the West right after “lights” left our site.
