hovering white dot

we were standing on EL-Mirage dry lake. to the south, in the direction of big bear, about 35-40 degrees above the horizon was a white circle. as bright as the moon, and similar color. if the moon was the size of a quarter, this would be the size of a Tylenol pill.
the object did not move relative to the clouds or earth.
there was a light breeze blowing which did whisk the clouds past the object.
as it was behind the clouds, it was at least 5 miles away and over 15,000 feet.

there are local reports of weather balloons. however, the object was stationary during a breezy day.
