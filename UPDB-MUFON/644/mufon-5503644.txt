{nd} Cigar shaped UFO that made two sharp 90 degree turns then faded away

i was sunning myself when I noticed in the clear blue skies an cigar/cylindrical object high in the sky travelling in a straight line towards me in a westerly fashion. It was very high in the sky, not as high as commercial aircraft but approxiametly height of civilian aircraft would fly in my opinion. It was dark grey/black in colour with no discrenable external features. At first I thought it was a recreational aircraft but the motion was too fast to be an aircraft. The next thing that came to mind was a large bird flying but the elevation was too high and object was too big to be a bird. After observing the object for about 20-30 seconds, it made a perfect 90 degree turn to the south. It did not bank into a turn like a aircraft or a bird would do....it was a perfect turn that happened in an instant. The object then travelled in an exact straight line going south for about another 15-20 seconds.The object then made another perfect 90 degree turn back towards the east where it appeared from. I observed it for the next 30-40 seconds travelling in a straight line easterly until it faded from my sight. I had to shake my head a few times to make sure that I was seeing what I was seeing but it is exactly how i described it. In no way was the object a plane helicopter or bird. It was something unexplained. I felt that it was nothing in this world that i have ever seen. The object maintained its shape and colour throughout the encounter.
