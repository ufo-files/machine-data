{jpg} 30 or more round ufos appear then disappear

My wife and I observed atleast 30 to 40 ufos coming out of the Gemini constellation around 5:45 am MST on 11 December 2019 from our home in Colorado Springs. It started with one then another, then another and so on. They were flying in a strait line with one behind the other with equal distance between them like they were marching. I am prior military but have never seen 30 to 40 air crafts fly inline instead of a V formation or similar. One after the other appearing out of nowhere, out of the Gemini constellation area of the sky from my vantage point. It was really cool to see and I don't know how to better explain what I saw. We tried to take pictures but they are all turned out solid black as the ufos appeared to be pretty high up and at about 70°. They were flying from North to South.
