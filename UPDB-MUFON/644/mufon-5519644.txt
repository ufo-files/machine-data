A light appeared, went in a straight line and disappeared.

I was at my house, laying down and watching the stars, when a light appeared, flew in straight line, and then disappeared. I took it for a falling star and paid it no mind. Minutes go by and another light appeared, this time much closer, flew over me and disappeared. I was shocked the second time around because it didn't look like a falling star, it looked like an object with multiple lights attached to it. I waited to see if another one would appear but nothing happened after that.
