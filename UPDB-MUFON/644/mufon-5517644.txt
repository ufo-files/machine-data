{ck} large set pattern of lights seen in distance flying in circular pattern, occasionally fading out then returning for about 10 minutes.

At our sons 3rd birthday party on July 3, 2012 at about 21:45, we were getting ready to start setting up for fireworks, when in the distance we (approximately 16 people) noticed a large semi-rigid pattern of lights about 1-2 miles away. The region is rural countryside, very low light pollution. It was flying in a circular pattern for about 10 minutes with a large radius to the flight path. During the observation, the lights would occasionally fade out and return, but with no set pattern to the fading and return to visibility. I had my camera (a Canon DSLR) and was able to snap 3 pictures. I was having difficulty focusing due to the distance and darkness. One picture came out fairly decent, but the other 2 suffer from camera shake. I zoomed in on the one good one and found 5 white lights and 1 red light.
