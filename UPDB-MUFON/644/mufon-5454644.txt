{cak} Rotating fireball

I witnessed a Orange Rotating fireball. 
It seemed to be about the size of a bus. The fireball hovered above a neighborhood. And dropped several smaller fireballs over the course of a few minutes. Then the craft extinguished, elongated into to a cylindrical/tick tac shape, and slowly moved towards the southeast of where i was watching. I continued watching the object for another few minutes until it was out of sight. This craft, and several other monitored me for the ensuing year or so. They would stobe, and then ignite into a rotating fireball, drip, extinguish, and leave. They would appear in different areas of southern California. I observed them from Fontana CA, north as far as Afton California. The Afton Canyon encounter produced up to 10 separate fireball crafts.
