{dr}gold colored spheres hovering over barn; left & returned in split second

My aunt and I saw 3 or more gold colored spheres hover over the barn on the farm;they rotated individually and around each other. They left vertically in a split second, and returned a few minutes later, and then again abruptly left in a split second. They were completely silent, and I am sure they were not weather balloons, because of the speed and maneuvers they were doing. It was along time ago,in 1959, but I have never forgot, and have never reported this to anyone.
