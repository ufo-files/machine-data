When I was a little boy in California I used to play softball. I remember it was a funny summer Sunday in August my mom and dad took me to a pancake breakfast at a local park where I was going to play softball with my friends. One of my teammates hit a home fun and the bill went into the woods so I dissided to go get the ball. I ran to the tree line where I thought the ball had landed after entering the woods I smelled something strange and heard buzzing sounds. After that I guess I blacked out cause when I opened my eyes I was laying on my back and the softball was laying on my chest
