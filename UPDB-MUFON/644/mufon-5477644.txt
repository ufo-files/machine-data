I do not know what this is, only that this is what I am filming.
Seems to me that when it changes from the Large size, to small size, it is moving away from me,
when changes from small dot to Large, then it moved toward me.

Went out side to look at sky to see if it was going to rain, was raining on other side of town.
When first looked up could not see the sky well due to street lights, so used my hands like binoculars and saw what looked like 2 lights 1 over top of the other. I ask another person to look at it, they thought it was funny looking also.
So I went and got my HD Video Camera and started filming.
Ended up seeing this for the next 3 nights, the 3rd night I saw a total of 4 of these in different locations in the sky.

Filmed with Panasonic HD video camera.
for the 1st video, about 1 hour, and the 2nd video about 30 minutes MAX Zoom was 10.

The 3rd Video, I filmed Mar. 2, 2017 the 3rd night, I changed the Digital Zoom setting to a max 700.
In 3rd video the Object is without lights or movement,
after I zoom out the 2nd time and when zooming back in about 2:45 the object will light up and start to move.
3rd video Resolution not as clear, but a closer view.
