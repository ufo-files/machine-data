{rjl} Looked out my bedroom window just after waking and saw object moving over house. Ran out front door to see better and it was gone.

It was very early morning (around 6:15 am) and I had just woke up. I looked out my bedroom window to see if I could still see the moon. I saw an object, triangular in shape with lights on all three corners, traveling over our house. I ran down the hall and to the front door to see it better and it had disappeared. 
It was triangular in shape with reddish-orange pulsating lights on all three corners. It made no noise. It was a very still night which made it very creepy. No wind whatsoever.
