While floating in our pool with my son my son and I were simply gazing up at the stars enjoying the evening sky. We both noticed a strangely lit object traveling south in a straight line yet the position of the object was on an angle which made no sense. It was headed South yet the object was pointed South West. There were Three distinct Lights which held a consistent brightness level. It never changed direction. The center light was more oblong then the outside lights. I told my son "that ain't no airplane no blinking lights" He was amazed. I was curious and enjoyed this experience. The craft, while never changing direction suddenly increased the intensity of the three light sources by several times held it for a few moments while the craft seemed to slow slightly. The three lights went back to the original level of brightness then the position of the craft turned heading south from front to back where it was originally facing further West. My son became uneasy seeing this event while I felt fulfilled seeing what I believe to be a UFO. The craft continued heading South and we lost sight of it as it traveled beyond the tree line. An Interesting way to end the Labor Day weekend. The time was between 8:30 and 8:45.
