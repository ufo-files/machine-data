5 craft flew overhead

I was outside just talking in the backyard with my girlfriend. I love looking at the stars, well actually looking for satellites and "other" things I might see.
I will sit sometimes for 3-4 hours on a nice evening with my lawnchair reclined all the way back just watching the sky. You know what,..I've seen stars vanished on 3 separate occasions with a witness on one of them. I've never heard of stars that can be the biggest in the sky just dimming out.
Gone . That's really weird to see. 
So on this night im in the back facing west..I see what looks like a shooting star ...
And within 1 second I saw 5 objects in formation zoom over my head coming out of the NW flying SE . No noise , looked like they were glowing, FAST,...IN FORMATION. ..gone...the first thought I had was wow..these guys are flying low enough to hit a tall building ...then it sank in to what i had just observed.
I went nuts telling my girlfriend what I just saw .
That was that ..5 egg shaped glowing, flying faster than I've ever seen? .there was absolutely no sound coming off these things .
I'm still in awe..I think about it a lot during the day.
