Bright star like light in sky...which moved slightly....then broke in to two with a form of light trail...

Went in to the rear garden to see if any Hedgehogs had turned up, sat drinking a glass of wine and rolled a cigarette....noticed a bright light in the sky, watched it for a few minutes, and it appeared to be stationary.Thought it may be the North Star, but then it moved slightly...called my wife to watch, which she did, but was not really interested....then I saw a pulsing red light, so I thought it may be a plane or helicopter, but it made no real progress either toward me or away from me, then it suddenly seemed to split in to two separate entities, with a sort of light trail inbetween....I have pictures of this....it finally seemed to settle, and just became a bright light once more....I stopped watching it around 11:30 and went to bed.....
