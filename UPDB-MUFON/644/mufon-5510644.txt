The object move toward the bus what I was thought was its front approaching the interstate. Then the white light went to orange like the others and what I thought was the front turned to what look like the bottom of it was showing it was then I realized that what I thought was the front was not the front it was the side of the object it started to move east bound on the interstate speeding up as it went but it was still remaining low. I look over real quick to the side and I saw another object like the other one moving toward to my town of Cloverdale as we past by some trees I try to find it again but then I saw it high in the sky until it went so high that it disappeared.
