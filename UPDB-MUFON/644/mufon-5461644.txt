{tjd} Unknown glowing object

I was driving home from Pendleton towards Walla Walla. I noticed an unusually bright star. At first, I didn't think anything of it, thought it was maybe a planet. I noticed that it was getting larger and larger, then realised something wasn't right. Then it disappeared. I kept driving and noticed a plane flying off in the same direction that I saw this object and thought maybe It was somehow that plane. I kept driving. About a mile down the road, the object reappeared to the west and appeared larger/closer. It crossed over the highway about a mile in front of me heading east, then quickly changed directions, traveled north towards Walla Walla and disappeared again. After it disappeared, the plane was still in sight my west. I'm certain this was something else. I was woken up in the middle of the night two nights ago by military helicopters flying around Walla walla area. From what I saw on Facebook they were doing "night training." Any military base is a long ways away. I've lived here for the majority of my life and have never seen military training in the immediate area let alone over people's houses in the middle of the night. I suspect these two things are related.
