{cak} Star-like light moving around in the sky. Left,right, up, and down

Appx 2:15am I observed a light in the sky towards the NNW that would change color and move about the sky in only that part of the sky. It would move to left, stop, then move downward, then move left, up, etc. It would move randomly in all directions. I strongly observed it for about 10 mins. Then periodically checked over the next 20 mins and it was still there but higher up in the sky and dimmer last I saw it. I also seen 2 shooting stars while observing the object. At first I thought it was a plane until it stopped and moved downward and then changed direction. It stayed in just that particular area of the sky. It would change colors from red to a bluish-white randomly. After all the years studying the paranormal this is the first time I have ever witnessed something like this. I would not report this if I knew an explanation but this thing changed direction, sped up and sped down, and completely stopped so many times that there is no explanation. This area does not fit the criteria for a UFO hot spot at all and all the years I've lived in this area I have never witnessed something like this at all.
