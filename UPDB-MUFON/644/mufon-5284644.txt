I was playing baseball with my friend and I looked up and I saw a a red circle with black smaller circles around it. I told my friend to look up and he saw what I saw. It was hovering over us for approximately 1 min, then started moving in a straight path away from us. I didn’t have my phone on me at the start so I went to grab my phone. Once I got back to the area where I could see the object I got some pictures. Unfortunately I wasn’t able to get the color scheme in the pictures that I saw at the start of our sighting. It took me about 1 minute to go get my phone and bring it back to the place where I took the pictures. In that period of time, the object moved approximately 500-1500 from the north side of where I was facing to the northwestern side of where I was facing. In the pictures, you can faintly see three white circles around a glowing object in the middle. My mom was there too. The entire it was spinning clockwise. No emissions. No blinking. No sound. Military helicopters with the door open and men with guns hanging out showed up immediately after the object disappeared
