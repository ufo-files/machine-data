{rjl} many lights moving in circular and random, some were small some larger

I was walking several dogs at dusk down Laurie lane in Belleair, FL when I noticed what appeared to be spotlights in the sky, 6-10 moving across the sky as if in search. I had seen police use searchlights to find people but these lights were not coming from an object. They were moving erratically, some were brighter than others. The only thing I thought was that they were angels. Thats what it appeared to me. I wondered if someone died in a nearby home, they seemed to all be over 1 house. I stayed for over an hour and they began moving down the street at above tree level. circular then in and out, it was really beautiful, not at all frightening. I called to a couple walking and said "look up!" they said "wow, what is that?" I said, :I think its angels. At that moment there were about 30-50 moving light orbs in the sky, not true orbs but there is no other way to explain it. I had to go and bring the dogs home. As I left for my own home 2 blocks away I kept looking into the sky, another hour had passed and there were fewer, maybe 10-15. When I got home there was 1-3 above my home moving back and forth swiftly. than after an hour only 1 bigger one. It was so beautiful. I looked online to see if anyone had sighted them and found nothing. I cant believe I was the only person seeing these. They were very obvious, I guess many people just don't look up. Every day at same time Ive been riding my bicycle to see them again, and havent since.
the attched picture is the closets thing I have that resembled this beautiful flying fleet of lights. I still think they were angels. I felt very calmly serene, and blissful after this and even days after.
