Was speaking to a friend when noticed something strange behind him. satted what the **** is that?
It was 9:55 p.m. in the Swansea Grove. A Giant red almost noodle shaped bright object was hovering 100 feet above a home on the edge of the pond in Ocean Grove with a super ember looking red glow. It then curled into an almost ball shape and moved silently over the Grove and over the coal plant and cooling towers which can be seen flashing red at the bottom of the screen. It then moves towards the Taunton River. It is followed by another one, a few seconds later which is captured in a video taken on the phone. It again features a long curved noodle like shape and at times a round shape. Every five or ten seconds it glows incredibly red and changes shape. Santa? A strange nighttime flying weather balloon or military? No idea. Three witnesses. One strange night
