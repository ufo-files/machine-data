{nd} Life Changing

Buddy and I were smoking a cigarette and saw this light moving between the trees. It was very cloudy and couldn't see any stars. This was basically the only object lighting up the sky. At first i thought it was a star or Venus but another friend had a star and planet finder app but couldn't recognize the object. We walked on the golf course near his house, and got a video of it. Unfortunately when we were taking the video it wasn't moving or anything. We ended up just going back inside, but when we went outside again about 10-15 minutes later and the object or whatever it was left.
