It was the shape of a bell

It was the shape of a bell, hovering over the trees, it had what looked like outside ribs that went horizontal and vertical that were spaced about 4 to 5 feet apart gold or copper colored. The sections between them had what seemed like colored glass material with hues of blue and violet that was reflective.
