Three bright orange lights in a triangle pattern. Each light moved slowly east while slowly swaying left to right. Then slowly just dissapered.

I was leaving my sisters house and when I walked outside to go to my car I noticed three bright large orange lights in the sky. I ran back inside and told my brother in law and niece to come outside. We obsereved them coming from the west and heading east. They were in a triangle formation, but I could tell that it wasnt one object but three because each one moved a little  to the left or right on its own. It took them about five minutes to reach us and they were almost right above us when two of them slowly disapered. The last one was bright for about another minute before it disapered.
