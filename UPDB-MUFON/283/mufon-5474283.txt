{cak} Large Yellow & White Glowing Ball of light energy with cylinder beam of light underneath

I was sitting in my office on my laptop at around 5:45pm today and looked out of the windows facing north to notice a large ball of white, yellow, and golden light emanating from the storm clouds over the town below.

This large ball of light was traveling at speeds towards my home so fast, that I knew it was not a plane. It did not have flashing lights or a sound. I did not hear any noise besides the wind turning up due to the thunderstorm approaching. This large ball of colored light was quickly gliding in the sky out of the storm clouds and closer to our home when I could see an almost translucent/opaque energy or beam of rays emitting out of the bottom of the light ball. 

I can not get over how quickly this ball of light seemed to simply appear and then start traveling over our home. I ran downstairs to call my family to notify to go outside and look. By the time I went outside in our backyard which faces south, I caught the tail end of the object and it was gone. This sighting happened so fast, I wish other members of my family could have seen it. 

The best description I could give for this entity or object would be that it looked like a floating candle with a yellow/white ball of light at the top and a somewhat translucent cylinder on the bottom that were emitting holographic light rays.
