Red object in night sky more brilliant than Venus in its finest hour.

I looked out the window at approx. 9:00pm, as I heard fireworks. I saw a samll red dot high in the SW sky. It became increasingly larger and brighter, as if it were descending. My wife thought it was a meteorite falling; I thought it was a missile; we both thought it was heading straight for our backyard! Once the red object reached its closest distance, it was far brighter than Jupiter, which was also in the sky. Its brilliance resembled that of the planet Venus when its closest to earth, only it was red and even larger than Venus. It stayed in place for several minutes; that's when we ruled out any type of aircraft. It then began to slowly move in a SE direction. I went inside to get my camera. When I came back a couple minutes later, it seemed to have moved quite a bit. It was now in the SE sky with the same brilliant red color. It stayed in that spot for a few minutes, then began to ascend farther and farther away. Its ascent was similar to its descent, and eventually, it was too small to notice. The whole event was so fantastic, I lost all interest in fireworks. It was incredible. I would describe it as a "once in a lifetime event."
