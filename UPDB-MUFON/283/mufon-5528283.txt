First spotted the lights in the eastern sky, as we drove along Hwy 71. It looked as if it was an airplane, as Drake field is less than a mile from the event. We watched it as we drove south and got closer. It was not an airplane, because it was not moving, only huvering. This is the second object event for us since 2007, and the 4th unexplained light event in the same amount of time.
