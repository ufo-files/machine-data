Brightly colored flashing sphere of light seemed to drift across the night sky. It appeared to be vibrantly pulsing, giving off many different colors.

I stepped outside to the backyard and noticed a sphere in the sky which was shining different colors like a rainbow. I decided to get my camera out, and I captured two videos which are somewhat decent. It appeared to be changing from being solid and very brightly colored, to almost entirely transparent. It had a bright white, bright blue, and even a violet color for most of the time. I was able to witness, reds, oranges, yellows, greens, all sorts of different shades as they flickered. When it became transparent, I could still see dimly lit in the sky with my eyes, but my camera had a difficult time focusing on it for some reason. I have seen this before when I used to live in Colorado, near the Rocky Mountain National forest. It would come and hang in the sky late at night some nights, then mysteriously be gone for some time. Only to return again seemingly randomly.
