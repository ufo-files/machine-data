{cak} Tiny red/yellow pin of light circled another danced around, then one left other zigzagged around

At around 6:45 I went out to the front yard and looked up From our area we have a very good look of the stars. I noticed that there were two small pins of light dancing and circling each other in unbelievable patterns, very fast. I went in to call my wife, though she didn't hear me at first. I went back out. One of the lights went straight east very slowly, came back, then went back east slowly then disappeared (I think. I couldn't see through the trees.) I went in and told my wife. She came out again and the other one was still there. My wife also saw it and saw how it moved in strange patterns, backed up, zigzagged around. She wanted to try binoculars so I went in and got them. When at first I looked through it I thought my hands were unsteady, but then I fixed the binoculars on a star and they seemed steady enough, but when I fixed the binoculars on this light, it was moving really fast in all sorts of patterns. My wife looked, too. She said it looked almost as if it was writing something in the sky, that's how it moved. It left a streak that glowed but it was more like when you move light really fast, that kind of streak, not one that remained and faded. My wife checked also, first with the stars around it, then with the object, and she noticed the same thing. We went inside at 7:22 and it was still there.
