Noticed a very bright object that appeared to have rhythmically spinning lights in the distant sky. It was stationary. Minutes later it had disappear

I had taken my dogs out to the backyard to do their thing. I looked at the sky while I waited for the dogs, and noticed a small circular object which caught my eye because the flashing was bright and rythmic. It was absolutely stationary as far as position.. I had my phone so I videoed the object for over two minutes. The clouds passed by the object, but the object remained in place. I set my phone down to check on the dogs. That took about 30 seconds. I grabbed my phone to continue the video, but the object was gone. I enhanced the video and edited screenshots. The results astonished me. I was able to discern different shapes. I am not certain if the editing features cause anomalies with the pics or if what I am isolating in the screenshots are real. I have videoed these flashing objects at least 4 more times since August, most recently in October. Twice, after identifying the flashing object in the sky, some large aircraft, perhaps jet, passed overhead into that direction. I also was able to video some of that aircraft as it flew low and slow over my trees. I heard it before I saw it so I was able to get it in flight.
