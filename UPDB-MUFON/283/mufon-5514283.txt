(GS) A spherical object copper in exterior color showed up in a photo from Shawnee National Forest in Illinois.

I was at the observation area of Garden of the Gods in Shawnee National Forest. I took a picture and noticed an odd looking object in the photo. After closer examination I had no reasonable explanation as to what I captured in my photo. I would describe the object as being spherical in shape. It looked like three spheres in total with a copper exterior color. I only observed the object through my camera. It was not visible by the human eye because it all happened within a second. I am confident that I did capture and Unidentified Flying Object and found a similar occurrence in Denver Colorado captured within the same week from CNN.com.
