{dr}bright orange sphere turned star like and asended out of sight

On New Years Eve around 9pm I and 3 friends were having dinner outside. I was seated facing west and looked up to the sky and noticed a bright orange/red circle of light. Immediately I stood up and said 1hey, what is that?1 there were no navigation lights visible, no strobe flashing and was very close to Luke air force base which is about 6 miles west of our location.
It appeared to cut to the south briefly then north all while moving east directly towards us. No sound could be heard and it stayed a constant glow. Just short of being directly overhead of us it stopped and hovered for about 5 seconds then turned into an incredibly bright white light, and shot straight up and appeared to be a star in a matter of a split second then faded out of sight. It was too high to begin with to be any type of fireworks not to mention traveling horizontally for too great a distance. this was not any type of Aircraft that we are familiar with. Later that same evening it was witnessed
Again, by 2 other friends and was described exactly the same, as turning into a star.
