Large platform hovering

I was driving home on Rt 9, nearest crossroad Columbus Avenue, when looking to my left I saw a large platform hovering maybe a half mile in the air and approximately a mile away from where my car was on the road over an open clearing. This platform had glowing exterior lights. It was perfectly still and silent. From my vantage, the platform looked to be the size of a football field. At this time of year, the sun was not fully set but not perfect visibility. At the time, I assumed it had to do with the nearby Pease airfield. It was not until later that I appreciated the impossibility of a large platform of that size, hovering perfectly stable in the sky. I remember feeling I had witnessed something profound and that I should not have seen.
