several bright spots as stars of rain with defined path

1. after dinner I went to the terrace of my house because my brother called me because I was to do something that he thought to be a rain of meteorites;

2. was watching the weather because despite a hot day had already thundered and was due to rain;

3. I thought that was a rain of meteorites;

4. several objects (50 or more) flying from east to west at constant speed and trajectories both well defined;
Flashing light and suggested vacillation of the object although an exact trajectory;

5. surprised and expected;

6. sight of the object was lost in the clouds scarce;

yet during the sighting was made a phone contact to a person about 8km SW which did not observe anything at all when would expect the objects in that area. only saw a plane flying low with supposed direction to the airport Francisco S1 Carneiro.

after the disappearance of objects has also seen another plane direction NS supposedly aiming to land at the same airport.

NOTE: in photographs taken not evidenced in "naked eye" any bright spot not even the clouds due to the limitations of the camera
