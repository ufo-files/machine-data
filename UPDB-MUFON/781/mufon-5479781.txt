{nd} Resembled a skinny Eiffel Tower. Stationary at 132 degrees from True North, dispersed, then reappeared at about 155 degrees. Second witness present.

Took the dog outside at 12:00 a.m., and looked at night sky while waiting on dog. Saw a series of about seven lights in a shape that resembled a skinny Eiffel Tower. Unable to photograph due to the low light. Used cell phone compass to get bearing. Was at 132 degrees from True North and perhaps 35 degrees above the horizon. I called a second witness who was nearby to come see it. They did. Then it disappeared slowly, the lights just faded out. Seconds later, I realized that it had reappeared at about 155 degrees from True North. Oddly, if the pointy end was its "12 o'clock" position, it moved toward its "10 o'clock" direction. In other words, it was pointed roughly northwest and moved west by fading out and then reappearing. I saw nothing but the closely-spaced lights. Sighting began at about 12:05 a.m. And lasted maybe seven minutes. I thought it was my eyes blurry, but confirmed that they were not. Then, I figured it might be an airliner, but it was stationary for a few minutes. Mufon investigators are welcome to contact me about this sighting.
