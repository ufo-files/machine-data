Green fireball in the early hours of the morning

At around 4:30 am, July 2015 I took my two-year-old to the toilet. As we crossed the courtyard I saw a bright green fireball streak across the sky, leaving sparks in its wake. I thought it was an uncommonly large green bright meteorite and left it at that. Then, a few months ago, I got up around the same time in the morning and went out for a smoke. That's when I saw another bright green fireball following the same path across the sky. The coincidence makes me question whether it was a shooting star in the first place. I thought you guys might be interested.
