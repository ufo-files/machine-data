{rjl} I looked out of my bathroom window at the mountain, and saw a strange light that I have never seen before near the mountains near my home.

I was bewildered. I have looked and gazed at the mountains many times. I've looked at them at this exact time, and never noticed any stars or planets in that area before. Grant you I was in my bathroom and looking through a screened open window, but this was no ordinary light. It has a "mist" like quality, and hovered very low towards the canyon of the mountains. I have to say, that I was almost in a trance like state while looking at it. My mind didn't know what I was seeing. I couldn't pull myself away from the window. I turned off by bathroom light, and just stared, not fully able to comprehend what I was seeing. It was just a very large misty glowing orb. If I had to describe it best, I'd sat that it looked like a moon hidden behind clouds? Except it wasn't the moon, the moon was to the east, and this was to the west. It was too low to be a star, or planet. I finally was able to call my boyfriend into the bathroom, and he saw it too. He was as affected by the light, so he kind of snapped me out of it. I decided I needed to go outside and take a photo, or a video. When I got outside to my backyard, the light was not there. It had vanished. I spend the next two hours so confused. I still don't know what it is I saw hovering over the La Quinta Cove.
