{rjl} Aprox 10 channels of white light

What I observed over two or three consecutive nights was a light event that had multiple streams of white light coming down in columns adjacent to each other. Each column of light flowed from the sky downward at 90 degrees. The light in each column was segmented, like Morse code , and each column appeared to be independent of the one next to it. The columns appeared to be about 6 inches wide, so the whole event was about 5 feet wide and went up into the sky and from an unknown source. I stood there staring at the lights for two or three nights in a row. One night I actually stood in the light to see what would happen. Nothing happened. The date and time are approximate. What made me think of contacting you is the YouTube video of debusseys Claire de line. My son showed ma video where the notes are played with virtual hands and the notes are shown above the keyboard as falling lights directly above the key that is going to be played next. Several notes are displayed falling down to the keyboard, in a fashion to an old style roll on a player piano. The lights I saw were falling about three times the speed of the Clare de line notes.
