Hi, nearly everyone in my family has seen a ufo.  I was skeptical myself until I saw one. The one I saw was similar to one Ive seen videos of on tv... moving in an up down, left, right, irrattic; yet, apparently a deliberate motion. (too fast for a harrier jet, or, a weather balloon). It was opening day of deer season, while I sat in the woods with my rifle Nov.15th, 1990. However, what has compelled me to write this is not my own personal experience, but, my fathers story, confirmed independantly 20 yrs. later by my best friend. My father, the best, and most honorable man Ive ever known told me this story......."I was delivering fuel oil to an elderly couples home when I noticed an object hovering, and shining down a beam of light. It didn't make a sound. I didn't want to alert and scare the elderly couple...and watched as it flew away toward the air base without a sound in the blink on an eye." My father told no one about this, yet, trusted me with the story. In the 70s "people would talk and think you were crazy". Heres the kicker-Talking with my best friend 20 yrs. later, he reinterated the story to me with the exact description verbatum . later. My friend had two witnesses with him, my father thought he had none. I ask you, what are the odds two people you trust the most, tell the same story independant of each other, 20 yrs. apart?
