Roughly 8-10 static lights in the shape of a half circle bottom and square centered top slowly floated down

While watching YouTube, I looked out my window facing north east and saw a large connected series of lights that ranged from white-blue to white-red. The lights did not blink and the object slowly floated down below a neighbors house. I am unaware if anyone else noticed the object but it was only visible from my location for a very short time. The objects bottom half was that of a half circle and the top looked like a small square in the center of the top, similar to a boat with its bridge. I could not see if it was a physical object, but the lights seemed to outline something and moved in unison. I felt afraid and an odd sense of being out of place. I regret not being fast enough to record the event.
