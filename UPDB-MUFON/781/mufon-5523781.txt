I saw a bright light that looked like a star. it was stationary for a couple minutes. It started changing colors from orange to red to yellow

My friend told me that he'd been seeing ufos and taking their pictures. He emailed me some pictures and I went over to see if I'd see one. I was looking South from Vancouver, WA when I observed a white light that looked like a star, but it was below the atmosphere. It hovered there for a couple minutes and then began changing colors. It did this for about 2 or 3 more minutes, then it took off horizontally to the east at a high rate of speed.
