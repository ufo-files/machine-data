heard buzzing, then 2 x large circular lights appeared, flew overhead

I was about 12yo at the time, out playing in the street with a friend, my friend went inside. I continued riding my bike then heard a faint buzzing which started to get louder, I assumed it was a broken street light so started looking around to see which one it was and from the Eastern end of the street I saw a light in the distance coming closer. At this point I assumed it was a helicopter but thought it strange that there was no engine noise. As the object grew closer I noticed that it was in fact 2 x circular lights (smaller light in front approx. 100ft across/ bigger light behind approx. 150-200ft) traveling no more than 250-300ft from the ground. When the object was directly over me it seemed to slow down and I could feel a large amount of static in the air, so much that all the hairs on my arms and legs were standing on end. As the object moved away in a Western direction it seemed to speed up, I tried to follow and had it in my sight for another 5mins when it seemed to accelerate at an immense speed in the blink of an eye. Unfortunately had nothing with me to take a picture or video with.
