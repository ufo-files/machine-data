Red & Orange Oval Fireball with a tail

I was driving north bound on S. Vienna Ave approaching Moss Mill Rd, when I saw in the sky a Fireball of an oval shape that was red & orange with a tail that was of a lighter orange & red (meteorite?) it was following the direction of Moss Mill rd west bound. It was very dark out so you could not miss it. It was very cool! It also happened to be at the same location I had seen a ufo back in August of this year.
