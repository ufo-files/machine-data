{rjl} 2 side by side very bright red lights w/orange center faded into single bright white light

That evening I was dropping my daughter off at gymnastics just before 6:30 p.m. when I got out of the car I glanced up and noticed what I thought to be an airplane. It was 2 red-orange lights side by side and very bright moving north-north west upon a second look I realized the lights were too bright to be wing tip lights and there were no strobes to indicate that it was an airplane. I then called to my daughter to come around the car and look at what was in the sky. I looked back at the lights and watched the red lights fade into a single bright white light far brighter than the stars in the sky this light then faded to the brightness of a star or satellite. The light then went behind a cloud and out of my sight. My daughter only saw the white light and commented that it was moving fast. to me it appeared to be climbing in elevation. After I brought my daughter inside I began trying to figure out what it was. I knew it wasn't an airplane my second thought was a satellite because I have seen satellites start off very bright and fade as they pass into the shadow of the earth but I soon debunked this idea because it was heading towards the sun not away from the sun. I can think of no other explanation for what it was and it has been on my mind since it happened I've been pretty excited this is the first ufo I've seen that I have not been able to explain and I hope my testimony helps
