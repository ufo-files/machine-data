Driving towards Saint Louis south of Rolla, noticed a string of 12 plus flashing lights in a parallel line hovering above the highway. Seemed unusual in their flashing regularity, We first assumed they were some sort of fixed objects but after about 15 minutes we realized they were hovering. Eventually they headed West. Over 20 people witnessed this and we videotaped some of the footage.
