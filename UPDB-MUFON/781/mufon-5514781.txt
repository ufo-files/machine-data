(GS) A cigar shaped object appeared to hover in the sky. It started to move slow before disappearing

Oct. 20, 2012 approximately 9:45 AM my husband and i were driving in our pick up heading north on roosevelt street in walla walla, wa. We noticed a man pulled over on the side of the road who was outside of his truck looking through his range finder pointed upward, we followed what he was looking at and saw a large white cigar shaped object hovering in the sky in front of us. At first we both thought it was a small jet but quickly noticed it was not moveing and had no wings. We continued driving 1 1/2 blocks north on roosevelt street, stopped at the stop light, crossed over isaacs street and quickly pulled over. The object appeared to start moving west at a very slow speed then it slowly turned north and slightly upward and diappeared into the sky with no trace. There were no clouds and the sky was very blue. We turned our pick up around to go back and talk with the fellow we saw looking through scope but he already left the area.
