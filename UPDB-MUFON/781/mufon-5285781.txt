7 Orange Lights/UFO/UAP Coming from South heading NorthNorthWest New Fairfield CT

On August 28 2021at 8:25pm I witnessed 7 bright orange lights/ufo/uap flying in a straight formation over my house in New Fairfield CT. The first was a bit ahead of the rest and took off upward towards space/upper atmosphere. The following 2 did the same thing and then 3 took off away from me on a Z axis almost heading west, and very quickly. Last ones also flew away but at less of a straight angle, and faded off into clouds almost in the same direction they were originally heading, it was tough to tell because of the clouds. 

These made no noise, and almost looked like the burners of a fighter jet with how bright they were, but made no noise , and left no visible trails. It seemed like I could make out a disc like shape but it was dark and seemed as if it were solid black. The light was also making almost an aura of orange so it was tough to tell.

My view is blocked by trees but considering the angle they were at I was able to use them as a reference to tell the crafts are definitely pretty high, and rather large, about the size of commercial airliner or maybe a little smaller.

I was able to capture some video and posted them to youtube & twitter. A local weather station said that there was not atmospheric conditions for ball lightning because I asked if there were any weather anomalies that could cause this.

On the youtube video I am going to try and earmark the times for each light/UFO/UAP and when they shoot upwards or away so it is easier to distinguish each light.
