{cak} Saw glowing red object rapidly changing directions at high speed

At approximately 8:45 pm I went outside to take the garbage to the alley. I always look up at the sky at night to see if I can see any stars. I noticed a red glowing object (not blinking) off to the east, estimated at about a mile or two away (I'm not a good judge of distances I admit), it was about 30 degrees above the horizon, at first I assumed it was just a helicopter or a plane since there's a military base and airports nearby. But I noticed it was travelling faster than any military aircraft I've ever seen and being as close as it was it made absolutely no noise. I saw it travel left to right really fast, then stop instantly, like a sudden stop faster than any helicopter I've ever seen, then go back in the opposite direction and instantly stop, then go back to the right again super fast. It moved off towards the southeast really fast and I lost sight of it.
