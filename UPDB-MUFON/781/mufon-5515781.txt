(GS) ROUND BALL OF LIGHT, INSIDE LIGHT WITH A FEW RODS STICKING OUT HERE AND THERE

MY HUSBAND AND I WERE DRIVING PAST A CANAL, SHOWING ME WHERE HE RUNS HIS RC BOATS. I SAW THE ROUND GLOBE LIKE LIGHT, ALL LIGHT, AND I YELLED AT HIM TO STOP AND TAKE A PICTURE. THE GLOBE/LIGHT CAME FROM ITS ORIGINAL POSITION TO RIGHT OVER US BUT UP A WAY AND MY HUSBAND FINALLY PULLED OVER. I JUMPED OUT AND WAS SO EXCITED........I STARTED WAVING BECAUSE IT SEEMED SO NEAR. I COULD SEE WHAT LOOKED LIKE THE THINGS THAT STICK OUT OF A SATELLITE. IT SEEMED TO BE PLAYING WITH US.........IT WENT UP, DOWN, OVER THERE, BACK HERE, ZOOMED AWAY, CAME BACK, UP, DOWN, AND NO NOISE AT ALL. NONE. BY THIS TIME I WAS REALLY JUMPING UP AND DOWN. MY HUSBAND TOOK A COUPLE OF PICTURES WHICH HE NEVER SHOWED ME BECAUSE HE SAID IT DIDNT COME OUT. AFTER WHAT SEEMED LIKE AT LEAST 10-15 MINUTES OF WATCHING THIS BALL OF LIGHT ZOOMING HERE AND THERE, IT TOOK OFF AND I SAID "OH CRAP, ITS GONE" AND AS SOON AS I SAID THAT, IT WAS BACK. I CANNOT BELIEVE HOW FAST THIS THING WAS. WHEN IT FINALLY DID GO, WE HEADED HOME WHICH WAS ABOUT 3 BLOCKS DOWN AND MADE THE TURN FOR OUR HOUSE AND I SWEAR TO GOD IT WAS OVERHEAD, ACROSS THE STREET. BY THIS TIME I WAS ALMOST HYSTERICAL....AND THEN IN A SECOND, IT WAS GONE. MY HUSBAND DOESNT REMEMBER MUCH OF THIS BUT I CANT FORGET IT. I HAD A FEELING IT WAS FRIENDLY AND WAS PLAYING WITH US. I KEEP LOOKING FOR IT EVER SINCE THEN.
