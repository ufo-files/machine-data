{nd} Two glowing orange balls of light that hovered for a short period of time and then took off extremely fast, one disappearing into the clouds.

Driving in the car, a passenger looked to the sky and asked the question, "What are those?" When I first noticed the two objects in the sky, I thought them to be two paper lanterns that contain candles (that are sent up in some traditions). Another passenger thought them to be visible planets. There were two glowing balls of oragne light hovering in the sky at altitudes lower than surrounding planes in the sky. After remaining stationary for a short period of time, the two took off headed away from the car ate extreme speeds. One disappeared into the cloud cover, while the other flew until it was out of sight. During the sighting we all were in awe, and after we were amazed and in disbelief.
