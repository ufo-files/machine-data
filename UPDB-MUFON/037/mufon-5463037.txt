{jpg} Black irregular shaped object in sky, floated like a bird in the wind.

I was at a stoplight looking at the moon. (Night of the super blood moon) The person next to me at the stop light was looking up at the sky, but not at the moon... They were looking at a black object in the sky. I thought it was a drone or a bird, but it1s shape was too irregular to be either. It appeared to be floating like a bird would be in the wind. It slowly ascended farther out of sight and higher until I could not see it anymore. I was curious to what it was after seeing it1s irregular shape. It was kind of shaped like a cigar but not symmetrical. It was floating horizontally. I was at a stoplight so when the light turned green we pulled into the nearest parking lot to get a better view but by then it was just a mere spec in the sky.
