Dear MUFON,

I have not reported this for years for personal reasons but ongoing reports, online videos, news reports and television shows have brought me out of the closet.

On or about 11/18/2009 ~ 11/24/2009 I witnessed an opaque/white shining/glowing very large shape-shifting noiseless object (what I originally thought was the rising moon) traveling north parallel to I-25 in Colorado Springs over the US Air Force Academy facility. As I was driving I tried to get better positions/views and make sure that I was truly seeing what I thought I was seeing and not confusing it with street lights or any other fixture. Once my mind wrapped around what I saw I clearly realized the object was moving North in front of the mountains behind the AFA facility and not over them. My drive line eventually placed me at the highest point of view (Northgate/Hwy. 83 and I witnessed the object travel more than 6 miles in just a few seconds without a sound or trace of propulsion (more than 20 but less than 60). 

Week(s) before the incident I watched a local news broadcast about a farmers animals being mutilated in a county south of El Paso County. Being new to the area I didn't think much of it. Then I witnessed my event. I only shared the story with a few people that I could trust (just in case something happened to me). The event sparked my interest and I began to search the web and began to find evidence that was virtually identical to my incident within 1 - 3 hours driving near my home in Colorado (particularly near military installations).  Then I found a report of a similar object reported over Helsinki, Finland when Obama was receiving a Nobel Peace Price. That made and the Ft. Collins video made me scream out loud in conscience! Last year I was watching a UFO show where UFO hunters cameras caught video of the precise noiseless object minutes from my location and incident on the same night and within minutes prior to my siting.

Sadly, I was driving and unable to document my event with video but know exactly what I saw.

Did I witness of a cloaking device of a W.E.A.V.? Could this be a military craft being tested? Was this a device operating in our time and space and momentarily being seen thus the shape-shifting as it was moving?

I ask myself how could I have been the only one or of a few people that could have witnessed this event. Why wasnt it covered by local news? Did the military order local news and law agencies to stand down?

Maybe this is another piece of the puzzle?
