Glowing, silver orb

While sitting on my patio, I looked up to observe an airplane passing nearby. I then looked up to take in the clear blue sky overhead when I noticed a glowing, spherical object hovering overhead. As it began to slowly move in a westerly direction, I thought it might be a balloon of some sort until it began to make very abrupt changes in its pathway and speed. It alternated from a hover to moving at different angles until it suddenly stopped for a moment and then moved vertical at a tremendous speed until it vanished.
At the beginning of my observation of the object I was sure it was just a balloon, until witnessing the movements and the speed of its departure.
