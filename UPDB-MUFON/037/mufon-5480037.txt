{jpg} five or so orange lights flashed in sequence became bright then faded out not moving in any direction

I was attending a football game at about 6:30 pm. looked to the eastern sky and noticed 3 orange lights that were way too high to be part of a building. The flashing of the lights seemed sequential it went from 3 lights to 5 when I looked away it had faded completely away. No sound was associated there was not enough time for me to notify anyone but I did tell my wife about the sighting then when we arrived home there was a story about other sightings and a video that was identical to the lights that I had seen. This sighting took place significantly south of the lights shown in the video that was filmed in Phoenix my sighting was in Eloy, AZ about 45 miles south of Phoenix.
