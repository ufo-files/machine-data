{nd} Bright magnesium flare traveling north on horizon suddenly lowered

I was camping with my son in the back yard. At about 5:13 a.m., we both were awake, (we didn't sleep well that night as neither of us is used to camping and we were just testing out the tent,) we saw a bright light out of one of the screens. It was bright white, like a magnesium flare, and moving very fast northward. The sky was brightened by this, but not like daylight or anything. The light moved quickly northward and then descended very quickly, like a parabola, near the horizon. If it landed, it would have landed somewhere near Sunbury or Galena. 

I haven't seen anything in the news about it, but one of my neighbors had a friend that saw it. (We were at a graduation party that night and it came up.
