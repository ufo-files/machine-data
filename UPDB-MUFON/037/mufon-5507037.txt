{dr}observed two craft that suddenly turned into several dozen, then back into two as they flew off

I was observing two craft flying from south to north along the coast of Aptos, CA. One craft had a blinking light similar to an airplane and the other was a solid beam of light, I started filming (Canon 6D, with 24-70 L series) becasue I had got some footage the night before of a beam of light in the sky... as I was observing and filming the few people that were in the area I was in left AND as soon as the people left the two craft turned into several dozen craft for about a minute. I tried to refocus my camera and get as much of the craft on video but it was difficult to get the all on film there was so many. I would say there was 30-40 craft in the sky that night.
