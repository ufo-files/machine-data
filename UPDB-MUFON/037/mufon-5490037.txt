{rjl} Bright orange light in sky (fire in sky) thought it was a plane crash but it didnt move, shrank up and moved Eas, blinking lights followed then vanished

Me and my boyfriend were outside stargazing and at about 10:45pm we saw bright orange fireball light appear in the sky. At first I thought it was a plane crash it was like a fire in the sky but it stayed still for a few minutes before shrinking up and moving right (EAST). 2 blinking lights followed it and then it all disapeared. We thought it was strange but couldnt record it since my phone was dead and it only lasted about 3 minutes. We went inside and googled it and got a little freaked out. There had been planes flying around beforehand and after so we thought maybe a military drill? We found videos and pictures that matched what we saw around the same time of day dated a few years back. The one on the washinton mufon network page of the orange light is very similiar but the one we saw was distinctly orange and fireball looking before shrinking up and moving east.
