I was walking down a road at night when I saw a large light above an are I knew to be farm land and woods. It was moving slowly flickering a strange somewhat color changing light. I blew it off as some plane in the distance. 30 mins later or less I found myself sitting under a tree where all the sudden I heard a low humming sound. Looking up I saw a vehicle just above a pecan tree I was under. It was not emitting any kind of light. I could make out a shape of the vehicle. The front was stout with the sides widing slightly from the nose to the tail. They were either wings or just a sharp outer shape. The bottom had lines like that of the rolling metal tank belt for the wheels. I have no idea why I didn't run to follow it. I felt calm at the time and just remember trying to fully take in the hum from the vehicle that was so low yet this vehicle didn't show any evidence of how it was only one tree length above me moving fairly slowly.
