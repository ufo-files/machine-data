Looked like a large sea horse in shape with a dome on top .

At around 17.30 I was standing on the ridgeway at belvedere road London se2 opposite the horse fields . As I looked over the horse fields I saw coming from my left a black and silver object that looked like a large sea horse in black with a silver dome on top . It crossed over the crossness sewage treatment works slowly and floated over towards the horse fields in a se direction . It came down in the field with horses running from the object but gave me some scale to it . it was around 8ft tall 3 feet wide and stood up right and never made a sound nor did it make any turns . It landed in the reeds so myself and friend went to look . we checked all over fields and treeline but found nothing . It still plays on my mind and am keen to find out what it was .
