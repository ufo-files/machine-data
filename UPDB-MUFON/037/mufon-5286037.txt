Star like object appeared, brightened, banked, and dimmed out of visible spectrum.

Early AM, after midnight. After 30min meditation. Three distinct flashes, like that of a camera, were observed. They appeared in a sequence of two nearly 90 degrees in elevation and in the same location. Then the third at the same degree of elevation but further to the east and more directly overhead. 

These flashes preceded a bright object originally thought to be an airplane on direct approach with its landing lights on. I live near SeaTac so air traffic is very common. However, the approach angle from the west to the east, from south of the airport is not used with planes as large as this would have had to be. It also appeared to be much higher than a plane on final approach. 

These two conflicts occurred to me and as I acknowledged internally that I didn't know what I was looking at, and that it may in fact be a UFO, it brightened intensely, to the level of close-proximity car lights (in appearance - no visible light was cast upon me or my surroundings that I could tell) in what felt like acknowledgment. As it brightened, and as I experienced an unexplained feeling of external 'confirmation'. the object banked gently to it's port side and continued in a Northeastern path slowly dimming until is was no longer visible. 

Total duration, 8-10 sec.
