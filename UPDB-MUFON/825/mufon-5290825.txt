What I believe was a V shaped or possible triangular craft appeared over Doak Campbell stadium in Tallahassee.

My buddy and I were on our balcony listening to the release of Joji’s new album Nectar when all of the sudden I thought I saw a helicopter appear in the middle of the sky. Why I don’t believe this was a helicopter is because it flashed lights along the under side of it that resembled a V shape with three white points at the tips of the V. It also seemed to emit a red glow from the center that I thought was typical of helicopters in the area. The difference I noticed, and my friend confirmed this, we couldn’t hear a thing in the night sky. Usually we can hear a helicopter when it becomes within eyesight. This craft flew closer and more erratically than any I’ve seen in my life on this earth. It seemingly spun while slowly hovering across the sky until it began to speed up at an incredible rate. It then proceed to fly as lower and closer than ever before abruptly taking off vertically into the night sky. I wish I could say this was humans but after some research, I doubt we have that type of technology. Although we did only see them briefly, it did not seem they came in malice as far as I could tell. Possibly just trying to see why humans get so excited to go to a football game maybe. Sorry for the lack of evidence but it happened faster than my friend and I could react other than just look up and stare while trying to gain as much insight as possible.
