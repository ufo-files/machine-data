I seen 2 ufo's while looking to the south east and one to the north east

I came outside during a break at work, as I walked out I looked to the east, I was actually looking at venus I believe, but something didn't look right. In between venus and another bright star to the left of venus, I seen a third bright light in between them. Then I noticed that it was moving in a north easterly direction. It moved slowly and after I would say 3 to 4 mins, it just vanished. This was a bright white light, not an aircraft. A few min. later I was still watching the sky, and as I looked to the north, I seen another bright light. This one was more intense, and was moving much faster. It was headed in a easterly direction. I watched it as long as I could until I had to go back to work. It was still headed in the same direction as I went back inside. I couldnt believe what I was seeing. Im a skeptic untill proof is in my face, and this sighting really spooked me. I really believe what I saw was not an aircraft, shooting star, ect.. This was different than anything I have ever seen. I tried to take pics with my camera phone, but could not get it to focus, all I could see was black.
