Straight line of glittering lights moving very very slowly

I was driving down McLellan Blvd and I saw way up in the sky a long line of lights that I knew weren't stars but they looked very far off in the sky then it started moving slowly down and I pulled over to a gas station and got out of my car to try to video tape it with my phone camera and another guy pulled over too and we were both freaking out and it foated in a line and went above us and past us. It looked like the side of a spacecraft that just had lights down the side that were all glittery and there was a very faint shape that almost looked like it could be triangular. I would have to draw it to be able to explain it. I recorded some on my phone camera but it did not pick it up. When I slowed down the video and blew it up I could barely see it.
