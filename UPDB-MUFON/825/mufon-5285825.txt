Saw shiny metallic object hover for around 15 minutes. My son said it looked disc shaped . My wife witnessed as well.

I was playing tennis with my family and saw about 50 degrees up in the sky due south an object that was a fair distance from us. It was metallic like aluminium and seemed to be hovering. It moved a little from one side to another but mostly stationary. The sun was out and no cloud. My son said it looked disc shaped but was it was to far away to really tell. It was like it was spinning because it would have a metallic flash then nothing. It wasn’t a plane or helicopter or balloon because it was stationary for to long. We thought it might be a drone but I felt it was to high up and stationary for to long. We observed it for about 15 minutes before we lost if it. We would watch for a while then hit the ball before watching again. I took a video but it showed nothing.
