{rjl} Spotted in photograph

I was viewing old photos when I spotted this unusual mark on one photo. I took the picture from a scenic overlook near Devils Lake, North Dakota. After enlarging the photo, I thought the spot looked like an object accelerating upward. I applied a filter to bring the shape into better view. The three photos I am submitting are of the original unfiltered and two with the filter.
