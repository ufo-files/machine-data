{dr}two long dark objects emitting a jet like contrail

I had just finished supper at our favorite restaurant at the corner of two major highways in town. I was standing outside waiting on my husband to come out and looked up into the sky. At first I thought it might have been a military formation because the two objects were very close in tandem with each other, they were less than a thousand yards apart going across the sky. I thought it was kind of a little too tight a formation for even a military one, and I know commercial jets would not be flying in such a tight formation. As my husband joined me, I asked him to look at the two objects because I thought it kind of unusual. Right about then the rear of the two objects broke off the chase and made a sharp left hand turn away from the other object heading west. As we passed through town heading back toward our house the object had a sharp reversal and was headed back east. I did not see if the object went back to his chase.
