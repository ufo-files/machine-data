futuristic dark colored airplane shape with small wings and a glowing pure white cone shaped nose

I was outside having a smoke at around 4:30am looked up and there was an object as described going right over my neighbors house a few feet away from east to west direction maybe about 50 kilometers per hour, not very fast, not very high maybe 100ft up. At first I thought it was an airplane because it was shaped like one however it looked a bit futuristic and the wings were small and didn't look light they could provide any lift then I though hey why is there no sound and the tip of the vehicle where the cockpit would be went into a cone shape towards the tip and the "cone" at the tip was glowing pure white... no airplane I know has these features. It went over me then over some trees when I realized I was seeing something special I ran after it to see it on the other side of the trees where its flight path would have been and it was gone. This is the second weird event in the sky at this residence, I had posted one here years back I would like to add.
