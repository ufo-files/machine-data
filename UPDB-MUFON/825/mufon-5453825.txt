{cak} 50 to 70 star like objects moving across the sky for 15 minutes.

Extreme clears sky's this evening due to off sure winds here in southern California. (Riverside) Me and my wife were out with some neighbors talking. I was just thinking how clear it was, and all the stars were out. I noticed an object that appeared to be a star, and definitely out in space moving at a moderate rate of speed for that altitude. I called my wife and neighbors that were 6 feet from me. We all were watching this object move from south to north. At first, I thought it was a satellite. At that point l realized all of the star-like objects in my vision were moving. Then everyone standing there started to point out numerous objects moving. For about 15 minutes we saw about 50 to 70 objects all coming from one direction the sky (south west) and moving to the other (north east). As I looked around I also saw some of them moving from west to east . I called my father who is former Air Force living 30 miles to the north in San Bernardo. I told him what I was seeing. I begged him to come out side and look to see if he could see them. He quickly looked up (while on the phone) and saw the objects. This meant to me it was definitely in space because of the distance from our locations and the objects proximity in the sky. Everyone was kinda of freaking out and started to yell for their children. Me being a Firefighter (Captain) everyone started to tell me to do something...I really didn't know who to call and I didn't want to call 911. If I don't see it on the News I am going to call the non emergency police number to at least tell someone or some type of Authority. We tried recording but our phones couldn't pick it up. Just our voices are recorded.
