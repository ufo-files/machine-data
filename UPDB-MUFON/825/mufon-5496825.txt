{cak} Driving home after work; spotted 7 lights in a boomerang shape

While driving saw the 7 white lights in a boomerang shape across my path; they just appeared suddenly. They remained in this position for about 5-8 seconds and then blinked out simultaneously; reappeared about 8 seconds later, but this time were in a straight line formation; remained in this position for about 8 seconds and then each light blinked out separately this time. I did not see them again. The lights were bright and surprised me by their sudden appearance. I went home and told my husband and daughter.
