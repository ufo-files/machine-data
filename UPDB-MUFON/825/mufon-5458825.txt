I was outside talking to my father on the phone. I saw something really bright red come up, and I watched it hover! As it hovered it went from solid red and changed color to green! This object was lit solidly. These were not strobes. The light was on continuous. It hovered in place, then it move quickly vertically. I could no longer see it as a tree was in the way. 

As I continued to speak with my father I got a step ladder and kept looking in the area. Some minutes later it came back. I saw it come up, it was again colored red, and it hovered. This time it move to the NW rapidly. it came back the other direction and was gone. 

I continued to watch the are where it was for at least 15 minutes. Also during this time the cop plane which had been near my house had moved over to where I would estimate this object to be. And the cop plane loitered at high altitude for at least 20 minutes in a big circle. The center of the circle would be where I believe this object I saw was. 

I have been watching planes and astronomical objects all my life. I know the difference between a meteor, a prop plane, a Jet a fighter, a passenger jet, a helicopter, a satellite, et cetera. This object was not lit and did not move as any of those craft move.
