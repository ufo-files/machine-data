{nd} black triangle hovered then slowly floated away

I live in a somewhat rural area not far from Little Rock, Arkansas. I was riding home with a friend when we noticed a strange orange light in the distance. As I got closer to home, we realized we were getting closer to the light as well. As we approached we noticed it was not moving and assumed it must be a helicopter. We kept getting closer and it became apparent this was no helicopter. At this point we knew we were looking at a black triangle with a blue light (rocket engine?) at each corner. and then sequentially pulsing white to red lights in a stripe down the center. We came very very close to it, it was not very high off the ground and it began to float away, practically right overhead, very very slowly. I had him stop the car and we rolled down the windows. The craft seemed to be completely silent. I then noticed a very very small flashing red light in the distance and in the crafts direct flight path which was timed to match the sequential flashing lights on the bottom of the craft. It continued floating off into the distance.
