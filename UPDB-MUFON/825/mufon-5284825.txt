It was to large to tell the shape. Hovered about 100 ft above me

I had a crazy UFO experience about 30 years ago. I lived on the north side of Kansas City. Driving home one night around 10:30 pm on a dark side street. For some reason, I looked up and saw something unbelievable. It was huge and took up as far as my eyes could see. It made absolutely no noise and was just hovering in place. It was so big that I couldn't make out the shape. But I could see it putting off some kind of wierd light in places. (Very low almost ambient light) I could see what appeared to be tubes and depressions in the underneath areas. My windows were down and I did not hear anything unusual! If I had not looked up, I would not have even known it was there. I still have a vivid memory of it all these years later.
