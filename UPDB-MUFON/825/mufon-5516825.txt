{dr}red/orange glowing orb seen from my front porch

I was sitting on my front porch around 9:30-10:00 and saw a red/orange glowing light that was brighter than I am accustomed to seeing in the sky by my home. We live near a local airport and see many different kind of airplanes in the sky at low altitudes around our home. This was not like any light that I have seen around here! I watched it hover for about 30 seconds before I called for my wife, who was inside the house to come out and take a look. She does not believe in this kind of thing and thinks that I am crazy because I do, but we both stood there watching the light for about another minute before the object looked like it was starting to move north while getting smaller in diameter before it dissapeared behind the clouds that were moving in. I looked for flashing lights on this object and there were none to be seen.
