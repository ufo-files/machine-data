Red & white lights in the sky hovering and moving in Sarnia ON Canada on March 30, 2014

I got a call from ****** and ****** that there were lights in the sky changing from red to white hovering and moving. I promised ****** I would go outside and take a look and I went into the dark field across the street from me here on Borden St. and what did I end up seeing? I saw these same lights and the best part was I had someone else there who saw the same things as me. I also saw one of the lights move towards and almost meet up with another light. I hope people with good cameras get out there now and shoot the night sky cuz something in our sky tonight is not normal. I made sure to message the Sarnia Observer to tell them they might want to have a photographer go take a look at the night sky. For the record the sky is very clear tonight and you can see everything. Stars are in the sky and are all white. These objects we are seeing are definitely not stars and sure as heck don't look like any planes I have seen before and based on the time line that ****** and ****** saw them to when I and another guy saw them if they were planes they would have been long gone by the time I got out to the field. I sent a message to the University of Western Ontario telling their observatory to take a look at the sky tonight in Sarnia if they could. They have a Facebook page for the observatory.
