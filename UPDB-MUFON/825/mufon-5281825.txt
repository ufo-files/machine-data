Lights appearing in Washington sky

My husband and I went out in our hot tub on the evening of Monday April 11th at 9 pm. 
We’d barely just gotten in the hot tub when we noticed a spot in the sky facing north, where lights where appearing to be coming out of no where. We were shocked watching for a minute or two before I ran inside to grab my phone and try to get it on video. It was one light after the next. Had to have been in the hundreds of lights.
