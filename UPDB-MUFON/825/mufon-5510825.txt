{nd} Triangular UFO with 6 lights above residential neighborhood

I saw lights (about 7) in a triangular formation hovering above a neighborhood. I thought it was just one of those really large street lights that you see at malls, but then I realized that it would be immensely strange to see one of those in a residential neighborhood. I slowed down to get a better look but the trees kept blocking my view (I was driving). I pulled over not even three seconds later, and it was nowhere to be seen. On my way back home, I drove up and down that street twice and could not find the source of the light I had seen before. It was the most bizarre thing ever. I live by the Naval Air Station in Fort Worth, so it could have been military craft, but my god that thing must have been going obscenely fast.
