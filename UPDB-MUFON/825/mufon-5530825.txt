While outside taking the dog to the bathroom, I looked up and saw a very very small object moving across the sky. I thought it was a plane at first, and for some reason kept watching it because it left no jet trail and I heard nothing. It got closer and lower and eventually I could see that it was cigar-shaped. I thought that it was 2 capsule-shaped objects which appeared to be attached, flying end-to-end. It was high enough to be going through the wispy clouds overhead. It maintained an even, yet brisk speed. I could see clearly that there were NO WINGS -- that was what really amazed me. It was very odd.
