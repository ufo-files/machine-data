{dr}tWO SEEN ONE MOVING ACROS SKY SILENT, OTHER BRIGHT STAR AND DIM STAR. BRIGHT ONE BECAME 10 TIMES BRIGHTER AND BOTH MOVED OFF AS IF ONE HUGE CRAFT NO SOUND.

The first time I saw the white and orange object I was standing out in my yard having a smoke looking at the stars. I first thought I saw a meteor but the object flashed again and then went dark.Then again father to the east and this time it went orange then real bright light then dark again. It continued across to the east repeating the orange to bright white pattern, then went dark to the south east and never came back again that night. I have seen it three other times over the month. The second object I saw was more startling. I was also watching the sky and noticed two stars that did not seem familiar to me looking from my back yard. I was about to decide I had just not noticed them in the past when the star in back became an extremely intense white and both lights took off toward the east at a fairly swift rate. They did not make any sound , and they did not take off at an ungodly speed, but it did seem they were both part of the same craft. I lost them after they passed the trees out back. I know satellite's and these were not. I would be glad to show them to you just show up around two am. signed: A wish come true.
