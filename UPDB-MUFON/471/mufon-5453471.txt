{rjl} A black triangular shaped aircraft swiftly changing velocity vectors

Looked outside the window at night, saw a silent black triangle moving quickly. There was a central light and it changed directions almost immediately and flew back to the same direction from which it came from. About 15 minutes later, I heard the sounds of aircraft flying that same direction, engines sounded louder than usual passenger aircraft, I assumed they were military.
