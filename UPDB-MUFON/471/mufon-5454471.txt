Series of Star Like objects in straight line moving at fast speed

At approx. 8:28pm, BST, My partner called me out from inside, to view the sky- we witnessed together, approximately 6 star like objects moving at a high speed in a straight line in the same direction- heading SE. They were not blinking, and were the same size as what the stars appeared to be. These were not shooting stars, their path was perfect alignment. All travelling with a consistent gap between them. The last one we saw ventured a bit off the path as the rest and slowly disappeared.
