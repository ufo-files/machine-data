{cak} Late one morning I saw an angular, star shaped UFO fly silently past my window.

It was late in the morning on May 26, 2014 and I was in my upstairs living room when I happened to glance out my window. I noticed a white and grey angular object fly silently by in front of the trees in a straight line, coming from the direction of some power lines. It was a little bigger than a basketball and shaped like a star. It had no wings, propellers or any kind of propulsion that I could see. I was stunned by the sight but did not feel threatened. It flew quickly out of sight and I did not have time to grab my camera. I did not tell the neighbors because I didn1t think that they would understand. They would say that I was crazy.
