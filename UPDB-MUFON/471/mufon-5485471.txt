{dr}I SAW THE CIRCLE IN THE SKY AT 1 AM IN THE MORNING.

FILLED OUT AT MUFON HQ WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER ON 2/2/201
============================================================================

I SAW THE CIRCLE IN THE SKY AT 1 AM IN THE MORNING. IT WAS DIRECTLY OVERHEAD AND AS BIG AS THE ROOF OF MY HOUSE. IT WAS DARK, CIRCULAR, DIMLY LIT WITH RED, WHITE, AND BLUE LIGHTS. THE LIGHTS WERE ON THE SIDE OF THE CIRCLE AND WENT ALL AROUND. I SAW IT FOR HOURS, I HAD A MAGNETIC LIKE FEELING, LIKE IT WAS TRYING TO PULL ME UP.
I HAVE HEALTH PROBLEMS AFTER THE SIGHTINGS, LIKE P.T.S. I FEEL USED AND LEFT BEHIND.
I HAVE ALSO SEEN HUNDREDS OF LIGHTS BEFORE IN THE SKY, LIKE THE FAMOUS SIDNEY LIGHTS FROM LONG AGO.
