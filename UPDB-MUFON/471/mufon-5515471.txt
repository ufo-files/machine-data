{cak} object in sky over norh eastern palm beach county, photos.9/20/12

Driving east on donald ross blvd at 10 am I observed a cigar shaped object low on the horizon moving north to nw west of i95..followed object on I 95 then headed west on indian town road jupiter to follow its nw heading. Continued trying to follow object on prat whitney road and another side road but object got too far away. Chase lasted about a half hour. Object first looked cigar shaped grey metallic and later more disc shaped with a wobbling flutter. Have several photos from beginning of sighting to the end..Incredibly strange and there were several people on i95 overpass bridged pointing and looking at object..There must have been hundreds of sightings.. hopefully there are others to confirm this report..Called local news and they said they had several calls and were interested in looking at my photos. If anyone else saw this object please make a report on this site.
