{rjl} Light changing color and direction and speed erratically

Before I began recording video, the movements were more noticeable - with quicker changes in speed and direction, and more times than the time I began recording this video, unfortunately. 

The light, which changed from dim to bright and from white to a red-orange color for different amounts of time (no pattern whatsoever) hovered north of the mountains (seen on camera). When it moved to another spot, it would move in a way that seemed like it wasn't propelled forwards but like it slipped into the place it wanted to go, and stopped abruptly but smoothly. If clocked, the speed it moved at some times was unusually fast and very unlike anything propelled in a way that the public is aware of.

In the far distant background of the video you can see an airplane that goes directly behind the top part of the light.
