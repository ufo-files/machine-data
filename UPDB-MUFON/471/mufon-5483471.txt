{rjl} Witnessed large light orange object in sky over radio towers

Left for work as usual at around 230 Monday morning. Driving near airport, Looked up and saw a bright light orange light similar to the size of a full moon when it seems to be its largest hovering in sky. My depth perception could have been off by the object appeared to be above the radio towers on Mt Uncamoonuc. Instead of seeing the object fly away, the size of the light shrunk until I could no longer see it. It was a very quick event but nothing that I had ever seen before.
