Big Circular Flash covering field

As me and my mother were driving on Rt. 168 North on our way into Hermitage, I observed a quick flash to my right. It created a huge ring shape. Im not sure how to best describe it but it happened as fast as a lightening strike, but it was like a perfectly oval ring of BRIGHT white/blue light that became softer/faded towards the outside edges. Immediately after seeing this as Im asking my mom if she saw it, (she said no), the passenger side headlight starts to flicker. BOTH headlights were replaced last week since weve been having problems with them. Maybe its unrelated, maybe not. But Ive been watching the sky every single night for almost a year now and this is the 4th unexplainable thing Ive witnessed. (The other 3 had multiple witnesses). If anyone has seen anything similar, or is watching the skies in Western PA, please let me know. Id love to know other people know what Im talking about. Thank you.
