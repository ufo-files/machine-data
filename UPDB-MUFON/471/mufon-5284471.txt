Unknown object looked like it was going to crash into the ground.

At first I thought that this was a meteor in the upper atmosphere "shooting star" but the light coming from it was different and instead of a trail of light it had light coming out from it on each side. The light coming from it fanned out as it left the object. Making it look sort of bowtie-ish or dragonfly-ish (with one wing on each side). The center or possibly the body of the object was white and pink. The light coming from the sides, or possibly its wings was bluish green. These wings/ light coming from the sides had a clear/abrupt end. It wasn't as if the light was coming out of the body/ center and dissipated the further it got from the object. I first noticed it coming from the NW heading NE as it passed over the Kansas turnpike. It was defending, rapidly. I thought it was going to crash into the ground and actually looked and listened for it to crash. It didn't, I think it just disappeared. There is no way for me to know how far away it actual was or how large it actually was. I do not know how to articulate distance and size well enough to explain that it looked close enough and large enough that if it had been an airplane I would be able tell. It happened so fast that I didn't have time to notice for reference if it passed in front of any structures. I am no artist, but I tried to draw an example of what I saw.
