Disk-like objects appearing in clouds.

Myself and my two brother-in-laws were outside flying a kite with my son and saw these objects in the far distance up in the clouds. Some would be stationary and some would disappear and appear in a different location. I ran inside and got my camera and took some pictures. They were there long enough that we spotted them moving around and tried to determine what they could be. Thus far, I cannot explain what they could have been.
