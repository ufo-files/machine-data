I photographed a sun halo and later saw what appears to be an orb in the 3 photos

My wife noticed a sun halo in the sky while we were at the pool on vacation in Costa Rica. I thought it was unusual, so I took three photographs with my I phone, trying to get it centered (I couldn't look at it directly). All of the photos were within a minute. That evening, we were looking at the photographs from our day and noticed an orb in all three photos at different positions. Photos are attached and numbered in the order I took them. Photos 1 & 2 were seconds apart. Photo 3 was about 1 minute later. I also have a video that barely captured part of the object for a brief second.
