UFO following helicopter

I was driving to work 2/26/21 southbound on the 5fwy before Castaic. I saw some ravens flying around and I was casually looking up to see them. I had reached an area before Castaic on the 5 where you can see the entire Valley, it’s like your on top of a hill looking down at the valley floor and you can see for miles and miles. While I was still looking to the sky for ravens I saw a helicopter in the distance and noticed a shiny silver pill shaped object hovering above it and just behind it. I initially thought it was a drone or something. The helicopter looked like it could have been military so I thought maybe it was a military drone, but the more I looked at it the more I realized that it was way too big and not shaped like any drone I’m aware of. It followed the helicopter while the helicopter was flying around the valley randomly like it was lost. This pill shaped object had its nose angled downward towards the helicopter and steadily kept exactly at pace with the helicopter moving in unison with it. The object looked like it was larger than the cabin of the helicopter but not as long as the helicopter and its tail. It was also very very silver looking, almost chrome and it very brightly reflected the sun at times. While I’m watching this thing I’m not at all looking at the road because my eyes were glued to it. Then it very suddenly disappeared. Didn’t fly away, didn’t slowly fade. Like faster than anything I could imagine it was gone. The helicopter kept flying around the valley, I even spotted it later in the distance when I pulled into work still randomly flying around.
