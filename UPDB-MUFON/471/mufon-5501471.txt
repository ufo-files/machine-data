{dr}Huge fireball flew over house and watched til disappeared. Slower than comet/meteor. Too large to be any known object.

At approximately 11:30 pm on 7/2/2014, I was relaxing in hammock looking at sky as I normally do on clear nights. I watched a satellite traveling NE direction above me, and within 2 - 3 minutes this HUGE fireball appeared flying in the same direction and seemingly same general area in the sky. I have never seen anything that big, with orange or amber light. I had enough time to run to house to call for boyfriend to come look. He came out and was able to witness it as well. It was flying at a controlled speed, not like a comet/meteor/falling star. It was a constant glow, not sure if it was cigar shaped or simply had a tail of some sort. Both he and I agree that we have never seen anything like that before. Was very exciting. We watched it for about 3 minutes, then it just disappeared.
