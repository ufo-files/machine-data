{nd} When observed object hovering with blinking lights that changed colors. Enlarged screenshots show rectangle shaped object with beam lights.

At 9:45 pm on July 3, 2016 my son and I observed an object hovering just east of our home. The object changed colors blinking frequently from red, green, blue, purple and white. Suddenly it moved east traveling out of site over the horizon. We captured almost 2 minutes of cell phone video. I also enlarged screenshots of the video, where a more defined shape can be seen.
