{tjd} Light traveling from North to South very fast

Wife and I were sitting out in the back yard having a fire. I was looking at the stars and noticed a particular pattern and was looking at it for a minute or so. I was curious to see if it was a constellation so I looked down to install a constellation finder on my phone and looked up one second later replying to something my wife had said then looked back at the stars and noticed one of them in the pattern had started moving very fast to the south. I pointed it out to my wife. We’ve both seen satellites pass before and it’s pretty tough to get the other person to see it but this was very evident due to the brightness of it and she saw it immediately. It passed over our house and into a cloud and we were unable to see it any longer.
