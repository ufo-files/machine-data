Triangle craft moving slowly over and past Spiceball Park

On my way home from a nearby gym, heard an extremely loud and unsettling humming noise over the music in my headphones and took them off to look around for the source. Spotted the object over Spiceball Park, moving slowly in a straight line heading NNE. I identified that it was moving too slowly and the lights were not flashing so it couldn’t be a plane or helicopter. Three unblinking white lights on underside, dark surface. Attempted to film it on my iPhone SE but I fumbled and by the time the camera app was open, the object was far enough that I could not film with any clarity.
