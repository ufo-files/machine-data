I saw a green light fall from the sky like a flare, then stop and fly away from me to the north.

I was walking in Ishpeming, Michigan by lake Angeline at 1:30 AM. It was a perfectly clear night, and in front of me and a friend I saw a bright green light in the sky. At first it looked like a fire work, or a flare floating in the air. Before I had time to decide what it looked like, it stopped briefly, and flew straight away from us, heading almost due north. As it was flying away I could make out a circular shape, with four distinct green lights in a circle. It happened so fast that I wasn't sure what I saw at first, and we just stood there looking at each other dumb founded.
