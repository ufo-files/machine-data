{nd} 2 RED LIGHTS APPEARED TRAVELLED SIDE BY SIDE. THEN 3 MORE APPEARED AND PASSED BY.

IT WAS FRIDAY AUG. 1, 2014 at 9:15P.M. I WAS TAKING A LETTER TO THE MAIL BOX FOR SATURDAY PICK-UP. AS I TURNED TO GO BACK UP THE DRIVEWAY I NOTICED 2 RED LIGHTS APPROACHING FROM THE SOUTHEAST. I THOUGHT THEY WERE HELICOPTERS, BUT THEY MADE NO SOUND. I WATCHED THEM PASS OVER TOWARDS THE NORTHWEST AND THEN VANISH.I THOUGHT TO MYSELF HOW LUCKY I WAS TO BE OUTSIDE AT JUST THE RIGHT TIME. AT THAT MOMENT I NOTICE 3 MORE RED LIGHTS IN THE DISTANCE, APPROACHING FROM THE SAME DIRECTION AS THE FIRST TWO. THE 3 CROSSED THE SKY ABOVE ME AND VANISHED AT ABOUT THE PLACE THAT THE FIRST 2 DID. THE OBJECTS NEVER CHANGED COURSE OR SPEED. I COULD NOT SEE ANY STRUCTURE TO THE OBJECTS. IN FACT IT WAS AS THOUGH I COULD ALMOST SEE THRU THE RED LIGHTS. I THEN TELEPHONED SOME FRIENDS IN THE AREA, BUT THEY WERE ALL INSIDE THEIR HOMES DURING THE EVENT. ONCE I REALIZED THE OBJECTS WERE NOT HELICOPTERS, I TRIED TO REMAIN CALM AND OBSERVE AND REMEMBER EVERYTHING I SAW. I HOPE THIS SHORT REPORT IS OF SOME VALUE TO MUFON.
