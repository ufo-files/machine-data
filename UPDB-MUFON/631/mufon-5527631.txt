Two flashing lights above horizon

I was getting ready to leave my friends house at 1:15 am, my two friends and I decided to go outside and have a cigarette before I left. After about 15 seconds of standing there, she asked what that glowing, flickering light in the sky was. Pointing above the mountain horizon, I saw what appeared to be a yellow/orange/white light glowing sporadically in the sky. I pulled my cell phone from my pocket and filmed it as steady as I could. We first argued that maybe it was a car pointing its light on the horizon directly at us, but then we realized that they would have to be very strong lights. We also talked that maybe it was a fire, however, that was disbanded because fires tend to glow softly from that distance. No, this was definitely above the mountain horizon. After about 2 minutes or so, my friends asked what the other light in the sky was. However, looking only at the original, I had no idea there was a brighter, bluish-white, much more active glowing one to it's left. I recorded both close together in the second video. I had to leave and quit filming because it was late. When I got home, my step-father saw the video and recommended to be placed here.
