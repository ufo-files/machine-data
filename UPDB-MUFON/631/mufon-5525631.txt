I didn't notice the object until I download the photos

In the summer of 2009 went to Ecuador for vacations and from my parents balcony I started taking pictures (don't remember the exact date) of the great moon that was rising from the horizon. Took 2 pictures and after I download them in the computer I saw that in the right side of the moon there was this very shiny object that I could not identify. You can see on it the light of the moon reflecting... very amazing!!! At that moment, when taking the picture, I was so concentrated in the moon that I didn't see anything... unfortunately!!!!
