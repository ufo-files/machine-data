{dr}On 6/24/17 @ 10pm I witnessed some Airial Phenomenon that intelligently descended twice to different elevations before ascending, hovering & instantaneously shooting horizontally.

On June 24, 2017 @ approximately 10pm I witnessed something unexplainable. I went outside between TV episodes to have a cigarette and enjoy the nice evening weather. While sitting I noticed a light in the SW sky that at first I believed to have been anice airplane but that perception quickly changed. While I watched the object it rapidly descend, stopped hovering, then descended againg. Each time the object moved it radiated an expanded redish aura for lack of a term. It then rapidly asended & stopped hovering motionless without any sound at all. The objects color began to change to a greenish hue. It pulsed rapidly becoming a whiteish greenish color seemed to contract while getting brighter before it shot instantaneously in a Westwardly direction a good distance from my vantage point at incredible speed & abruptly stopped, hovering again motionless momentarily before shooting back to the point of origin where it again stopped motionlessly hovering. The object began pulsing again all the while brightening from greenish to greenish whiteish before seeming to contract & vanishing completely. Did anyone else witness this event or report on it?
