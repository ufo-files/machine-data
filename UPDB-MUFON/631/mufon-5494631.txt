{rjl} Massive, totally quiet triangle shaped object flew slowly over my neighborhood right over the trees.

A massive, black craft flew very, very slowly over the treetops in my neighborhood. It was so large that when I stopped my car to look, it was all I could see out of my windshield and windows. It didn't make any sound at all, and the trees didn't sway or make any noise, like they would if something like a helicopter or other craft flew so close to them.It was right over my head, only the distance from the ground to right over the trees. It moved really slowly over the neighborhood going north west/west and I watched it till it disappeared. Again, it made absolutely no noise. When it flew over, it blocked out other sounds...either that, or I was so focused on the craft that I didn't hear nearby traffic.
