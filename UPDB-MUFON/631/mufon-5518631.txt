I was at a daughter home in the city of diamond bar reading and listening to music with earphones in the upstairs bedroom. I felt a strong urge to look out the window and observed a silver metalic probe hovering below and across the street between the two front houses. I watched and studied it for about ten minutes. A car approached and it went behind a large bush. I didn't go outside or told anyone till months later. Again I felt thtpiianonet I should not mention it.
