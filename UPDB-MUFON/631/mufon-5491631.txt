{dr}Lighted object changed directions and then hovered in one place before ejecting another bright lighted object out from the bottom of the craft.

We were traveling south on Interstate 5 about a mile from the Sutherlin exit when we first noticed the light. At first we thought it was a satellite due to the size and brightness of the object. We watched it flying forward toward us for at least 5 minutes before we pulled over and exited the vehicle to get a better look. When we got out of the car I started video taping and the object changed directions. After it flew the other direction for about one minute, it appeared to stop and stay in one place. It stayed stationary for about 3 more minutes at which point another brightly lit object was ejected from the bottom of the craft. The object that was emitted flashed several times as it exited and then disappeared. At that time, the original object seemed to fly straight up until it was so high we couldn't see it anymore. I have a great video of this and 3 other people that were also witnesses to the event.
