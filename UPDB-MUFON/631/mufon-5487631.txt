Did not know it was there.

Never been to London before. So I was taking pictures of everything. Waited till I was able to get the best shot of places with least interference of objects and people. I did not notice anything in the sky as I was taking pictures. I noticed this object in the left hand corner of the Buckingham palace a few weeks after my trip. I was looking at pictures of my trip when I had reached this photo and noticed it right away. I showed it to my wife, and she didn't think it was nothing much, but possibly something on my lens or a plane. I told her that she was probably correct, till I showed her another photo 3-4 minutes later of the object in the background moving, that photo is not very clear (of course). Have no idea of what it is, just thought it may be of interest to other people.
