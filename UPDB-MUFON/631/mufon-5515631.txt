(GS) Orb followed by triangular object descending in isolated rain in Las Vegas

On my porch, which is on the 2nd story, I noticed an interesting cloud formation with rain falling near the Las Vegas Strip, which I can see from my apt in western Las Vegas, (on Charleston Bl.) After reviewing the two photos I took, I found not only an orb which moved straight downward (yellow in color) but also noticed an unusual triangle of 3 green lights on the 2nd photograph both seemed to be "hiding" in the isolated rain shower. I have photographic proof time stamped 8/13/12 @ 4:40 & 4:41am. I am interested in knowing more of this particular sighting, where there any others this same morning?
