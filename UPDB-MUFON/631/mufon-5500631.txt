{cak} Large blue mass of energy? appeared. Upon it disappearing? I felt a wave of energy/radiation travel through my body. Reappeared higher, and farther in the sky about 20 seconds later.

About 1 am I took my dog out to pee. She ran off, and my neighbors dog who is in a pen started whimpering. I got nervous thinking there might be an animal in the woods. Next thing I know there is a silvery, blue disk shape right above my neighbors roof. Only way for me to describe it was it looked like energy. It wasn't solid, but not translucent either. I wasn't sure what I was seeing whether it was from this universe, or spiritual. If I wasn't paralyzed from fear I would have enjoyed it's beauty more. Within 5 or so seconds it dispersed? I felt the energy go through my body. I honestly didn't know for a few minutes if I was dead, or alive. I didn't feel any pain, just warmth. Logically I thought maybe I had somehow got struck by lightening. It looked nothing like lightening though, and I had no pain, burns, anything outwardly different at all that I could tell. I remember saying "What the heck was that?" A few moments later I saw it up higher and farther away. I never saw it move from point A to point B though. After it took off? it was out of sight till I saw it reappear those few moments later. I would describe the size the first time I saw it as around the size of the diameter of our above ground pool. So around 20-24 feet. That's an estimate. I was closer to the ground than it was, but saw it from the side, not underneath. I saw no craft, only the blue energy.
