Over Decker Canyon off PCH in Malibu

I was driving towards Mulholland Drive on Decker Canyon. I had just left beach on got on Decker Canyon from PCH. I was taking a picture of the road to show a friend how windy and cool the road was. I did not notice the crafts in the sky at the time I took the picture. It was not until I got home and reviewed my text message that I noticed the white spot in the upper left of the picture. I zoomed in and then saw what appears to be multiple crafts. The two large crafts appear to have the same front nose and the they both have matching holes on the side. One large hole or window closest to the front of the craft and two smaller holes or windows to the rear. To the bottom right of the two main crafts are two others. One looks more like a bus with a rectangular from windshield there appears to be something next to the smaller craft but is 90% invisible but with the same shape.
