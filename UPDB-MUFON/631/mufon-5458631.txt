Reflective sphere seen in clear part of the night sky during the lunar solar eclipse

During the lunar solar eclipse around midnight, myself and another witnessed a what appears to be a reflective sphere in the clear part of the night sky.
This was only noticed through the photographs on my camera phone after taking them. The sphere was not visible to the naked eye but when whilst I was trying to capture an image of the eclipse, I noticed the sphere in the photograph. Took a couple of pictures in the same direction again to verify and once again, not visible to the baked eye but a sphere could be seen on the photograph.

Checked to see if it could be something such as falling leaf/tree/lamp/airplane and found nothing. That space of the night sky where the sphere appears, was completely clear to the naked eye.
