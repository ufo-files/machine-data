{rjl} Glowing sphere moves south to north.

I came onto my porch and looked up to see a glowing light. I called for my girlfriend Emily to hurry and come see. It moved slowly and silently across the sky from south to north as it gave off a bright almost orange light. My girlfriend and I witnessed it until it flew out of sight with no sound and no marker lights that any plane or helicopter would have. I am 100% sure it wasn't any conventional aircraft of any sort.
