Observed and recorded something I can’t explain

Visiting a friend in Sarasota Florida. I was outside on the screened in terrace when I had a feeling I should turn to my left and look at the sky. I noticed a bright, white pulsating, spinning light coming closer. I ran inside and grabbed my iPhone and began to record. The object kept getting closer with no noise at all. It moved erratically in the sky. The right screening on the terrace made the ball of light look like it was seperated into four parts. It wasn’t. It was very solid and was pulsating and spinning constantly.
