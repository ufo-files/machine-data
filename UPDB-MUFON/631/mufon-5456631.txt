{jpg} 2 white lights blinking at the same time, moved east slowly.

Two lights spotted hovering in the north. Constant flashing. It looked as if the two lights might have been attached to a solid object but I could not tell. They flashed once every second or so. It slowly moved east, and continued flashing at the same rate. I recorded a bit of it on my phone. The lights then mysteriously vanished. 

The thing about these lights is that if it were a plane, there would have been some sort of constant light that stays illuminated. This object did not have that. I'm not saying it was extraterrestrial, but it was definitely unidentified.
