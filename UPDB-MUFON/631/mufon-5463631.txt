Husband and I were headed south on US 101 between Petaluma and Novato, at approximately 5:45 pm on December 29, 2018. A very bright, silent, green round object, traveling southwest, decended steeply and rapidly, over the freeway, disappearing over the distant hills toward the ocean. No trail behind the object or sonic boom was heard or felt. We thought a plane might be in trouble and going down, but was definitely NOT a plane.
