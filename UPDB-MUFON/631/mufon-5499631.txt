At approximately 3am. 2 September 2014, I saw two bright blue white light travelling in formation. It was only for a brief moment and the lights when dark. My friend came by and told him what I saw and a few minutes later he also saw two bright blue white lights in the sky turn on and then off just west and a little south of where I first saw the lights originally. There is an airport 45 km south of our location, but aircraft normally come from the west to land. And the lights of these objects did not have the characteristics of what are normal marker lights of a plane.
