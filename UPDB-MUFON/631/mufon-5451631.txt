Rose up 2 headlights. It hovered at 50ft flashing lights turned left to right about three times, went over block of flats and sped off at great speed

I saw the object when it was rising up from the ground in the fields beyond our housing estate. There were only 2 headlights at this time. It stopped and hovered at 50ft when it started to turn right I could then see flashing lights which seemed to be all around the bottom. It turned left and did the same movements about three times. It then went over my block of flats stopped and hovered for about 2 seconds then sped off at great speed.
