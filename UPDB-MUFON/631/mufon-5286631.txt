Glowing disk with two lights(circles) below it, then disappeared to the naked eye

To start, I live on top of a Mt in the Appalachian's of WV(1400 foot above sea level). There is very little 'light pollution, except for a outside light in front of my house no city or public lighting for miles and miles.

My old man bladder woke me at 4 am, I stepped out the back door to check the sky(always do) and Bam! there it was. A glowing white light with a smaller blue light below it, and a yet smaller orange/red light below that one .. they were lined up and clocked left the largest one be at the 11 O'clock and the smallest pointing at the 5 O'clock. So I started to wake my wife, but decided to grab my phone and try to get a pic/vid.

Not more than 15 feet from the table to the door(10 second walk) and I popped back out the door, looked up and the bottom 2 light were gone, but the top was there BRIGHT and wobbling. I pulled up my phone started taping, zoomed in X2 and looked back at it and boom! it was gone.. 

Now here's the kicker.. I could NOT see it with my naked eye, BUT it was still on the screen, I looked at the sky, then back at the screen, and kept doing that COULD NOT see it with my own eyes but the Samsung Note 9 could.. so I taped it for about two min's, until it disappeared completely... 

(btw not my 1st sighting, that would be Jan 1990 Rock hill SC.. No cell phones then:(
