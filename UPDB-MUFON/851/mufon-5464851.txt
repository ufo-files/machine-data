{tjd} While watching crows circling I noticed a very slow moving object the speed was much slower than an aircraft

While at my place of employment I had a a few minutes waiting for customers at our drive up service area. I was looking at several black birds flying overhead in circles. Then just near to this I noticed an object which did not seem to be an airplane. The object which appeared to be reflective like most aircraft when sunlight is present was barely moving. I thought maybe it might be a commercial aircraft which may have recently taken off from HIA airport. The object seemed to be moving in a more or less straight line but again the speed was way slow almost like not moving at all. There were some other people outside at my location but did not think to question them about what I was looking at.The one person was a postal carrier worker and was leaving at the time so I did not want to interrupt him. The object went out of sight around the front of the building from where I was standing and I was going to walk that way but that was away from my work area. also I tried to get a picture of the object with my phone camera but nothing was visible in the image. Possibly I did not get the angle correct or the camera can not capture objects at that distance.
