{dr}Walking friend up driveway when we noticed 5 bright orange orbs on sky. They hovered then left. More appeared theno followed the first set of orbs

My husband, friend and I had driven home from a concert late Sunday night. My husband and I were walking my friend home when we observed bright orbs in the sky. They were extremely high. One set was in a triangle shape, followed by three that were in a row behind it. They hovered for about 3 minutes then moved rapidly out of sight. Additional orbs came into view, hovered, then followed the first set of orbs. We observed this for approximately 15 minutes before no additional orbs came into view. 

They were orange in color and looked quite like the international space station did when it passed over our area the previous week. We observed approximately 12 orbs.  They left no vapor trail.
