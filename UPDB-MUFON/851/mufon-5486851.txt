While walking on a gravel road called ''Bentley''in Washington State, I came across two separate conical impressions in the road to the left of fallow wheat field. On an almost daily basis during the summer i walk to a weather tower and back again to my vehicle. I noticed these impressions while on my return walk from the weather tower and after seeing the first one,then back-tracked and found the other impression about 45ft. in a nearly straight line from the first. Each was about 1/4th of an inch deep. I noticed a powdery-looking residue that seemed to be from the lighter crushed gravel, the heavier gravel being pushed underneath.No one seems to know what made them. My uncle took me back out to the area a week later so i could show them to him,but they had either been ''graded'' out or run over by heavy farm vehicles.I have no idea what made them.
