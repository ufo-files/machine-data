{nd} bright moonlit night, witnessed while scanning a field with binoculars

Watching coyotes across a field, with binoculars, saw this from quite a distance. Thought it might be an aircraft. but when parallel to directly opposite from me, I could see with my binoculars there was a trail of what appeared to be burning debris. It was not going fast enough to be thought of as a meteor or such. Distance was hard to estimate, as well as elevation. Living in a valley, it continued out of sight behind the mountains. I am sure this was seen by others, due to the skies clarity and the brilliance of the orange glow. Hopefully this was seen by others. I am sure the distance traveled would be quite extensive.
