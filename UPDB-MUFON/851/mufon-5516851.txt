Several orange glowing lights appeared in the sky two or three at a time. The glowing lights moved vertically across sky and after approx. 45 seconds disappeared completely.

WE were outside of home at the fire in late evening 11:30 pm when someone first noticed bright orange lights. My sister-in-law and myself watched them disappear and then three more appeared. the pattern appeared to be vertical movement and then disappear two or three at a time. I attempted to take photos but was unable to catch them on most of the photos. Some photos have small teardrop light - this is not what it looked like in person. 

When we started to discuss our first thought was ufo because there have been numerous sightings in the Peterborough area and rumours of such lights in sky.

My feeling at the time was that they appeared beautiful and calming in a way almost alive. they did not look like lights from aircraft or anything else i have ever seen.

There was absolutley no sound at all from these objects and after perhaps 12 -15 of them passed in sky we did not witness any more
