{cak} Object flying at high rate of speed. I saw multiples of them flying in a V formation 30 - 40 minutes after a mounted camera filmed the first one.

Video was taken on a GoPro camera that I set up during severe thunderstorms.  I also took my drone up to film storms off to the South East at an elevation of 250’-300’. After about 36 seconds of filming my camera on the drone froze, but still showed it was recording. Shortly after the drone camera froze, my drone went a little haywire and acted erratic during flight. As I brought the drone back down, is when I noticed 5 lights in a V formation at least 1000 yards in elevation flying rather fast and silent to the northwest.  Shocked at what I witnessed I checked the GoPro footage to see if anything was captured and sure enough there was footage.
