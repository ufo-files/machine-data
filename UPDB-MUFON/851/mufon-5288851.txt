On a perfectly clear night...A solid unwavering red light moving very fast in a straight line parallel with the ground making no noise and flying into the northeast. Seemed relatively close to ground and myself. I feel strongly that it was neither a star, satellite, meteor, helicopter nor plane. It’s possible it was a very advanced drone unlike ones you typically see. It moved so very fast that I don’t think it could have been a typical one. Maybe if the military has very fast ones. I saw nothing of its structure. It appeared as nothing more than a flying bright red light. It was neither pulsing nor changing in any way. Just a solid unwavering light. It was not like a plasma orb.
