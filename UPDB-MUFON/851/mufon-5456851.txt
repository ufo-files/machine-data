{tjd} Glowing Sphere Reddish Yellow Orange Pulsating and blinking out

Just wondered if anyone saw a large Orange - Red- Yellow glowing and pulsating Orb object hovering over Nw Vancouver in the first week of October 2019 between 6-8 pm? I was walking my dogs in Community Park the sun had not yet set so it was still daylight I had a 360 view of sky and were watching the air traffic. I mention the air traffic because out of corner of my eye I see a much larger bright orange light I think must be tail of jet so I turn and as I was watching this globe, it seem to glow in a perfect pulsating of orangish yellowish reddish fashion. Then I realized it was completely silent and stationary it hadn't moved when I realized this was odd I was looking straight at it and it just blinked out and gone. I report this as just a data point for better witnesses to confirm.
