Walking to park to go sledding. saw 3 lights in sky, they were aproching , we ran home

One evening in my home town, me and my siblings wanted to go sledding, We ask moms permission and left, only a half block away, go down the street turn left, pass one house and the gates for the park are there... as we approach( me, my triplets brothers and a neighbor )
Are getting to the fence entrance , I stop and look up to the horizon, I see 3 lights in a straight line of the horizon. I shouted to my bros to look up... the light seem to get closer to us, and more apart as they where getting closer. Suddenly they went in a triangle patterning tried to move but couldn1t .. mesmerize ..felt like an eternity , I ran home, with my brothers in my tail.... I let mom know of the event that just occurred , she replied that this could has been the army... I still believe that these event happened .... it has been about 40 years since the said event... thank you for listening
