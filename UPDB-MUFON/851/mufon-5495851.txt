{rjl} While driving saw white light decend and stop at treetop level and started to flash white light

While driving south on Rochester road (about 6:45)I saw what looked like a bright star falling very fast (in a straight line down) from about 60-70 degrees to sight level until it got just above tree level. It then stopped and began to flash a bright white light and traveled to the west until I could not see it because of tree obstruction. I also saw a helicopter within a few degrees of sight from the object. It is hard to say if they were close to each other but the helicopter had to have seen it. Most likely a traffic helicopter.
