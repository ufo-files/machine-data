4 Round Orange Glowing Objects

This happened very briefly, was outside bringing the trash out when I looked up into the sky and saw this glowing orange circle which quickly turned into 3 other circles (so 4 total) they went downwards at an angle about 45 degree angle, the objects all appeared to be connected to each other, then quickly went out, didn't fade out went out like you would turn off a light switch. Then about 5 minutes later in the same area this bright orange circle appeared and quickly went out. This time it was only 1 circle. Saw nothing again after that.
