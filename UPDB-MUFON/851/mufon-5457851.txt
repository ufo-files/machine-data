{tjd} A bright boomerang shaped object moving at a high rate for 3 seconds and vanished

I was driving home from stopping at the gym after work last Friday. As I neared the local Rite Aid store I saw a bright, silvery white light zip across the sky through my front windshield. It was roughly boomerang shaped and appeared to be somewhat flat. It was only visible for approximately 3 seconds before it completely vanished as it was moving in an East/Southeast direction. I initially thought it may be a fireball, but it did not seem to match the other fireball I had seen in the past. And it was certainly not a meteor because it was too low in the sky. I heard no noises when I saw the object, which seemed odd for as quick as it seemed to be moving through the sky. It was as if it appeared out of no where and just as quickly vanished.
