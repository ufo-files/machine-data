Two pair of what appeared to be triangle shaped objects with orange lights

While at a early 4th of July cookout with my family, we observed one pair of low flying craft maybe 500 to a 1000 feet up traveling South to North. The objects had a very bright orange-red light on the bottom. The color looked like the color of a flare. After they both passed a certain point the lights went out but you could still see the objects which looked like they were somewhat like a three dimensional triangle. We watched them for several minutes until they were out of our sight. Just a couple minutes after the first pair went out of sight, a second pair appeared traveling the same path as the first, and with the same bright orange-red light which went off at the exact same point as the first two. We set realativly close to an Air Force base that uses C-130s and having been in the military and around different types of aircraft, both in the military and in my job as a police officer, I am sure that this was not a plane or helicopter of any type. There was absolutley no sound of any type with these craft. The craft were not flares or fireworks as they continued to travel on a straight and level path. There were no navigation or marker lights on the craft.
