Lost time

When I was 15 years old me and a group of my friends was walking back to my relatives house this was around 8pm we was only maybe 30 minutes walk from the house and all four of us notice a bright light shining on the concrete we all noticed a bright light shining above us in the dark thinking maybe it was a street light and my friend said where is that light coming from and we stop walking and looked up noticing a very bright light shining blinding us my friend said he couldn’t see anything and just like a blink of a eye we were walking back on the same street in the dark it was like coming out of a daze we walked quietly for a few minutes and I ask my friends what just happen laughing nervously and at that time my other friend said it’s a hour and a half later not believing him I look at my phone where I had 3 missed calls from my cousin and I notice it was now 9:30 when I just looked before it was a little after 8. As kids we jokingly said we was abducted but the seriousness of the situation set in as I finally got to my cousins house and telling her what happen and her not believing us I never told anyone else about it because I didn’t want to be looked at as crazy. Me in my 3 other friends never really spoke about it after that day but to me it’s like a giant weight on my shoulders wondering what happen. The more I get older the more I see ufos in the sky for a slit second in it’s just normal now. I don’t know if I want to dive deeper into what happen because it makes it real but I felt I needed to tell my story to get a since of relief about what happen all those years ago.
