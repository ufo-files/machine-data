{rjl} I witnessed 2 enourmous spacecraft hovering just east of Hwy. 101 between Novato, CA & Petaluma CA.

On 7/1/2013 at 10:35 PM PT I was driving northbound on Hwy. 101 between Novato, Ca and Petaluma, CA when I witnessed 2 enormous spacecraft on the east side of Hwy. 101 hovering for 15 minutes. The spacecraft closest to the highway (approximately 300 yards east of Hwy. 101 & approximately 500 feet in the air) was outlined in white lights with rotating circular colored lights near the front of the aircraft.  They were not helicoptors or airplanes.
