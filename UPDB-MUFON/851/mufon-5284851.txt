3 white orbs stationary

I saw three white orbs less than a mile away, they stayed stationary the whole time I observed them. They were so bright that the stars looked dim in comparison. There were stadium lights nearby and I could debunk that they were stadium lights because the orbs were much higher in the air. When I went back to the gym where I saw them around 6:15 pm the first time, I went back to confirm that it was a UFO and the orbs were gone. I went back around 11:45 pm, they were no where to be seen. I got chills because I realized what I had just seen. I have always wanted to see one in person but I was just frozen and speechless. I knew it wasn’t an airplane or a meteorite or a star because it didn’t fit any of those characteristics. It wasn’t a satellite either. They just stood still like they were staring at me, and I was staring back from the window. They were so bright at first I thought they were just the stadium lights but then I compared them to the stadium lights and they did not look that similar and the stadium lights were much more to the east. The orbs were straight north, 3 bright white lights just stationary high in the air, I’ve never seen something like this before.
