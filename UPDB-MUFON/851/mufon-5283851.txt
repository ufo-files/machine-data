Black object similar to a wilting rose or a kite photographed in skies, but not seen with naked eye.

On January 5th, 2021, I was working on a man lift about 60 feet in the air off the I24 interstate of Chattanooga near Boynton Park. We were painting a retaining wall outdoors. I love taking photos of the clouds and skies and it was a particular beautiful morning with the sun beaming through the clouds on downtown Chattanooga, TN. Since I had a great view, I pulled out my cell phone at 9:24 a.m. and took several pictures of this beautiful scene. At the time I didn't see, hear, or feel anything out of the ordinary. I went back to work and finished the day out as usual,. When I got home that evening, I showed my wife the pictures I had taken. She noticed something right away in the very center of only one of the pictures I had taken that morning. She thought it looked like a black wilting rose in the skies. I looked again and I saw it too. I thought it looked more like a kite. But it was pitch black and in relation to the rest of the picture, it didn't seem to size up to that of a kite or a leaf, and it was too sharp and distinct to be a smudge on the camera. Besides that, why didn't it show up on the pictures I had taken right before and after it? Nothing made sense. I still didn't think much of it, because I never saw it with my naked eye. But there is no doubt from this picture, that something was there. My wife finally convinced me (almost a year later) to make a report with Mufon. This photo is, of course, unedited in any way.
