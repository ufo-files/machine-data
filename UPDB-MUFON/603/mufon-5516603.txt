(GS) Starlike object moved accros sky

I was outside observing the stars and learning the consolations. When over towards Cepheus there was a star that seemed to be moving. So I thought perhaps it was a satellite. I called out 2 people from the house to come and see. The object got a lot dimmer and was a bit harder to locate, but 2 of us were able to find it again. It1s flight path slightly changed directions and faded away.
