These were not satellites!!! Two lights became super bright blue white over Luke Air force base very high up. They both hovered near each other and after 1 minute or so not moving. Then one traveled NNE climbing higher, while the other went NE both disappeared at almost same time. They did not have any other light colors and didn't pulse(marked by accident). They just got bright out if nowhere and went dark out of nowhere. Things strange over Luke does happen, like the bright red I light that went right over them lighting up the clouds. This is nothing new.
