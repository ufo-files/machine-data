I see them often now. My ears ring when it’s present. It just appears and reappears in different areas. Spinning lights of red, green, purple.

I’ve had multiple sightings of three different crafts. I have footage on my iPhone X, but it could never pick up the colours and detail. I purchased my 13 model and I was finally able to zoom in. They just appear and then reappear in different spots in the sky. It’s spherical and hovers. To my eyes it’s flashing multiple colours: Red, Purple, and Green. When I slowed down the footage I took, the lights rotate. 
Sometimes the two multicolored ones show with a larger one that emulates a light blue glow. I also find my ears ring when they appear. Out of the week I get two to three sightings of them. It’s when I take my dog outside and no matter where I move it’s there above me or in front. It just hovers. I always feel immense fear. The only physical sensation that still bemuses me is the first time I saw it. I felt weightless like a balloon. I felt like I was going to fly into the air. My dress even ballooned up as if air was being blasted from beneath my feet. There was no air. Just humid, hot Texas weather. I gripped the side railing of the stairs of the complex I reside in and gasped. That was such a bizarre sensation. I’m a fairly rational person, but in that moment I felt I was just going to float upward. I’ve never experienced that since. 
The video and photos were taken with a iPhone 13 (not Pro version or mini)
