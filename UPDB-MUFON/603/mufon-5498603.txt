{dr}It was a very bright star like object moving accross the sky.

I went outside of my house to get some firewood. I noticed a very bright star like object very high in the sky. It moved in a somewhat guided course. It was very bright and very high up. I watched as it moved across the sky. In Byck here in Northern MN there are few lights and you can see the stars so well. This wasn't a star as it moved across the sky far above the earth. What it was I have no idea as I have never seen an object like this before. I was thinking it could be a sattelite but it was so very bright outshinning the stars behind it. I may be a little hard to get a hold of since there is no cell service and only 11 people in this town. I just don't know or have any idea what it could be as it was so bright and was brighter than the stars. Up here in Buyck with few lights you can see the stars and they appear so clear. Its like nothing I have ever seen so far being of very high altitude. [name removed/cms/dr]
