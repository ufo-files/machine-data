Stationery object x 2 high in clear sky

Out for walk with my wife in coastal town of Largs situated on the south side of the river clyde in Scotland. Perfectly clear blue sky 4pm afternoon walking back to our car I noticed a plane high in sky that had two objects that were stationery in its path or were visible at same time as I could see the plane . The plane continued on its path and the two objects stayed in exactly same spot which caught my attention and I stopped and said to my wife to stop and look at the objects. They were the same size and horizontally in line with each other to start with then the right hand object moved to 90 degrees above the left hand object but appeared to my eye to still be the same distance apart . I watched them for another five to ten minutes and they never moved or flashed or changed shape or emitted any sound or vapour .
My wife had walked on and I followed her back to car.
