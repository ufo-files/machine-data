red white and blue lights flickering in a triangle pattern hovering over a car dealership

Driving on manchester road with my girlfriend on passenger seat near sams club by west county mall. she sees the weird lights and asks me about it. i told her it was probably an airplane. she persisted that it did not look normal. i took a second look and realised that there were no headlights just flickering lights. we took a left turn into the car dealership where a couple of people were also parked and looking at the sky. the object was not making noise and was just hoovering. we did a u turn and left because i was freaked out.
