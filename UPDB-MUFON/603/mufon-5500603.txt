my ex and I was driving along main road when we observed a bright light in sky,we drove towards her mothers house which was where the light was observed at ,as we neared her road I saw 2 beams of light scanning the field adjacent to the road we were travelling on,I shouted to stop the car where upon I watched the beams of light for about 3 minutes at about 100 feet distance,I told my ex I was going to get a closer look but she freaked and started screaming to get back into the car,at that moment a string of little white lights spiralled from the object which meant it was time to leave.I was pumped and excited at the moment of seeing this in front of me but my ex was visibly shaken,I think I would have approached right under the beams.
