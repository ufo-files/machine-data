UFO low altitude

I was driving home one evening in my neighborhood when i turned on my street and saw an object with an unlinking light moving very low to the ground and changing direction without emitting any sound. The only thing that could fly this low would be a drone. It seemed to angulate and stealthily move. It scared me so much I accelerated away from my house after briefly driving slowly toward it and filming it. I have not released the video until now. I muted the audio because there was so much profanity.
