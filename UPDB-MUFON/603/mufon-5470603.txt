{dr}At least 50 plus objects flying from East to west. The event took over 15 minuets

Saturday, January 27, 2018.
About 5:30 EST, I was in the parking of Shop Rite Supermarket Bensalem Pa. waiting for my wife to finish shopping. I was with my grandson, who is 11 years old. The market is located at Bensalem Blvd & Hulmville Rd. I noticed an object in the eastern sky probably 500 to 10000 ft. It looked to have a red orange glow from th front of the object, much like the afterburner of a jet aircraft. The first was then followed by a cluster of three , same size and color. Then more came into sight from the same direction east to west. This event went on for about 15 minuets I counted a total of 50 plus.repeat all the same size and same direction, not in a straight line but same direction, speed and size and color.
From my vantage point the were all about the size Mars on a clear night.
I called the FAA, two airports and the Mc Guire Airbase in New Jersey.
They had no answers to my inquiry.
There were at least 10 other people that witnessed this event, including my grandson.
