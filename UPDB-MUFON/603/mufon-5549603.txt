innertube shaped

I was Witness 3. I remember it just like yesterday. I am 24 now. I don't talk about it much 'cause who would believe me...But it was high in the sky on a clear day I remember. It appeared to be a black innertube shaped object. It went from one side of the horizon to the other in a couple of seconds. I'd say about 7-10 seconds. We were all together in my driveway. I remember thinking how cool it was and telling mom and dad but they just smiled and laughed. Doubt they believed us. But anywho. Maybe you can tell me if this was a real sighting or a weather balloon. It had no lights or any other visible charactaristics other than moving fast and it being a dark, innertube shaped object. Thanks and have a great day-J. Rios-Huntsville,Al
