Huge circular shape on European weather radar covering almost all of croatia

I was watching European weather radar [link moved/CMS/dr] when suddenly, only for 3 or 4 radar sweeps, from from 4:15 am to 4:45 am CET, a huge circular shape appeared, covering most of entire Croatia, parts of Bosnia and Slovenia. First I thought it was a temporary display of a local weather radar station, but the circular shape was totally different from the other precipitation images of the satellite. The circular shape appeared like kind of overlayed on the weather images. The shape was motionless and appeared in the radar image only during 4 radar sweeps in the time span 4:15 am-4:45 am CET. The easternmost part of the circular shape was not picked up by radar as if these parts of the object were out of reach of radar, like if a huge disk hovered in a slanted position. As the meteox animations cover only the last 3 or 4 hours, when time progressed the circular shape was not shown any more.
As a regular watcher of the weather situation, I remember to have seen a similar circular huge shape on weather radar over Europe, but in a different location. Pls see attached images. Maybe artifacts, or mothership?
