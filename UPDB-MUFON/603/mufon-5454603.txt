Three Orange globes fly over head

At exactly 5:18 am (Had phone in hand) I was working at the Rideau Center shopping mall . When I decided to sneak out to the roof for a smoke . As I opened the door to exit immediately felt a large dose of what felt like static electricity as if a larger lighting storm was in the area. I then noticed Three sphere shaped objects directly flying over head in a "V"pattern at no more then 50 feet . They where approximately 50 feet in diameter & flew by at a low rate of speed coming from the Northwest ending southeast . They all had what appeared to be electricity surrounding them with an outer clear casing that contained the electricity. The sighting lasted a little over two minutes as they flew out of sight .
The thing that causes me the most worry is that their path was such that they flew between Parliament Hill & the USA Embassy on Sussex Dr & to the left of Canada`s National Defense building witch is directly across the street from the main entrance to the mall .
