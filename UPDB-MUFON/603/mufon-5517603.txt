{ck} 14+ orange glowing objects sighted, appeared in the west, headed north then disappeared

We were outside my friends house watching fireworks. We had seen one of these orange lights a few minutes before but dismissed it as a plane but after seeing several more close together heading in the same direction we thought it may be something else. The objects appeared in the western sky and had sort of a fire-like color to them (reddish orange). They had a slight flicker like stars and headed north to where they disappeared. They all vanished after passing the same point in the northern sky. We were pretty shocked at what we were seeing. We tried guessing what they could be but just couldnt decide on any one thing. There was one object in the first sighting (which we thought was the plane) then 3-4 about 5 minutes later. About 5 minutes after that we saw another 9 separated by a few inches in the sky. We hung around a while longer and eventually saw one final one about 20 minutes after the large sighting (heading from the east to the north).
