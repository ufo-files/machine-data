5 lights in a row appeared and vanished

Sitting in the hot tub in the country with my wife and kid. I look up and see 5 glowing lights in a row sweep in from out of nowhere and follow each other. I pointed this out to my wife and kid. This is my third sighting thus far in 2021. It helps to live in the country, we see lots of stars....and UFO's?
