lined grouping of lights

@ about 1030 to 1045pm roughly I went out to smoke a cigarette like any other night. I observed many stars in the sky like normal as our skies are usually clear then a singular light popped into view out of no were. as it moved it came closer but kept a high altitude. Then it shifted and looked like an elongated beam of light which seemed to turn slightly and revealed a streaming peal of lights. It stayed like that in flight heading NE and climbing even more. No sound emitted that I could hear. I called to my partner in the house at the time to check it out with me and she seen the same pearl chain of lights too. Both of us were stunned as it rose into the skies further and disappearing. No clouds were in the direction of the disappearance of the lights. Its like they ascended higher until they were just gone. I'm former military my background is aviation. The length of the chain of lights was longer than anything man made and the speed it moved was also impressive even at its altitude. I know it wasn't the known star link satellite's because at the first site of it the object was too low for a set of satellites to be moving. and Star link no matter how well placed would not have had this precise of a formation to it.
