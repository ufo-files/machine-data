{cak} triangle fast descent

on appx. April of 1990 I was traveling in the car along with my mother , grandmother and 3 cousins . we were coming home from my uncles funeral it was appx. 1 to 3 am on a very dark stretch of road when all of a sudden a VERY BRIGHT light hit us when the light began to fade we all observed what appeared to be a huge dark triangular shaped vessel with many what im guessing windows all with a white light coming from all of them my mother instantly stopped pulled off the road and we all looked up at it we were directly across from it on the other side of the road we could see a solid dark vessel with as previously stated many window like things with light emitting from all we watched it for a few seconds it looked as if it were simply sitting on top of the trees after a while we ( the children ) began to get scared at which point my grandmother stated " Go just go ! " as soon as my mother pulled back onto the road we watched the vessel INSTANTLY SHOOT DOWN ! BUT THERE WAS NO CRASH NO BOOM NO NOTHING NOT UP BUT DOWN !
