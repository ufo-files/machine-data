Seemed to be stationary over Miramar air base .

I was tying down a load of empty pallets on my trailer when I looked up and noticed a chrome object in the sky . I just wrote it off as a Mylar balloon that was floating in the wind, so I observed it for a minute and noticed that it was not moving at all ! It was a pretty clear day with a light band of clouds moving from the South too the North . As I took the pictures the clouds passed in front of the object that did not move, you could see it through the light clouds as it passed in front of it . I looked at the pictures after it happened and couldn’t find it .Year’s later I was deleting photos on my phone and came across the photos of the object and decided to take a look at them again, took me awhile but low and behold there it was ! I’m one of those I have to see it before I believe it...
