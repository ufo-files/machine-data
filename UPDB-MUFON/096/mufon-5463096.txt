{rjl} Observed four twinkling star-like objects and one fireball above South Austin, Texas.

Sixteen-person film crew on location at McKinney Falls State Park in Austin, Texas, observed a horseshoe formation of white, green, and red twinkling objects with hovering orange fireball in the center above the tree line of the park around midnight on January 17, 2019. The fireball eventually vanished and the remaining lights remained hovering - ascending and descending with slow lateral moves - for about five minutes. One crew member reported hearing a slight hum emanating from the objects.
