Captured in a photo

I didn’t notice that I had captured something in one of my pictures until the next day. I was up early that day and decided to go up to Timberline to watch the sun come up. I was a little late but went anyway. I was about 2/3 of the way up Timberline Rd. There is a corner that looks Southeast and has a great view of Mt. Jefferson which is due south by 70 miles. You can also See Broken Top and The north Sister from that corner. I took about 10 pictures all within a few seconds of each other. The next day I noticed that the second picture had something in it. I used an app to zoom in and clean the image. It appeared to have some sort of an air pocket around it like a field or something. This is the original and cleaned up pictures.
