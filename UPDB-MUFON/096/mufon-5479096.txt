{cak} I have been signalling the Orbs with a laser to the point that they now come into my home. They get to the point outside and than they appear inside, while at the same time being outside.

While they are still outside, they will replicate inside with a flash.
They are about the size of a fist to a balloon. They are purple in color.

There have been several times we have seen the orbs emitting lights into
the heavens above them. You can always see a haze around them. Last night
it was like they turned on the major flood lights. We could see an inverted world in the sky, complete with building, roads, waterways and
white lights. It was a major part of the sky. Most of the images were grey
with white lights. I have a digital camera binocular adapter that works
well. I believe they are trying to show us something.
