(GS) Fast moving light just above horizon

On a partly cloudy night, star-sized orange-red light 2 inches above horizon traveled extremely fast in a straight path from the south to the north. No sound accompanied the light during the four seconds it was visible. After the light was gone, an obvious airline jet was seen traveling from west to east at cruising altitude. No similarities between the two types of lights.
