{nd} Small, light blue light moved in a slight arc across sky

My friend Shannon witnessed this event in her room in her house. She looked up at the window, and right as she looked, a light blue star-like dot of light appeared out of nowhere and moved left a short distance in a shallow arc path. Her window faces the street. At first, she thought that it was a shooting star but realized if couldn't be because no other stars were visible, and it was blue. Shannon says she felt somewhat nervous, after the initial suprise went away. The object simply disappeared suddenly as it had appeared. It lasted about one second. No sound was heard.
