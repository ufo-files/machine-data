Two witnesses observed three triangle objects with enormous speed, without sound.

My wife was enjoying evening on the terrace laying on the beach chair, watching the evening sky. At 22:15 (23rd July, 2016) she called me outside to see something urgently. I rushed outside and observed three triangular shaped object flying in formation from SE to NW. In the first moment they looked as military airplanes. But objects were much faster, shape was not of the airplane and lights were not nautical. Objects were in reverse triangle formation (two in front one at the back) with steady yellowish light. No blinking lights, no red or green. The triangular shape of objects them self, could be easily seen against lighter sky. There was no sound. The speed was enormous since objects covered whole sky from SE to NW in less than a minute! Much faster of observed airplanes. The object flew whole sky and disappeared behind city scenery. The phenomenon lasted about 40 seconds. I am astronomer so this was not like any astronomical object. Few meteors were observed after this appearance. There was no time to grab a camera or anything to record event. After event, the area is scanned for airplanes on virtual radar (FlightRadar24 and RadarBox) but there was no object flying in such manner.
