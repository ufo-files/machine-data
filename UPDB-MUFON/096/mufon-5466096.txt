{jpg} We thought it was the space station and it wasn't when I zoomed in with my phone.

My husband and I were on our deck having dinner on September 3 2018 at 8:43 pm. We looked up at the clear sky and saw a planet and a light moving across the sky passing by the planet from the southwest heading to the northeast . It was a solid white light moving at a consistent speed. We assumed it was the space station. I used my telescope phone app and zoomed into the moving object. It was not the space station. We only know that because my husband looked up where the space station was at that moment. NASA reported at 8:43 pm on September 3, 2018 that the space station was on the southern tip of South America. I continued to photograph the fast steady moving object. 

To us, without the camera, it looked like the space station going by because it was a solid white light moving at a consistenly fast rate of speed. In the camera when I zoomed in, the moving object looked totally different. It had tentacles and black bumpers on the edges. And it was changing shapes.

We both were not nervous at all. We thought it was weird looking at clearly not a satellite or aircraft or planet. We have seen weirder stuff fly overhead here.It flew over our deck and over the building we live in.
