4 discs

At approximately 4:30 PM 11/20/2021 I heard anoise, that was different than the passengerplanes in the area, I was walking my brothers dog. At the moment I heard the noise, I looked up, and saw 4 disc's, more like squished eggs, flying at about 5-7,000 ft, directly over my head. I focused on them, saw it was metallic, with only a darker grey panel of what I think was a vent. Upon focusing, they went completely out of sight, and over the horizon in 5-7 seconds. My math has jets at 500mph, taking about 1.5 minutes to go from horizon to horizon. That'd make this 3,000mph and it was accelerating.
