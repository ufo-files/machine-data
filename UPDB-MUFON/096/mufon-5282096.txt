Red Object with vague shape underneath light came towards us

On 4th of July, 2019 spouse and I had guests over for the holiday. The 4 of us were sitting in the back yard watching fireworks to the west in Clarkston, WA. for some reason, I turned and looked to the NE. I saw a red object appear in the sky. It was slowly coming toward us in our small back yard. I said to the others, "Hey, look at the helicopter", It clearly wasn't a helicopter but I figured it would get everyone to look. If it was actually a helicopter, then I wouldn't have to endure ridicule for weeks, as I have in the past. 
Our guest, his child, my spouse and I watched the red object, which appeared as if it was composed of multiple orbs. It came towards us, with no sound, heading towards Clarkston. Suddenly, as we were all watching, it just disappeared in the sky. 
I was grateful for all of the witnesses. 
The next week, I was chatting with a neighbor across the street. Their family had actually seen it on the same night, July 4th. Interestingly, their sighting was in approximately the same place, hovering over [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/cms/LLL]. They (man, wife and adult son) said that they saw it earlier than us and it was a red spinning orb. So, in all, 7 people witnessed this object on the same night.
