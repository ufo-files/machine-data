{cak} Rhombus-Shaped UFO

This UFO noticed me from the distance and well... I got a better look. It was very awkward when we were facing each other and the craft kept approaching, I just didn't knew what to expect. It emitted a noise similar to a jet engine, the craft was very dark in contrast to the night sky and had one pulsing red light on front, to the left side a white light and to the right side a golden light both unwavering, later I saw its back light and saw the rhombus or diamond shape figure. It came peacefully overhead me to the side at medium speed, below cloud level altitude. Overall, I am grateful!
