Bright Flashing Light

I first observed the unknown light on the 18th of March 2021 around sunrise. The lights were extremely bright and in pairs, as bright as sun light. I wrote them off as being celestial oobjects. The lights were to the West of Plymouth City Centre

On the 19th of March around 7.30pm, I set up my digital camera to take photos of the moon and noticed the same lights in the same direction, West. The footage is recorded on a 75-300mm lens and I took care to avoid the footage having too much grain. 

Even at night, the light was considerably brighter than street lights and emitted more light than the moon or any other stars. This is including the light pollution in the city centre. I have cross checked Starlink trackers to confirm that what I saw was not a man made satellite.

Due to the distance, it is difficult to estimate the size and speed of the object.
