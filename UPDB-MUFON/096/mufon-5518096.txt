Orange object moving silent across sky

Went out to get the mail last night around 9:00pm, noticed it was a nice clear night, paused on driveway to look at the stars, noticed big dipper directly overhead. My eye caught an object through the treeline across the street. We live about 6 miles SW of Flying Cloud Airport so I figured it was a small plane. As it broke the treeline I noticed it was orange in color, couldnt really make out a shape but about the size of a small plane at 500ft or so but there were no red or green or flashing navigation lights, just an orange glow, it was traveling in a West to East direction. Didnt hear any sound other than nightime crickets, it continued on a straight path about the speed a small plane would make just after takeoff. As it was further away it dimmed about 50% then returned to original brightness. It did this about three times then it dimmed out completely and I lost sight of it. No clouds in sky, very clear night. Thought it was kind of a strange.
