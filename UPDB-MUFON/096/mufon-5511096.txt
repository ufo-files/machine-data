{cak} saw wedge-shaped translucent object in sky travelling from S to N over SW Austin

I was lounging in my hot tub around 10:00 PM, looking up at the night sky as I often do, after an evening run. I noticed movement from the south in the sky, I was facing west in the tub. I looked and quickly saw what at first I thought was a high-power search light tracing along the bottom of the clouds. But almost right away I noticed that there WERE no clouds in the sky at that time. This of course startled and interested me so I sprang up and watched as this thing pass directly overhead, Im guessing at about 2,000 ft, traveling exactly from the south to the north. Very deliberate motion, no wavering. As I watched it go by I noticed it was very clearly wedge-shaped and translucent-white, like the color of a cloud almost. But again, no clouds. To describe the shape, it was very much shaped like an Army Private chevron arm stripe; angular and crisp. Almost exactly the same angle and width proportions of a chevron stripe in fact. It made no sound that I could hear (I had one low-power hot tub jet on, I couldnt turn it off since the tub was in filter mode, dang it). I didn't notice any lights on it, just a very slight glow to the whole shape. I waited and watched for another 10 minutes for a search light to cross the sky, but none did. A few wispy clouds drifted by while I was waiting - if there was a search light it would have lit those up. No video or pics unfortunately.
