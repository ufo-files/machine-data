White lightbulb shaped object

About May or June of 2008 up to about 2010. (can't remember exact date) , while driving northeast on route 42 Churchville, Augusta County, Virginia, I saw a solid white lightbulb shaped object about 50-75 feet in the air. Moving at steady level and stable in a Northeast direction but faster than my car (50 mph). I was about 150 feet from object. Size a bit larger than a pick-up truck. Dull, not shiny, smooth white surface, no openings. Surface finish like white enamel on a refrigerator. Thought it was a weather balloon. It looked metallic or solid, not fabric or soft material. But no visual means of propulsion or vents or openings anywhere. Appearance of a separation line at base of "bulb" between lower portion and top. Just like a giant white lightbulb. No "screw" lines at bottom portion. Duration time of observation about 2 minutes until my driving direction changed at curve in road and I lost sight. Never reported.
