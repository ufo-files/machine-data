{rjl} Globes hovering at end of street then disappeared all at once.

This is my neighbor's sighting, and she wants to remain anonymous. She was sitting outside on her porch (we live two houses apart) and noticed many whitish objects in the sky over the west end of our street, about half a block away. She took a photo and watched them for a minute or so. They seemed to be moving all together, and then suddenly they vanished. I did not witness the event but I asked her to send me the photo so I could submit it. My first impression was a balloon release, but I thought it was worth checking on when she told me they all disappeared at the same time. Full disclosure, I was briefly a field investigator many years ago after passing the course and test, but then I took a fairly high-profile position and didn't pursue investigations. And I can't answer the questions you've listed because I didn't witness the event personally, but I can tell you that my neighbor was questioning whether she was really seeing what she saw. She's still shaken. I would take her as a reliable witness.
