Orange starlike object

Orange starlike object about 15 degrees azimuth and 80 degrees east, appearing around the same magnitude as Saiph. The sky was clear. Noted the object changed position rapidly by about 5 degrees north and then 5 degrees south several times. It then rose more slowly (normal aircraft speed) toward the south in a stairstep pattern, gaining approximately 10 to 20 degrees of altitude. It then disappeared. 
The Geminids and Cancrids were in the eastern sky. This was not a meteor.
The object, or one similar, reappeared at the same general position where it was seen originally and repeated behavior similar to the above, though not exactly. This happened several times. Observation was discontinued after about ten minutes due to weather.
