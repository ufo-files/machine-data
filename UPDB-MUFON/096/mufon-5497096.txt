{dr}LOOKED EXACTLY LIKE PHOTOS OF THE TR-3B

I WAS STANDING ON MY FRONT PORCH FACING BASICALLY WEST. THERE WAS STILL A LIGHT GLOW OF SUNLIGHT ON THE HORIZON BUT THE RED GLOW OF SUNSET HAD PASSED. I WAS JUST LOOKING AT STARS AND GOING OVER RECENT PERSONAL EVENTS IN MY MIND WHEN I SAW THREE POINTS OF WHITE LIGHT SHAPED IN WHAT SEEMED LIKE A TRIANGLE, THOU NOT A PERFECT TRIANGLE. THEY CAUGHT MY EYE BECAUSE IT SEEMED TO ME MOMENTS EARLIER THE LIGHTS WERE NOT THERE. THAT MADE ME FOCUS ON THEM AND EVEN THOU THEY WERE APPROXIMATELY THE SAME INTENSITY AS AN AVERAGE STAR I NEVER FELT THEY WERE STARS. I GOT THE DISTINCT IMPRESSION THEY WERE HOVERING. AT THIS TIME I WAS LOOKING SLIGHTLY LESS THEN NORTHWEST. I WATCHED THEM 'HOVER' FOR APPROXIMATELY 3 TO 4 MINUTES. THE LIGHTS THEN STARTED MOVING FROM MY RIGHT TO LEFT IN A SOUTHEASTERLY DIRECTION. AT NO TIME DID I SEE ANY DIRECTION CHANGES. WHEN 'HOVERING',THE LIGHTS APPEARED TO BE A MILE OR MORE AWAY. WHEN CROSSING IN FRONT OF ME THEY APPEARED TO BE LESS THEN A MILE. AS THE OBJECT GOT CLOSER THE TRIANGLE SHAPE BECAME MORE PROMINENT. IT WAS AT THIS TIME I COULD CLEARLY SEE AN OUTLINE OF A DARK TRIANGLE SHAPED OBJECT WITH A LIGHT NEAR EACH TIP. IT WAS ALSO NOW THAT AN ORANGE/RED LIGHT IN THE MIDDLE BECAME CLEAR. COMPARED TO HOUSES AROUND ME THE OBJECT HAD TO BE OVER 100 FT ON EACH SIDE. AT NO TIME DID I HEAR ANY SOUND OR HAVE ANY SENSATIONS. I TRIED TO TAKE VIDEO OF THE OBJECT BUT I COULD NOT GET MY PHONE TO FOCUS FOR WHATEVER REASON. THE SPEED OF THE OBJECT DID NOT SEEM TO BE VERY FAST COMPARED TO THE OCASSIONAL F-16 THAT FLIES OVER. HOWEVER AS THE OBJECT GOT PAST DUE SOUTH IT ACCLERATED STEADILY AND WAS OUT OF SIGHT IN SECONDS. FROM HOVER TO DUE SOUTH ON THE COMPASS WAS APPROXIMATELY ONE MINUTE. FRON DUE SOUTH ON THE COMPASS TO OUT OF VISUAL WAS LESS THEN TEN SECONDS.
