Fleet siting followed by aircraft

I went outside to my back porch to have a cigarette and stair up at the sky as I normally do. From the North-west i saw a light appear that looked like a star moving east. At first I thought "ok, could be a satalite", until another followed shortly behind it. And then another one, and another one, and another one, and so on - i didn't keep count but it seemed like there were alot. They all went for quite a distance, and then disappeared, looking to go upward into space. Then I see a plane 15min later, high altitude, lights red, blue, going in the same direction. After that I didn't see anymore.
