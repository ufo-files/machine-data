Metallic Saucer-shaped object seen in Northeast Te

Sulphur Springs Texas, driving in car with mom due west to evening grocery storedowntown. as approached destination, noticed craft not too much higher than thewater tower or the police communication tower. it seemed to fly toward thecommunication tower and hover and then slowly begin to head east again until itleft our sight. we were excited and in disbelief but really saw a saucer likeobject silently flying within easy visual range. We tried to follow it in itseastern path but it eventually went out of our sight going much faster than wecould on the city street. we first noticed it because it came so low down thatit was almost unavoidable visually. It appeared to approach the policecommunication tower and stop and hover before going back eastward.
