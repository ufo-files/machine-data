Metallic object hovering over Berlin, Germany

I was walking my dog and I noticed a plane in the sky and caught my attention because it was so extremely clear. I could see the plane in detail. It was a big one and really high. As I was watching the plane another object caught my attention. At first I thought it was a fvery bright and big star, too bright and too big for this sunny summer day at 14pm so I stared ad it a noticed very soon that it was a metallic object that sometimes disappeared for about 2 seconds and appeared again. I took some photos and videos with my phone but this object was so far away that my motorola didn't really take good images.
