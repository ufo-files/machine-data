Object rose from water, approached changing form, I blacked out, recovered in time to take these two shots.

I was driving out on an isolated narrow peninsula alone checking out the beaches. I had stopped and was looking out over the water from atop some sand dunes. I recall something like a waterspout that rose up out of the sea into a more fixed object, kind of elongating into a horizontal cylinder, still appearing with water qualities. As it darkened into a more solid appearance I remember a rush of fear and excitement, a real phenomenon and I recall it rushed towards me, or getting much bigger at least, like filling my vision. I have a sense of looking into the wide opening of a waterspout or whirlpool, opened towards me. I blacked out. Foremost in my mind was the need to capture this on my phone camera. I remember intense fear. I next remember thinking about it later and the conviction that I had really seen something and of course doubted myself. I was just going through some recovered files off an old wiped hard drive when I found these two photos that must have been as the object was leaving. I don't recall taking them, more that I wished I had.
