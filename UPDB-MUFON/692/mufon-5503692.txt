Ok, so I open the door to let dog out and there were four red lights floating over the house across the street. At first I think they are flares then they start moving very slowly toward my house. They fly off making no sound. I turn and see another one coming toward me and then another and another and another. There were 8 in all. I grabbed my phone and took pics but of course they didn't come out very well and I don't know how to get the pics from my regular old phone onto fb. At least I thought to call my mom's house and get them up to see them so I know I am not loosing my mind. It was between 8:30 and 8:45 pm. They had a red light on top and white light on the bottom. Kind of shaped like a football. The objects disapeared into a cloud. I was very excited and nervous, I wanted people to see it with me and I was alone. I went to call my family and fumbled with my phone so much that they only got to see the last of them as they went off into the clouds. My dad commented that they were making no noise. He said something that low you should be able to hear something. The first four stayed in the shape of a square as it flew off and the others all stayed in a straight line. Some of my neighbors also reported that they saw the objects as well.
