{rjl} Orange Rotating Orbs Observing 4th of July Celebration

On July 4, 2015 I went outside to my back yard to observe fireworks celebrations going on around city. I began to film the fire works explosions. Suddenly, I caught a orange glow to my left and so began to film one of 3 orbs I saw this night. They never went down and they made no sound. 

I am including a digital video of the event. It is copied from my original iPhone I no longer own.
