{kt}Bright glowing white object, changed direction

I was on the back porch of my cottage on Lake Huron watching early Persied meteor shower at about 11:40 pm, on 8/10/13. I saw a few satellites pass by also. Then noticed what appeared to be a satellite except much brighter (Like a star is bright)heading from North, to South, toward my location for about 8 seconds. I started watching it at about 65 degrees above me. At about 87 degrees above me, it hooked (close turn)an immediate left turn, and headed east over Lake Huron toward Canada. About three seconds after it made the turn the brightness totally disappeared. It blinked right out. I have five telescopes, and am an amateur astronomer. I sky watch all the time. It had a brightness almost of Venus, thats what caught my attention. So I knew it couldnt be a satellite. Then came the abrupt left turn to head East. Unless their was a collision up there, it could not have changed direction, and proceeded as though it was an intended maneuver. After it headed East, about four seconds later the light blinked right out. I might add it was moving faster than a satellites steady pace.
