{rjl} A gathering of Objects

Sighting is an approximate time back in 2007, in late Fall/Early winter in S. Carolina. It was about 45 degrees outside and clear skies.
I smoke outside the home, so I was standing in my driveway having a smoke and looking at the stars. Occasionally one can see a satellite moving by. Not the satellite itself, but the reflections off of the panels by sun/moonlight. I noticed what I thought was a satellite above, moving from South towards the North. I watched it for a few minutes as it proceeded on a slow straight line path. As I looked around to the north, I saw another object moving to the South. As they neared each other, I then noticed other objects moving towards this same point. there were 5 all together, that came in from opposing points and met in one place in the clear night sky. Their convergence speeds were all the same, about the speed of a commercial airliner at that height, and came to immediate stops when they neared the proximity of the others. Their white flashes were quite random, even as they hovered in the meeting area (from 3 to 12 second intervals). They hovered in what appeared to be a circle for a few minutes. I could not ascertain the height, but my guess would be about 5k to 10k feet. After this time, all the lights started getting smaller and dimmer as they all ascended upward all at one synchronized time. After a matter of seconds, they were not visually identifiable.The approximate location of this gathering was right above Charleston Air Force Base.
