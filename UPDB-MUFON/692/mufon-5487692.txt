IN THE SUMMER OF 1945, MY FATHER AND I SAW 7 LARGE WHITE DISC FLYING TOGETHER OVER OUR FARM. THE CRAFT SHOT STRAIGHT UP AND LEFT. WHEN MY FATHER CHECKED THE CATTLE HE FOUND ONE DEAD ONE. IT HAD BEEN BURNED, LIKE WITH A CURLING IRON. MY FATHER CALLED THE FEDS TO REPORT THE DEATH OF THE COW BECAUSE OF RATIONING. WE WERE NOT ALLOWED TO KILL OUR OWN COWS. THEY CAME AND TOOK THE COW AWAY.WITNESS WAS 15 YEARS OLD AT THE TIME. WITNESS HAS HAD OTHER EXPERIENCES SINCE THEN. HE RECENTLY FELL AND HIT HIS HEAD AND DOCTORS TOLD HIM HE HAD A PIECE OF METAL IN HIS HEAD NEAR HIS SPINE. HE HAS NO IDEA HOW THE METAL GOT THERE.
