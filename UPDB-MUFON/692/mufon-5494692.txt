{cak} Occurred during Military Aircraft Activity

I was in a restricted area of Holloman AFB watching with several others, watching a F-89 launch an air-to-air heat-seeking missile at an F-80 drone aircraft. As these aircraft approached from south-to-north, We all saw a disk shaped object hovering above the aircraft. The F089 fired the missile and we watched it hit the F-89 drone and shot it down. Then we all looked back at the UFO just as it departed at terrific speed in a curving direction to the north-east (in direction of Roswell). It disappeared behind a mountain range east of Alamogordo.
