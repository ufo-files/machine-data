{cak} Two objects with accompanying lights

Per this article [Link moved to FI Section/cms/tg]. I saw the same thing in later October or early November of this past year. Only I saw two of them headed due West at sundown. The first one had three "Foo Fighters" accompanying it. The second one had two accompanying it. all the "foo fighters" blinked off like light bulbs. I watched them as long as I could until they went behind trees. They appeared to be several thousand feet in the air with the contrails appearing to be like boat wakes.I sent the information to Coast to Coast AM, but never heard anything back. I was in my front yard. it faces West, watering plants.I first noticed the "contrails' as it was a perfectly clear sky, they stood out well. I knew that it was not the way the emissions appeared as I have seen contrails for 65 years and have never seen any like these. It appeared to be a solid object, I was unable to see a shapes because of the reflection of the setting sun. The accompanying lights were just orbs of very bright white light that blinked not faded, off. All never deviated from the due West direction. I felt they were more than likely government x planes until I saw the orbs. I just kept watching them until they flew behind trees in the distance and I could no longer see them.
