Reddish-orange light passing over SE Portland night of May 20.

I was out in front of my house taking out the trash. I decided to look up where I was watching the solar eclipse the evening of May 20, 2012 in Portland, OR. I looked up at 9:30pm, so completely dark/night, and saw this bright red light in the sky. It definitely was not an airplane. It was silent, moved slowly and mostly in a linear direction, but not always. I thought I was hallucinating at first because I had been looking at the sun, but that was several hours ago and so I checked my vision with many different objects and could see just fine and clearly. The lights changed in brightness, sometimes dimming a little. The light was mostly red, but had an orange tint to it. Maybe there were more than one light. If so, it was really close to the others making it look like one big light to me. I couldnt believe what I was seeing, but nobody else was around to view it with me. I lost sight of the object when it went behind this large pine tree and behind my roof. It seemed to get smaller and fainter as if it were moving away.
