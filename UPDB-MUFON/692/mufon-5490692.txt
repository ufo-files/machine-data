possible abduction

The event took place in Munster, Germany in the early 80s.
My Father was in the British army and my mother and we were stationed and living in married quarters in Peter Buscher In Munster. We lived in a first floor flat and I must have been about 5 or 6 years of age at the time.
I only have two vivd recollections of the event.
1) I remember waking up during possible late night early morning for no reason I can identify. The door to my bed room was slightly open and the light was on in the hall outside of my room (as usual) and I have a memory of a hand coming round and gripping the door frame closest to me. The fingers were very long and there didn't seem to be enough of them. I remember suprise, fear then a blank.
2) I become aware of myself again and I am up near the ceiling of my bedroom. I have no idea how much time has passed. I sense that I am being held there. I realise this is VERY wrong in all respects and I fight, and fight as hard as I can to get free from what ever is holding me. The "something" lets go and I fall back to the bed and again all goes blank.

I have no idea if what I experienced was a dream or something else entirely.
