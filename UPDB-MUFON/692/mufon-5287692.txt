4 LIGHTS IN A ROW

A FRIEND AND I WENT TO HIS VACTION PROPERTY IN NORTH EASTERN ARIZONA. ON SATURDAY NIGHT WE WERE GRILLING DINNER ON THE PORCH AND I LOOKED AND SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A SINGLE SATTELITE AND STOOD TO POINT IT OUT TO MY FRIEND. I WAS FOCUSED IN CLOSELY TO THE MOVING LIGHT WHILE HE WAS SCANNING THE SKY TO CATCH UP. HE THEN SAID THAT FOUR LIGHTS WERE MOVING, I ADJUSTED MY FOCUS TO THE WHOLE SKY AND INSTANTLY SAW THE THREE TRAILING LIGHTS; SEPERATE, BUT EQUAL DISTANT FROM EACHOTHER, TAILING BEHIND THE OTHER (FIRST OBSERVED) LIGHT. WHILE LOOKING AT THIS EVENT WE NOTICED THE SECOND LIGHT SEEMED TO SPEED UP TO THE FIRST ONE THEN THE OTHER TWO SEEMED TO "CATCH UP" TO EACHOTHER AS THEY STARTED TO FADE FROM VIEW HEADING A LITTLE MORE SOUTH THAN FIRST SIGHTED. I THOUGHT OF ELON MUSKS STARLINK PROGRAM BUT MY UNDERSTANDING IS THERE ARE MORE THAN 4 IN A ROW.
GOD BLESS!
