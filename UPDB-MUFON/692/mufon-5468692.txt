Flashing orb seen heading North, Seemed to come from the Gulf

I was working under my son's truck and when I slid out from under it this flashing light caught my eye. My first thought was that it might be a balloon. All of my family has been in our military so I have spent all of my life living near some sort of active airport watching all types of known aircraft. After watching this for a minute the size and distance did not look right for this to be a balloon. As I watched it seemed to be both flashing light it was producing and reflecting the sun. I was bewildered by it enough that it took me a few minutes of watching it before I thought about trying to record it with my phone. At that point it was almost behind the tree line and my phone did not truely do it justice. As I already said, seeing/watching aircraft is something I am very familar with. At the distance this object appeared to be if it were just a balloon it would have been at least 30 feet across, but it was moving like a child had just lost it. That coupled with the flashing red and white light really left me wondering what it was. Two additions to my statement: 1st my current home is in the flight path of both the local international airport and one used by an Air National Guard unit. 2nd the copy of the video that i uploaded to my Google drive will no longer play. I have played it a few times for family and twice this morning before deciding to share this. It's just a really weird coencidence, but makes me gald it was not my only copy.
