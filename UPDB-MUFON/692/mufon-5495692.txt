{dr}Glowing unknown objects

On March 5, 2015 at 4:45am PST, wife and myself where driving into work. As soon as we left the home, wife noticed a strange orange/yellow orb far in the distance. I turned and looked and commented that it may be a helicopter or a star, but to my surprise, the object dropped another object and then it became two orbs hovering. I asked wife to record using the mobile phone. After few seconds, that orb move back towards the primary orb and appeared to have been absorbed making it a single orb again. One that happened, the orb hovered up slightly and went dark for about 30 seconds or so, then it reappeared and glowing brighter. After few seconds once again, it appeared to drop something that was glowing and appeared to reflect a slight streak of light downwards towards ground. a few seconds after that, the primary orb release a second orb once again, which hovered a little distance away from primary, of which then was quickly absorbed back by primary orb and went dark, loosing sight. We have a video of which unfortunately is not the best and does not serve justice to support what we truly saw.
