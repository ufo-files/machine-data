{rjl} Red Firy Orb

June 24, 2017               Time:approximately, 20:13

 On the above date and time of occurrence, this writer and another witness reported while watching a fire works display from their front yard, they witnessed a fiery, red orb hovering in the south east sky. Witness believed at first, it was the orange K giant celestial event reported to occur on a local radio show. This red fiery, orb appeared to hover while watching the fire works display. When the show was almost over the orb, began to move erratically. It began to shoot upward and then abruptly stopped. It then appeared to float toward the ground like a leaf falling off the tree. The above writer attempted to record the event via cell phone but was unsuccessful. The orb then appeared to fly a above the tree line and then witnesses then lost sight of the orb behind houses and trees.
Writer cleared without further incident.
