Unknown white object in sky hovering and moving slowly.

This video taken in Oro Valley, Arizona on August 28th, 2021. A summer monsoon thunderstorm was over a nearby mountain range. The cloud ceiling was around 9,500' ASL. Came to my attention because we had hundreds of mouths or small butterflies come into the area. They were everywhere. I looked up and this object caught my eye. No sound that I could hear. It appeared white and I could tell it was moving. At first I thought it was a drone, but as I looked at it more, it went higher and higher and I could not determine what it was. I tried to keep it in view but as it got closer to the cloud ceiling I lost it in the glare. Never saw it emerge or come down. It appears to have vanished into the clouds, which would put the object at least over 9,000' ASL.

About ten minutes later, I saw another unknown object come flying low right where the other object had been. It slowed in this area, then continued low going north and then gained altitude and in less than 10 seconds I saw it hovering to the north. Unknown what this was. Moved to fast to be a drone and traveled more than a mile at high speed. No sounds heard. This object just vanished too. This object was orange and gray in color.
