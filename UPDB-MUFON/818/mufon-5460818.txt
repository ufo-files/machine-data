I was camping on public property on the date listed above for one night. I felt a pull effect because I knew I was going to encounter something supernatural. I got to the top of Moody Park at 6:30PM Eastern. I set up camp and lit my campfire around 7:30APM. I was sitting by my fire enjoying the warmth in the cold winter air and around 9:00PM I felt a telepathic connection with something out of this world. I thought about my campfire in such a way that I was able to manipulate the flames into a vortex of spinning rods and funnels of fire. I could raise and lower this vortex with my mind. I saw something bright to my left and I saw at about 70 feet away from where I was camping I saw two orbs about the size of basket balls hovering about 3 feet above the ground moving in a straight line towards the tree line. These orbs where one red and one blue. After getting ready for bed I let my campfire die down to coals and went to bed in my tent. I woke up at about 7:30AM the following morning with my tent collapsed on top of me. As I did not secure my tent to the ice I was sleeping on top of during the night. My tent slid all the way over where I saw these two orbs. I felt shaken to wake up that way and went home fine, but tired and sore.
