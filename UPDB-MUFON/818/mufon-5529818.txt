3 red orbs seen travelling very fast in parallel lines, then 2 more behind them dissapearing on the horizon

Fireworks were being lit, but sighting occured during very brief pause in display. 3 red orbs appeared just over tree line, cruising in unison, perfectly parallel together & very fast and disappeared on the horizon. After that, and a split second behind them, 2 more appeared over the trees and disappeared on the horizon. I could not see inside orbs, they strobed darker red to lighter red. They made only a slight swoosh when they moved thru air over our heads and disappeared over the horizon. They travelled very fast and did not slow as they passed over us. Sighting lasted 3 to 5 sec. possibly. I heard an older woman say those were'nt part of the fireworks. I knew i saw a unidentifiable flying object immediately, I believe they were trying to blend in with all the fireworks, but they were clearly very High tech and did not behave like fireworks would have! I asked others if they saw what i just saw. Others there saw it also, but maybe did not have as clear of a view as i did, or they maybe could not process what they had seen. Was seen by my wife and daughter and a few others there to celebrate the 4th. I have searched internet to see if such a thing was seen by others that evening or pictures, no info though.
