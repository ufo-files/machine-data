Came up though the trees then changed direction

My wife and I were outside sitting on the step of the deck facing west complaining about our family. When my wife noticed I large organe light coming up from the trees to the north west. The light was about the size of a marble in your stretched out arm when it was coming up through threes but was pretty far away. As it got higher it seemed to get smaller to the size of a pin head or that's how fast it was moving. The light turned white and made a 90 degree turn and started going east. It traveled in a straight line until it seemed to speed up then it vanished. We're not sure if it vanished because of it leaving our sight or it shut off. This all happened in a couple of minutes at the most
