{cak} hundreds  starlike dots flew intricate militaristic patterns splitting into quadrants flying varied flight patterns horizon to horizon perfectly synchronized then in one formation zoomed away

my wife and i stayed at a campground in rio grande, nj for the summer in a tent and worked on the boardwalk in the wildwoods. we had gotten home after work and were hanging out in the campground's open field at about 1:00 am. i was the first to see the objects flying patterns in the sky and after watching them for a brief time i asked my wife to describe what she saw and was certain that she was seeing the exact happenings that i was. hundreds of starlike objects in one formation flew intricate military like flight patterns from horizon to horizon, they flew in perfect synchronization changing direction and speed. after a few minutes of us both describing to each other what we were watching the objects separated into multiple groups and flew different patterns in quadrants each group was still in perfect synch with their group. then they reunited into one formation and zoomed away beyond our visibility. at first i thought it could be a laser light show since it was the jersey shore 4th of july week. but after realizing how clear the sky was and the intricacy of the maneuvers they flew we knew that what were witnessing was awesomely beyond anything we had ever seen and would be impossible for the technology of 1975.
