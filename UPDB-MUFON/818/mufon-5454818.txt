My daughter and I live in a small apartment in the bright city. Over the last year or so we've noticed what we thought at first was a star or planet and looked at it and was amazed at how bright it was in the city even when it's still a little light outside. One night we saw 3 in a triangle shape then saw the bottom right one go straight up to the top brighter one, that's when I started taking pics. More recently, mostly Jan. and Feb. 2020, we have been seeing this in the Western sky. I went back to the July 2019 pic I took and zoomed in and saw also a hollow center. We still see this a lot and believe we have been abducted, but have no memory of this. This has affected dreams and even strange occurances in the apartment similar to ghost activity. My daughter has an Rh negative blood type, I don't know mine, but that seems relative.
