{nd} Shaped like a cylinder emitted blue red white lights with red ring aura hovered with periodic ascending and descending

I was up late studying for school and walked outside to fill the cats water bowl before bed. I looked out into the distance and saw this cylinder like shaped object hovering in the sky. At first I thought it was a planet because of the size being larger than a typical star. However, after further observation I noticed the object began to sway back and forth then stopped and periodically ascended and descended in a fast motion. I have never seen any plane or helicopter move the way this object had. Wondering if any one else saw the same thing. I didn't stay up for more than 1 to 1 1/2 hours after initial sighting to see it disappear. I had continuous sight of the object up until the point when i went to bed around 4:30 or 5:00am. I tried to record the sighting on my phone and it would not pick up on the camera.
