Bright lights, shape shifting invite in the sky for 3.5 hours.

At around 3 am on September 20th, 2020 myself and two other witnesses saw two bright shooting stars one after the other, then moments hate we noticed a bright light just over the tree line. As we looked at it we noticed it was moving and was not a star. We watched the object in the sky for 3 1/2 hours. During this time we watched it change shapes, change colors, change altitude, grow in brightness. At one point, we turned on a laser machine and aimed it at the object. At which point, the object stopped moving and we could visibly see the object become brighter, almost fixated on our lights. As the sun started to rise, the object began rising in altitude and turned away and went out, up, and away from me until it was too far away to see.
