{kt}Shiny star-like object above and among the clouds, descended below tree line or moved out of seeing distance

My family and I were leaving the grocery store when I noticed a starlike object to the west looking above and beyond the grocery store. Object was very high in the sky, appeared to be above and among the clouds. Object appeared to either be descending slowly or moving away from us in a fairly straight line. Object was seen in sky for only 2 mins (roughly) before either fading out of sight because of distance or dropping behind the building/tree line.
