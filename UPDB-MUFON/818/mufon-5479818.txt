{nd} Ustream ISS sighting

I love to watch the Ustream International Space Station channel because it has a wonderful view and it is exciting to thing we are seeing space in real time. Then I noticed a dot that was keeping pace with the space station. I thought maybe it might just be a light from the camera or maybe some kind of measuring laser instrument or something. But then I noticed that the dot went under the clouds so I decided to record it. I didn't think that a man made aircraft would be able to keep pace with the International Space Station while traveling through the atmosphere.

I had just learned how to use Snagit and used that to screenshot record. I hadn't figured out the audio yet and I was talking, which is what the awkward whispering sound is in the background. Mainly I was saying, "What is that?" "It is going under the clouds" and things of that nature.

I thought of submitting it right away but decided to do a bit of research and see if anyone else had noticed it. A few people mentioned it while watching but nobody seemed to know what it was.

It is very tiny, but I pointed it out with my cursor. I just wanted to know if I was seeing things or if this is really something I should wonder about.

It was going for quite some time until finally the screen went blue.

The file I uploaded was downloaded on May 29, 2016 but I recorded it on April 25, 2016. The Snagit program records to Google Drive. The recording was taken in the evening, most likely around 7pm.

I would like to remain anonymous because I am an English teacher and I do not want to be vilified for my nightly star and planet gazing.

Thank you for your consideration.
