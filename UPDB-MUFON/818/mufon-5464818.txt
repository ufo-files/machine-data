{tjd} Witnessed 5 horizontal lights equally spaced apart hovering at dusk. Then sudden disappearance!

I was playing professional baseball in the Seattle Mariners organization, for the Lancastor Jethawks, in Lancaster, California. I was sitting in the bleachers right behind home plate when I noticed something strange in the distance beyond the centerfield wall. There were 5 blue-white lights, equally spaced apart, that seemed to appear out of nowhere. They weren1t approaching nor retreating. There was no sound or movements. I pointed it out to a teammate sitting next to me and we were both intrigued. Neither of us had an explanation for what we were seeing. We were aware of an air force base near our location in Lancaster, Ca. The stadium lights had been turned on an hour or so earlier but there was still enough daylight to see the 5 lights. As night grew darker, The stadium lights were too bright to see the lights from the UFO, leading me to believe it was a good distance from the stadium. My first thought was that they were 5 helicopters flying in formation. Yet they weren1t moving in any direction. It lasted for about five minutes before the stadium lighting took over the night. I did not see the lights leave erratically, or at all for that matter. Lancaster California is a small city in the middle of the desert and I know for a fact that these were not city lights or reflections from a city building. There were no buildings in the city that would have been that tall. My feelings led me to believe more than ever that night that WE ARE NOT ALONE!
