I was at home in my backyard.

I had just gotten home and I was talking with my sister. When we were talking,something caught my attention. It was a very bright blinding light. My sisterasked me, What are you looking at? I told her to look up in the sky. She saidthat it looks like the sun. I told her that it wasnt. The reason that I saidthat is because it was smaller than the sun but it was big. It was about halfthe size of the sun at a distance. Then, the spacecraft landed approximately 15feet away from us. To tell you the truth, I did actually think that it was aflying object. When I first saw it, I was very excited because it was new to meand I like things that are new. The object was saucer like, it was hooveringback and forth. Then it regained control and landed. I lost sight of it after ittook off.
