Viewed a light hovering and then moving quickly in multidirections

I live in a rural part of the west of Ireland with no light polution. I, with another two persons, observed a strange light in the sky where we would not generally see a light. We assumed it was some form of helicopter or drone. It was very much in the distant. As we are rural and in a flight path to knock airport we are very used to viewing planes, helicopters etc and this was very unusual in its brightness, hovering and sudden movements. This was at approximately 9.45pm. We ignored and went about our business. At 1.45am approximately I went outside and observed that the object was still in the air but much much closer. I again thought it was maybe a drone but I could very clearly see its movements which went from hovering to very sudden up and down movements and then moving very quickly from side to side. I proceeded to take a video of its movement. The first video was without zoom and shows its sudden movements. The second video I zoomed in on the object and could not find any reasonable explanation as to what this could possibly be.
