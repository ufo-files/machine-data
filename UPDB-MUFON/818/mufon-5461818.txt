{rjl} Bright Shiny Object Hovering Over Prescott Valley

Driving from Prescott to Prescott Valley on Hwy 89A, I noticed shiny object hovering over Prescott Valley (PV). Seemed motionless at first. Cylinder center, flat on top and bottom. Reminded me of a bullet after it's been fired. Stubby shape, no sharp edges. Reflective body, silver-like. It was clear skies with a few puffy clouds. It hovered beneath the clouds in one spot the entire time until I reached Glassford Hill Road, turned off and finally stopped at a light at Sante Fe Loop Road, where I snapped the pic. At the time I stopped, the object moved straight up and created a tail below. It wasn't moving very fast.The light changed to green and I drove home.
