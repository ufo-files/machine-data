Rainbow Phoenix

On 6/15/21 I was traveling west to get on the 15 Hwy above Casa Grande Ave. I notice a large rainbow colored wide wing bird which resembled an Eagle which I called a Phoenix. I was so impressed that I pulled over on the freeway a took pictures. Today, 6/28/21 I was reviewing my pictures and noticed in the upper right of picture is a white object. I enlarged the picture and saw the object is an ufo and in the middle is a heart shape. 
I answered but not all applied because the Phoenix was like a rainbow.
