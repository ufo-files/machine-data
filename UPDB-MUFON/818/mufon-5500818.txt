{rjl} Orange orb returned in less than 24 hours - fast moving 45 degrees sideways, then hovered in exact spot

Refer to case 58221 for previous event that occured around 1AM in the same 24 hour period. Once again, I was sitting outside in my backyard with my same witness. We were sitting on a low step and saw a golf ball sized orange orb light shining through my fence. I immediately stood up, knowing it had come back. We saw it sharply fly at a 45 degree angle from southwest to west and it sat hovering in the exact same location as the sighting last night. By the time it was hovering, it had changed shape from larger to smaller and was flashing glowing orange intermittently and disappeared and reappeared in the same manner as last night. I told my friend to get out her phone and she was able to take a picture and video, although the low quality makes it difficult to see. A plane flies above it from east to south at the same time. There were stars in the sky which do not come out in the video, so the brightness of the orange light can be seen. It disappeared so she turned the video off, but it reapeared afterwards in the same spot and then dimmed and vanished into a gray circle and then nothing. We felt excited to see it again. Unlike the previous event, there were no searchlights and the animal noises remained throughout. Both events had no wind.
