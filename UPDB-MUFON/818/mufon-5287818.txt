Beam of light emanating from a single point of light

I was driving north on boomershine rd on my way to Farmersville when I was startled by a bright beam of light above Germantown metropark. I slowed down to look at it and saw that the conical beam of light shining on the ground was being emitted from a shape I couldn’t make out that had a single point of bright light above the beam. I slowed down and my adrenaline kicked in when I realized it didn’t appear to be a helicopter. I drove slowly for about 15 seconds getting closer. I then stopped for another 30 seconds. As I sat in my car transfixed on the object the beam and the point of light suddenly winked out altogether with no sign of any object left in the air. I keenly listened for any noise but heard nothing. I was mostly a UFO skeptic until this day.
