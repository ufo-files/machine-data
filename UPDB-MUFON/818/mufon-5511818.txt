Unidentified flying objects - double or triple cigar shaped photographed over Cologne on March 27, 2013 at 9 pm.

This afternoon I originally wanted to photograph the moon and was looking for the best focus, as I suddenly discovered nearby a fast-flying objects. I changed the direction of my camera and shot a few pictures successively. There were two different objects, one which flew from the top down, and 1 minute later, another object that flew from the bottom upwards. I photographed these objects from my window, in the apartment where I live in Cologne. It happened in the East. While taking pictures I thought they were quite normal planes or jets, but then I saw something else in the photos. In the photos they look like flying double-or triple-rods, and do not look like us famous flying objects. In some of the photos you can see many small unidentified flying objects that accompanied the large UFO's. When I saw the result in the photos, I was very excited and decided to report my sighting.
