While I was walking my dog, I noticed someting strange in the sky.

I was walking my dog on Sunday, May 13th, 2012 at approx 9:35 pm. When I walked 1 block from my house, I noticed something strange in the sky. The object was approx. 75-100 feet away in distance, elevated approx 5 stories (slightly above a house). This circular object was approx. as wide as a normal row home, hovering around 10-15 feet above the rooftops. It had a green glow around it, with blinking red and blue lights around it. I was able to snap one picture, though it was poor quality, before the object dissappeared.
