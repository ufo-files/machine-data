At a local park I observed a bright pulsating or reflective object mostly stationary in the bright blue sky. I observed it for at least 30minutes. and pointed it out to others in the park that were baffled as well. I started taking pictures and videos. After looking over my photos and using filters, there are more objects that can be seen as well. A jet plane even flew through some of my pictures giving a size and distance reference. The object appeared to be hovering and very slowly moving across the sky at great altitude.
