{cak} At stoplight and saw an orb in the distant daylight sky

The reporting witness was heading northbound on Ward Parkway and stopped for the traffic light at 55th St. Looking forward, he saw what he described as an amber colored orb around 7 PM. The object blinked on and off as it traveled. He saw the object for about 3 seconds somewhere between Pembrook Hill School and Woodside Racquet Club.
