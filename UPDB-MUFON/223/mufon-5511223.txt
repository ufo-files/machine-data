Multiple UFOs were caught on film briefly passing from the North West to the East over the North of Shrewsbury, Shropshire UK on 1st May 2013. They were caught on film with a Pulsar Recon 550 digital night vision monocular but could not be seen with the naked eye. They are not sky lanterns as they did not glow or flicker or give of a bright enough light, but they could be birds, although as this would have been above fields late in the evening, it is unlikely but cannot be ruled out. You can see in the footage that a civilian airliner was also traversing the sky at the same time and at 1 min 25 secs in you will see a small shooting star or meteor enter the atmosphere. Weather was calm with no wind and no clouds. Aircraft and balloons have been ruled out here so they remain unidentified. Once again this shows just how useful night vision equipment is when viewing in low light and dark conditions.
