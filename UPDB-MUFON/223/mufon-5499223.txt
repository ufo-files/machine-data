{cak} Two bright white objects in unison making loops in the sky, then they shape shifted and had red and blue flashing lights.

I was driving South on coors Blvd near Paseo del Norte. I saw two bright white lights flying in unison while making loops and spirals in the sky. They were in perfect unison the entire time and performed maneuvers that are impossible in any type of known aircraft. After about ten seconds of this, both lights changed from white to red and blue flashing lights. At this point both objects were heading West and I watched them until I couldn't see them anymore.
