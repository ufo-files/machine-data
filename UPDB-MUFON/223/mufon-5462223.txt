{tjd} Strange object in the sky.

Hello,

My wife and I where at the pool one evening in Arizona. I was there for work. I took a photo of the pool as the sun was setting behind the trees in the distance. This was May 14th 2018. While going through our pictures almost a year later. We came across a photo with loop mode on. We saw something strange moving in the picture. Not sure what it was, we took the loop effect off and zoomed in on the object. With loop mode off you can clearly see an isometric object. This isn1t even the best part.... I took this photo with my IPhone 7 Plus which has the option of Live photos. I had this option on when I took the photo. When you use this option you can clearly see a object moving at a high rate of speed, up and across and then right up out of the frame. The object illuminates as it is moving so you can easily track its path as it maneuvers out of the frame. Please review the picture and let me know what you think.
