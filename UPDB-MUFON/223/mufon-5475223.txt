{cak} Star/Satellite-like. Two separate craft heading north 1-2 seconds apart. Extremely high speed & sharp random directional changes

My 2 yr/old grandson and I had just got back into the car after picking up take-out food. I opened the sunroof to show him the stars. The first craft came into view traveling north at incredible speed but not in a straight line path. Each deviation in course was mostly slight, noticable and instantaneous with little or no change in velocity OR acceleration. The craft then made abrupt turns, stops, and then turns completing a segmented loop and then continued northward fading in the light-polluted sky. Immediately following was the second craft behaving just like the first, excepting the loop, and faded in the same direction as the first.
The speed and random, instantaneous flight path deviations were unlike anything I have seen before.
