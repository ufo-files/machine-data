I took a 30sec long exposure of the night sky and captured this object

I took a 30sec long exposure of the night sky from my window at around 1:30am using my mobile phone (Huawei P9) and captured this object which is considerably different to anything else in the image, (background stars etc) The camera was completely stationary during the exposure. The night previous, I spent a long time looking at a particular star which was flickering red and blue, and this object seems to have appeared in a similar area of the sky. It has a distinct form, almost like a donut or globular sphere.
