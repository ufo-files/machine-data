Bright light in Jupiter Florida

I was driving home on the I95 southbound and exited on Donald Ross rd heading east. As I was waiting at the red light just east of I95, I noticed a very bright white light, thinking at first "that is an odd street light so high up" taking note also that the street light have a "yellowish tinge" to them. I also noticed that it was not moving towards me or away or up or down from me and it was very steady with no blinking lights but very white like a PURE white light. As I continued to observe this light, it just "blinked out" literally, there was no after glow when it "blinked out". The traffic signal turned green and I scanned the sky where this was and there was nothing. I arrived home approximately 5 minutes later and tried to make sense of what that was.
