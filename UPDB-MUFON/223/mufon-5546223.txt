Red rectangular UFO.

At 8:45 PM on July 30, 2009, my then 5 year old daughter witnessed a large red rectangular UFO from her bedroom window. She is very observant and noticed it immediately as it came in to view. My daughter quickly sketched the object and described it as a large glowing red rectangle. The object moved quickly from the north, moving south to the horizon, just east of the crescent moon. She said it "disappeared" as it moved behind a large tree across the street. The incident happened so quickly that by the time she had alerted me the object was already gone. Though she was only 5 at the time my daughter is precocious and scientifically minded. I asked her if she "really" saw it and she said she did--she does not fib to me and knows the difference between reality and a "fooly".
