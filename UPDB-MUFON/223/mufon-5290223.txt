triangle dark shape with lights on each tip.

while looking at the stars from work, noticed 2 stars/lights that very slowly moved from NE to the N. when a star would go between 2 of the lights, it would black out the star, then the star would appear again after it passed, but high enough that the little clouds we had would block sight of it. the lights looked like very dim stars. after a few min of watching a 3rd light appeared creating the triangle. I'm in Crockett Ca, and this flew over Benicia Ca. Travis AFB is near by but this was going past the base. I did work in Aviation, and never seen anything fly this slow. too dark to take a picture, (need night vision) but I tried to get my coworker to see it but his eyes could not focus on it. I estimate that it was between 5000ft and 15000ft. but hard to tell at night.
