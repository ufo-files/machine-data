It was approximately 9:30 P.M. Central time and I was walking towards my front door when out of the corner of my left eye I saw what looked like an orange-red glowing and pulsating orb. I couldn't believe what I was seeing. I stopped abruptly to look at it and as I did it seemed as though it slowed down as if it had seen my sudden movement. I got a little scared when it did that, so I decided to hurry into my house to get my camera. It took less than 30 seconds to get the camera, but when I came back out to take a picture of it, the orb had traveled a couple of miles to the North and was too far to get a picture. It continued to travel and as it got further away it seemed to go down below tree level when I lost sight of it.
