{tjd} Observed Rectangular shaped, first red then white, front half looked solid, back half looked like short flame go across sky horizontally

I observed 1 adult male and 2 children looking in the sky as I left grocery store. I scanned the sky and found a bright red object that was rectangular shaped in the distance heading across the horizon just above the tree line from my vantage point. The front half of the object looked solid and the back half looked like cartoonish flames also in a rectangular fashion. I lost the item for a moment and then found it again while I was driving but now the object was completely white but looked the same otherwise. I tried to find a clearing to pull over to so I could observe it better but when I did I could not locate it any more. I do have a short video from my dash cam that isnt very good but is included. I did report this the night it happened to the NASA fireball site and 5 others reported the same thing. The were from West Palm beach, Port St Lucie, Orlando, and Tampa. The site says that this appeared to be a possible rocket launch from Cape Canaveral but I looked up the launches from that location and a rocket launched the next day on the 18th not on the 17th when I observed it.
