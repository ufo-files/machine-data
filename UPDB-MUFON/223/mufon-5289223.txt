Bright Green Sphere seen headed in a northern direction in Dallas Pa at approximately 12:38am on 3/13/21

At approximately 12:38am on 3/13/2021, while traveling north on rt309 in Dallas, Pa (just north of the Kunkle Fire Station), a green object was seen by myself (driver) and my girlfriend. The object was seen traveling at a high rate of speed in a northern direction to our right (east). It was no more than 200 ft above the ground, and approximately 500 ft away. The object appeared to be be a green sphere with a bright green aura around it with a small tail. The shade of green was similar to that of a traffic light. It appeared to be descending, but lost sight of it before it got to the tree tops.
