Very bright zig zagging across night sky 12 - 12:30am

My father my sister and i were having a fire outside from around 10pm to 12:30am so it was pretty dark my sister had just felt sick and went inside she said she had a head ache and felt like she was going to pass out but she was fine after (we don't know if this had anything to do with the UFO) and litterly 10 seconds after she went in i was observing the stars above my house and all the sudden a really bright light appeared i thought it was a satellite or the ISS and then it started zipping across the sky it was very bright white round and it came with a faint whirring sound and another identical circle joined and they were zipping in zig zag formations across the sky this lasted for at least a minute it was impossible to be a air plane or helicopter it was too fast and was flying in dimensions they can fly in. I tryed to get my fathers attention and every time he looked they stopped but then we both saw the circles zip across the sky and disappear we were both astonished.
