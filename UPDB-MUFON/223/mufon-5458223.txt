{jpg} Brown Mountain Light UFO

Drove up to Wiseman's view overlook northwest of Morganton, NC in order to check out the mysterious Brown Mountain lights. Before all the sunlight was gone, mysterious orbs started to appear one by one and hover in place south of Table Rock Mountain, about 4 per hour. There were other witnesses watching these orbs and they were used to the sightings. It got to the point where I stopped filming them since they would appear so often then vanish within couple seconds or sometimes even a minute or two. 

A regular plane flew Southwest through the "area of sightings" marked in the image and there appeared to be many smaller, very dim orbs buzzing around it. This however may have been an optical illusion so I'll simply add it as a possibility and not a fact.

Later that night, we witnessed a very bright yellow light that flew towards us, it turned out to be a plane of some sort but it flew low and it made a jet noise. 2 of these flew from the same source (Southeast of Table Rock mountain). One flew toward us (Northwest) and the other one went North. 

There is a chance these orbs may have been drones flown upwards from Table Rock mountain by hikers, but I don't see a reason for emitting that bright of light only to just slowly dim it a minute later.

There were campers and hikers along the ridge line. The flickers of light shown on the video were their flashlights.
