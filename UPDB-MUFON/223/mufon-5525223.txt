Dark/Dull craft with intense white/bluish lights around boomerang perimeter which dimmed to reveal a green light and a red light at each tip

My wife was on her way home from work at about 3:15 a.m. on Tuesday morning August 2nd when she noticed something to the south/southwest of her that looked strange to her....At first, just intense white/bluish light could be seen but as she approached it, those lights dimmed to reveal there were also a green light and a red light, one at the end of each tip of this boomerang-ish craft....The closer she got, she realized it was a craft/ship of some type....It hovered at about the same height as the light poles on I-275 South (the road she was traveling).....She drove almost right under it, and said she almost pulled over to look at it (which is very uncharacteristic of her, as she is scared of her own shadow) but it vanished.....no trail, emissions ect, just poof! gone.....she told me about this weird feeling she got as she approached it....the hair on her arms stood up and i asked her if it was a feeling of fear, but she said not really, more uneasy...there were no other cars around at the time on 275 as she told me she was swerving due to her looking up at it the whole time..i arrived home about 15 mins after her and got a pen and paper and had her draw this thing and i asked her as many questions about it as i could before she told me she just wanted to go to bed and not think about it anymore....I reported this to the Livonia Police Department and they told me they'd had no reports of anything of that nature, but the officer told me he'd check with surrounding departments and took my name/number ad would contact me with any details...the officer also informed me he had seen a UFO when he was a teenager and didn't think i was crazy at all....I don't really know what to think at this point or if i should leave me email or some contact info in case anyone else saw the same thing she did, but i will continue to research this....i don't have a scanner, but i do have her sketch that she did for me about 30 mins after witnessing this.
