{dr}Seen what looked like a constellation of stars but then i noticed they were moving around.

I was driving about to make a left turn onto the street I live on when I noticed 6-12 star-like lights that was in a formation that made it at first look like a constellation of stars, but only brighter, and they glowed orangish-red. I immediately stopped the car and turned and asked the passenger who is my cousin, "What is that?" I knew what it was even though I never witnessed anything like this before. My cousin, said, "I don't know." So we watched it for a couple seconds and noticed that the lights were moving around eachother. ALmost like they were changing into another formation. It was nighttime so we could see the stars and also airplanes around us. We knew that this wasn't an airplane or a star. The lights were brighter than stars, they glowed an orangish-red color. Compared to the stars they were much bigger in size and glowed orangish red. They moved from left to right, open & down around eachother unlike the motions of a plane. We drove around and watched it for a couple minutes. Then the lights started disappearing one by one. Until there was one left. The last one stayed in the same place for a couple seconds and then disappeared like the other ones. There was no sound coming from the lights. I know people are going to think I'm crazy. But I had someone with me that seen the same thing. I wasn't scared, I was more excited than anything to be a witness to it.
