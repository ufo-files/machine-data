{rjl} My father heard a very loud and powerful object moving over our neighbors house then above our own home, shaking trees violently, and finally to the other neighbors house, where the tone increased and suddenly whooshed towards the sky.

My father came home from work at around 1130pm and when he left his vehicle he heard a very loud mechanical sound coming from our neighbors home. My father first thought our neighbors ac was possibly breaking, but as my father approached the gate at the end of our driveway to close it, he turned around and realized that the object was directly over our house. The trees in the yard were blowing violently as if in strong wind (there was no wind this night). The loud, invisible object moved towards our other neighbors home, when the pitch of the noise changed to a higher frequency and made a whooshing sounds into the sky. All was silent after that. My father was trying to figure out what he just witnessed when our neighbor from the first house called out my dads name and asked him f he had heard the noise too. They both had. It woke up our neighbor. The next morning my father went to the backyard next to an open field to feed our local deer when to his dismay, his backyard canopy was some slightly crushed and had been lifted and shifted off its original placement. My mother investigated it this afternoon and took photos. This isn1t the first incident in the area.
