Two flaming objects came up from the ground then danced around each other and took off

I was headed home with my girlfriend on 24 Hwy when we saw two red/orange fires rise up from the ground behind trees about three blocks away from our location. They headed towards us. We pulled the car over to get a better look and watched these objects dance around each other for about five minutes, then cross 24 Highway and head over an auto repair place. They were not much higher than the trees and only about 30 yards from us. They were about the size of a basketball or larger and there was no sound. Then they lined up one behind the other and took off faster towards the NE. It looked like giant flames, not a craft or light. But they did not look like sky lanterns. As close as they were we would have been able to see if they were sky lanterns. But when then lined up and took of faster, we knew then that they weren't drones or sky lanterns or anything normal. There were quite a few cars that pulled over on the highway to get a look so I know that more people saw these. We were too shocked to get video.
