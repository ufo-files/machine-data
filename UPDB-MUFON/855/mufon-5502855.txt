{nd} Orange spheres hovering high in the sky. They would appear from nothing, dissapear and re-appear somwhere else.

I was standing on the east side of the St Johns River looking across the water to the west, on the other side 2 clusters of orange sphere like lights were hovering very high in the air. I was immediately intrigued and began to observe the objects. I know they were over 2 miles away because the width of the river in the area is about 2 miles, making the objects seem very large based on the distance I was observing them from. As I was watching I observed each light disappear and another re-appear shortly after. There were two clusters each making a strait line angling 45 degrees in a negative slope in accordance to the horizon. They resembled the stripes of red dots one would acquire when having a brush with poison ivy. For about 15 minutes they disappeared an re-appeared until the disappearing became more frequent than the re-appearing. Eventually there were only four left and they methodically dissipated into thin air.
