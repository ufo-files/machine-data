Orbs

As I was getting ready to go to work it was still dark outside and the night was clear, no cloud in sight. I noticed I heard a very odd hum pulsing every second once I stepped outside. To the east was 6 to 10 orbs in the night sky at first I thought I caught sight of a meteor entering the upper atmosphere but I noticed that the orbs were not falling they were in place in the sky and they had light trails following behind. I immediately took out my phone to record the bizarre sight. The objects then broke off as if they were synchronized then got into a perfect line then the middle orb was seemingly bigger than the rest emitted a reddish glow just a bit brighter than the others and all at once they rose higher and higher and in a blink of an eye they zipped up into the sky and disappeared amongst the stars
