{dr}Another siting like the one I saw on 8/30/12, lights and hovering for almost 2 hours

My daughter and I went out onto the back porch of our home and I looked back the same direction (WEST) as I looked on 8/31/12 (I also reported that siting) and saw the exact same thing. Hovering craft, stayed in same place for about 35 minutes and slowly kept going west. It was barely moving when it did and took about 2 hours to get so far west towards the treeline I could not see it anymore. It flashed red, green, and blue lights. This time I was able to get my camera to focus and capture it in pictures (uploading as one to show the difference in light colors) and a video.
