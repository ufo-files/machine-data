Small Orb (elevating up and down) trailing a slow moving larger Orb travelling South to North West side of Yate

After discussing this, I was recently directed to your site by a friend and old comrade to inform those who may be interested and find it useful. Maybe shed some light of information for my self.
I had seen a Small Orb (elevating and undulating up and down) that was matching the speed trailing behind a slow moving much larger Orb travelling "purposely" slow and steady from the South to North on the West side of Yate between Coalpit Heath and Frampton Cotterell.
This happened in the early hours 3 to 4am as my Dog woke me as he wanted to go out to relieve himself. 
Saw this above and passed the roof line, and travelling beyond the tree line and that's about 500 metres away from me. There was no sound . Plus no adverse effects to myself or my Dog to the best of my knowledge.
It took around 20 seconds to cross my field of vision from above the houses behind the line of trees. Photo downloaded of the view and distance, not of objects.
Personal assessment : Not Ball lightening . Not a Military Helicopter or Drone.
