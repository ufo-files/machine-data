{cak} In reference to the Laguna Beach Orbs sighting featured on home page of MUFON site.

In order to try and see more detail in the video submitted to MUFON on the Laguna Beach Orbs, I turned brightness all the way down and cranked up the contrast while playing video. As video played I took pictures of individual video frames using a separate camera. The results were pretty amazing. I wanted to share these images with the MUFON community. Anyone can create the same light filtering playback to see how these images were captured. Ended up with 900 images (got carried away). You can see all 5 orbs flash various colors and they are staggered at various times.
