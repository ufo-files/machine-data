{nd} Bright Object

I was driving home from work and noticed bright object in sky. At first I thought it was a bright star or plane but as I drove West the object looked bigger and brighter. At the stop light I took the picture. When I got home in five minutes the object was still in sky but had moved West towards the sunset. I went inside to get husband to show him but it was gone when we returned outside.
