{tjd} Multiple star like orbs.

On Thursday March 5 around 8:20 pm my son and I were taking trash out to the road. I started showing him different stars in the sky. He didn’t have shoes on so he ran back in to put some on. As I was waiting on him to return I looked to the west and observed 10-15 white star like objects following in a straight line only to reach a dark area in the north western sky and disappear. I called my wife on the phone and told her to hurry out side. She came out and observed the tail end of the lights. We watched the sky for around 15 min after and saw two more with a red tint follow the same path as the others and disappear in the same spot. We also saw a cluster of lights that looked further up in altitude. My wife and my son could see them swarming like flys around each other. I tried to capture it on my camera phone but was not successful. I would be more that happy to talk to someone if further information is needed.
