Blue glowing orb in bedroom

It was around 11pm at night, I had just gotten home and was relaxing for the night before I went to bed. My room is laid out with my tv on the side wall and my bed, which takes up almost all the space in the room. I was sitting upright with my back pressed up against my bedframe, with the blankets over my waist watching tv, when I saw an orb bigger than a baseball but smaller than a basketball hovering over my lap. I'd say I observed it for at least 5 seconds before it just disappeared Infront of my own eyes. It didn't leave any trail or go any direction just vanished. The orb had an ultramarine blue color for the outside and more towards the middle it was a lighter more cyan blue. It almost looked like encapsulated energy to me but I just wanted to share my up close experience, since when I was younger I had an incident where I physically saw and spoke to a loved one in my family that had passed away.
