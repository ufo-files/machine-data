I was driving home after a business meeting at approximately 9:30 pm. Deer mating season and I was on an unlighted highway so I was particularly alert. Completely clear night with no cloud cover. I saw a glowing, pill shaped object fly by above me from due West to due East. The object was bright green and was visible for about 2 seconds before disappearing over the horizon. Obviously moving at a high rate of speed. While not a pilot or trained observer, I have served in the Military and can state without hesitation that the object was like nothing I have ever seen before. I immediately thought that I had just observed a UFO. I did not mention the incident to anyone, but have been unable to forget the incident , and felt that I should report this to you to determine if anyone else had seen this object. I might mention that incident occurred less than 3 miles from a nuclear assembly assembly plant, but does not have nuclear material on sight.
