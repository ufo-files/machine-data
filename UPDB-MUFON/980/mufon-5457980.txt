{rjl} Was walking down my street with a friend and saw what we thought was the sunset.

I was walking down Amberwood estates on Grand Island NY with a friend and I said look at the beutiful sunset. He said it sure is but was looking west and there was the real sunset much bigger so I said theres another one here. He turned around and we where wondering what it was so we started to walk towards it in the field as it was just over the woods. It came towards us as we approached so we ran as fast as we could back to my house. I witnessed the same orange ball in Courtney on Vancouver Island with my wife and her family camping and it was moving in directions at speeds that were incredible. We watched it for about 5 minutes. That incident accured about 25 years ago. 

When i was young about 11 on sweet home rd in Amherst NY everyone saw three v shaped disc fly over the field with no sound. Also saw the same style objects fly over West River Grand Island very close up from shore under 500 feet with no sound traveling at approximatly 250 MPH in 1977.
