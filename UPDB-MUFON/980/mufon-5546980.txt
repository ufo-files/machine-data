cone of light with mist

I was driving home from my brothers house with my son around 8:15ish, I looked off to my left which would be looking south west while driving west on W. main st.As I looked up I noticed a cone shaped beam of light that was whitish in color. There seemed to be a fog or mist accompanied with the light. The object/light didn't seem to be that high up and was at about 45 degrees up from the horizon. I told my son to look at the object and he was kinda surprised to see this as we had previously seen some strange lights in the sky 2 weeks earlier. We witnessed this light and mist for maybe a min and appeared stationary then it just vanished like smoke dissipating. The light itself appeared to look like a street light kinda flat on top and round on the bottom with a cone shaped light emanating from it into the mist/fog. Sorry for the crude drawing.
