{rjl} Many many lights floating together, breaking apart and moving quickly before disapering

Parked my car got out and started walking but Northwest and noticed orbs moving oddly in the sky. Pulled out my phone camera and started to record the phenomenon, they were reflective on one side dark on the other. I continued to video for about 40 seconds until I put my phone away as the object started to disappear. Formations would break off from the original formation and move very quickly towards the west across the sky with other objects chasing behind. I at first thought the object was birds until I saw how quickly they were moving and how they stayed together in formation very high in the sky. I was not scared but I didn1t know how to feel it was something I have never witnessed before all I could do was look on in awe.
