{cak} we observed something hovering high in the sky, and watched for over an hour as it frequently changed direction and altitude

My husband and I stepped outside so I could have a cigarette at 12:11am, November 9th. We noticed a bright light in the sky that resembled a very large, bright star. We soon realized it was ascending and descending and hovering. It frequently moved from left to right, up and down unlike any aircraft we've seen. I tried to take video, but my phone could not pick it up. It appeared to pulsate and sometimes seemed to change from red to white lights. It zig-zagged around, and hovered for periods of time. There appeared to be occasional beams of light coming off of it. Eventually it traveled away from us and we watched it get smaller. We could still see it as we reentered our house. We wanted to wait for it to disappear, but we were too cold to remain outside for longer. It made us anxious to see what we believed to be a UFO. We were hoping it would dart off, but it continued to hover, just farther away.
