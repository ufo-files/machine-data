3 Crafts total, 2 blast off as 1 hovers in the distance

While watching the full moon in the backyard I noticed a white object in the sky that was moving erratically in circles and back and forth in the distance. I took out my phone and began to film it. As I did this, 2 other bright white looking objects appeared out of nowhere and rapidly flew past where I was filming. One of them left a light trail behind. The objects were not seen by me while I was initially filming. They were not seen until I went back to review the video of the object I had seen hovering and moving in the distance. The two crafts or objects that are seen at the very beginning of the video near the bottom are the ones I did not see with my naked eye. I had no idea they were even there until reviewing the video later when I found out they went right past me. Based on the video, they're extremely bright and extremely fast and left a trail behind. I took a screenshot of the beginning of the video and they appear to be two separate types of crafts. I can make out what looks like windows and other detailed things on the different crafts. I hope other people will do the same and can provide more insight. If I had known the other two crafts were there I would have followed them with the camera and tried to have seen where their path led, but unfortunately I only saw the one in the distance (until viewing the video).
