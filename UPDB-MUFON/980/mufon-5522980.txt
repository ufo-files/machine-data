A friend and were golf ball hunting and we were running down a hill when we both felt something that made us both stop and look up. There was a glowing orangeish ball up in the air. It flew away in an instant.

As i stated in the short version, it was a glowing orangeish ball floating nearly straight above us. We both stopped running down the hill at the same time. We looked up and saw the object. We didn't say a word until it was gone. I felt numb when it was above us. We watched it for a few minutes and then it went from stationary to all the way out of sight in the blink of an eye! There seemed to be a brief trail as it went. Though it lasted only seconds. I never heard of anyone else seeing but I know what we saw and I will never forget it!!!
