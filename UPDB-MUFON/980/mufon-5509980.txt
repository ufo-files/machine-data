{nd} Bright orange balls of light

My wife and I along with several people were leaving a show at the local high school. As we left the building, we noticed three balls of light moving slowly from west to east. The were almost the same color as the street lights, so I moved to a clearing away from the street lights to get a better view. All three eventually disappeared into the clouds, but were later seen by family in a town 45 miles away only a few minutes later. All three flew in unison almost directly over the Snake River Canyon. There were at least 20 to 30 other witnesses.
