Something passes by the moon twice, may be a satellite, but I don't know, I only discovered years later in review

I was filming the moon and the clouds passing in front of the moon approximately four years ago in the town of Fort Erie in Crescent Park, but I never really got a chance to review my video on the computer until recently when I was cleaning up my picture/video files and deleting files deemed unnecessary, when I discovered this particular file. I never saw the object when it happened, but upon reviewing the video there is indeed an object that passes by the moon on two occasions. The only reason the object is visible, is because of its close proximity to the moons light. I'm thinking this could have been a satellite, but I am not sure, because it passes twice by the moon. I also do not no how low, or how high satellites fly, nor whether the object in question is passing in front, or behind the moon, or how large the object is, but when I saw the video for the first time I got a chill of sorts, like discovering something sundown, what is this that flies by the moon and is it indeed a UFO, or just a satellite?
