Red/Yellow/Orange Oval UFO Silenty Passed Overhead at about 2,000 ft

A UFO that was colored a bit of red/yellow/orange passed silently over ahead around midnight. 5 of us adults were sitting out around the fire after a graduation party. A noticed a bright glowing light in the sky. At first glance, the thought was UFO. All five adults reported seeing the object and noted that it moved quickly across the sky from the Southeast moving North. Did not move like normal aircraft. Object disappeared over the horizon.
