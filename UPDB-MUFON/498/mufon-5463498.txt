{dr}Horizontal beam of light that rotated then shot through the sky

We were stopped waiting to turn heading west on Warner Road in Tempe when we saw this object in the sky (see attached photo). We (my son and I) both saw it through the windshield and I asked my son to take a picture of it. He took the attached photo through the side window. A few second later, my son started to shoot a video of it but it started to turn which freaked him out and the video stopped (video attached as well but it is less than one second long. Please excuse his language). When turning, the left side turned towards the sky and the right side downwards. It leveled off, dipped slightly then slowly accelerated (about half a second) then shot at incredible speed over us. There was no sound. We looked behind us but couldn't see it anymore. We were shocked and still two days later, we can't believe what we saw.
