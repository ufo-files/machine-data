Was watching for Comet Leonard starting at dusk that evening, first saw the Starlink satilites looked like a line of lights then saw the orb

On 12/4/21, I was outside scanning the sky for Comet Leonard which was estimated to be visible under Venus in the Southwestern sky.
During this time I noticed the StarLink satilites that looked very much like a train of light in the Northwestern sky.
As they disappeared from sight, it appeared the last light dropped out of the line and slowly drifted downward and disappeared.
Strange but not disconcerting.
I returned to watching the Southwestern sky and a light appeard above and in front of me at about a 30 degree angle at a distance of between 100 and 500 feet. Difficult to judge with nothing near it for scale.
It appeared to be pulsing and spinning and when it stayed for a while, I ran to get my phone to take a picture.
Came back and it was still there but approximately a mile away.
Again, the light went out and it returned to the same spot as it was when I went for the camera.
It was able to move in all axis freely and seemed to float.
I started to film it and it seemed to just hang there shifting slightly back and forth.
I assumed as it seemed to be staying I'd get another person out of the house to see it but when we returned it was gone.
I attached the video clip for review.
I was told later that evening that the same Orb had been seen a few miles south of me by several men at a local restaurant parking lot.
