When i looked in the sky where the object should have been, it wasn't there it was over me. i walked a straight line to the end of garden and looked up and it was over me again, so I walked another 50 ft. and it followed me each time. I went to my house and it followed me. There to. There were several others way off just hanging in the sky also.
