I was dog watching and let the dogs out in the back yard getting ready to go to bed. I noticed a greyish/white object in the sky. I didnt recognize what it was so I kept watching and became a little scared. I looked at my watch and it was 8;34pm. It wasnt windy just a few clouds in the sky. I then took the dogs inside and then went back outside. THe object stayed in the same general area flying around randomly. I didnt hear any noise...I kept watching then went back in and got my phone to record. However, the object was to far away so I couldnt record it...I went back inside and then went back outside about 15 minutes later and then there were two objects exactly the same. They were really high and I just watched in amazement. I never lost sight of the object I went back into the house after about ten minutes. I didnt know anyone in the neighborhood so I couldnt ask them to look at the sky.
