{rjl} we saw 3 objects moving in the sky, and then a falling object about a mile away from our location

My sister and I were hanging out at the park, watching the stars. We were watching an object in the sky. The object was moving back and forth and in circles above us. We then saw 2 more objects, moving around. As she was watching the first object we saw, I looked around and watched another object about a mile away from us falling from the sky. She had also seen it out of the corner of her eye. This object was was really bright, and looked like a firework descending from the sky. When we looked back to where the 3 moving objects were, they had disappeared. We decided to walk towards where we thought the object had fallen. As we walked around we could smell an usual burnt fuel. Street lights around us would turn off an on, even though they are light censored, not motion.
