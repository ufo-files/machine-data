{nd} Single non flashing light moving sw to ne in a straight path

It was a clear night, and I always notice the stars and at first I thought it was a star. Then I noticed it was moving. First observed in the SW, moving slow or about the speed of a plane landing. aboout 500 feet? The night was quiet and the object made no sound at all...It was heading in the direction of the airport but at that angle it would have been approaching the 4000 ft mark on an 8500 ft. runway. Also the runway points NNW to SSE (338 deg.) so it would have crossed over the runway on an x diagonal, and planes do not fly over the runway that low. What got my attention was the absence of red and green navigation lights and a strobe light. I lost sight of it over a tree line.
