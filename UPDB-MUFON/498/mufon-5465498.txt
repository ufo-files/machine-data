On September 29, 2018 at about 9 pm my husband and I were sitting on the patio overlooking the Gulf looking at the stars in the dark night sky, when, what seemed to be a fast-moving gray cloud caught my eye appearing above a tall palm tree. I pointed it out to my husband who confirmed what I was seeing as a large, misty, dimly lit boomerang object, which covered all the stars in its path. It looked about as big as my hand. It made no sound and continued in a straight path towards the horizon, when it appeared to flip sideways into a straight line and disappear. I observed for about ten seconds. I didn11t know what I was seeing and did not feel any fear or excitement, just curiosity.
