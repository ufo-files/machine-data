{rjl} Triangle shaped craft. Then 2 sets of straight lights.

I Was outside looking in the sky talking to my family about the position of venus. It was four of us my mom, my sister and her husband, and myself. I saw what looked like a dark triangle craft with lights flying in east to west overhead. My sister and her husband were also able to see it. As it approached the western area of the sky it flipped at an impossible speed before disappearing from view. About fifteen minutes later we witnessed a strange set of lights fly in from the west. They appeared in a straight line which then ascended slowly to the NW. There was approximately 40-50 lights and was very far off in the distance. Another few minutes passed and again another set of lights about the same size that followed about the same path. Along with those lights there appeared a single orb like craft that seemed to follow the bigger craft. My phone was unable to get video of the craft and my mom got a picture but it does not accurately represent what we saw.
