(GS) UFO was witnessed by 3 people hovering and then decending with pulsating and flashing many different color lights

I spotted a UFO hovering about 100 yards away in front of my apartment complex. I then went back in to my apartment to get my 2 guests to confirm what I just saw. We all witnessed the disc shape craft rotating while hovering stationary. Green, blue, white, red, and yellow lights flashed and pulsated. The craft then descended and looked as it may have landed. All the street lights flickered on and off when the craft descended. For all I know its still out there. We watched the craft for 20 min and I decided it was time to leave.
