{dr}rapidly descending light, stopped & hovered above trees, changed direction & floated away

On June 15, 2014 at approximately 10:30 p.m., my husband & I were sitting on our apartment balcony. It was a clear night with lots of stars. We were looking up, & commenting on how many satellites we were seeing. Suddenly one of what we initially thought was a satellite fell out of the sky towards us. Within seconds it went from being a pin point in the sky to a large object hovering right above the tree tops across the street. The object fell extremely fast, within a few seconds & appeared to be headed straight for us when suddenly it stopped, hovered briefly over the tree tops, looped around & slowly floated away from us until we lost sight of it. It was a very bright ball of white light with a slight blueish tint to it.
