{cak} About 8 glowing Boomerang UFOs flying and formed what looked like the big/little dipper before just disappearing

I was outside taking my dog out to go to the bathroom before bed - I'm always looking up at the sky to look for shooting stars. Last night was very very clear so I was excited because I usually see one on nights like it. I then noticed to my left (I was looking West) a movement (I thought it might be a shooting star) but, it was 8 closely clustered glowing boomerangs flying so fast from the right to left! They all moved at different speeds and they rotated from < to ^ and formed the shape of what can only be described as the big/little dipper. After it was completed (all in about 2 seconds) they all just disappeared by no longer glowing in unison.
