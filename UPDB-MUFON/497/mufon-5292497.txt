Saw 2 UFOs above the ocean at 4am

A friend and I were staying right on the beach in Narragansett, RI and decided to go for a walk. It was very foggy out and creepy so we went back to the hotel to sit on the deck when we saw 2 very bright lights in the sky directly above the ocean line. I knew they were way too bright to be stars so we both recorded each of them. They were hovering in the air and at one point, one of them looked like it dropped 3 orbs out of it but I don’t think that translated on the videos. We each recorded the 2 prominent ones from different angles (because there were 2). Zoomed in it looks like they’re rotating and has a black dot in each center, and a yellow ring around it. Not sure what it is but would like to know!
