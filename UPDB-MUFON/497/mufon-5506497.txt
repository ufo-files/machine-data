{rjl} There was a parade of UFOS that did a flyby past my front walk.

I was standing outside my house, holding a cup of Earl Grey, when suddenly I heard a great racket. Grabbing my handy pair of binoculars I looked for the noise. At great speeds, suddenly thirteen UFOs swooped down, after frantically flying across the sky, looking for passersby. Then, they swooped down and landed on the street, moving a good 20 mph. Each had its own color scheme and shape, and had different pulsating lights. One alien stepped out of the front entity. It had crocodile-like jaws, but also had humanoid qualities. It seemed dim, and hovered on insectile, but seemingly cyborgian wings. Instantly, I could understand what it was trying to say to me. It said the following- "Want to join us? Grab your ship and lets go!". I, of course, informed it I could not. Thus, I grabbed my tea, and climbed aboard for a tour of the town in the craft. After several flybys, and a lap around the earth, we docked at the mothership, which must have been over 300 miles across, where we had a lovely dinner. I had a large 8lb steaklike meal, and afterwards was returned unharmed to my home. The alien promised wed keep in touch via the postal system, and thus we went our seperate ways. Even today, I think about our encounter, and the two of us are planning a reunion party three months from today.
