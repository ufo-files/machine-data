{dr}Cylindrical metal object that seemed out of place near the Key Bank Building in Ohio City

We were both at The Pier in Lakewood, Ohio hoping to catch a beautiful sunrise of the skyline of Cleveland. When we both are photographers we tend to only notice the scene, not many specifics. When we got back from our morning I did my normal photo analysis and found a weird silver stick-like figure by the Key Bank building, and so I zoomed in and found that the figure has a metal look to it. I first got other opinions and when even my professors told me "I have no clue what that is" I knew I caught something literally out of this world. At first I was a skeptic and thought it was a plane, but there are no wings, maybe a bird, but again no wings and no head. I finally smartened up and concluded it was a UFO. The object was unnoticed until my photo analysis was finished, so the movement on the second photo was the little light that shot into the distance. I felt as though there was a different presence while we were both shooting, but I could not put my finger on it. It was a weird comfort I had up until the object shot off. The sunrise was suppose to be at 7:45am and the photos were documented at 7:15am. There was cloud cover all over, but the object was in sight under the cloud cover, which means it was flying too low in the city for a plane. Sight was lost when it shot off towards the lake area. This raises the question, is this a UFO or a USO? We may never know, but my findings could become key.
