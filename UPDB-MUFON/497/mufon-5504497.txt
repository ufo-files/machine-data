{nd} stationary light that changes from solid red to solid yellow-orange to strobing white witnessed for 15 minutes by two witnesses

On 2/26/14 I was inside the home that I work at when one of the ladies told me to come outside to see this light that she was watching for about 5 minutes that was stationary but kept changing colors. I watched for another 10 minutes while the light remained stationary but changed colors. It went from a solid red to a solid yellowish/orange and then to a bright white strobe-like white light. It changed the colors several times but did not have any type of pattern to it. It stayed still and then just disappeared. There was no noise that we could hear.
