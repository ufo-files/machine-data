{rjl} Large Black triangle hovered for several Minutes and darted out of sight with a soft

A friend and I were on his porch talking and hanging out in October of 1999. He went to the north side of his house to take a leak, I went to the south end. We were not in each others line of sight. It was a clear night. I looked up and noticed a big black area where there were no visible stars and it caught my eye so I looked. It was a huge black triangle shaped thing. The corners were rounded and not pointy. It was hovering several hundred feet above the ground. Under each corner was a dark circle thing. (A blacker black than the rest of the triangle if that makes sense). It looked to be hovering but there was movement a little. Kind of like a slight wobble or rotation of a few feet. I just looked up and stared at it speechless. It was kind of all I could do. Then with a sound I can only describe as "whisp", it shot off to the north and was gone. It moved at a speed like no other man made object I know of or have ever seen, except in sci-fi things. Almost like it was slung out of a slingshot. I walked back to the porch to tell my friend, but he was as speechless as I was because he saw it too. We looked at each other and he said "You saw that s**t too didn't you!?"
We verified we saw the same thing. And of course, nobody believed us so we just chalked it up to us being added to the countless eyewitnesses to a UFO and nothing will ever come of it. I will never forget that night. It pretty much changed the way I think about things in a grand scale sort of sense.
