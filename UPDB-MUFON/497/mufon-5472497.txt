{jpg} Twin, silver, orb-like UFOs sighted high in altitude moving with irregular, figure 8-like flight path before bursting out of the atmosphere like a bullet.

Two silvery, orb-like craft were observed in Pantego, TX near Arlington on September 4th, 2012 at approximately 7:30pm CDT. These two UFOs were observed much higher above (approx 75,000 - 80,000 feet above ground) what appeared to be a military or commercial aircraft that was tan in color. The UFOs' flight path was irregular and similar to a figure 8 pattern. They appeared to be observing the human-made aircraft below them. After observing these twin UFOs for approximately 10 minutes, they both shot out of the atmosphere extremely fast like a bullet disappearing instantly leaving behind a small vapor trail.
