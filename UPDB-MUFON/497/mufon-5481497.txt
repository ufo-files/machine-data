{cak} Witness observed a staionary glowing object with rotating blue & white lights about 500' up and about 500' away with half-moon shape for about 20 minutes.

This was my 2nd sighting, which occurred on Nov. 28th, at 5:15am, again letting our dogs out for potty run, I looked up in the sky toward the same direction as the 1st sighting. I noticed an object of similar shape as the 1st one and at about the same height, about 1000 feet. I believe this object, like the 1st one, was not moving. Again, the sky was clear with lots of stars around it. The lights seemed more brilliant and brighter than during the 1st sighting but the shape seemed to match. It gave me an eerie feeling again. I was afraid to even keep watching it. So, instead of waking my husband up again, I felt like I might be able to get a picture of it with ages old Kodak digital camera (Kodak Easy Share C 613 200-AF3X). While I was trying to get a sighting in the view panel, I could not belive what I was seeing. I clicked and shot 4 or 5 pictures. The 1st shot that I had taken when I printed out the pictures, came out blank. What I remember of what showed up on the viewer, was a fan-like shape-3 blade shapes, looking like a propeller. However, all the other shapes, once I enlarged the individual shots, left me stunned and bewildered, as well as confused as to what the object really was. I feel that this sighting was something unnatural. I can only speculate that it was a UFO sighting--and hopefully, friendly. Now, when I let the dogs out, I don't look up into the sky. This 2nd sighting was about the same time span as the 1st--about 20 minutes. I let the dogs back in and went to bed.
