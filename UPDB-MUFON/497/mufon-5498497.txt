As i was walking east bound in the park i noticed the object wasn't moving like a plane. So i watched it as i continued my walk. When i got to the closes point of the craft i noticed it was hovering about 1000 to 2000 feet about a half mile away above the park. I stopped to observed and realized this was not plane. It was jet black and shaped like a boomerang. No lights and no noise. It bigger than a large plane. It rotated at about 2 revolutions a minute again clockwise. I stood there for about 2 minutes watching it. My cell phone was in my truck. I was about 50 yards from my truck. So i turned to walk to my truck and took my eyes off the object. It took about a minute to walk to my truck. When i got there i looked up and it was gone. No noise, no trail, it had just vanished. That made me realize this was not manmade here on earth.
