{rjl} Very Bright Light In Sky

Update to first filed mucin case report 108117. Tonight the object came from the west and headed north west. I went out at 8:04 pm central time and it was very bright in the sky even though the sun hadn’t fully set. It remained bright but would go in and out of brightness like it was slowly pulsing. It traveled slowly and just blipped out of the sky at 9:41 pm central time and was no longer seen in the sky. It acted differently than we had seen it the rest of the week. It started out in the west not in the north and instead of going due west it went from the west to the north and east. It didn’t stay as long as it had been either. I got some good photos and some video on my iPhone but it isn’t the best camera. I tried to get some close up and far away shots. In the sky it looked diamond shaped but when I took photos and videos it looked circular. We live close to Lake Charles Regional Airport. There hasn’t been much air travel but tonight there were a few helicopters after the sighting all headed for the airport. We saw one plane fly low over the back of our home. Not much else happened after that.
