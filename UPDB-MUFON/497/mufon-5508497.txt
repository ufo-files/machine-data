It flew along the area of Hartsfield Airport. went across the flight path, three times. Then assended.

I live in a high rise apartment bldg. I was lying on the bed looking out the window, when I noticed it. I can see the jets take off from the airport and I was just looking at them. I noticed an object, all I could really make out was that it was two blue lights. They were side by side. It was in the distance and it went straight for a few miles, then it returned in the same direction it came from without stopping. That was when it got my attention. It did this a few times, then ascended at about a forty five degree angle. Then it disappeared.
