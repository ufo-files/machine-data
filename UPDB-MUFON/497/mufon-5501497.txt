{dr}Brightlights, appeared to land, human like beings spotted.

I was about 4 years old. I know that may make this seem less interesting. But To this day 35 years later I can recall every moment of the encounter. I was laying in bed looking out my window when I saw a bright light behind our apartment complex, a few moments later I see 2 human like beings walking behind the complex in the dark..they had what appeared to be glowing eys and unusual odd shaped heads and seemed to stare right at me throught the darkness. After they walked around the playground area which was behind the complex in a wooded are they returned to the woods where the lights were and seemed to take off into the sky. This was around 1979 in the fall I believe. It haunts me to this day..because I cannot rationalize what was happening.
