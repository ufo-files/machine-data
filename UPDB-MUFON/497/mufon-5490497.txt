Observed white grey object with reddish aura hover then accelerate

At 11.30 pm over my residence; I observed a dull reddish light moving rapidly downwards from south to north, midway above the horizon. I first thought it was a shooting star, until it became larger and stopped moving for a few seconds; my next thought was a large bird, until I realised it must be the size of a Jumbo jet and had a dull reddish aura; it's colour was matt mid grey / white. It was shaped like a boomerang, but held vertical, with a teardrop centre; the object had no markings, no moving parts, and made no sound. It then accelerated horizontally and disappeared. I have some understanding of physics and engineering, but I can't explain what I saw; as it moved too fast for its relative size, had a perpetual aura and no noticeable engine or cockpit; I was left feeling nervous but excited, to witness something unexplainable and hope reporting this helps all of us understand these phenomena.
