(GS) Breathtaking, speechless.

It was a dark summer night, I was getting ready to sit down in my living room. I looked outside briefly, as i always do. I see airplanes sometimes or the occasional helicopter. But this was none of those things. I saw a ball of light, almost like an orange, amber-red color. I told my dad What the h*ll is that? you have got to see this me, my father and my brother all went outside to see this mysterious object. I said to myself No, no this is nothing. But this was roaming around the trees and suddenly vanished. Another one came, and another one came in a straight line formation. They were all the same color. And when the color would go away, i saw a small dark object far up in the sky. A few more past and it was just breath taking to me, it stopped me in my tracks and i didn't know what to say or what to do. I started to cry and i couldnt breathe. I even had trouble sleeping last night and now its all i can think about. I never thought i would see something like this. I waited to see if more of them would pass but they would become more bright and than slowly fade away as if turning off a light.
