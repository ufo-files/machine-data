{cak} saw a hovering UFO while walking down my street as a child

My friend and I were walking down the street.... North.... when we looked up and saw a shiny disk shaped object above us. I'm not even sure what year it was. I believe from 73 to 75? It was warm out.... Not winter. There were so many UFO sightings at the time there was a UFO hotline in the area. I do not know who ran the UFO hotline. 

We tried calling the hotline once we got back to the place were we were, but one of our other friends hung up the phone on us. 

Years later I was at work, when I answered the phone, and it was [name removed] making a reservation.... I asked her..... Hey, am I crazy? or did we really see a UFO that day..... She confirmed to me..... yes, we saw a UFO......
