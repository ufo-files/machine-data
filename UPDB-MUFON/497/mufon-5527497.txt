orange light 75 to 100 ft up came within 100-200 ft. silent moving. light went out and back on several times

I was 7 years old. Entire family fishing on a bridge on Loop rd in the everglades. We continued fishing after dark the warmouth were biting well. A orange light appeared about 200-300 ft away and slowly approached us. It was silent no smoke no sparks. Dad made us pack up and we sat in the car watching it. His hand was on the ignition key as he talked about it with my mother. When the light went out he decided to go to a turnaround point about 1/2 mile away {road was narrow} as we drove towards it the light came on and followed us part way there. We sat at the turnaround point as dad discussed whether we should drive back by it or go the long way around.He finally decided to drive back past it. We drove back to the tamiami trail and there was a car that had ran into the canal . Dad never reported the sighting. Mom and I are the only two remaining witnesses.
