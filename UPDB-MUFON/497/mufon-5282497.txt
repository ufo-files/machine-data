Multiple blinking lights mirrored our flight path from MSP to LAX

A lady sitting next to me was capturing video out of her window on Spirit flight NK956. At around 0200 hrs her phone picked up various blinking lights off of the left wing of the plane. One blinking light appears and the others, it is unclear if it is one object with multiple lights or multiple objects. We made every attempt to ensure that the lights were not being reflected from inside the cabin of the plane. At one point there may be up to seven lights. All seem to blink randomly and independent of each other. The lights stay with us for about at least an hour and a half. Video was taken with and iPhone 12. The lights were not registered by either the naked eye or my Samsung S9+. She took a total of 15 different videos throughout the flight and the lights remained in approximately the same point in space for the duration.
