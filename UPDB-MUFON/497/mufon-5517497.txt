{ck} Glowing orange sphere over Alum Creek Dam area after the Fireworks

Right after the fireworks, which I estimate was around 10 pm, at Alum Creek Dam, my family and I were in our boat motoring back to the dock and I looked up and noticed a glowing reddish-orange ball that seemed to float across the sky below the cloud cover. We listned to see if we could hear a motor or helicopter blades, but there was no sound. The object seemed to move at a good pace and the light was steady. We watched it until it disappeared into the cloud cover at the horizon. The orange glow was prominent when it was overhead, but not as much as it went farther away. It reminded me of the ISS with the way it moved, but the color and the fact it was below the clouds made it clear it was not ISS.
