Glowing light

I experienced the “white light” in Rendlesham Forest on various occasions. I have decided disclose the incidents that were experienced in the Forest, after so many years, due to the constant deniability from the various sources. I was stationed at Bentwaters AFB between the years 1975 and 1978 and was a “visitor” to the Forest on a regular basis. The incidents happened either in 1976 or 1977 (too long ago and don’t remember specifically.) i could be contacted for specifics.
