We seen a redi/orange orb

My buddy Brian called on the first week of October to do some Fall night fishing up at Bull'shead bay up at the Lackawanna State Park in PA..We got up there around 2am, the feeling was a bit weird and dead quiet not a sound at all and nothing was biting our bait. Then a red/orange orb came out of the lake less then 20ft from me. I asked Brian if he can see this and he said what the hell is it. I said I have no idea maybe swamp gas, he said yeah OK. Then I said to myself cool if you are a little different then us humans, please do something to prove that you are real. This orb went so fast and jumping all around, then was disappearing and showing up all over. This was going on for at least 20min or even longer. Brian got a little scared cause he screamed let's go now. I truly believe now.
