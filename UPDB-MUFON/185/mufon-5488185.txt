I was turning left from us hwy 19 turning onto Bonita Road. It was around 4:30 PM. I was waiting to turn and looked up to see a cylinder shaped white ufo. It was longer than an airplane and had no wings or anything that looked like an airplane. It was quite a distance away and was quite big and long. I noticed the object because the cloud moved and the object was stationary. I pulled into the Speedway and tried to get a picture but it was like the object just disappeared. It was no where to be found in the sky as it only took about 4-6 seconds to pull in and stop by hwy 19 at the corner.
