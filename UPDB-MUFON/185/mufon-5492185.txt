{jpg} A bright flash, that disappeared, then reappeared, less intense, and repeated, until I lost sight.

I was walking up my driveway, and I just happened to look right, over my shoulder, and into the air, I say the bright flash, and a streak of light left by the object, it quickly dimmed. It was a pale white color flash, and the glow left behind in the light streak was like the afterglow of a bright light in your vision. The streak disappeared. I thought that I had just witnessed a fireball. I said "Wow" My buddy says "What?" He had not seen or noticed the flash of Light. As I was telling him, the object reappeared on the same path as the streak of light. At first it was dim, but it increased in brightness, then quickly faded away. "Look" i said to my friend and pointed he looked, and sure enough, a second or two later, it came back. He saw it this time. The color had changed, the object was now a red/orange color, and not nearly as bright. This happened a total of five times, including the initial flash, each time it came back, it was harder to see it, then it finally was gone. This lasted 10-15 seconds. I first noticed it to my north, it was traveling, west to east.
