{cak} Two UFO's spotted last night. Second Object was moving extremely fast.

Two objects were spotted last night on the 7/14/2017 one after the other. The first object was seen travelling from the Eastern Sky to the Northern Sky in a straight-line or from my right to left. The Second object was seen immediately after the first and was travelling from the Northern Sky to the East or from my left to right.
