{jpg} Motionless bright orb that remained still and then either left abruptly or shrank

I was at work, outside on lunch break, when me and a few of my employees witnessed a motionless bright spherical object in the sky (west). The object stayed in place for about 9 minutes, with no flashing safety lights (which would be indicative of an airplane or helicopter), after that the object slowly moved to the right (north), and then the object either shrunk or it was exiting our thermosphere, until we could no longer see it. We also noticed that the object was bright, but the object did not emit any glare; as if it was "solid light."
