Childhood Encounter

This happened in 1975 in Snohomish WA. Other than my husband, no one knows this happened.

I was 4 years old and I was with my parents visiting some friends of theirs. It was the middle of the day. I was alone in their house when all of the sudden these 3 little creatures appeared. They were between 3.5 and 4 feet tall, black with rounded/circular heads. There wasn't anything scary about them, we actually were playing either tag or hide and seek. I hid behind the sofa and watched as they ran out of the front door. It appeared as if there was a portal in the front doorway.

I'm not sure if it's related to the fact that my grandfather saw aliens in a room at Roswell or that my mother and great-grandparents were followed by a UFO. It has me wondering if they follow families like that.
