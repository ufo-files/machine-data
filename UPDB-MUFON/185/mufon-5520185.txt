A jet fast flying object with single light making curve directional change

A friend and I were leaving my house driving down the street when I noticed a bright orange redish light moving north to south over lake simcoe it was a cloudy night and clearly visible but moving to fast to be a normal plane. We stopped and got out of the car to look at it better It flew in a straight path as it got further away it made a large right to left curving motion as if it was going to turn but never did. We were fascinated by the speed of the flying object and watched it fly out of site towards the south.
