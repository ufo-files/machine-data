I photographed the horizon three times in a row and a saucer of light appeared in each photo.

At around 11:42pm on July 30th 2020 I heard a noise outside and looked. It was a craft with a strange pattern of lights heading towards the horizon. Fifteen minutes later, at around midnight, after the craft was gone and the sound was gone, I grabbed my cell phone and went out to my front porch to photograph the horizon. I took three photos in a row of where the craft had gone, even though I saw nothing in the sky at the time. When I looked at the photos I saw a white saucer of light in each photo. It appeared to move back and forth on the horizon, within a minute.
