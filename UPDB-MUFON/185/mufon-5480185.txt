{rjl} Silent, solid round light, steady speed, ascended out of sight, turned yellowish to gold

On the evening of Tuesday October 18, 2016, on a starlit evening at 7:30 I was relaxing, doing my hobby of shining binoculars to watch airplanes using Flight Radar 24 on my iPad for reference. It's just something I enjoy, spotting planes. When suddenly out of the NW sky moving smoothly, quietly at what I surmise to be about 10,000 feet there was a solid bright white ball of light. It was eerily quiet, I grabbed my Bono's and could tell it was a solid object that seemed Aglo, fascinated and trying to ascertain just what it was, I watched it for nearly 5 minutes. It seemed to float at a steady speed toward the South East, and then began to methodically ascend, while turning a shade of yellow to gold, it continued to move away and up until it became a dot heading out into space. There were many airplanes visible at this time all around in different directions mostly coming out if going in to PHL. It was the most unique thing ever witnessed, and for me at least it truly was an unidentifed flying object.
