{td} Chrome disc with dull silver-gray bottom silently heading in one direction, then changing directions, reflecting sunlight at all times

12:35 pm, walking through a nearby neighborhood, sparkling silver disc or orb (looked like a dull gray bottom) flying silently overhead area, west to east, no sound. A business jet even farther overhead flew SW direction to Centennial and I could hear that jet fine. Object was below jet. It sparkled in sunlight but wouldn't stop sparkling, which is really what caught my attention. Sparkled continuously (nearly cloudless bright-sunshine day) as it flew ENE (maybe toward Buckley AFB area?) but sort of curved north then curved back south, and then I lost it behind the trees. Never once stopped reflecting sparkly sunlight. Was very curious and intrigued, not overly excited, as I'm a natural skeptic, but it defied a few natural things I expected (namely noise and stopped-reflections). My first reaction was UFO though. Smooth surface, strange gray color on bottom, unable to distinctly decipher the shape, despite watching and noticing planes overhead every day otherwise.
