TicTac

Looked up and saw large Tictac in sky. No sound. Close. Large. Continuous row of large bright white lights along side. Moving fairly slowly offshore towards Cozumel island. I was stunned. Went inside to draw what I saw. Wrote date but I wrote Sunday Feb 17 so not sure if Sunday or Monday. Told my husband and he laughed. There may have been a few wisps of vapor coming off it in places.
