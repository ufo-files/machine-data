TRIANGLE UFO OVER PEARL HARBOR, DEC 6 1941 
youtube title - Japanese Planes Bomb Pearl Harbor, USS Arizona Explodes & Sinks

This clip – about three minutes long shows some of the Dec. 7 attack on Pearl harbor.

Stop clip at 2:30. Slightly below and slightly right of center are two flak bursts.

Immediately to the left and about half the distance between the two flak bursts is a triangle UFO.

If the UFO was the size of a 747, looks like its at about 10 thousand feet flying straight towards the camera.

Each individual frame of the UFO is hard to see, so starting at 2:30 carefully watch the area described, and start the clip, the UFO travels in a straight line up the screen until the clip cuts to another scene around 2:35. 

The UFO flashes a white strobe after several seconds.
