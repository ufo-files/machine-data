I DID SLOW DOWN RIGHT BEFORE THE SCHOOL ON THE RIGHT ON EL D0RADO DR. I SLOWED TO ALMOST A STOP,BUT KEPT MOVING WITH THE WINDOW DOWN. I DID NOT HEAR ANY SOUND. I TOO WAS WONDERING IS THIS A NEW LIGHT TOWER? OR WHAT CLOUD IT BE. IT MOVED SLOW TO ALMOST A HOOVER THEN SEEM TO KEEP MOVING NORTH EAST WARD. I WAS NOT THE ONLY ONE SLOWING DOWN I SAW TWO OTHERS JUST SLOWING AS I WAS. I DID NOT SEE AN OBJECT JUST A GROUPING OF LIGHTS THAT SEEM TO BE CONNECTED SOME WAY AS PART OF AN OBJECT. IF YOU LOOK AT GOOGLE MAPS FROM WHERE THE OBJECT OR LIGHTS IN A GROUP MOVING WAS AND MOVING TO IS A LINE TOWARD ELLINGTON FIELD (AIR FORCE BASE).
