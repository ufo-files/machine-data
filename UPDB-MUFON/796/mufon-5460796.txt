White light is seen floating and moving as fast as light

Sorry for my bad English, but I'm trying to tell the story 9 years ago in my home in Indonesia. The first time I saw this white light from the south, from the beginning I'm already suspicious because the light did not flicker like a commercial airplane. So I decided to give signal using my flashlight to the strange light. Shortly thereafter, the light floated above the four of us. When floating it was long enough, and at that time I also still gave a flashlight signal to that strange light. But soon after, my younger brother and mother was yelling at me, so I just obeyed and not gave that strange light signal again. The full story I made in this documentation here (in Bahasa Indonesia).
