{cak} They Looked like stars

Standing on apt. Balcony looking at stars and then one moved by 20 feet and stopped. A second star like object moved 25 feet alomgside the first object. They remained stationery for 
5-10 minutes then simultaneously flew away not super sonic, but slower than a falling star and appeared steady and intelligently steared. There was no sound, glowed golden colored and looked exactly like a star...but clearly wasn't a star.
