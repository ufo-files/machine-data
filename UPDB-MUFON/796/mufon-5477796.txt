My husband was driving and I was in the passenger seat we were headed to my parents home we drove down from Columbus, OH. We were headed west on route 348 we were around Duck Run Rd when we both seen it at the same time. It was a crystal clear night around 7:30pm. There was stars in sky and at first it showed up out of nowhere like the North Star it was brighter then the other stars then out of nowhere it moved and got closer a lot closer and so big bright, so very bright big white light then another one appeared right beside the first one, then another one, then another one and another one. They were so big bright white light almost Round to then an oval. then they would move all around close together sometimes in a straight pattern then it was like they would take turns disappearing then reappearing all over that one area of sky. At first when we saw them they just hovered above then started disappearing and reappearing moving all around. There had to be at least 8 of them. I was scared to death I quit looking at them I made my husband pull over at the dollar general store about 1mile up 348 and I went inside the store. When I came back out my husband said you can't see them anymore.
