Bright light/object north-northwest from Bay Area skies

Saw this last week and again tonight - a single bright object in SF Bay Area skies to the north-northwest as viewed from San Ramon CA. Tried to look at it with binoculars, which were difficult to steady. Object was moving north or northeast, so it became lower on the horizon as I watched. Light was too bright and moved too fast to be a star. If I had to narrow down the description, Id say I was watching flames from the back of a rocket of some kind - bright white to yellowish white, but red at the trailing end. If it was flames from a rocket, I could not see the rocket itself. Last weeks sighting was at about 9 PM and it was farther west; tonight around 10 PM it was farther north. The International Space Station does occasionally pass overhead, but it only stays visible for a few minutes at a time, so this object was not the ISS. Never saw anything in the newspapers last week about such a sighting.
