Two separate, sustained beams of light, projected at my vehicle, seconds apart, three people in vehicle witnesed

Two out of the three people in my vehicle were fulltime city firefighters. Third was a friend. We went on fishing trips every year. The year of the encounter, we were fishing Stoney Lake, Ontario, Canada, just outside of Peterborough. We were on our way back to the lodge at 11pm-12am. Calm, windless, clear, warm, summer night. Heading down a pitch black backroad with no streetlights. As vehicle was moving at approx 60-70 km/hr, a light from above was projected over the vehicle, for about three seconds. All three of us noticed, but remained silent and continued driving. What seemed to be 10-20 seconds later, the light projected over the vehicle again, for the same duration, then went out. We skidded to a stop and were confused, afraid, and shocked at what just happened. We looked at eachother as if we had seen a ghost. Out of the three o us, I am the only one willing to openly admit to what we experienced.
