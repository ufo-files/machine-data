{dr}Two yellow lights moving in tandem, out of atmosphere, with no blinking lights or sound. Second sighting red in color.

A friend and I decided to walk from my apartment to Woodward ave to watch fireworks. Halfway to the st. We noticed one then two constant lights in the sky moving in tandem upwards and away at a regular rate of speed. We stopped and observed and discussed what the objects were, and could not make a solid conclusion. We continued in discussion of the objects to the end of the st. Watching surface to air fireworks. Ten minutes approximately, we noticed a red light moving in the opposite direction followed by on other as e had seen earlier. Instead they were moving north east to south east same trajectory speed but with a red hue. We both agreed it wasnt anything we
had seen before. And continued to discuss our own past sightings.
