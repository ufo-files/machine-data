Bright White Light in what looked like a a cloud.

I was outside in the evening when I saw a bright, white light in what looked to be moving through thick, dense fog or through thick clouds. It caught my eye due to it's strange nature. There were very little clouds in the area, stars were out shining around the object. There was a small airplane that flew from the West to East over my house during this event. The object was moving slower than an airplane would. I grabbed my spouse to see what I was seeing. We couldn't figure out what we were seeing! I knew it waa something I'd never seen before. As it moved South, the bright, white light was gone, but the glow and cloud/fog was still there. It then disappeared or I lost sight of it. I took 1 photo at the end of the event before it disappeared. Not the best photo.
