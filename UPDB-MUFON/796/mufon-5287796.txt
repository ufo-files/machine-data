4 or 5 balls circled around each other and vanished.

My mom called me into the backyard when I was about 13/14 and she sounded super frantic. When I got to the back yard I looked up and there were 4 or 5 bright reddish orange balls of what looked like fire in a straight horizontal light. They began to slowly doing almost figure 8 like motions and then started to erratically move around in no pattern or maneuver. After about 45 seconds or so they lined up and just *poof* they were gone as soon as they appeared.
