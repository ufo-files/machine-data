4 persons observed for 20 minutes an object with diferent lights and colors flying in diferent directions, the speed and directions taken were amazing.
I was playing video game in the first floor when my wife cried me from the second and we saw a object with multiple lights up and down, suddenly stopped and after a few minutes flight on horizontal (south-north)direction very quick, stopped again for a few minutes when i called the next door house and the security of the street to observe the event
The object start flying again in diferent directions, changing lights, sometime it desapeared and show up again
It suddenly came back to the first position we have saw and then disappeared getting down behind a tree 
When the object first moved horizontaly, a never sound before of static came strongly from my baby monitor
When the object disappeared behind the tree i took my bycecle and run after it when i observed another 3 person observing it from another (better) positioning, they attested the same thing i did and as they were in the margin of a lake the could attest that the object lights reflected in the lake water
No one could attest the form of the object because the lights were too strong and do not allow the object to be seen
