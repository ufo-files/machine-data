{tjd} Redish orange upright triangular shaped craft hovering.

At 4:05 A.M. central time my dog woke me up barking. I took her out to use the restroom and there was an upright triangular shaped craft hovering over my neighbors house. It was very low. It had a red/orange hue to it with many lights that glowed orange. I also observed windows (port holes) and even a hatch. It had a dome with something sticking up from the top. It disturbed the air around it. I went to take my dog in so I could get better pitcures and it zipped off toward the NW. In the pic the light on the bottom left is my neighbors streetlight on his barn. The object is up and to the right of that. My camera did not show it's true colors and it was a bit foggy and cloudy out. The craft was well below the cloud deck
