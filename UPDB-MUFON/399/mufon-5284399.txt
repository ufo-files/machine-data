Bright object with subsequent being photographed

Driving from Calgary to Lake Louise Alberta Oct 23 approx 11:30 am. I was in passenger seat. Noticed very bright object near moon. It was brighter than the moon. Thought it was an airplane reflecting the sun. Looked down to get my camera and when I looked up it was gone. Scoured the area for any signs of an aircraft. None to be found.

Hours later at Lake Louise was taking random pics. Took pic of leaning curved tree. No one was in the shot when I took it. Upon later reviewing the pic, noticed an white smudge in the image. Zoomed in to clean it up and realized that it looked like an astronaut in the water at the shore line. Appears to be thigh deep in the water except the water at that location was just over ankle deep. No other pics from that shoot showed any anomalies.

I did not manipulate this image in any way other than cropping the larger image to put focus on the tree.
