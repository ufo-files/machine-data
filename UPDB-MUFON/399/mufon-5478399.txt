{jpg} I was going to my bus stop in the morning and I filmed an object in the sky.

It was 01/25/17 at 7:33 a.m. eastern time. My son Travis was leaving our house to go to his bus stop in the morning. When he got to the sidewalk between our house and our neighbor's house, he saw a spherical reflective object dancing among the clouds. The sun was shining. The conditions were bright and clear. He took out his cell phone and filmed this object for thirty-six seconds before it disappeared behind a cloud.
