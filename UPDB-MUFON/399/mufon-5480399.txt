I was about 6 or 7 I am now 28 years old I'll never forget this night I was with my grandmother and my mother on my way to my nighttime communion class as we approach my mothers car this black object shape like a disc came out from the north so fast it hovered over me with no noise and I wasnt even afraid i felt like it was just observing me I just looked up and it had white lights on the bottom of it going in a circular motion so fast i was speechless I stood there for three minutes looking at it then it left like the speed of light towards the West I was confused of what I saw because I thought it was a weird shape plane so I asked my mother what was that?and she looks at my grandma with a surprised and scared face and says let's go get in the car it was so quiet the whole car ride to church i remember being in class and feeling so tired and slow the whole time we never talked about it again I tried bringing it up so many times a month after what we saw to them but they kept telling me to change the subject I remember bringing it up once when I was in 6th grade to a group of friends that I had and they thought it was crazy and they all laughed so I never brought it up again then I saw the show so I decided to write you guys to see if you guys have any records of sightings in the years 1995-1997 in white plains NY.
