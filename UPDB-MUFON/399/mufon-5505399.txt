{cak} First saw two bright glowing objects comming from the East to West then saw 3 more comming in opposite direction and they seemed to merge into a V.

My father and myself were on the way to the store and upon getting into the car we looked up and saw two glowing objects coming from the East and headed West. We called my mother to come and take a look. The Best way to describe the objects is a bubble of fire. These objects were moving slowly. My father and I continued in the car to the store and saw 3 more of the same objects coming in the opposite direction. From the store we saw the 3 objects merge with the other two which formed a V formation. Once the V was formed it appeared to have six of the glowing objects making the V. We also observed a helicopter hovering and looking in the same direction of the objects. There were also people stopping in their cars to watch. We watched the objects for about 9 minutes, went into the store and when we returned, they were gone.
