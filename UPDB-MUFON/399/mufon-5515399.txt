{nd} starlike but larger than star, ruddy red, twinkling light, night time, 8-10 units

Petaluma
Sonoma county
California
Sept 22, 2012
7:45pm

Traveling south on 101, just past the truck pull off area on the hill between Petaluma and Rhonert Park just dark. On the Petaluma side.

We pulled off the highway to watch. Red Honda Civic in case anybody saw us.

We saw 5 lights, in a generally straight line and roughly equidistant.

They were a light, muddy red color, 2-3x larger than a planet in the sky and noticeably twinkling. Twinkling a bit too much for a lower altitude "light".

When we first noticed them, the furthest away and lowest light was at our right and just above the hills on the coast hills on the west side of the north Petaluma farmland, about 6 miles away, perhaps near and above the Two Rock Coast Guard station / Tracen Petaluma (707) 765-7000. Maybe at 500-1000 ft above the ground.

The closest light to us was higher than the one to the right and to the south and in front of us.
Equidistant.

Then, 3 formed a loose group up high and winked out in front of us. The other 2 traveled towards where the loose group was and winked out, one by one, in about the same area, at maybe 6000 to 10,000 ft. They stayed about the same size, maybe a little larger as they traveled from our right, to right ahead of and above us.
After the first 3 disappeared and the other two started traveling in the same direction, 2-3 other new identical "lights" winked into existence low over the hills to our right, roughly equally spaced and followed the others to the same general area and winked out.

We tried to call Petaluma Tracen. Not open. 

It wasnt airplanes or helicopters - no red beacon or red/green nav lights.
They moved too quickly to be balloons and too slow for jets and who ever heard of jets forming a sloppy 3 unit group in 6 miles?
And, no matter which direction these objects were traveling (it seemed that their direction varied slightly), their brightness remained consistent.

It wasnt high altitude objects with the setting sun reflecting on it, because we were on the wrong side for a reflection.
