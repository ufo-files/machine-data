{jpg} Was traveling in car. It was hovering in the sky. Looked like two circles with a dark spot in middle. Disappeared ?

I was in the passengers seat, riding home from a track and field event (5/23/15) at shippensburg university. Looked off to my right and saw what thought might be a plane but quickly realized it wasn't moving and it looked odd. My witness was driving and couldn't make it out, said they did see something was there. It looked like two circle with a dark spot on the middle. I knew immediately that it was something unworldly(my opinion) and took out my iPhone and took 4 pictures. A tree was in the way when I snapped the third picture and can't make it out from behind it. After the fourth picture I changed over to video and hit record. There were trees in the way again but a few second later the field opened up again and I could clearly see nothing. It was gone from the area.(I'm sorry for any profanity on the video buy it is important to me to let others know that the pics and video are only a second or two apart.) that it really. After I got home i blew up some of the pictures and even ran the best one thru some filters. The filter pic I think clearly shows better definition of the object(s). But my eyes saw it better than my iPhone camera could. It was too faraway to get a good pic but I believe it's one of the best I've seen!!!
