(GS) It was triangular, silent, low flying, lit up at the 3 points and flew very fast.

My daughter and I went to get her backpack out of the car. It was dark out. My daughter, who was 4 at the time, said, "Mommy, whats that?" and pointed up. I looked up and saw a large triangular shaped object slowly moving towards us. It was low flying and completely silent. It flew over a football field that was right across the road and it was about 1/3 the size of it. It hovered just above the tree tops, right above us for about 2 minutes. We just stood there and watched it. We felt no fear, just curiosity. Then it zipped over to the football field (the direction it came from), made a right angle turn and flew SUPER fast northward and abruptly disappeared after it was maybe a block away. It had solid amber, red and greed lights at its points. It was completely silent the entire time and it left no jet trails or emissions that we could see or smell.
