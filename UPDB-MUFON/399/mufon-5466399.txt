So I was sitting in car around 6:30 on my phone.
It was dusk, just becoming light.
I couldn't get off the phone quick enough as I saw this light traveling
across the sky, which appeared to have come from the west coast, the 
ocean is a few KM's away from where I live(West Coast of Western Australia)
So I quickly started phone camera(Samsung Galaxy S5) & filmed it for
a few minutes, it traveled in easterly direction, making no noise at all.
There is an airport just out of town & I hear them flying most days 
very clearly, however this light object made NO noise at all.
It disappeared into smallish cloud & came out other side, then
I followed it as it moved into more cloud cover & never came out
other side as I waited to see it. Strange.
I copied video from phone to laptop, it is raw & completely unedited.
