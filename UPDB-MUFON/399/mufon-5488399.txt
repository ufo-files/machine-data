{nd} Two white rectangular stationary objects hovering in evening sky

Each evening between 10/5/15 and 10/7/15 I was driving home from work and was within a half mile of my house when I observed two white rectangular objects spaced some distance apart. They were both at the same altitude approximately 25 degrees above the eastern horizon. They caught my attention because even though it was cloudy each night, they were prominent objects. I originally thought they were airplanes or blimps, but they did not have their characteristic shape after longer observation. They did not move during the time that I observed them, and they appeared to be in the same location each evening.  I was very nervous by their sighting and have not seen any local news indicating they were easy explained by a human made object. I only saw them when I was high enough to see over the tree line.
