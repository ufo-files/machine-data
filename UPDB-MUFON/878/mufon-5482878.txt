{cak} Bright light seen in western sky early morning

Early morning just before the sun rose I observed a light that looked similar to a commercial passenger plane with it's landing light on over the western horizon. The object seemed to hover or appear as a commercial passenger plane does as it approaches for landing; for several moments before dimming to a star like brightness. The object started to ascend into the sky. It traveled on an upward path slowly for only a few seconds before shooting straight up as fast as a bullet. It then appeared to travel straight into space as the object did not traverse the atmosphere as an aircraft at very high altitude would. The sky was still dark enough to make out stars in constellations. Seeing the object shoot out of sight into orbit as fast as it did I followed it with my eye till I could not see it any more. I've grown up seeing many shuttle and rocket launches to know that this was not a conventional aerial object but something unexplainable in terms of modern known crafts.
