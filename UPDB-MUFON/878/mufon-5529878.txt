The UFO was shiny white

I was driving south on the 5 freeway coming over the 5/14 connector and SE of me I saw a shiny white circular object in the sky. It was moving slowly east and as I watch the sky all the time I was waiting for a sign to debunch it. But it was moving slower than a plane could and slower than a helicopter normal moves. This lasted for about 15 seconds. It seemed to be traveling toward the strange cloud formations that had produced flash thunder storms over the last day. The UFO however never went behind the clouds and stayed within the open, clear sky. So I watched it the entire time then out of no where it seemed to flash away to nothing. There one second gone the next. I tried searching for it in the sky but could not find anything. This was pretty cool and wild at the same time. This doesn't make any sense to me so I am pretty certain it was a UFO.
