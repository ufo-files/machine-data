Large orange lights appeared one by one

A very scary moment for me. I went out the back door about 8:40 PM to go to the barn. We live in a mountain area and have a beautiful view of the night sky. No lights anywhere to block the stars. As I went down the stairs and headed for my truck about 20 ft away I glanced up at the eastern sky. It is a clear night and the stars are numerous. All of a sudden a bright orange light appeared in the sky. Then spaced a good distance from the first, another orange light appeared, then another and another..I was froozen. Then after so many seconds, not sure how long, the # 4 light went out, then the #3, 2 & 1. Just the opposite of how they appeared. It scared the crap out of me and I ran in the house and told my husband and we stood on the back porch in the darkness. As I told him all I had seen I pushed record on my phone, just incase and I pointed it at the sky in front of us which would be to the south. As we are talking an orange light appeared in the sky much lower than the first ones I saw. It was only there briefly then disappeared with no other lights to be seen. We both saw it and I saw it recording on my phone. For some reason I haven't been able to get the video to download to my pc or I would have added it. The thing is it is a brief showing of red. Then something we did not see showed up on the video, it is quick but twice more it shows up more red than orange and zooms by the camera. Also at the very beginning of video there is a pinpoint of light doing weird movements. We have both seen a UFO years ago in the desert and that is another crazy experience but that one didn't scare me near as much as this one. I am still shook up. I will be glad to share video as soon as I can get it to download.
