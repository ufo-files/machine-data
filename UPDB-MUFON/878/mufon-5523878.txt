Moving red light being chased by plane (parallel). Witnessed what looked like a meteor (light streak) come from left went into red light. Light continued on.

9:20 pm, looked out my kitchen sliding glass door towards the South-West and saw a unwavering steady-on red light in the sky, about airplane altitude, moving slowly towards the South-West, being paralleled by a plane with a flashing beacon light on it. The plane appeared to be approximately 1000 feet from the red light and followed along the entire time I watched. When I saw them, they were several miles away from my location. As i watched the red light, what looked like a meteor, like a streak of yellow-orange light, came from the left out of the dark sky, again like a meteor, and ended at the red light. The path of the "meteor" light did not waver. It was straight into the red light. I told a friend who was with me to look at it, and he saw the red light but was too late to see the light streak. We watched them slowly head S-W for approximately 5 more minutes until both the red light and the plane went below the roof lines of the near-by houses.
