{nd} I noticed two orange globes from bedroom window.husband and i went outside and saw more. there were 6-7 orangey globes. they lasted for about 5 to 7 mins.

It was about 7:15-7:20 in the evening, Jan, 6th, 2015 .I was laying on our bed, curtains were open although it was dark outside. I noticed two orange amber colored lights from the direction of the mountains. The Santa Rita Range in the east. At first I thought there were two large fires up by the Wrightson observatory. So I got up and went outside where I noticed two more orbs. I called to my husband to come out and see. By the time he got outside there were 6 to 7 of them still by the mountains. Some were stationary (hovering) and some moved slowly. I felt highly agitated and restless. I ran around to the front yard to see if I could see more and maybe an even better view, but couldn't, so ran back into the backyard where my husband and I watched them for a couple minutes. Then suddenly they were gone but they didn't fade out, it seemed they blinked out, but I do NOT recall seeing the actual blinking out or on of them. They were just there and then not. It felt like a skip, like I missed that action of them appearing and disappearing. This left me feeling strange and disoriented. It still does. The following evening we went outside at the same time just in case we saw it again, but we saw nothing.
