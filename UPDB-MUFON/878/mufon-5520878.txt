5 flying objects flashing red within 10 mins on 1/2/12 at 6.22

I was in my sisters room on the phone to my boyfriend looking out at the pitch black sky when a red flashing light caught my eye above the houses oppisite. It was 6.22pm on the 1/2/12. When i first saw the flying object I thought it was a plane because it seemed to be in the same flight path as planes when they fly past. I waited to see the green light when it turned a different angle but it didn't. It stayed flashing red. I called my sister up to have a look when she noticed there was another red flashing object around the same height above some houses a little bit further along. It started working its way along towards the other red flashing light at a smooth speed. Then the first light flashed two red lights at the same speed as before. We both went downstairs and told our mum and dad when we were explaining it came back around again. We all rushed upstairs turned the lights off so we could see it better and we noticed 3 red flashing objects hovering in the sky this time, working there way along the same path as before. This time there was some sort of plane in the sky. This could be a possible witness as it wasn't far from the objects. The first red flashing object started flashing two red lights again as the other two made there way along flashing just one. Eventually the went out of sight and did not manage to see them again.
