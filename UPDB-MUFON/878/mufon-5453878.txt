{cak} Thought it was a satellite but they just kept coming. Evenly spaced witnessed at least 25 over a 25 minute timespan. Was standing on balcony.

Standing on balcony of my apartment looking at the alignment of Mars, Jupiter and Saturn with a half moon underneath the three planets. I Saw what I thought was a satellite coming out of the North, Western sky going South towards San Diego. Then another appeared and another and another. At least 20 of them Moving slowly, evenly spaced traveled this pattern. Then another 5 came from the West to South East with the same same spacing. They looked like satellites moving gracefully through the sky. There were no clouds and no other moving objects in the sky during the twenty five minutes we watched them. This happened during the Covid 19 Stay at Home Mandate so very little traffic in the sky or on the ground, so the sky was extremely clear and the three planets Mars, Jupiter and Saturn all in alignment with the moon underneath was just a stunning sight. About three or four minutes after we saw the last one we saw a plane traveling West to East.
