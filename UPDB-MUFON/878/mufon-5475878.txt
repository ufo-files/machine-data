{cak} Strange light. Flew sporadic. After passing about 50 feet under plane it disappeared.

It was 6:20 in the morning and I was walking home from a friend's house. I always just stare at the sky and think while walking. I saw a strange orb of light moving sporadically and tried to record it. However, it would not show up on my camera. I watched it for about 30 more seconds before I saw one of those planes that produce trails heading for it. The Unidentifiable Object flew about 50-100 feet below it and right as they were lined up, disappeared.
