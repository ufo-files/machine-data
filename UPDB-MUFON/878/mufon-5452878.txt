white and green light observed

I had been told by my Mother in law who lives about 1 mile south from me that her and her neighbour had observed a white light and a green light over the past few days at the hours of 10pm to 1045pm so i went out and saw to the left of object what i think was Venus or a bright star at about 45 degress to north...then i saw a a real bright light as described hovering... i ran indoors picked up my camera set it at 1-10th second f5.6 iso 6400 and fired off 6 shots ...i enclose the first 5 as the sixth was black and the object was gone ...i have never previously before seen anything like this...I have had an open mind on this subject but firmly believe this is an unidentified aerial phenomenon...no one I know since this date has locally seen this again the object changes orientation then shape then flies of to north  the right in the picture in a blink of an eye
