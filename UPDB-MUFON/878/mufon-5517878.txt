{nd} Globe Like Orange Molten Orb

Appeared out of nowhere from the South as I was sitting in my familyroom with my husband watching TV. I looked out our windows and up out at the sky and my attention was immediately drawn to this globe like Bright Orange Molten light. I knew it was not a plane, no plane I had ever seen glowed bright orange! I turned to my husband and said "did you see that?" We both jumped up from where we were sitting and ran out our front door into our front yard. The Orange Fireball Orb had made its NE direction change over our house and was moving away from us, faster than any plane we had ever seen.There was also a plane heading South. We knew it was a plane with the sound of the engine and lights associated with planes. We see lots of airplane traffic since we are not far from Port Columbus.Our encounter lasted approximately 3-3 1/2 minutes. We watched it disappear until you couldnt see it anymore. We were in awe during our sighting, my husband and I have wondered if it wasnt meant for us to have seen something so awesome.
