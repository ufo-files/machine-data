Disc triangle shape was hovering about 60 feet away above power lines

In the summer time 1969 around 8:30 pm a Disc shaped craft , it had a upper /top with windows and was hovering about 60 feet away above power lines, My mother and I both witnessed the UFO, we both were standing on 2nd story porch of our apartment ,that was built above our family business ( second story level) , we were almost level with the UFO , the craft just hovered for a long time the windows seemed to disappear after a while into the metalic dark surface, after we both saw what appeared to be a human like shape staring back at us. we both felt panic and fear at that moment as if they knew we saw them. I had the feeling of communicating with my mother, but we never physically spoke out loud,as if we knew what the other was thinking. there was a big time gap of about an 45 minutes. then my mother and I both watched the craft take off rapidly and as it did a brief turn it seemed triangle in shape with glowing lights and with a upward SW thrust it just disappeared into the sky. My mother and I never spoke about it after that night. although we felt it and somehow mentally acknowledged each other.
