I was standing in my backyard in North Medford, Oregon when a flash at about 60 degrees in the Southwest caught my eye. At first I thought I'd seen a lost mylar balloon but as I focused on the area I realized there were at least a dozen lights in a V formation. My sister and husband were outside with me to also witness these lights (my six year old came outside during the 10 minutes we were able to see them). As we watched I called my grandma to tell her about it. During the 10 minute conversation the lights moved out of a V formation and began to move slowly but in no formation to the East. Sometimes one or more would spread away from the cluster, but they all seemed to be heading in the same general direction. At about 5 minutes in is when I saw one or two flashes of red instead of the just white lights I'd been seeing. The flashes of light from the objects didn't seem regular. There was some slight cloud cover and once they were behind the cloud they failed to reappear although we watched for awhile.
