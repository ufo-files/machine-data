{cak} Pulsating lights, in and out from darkness

pulsating slowly. phases in from darkness to very bright, then fizzles out. lasts 10-30 seconds, and sometimes changes locations. Seemed to have been moving slowly at the beginning, but after 20 mins it has been stationary. Clouds in the area, and no stars are visible in that direction. I work nightshift outdoors and I am constantly looking towards the stars. This is the 3rd sighting like this in March. The first light caught my eye off horizon. It was very bright and in front of clouds. It seemed stationary for 5 minutes and then slowly disappeared. A few minutes later, about 5 degrees higher and to the east, another light was visible. This light "came on and off" for over 30 minutes, only changing position slightly.
