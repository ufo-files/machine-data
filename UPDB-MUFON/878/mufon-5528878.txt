(sg) brightly lit ball of light descended then disappeared.

I was heading home from my son house in mullica hill when I saw a brightly lit ball in the eastern sky. I was heading east on harrison road and I made a right hand turn onto Clems Run road. I then noticed the object out my drivers side window. The sky was clear and I could see many planes flying about in the distance. The object was about the size of a dime held at arms length. The object stayed stationary then suddenly decended and vanished.
