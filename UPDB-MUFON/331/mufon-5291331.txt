Window shaped object in sky

I was driving W/SW on TX 130 South about 31 miles north of I10. It was partially cloudy with a lot of blue sky. I saw a rectangle up in the sky at about 50 degrees up and about 10 degrees to the right. The sun was about 20 degrees more to the right. The rectangle was a perfect rectangle with good 90 degree corners and straight lines for the sides. It looked like a window in the sky. It was grayish and did not move. When a light cloud passed in front of it, it then seemed to glow very bright white thru the cloud. A few seconds later a darker cloud passed in front of it and it disappeared. With the wavy road and the fact of driving an RV at 75 miles per hour I lost track of it. There were 3 other people in the car and I yelled at them to look but they were all asleep.

It reminded me of a scene in the movie "The Truman Show" where he lived in a dome that looked like the sky and a panel fell off leaving a rectangular hole in the sky. I have watched planes for years and have never seen anything like this before.
