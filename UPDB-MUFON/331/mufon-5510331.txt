(GS) MOVING FAST DESCENDED FAST AND MOVED OVER HORIZON

just stepped out for lunch. fast moving and reflecting the sun. first thought was fighter jet headed to mitchel airport. Object was moving at very fast rate then descended sharply without changing attitude pitch or inverting before dive. I was sure, wish i had time to get my binoculars. object moved over horizon. I was south and east of object with noon sun high overhead which gave this object the reflective look. If we don't report this stuff we will never know.
