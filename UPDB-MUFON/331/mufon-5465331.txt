2 objects that was not visible by human eye, just through mobile camera was hovering for hours

As astronomer amateur, I was filming sky in Podgorica, Montenegro, Europe, on 27th April 201/8. During filming of sunset with timelaps app over mobile phone camera and stationary kit for filming, I directly saw 2 objects on SW of my filming spot. As the mobile was in front of me stated on the filming kit, I saw on the screen 2 objects hovering on the phone, which I cound see with my eye. Than I took another mobile phone, and turn camera, and I saw these 2 objects also on the mobile phone. As I saw throuth telescope ( Skywatcher 90/900) in that direction trying to find in on the sky, I couldnt, there was nothing. Than I started Stellarium program to see is there any planet/star, either almost every night we are watching Venus, Jupiter, Saturn, Mars, over the summer nights from our position in Podgorica. Either I know that on the right side is Venus, those 2 objects were not on the Stellarium either. Look the video which is recorded and stellarium sky over Podgorica on that night.
