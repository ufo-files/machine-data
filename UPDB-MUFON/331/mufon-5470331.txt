Fast silent very bright lights

Around 9/45 pm on 17 January 2018 I saw six UFOs flying in formation from south west to north east. Very bright white lights very fast and no sound. They stretched apart horizontal then came together then stretched vertical came back together again.1 Then sped off at a very fast speed and vanished into a a hole in the sky a pale blue light circle appeared and the white lights disappeared through the circle directly in front of my line of sight.The event was all over in about 6 seconds.
