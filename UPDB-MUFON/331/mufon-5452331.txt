Dark Spherical Object

While swimming in the pool, I just happened to look up at what I first thought was an airplane. But as I kept looking at it, it bagan to slow down? I was thinking to myself, why is this plane slowing down? How is this plane slowing dow? Then it suddenly started going in the opposit direction. I was a bit in shock... so much so that I didn't even think to go to get my camera. The object slowly flewinto some clouds. Finally I did get out of the pool to get my camera. When I came back outside, I started taking pictures of the area of the direction that I saw this object heading. Even though I no longer saw the object, I still took pictures of where I last saw it.

Today, 6/7/20, I just happened to start reviewing the pictures that I had taken... in the pics I saw I dark orb, what I believe to be some sort of unidentified craft, up in the clouds. This object moved between three locations within the clouds. At the time I did not see this object what so ever.

It just looks weird to me and wanted to share my experience. I have uploaded the photos for your review. I did not point out the object in the pictures to see if you can see what I see. If you notice, the cloud formations are all the same because 2 pics were taken within a minute and the third 2 minutes later and the object was still there.
