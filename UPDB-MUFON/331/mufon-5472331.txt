{cak} A triangle ship like thing on the sky in the middle of the dessert in ship rock NM construction site.

Friday November 2017 
Shiprock New Mexico 

It was about 2pm and I saw in the sky what looked like a triangle flying object in the sky. It had like trail of flames in the sky. It fell like a shooting star that's how it fell it disappear really fast that I couldn't get my phone out to take a picture. I work in a construction site in the middle of the dessert. I went home and told my daughter I sat and explain to her what I had saw. She quickly pulled out a picture of the Phoenix lights of 1997. She showed me a picture of the triangle object.
