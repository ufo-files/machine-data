3 bright hovering/moving lights

11 pm saw 3 extremely large very bright lights in the sky to the southwest. Appeared to be spherical in shape, bright white light. The 3 lights seemed to hover for a period of time, then one dropped lower and out of view. The 2 remaining lights moved together, then apart again. Then one began to fade out while the last one remained. The last one moved around erratically and then vanished.
