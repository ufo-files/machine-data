(gs) We witnessed a large disc-like object that was stationary and shot red beams in the air and towards the ground

My wife and I were enjoying the evening air after a thunderstorm. The approximate time was 2203 hours. My wife noticed a bright light in the northwestern part of the sky. The sky was very clear and we were able to discern commercial aircraft at the 35000 elevation mark. The object possessed whitish/yellow lights that pulsated and disappeared intermittently. The object would re-appear every 20-30 seconds in the same spot and I was able to make out a disc like shape. The object then began to shoot out red and green beams on a ninety degree level and then shot multiple beams towards the ground. I then became concerned and phoned the local Sheriffs Department to have deputys observe the bizarre scene that we were witnessing. While waiting for the deputies to arrive, we estimated that the object was at least 400-600 feet in diameter. we then observed smaller lights traveling towards the larger object. It should also be noted that we observed no sound coming from the object. Two deputies arrived and observed the sky for a couple of minutes. The advised that the would drive to a spot with better visibility. The object appeared briefly and we returned inside. It should be noted that I am a military veteran who has had extensive training in observation. I also have worked in the law-enforcement/security field for the past three years. This is my second encounter with an unknown object in the sky, with the first encounter being much more detailed and closer.
