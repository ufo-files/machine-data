{jpg} Reddish-Orange Light Flying at Various Speeds Then Vanishing

I was walking with another witness when I looked up, facing SW, to see a reddish-orange light flying NW. I pointed it out to the other witness and they confirmed the sighting. The object seemed to change speeds as well, at one point appearing to slow down dramatically, almost to a stop, just to speed back up and dim out until it disappeared entirely. During the sighting, the object was far away and pretty high up as well, possibly higher than a typical airplane sighting.

The light seemed different enough from any usual airplane light to immediately catch my attention. We see many planes every night we walk so we know what they look like. As a matter of fact, we saw a plane immediately before the sighting, and at least 2 immediately planes after the sighting all at different altitudes.
