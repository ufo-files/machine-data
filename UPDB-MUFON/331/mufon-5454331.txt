I noticed two very bright lights on the horizon that appeared to be moving that caught my eye and I decided to capture them on my iphone. I am currently living on a cattle ranch in the state of Goias, Brazil. For the following 2 night March 26 and 27, I was able to see this strange object. On the 26, only one. On the 27th, two objects.
