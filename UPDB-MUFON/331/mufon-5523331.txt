2 sightings within 5 minutes- Starlike

My dad and I were in the car going down a somewhat busy street on a Friday night. I am always looking at the sky in hopes of seeing a UFO. Well I always end up seeing weird lights, and immediately classify those lights as jets/planes/stars/satellites. Well Friday night we were cruising down the road and I notice a star. I take a quick look at it, and see the bright starlight shutoff, and then the light came back on. As if it was pulsating or blinking. As we come to a stop, I realize it is moving pretty fast across the sky. I try to keep watch and tell my dad to pullover, he is not interested so we keep driving. I try to keep watch as trees and buildings pass by, but I lose sight of it. I finally convince him to pullover at a baseball field, as we turn down the street we come up to a round a bout. As we are circling the round about I see 1 star in the sky. Within about 2 seconds, I see that 1 star split into 2 stars and the second star descended rapidly, then ascended and came back together with the original star. Then once again it split into 2 stars and the bottom star descended and disappeared. So I told my dad to rush to the field, I get out of the car and quickly run out to observe the sky. I see nothing but stars and airplanes. I know I saw something, its just the timing was off. We didn't get to the field fast enough. As far as im concerned we never have UFO sightings in Raleigh.
