(GS) I saw these three lights looking like a very bright star, but moving toward the mountain, so i stopped to watch.

I was driving home from a friends house when i saw a very bright star in the sky. Then as i kept driving i noticed that it was moving. And it kept moving toward the mountain around where i live. So i turned down my street and stopped to see if i could hear anything or see blinking lights. I sat for around 5 minutes watching it as it remained silent and flying toward the mountain. I first noticed it because how bright it was and i love looking at the stars every night so it was out of the ordinary. I thought it was a star when i first saw it. I thought that UFOs didn't visit Georgia, but after tonight i have no doubt. Finally after being stopped at a stop sign watching the object with my car turned off, i lost it behind the trees, where i started my car to see if i could catch a better view around the hills. but i didn't.
