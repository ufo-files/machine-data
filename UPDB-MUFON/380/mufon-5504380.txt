{nd} Glowing orb traveling in between trees

Me and my wife went outside to close our animals down for the night and were standing at the edge of our newly cleared pasture . We were standing there talking when we saw a glowing orb at first thinking it was the neighbors with a flashlight . The distance was about 100 feet away or so very close and would put them on our property . But instantly noticed the orb itself and it was traveling about 20 mph south about 4 feet above the ground weaving in and out of the trees . When it would go behind a tree you could still see the light . We were both terrified and don't know what it was .
