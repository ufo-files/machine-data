{nd} Bright object that was headed towards me then changed direction and immediately shot up and out of atmosphere

At a bonfire around 3am when looked up towards the north to see a bright white light just over tree line coming toward me. At first thought it wasplanks flying plane but the object had no marker light just bright white and no sound. It immediately changed direction and speed and headed up and out of the atmosphere becoming smaller and glowing like a satellite until it disappeared within my view straight up out of the atmosphere. I immediately knew it performed impossible maneuvers and speeds for anything man made. Was also witnessed by a friend standing right next to me, neither one of us knew we were both looking at it in the moment. The object went from very close to out of the atmosphere and to far away to see within seconds.
