Dark large object hovering and moving slowly

It was approximately 6pm on Friday the 5th October 2018. I1d gone out of my back door to take out the rubbish. I looked up, I often do being a bit of a twitcher, a large dark object caught my eye. It appeared to be very high and initially it was difficult to tell if it was moving. At first I thought it was a paraglider. It was moving very slowly from a north east direction towards the east. I assumed it must be circling as it appeared to change shape as it slowly moved. I considered the possibility of it being a bird but it was far too big and didn1t ever flap its 1wings1 It was an overcast day and the object appeared very dark with a very faint halo of light surrounding it. I went inside and grabbed my binoculars which are to hand in the back office room. If with those I was unable to identify the object. At some points it looked similar to batman, with raggy wings. It seemed organic, not like any aircraft I1ve ever seen and it was moving far too slowly, I live close to an airport and it crossed my mind it could be associated with this. I was watching it slowly transverse the sky for approximately 10mins or longer. it appeared to be getting higher and slowly went out of sight when it got too far away. I was very perturbed by this sighting, it made me feel pretty anxious and I have never experienced anything like it. I rang my mum and we spoke about it, but the more I thought about it the stranger it made me feel.
