{jpg} Spotted a swarm of about 12 UFO's over North Phoenix/Scottsdale

My friend was leaving my house at 9:05 pm on May 21st in Scottsdale, AZ. He came running back in to tell me to come outside because there was something I had to see. As I stepped outside I looked N/NW and saw a swarm of about 12 glowing objects in the sky moving from Northest to Northeast. I live right along the flight path for landing at PHX airport and these were farther away than the path planes normally take and moving much faster. Not to mention they were in a swarm and also changing altitude rapidly whilst moving from NW to NE. Entire event lasted about a minute. My first instinct after the initial shock was to take a video with my iPhone. However the street lights around my house made it really hard to focus so I had to settle for a picture. By the time the picture was taken all but two lights and disappeared and they can be see left center with a red hue.
