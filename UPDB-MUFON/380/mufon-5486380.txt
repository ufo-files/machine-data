{rjl} Watched an orb ascend from the woods across the street and fly over my neighbor's house before blinking out

I had just gotten home to my parents house in Apple Valley Ohio at 10:17 p.m. and as I was walking towards the door I noticed a light ascending from the back of my neighbors woods which borders the lake. Once the object reached about 75 feet above the ground it instantly began accelerating horizontally in a northern direction. As it moved over my neighbors house I realized it was a UFO so I pulled out my phone and took three pictures before the glowing orb blinked out. Immediately after the event, I looked over to see my neighbor gazing up into the night sky while standing in her driveway. I went across the street and asked "Did you just see that?" and she responded "Ya! What was that?". We both agreed it wasn't a Chinese lantern as it was moving against what little wind was present and she was amazed at its color, acceleration, proximity, and lack of noise or any aircraft navigation lights. This witness also happens to be a manager for Delta Airlines in Columbus. Of the three pictures, the last turned out best as I was a bit shakey on the first two, however, the last picture does have some motion blur from the objects movement. If you zoom in you can see the spherical shape of the orb.
