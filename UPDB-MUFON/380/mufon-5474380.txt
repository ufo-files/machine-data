Thought I seen a star but on close the inspection there was flashing lights but after observing realised it wasn't plane/helicopter

I was letting my dog out for a tinkle around half nine and I always look to the stars especially tonight in case of any straggling meteors from the recent perseids. Looked at a really bright star in the sky, right of the plough constellation. I watched it for about five minutes then noticed flashing red/orange/white lights on it. Observers the object going vertical slowly then move to the right and then descend but stayed hovering in the air. Assumed it was a helicopter but I thought it was too far away tonne able to see flashing lights. Object stayed stationary for at least 40 mins. I then observed it swaying slightly side to side. Then as mended slightly again and then stayed stationary. All the time the lights were flashing. Decided since it was stationary I would grab my telescope and take a look. Before focussing with the lens it looked like a star but upon zooming for a clearer picture, seen it looked like a teeny silver boomerang in the sky. This object was many many miles away. It's currently 11.23pm on 15 august 2017 and the object is still there.
