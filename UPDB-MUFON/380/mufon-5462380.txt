I was going home on the gravel road with a friend and we turned to the north and to our west just above the corn field was a object with lights all over it silently hoovering I let off the gas and stared out the window in amazement my friend was hanging out the window on his side yelling profanities, do you see that, he was banging on the roof of the car all I could think was shut up I was scared it had 2 lights that seemed to come from the same point of origin on the craft the lights were moving around in various directions but when 1 of the lights shined on the car both lights focused together in a forward looking position away from us and then it was gone no noise no smoke no nothing just gone I would not talk about about this for years despite my friend trying to. I am still freaked out over this after all this time
