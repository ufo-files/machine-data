{nd} Saw star like UFO traveling from the west went over the house and turned southwest turned red blinked out

My dad and I and two of my friends were talking around a campfire. My dad saw it first looked like a big bright star in the sky. Traveling out of the west fairly low. Went right over my house and seemed to slow down a bit moved left and then right slightly. Then continued south by southwest lowed in altitude a little then turned red blinked out and was just gone. Disappear while we were all watching it. This is the second UFO we saw within 2 nights both from the west same altitude and speed no flash no sound both just disappear.
