Orb seen

I went outside to walk the dog at 2:55 am on Thursday 6/3/2021. After I walked off of the front porch I saw a bright light from the east side of the preserve but high up in the trees 4 houses down. I was trying to figure out where it was coming from and looked to see what it was and it was blinking. I stepped into the yard to get a closer look and about 10 seconds it was gone. I started to walk the dog and then it showed up by the roof of the next door neighbors house and just sat in mid air blinking. Really small object about the size of a dime if not smaller but really bright. I sat there and stared at it trying to figure out what it was and the blinking stopped and it seemingly was gone again. Than all of a sudden it was there again but this time at the top of the tree on the east side of our house, suspended moving from left to right blinking. Than it went to the top of our house in front of a second story room in my house and moved side to side for about 5 seconds, then it moved to the top of the house and was gone.
