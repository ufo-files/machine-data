I was photographing what I thought was a very bright star.

On Valentines Day 2011, at midnight, my boyfriend and I were at Beaverkill Pond which is at the end of the private road where we live in Woodstock (on Ohayo Mountain). It was a clear night and I had my camera with me. I took a few photos of the moon and then focused my attention on a very bright star. Took a few photos of that. It wasnt until I got home and uploaded the photos that I noticed the star had a triangular shape and when I zoomed in....saw what appeared to be a UFO. 
I have 5 photos of it: 

1. Triangular shape: Pointing at 9:00, 11:00 and 2:00
2. "S" Wormy Shape
3. Bright light with an illuminated trail behind it
