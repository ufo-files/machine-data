Came over the trees, looked like a flare at first but got brighter and higher in the sky. A total of 4 fiery objects came over the tree tops and headed due west over the gulf, making no sound

It was 10 pm, Mom and I were getting out of the car, when i saw a bright light came over the tree tops. It looked like a flare, but got higher and started moving west. we watched it go right over us, then another came over the tree, and then 2 more. They were exactly the same and flew directly behind each other right over us and continued west to the gulf.They looked like a hot air balloon with fire at the bottom. No sound, I watched them till it seemed the fire went out and I couldnt see them. I was able to take a pic with my cell phone. I was standing northwest watching them come over the tree tops. It was probably 1-2 mins.
