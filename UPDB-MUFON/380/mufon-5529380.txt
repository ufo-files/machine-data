Me and my friend were on a walk down Caves Springs Rd off of highway 53. First we saw a really bright light above the treeline. At first it looked like it was not moving. Then it started to fly right over our heads not to far up in the sky. We Saw three lights in a triangular shape,two white and one red. After it flew behind us it started to go back up into the sky with increasing speed. No sound at all.
