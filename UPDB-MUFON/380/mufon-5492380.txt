{rjl} 30 people witnessed 5 objects going over table rock lake

I was staying at chateau on the lake in Branson , MO, it's a very high class hotel over looking table rock lake on a 500 ft bluff. At 9 pm my wife and about 25 other people were over looking the lake after sun down and a woman started screaming "hey what's that in the sky?" as we looked out a red ball of light went across the sky, then out of nowhere a second ball came up next to it and they flew for about a half mile then totally disappeared then poof 2 more came where those 2 started and took same path and disappeared same spot , then poof 1 more out and traveled same thing and disappeared same way after a half mile. I didn't have my phone on me but there were a few people taking video, they were all there for a users group convention I have 1 of the guys business cards he saw it too. We had the best view to see these objects nothing in our way we were high in the air and we knew it wasn't sight seeing helicopters because the lights came out of nowhere, and then vanished within a half mile, it's like they came to see us looking off the balcony. Ya I had a couple beers but was not drunk, but when 25 to 30 people all see the same thing, it's not coincidence.
