Black ink like, solid floating, shape changing for 3-4 hours

On Sat the 7th of July around 11pm our attention was brought to a strange cloud like object changing shapes in the sky over the sea. https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCkL-rHor1Kih26ZKMt1gT-w The object stayed at the same height and barely moved up or down I.E it was very stationary.I started filming this object on and off for the next few hours. As it was quite dark the object was quite hard to film. Also when we first started viewing the object as you see in the video it was changing into 4 separate objects but as it got darker it seemed to stay in a longish shape (you will need to watch all to see that). Also you may notice that the clouds were of strange brightness but as it is mid summer this may have just been the sun on the other side of the world. We viewed this object through binoculars and it seemed very black and solid (spilled ink in the sky). This object brought to mind the Barnsely UFO (100 milesish east of us). I am hoping that you will be able to lighten the videos so the object can be seen better. Sorry some of the video is blurry. I also have some photos that came out quite well if you would like to see them.
