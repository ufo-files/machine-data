{nd} I was Ridding my motorcycle in eastern tehama county. I saw craft hovering over high power lines.

in may of 1973 approximate date I was riding my motor cycle in eastern Tehama county when I saw what looked like circular object hovering over the high power lines off in the distance. I kept riding toward the power lines watching the object. It just seemed to be hovering over the power lines. I wanted to get a closer look. The next thing i remember I am riding my motorcycle in the opposite direction toward my home and the object is nowhere in site. I didn't tell anyone about it at the time because I didn't think any one would believe me.
