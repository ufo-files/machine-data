High Speed Object

At 11:00 p.m. on July 30, 2011 I was looking at the stars and noticed a star-like object moving at a high rate of speed form horizon to horizon. It was smaller than a star but appeared to be traveling ten times faster than a satellite. The object was up very high in space.
