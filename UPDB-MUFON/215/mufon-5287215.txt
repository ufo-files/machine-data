My son came home from work. He was looking up at the stars in the clear sky when he noticed one moving around. I went outside to see what was wrong since he usually comes straight inside.  He showed me what he was looking at and I grabbed my phone to start recording. He thought it was a star in the beginning. I told him stars don't move around like that. It looked like a ball bouncing around in the sky. It looked like it would explode in one place and reappear in another spot. It really never stayed still. My son was very excited to see his first UFO. I was also excited to see it but, this is my second UFO sighting. We didn't stay out recording for very long because it was really cold that night.
