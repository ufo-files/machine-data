UFO in a square shape, hovered then disappered.

It was late Wednesday night/early Thursday mourning. I would estimate the time around 3:30. I was taking my new puppy, out to go potty, when sighting occurred. It took me roughly a minute or so before my dog barked, because she sighted the object. In the back woods, of a state park, behind my house, is where the object was sighted. It appeared to be square in shape, and was hovering above the treeline. I noticed lights on the object, looked to have 3 red lights, and 1 white light. As I watched it, it was traveling southward, and then seemed to almost just vanish. I could semi-clearly make out the object at first and within a minute it almost faded away.  It never seemed to land, and was definitely making distinct fast movements, when not hovering. I personally do not follow or have a believe in UFOs, but I can not explain what I saw, and as I was watching, I had a certain fear that it would come towards me. I am unaware of what the object was, but it has "shaken" me up, and made me re-think everything I believed in.....
