{nd} Light orbs, too many to count, as well as 4 lights hovering in the sky.

The first sighting was in July 2012, when I witness light orbs, too many to count. Most of them were white, one or two were red, and they blinked on and off. Over a period of approximately 2 hours, they slowly came closer to the cabin I was staying in with a friend. Eventually, they were approximately 30 feet from the cabin. One orb glided to the cabin and around the side, not more than 10 feet from us.

Then, August 6, 2013 and during a lightening storm, I witnessed large glowing lights in the sky, directly in front of the same cabin. First there were two lights, then three. They stayed in the same general area but changed positions, forming a straight line, then a triangle. Then the 3 lights became 4 lights. Its hard to judge distance, but I would say perhaps a 1/4 to a 1/2 mile. This lasted more than an hour and a half. I went into another room for only a few seconds. When I returned they were gone. As soon as the lights were gone, one light orb, similar to what I saw last year, came from my right and soared along the tree line in front of me, went up and down in the trees, then soared off to my left. It was not more than 30 to 40 feet from me.
