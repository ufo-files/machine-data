Triangle shaped object

I spotted lights in the distance from inside of my house. I headed outside to take a closer look. There is a drone school directly opposite where I live so initially I thought it maybe drones. On closer inspection I saw a triangular shaped craft made up of 3 bright fixed lights. It was much further in the distance than where the drone school operates. I could tell it wasn't drones. It was stationery. My heart started pounding as I got out of my phone. I tried to zoom in to take a good shot. It took several snaps on my Samsung galaxy ultra. I didn't think I captured it as it was so dark. I looked for a few more moments then went in. I must add that before my initial sighting I did see something there about 10 minutes before I went out but didn't think much about it. I didn't see it leaving. I was in the middle of getting ready for dinner and waiting for my Mrs and son to arrive home. It was later in the evening that I took a look and saw what I had captured. Better pictures than I thought.
