2 large metallic disc UFOs flying several times by plane at supersonic speed

I was filming the sky and planes in South East London with a Panasonic camcorder when I spotted several objects passing on the screen at very high speed. I thought it might be insects or birds but upon reviewing the footage and other clips I filmed earlier on I noticed clear sizeable UFOs. There have been several similar sightings in the area which is on the path to Heathrow Airport. The plane is at an altitude of 4,500!feet. The objects were about 45 to 50 feet in length- about a third the length of the plane. They were metallic/solid in structure. The 2 UFOs returned to fly around the plane several times including other aircrafts.
