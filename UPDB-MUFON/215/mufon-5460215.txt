{cak} observed three lights moving together N-NW over Hudson River Valley, fluid motion in formation, cleared sky in 30-40 sec

I was sitting on the front steps at sunset when I noticed a group of lights low in the sky over the Hudson River Valley, moving North. Three unblinking lights were observed in a loose and fluid formation, with two in front staying close and the third lagging behind. The patch of sky in front of me is frequented by airplanes (Westchester County Airport) and helicopters. What got my attention was the way that the third light drifted away and rapidly rejoined the group. Three lights continued to be visible as they moved further away to the North in a straight path. My view was obscured as they passed behind some plants, but I stood up after maybe fifteen seconds to take another look and they were still visible for a time until moving beyond the distant treeline.
The sun was setting to my right and I had at first thought that the golden lights were reflections off of a metallic surface. With continued observation, it was apparent that lights were emanating from the objects themselves.
