Flattened blimp shape object moving rapidly east to west with flashing lights

Was standing outside in my backyard at dusk when observed a flattened blimp shaped object moving rapidly from east to west in a 45 degree downward path. There was a white light that rapidly flashed from end to end approximately in a half second sequence. Object passed in front of a jet that was farther away and moving in the opposite direction. Object was closer to me and appeared much larger than the commercial jet (which had red, white and green lights) and when passing by covered up the sighting of the jet. Objects passed behind some trees and I lost sight of it for about 2 seconds. Total sighting lasted about 10 seconds and I lost sight of object as it moved downward from sight.
