I looked up and saw a dis-like orb of light hovering across the sky, then shot off at twice the velocity! !

I was outside of my room smoking and happened to look inland at the sky to what I thought was a shooting star. Amazingly the object flattened out from its downward trajectory, and started to wobble and hover at an incredible speed. Then, before I could get a camera shot at it, the orb
increased its speed by a lot and shot off at a 30 Degree angle out of site. Most surreal moment of my life, felt like 2 minutes went by. I was mesmerized by it and ran inside to alert my family. At this very moment I have a camera looking up to that portion of the sky! Scared? Yes.
