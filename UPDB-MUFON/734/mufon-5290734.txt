I saw at least 10 , what looked like stars evenly spaced out in a straight line moving. It looked like it was out ispace. Or orbiting

I was walking the dogs and was occasionally looking up at the stars. I live in the country and there’s no « light pollution » and the stars are quite visible. 
As I neared the end of our walk I looked up and could see a string of lights evenly spaced and in a straight line. I thought that was weird to see stars lined up like that. I thought I was just making out a line out of different stars. 
But as I continued to look at them I could see that they were moving slowly , following the lead light (star). I texted my wife to go out and see what was in the sky. She wasn’t able to see because of the tall trees surrounding our house. 
I couldn’t hear any noise that a plane would make. 
I’ve never seen anything like this. I don’t go looking for UFOS. 
I tried too record it with my phone but all I got was darkness and one light shooting across the screen. I think it’s because I brought my phone down because I couldn’t see the lights on my phone.
