I was with a friend up at Detroit Lake area, living in Idanha. We were going to the store in Detroit it was early evening. We enjoyed going up to the overlook, a road leading up called Boulder. We drove there and was sitting looking down at the town, when noticed 4 or 5 Amber lights above the tree line off in the distance. It almost looked as if they were busy doing something. Next thing I remember was seeing a thick fog roll in and cover the town.We were still watching the lights hovering above the trees busy and I felt something behind us so I looked and it was a huge black mass sitting there above ground watching us.hovering behind us and above us. I literally was freaked out and scared. I had emotions and a feeling like nothing I've ever felt before running threw me. I can't explain it. It was like they were controlling me or how i felt. Only way to describe it is that it was like every emotion all at once. I whispered to my friend not to move because although it was obviously watching my reaction was to stay silent or so they don't see us. My friend was driving and said I'm going to start the car and go quickly but I was scared to move.he started car and immediately pulled out like a batt out of hell. I was watching to see if it followed. But it didn't as far as I know. When we got back to my house my parents were angry because we had been gone over 3 hours which completely didn't make sense. There was no way we were gone that long! I wanted to report this Long ago but was scared.
