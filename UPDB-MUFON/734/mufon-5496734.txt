{cak} Silent, low flying V-shaped ufo with 7 lights.

I was outside admiring the stars. When suddenly a super low flying, silent V-shaped ufo flew right over me. It was black and had seven lights the were on the whole time. Strange thing is the lights werent just shining because they werent it was like it was fixated light. Hard to explain but there was like no illumination. Very weird. It was at least 100 to 200 feet in the air. I continued to watch it fly over the rest of my little city ridgecrest. It went right over the homedepot down the street it was about 50 feet above it and just kept flying at a constant, relaxed, fixed stright path over the city.
