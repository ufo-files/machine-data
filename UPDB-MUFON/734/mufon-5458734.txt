I have been searching the internet trying to see if anyone has reported a UFO over the Des Plaines, IL /O'hare airport area on August 4th at 00:30hr.

I have lived in this area for over 20 years, I have seen nearly every aircraft that is flown by man.
I have just got off of a 10 hour shift at work.
The night in question, I was......perplexed.
I saw an object that resembled an Orange Orb flying from west to east, then turned south Over the airport.

At first I thought it was a meteor from the Perseid meteor shower, with no noise or sound of any kind.

It made no quick maneuvers, it paused a few times very briefly as it began to turn south.
There were no other visible lights on or coming off of or blinking from this object.

I had my camera out and started recording, I tried getting a perspective with the trees and the home across from me.
When I finally got into my home, where I thought I was recording, I anxiously played back what I recorded, only to find I
was recording me walking into my home. Very disappointing!

I was hoping to see IF someone else had seen something that night.
I couldn't find any reports of this incident, anywhere on the internet.
One thing that came to my attention while filing this report is, "Also in area" before, during or after"
The strange thing now to me is NO, because this is one of the most busiest airports BUT THERE WAS NOT 1 aircraft in the sky !? 

This ended with the UFO ascending over the airport, above the low ceiling through the clouds and disappearing.
Every aircraft I have ever seen coming to O'hare Descended to the airport, or Came from the airport and ascended.
Every aircraft I have seen coming any going had bright blue/white running lights with blinking red or blue wing lights.  

I hope that someone had also reported this Anonymously, and the truth will come out.
