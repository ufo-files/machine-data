Star like object flying far over us suddenly make a 45 degrees u-turn.

I was out for a smoke a late summer night 6-8 years ago with a friend.
We were playing computer games with a few friends when me and my and another person went out for a cigarette. This night there were clear sight and no skies. 

We were talking and looking at stars and meteors who lighted up the horizon. after a few minutes i first noticed a glowing object moving quite fast across the atmosphere/heaven . Ok so this is very normal for a satelite,meteor etc, BUT suddenly this object stops completely and make a 45 degrees turn and start flying/moving in another direction. 

Me and my friend stood and looked at this happen and couldnt figure out what satelite or airplane that looked like a glowing star could make such a maneuver.

after we lost sight i ran in to the other guys who were playing computers and told them what happened. Me and my fellow witness to this were both a little shocked and confused to what we had just seen since this doesent happen here in southern Norway very often that we had heard of.

i did some reasearch on my own in the days and weeks after to find out if there where any satelite reports that day but i couldnt find any.
