{cak} Shiny objects coming in and out of storm clouds

It was around 3pm (02-Mar-2015) and I was at taking a break from work and saw something outside the window. I went close to the window and noticed there were 2 or 3 shining objects flying/hovering in the sky. It looked like an airplane at first, coming from far (from North-East) as it was way up in the sky, under the clouds (dark clouds as it was about to rain). But I realized it wasn't a flight or a bird as it was hovering up/down, side-to-side and in-out of the clouds. I called a friend to see it and then I took a video of it, but only managed to get one of the object in the frame as it was very faint and far.
