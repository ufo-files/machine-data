{ck} I saw about 20 of these in palatine? IL and they just kept coming nonstop for 15 min. In a straight single file line at the same speed.

On 6/30/2012 at 10pm in a suburb of chicago I was letting the dog out and saw a series of more than 20 of these objects going overhead in the exact same path, at the same speed, altitude and in a straight, single file line, and separated at equal intervals. At first I just saw 1 but more kept coming. I was sort of concerned. they were reddish orange, round and sort of pulsated/flickered in the middle. They were traveling at a speed slightly slower than an airliner would. They made a faint humming noise, and looked like they had an intended destination. I looked and found many YouTube videos showing the same objects, but never in the quantity I saw them. This video is from Utah, 10 days before my sighting. These are the same objects saw.
