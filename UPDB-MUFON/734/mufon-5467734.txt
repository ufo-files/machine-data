{td} Light Orb appeared in sky from NE and flew straight for approx 30 miles (est) then stopped and disappeared

I was sitting in my backyard watching my fire pit and having a drink before going to bed (only had two). A bright White Light Orb appeared in sky from NE and flew straight for approx 30 miles (est) then stopped abruptly in the sky and then I remember it just disappeared (I looked for several minutes and listened and did not see or hear any other type of aircraft, including drones). The next day I woke up with a large bruise on the front and back of my right arm, and I had two "new" scoop marks on my left shin bone, about the size of a green pea. I know that when I first saw the object, I was like wow, that is weird and I stood up to get a better look! Then, all of a sudden I was sitting in my lawn chair again, on my cement patio by my fire pit. However, I had a feeling that "something" happened, I was scared and breathing odd, like I just did some work or something!? I was very sleepy and went inside and right to sleep. I have reported this to MUFON, by suggestion of my wife and daughter. I can't get this out of my thoughts....no matter how hard I try.
