Series of lights hovering in sky

Five orange lights change from the direction of South Dublin. Two were in a horizontal line to the front. Another three followed behind so that they formed an inverted "L" shape. They all appeared to be the same distance apart from each other. 

Over roughly the area of the N11 motorway where it meets Bray town they stopped and hovered motionless. After being like this for about 20 seconds they faded out one by one starting from the rear and moving up until the "head" of the "L" shape was the last to go fade. 

This area of sky is part of the flight path to Dublin Airport but the airport would have been closed at that hour of the morning. 

I would rule out military involvement (e.g. night exercises, etc) as the Irish Aer Corps (air force) has no modern jet aircraft (fighter or otherwise)
