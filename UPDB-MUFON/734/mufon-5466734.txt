Driving on highway 16;observed object at 5 o'clock position in relation to the moon

I was driving a hot shot to vanderhoof and I noticed a bright light off to my right at about the 5 o'clock position relative to the moon. Observed it for 20 minutes then decided to take a couple of pictures. It looked to big to be a satellite and had no running light's or contrails. Thought maybe space station's solar panels reflecting light. I watched it on and off for over an hour before it disappeared
