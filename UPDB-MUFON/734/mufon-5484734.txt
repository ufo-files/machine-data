{nd} on flight

I am hesitant to report this since there was an eclipse yesterday but I am not certain when or if they happened close to the same time. I was on flight DL1800 from Atlanta to Boston, an hour after take off I saw the object out my window for a brief time. It was cigar shaped with a hot white glow and a flat black area inside it. It left no trail or had any flashing lights. The view was clear as there weren't many clouds in the sky at the time. It was flying at a 45 degree angle away from the path we were on. It was blocked from my view by the wing shortly after I spotted it. No one else on the plane seemed to see it as I heard no one say anything but I didn't see anyone else looking out the window at the time either. It very well could have been something easily explained but I am left with no answers.
