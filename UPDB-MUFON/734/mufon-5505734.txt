{rjl} First noticed 7 lights in an askew formation, the pack moved together unwavering unblinking, disappeared one at a time.

Driving home after New Years we noticed a formation of lights right above the horizon line. We pulled over immediately to observe. The levels of elevation were varying. No blinking lights, appeared to be much larger than any man made explanation ( ex. Chinese lanterns) plus the lights did not flicker. They were deep orange fluctuating to light yellow. We knew immediately we were seeing something we couldnt explain. One by one the lights disappeared until the last one which seemed stationary at this point eventually did the same.
