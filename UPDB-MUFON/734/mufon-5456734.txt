{rjl} Large, cigar shaped, shark-gray something moving very, very fast.

I was outside with my dog, looking south at crescent moon. Very clear sky, with 
great view of stars. Object appeared in my line of sight from southwest moving northeast and was only visible because moon was behind it, for approximately four to five seconds. Certainly no wings, fins, windows, lights, or any other markings or colors. Like a large, gray bullet shooting across the sky.
