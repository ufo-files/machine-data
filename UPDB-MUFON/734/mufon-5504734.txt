{rjl} Noticed two star-like objects zig-zagging above me in the sky

My wife and I were leaving a dinner party at about 8:00 pm on January 18. I left first and was waiting for her out in the parking lot. I was looking at the night sky above me. It was dark and I could see a lot of stars. I noticed one star that was moving. It was zig-zagging around the sky. It would move in one direction, then another, then another. Almost as if it was searching for something. My wife came out to the parking lot and I asked her if she saw what I saw. She noticed it too. We both noticed another object near the first. They were both moving erratically. I was thinking maybe atmospheric effects but the movements were really pronounced and seemed intentional. We observed them for five minutes then got in the car and drove home. It was nothing spectacular. They just looked like white stars. If they were closer we might have been more interested but they were so far away. This wasn't the first sighting I've experienced so it explains my lack of excitement.
