{rjl} Objects moves independently and hovered, then came together and flew out of sight.

My husband and myself sat on a chair lakeside after dark. The sky was very dark. I noticed a star-like object moving in the sky, moving in ways that no known airship would be able to do. I then noticed 3 more of the same kind doing the same kind of moves. We continually talked, confirming what we were seeing. The objects moved at a speed that seemed impossible, given how far away they appeared to be. After each move each one would hover for a time, then move again. We felt sure they were some kind if craft. They were too far away to notice any details. They looked bright, like large stars. After about 15 minutes they all came together and flew away. The effect was a swoop, something I had never before witnessed. The next day we heard reports of sightings in three states, which made sense, as that corner of Maryland is close to Pennsylvania and West Virginia. We didn't report our sighting because we were on our honeymoon and felt that we would not have been taken seriously. We were both excited and a little frightened as a result of the sighting. We were never able to doubt the existence of UFOs after that experience.
