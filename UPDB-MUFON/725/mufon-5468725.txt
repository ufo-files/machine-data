{td} Seen a large wooden vessel, no windows and it was cigar shaped

I was playing basketball with 2 of my brothers.
I seen a large wooden vessel, no windows and was going at a steady pace . 
It was cigar shaped and looked like it was made of wood. 
I would estimate it probably 100 to 150 feet long and a height in middle of around 50 feet . 
I tried to get my brothers to look up and told them i seen an UFO but neither would look no matter how hard I pleaded with them. 
They thought i was pulling a joke on them and trick them into looking . 
It disappeared about a minute or so later . 
Never seen it again and very few believe my story . I rarely tell it cause I don1t like the ridicule i receive. 
The year is just an estimate cause i never recorded it . It could of been as early as 1979 or as late as 1982 . I do remember it happened in the summer time during summer break from school and around midday . The sun was almost directly overhead.
