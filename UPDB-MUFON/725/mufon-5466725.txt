Disc shape objekt with a lightening dome on the underside !

It was between 1979 and 1981 i and a friend was out playing ! Then suddenly a Disc shape objekt was howering over us ! It was like 10m in diameter and it had a light on the underside ! The Ufo spoke telapticly to me and said that i had to come with them , i answeared that i already was with them !I and my friend was watching the Ufo to it flew a way ! Then i moved away and i havent seen my friend since and we newer spoke of it again !The sound of the Ufo was like the wind !
