{rjl} observed a triangular shaped amber glowing object above Dale City, Va. around 10:20Pm on 12/31/13 it was at exactly 215 degrees SW and flew vertically then horizontally

while waiting to give a friend a ride to a New Years party, I was in my car in front front of his house located in Dale City, Va. I was listening to the radio and happened to notice something out of the drivers side window that caught my eye. It was a almost triangular shaped form glowing Amber in color. It travelled vertically at first and kind of briefly hovered. It was pretty slow and deliberate.It almost appeared to have windows with the glowing coming from within it. The closest thing I could relate its resemblance to, would be the traditional floating Asian lanterns that are lit and released into the sky. I tried to video the form with my phone, but the video is not clear. And it appears farther away in the video than it was to my location. I shot an azimuth with a phone app in the direction of the object and at approximately 10:20pm the object was a. 215 degrees SW.
