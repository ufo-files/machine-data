orange white spheres in sky

Stepped outside to have a cigarette and noticed a bunch of white spheres over the water interlapping and moving roughly around a V formation. I thought at first because of this they must be planes but then one by one they seemed to disappear after pulsing brightly. I noted that their reflection was visible on the water in the dark. I felt great peace when observing the spheres. I lost sight of them when a cloud covered them
