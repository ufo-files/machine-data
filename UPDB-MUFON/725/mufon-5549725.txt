Saw multicolored lights appear in the sky

We were driving down interstate I-20E towards Atlanta,GA early night. about anhr. from atlanta. We noticed a bright star high in the nighttime sky. Itgradually grew brighter and brighter and appeared to pulsate. It seemed tomesmerize both of us. The atmosphere in the car silenced and we watched in aweas it pulsated into a multicolor lighted trianglediamond shape object, shootingfrom one point in the sky to the other, right angles , zig zag type formationextremely fast, faster than anything ive known of to have an earthly quality.before i saw the craft the sun was setting and the car was quiet except for theradio. I thought I heard the driver say, follow the sun, I was wide awake andasked him. He said he had said nothing for an hour.I have a memory lapse of what happened from that point until I spotted theunusual lights.After the craft zig zagged for a few seconds, it pulsated back into a star,remained stationary and the light pulsated into darkness.
