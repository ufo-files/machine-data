Orange Orbs over Breckenridge, TX @21:12 CST on 12/5/2020

First object noticed in the Central / East area of Stephens County Texas. Two witnesses observed the object to be an orange glowing orb, looking like a sodium street light bulb, traveling from North to South / South-East direction a 21:12 CST on December 5, 2020. More of the same type object began to be visible within a few minutes, all traveling in the same direction and speed and varying in elevations between 25-35 degrees and totaling between 6-10 objects. From the observers' viewpoint the objects were in relative size to an aspirin or smaller. Observers were unable to determine the speeds of the objects but the orbs all seemed to move at a constant velocity about the same as a conventional small aircraft would travel across the sky, possibly around 100 mph. Slow enough to be visible for approximately 3-4 minutes. Objects would sporadically blink on and off and seem to disappear at times before finally "fizzling" out after traversing across the skyline.
