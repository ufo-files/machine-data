FLYING DISC THAT EMITS LIGHT

I was returning home on foot from my grandmother house that lives near me, when suddenly I noticed a yellow and orang thing flying in the sky over me.
It looked like a fresbee and he wasn't so fast, even less than a car, but when I noticed it, it was really near to the hills where I live, and then disappeared behind them.
I never seen something like that before, and it doesn't looked like a normal aircraft.
