Strange lights small and dim changed to big and bright. Moved in a vertical line. Disappeared right in front of us.t

My son and I came home from our camp behind our house roughly 11:30pm on Saturday May 22, 2021. As we got to the back of our house, my son said he saw something in tye sky. We both saw small dim lights that turned in to big bright lights as they moved upwards then became small and dim again. This continued for about 45 seconds to 1 minute then the lights seemed to finished what I'd call a cycle, faded away and just dissappeared.
