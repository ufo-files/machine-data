Bright white star like object moving very fast, then disappearing in a bright flash.

I was driving home from getting fast food. I pulled into my apartment complex and saw out of the top of my vision a fast moving object. When I first saw it I thought it was a plane or a falling star, but noticed it was far too bright to be a plane. The light was very bright and clear. I stopped my car and looked up and it was traveling very fast, then all of the sudden dissappeared. When it dissappeared, a bright flash of light about 5 times the size of the light appeared then dissappeared very fast. Ive seen a lot of falling stars, but this one was very different. The light was very strange compared to other lights you normally see at night.
