{rjl} THREE MONTHS AGO I SAW AN ENITY RUNNING AROUND, IT APPEARED TO BE 4 TO 5' TALL AND WAS TAN IN COLOR. IT HAD WEIRD HANDS AND FEET. A BIG HEAD AND EYES

FILLED OUT AT MUFON HQ BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18806, WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE. JAN. 20TH, 2016
------------------------------------------------------------------------

I HAVE BEEN SEEING ENTITIES RUNNING AROUND MY AREA FOR A WHILE NOW.THREE MONTHS AGO WAS THE LAST TIME I SAW AN ENTITY RUNNING AROUND, IT APPEARED TO BE 4 TO 5' TALL AND WAS TAN IN COLOR. IT HAD WEIRD HANDS AND FEET. A BIG HEAD AND EYES. THEY SEEM TO BE FLOATING ON THE GROUND, NOT QUITE TOUCHING.
THE HANDS WERE MISSING FINGERS AND THE FINGERS WERE LONG. IT WAS TAN IN COLOR. SOMETIMES I SEE IT IN THE MORNING, DURING THE DAYTIME, AND SOMETIMES I SEE SOMETHING RUNNING AROUND AT NIGHT. MY FRIENDS HAVE ALSO SEEN IT RUNNING AROUND VERY FAST. IT SEEMS TO RUN AROUND CORNERS WITHOUT SLOWING DOWN. I HAVE SEEN TALLER ONES TOO. I DON'T KNOW IF THEY ARE THE SAME ONE WE SEE OR DIFFERENT ONES. I HAVE A RIVER BED BEHIND THE APARTMENTS WHERE I LIVE, AND THEY SEEM TO RUN DOWN THERE TO GET AWAY. MY DOGS BARK AT THEM AND MY CAT GETS VERY SCARED. I DON'T RIDE MY BIKE ON THE TRAIL IN THE RIVERBED ANYMORE BECAUSE I'VE SEEN THEM THERE TOO. I ONCE SAW ONE TRYING TO CLIMB OVER A FENCE AND WHEN IT SAW ME IT RAN AWAY. 
I HAVE SEEN FOOTPRINTS THAT LOOK LIKE FEET WITH CLAWS.
I LIVE NEAR A CEMETERY WITH A LOT OF TREES AND I HAVE SEEN THEM THERE TOO.
I WANT TO KNOW WHAT THE HECK IS GOING ON AND WHAT THESE THINGS ARE.
ALSO SOME OF THE PINE TREES ARE BURNT AFTER I SEE THE CREATURES IN THEM, AND SO ARE SOME OF THE PALMS TREES.
