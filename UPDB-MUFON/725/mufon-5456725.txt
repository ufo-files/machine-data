{rjl} Star or planet like light that moves quickly

Starlike light object in Southeastern sky from 10 pm to midnight that moves along with rotation of the earth makes quick darting movements and has red balls of light that fade around it. It splits into two lights and then reabsorbs into one light again. Two nights back to back I observed it between 10 pm and midnight.
