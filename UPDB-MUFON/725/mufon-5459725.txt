While doing the experiment we both saw it move very slowly. My father was gone somewhere and I think it was to help my uncle do some Plumbing or to his mother's house. It was near her birthday so I'm not really sure. I know now it was basically cloaked in camouflaged with the Stars but I wasn't really sure what I was seeing. It was the sixth grade and I hadn't done any drugs, but I did have glasses so I've factored in floaties or smudges on my glasses, but my mother's side too. Since then she's slept It Off and I sort of looked at it as my eyes playing tricks on me. I know what I saw though. Later after seeing other people's eyewitness reports I was sure. It could either turn off its lights and make the illusion that it shot off fast or it just shot off fast as shit..... the subsequent other sightings in the area and my research as just backed up my feelings on it being completely factual. I don't know if there was time lost, but something felt pretty strange afterwards I guess it could just be dumbfoundedness that made us that way. I'm not going to say I was abducted that's for sure. My brother-in-law who lives down South has also seen the same type of crafts and we are pretty close now to Cape Girardeau which is the original oldest supposed crash site. Little Egypt has a lot of Secrets and questions... when I drew a picture and Mark this on the mufon map back around 2012 and the man called me I couldn't give him a specific date. All I know is that on the National Geographic Channel August 2012 the Phoenix Lights episode is what made it come flooding back to me 100%.
