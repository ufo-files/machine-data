3 intense red-orange lights moving in formation

I was out on my back porch in between New Years Eve events - I happened to look up and saw a large faraway spread of three red/orange lights moving in formation in a line, hovering for a bit, then continuing on past my house out of view. Ive never seen anything like it and don't see how it couldve been jets, planes or helicopters.

The next day I was looking around for news of it and didn't find much but a mention in the examiner of a simiar sighting at the same time of two lights. That brought me to MUFON where I discovered case 34478 which Im pretty sure is of the same entities.
