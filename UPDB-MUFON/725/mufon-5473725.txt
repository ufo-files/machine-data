{cak} Sun reflecting through the clouds like a portal and the object stayed below portal.

My husband was at his brother's house helping him clean up after Hurricane Harvey. They noticed the sky and it looked like the sun was shining through a hole(portal) in the sky. My husband snapped several pictures of the sky. It wasn't until he started looking back at the pictures that he noticed the orb. In the pictures you can see the orb above the tree line and begin to ascend.
