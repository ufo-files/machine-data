It showed up on my camera when I looked at it the next morning.

Walking from the beach after rain cut off my Meditation time. I thought a full moon was out that night, but could feel something overhead. The clouds were streaked with wind and I thought that the moon would not show through the clouds, but there was a bright light above me. It started to rain harder so I took my IPad and shot a picture of the object(s) above me. Then I looked back behind me and saw the moon was farther to the south then straight above me. I looked at the picture the next morning and found 2-3 white orbs parting the clouds.
