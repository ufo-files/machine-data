Bright Light over Lake Erie

Driving east on 90 from Cleveland to Buffalo, I saw a bright ball of white light flash on in the [dark cloudy] sky over Lake Erie for about 3 seconds and then go off. Didn't think much of it. Then it reappeared a few seconds later and quickly dove down towards the lake in a "U" pattern, behind trees and reemerged hovering for a second and then diving down quickly towards the lake, until being obscured behind some trees.
