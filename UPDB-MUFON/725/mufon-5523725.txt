Saw a UFO in Iran, Tehran

It was 30th Desember and I was going home from my Grandma's house. I was sitting in the back seat of the car, with my Mom driving and my sister sitting next to the driver's seat. I looked to the sky, and suddenly I noticed something in the air hovering. It was grey, hovering and looked like a boomrang. First I thought it was moving, but I was wrong. I've never seen such thing like that, and I told my Mom and my sister and they both agreed with me. I think it was 10 ft away from ground in the sky. It didn't make any sound, it just hovered there for 5 minutes or less. I was scared, really scared. 
Suddenly the boomrang like UFO changed shape. It vibrated and then it lost its boomrang like shape. I don't know what did it change into. The only thing I know is that it changed its boomrang shape.

Then we turned in a another street. I looked back and I didn't see the UFO anymore.
