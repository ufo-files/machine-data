{cak} Saw these lights flashing, hovered there for almost two hours

It was around 5:30pm and I looked in the sky and I saw this flashing. It was not a star, could possibly be a helicopter but I don't think there is anything in that direction except Penasco and not sure it was that far over. I looked periodically for an hour and a half and it was still there. The flashes were red and blue and white. The lights were in a the shape of an array. Didn't remember helicopter having that. It made me a little nervous since it was there so long.
