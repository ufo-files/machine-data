{nd} CLOUD LIKE UFO MATERIALIZED

I was driving home on a overcast afternoon and saw what appeared to be a small dark cloud moving across the sky. i then realized that it was moving way too fast for the wind speed and the thick cloud cover above it was stationary. In an instant the cloud seemed to stop and condense itself into a sphere. It changed from a darker grey color to a bright white/transparent. It then flew directly at me and did and abrupt 180 degree arcing turn too sharp for any other thing i have ever seen in the sky. It moved away at an extremely high rate of speed, gaining altitude until it reached the cloud cover and burned or pushed the clouds out of the way disappearing. It left a distinct arcing shape cut in the clouds. Due this object's transparent/mist-like nature it would very easy for it to travel unnoticed. It made me wonder if what i saw was some type of invisibility cloaking technology.
