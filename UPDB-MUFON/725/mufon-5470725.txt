{jpg} I saw a rod flash in the daytime sky and disappear

I was walking with my partner and dog along a trail in Santa Cruz when I saw a rod, very long and thin, white with red stripes, appear and disappear in the sky in front of me in the course of a couple of seconds. I wasn't sure what it was. A woman on a bike behind me asked her bike-riding partner "What was that?" and proceeded to describe what I had just seen as well. A long skinny rod. I don't think it was moving, it appeared and vanished. I kept looking for a drone but didn't see any. It didn't look like any drone I've ever seen. It was there for such a short amount of time I can't tell if it was ten feet long and close or 100 feet long and far away. 

I kept watching to see it come back but it never did. There was no trail or sound at all. Sorry about the lack of details but I only saw it for a millisecond. I wouldn't even be making this report if he woman on the bike hadn't seen it as well. 

-David
