Extremely Fast moving sphere traveling horizontally.

At apprx 8:12 pm I observed an extremely fast moving white sphere traveling horizontally from east to west. It passed just under Orions Belt. It traversed the entire southern sky in apprx 1/4 second. About 3/4 of the way across it’s path, it made an apprx 20 degree directional change heading upward. It was a clear night and I had a completely unobstructed view of the sky. I have never seen anything traveling at that speed before. It was mind blowingly fast.
