Me and my 13 year old daughter got up at 4 to watch the lunar blood moon on october 8th 2014. Sometime shortly after 5 we were stargazing and we just happen to look up at the little dipper and I was explaining it to her. Just then we both saw a object fly over. It had no noise what so ever. It was very large.It moved very very fast. Maybe twice the speed of normal airline jet craft. It was all black yet it had red dim lights about it like red lights you would use to still have night vision. However it appeared shadowed as if to conceal it somehow. It was boomerang shaped with maybe a slight W shape. We never heard it, in fact if you were not looking directly into the path of this thing you never would notice it. It appeard cloacked. We just stood there with our mouths hanging open, then ran back inside to tell my husband what he had just missed. I have seen 3 other anomolies in the night sky, but this left me feeling weird like I had just seen something not of this world.
