silver sphere

I was leaving work in Ontario California in October of 06 it was about 5:00. There was a plane leaving the airport so I looked up to watch it. I was south of the Ontario International-otherwise known as the Los Angelas International Airport of Ontario California. To the north in the background and at about the same low altitude as the departing plane I saw a silver sphere between the plane and the mountain range. The sphere was moving west and steadily gained speed and altitude. Within a matter of about 10 seconds it had flown out of view. It never changed shapes and toward the end of the event it was really hauling ass. I had mentioned this event to a friend who sent me an e-mail response on Oct 24th 2006 so it was before that date.
