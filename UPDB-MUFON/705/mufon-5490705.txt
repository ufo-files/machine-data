{jpg} 2 lights at first looked like lightbulb shut more like fireballs moving simultaneously. Then stopped and changed direction.

My husband & I were on vacation staying at a hotel in Camden, Maine. We were sitting on the balcony taking in the fresh air & looking at the views to the harbor we had from our 3rd floor room. All of a sudden from behind one of the building I saw 2 lights. They looked like they were over the harbor and flying straight toward me. I ask my husband. What is that? What in the world is that? He stands up & looks and says"I don't know. Maybe a plane". But as we continue to look they keep coming closer toward us but start to slow down. Then they stop. One of them gets closer. This is when we confirm it's not a plane. Then they separated a bit again & very slowly started coming towards us again.  Then one stopped again and the other one went 360 around the one that stopped. Then both started going away towards the moon and slowly got further then they're lights got smaller & looked like 2 stars getting smaller & faded away into the sky. I can't believe it. I never thought I'd be one to see or encounter such a thing. I didn't move or blink. just couldn't loose sight of it because I felt like it was coming straight toward me. At one point I felt like I might have to run in to the room because they seemed like they were coming straight at us.
