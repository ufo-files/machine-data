Rectangular Bright Orange Object with fuzzy edges on one side. Traveling slow at about 500 ft altitude. Followed the object's path by car until gone.

I was leaving work at 9:00 pm. I walked out the door with another person. We talked for a couple minutes and he drove away in his car. After he drove away I noticed the object in they sky in the east. I immediately knew it did not resemble any kind of known craft. There was no doubt it was a UFO. It was a rectangular bright orange object with fuzzy edges on one side. It was traveling slow at about 500 ft altitude near Haddonfield-Berlin Rd in Voorhees, NJ at 9:00 pm, July 29, 2020. The object first appeared in the east then traveled northward crossing over Haddonfield-Berlin Rd in Voorhees, NJ on July 29. 2020. I got in my car and followed it driving on side streets for about 5 minutes. It's altitude increased as I was following it and I eventually lost track of the object because of trees obstructing my view. I felt disturbed and irritated because i could not identify the object and follow it for longer time..
