i moved to a new house a few months ago. I live down a long road with a clear view of the mountain near me. one night I was sitting at the end of my drive way and I saw a light moving in a very crazy pattern. up and down and side to side but still staying in a general area. I watched this for about 3 min when it seemed to absorb in to itself and disappear. since then I have seen the same light 4 more times. doing the same thing each time. last night around 2 am I got into my boyfriends car to leave. I looked up and saw what looked like a strobe light in the air. it was dim, and looked kinda far. it also didn't have a set pattern the lights were going off. so we decided to drive towards it. when we reached the end of my neighborhood we saw the light. way clearer now and we saw that it was a flying object. it was moving very slow, and it passed directly over us. it was lower than a plane, but higher than the treetops. when it got directly over us we looked out from the car. the bottom of the craft was two red glowing circles. and that's flashing light we originally saw. the red lights reminded me of a stove burner. very red, but not shining. it looked almost like a triangle. when we started driving again and got on the main road we still saw the strobe looking light, I could no longer see the red lights. we watched the strobe light for about 4 more min until it disappeared altogether.
