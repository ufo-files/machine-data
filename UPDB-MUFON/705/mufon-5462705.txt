Looked out of window, saw weird object in unknown distance, made photos and it stayed for long time there. I stopped looking long before it was gone.

I was laying in my bed, when I wanted to stretch and look out of the window. I suddenly spotted a flying object near the horizon in an unknown distance. It was already turning dark, so I made a few pictures. I looked for some tie at it and ran around the house to search for food. Multiple times was I going around the house and looked after every walk out of the window. Eventually I was bored and stopped looking at it. I didn't look in the next morning in the direction out of the window but I assume it would have been long gone there.
