2 glowing orbs about an hour apart

We witnessed 2 fiery glowing orbs about 1 hour apart directly over head about a minutes worth of flight coming from the west heading east. They were definitely NOT any type of fireworks they flew horizontal and with intent. The first one was in and out of the clouds. The full moon cast a shadow on the cloud when it went above the cloud line. It was saucer shaped and seemed like the orange light was on the front right side it blinked out and then it was gone. The second one was in clear sky and there was a lull in the noise of the fireworks all you could hear were crickets and frogs. It was a pulsating , fiery, silent ball. It seemed to follow the same exact path as the first. it also blinked out and was gone. It was not as if it just traveled out of sight. It didn't get smaller and smaller as it traveled away, it was just suddenly gone.
