{rjl} White triangle with wings, sounded like a helicopter, but definitely was not a helicopter

I was playing cornhole in my front yard with my family, when I saw the UFO flying over the lake. when i first saw it from far away it sounded like a helicopter, so I assumed that it was a helicopter. Once it got closer I realized it looked a lot different from a regular helicopter, and if I had to describe it, I would say it looks like a right triangular prism with boomerang wings on the top and was all white. It's movement was extremely peculiar because it looked like it was just moving forward but there was no source of push, as if it was hovering and moving forward at the same time. I loss sight of the object as it slowly hovered over the tree line and the sound slowly but surely disappeared. Definitely was not a helicopter or plane.
