{cak} Two Lights one smaller than the other moving across sky, then dissappearing

Around ten o'clock pm me and another observed two lights moving across the sky in a steady pace. The first light was larger than the other, however dimensions are unknown. The second smaller light followed directly behind the first light, and kept pace steady with it. 
We had been laying in the grass looking upwards all night and we looked away then noticed them. we then looked away again and the two lights had gone. we tried for a couple minutes to try and find it again but could not.
