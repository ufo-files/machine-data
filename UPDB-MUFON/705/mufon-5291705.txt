Orange orb shaped object came through house

In 1982 around May time I saw an Orange light come down by the house opposite 30-35 foot away at 45 degree angle. The object flew towards the house I was in around 10ft from the floor and looped towards me as if it had noticed me and came towards and stopped 4 foot short of the door and a foot off the floor. The object then came through the window (Glass) and flew through 2 rooms of the house passing within about 4-6 inches of me. I had to step out of the way of it. It then flew out and disappeared.
