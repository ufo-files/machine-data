{dr}As I was driving down the freeway (I-15) I saw 6 orb lights just hovering to the West. An airplane was circling appearing to get a better view.

I was driving north on I-15 near Blackfoot and saw 6 orb lights hovering to the West. An airplane was circling and appeared to be trying to get a better view. The lights/objects just hovered. I counted 6 of them. I couldn't tell if they were lights or if they were reflecting light. When I first noticed them I only noticed 2 or 3. As I got closer I counted 6 of them. Three were above in a line and the other three were below as I recall.. 

After I had been watching them I looked at the clock and it was 2:00 in the afternoon. It was a clear, sunny day. 

I was unable to take a picture as I was on the interstate.
