{tjd} Flashing blue light over barn in backyard, then changed to bright white/yellow lights.

I woke up at around 430 am to use the restroom. Looked out the backdoor to check on a cat someone had dumped that had kittens on my backstoop. A light directly in the center of the roof of the barn in my backyard was blinking blue,& bathed this barn in the blue light. I yelled for my husband. The light zipped to the side of the barn approximately 4/5ft off the ground,& started swirling in undetectable rate of speed. All of a sudden it exploded in a million pieces in a perfect circle. One larger orb hit the ground,& turned red. They all flew into the sky,& just hung there. My husband and, I went outside to see if we could observe anything more. I could literally feel it there. I told my husband it wasn't gone. I could feel it like it was looking through me. we stood in our driveway close to day break,& all of a sudden this baseball shaped bright yellow/white light made a strange quick movement above our house,& disappeared. This happened very quickly.  This was very strange. Whatever it was, I know this was something extraterrestrial! This barn that it was flashing light over was recently constructed. I got the impression whatever this was, was almost mapping the new barns existence.  Just my feeling on this strange event.
