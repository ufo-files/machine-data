{jpg} Two very bright lights, pulsating alternately, with a short white beam issuing downward

I was standing in my back yard, facing in approximately a NNW direction, sort of scanning the sky as usual, when I spotted an object comprising two very bright red and blue lights, moving at a very high altitude, perhaps 20,000' or higher, slowly moving toward the SSW. It was impossible to guess its size, as its altitude was very high, but its size in the sky was tiny, almost star-like.
The object was pulsing alternately blue and red, neither color giving way completely to the other, while a short white conical beam projected downward at an angle toward the east. 
I at first took it to be a drone; its movements at first seemed to mimic those of a drone, but thought it was just too high in the sky for that. 
It was moving slowly, then it made a slight turn, then moved slowly for about 2 degrees or so, and then hovered for about 2.5 minutes, motionless and, as far as I could tell, noiseless. 
I ran into the house and snatched up my video camera and ran back out. The object was still hovering motionless, and continued to give off a pulsating blue, then red, then blue light.
It then accelerated down the sky at what I estimated as 500-1000MPH and possibly faster, continuing to accelerate, completely noiseless. 
It slid down the dome of the sky in a WSW direction, disappearing over the horizon-- or into the ocean. 
I regret that I have no video evidence because my obsolete video camera could not focus properly on the object. I made a promise to myself to invest in a camera that will be useful for spotting UFOs!
