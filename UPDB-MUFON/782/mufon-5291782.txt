Star-like with changing shape as if calligraphy and other flashing lights

I saw what I first assumed to be an usually bright star, but it had no fixed shape and frequently sharp lines of light would slowly extend from it into different shapes. Dimmer lights seemed to flash around it sequentially to the far left, close left, close right, and immediately below the object. These flashing lights happened occassionally and were not continupus. Attempts to photograph it were unsuccessful on all phones nearby and produced an oblony "orb" not consistent with what was viewed with the naked eye.
