I saw a disc on my lawn, with two human-like entities walking around it.

It was Spring of 2014, I was in bed, heard my dog barking, I looked out the window, and there was a dull silver flying saucer, with a round bubble on top, just sitting there in my back yard. 
There were two men walking around, looking at the thing, as though they were having engine troubles or something. 

They were wearing silver space suits, without helmets, it looked as though they had face masks on.

I went to get my wife, but she became angry at me, and said I was ‘seeing things’ again, when I returned to the window, the ship was gone. 

We must’ve had a portal out there, because we had an infestation of big black crabs in our house, you could only see them out the corner of your eye though. When I’d turn to catch them, they’d go invisible again.

I also saw a tribe of American Indians come out of the portal, and into our backyard.

Another time, I saw a big black man, muscular as all hell, come out of a portal, he chased me around the yard, waving a machete. 

My wife called the police on me, because she couldn’t see him, only I could. They actually put me into a mental health hospital over that sighting.
