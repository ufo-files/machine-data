{cak} MY FRIEND AND I WERE DRIVING HOME FROM WORK AT 2:08 AM

Filled out and filed at MUFON HQ by FI 18809 on August 15,2019 from a paper report mailed into MUFON HQ. State Director may request the hard copy to be mailed to him/her.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
My friend and i were driving home from work at 2:08 AM. We saw an object above the road in front and above the car. I asked my friend what it was. He said it was probably a helicopter. i rolled down the window and heard nothing. I got out to take a closer look. I stood underneath the object and that is when bright lights from the object shined down on me. My Friend yelled for me to get back in the car . I did we were both terrified. We waited about two minutes to see what it would done it. It turned out the lights and moved very slowly away to our west. I still remember this like it happened yesterday.
(the witnesses said on the report their were two witnesses but did not give any information on the "friend" he was with. Witness also noted on the report that the sound he heard was like a vacuum cleaner only fainter.)
