{rjl} 3 bright lights in an oval shape

On Wednesday night June 5th 2019 I was traveling near Alapaha Georgia on hwy..82 . I saw a low flying craft with 3 huge bright round lights land in a field...it was about 11 pm.. I was a passenger in a double cab truck and could not investigate further but today was on that same hwy this morning and I could not see anything but a huge peanut field where that craft landed??? Went to investigate ..only a peanut and corn field was there. Moody air force is below that area several miles south. The craft was slow moving...no flashing lights...just 3 steady round lights. Only possible explanation could be a helicopter....but did not look like one....not a drone...too big. The craft was flying just above the tree line and I watched it for several miles until it seemed to land when I got to Alapaha area. I was traveling east and it was moving from south to north. A friend was driving...we had been to cracker Barrel to eat supper after Satilla church.
