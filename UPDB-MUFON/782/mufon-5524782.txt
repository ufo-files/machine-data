bright orange ball of light that dropped 2 smaller orange balls of light like shooting stars falling straight down from it

Last night around 8:30pm I was out on my porch talking on the phone with a friend when I saw a strange orange light, it was very bright, and it appeared to drop 2 "fire balls" out of itself and they headed toward the ground, almost like orange flares, then the bright orange light moved slowly in our direction and then moved off to the right and then disappeared. I tried to capture video of it but my camera is cheap and it didn't register on my video. My heart was racing, I have never seen anything like it in my life... there were no blinking lights or indications that it was a normal aircraft. It really freaked me out... my husband saw it as well.
