Triangle hovering next to interstate

I was driving home from work last night on I-81 south and around 2045 close to mile marker 132 I saw white lights above the tree tops approx 100ft off the ground. There is a small airport about 15 minutes north so I am used to seeing airplanes coming from the west to land. At a distance I could tell it wasn't normal though and as I got closer to it I could plainly see it was a black triangle. The triangle was black with a white light at the front, a white light on both sides midway down the craft and a light in the rear at each corner. One of the lights on the back was blinking. The craft was stationary right above the trees. I heard no noise coming from the craft. At the closest point I was approx 150 to 200 yards away from it.
