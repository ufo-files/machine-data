{rjl} Metallic-looking balloon-like object drifting at a uniform speed and altitude. Approximately 10 witnesses, no good explanation provided.

A group of 10 of us were having a Father's Day dinner outside on our neighbor's deck. My dad first noticed the balloon-shaped object moving across the sky from north to south. It appeared to look like a balloon and was very metallic looking, but came across the sky in a linear path and did not rise or fall. We thought this odd as it did not behave in the way a balloon typically behaves when released into the sky. It lasted about 30 seconds until the object disappeared behind the tree line.
