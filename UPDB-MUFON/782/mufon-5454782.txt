{jpg} Saw 50 UFOs traveling from west to east in single file straight line one after another.

Walked out of my house at 8:05 p.m. Had just got dark out. Sky was clear. Looked to the West at the planet Venus shining bright. Saw lights traveling past Venus out of the West towards the east. They were about the same size as the surrounding Stars, traveling in a single-file one after another. I called my brother immediately when I began seeing them. He was driving but pulled over. He was able to see them as well. We both watched as 50 UFOs crossed the sky near where Venus was visible. They were obviously only visible because of reflecting the Sun's light. As each one traveled further to the east it entered the Earth's shadow and disappeared.
