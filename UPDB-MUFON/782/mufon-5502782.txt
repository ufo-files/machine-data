I was visiting a farm near by my residence point. Theres nothing but animals and a on that place, but a few neighbors actually work and live in their own plots.

I usually watch the night sky, specially at the country side, beacause of the absence of light interference.

I could describe it like a chain of sightings that occur from dusk till down. You can find little bright points that can be easily taken as stars because of their slow movement, and we can also sight some U.F.Os with dish shape botom. 
When the O.F.Os reach lower altitudes we can distinguish blue-white glowing surface whit a black central circle, at bottom.
Te objectos usually pass folowing the same direction, but in some occasions wen get excited when talking about them, they just fly away o change their path circling the area.

We watch them as usually. But if someone jokes by talking to them, inviting them to help us whit some diffcult task, they seem to listen and begin to approach while circling. When this happens we retract a little scared and we tell them to go. That's why we only see them.

The objects usually disappear rising untill they miss on the sky or they just accelerate their march.
