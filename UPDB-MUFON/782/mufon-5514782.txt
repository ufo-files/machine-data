(GS) Silver gray tube shape

My daughter and I were four wheeling on the side of the grand mesa {co)
we just happen to look up above the rim of the mesa and noticed a silver tube shaped craft, no wings just a tube,we knew that it did not belong where it was, we watched for about two min as it headed west there was no noise,, it headed west then it just dissapeard, we talk about it for a little bit then the next thing it was flying back to the east we were not more than a mile from it. Its speed was kind of slow we watched for another min or two then it just dissapeared again. It gave us both a feeling of being observed, no threat
almost peaceful, anyway thanks for the chance to tell about our event..
