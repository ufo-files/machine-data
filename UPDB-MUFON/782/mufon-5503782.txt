Car Buzzing and ground buzzing object hovering over vehicle

Driving to work, rolled window down partially. Suddenly felt and heard the air vibrating and it sounded like a turbine but no planes were taking off or were around. Stopped in the midde of the road and whatever it was was above me, it seemed to be hovering and thrusting upward affecting the air around it. I was shaking, my car was shaking, the ground shook. Broad daylight i looked around and saw nothing. It was very bright out that day but the sky was clear. It was not normal and the feeling of the air fibrating was unusual and the sound was unidentifiable. No plane took off after the sound had stopped. Lasted about 3 minutes.
