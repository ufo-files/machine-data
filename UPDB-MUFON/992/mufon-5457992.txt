{rjl} Constant Red light(s) with a flashing white light

I noticed a red light in the sky, with a flashing white light. Very similar to an airplane. I thought it was a plane flying straight away from my vantage point, as it was not moving in any direction in the sky. After ~5 minutes, I noticed that the red light was still in the same position in the sky, again with a white light flashing occasionally. I then went inside to get my wife. We went back out, and the red light was still ‘sitting’ in the same position. I started trying to take a video of the light(s). However, I had nothing I could reference on to show the lack of movement. Suddenly the white light seemed to start flashing faster, and it simply vanished. I will say that if there was any movement it would have been a slow back and forth, like it was hanging from a string. It all lasted around 15 minutes!
