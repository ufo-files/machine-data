A bright white that was headed east then turned blue and headed back west downwards at a very high rate of speed.

Me and my mom had just finished some shopping and we were heading to the gas station at the corner of Loop 336 and 105 West Davis. It was roughly 8:35pm and my mom reacts to seeing something right as I am seeing it out of the corner of my eye. So I glanced over and we see this really bright solid white light in the sky heading east over the woods, we thought it was a plane at first that was really low but then it changed to a blueish color and double backed on its self and was going west, in a downward approach at a very high rate of speed. Then we lost site of it behind the trees. We had no idea what we saw it was very strange, i kept looking over the woods for some kind of explosion but saw nothing. We were in awe, especially my mom because she doesn't believe in that sort of stuff and to be honest i don't think she wants to believe but we cant deny what we saw.
