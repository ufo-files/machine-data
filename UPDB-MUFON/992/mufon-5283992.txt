My eight year old saw it from her seat in the car, described what she saw and then said it faded out of sight.

It was about 7:15am, last Wednesday, when I was driving my eight year old child to a doctor's check up. The sky was a twilight blue with blue, somewhat puffy clouds. I was driving north. My child was sitting on the passenger side in the back seat. I could hear in her voice that she was seeing something weird. She went on to describe what was seen. It was a turquoise diamond outline, clear in the middle, "with fingers on it." and then it just faded into nothing. This happened over the span of about 45 seconds or maybe one minute. I had her draw on my phone what she saw as we were waiting at the doctor's office a few minutes later. I superimposed her doodle on a photo of a sky similar to what it was like that morning.
