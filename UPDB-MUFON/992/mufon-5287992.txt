Watched this very bright light very high up move and stop snd move again to the NE

I was out on our deck watching the stars and had just thought to myself how great it would be if a UFO made its way across the sky and right above my head coming from the SW, this really bright star like object came across my line of view heading NE. I got up to focus on it and it had no flashing directional beacons that a plane would have and it seemingly stopped for a second but continued on the same path but went behind some redwood trees when another, dimmer light went NE to SW quite quickly. A third one was seen briefly going S to W but it was very faint. I stood and waited for the first to reappear after losing it in the trees but it never reappeared. This thing was very bright and I know what Starlink satellites look like. It wasn’t one of those. It just seemed odd that this appeared and the other 2 quickly followed in various directions. Thought it would be good to document this.
