bright oval light with cigar shape in the middle moving very slow and quite big

I was leaving world gym off south cobb drive at approx 1815. i was on atlanta road waiting in traffic to get onto 285. Im facing the atlanta downtown skyline and I notice a huge lit oval shape which was moving very slowly. it appeared to have a cigar shaped object in the center of the light. the reason i know it was moving because i was sitting still waiting to take a left onto 285 and in my field of view were a few sky scrappers. it was slowly moving left to right across my field of vision. I observed this object for approx 2-3 mins. Also during that time there were several civilian jet liners and helicopters in my field of view and they were dwarfed. When i 1st observed it i said to myself thats a really bright blimp, then after a few seconds of watching it i concluded that i did not believe it was a blimp anymore. I lost sight of the object because i was in traffic and had to take a left onto 285. There are a lot of sporting events this weekend so i guess it still couldve been a blimp, but i thought i should report this after acouple days of thinking about it.
