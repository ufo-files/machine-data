10:23 pm 3/20/12 rt.1 Kennebunk Maine. Saw object with row of lights on rear moving slowly north above the houses.

10:23 pm I was outside walking around my yard looking at the stars and planets as it is super clear tonight. I looked east between my house and the neighbors and I noticed a strange set of lights go by. I heard no noise like a helicopter or plane and I know these noises well. I ran down my driveway and right out into the street and I could see this object move slowly north over town. I called a friend who lives about 1 mile north of me in the direct path of this object and I told him to ge outside or look out his window to see what I am seing. Within the time of me calling my friend and losing sight of the object it must have changed course because he did not see it. I am hoping someone else saw it and is reporting it as well. I am a very rational person who does not sugar coat anything ! I tell it how it is.I have always been interested in the phenomenon but this is my first close up sighting for myself.
