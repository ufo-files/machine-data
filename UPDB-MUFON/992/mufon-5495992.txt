{cak} Two lights appeared then vanished

I was just north of my town on a gravel road taking sunset pictures.
There where plane in the area I was taking pictures of, and as i looked to the East the lights appeared. when they were not there before.
I was unsure what to make of them as they were to close together to be planes.
They appeared together, and then after a few mins. the bottom one vanished, then about minute and half later the top light abruptly turned right and was gone. 
I was shocked, baffled, stunned, confused, as to what i was seeing.
They vanished.
