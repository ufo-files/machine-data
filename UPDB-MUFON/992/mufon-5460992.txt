Sightings nearly daily for months

Sightings began as a single event sometime in March, 2019, didn't think much of it. As I began to pay more attention the object was clearly moving in an odd fashion and emitting its own light (I've got some photos). Often appears as a "star" (white light) and then will expand vertically to show more colour spectrum of blue/green and red as well as white. Then it will revert back to an orb and continue this all night (The pictures I have taken April 12 at 9:12pm - 9:14pm were attempting to document the objects movement, several shots close together in time - of the object and taken with an iPhone6, understanding this is not ideal; must zoom in). 
As time has progressed there are more of these objects appearing now surrounding my house (4 documented) and some I'm not sure yet. - Binoculars work ok, too close for a telescope to focus on. 
May 7th marks the most insane thing I've ever witnessed. Over the lake, there were two objects vertical with colour spectrum visible pulsating and moving. The objects would disappear and reappear and appeared to be moving in unison as if connected but also not. One of the objects would descend in the sky and then let of of a little white light which appeared to go into the lake (3 times witnessed in one hour). Myself and my spouse have witnessed several these occurrences. These occurrences range from simple hovering to more active day to day.
