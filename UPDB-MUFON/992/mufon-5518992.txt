Saw two orange/red patterned diamond shaped objects over SF Bay Area

Driving on 24 west in between Oakland and Berkeley California. The objects we moving very slowly and of a diamond shaped tiled structure. There was an amorphous orange/red glow to them. The three of us in the car were stunned because it was unlike anything weve ever seen in the sky. Two of us could clearly see it was a diamond shape. If I was not driving 65 miles an hour on the highway I would have pulled over and tried to take a photo. They looked like they continued on over the Berkeley Hills.
