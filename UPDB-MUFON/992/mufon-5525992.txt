One craft over my house

I was on my deck, chatting on the phone with my friend and star gazing. I saw a craft in the south-eastern portion of the sky. It was starlike, glowing like a star with no flashing lights and moving very swiftly in a straight line over my house. I observed the craft for aprox. 30-45 seconds, until it went over my house and the roof blocked my view. I have seen craft similar to this on several occasions over my hometown.
