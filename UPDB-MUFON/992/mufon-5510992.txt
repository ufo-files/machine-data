White object observed near airliner

Was at Sudbury hill Salisbury conducting military exercise it was 0300 am and was on sentry had to hand laser range finder thermal sight military grade military grade nvgs(night vision googles ) it was a clear night and was looking all around at the stars was amazing view when I noticed an airliner then all of a sudden thee was An object next to the air liner at first I thought it was a satellite but I have witnessed Ufos before all over the works and since I was a boy so no stranger to this phenomenm the object was a bright sphere and moved with speed it quickly overtook the airliner I could not get an accurate reading on the laser range finder I watched with nvg and thermal imager follows it across the sky for about 5 min it then went 90 degrees very fast and then just disappeared .. I was amazed its been a while since I had a sighting
