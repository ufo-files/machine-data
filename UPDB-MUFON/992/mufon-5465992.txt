{jpg} Diamond shaped triangle / gray / rounded / no wings / no tail / no engines / no windows / no openings

My wife and 4 children ages 5 to 14 were driving into town in a ford club wagon window van on a dirt road in the country on a Saturday morning clear skies sunshine, the windows were open. 
They were coming up on farm house with various out buildings on the left side of the road. As they got closer they saw a triangle shaped object hovering over a tree and barn just off the other side of the road.
My wife pulled over directly in front of it about 80 feet away on the side of the road. She thought it was an airplane. They stayed there about 10 minutes. 
There was no sound, no windows, no wings or tail, no markings. Just hovered there half over a large tree half over a barn. 
My 9 year old son and 14 year old daughter kept telling my wife that it wasn't an airplane and they needed to get away from it. 
She thought it was the coolest thing she ever saw being that close to a flying airplane hovering in the air! 
So she pulls away with it still hovering there. 
They get about a 1/4 mile away and my kids start yelling pull over, pull over look, look!
She pulls over and looks back, this thing is pulling up and tearing off north and in a couple seconds gone! She said like so fast it was just gone, blink!
She still thought it was an airplane!
