2 strange figures

I was walking with my dog that evening at about 11.00 when I came at a field nearby (street called Geulstraat) there where 2 strange light coloured figures from about 2 feet high, 1 in the front, the second behind it. They had the shape of a owl, no arms, looked like they where wearing a cape but the same color as the body. They had strange human-like faces, both exactly the same. It was the strangest thing I have seen in my life. Could see them very clearly for about a minute. Then my dog became very nervous and barked at them. Then they disappeared at once, no sound, nothing, they where gone. Nobody believes me, but I know what I have see.
