{rjl} Flash of green light

I was driving home from work. The long commute takes me through lots of farmland so the skies are very clear. As I was driving I saw in the sky a huge flash of green light. At first I thought it was a shooting star when I noticed the light in the sky, but then it showed to be much bigger than any shooting star I have seen, and it turned green immediately. The whole thing happened in about 1 or 2 seconds, I only had time to scream out in shock as I saw the flash. I was with three other co workers in the car, but they were all on their phones so I was the only one who saw anything. The object was gone just as quickly as it appeared, like I said I only saw a huge flash of green light. I was surprised not only by the color of the flash but by the size of it. If I were to hold my fist out in front of me, that would about sum up the size of what I saw. It shot across the sky out of nowhere and then disappeared, similar to how a normal shooting star would be. Maybe it was just a shooting star and I am seeing things, but the size of it was incredible. I didn't get a chance to film or take a picture or anything, and no one else in the car saw it except for me so that is all the evidence I have. I am extremely curious if anyone in the area saw it too.
