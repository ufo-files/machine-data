It had no sound. I immediately grabbed my phone to take pictures of it. The craft then became to move so quickly in different directions that didn't seem possible. I was so terrified. I have been deeply interested in aliens for the past 6 months. Ever since this encounter I have been having terrifying dreams of aliens and ufos that I can't even explain. I am 100%. sure that this was not a object from our world.
