A glowing, hovering object that changed color and went straight up at incredible speed.

At 4 a.m. I left the hospital to the rear parking lot. There were 2 Military Police vehicles parked by my car, which was the only one in the lot at that hour. 3 MP's were standing there observing a round, red glowing object, hovering over the nearby flightline. I joined them and watched it for several minutes. They were on the radio with someone checking on flight activity in the area. It came over the radio that there were no civilian or military flights in the area at that time. After about 5 minutes, the object glowed brighter, changed to a bright orange color and shot straight up, and was gone instantly. I watched the papers and news for a report for several days, but there was no mention of this event.
