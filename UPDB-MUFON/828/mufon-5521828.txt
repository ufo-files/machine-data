My wife and I saw 3 shimmering silver white crosses boucing in unison above the tree line probably 300-500 feet away. We watched them for about 10 seconds then 2 drifted off to the left behind the tree line. The 3rd shot off real fast.

My wife our 5 year old daughter and myself were driving on Blue Lane a mile south of Columbia Pa. Coming up a hill my wife alerted me to objects in the sky straight ahead. I pulled over and we watched 3 shimmering cross like objects bouncing slowly in unison above the tree line. We watched them for about 10 - 15 seconds. I watched the two on the left drifted off to the left over the tree line. The third object on the right was being observed by my wife saw it flip over and appeared as a saucer shape. It shot off very fast heading north toward Columbia. I immediately checked my watch to make sure we didn't lose any time. It was 4:10 pm. with no missing time. It was a cold Sunday with gray sky.
