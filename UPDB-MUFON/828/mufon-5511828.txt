{dr}Blue light moved across the sky -quicker than most planes - no identifying lights

Was driving up RT 55 in southern New Jersey at 5:40 in the morning and looked up through my windshield and observed a blue light in the sky. At first I thought it was just just typical air traffic, but the light had no other lights nor were there blinking lights as usually associated with airplanes. The light moved from an west to an east direction quickly, at a speed not typical of air traffic in the area.
