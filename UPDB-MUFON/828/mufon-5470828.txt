UFO sphere Olympijskaja street, Tyumen, Tyumen region.

The video was shot using a video surveillance camera, the shooting was conducted at night through the window, the field of view of the video camera was about 60 degrees, the video stream was 20 frames per second. The sky was clear and the stars were visible on the video.
The UFO moved from north to south through the zenith, the video camera facing south. Bright white light in the form of a sphere, the exact distance to the object and its dimensions are not known. The flight speed is too high for the satellite and slow for the meteor if we assume that the object was in orbit.
