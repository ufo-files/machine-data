{nd} Red Orbs flying in a boomerang fashion

7: 25 EST
1/19/13
In front of house

Sitting on the porch enjoying the weather, when I noticed a red orb flashing and then it blinked out and 3 appeared, it flew over my neighbors house in a boomerang shape, went over my right side of my driveway, over the trees. I heard my neighbor come outside, I ran to the side of my yard, yelled at him and he saw them. They were headed NE and then they turned and went W and a new orb joined them and it was flashing white and red. After that, heard a few military planes about 5 minutes later heading S over my neighbors house.  I did try to tape it on video, but my camera could not focus for some reason.. =( I will submit the video when I look at it.
