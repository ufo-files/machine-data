Nocturnal Light stops in Mid air near Milan, Michi

Witness describes first seeing the light while shaking rugs on back porch. Looked up and saw light.First seen at about 30 degrees to horizon directly to west and was heading dueeast. Appeared as brilliant (but not dazzling) white light. This initial anglewould place light over nearby woods. Light continued toward witness (east) atestimated altitude of 3-4000 ft. In 15-20 seconds light had gone from 30 degreeelevation to 60 degree elevation relative to witness. Then light stopped in midair for about 5 seconds, then winked out. Witness first believed light wasaircraft landing light (grass airstrip 3 miles west of witness) but changedimpression when she could not hear engine (very little surface wind) or seeconventional craft outline (dusk-but no stars yet visible) - thinks aircraftwould probably been outlined.When light stopped in air witness became frightened. When light went out, shewent in house and locked her doors. She claims she had impression that thislight was aware of her presence. Through out the sighting she heard no noise and experienced no EM effects. Witness felt object was climbing during sighting. Witness claims that to herknowledge, no one else saw the light.
