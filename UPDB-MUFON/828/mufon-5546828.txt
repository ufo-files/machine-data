Shaped like the spacebar on a computer keyboard. Dark gray to black in color. Hovering far enough away to be obscured by the haze.

I was in the midst of the afternoon commute on the Diagonal Highway which connects Boulder, CO with Longmont, CO. I was driving northbound around 5:00 pm--still broad daylight--when I noticed an oddly symmetrical dark rectangle hanging in fairly heavy haze about a mile east (right) of the highway. It was hovering at approximately 301 above the horizon (901 being straight overhead) It had the same aspect ratio and rectangular, rounded-cornered shape as the spacebar on the typical computer keyboard. There were no clouds in the sky at all, looking in that direction. I kept stealing glances at the object as the traffic flowed along, to see if the shape would change or the object showed any signs of movement. As I drove further, a stand of trees was coming up on the right, which I knew would obstruct my view of the object when they went by, I prepared myself to take up the observation again when the trees were passed. When I cleared the trees the sky was completely empty. I kept glancing to the right for several minutes but the object had completely disappeared.
