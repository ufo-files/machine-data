{rjl} It wasn't a satellite.

On or about the 22 of December 2020 at around 20:00 hours I went outside of our residence to the deck area. I looked up at the northwest quadrant of the sky and saw what I thought was a satellite. As it was rapidly moving across the sky in a northeasterly direction another object appeared behind it. As that object passed over another object appeared in the sky and repeated the same path. This went on for several minutes. Several minutes so that I walked back in the house grabbed my cell phone, told the wife about it and walked back outside. I tried to take a video of it but couldn't get it in focus. This all transpired in about 3 to 5 minutes. The sky was clear and there was no aerial activity at the time.
