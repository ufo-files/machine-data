Me & and a friend were going just on a walk to the swamp behind our home near the oil tanks. When your a 13 year old kid we just like to look & see things. What is that! There not moving and no sound from them. Then they just got off -very very fast to the south & we ran home. Those 2 objects that were just still in the sky we new that they were not ours, because no human can move that fast & live. We were very scared & ran home. We lost sight of the 2 objects, when they left at a high rate of speed and I mean very high rate. We told our family but no one believed us, so what's new.
