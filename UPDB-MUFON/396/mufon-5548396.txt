huge elongated disc with flashing red/green lights, no sound, hovering, floating over high school field

I was outside with my dog at 9p.m., and turned the corner around the side of the house to come back inside, when I saw a huge "craft" with red and green flashing lights floating/hovering over the high school athletic field next to my house. It appeared as an elongated disc-shaped object and seemed to be floating mid-air, a few hundred ft. off the ground. I watched it for several minutes, awestruck at what I was seeing. I took the dog inside, and called the police non-emergency #, to see if anyone had also reported seeing it. They said, "yes", and that it had also been seen over another school in a different part of town. I described what I was seeing, hung up and went back outside to view it again. I watched it for another few minutes. It would sit still for a time, with only the lights flashing, then it would move up and down slightly, and turn from side to side. I came back into the house and called a neighbor, who went out and called me back to say both of them saw it. I went back outside, it was still hovering, then turning slowly, and then it looked like it was descending, which made me feel a bit fearful, so I went back in to call the police again, and when I came back out, it was gone. That was the last I saw of it. I watched it for a total of about 20 minutes. Unfortunately, I don't own a video camera, but it would surprise me if no one else caught it on film. I called the police this morning to see if they had figured out what it was, and they said no, but they filed their own eyewitness official police report about it. Evidently, they had seen it, as well.
