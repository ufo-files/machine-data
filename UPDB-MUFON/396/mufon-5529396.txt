bright star like

standing on deck looking up into southern sky saw a star-like object that was moving & very bright.At first i thought it may have been a jet with a bright landing/headlight(although it was moving away from me) but there was no blinking lights at all,no sound,it was approx.5-10,000 ft & moving at a speed like a jet would into the southern sky.Wathched this for approx. 2-3 mins.All of a sudden it disappeared for 4-5 secs & then came back for a few secs still moving south,then it seemed to get about 10 times brighter & disappeared completely like it was being sucked up into the darkness.I don't think this was a meteor or a falling star-I've never seen one disappear going up.
