{rjl} White Sphere/Circle Flies Over Logan Square Home

I witnessed a white spherical/circular object flying over my neighbors house in Logan Square. I've seen this object as well as two other types of unidentified crafts on separate occasions that I'll be reporting separately. Almost every evening, I spend some time outside gazing at the sky, UFO's exist and I've seen several. On this evening of August 27th @10:38pm CST I was on my back porch and witnessed this object flying from the Southeast, towards the Northwest, it seemed to keep a steady pace until it got close, this is when the object seemed to blink in and out of existence, in a different spot each time. If you watch the :17 sec video, you can go frame by frame as it blinks and get a decent look at the object. I also took photos. There were no satellites in the area at the time, I'm very well aware of what planes, drones, helicopters look like and am 100% positive this object is not of known variety of manmade objects. There's typically a change in energy that's hard to describe when witnessing one of these craft, not scary, but a knowing, peaceful feeling. After losing site of the object after about a full minute, I went on with my night, showed a few people since and here I am with my first reported sighting.
