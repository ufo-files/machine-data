random, erratic flashing lights

I’m writing to share my observation, and wondering if any other reports were received about this.

On April 2 2021, between 9-9:30 pm I observed numerous erratic light flashes in a section of sky in the constellation Monoceros. I was observing only during this half hour. I was looking SW observing the zodiacal light and other things, when I noticed these flashes. They appeared on either side of an imaginary line between Orion’s Belt and Sirius, about midway.

Some flashes were brighter than others and I had the sense that sometimes I was looking into the light, and other times the light was pointed in a different direction. I could not detect any traveling between the flashes; they looked like random flashes, approx. 20-120 seconds apart, within a section of space a bit larger than my fist at arms length. I’d say these were about 35-40 degrees above the horizon and very distant from me. Sorry, I have no pictures.
