{rjl} We observed Multiple starlike objects move across the sky, using the same trajectory.

On 3/26 at 8:20pm, I observed multiple (about 20) starlike objects move across the sky, using the same trajectory. When I first noticed the first object, it appeared in the west side of the sky between the moon & the brightest star which was directly above the moon (visual distance: about 6 fingers above the crescent moon). The object appeared inbetween the moon & the star, it appeared like a faint star moving in a steady northerly direction. 
At first, I thought it was a satelite because of the steady movement & the faintness. I could tell it wasn't an airplane because there were no blinking lights, just a steady starlike object & faint. I called my daughter inside the house to come outside to view it. When she came out (approx 20 secs after), we noticed another object appear again between the moon & bright star moving in the same direction as the first object I noticed. By then the 1st object had moved to the right & had become too faint to view or disappeared. As we viewed the 2nd object move across, we noticed a 3rd object appear from the same vicinity as the first 2 & move in the same direction. Then we saw another & another several more times until we lost count (20 or more). Each object appeared in approx10-15 second intervals. 
Oddly, after about the 6th or 7th object, we started to notice that the objects were now appearing above the bright star (about 3 fingers high from the star). 

This occurred for about 10 mins. After that, the objects' appeared less frequently. The last object that we observed appeared over the bright star to the left. At first, it was faint, then suddenly the light turned BRIGHT as if it was an airplane & it had shown it's headlights in our direction. The light was as bright & bigger than before (yet, not as big as the bright star). At that point, I told my daughter that it must be an airplane because of it appeared to be moving toward us in our direction. But as I said that, however, the light dimmed & started moving towards the right again. We waited a few mins & didn't see any other lights & we went inside the house. Eerie. 

Unfortunately, I wasn't able to take video or photos because the lights were too dim to appear on the camera. My other daughter in Peoria, AZ, also witnessed one of the last objects after I called her.
