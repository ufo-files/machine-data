looked out west side of house for snow, saw object in sky moving eastward - went to east side of house and saw again moving away

I went to look out back door for possible snowfall as reported to be possible tonight. I saw the object in the sky coming from the west and moving steadily toward the east. I proceeded to the front of the house where I saw it continuing east. I asked my wife to come look, and she commented on it as well. It continued travelling in an easterly direction, but I lost track of it as it was too cold to stand outside w/o shoes on. During the entire experience, there was no noise from the object observed, or from anywhere else since we live near edge of a very small town that "goes to bed" pretty early at night.
