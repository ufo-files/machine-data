Just before the midhnight (new 2012) on NY eve

Very beautiful summer nigh on NY eve 2011. I was outside in the front-yard with good friend of mine (who also is eyewitness). We where preparing to celebrate the NY 2012 and we had nice conversation outside when suddenly big red shining object appear high in the sky. 
I was overwhelmed with the even as never before of my life. I strongly believe in life outside our living and visible sight on Earth. 
I knew immediately what was it so I thank universe to give an opportunity to see it. Of course I took my iPhone and start filming. My friend (Ivan) also recorded on his mobile. The object was flying high predominantly red but with some yellow-orange shining on it. After some time the object "switch-off" the lights and continue flying and then vanish into the universe. 
The footage is not so good as at night time iPhone is not brilliant for filming, but I was able to record more than a minute.
