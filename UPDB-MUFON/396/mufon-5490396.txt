{cak} 2 large orange lights directly above each other. Stationary. Disappeared when looked back to check on them.

Was leaving house to go over to friends house. Wife left at same time in different vehicle. Right after leaving driveway, we both noticed 2 large orange objects directly above each other in the southern sky. They were larger than any other objects out that night. Initially though it was a new radio tower, as we had never seen them before. Observed objects and discussed with wife before going about our business. Rode to my business to check the mailbox on my way out, objects were no longer in the sky. Never appeared to us again, no new radio tower, and 2 close friends also observed the same described objects about 2-3 months later.
