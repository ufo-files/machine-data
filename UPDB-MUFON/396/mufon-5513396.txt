two ufos playing an apparent game of chasey!

On the night of the 20th december 2012,at 11:15pm, my partner and myself took the time to look upon the stars, when she alerted me to the moving lights. We watched on to see them dart around as if one was actively chasing the other, this was at some great distance away although they soon came closer to our position, they both appeared to be of exactly the same type of kraft brightly light with a white glow, they continued on with the chase at varing proximity to one another, as close as nearly touching to around 1000 metres apart. they seem to be able to increse in brightness then almost go so dull as to not be visable. the best veiw was when the chasing craft made a swooping decention and it turned as it assended, we were able to see the crafts shiny silver oval shaped outline. It glowed blue above but still maintained a white clouded mist about it. it then left in the direction of the other craft to eventualy become lost in the c above the city.
