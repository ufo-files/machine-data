Beautiful sky, was 'drawn' to snap some shots of the sky, I did notice this cloud and think I must enhance this but then I took other shots and didn't check back on this cloud.

#1. I was going and coming from work: On the way towards the foothills Village of Robb Alberta being a hobby photographer I felt 'drawn' to take some pictures of the beautiful sky. I stopped at a well site road and took a couple of shots. When I arrived at my work site I reviewed the pictures I took. I became very excited and apprehensive, then decided to leave the work-site (optional workday)to return and take more pictures. I scanned the sky for metallic bird-size shapes but all I could find of interest were clouds. I Noticed a particularly strangely complicated cloud and thought I will have to have a closer look at those pictures on the computer. The cloud may have been moving slowly cloud-like or it may have been hovering For some reason I didn't think much more about documenting size, distance, with my outstretched fist but guess it would have been about basket ball size at arm outstretched. I have a feeling there were many more of these shapes traveling under that grey ceiling cloud. I get the feeling there is more to come, I should be there.
