Saw a grey after egg shape landing

Walking around an abandoned base in rudloe wiltshire which is well know for ufo rumours. Quick google search will tell you what it’s about there. I was walking around the area which I do to get some air and alone time. I saw a egg shaped shiny object slowly hovering over the tree line and then behind. I assumed it touched down so I went to check it out. When I get to the area I saw it go down/land I started feeling massive static electrical charge around the area and a metallic kind of smell. I then saw in the corner of my eye a tall looking skinny large headed man. On closer look it was what I have read about a grey ? There was 2 of them looking at the main large house which was used to investigate ufos many years ago. I saw them looking around and I could here them talking but their mouths did not move. It was coming from inside my head. I didn’t understand what they were saying at all but they were conversing. I stayed back I was frozen in fear, I took a step back breaking a small stick which they both quickly looked and saw me. They disappeared behind the building and the eggs shape quickly rose up above the tree line and at a click of a finger it’s was gone.
