Long line of lights flying single file

A straight line of 40 to 45 lights heading from west to southeast. We first saw them when we were looking at the moon, the evening star and the constellation Orion. There was no sound and they were faster than any jets I have seen. About 20 minutes later we saw more just like the first but not as many. We could tell they were not one big long object because their spacing varied as they flew in a row.
