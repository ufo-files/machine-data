{rjl} craft appeared wingless fuselage, no contrail, Black stripe or band in the center

While scanning the sky for strange clouds I noticed a white object approaching from the east. When it got close enough it looked similar to a craft I had seen enter a cloud but not come out of it in march 2017. I watched it until it was out of my sight. I saw another white object approaching from the same direction. I waited to observe it. Ditto. no visible wings, no contrail, black band around the center. I watched it until it left my sight then saw a third object approaching from the same direction . It was a repeat of the first two. After the third object was out of sight I waited for a couple of minutes before getting in my car to continue shopping.
