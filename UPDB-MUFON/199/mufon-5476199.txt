{jpg} Fast Moving Bright Orb encircled my friend and I and continued down the street, turning into random homes and turning at the end of the street

Biking down Rabbit Road toward San-Cap Road on Sanibel Island Fl at night with a friend. When a bright green orb (we think it was green, our memory could be a little hazy on the specifics about the color) came up from behind, it seemed to have scanned us somehow, it put every hair on my body on end, and proceeded down the street leaving a glowing trail, similar to when someone moves a glow stick really quickly in the dark, except this trail went all the way down the road due to the objects speed. The objects path was strange because it had straight lines and organic lines as well, appearing to go in and out of driveways on either side of the road as it headed north toward San Cap Road, turning east toward Captiva. 

Once we arrived home a few blocks distance up the road, we received a call from a friend who had been on his way to meet us from off the island. This Friend informed us there was a UFO with lights on it moving over the island towards Captiva, in the direction the UFO we encountered was heading. Except wherever this UFO went, the power grid went out. That was when the power to our home and block went out.
