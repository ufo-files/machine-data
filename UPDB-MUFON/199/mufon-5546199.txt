Orange ball spotted in E/SE sky twice in one half hour. Moved rapidly. Stopped for 30 seconds, then shrunk or flew away.

During a 4th of July bbq, approx 10 guests spotted and orange ball in the E/SE sky over the Tunkhannock/Falls area. The orange glow made no sound and moved a fairly rapid speed, then stopped completely for approx 30 seconds. It seemed to just disappear after that. Some witnesses thought they may have seen red lights going around it. About 20-30 minutes later, the exact same event happened again. this time, there was approx 12-15 witnesses. Two witnesses thought it may have possibly been a helicopter while others said that it didn't at all resemble one. It was a very obvious large orange glow. I'm interested to see how many other people saw this.
