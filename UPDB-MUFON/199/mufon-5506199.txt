{cak} Pictures in night camera appearing within 8 days of each other.

Our sightings were captured in night camera photographing deer feeders. My husband was unbelieving of the Cotulla, Tx. sightings until he saw the objects in the sky in the night camera over our ranch, approx 35 miles from Cotulla. Photos spanned from Oct. 25 to Nov 1, 2013. Sighted at least 5 different shapes, all at night with ligqhts glowing. There are 2 daytime sightings, one being a disc, the other being a muffin shaped. These were all in the sky and not lights from a land vehicle. We are located in the Eagle Ford Shale oilfields.
