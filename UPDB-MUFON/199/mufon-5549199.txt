I was on the 4th floor balcony overlooking the Atlantic ocean. I was looking at planets and stars with binoculars. I saw a fast moving object tht i thought was a plane. I began watching it through the binoculars. It caught my attention because it was moving very fast across the sky. It appeared as a flashing white light. It was quite far off in the distance, beyond the pier which is approx 2 - 2.5 miles from my building. As I watched, I thought my hands were moving because the object appeared to jump around. I tracked the object and I steadied my hands against the rail and as I looked for the object, it had disappeared, however, there were many small red sparks or flashes of light above, around, under, and over where the object would have been. The red lights sparkled one at a time, and then disappeared also.I thought maybe the object had gone into the clouds and I just couldn't see it, however, it never reappeared. I felt like it disappeared because someone on it knew it had been seen.
