I was video taping the night sky when a flash caught my eye I then zoomed in to see this object

I have been taping the nights sky for some time now because I have been seeing this star like object in the southwestern sky that looks like nothing I have seen in my 37 years of star gazing. When on March 27 2011 at or around 11:30 that night I was outside in my front yard video taping that object that I have been noticing when a flash that looked like a shooting star caught my eye it shot across the sky from east to west and then suddenly stopped which amazed me because of what it was able to do I then proceeded to zoom in for a closer look. There were two objects one was a flashing light that was above and to the right of the other which I could not focus on and the other looked like a cigar or some sort of cannon and what look to be something above this object honestly it looked like the sky above this object was warped or opened opened up I wish I could find the words to describe what I saw very humbling for me. Unfortunately my battery started to flash because it was low and I could only get approximately 7 minutes of video of this object with my cameras battery being low it kept me from getting a better picture because it wouldn't let me use my night vision which if I could have used that I would have seen this ship with a better view This object did not move it was stationary in the western sky very very high in the sky I can only wish I had a full battery so I could have gotten more video time of it
