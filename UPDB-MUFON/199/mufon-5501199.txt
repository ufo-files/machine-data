quite well defined shadow

i have video footage from a gopro camera mounted on the bonnet of my car, i had been filming the road as i drove for about 2/3 miles. i arrived at road works and when i was pulling away i say a shadow on the ground, it was following the line of the road. I thought it was a low flying aircraft and checked my mirrors behind me but saw nothing, i also noticed it made little or no sound. i am guessing it was doing about 60mph.
On the video you can clearly see a shadow moving on the road, it takes up over half of the width of a normal A road and is quite well defined suggesting to me that it not very high,there is no aircraft seen in the video and you can clearly see the sky above the shadow and i am sure you would notice an aircraft.
There is a commercial airfield nearby but as the plane like shadow was quite small, quiet and low i don't think it was from there. The shadow shape appears to have 3 parallel tubes/ fuselages and 2 small tubes shapes at the back.
