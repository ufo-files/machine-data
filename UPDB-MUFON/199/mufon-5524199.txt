I was in airliner. Obect came toward us at high rate, stopped and turned.

I was moving to California. I was aboard a Southwest Airline flight in the last leg toward California. I would guess we were somewhere between Arizonia and California sometime during late morning. We were at speed and altitude. I happened to look out the window and saw a cylinderical object coming at us at maybe a 60 degree angle at a SUPER high rate of speed. I estimate at probably 6-8 times our rate of speed. It was probably a couple of thousand feet below us. It had things on the outside. I can't remember exactly what. When all of a sudden it came to a complete stop, then turned 90 degrees to the east and accellerated out of sight at an extreme high rate of speed. I heard the guy sitting behind me say " Holey S*** ". I turned to him and asked "Did you just see that?". He said "I don't know. I just don't know". I asked the pilot about it when deplaning but he pretended he didn't see anything. Yea, right.
