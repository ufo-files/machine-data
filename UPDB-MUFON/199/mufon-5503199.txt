{rjl} Bright fuzzy light at very high altitude at least 5 times brighter than the stars, absolutely no sound.

My grandson and I were sitting in the hot tub when this object came out from behind our backyard trees almost directly overhead at a very high altitude. It was obviously not a plane cause it was round and bright white with a fuzzy edge and actually seemed to be glowing. There were no blinking lights like a plane would have and it was at least 5 times bigger and brighter than a star. It was traveling almost due south but slightly easterly and made no noise. I continued to watch it until it disappeared over the horizon. A plane came over a few minutes later traveling at high altitude in a slightly south west direction. The sound of the plane was trailing behind it by approximately 90 degrees so it was traveling pretty fast but only about half as fast as the ufo.
