{nd} I've never been this scared. The lights were too close this time...

It's April 5th, 2016. 5:06am and I cannot return back to my usual sleeping pattern. At 3:37am I woke up to use the bathroom. As I was sitting on the toilet I looked out the window to the right of me and saw blue lights zipping back forth horizontally across the road from where I live. They were so blue it was impossible not to see them. My first glimpse took my breath away. I had forgotten how to breathe and I just sat there shaking with fear. I'm in awe as to what I'm witnessing in this moment. Even as I am writing this I am still shaking with fear. There were two blue lights hovering back and forth as fast as a lightning strike would hit the ground. That was as fast as they were moving. I'm terrified! This is not the first time I have seen them out in this area. I'm not sure what to do. I'm scared for myself and my family. Oh and get this, I live between 4 cattle farms and the area that these lights were hovering were over at the one farm several hundred feet away from my house. Coincidence? I Only wish I could have gotten video feed of this experience. I know this won't be the last time I will see this but I really wish that it would be. I can't just be the only one who is seeing this out here.
