I left my house to get fire wood. I looked past my stack of wood. The disc shaped ship was just past it. I was around 15 feet of it.. I just stood there and looked at it. I don't remember how long I did. I just turned around and went into my house.
I worked outside for a living. For the next week or so I could see a light following me at times.l
