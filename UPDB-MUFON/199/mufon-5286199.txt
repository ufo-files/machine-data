3-4 Orbs potentially over Woodburn OR_7-29-21

Started out as 1 orb that i saw coming from the west and going east. The speed it was traveling seemed not humanly possible. At one point the orb looked like it was going to crash; it made this initial steep descent and then pulled up immediately and froze to the spot i took a photo of. Where the cigar/orb was in what seemed to be a vertical stand in orbit.

It was only after I reviewed the photos that I realized there may have potentially been up to 3 more orbs (one looks like its phasing out in one pf my photos) that were in the sky near where the 1st orb decided to stop. Actually what i would say here is that the other 3 orbs were waiting. the 3 orbs phased, while the one i photographed decided to keep heading east.

I lost time...the photos after 9:59pm went to 10pm and never changed time stamps...i moved to different spots to take the photos. The time stamps should've changed...like 10:01, 10:02, 10:03, at least if not beyond that.

the 1st orb that i really saw and photographed eventually continued east, and when it finally decided to continue east, it changed from the white glow to a yellow glow and was on a very straight unwavering path. When it turned yellow i would say that the speed seemed even faster than when it was glowing white as seen in the photos. It was so fast i couldn't catch it on the camera when it turned yellow. 

After the orbs were all gone, a few minutes later, out of the corner of my right eye, I noticed a glow coming from the wood, which is the direction and treetop that the orb went over. I never went to check it out or investigate. I was nearly having a panic attack at this point. The glow only lasted a few secs. I cant say for sure what it was, but I can say i do know it wasnt a fire that the neighbor had in their yard nearby. Wasn't the right color and really was to vibrant to be a bon fire.
