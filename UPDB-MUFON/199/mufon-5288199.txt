observed around 25 craft go across sky. SW to NE.

Phelan,CA. Sat outside around 8:50 PM. First saw 5 lights that were eliminated by horizon sunlight. Then dimmed down after about 5-6 seconds. To where it was hard to follow them. They came across and finally disappeared from sight. About 30 seconds. Then there was a string of them, right up 20, that followed the same path, speed, and direction, and altitude, and no kind of blinking or otherwise lights. I commented to myself. What the heck is going on here. Are these ours or theirs? I have observed other craft from time to time. Going over my head, where I live, going south to north. Or North to south. Sometimes veering towards the southeast. I have lived here for going onto 7 years. And have observed several craft doing this. Sometimes just one or up to three. Just a light.  Very high up. Just able to catch the movement. And their reflected sunlight caught off of the horizon. Until they are no longer observable. Or they go out of our atmosphere. Or come into our atmosphere. I have been a truck driver for 30 years. 25 of it going up and the I-5 corridor. And have had several unusual sightings. So I have seen enough of these things to know what is a commercial craft and what is not.
