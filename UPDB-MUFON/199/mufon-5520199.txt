I was in the bathroom and glanced up through the window and saw what at first thought was a glance gliding through the sky. It was near the area of the Toledo Express Airport in the southwest part of the sky. I watched the object and it began moving irradically up,down,side to side and I knew it wasnt a plane. I found my binoculars and looked at the object and could see a disc shaped object with the lights spinning around. I watched it approximately 5 minutes and took another look without the binoculars. When I tried to view it again with the binoculars it disappeared. 
 
March 8 the same object was appeared in the same area and I watched it for about ten minutes and it had the same lights and appearance.
