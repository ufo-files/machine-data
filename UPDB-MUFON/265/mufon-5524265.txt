Multiple UFO sightings on way to work.

About 9:19pm while driving to work I notice 4 or 5 bright yellow/organge orbs in the sky. I pulled over to see if I could get a photo. But they flickered out before I could. I then perceded on my way spotting only a few of them closer to town. Again flickering out quickly. Only a mile or so to work I looked up again and notice this time about 5 star like light blinking really rapidly. They were not in a specific formation or as best I could tell. I was so amped up at the sighting that I tried to call my friend. Though it took about 5 minutes to get through since it kept saying no network. Which is strange since I always get a signal. Finally, I got through to my friend and told her. Though while telling her it hurt my head. Then faded back to normal. Later that night I went on break and had another strange sighting this time it was a blue orb that shot across the sky. It looked similar to a shooting star only it was huge. I only wish that the people I was on break seen it to. Needless to say it was an eventfull night.
