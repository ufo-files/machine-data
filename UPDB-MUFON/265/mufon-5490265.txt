Two bright rotating lights

Driving between Spalding , Lincolnshire , UK and Gosberton , Lincolnshire , UK I observed two lights rotating clockwise around each other flying at great speed out of the window of my car , I was so amazed by this sight of agility I stopped in a lay-by to watch the display , the lights seemed to move at phenomenal speed always rotating around each other but moving freely about the night sky , stopping in places for a few seconds then darting off to another point , this went on for 7 or 8 minutes then quicker than anything I have ever witnessed before the ascended into the inky blackness and vanished , there were a few cars that had pulled over to watch this but nobody I knew , I then went home and told the family about it and thought of more of it until encouraged by a friend to report it to you.
