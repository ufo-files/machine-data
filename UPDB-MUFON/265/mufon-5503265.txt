Extremely bright lights, pulsating sound, metallic structured craft.

Approx 1 am April 19th - It started with the dog barking outside in the backyard which is fairly common. Upon opening the door I was taken back by a pulsating noise which seemed to run through my body. As a walked outside I noticed lights rising from behind a fence located approximately 20 feet away. At which time I was stunned and fixed on what I was seeing. The lights were very erratic ranging from red,white, blue and green. The objects and lights were facing me as it began to rise, at which point I yelled for my girlfriend to come outside. We both stood in amazement as we couldn't explain anything about what we were witnessing. The way it rose/flew was so effortless and steady, the noise it made was a slow but heavy pulse felt throughout our bodies. As it rose above the surrounding buildings the street lights allowed us to view the underside of the craft. It was somewhat triangular/ teardrop with a slightly rounded front where the lights were mounted coming to a sharp point at the rear. The craft was dark and metallic looking and appeared to be extremely heavy despite how effortlessly it moved. It made a very large circle around our house covering a vast area in a unexplainably short period of time passing right through the original viewing area before it took off at a steady angled incline. As it past through the original viewing area we manged to snap a cell phone picture which seemed to catch the lights on the object as well as some of its shape.
