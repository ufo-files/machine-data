Flying due north from the south.

I thought it was an aircraft at first with flashing navigation lights but upon further examination it appeared as though it firing some sort of thrusters. There was absolutely no noise coming from it. It was flying, I'd say, at 3-4000 AGL at aprox. 2-300mph. It continued a northern tragectory until it left our line of sight.
