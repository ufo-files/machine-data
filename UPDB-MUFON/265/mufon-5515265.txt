{nd} Noticed object moving SW to NE at high altitude.  There was no contrail, and when it went into clouds I lost sight f it

I was in my car at work and happened to look up at the sky.The object was in my line of sight. I have no idea what the object was, but it appeared to have no wings or fins and was not emitting a contrail like a jet does at a high altitude. The object was ascending from SW to NE in a straight line. My only reaction is curiosity that something that high in a cold atmosphere would leave no contrail. Afterwards I went back to work, no afterthought except curiosity. I lost sight of the object when it went into the clouds. Cloud tops were high approximately 25,000 ft. Object would appear the size of a baby aspirin at arms length.1
