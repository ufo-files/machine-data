{tjd} 5 objects going E to w in mostly straight line. 2-3 objects on same course followed. Then 1 following

While getting mail in Arlington Texas at home I looked up at the stars. I had been looking at the planets in early September with my telescope. Sky was clear at 8:45 om CST. I saw 5-6 objects traveling on a straight course mostly in line with each other. This was followed by 2 objects going same course. This was followed by 1 object on same course. Distance between objects did not vary. Color all the same which was similar to color of Mars. The sighting lasted less than a minute. Wasn't a meteorite. Too slow. Too fast for a plane. The objects traveled in same direction until disappearing. My first though was satellite when watching them.Only feeling was I wish I could have videoed objects.
