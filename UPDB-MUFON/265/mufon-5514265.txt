(GS) 8 fire ball like orbs traveling horizontal east to west at a speed that is impossible

I was outside on a break at work and looked up at the sky to observe Orions Belt and was shocked to see 8 HUGE orb shaped objects traveling from east to west across the sky at an amazing speed that can not be humanly possible. In a matter of seconds they had traversed the sky from east to west in a straight line horizontally. It was clear they were far beyond our atmosphere yet they were not shooting stars or falling stars or any other kind of space debris. These where objects traveling in a path at an amazing speed and traveling together. The event happened so quickly that there was no time to grab a camera or a witness. It was an amazing sight to see and so hard to describe it properly!
