Thirteen to sixteen objects moving and hovering, continually changing direction  . No aircraft militarily or otherwise in vicinity. i

My wife was walking the dogs. 23:55. Along our street when she looked up and noticed a number of moving star like objects in the southern sky. She quickly came inside and notified me. I got my binoculars and observed them going to and fro constantly changing directions. I counted between 13/16 of them. We observed them for about ninety minutes and then went to bed.
I had seen one or two of UFO's many years previously. We both found the observation very exciting. Because it reconfirmed our belief of other inter planetary life.
