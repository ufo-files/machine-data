{cak} I saw an object in the sky that caught my attention! It was amazing and I shot a video of it.

I was coming home from the Detroit area when I noticed an object in the sky just west of I75. It wasnt moving and it was rather large in size. There were planes in the area however; they were small in size compared to what I saw. As I got closer to the Downriver area it began moving south still west of I75. I tried to take pictures of it but was unsuccessful. Part of this object had a partial shape of a star (3 points) that I could see. I could see a small amount of color protruding from the points. As I got off on my exit it switched directions due east, toward the lake. When I was dropped off at home, I hurried up and got in my car and went down to the lake. The object was hovering and I didn't notice any sound(there were NO planes in the area at the lake). I got my camera out and took a video. This was one of the most amazing things I have ever witnessed. I shot one quick video, then I got scared and jumped in the car. Then I decided to go back because I figured, Ill never get to see something like this again! I took a video that lasted about six minutes or so. This object was incredible. It moved like something I have never seen before and the colors were beautiful. The object was moving side to side incredibly fast so quick it appeared to change shape. The movements were erratic - one second it would be still, then it would go straight up, zig zag then drop straight down. I drove back home and called my brother so he could see it for himself. When he got outside it was gone! I truly wish I would have stayed at the lake to see how it took off or at least caught that part on video.
