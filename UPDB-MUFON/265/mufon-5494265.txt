{nd} A small craft with a weird wing

Upon my return from New Mexico I was driving on H-287 towards Ft. Worth when I saw this bright object ... round with a black wing(?) protruding from the craft. I couldn't make out if it was a wing or just part of the object. It was between the clouds, got brighter as if ready to leave the planet and off it went and disappeared. Lasted about 5 seconds if that. I could not track it nor did i see it again.
