{cak} Multiple UFO's sited, that as a unit looked like a moving constellation

I was with a group of friends at the Baseline Woods Apartment complex to hang out and have a few beers. It was during the weekend of the annual Oregon International Air Show. I only filmed a minute and a half of the action, and part of me wishes I kept filming. The original video is on my Facebook, where only my friends can view it. The footage I am sending is screen recorded from my phone. 

When we first noticed the UFO(s) it looked like one giant flaming object was on its way to crash onto the earth's surface. It then suddenly started splitting into multiple vessels. These vessels then started moving in some strange formations, many of which looked like constellations. At one point they formed what looked like the Big Dipper. I don't know what I saw, but I don't think it was drones.
