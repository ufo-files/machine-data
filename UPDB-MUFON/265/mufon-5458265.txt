{jpg} Observed hovering triangle shaped three lights, changed direction and appeared to disappear quickly

Traveling north on I-15 between mile 120 and 121 near Manderfield Utah. Looking NW, near Granite peak, three bright lights in a tight triangle shape clearly observed. Lights hovered for approximately 3-4 seconds while I was looking at it — at this time, two other aircraft at a higher elevation and quite a distance away were also observed. (Not a commercial aircraft area; but these two were clearly planes). The three tight triangle lights then appeared to “fall” rapidly to a diagonal, still remaining in the NW sky, but quickly losing elevation. After approximately 4 seconds of falling, the lights all completely disappeared.
