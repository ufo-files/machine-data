Three orange lights hovering independendly in a triangle formation.

I had left a friends home late when looking in a northern direction i notice three orange lights hovering independently in a triangle formation. The brightness of the lights would fluctuate very slowly. No air traffic around. No noise could be heard. I observed the objects for a long time and was confident it wasn't of human engineering. I estimate the lights were at least 3 to 4 miles(approx 6km)away. It was late and had to leave. Years later i met a UFO enthusiast who ironically had seen and filmed the same lights on the same night from a completely different part of town, long before i had ever met him.
