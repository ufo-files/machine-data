Seven bright lights loosely in triangle formation

My wife and I were on our balcony around 10pm. It was night at that time of the year, but there was lots of glow from the city lights as usual. The sky was cloudy but not completely overcast. We saw seven bright green lights flying roughly from the lakeside in an approximate SE-NW path. For those familiar with Toronto, it looked like it was flying over Spadina or University Ave. 

To be honest, it looked like a flock of geese, but they seemed to be flying unusually high. After a few seconds, the formation disappeared above a grey cloud, never to be seen by us again. Also, it seemed to me very odd that geese can reflect that much greenish light at night from that distance. It might have been a group of drones? It made no sound, but on the other hand it looked very far high in the sky, probably as high as piper aircrafts fly.
