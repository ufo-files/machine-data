(GS) A large yellowish-white pulsating light caught our attention.

We were driving back from a party when we saw a large pulsating yellowish/white light in the sky. At first I though it could be a headlamp from an airplane but the duration and characteristics seemed to void that. Another thought was a planet but I have never seen a planet in that direction (NW) nor have I seen one pulsate like this one. It began at about 40 degrees on the horizon and slowly sunk to a mere 10 degrees or so over 20 minutes. I am always keeping my eye to the sky so this really caught my attention. I have seen objects in the past but I don't like to report much anymore yet this seemed to warrant a report and I was able to capture three separate videos which I have posted with this report. We had some pics but they turned out way to smeared during to a moving vehicle to be of use. My wife and I eventually lost sight since it ducked behind a distant hill.

At 40 secs into the "mufon2" video you see it peak through the trees over the rogue river as we crossed the bridge in town.
