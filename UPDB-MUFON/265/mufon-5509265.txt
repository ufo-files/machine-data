{nd} Cigar shaped day ufo changes to sphere, vanishes.

I was waiting for a bus at the station and off in the distance (not sure how far), a faint flash caught my attention in the sky. It blended in quite well to the cloudless, sunny sky. I observed an object, cigar-like in shape, on a straight and downward path at first, then the object became smaller and spherical in shape. It stopped moving after a bit and stayed in one spot, where it seemed to have vanished completely. I was unable to continue to view it as I was traveling at that point. Prior to leaving the station (and after it "vanished"), I had noticed an airplane in another part of the sky opposite the ufo. It was lower in the sky than the object I observed, and there was a clear contrail whereas the object made none.
