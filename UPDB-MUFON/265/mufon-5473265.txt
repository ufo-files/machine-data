{dr}Two UFO's outside house near school

On October 4th 2017 at 6:12 am in Chester field County Virginia, I was leaving my house to head to work. It was still dark outside since the sun hadn't risen yet. I looked across the street at the school while walking to my car and I saw and object hovering in the sky. It had Two Main lights on either side and a few between the two that were flickering different colors. I then turned around and looked above my house toward my backyard and saw another craft looking exact to the first one. Both Just hovering. I then took out my phone and attempted to take a video but didn't have that great of a video because of the camera. I put the phone away and kept watching them, the first one by the school began to come toward my house at the same elevation and as that one moved the other began to move away and change direction to the right of my home and i lost sight. I watched the first UFO pass over my home and i could see the underbelly, it was lit up and i could only see that it was shaped like a cigar almost and it had no sound what so ever. I then ran to my car and because i not only had to go to work but I was also frightened. I edited and slowed down the video i took so you can get a better look it is only of the first UFO because i couldn't get my camera to focus on the second.
