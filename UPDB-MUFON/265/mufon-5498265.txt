Roughly at low helicopter level. As it continued its path a stationary light from below bounced reflected off the bottom of the craft. The body was lack & shiney and reflective with two non-reflective panels under each wing.
As it disappeared behind a treeline heading away from me, towards the airport, I can see two aircraft flying slightly towards one other, yet some distance from each other. Suddenly they both simultaneously took evasive action. Trying to avoid something in the air directly ahead of their own flight paths.
