Captured on live recording through ISS Live app

I'm an avid user of the ISS live app and this particular incident caught my attention due to having never previously seen light activity within the atmospheric layers of earth. The objects became visible as the ISS passed over Argentina (travelling South East direction) at around 20:50hrs within the respective time zone. There were many intermittent lights - some just flashed momentarily and others were like shooting stars and travelled downwards, and some upwards from earth - all of this appeared to be in the general area of the south Atlantic and Antartica.
ISS was still in daylight but travelling towards the dark side of earth where the objects were.
At first I thought the objects were perhaps particles or interference however when I took screen shots of a number of the objects this clearly was not the case - they were giving off their own light source.

I have used the ISS app for a long period of time and have never seen anything like this before.

I cannot view all of the footage frames as I do not have a programme advanced enough however I have managed to produce a small number of enhanced screen shots.
