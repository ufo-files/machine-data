{jpg} Glowing, spinning, blue, silver, white light. Hovering, 5 min, then zig zagged at amazing speed.

We were vacationing in Ocean City, New Jersey. I was sitting on the back deck facing the bay. I noticed a very bright star, as I watched and compared to other stars, this one seemed to be hanging from a string almost, like not stuck in the sky as the other stars. It then appeared to be spinning, changing colors, silver, blue and some red. At this point, I called my Husband and his cousin out to the deck. My husband was a Humane Officer, his cousin was a supervisor at a Refinery. My husband said let's line the object up w the pole on the porch, to see if it was moving. We could then see it was moving very slowly. We are not sure how far away it was. The next day I drove to where I thought it was, which was 5 miles, but we don't really know.
We watched the object for 10 min. or so, then it zig zagged and sped off at amazing speed in a northerly direction to out of sight. We were certainly bewildered, though not concerned, took no action ie contacting the police or airport.
