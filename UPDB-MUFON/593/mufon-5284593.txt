UAP disappeared and only visible on film, it is observed again, but as an orb.

Footage was taken on a Sunday late afternoon 29 August 2021 at approxamitely 16:00. The black object, visible far left of the frame, was moving from East to North West, at quite a rapid pace, but then disappeared. After the footage was reviewed, an orb is observed to appear as a flash that was not visible to the naked eye. This is not the first nor last UAP observed by the filmmaker. A balloon filled with helium was observed at very close range (about 20 meters from the ground) about 30 minutes later floating over the same residence but from North to South. The filmmaker had various sightings of UAP's and an encounter with a luminous big oval eyed, angelic being in 2016 with featherless wings. Proof of this exist.
