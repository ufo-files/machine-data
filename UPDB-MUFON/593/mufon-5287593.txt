Brightly lit orb of several colors high in the sky

I was outside to view and take pictures of the Super Bloom Blood Moon Total Lunar Eclipse on May 26, 2021. I was outside for about 20 min. I was focused on the moon so I didn't pay much attention to the rest of the sky. There were also tall trees obstructing my or making it harder to see the moon. The sky was clear of any clouds, there was no breeze. I was sitting on a small hill behind my house, there was one lamppost off to my right side down the hill. After taking several pictures of the moon, and getting frustrated with my camera equipment and the limited time I had to capture the eclipse, I was getting quite chilly so I packed up and went back inside. It wasn't until I uploaded my pictures to my computer that I first noticed and a friend I had sent it to noticed it as well before I had mentioned it. There was no editing of the picture except to zoom in on the object in the sky. In proportion to the moon at that time, this was higher in the sky and appeared larger than the moon from my vantage point. You can see in the picture the moon between some trees and to the left upper corner the UFO stood out even among the other stars out. It has a sort of square or rectangle shape from the camera's perspective. You can clearly see colors of green, yellow, pink, white, with some in very distinct circular shapes. There was also some sort of lighter mist or trail coming away from it.
