I went outside to let dogs out and saw lights hovering above a tree behind my neighbors house.

I was at home watching my sister and stepped outside to let the dogs out. When i walked on my front porch, i saw three lights that were not consistent with aircraft, almost like an orange-blue color, above a barren tree in my neighbors yard north west of my front door, but the lights stayed still and did not move. It was a clear winter night. It was at a fairly low altitude and i watched the lights for roughly 30 minutes before they vanished before my eyes. I was absolutely dumbfounded by what I saw. I had never seen anything like it before in my life, and told my father about it later that night. I have always wondered about the incident, but thought nothing of it until this morning when I saw Chris Bledsoe saw the same thing around the same time. What astonished me even more is that Fayetteville is an hour away from Rockingham. There are many aircraft I have seen fly over the house before, but nothing like what I saw that night.
