3 slowly blinking lights

I got up early to get a drink of water and looked out the kitchen window. About 3/4 of a mile east of me, over a nearby subdivision, I saw what I thought at first was a plane going to the nearby Aurora airport heading west. It had slow blinking lights. It seemed large and flying very low. It then stopped and hovered for about 10 minutes, I am not sure exactly, it was a long time. I took a picture and then went back to bed. It was still there when I looked out my bathroom window. It just kept on hovering over these homes. Unfortunately, my camera could not get a clearer view (of course). I am writing in hopes someone else saw it, as it was there for so long.
