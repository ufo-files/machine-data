{dr}At a 4th of July fire work display 3 saw darting lights in the sky away from event.

My husband and I were at my mothers 4th of july fireworks display along Cuba Lake NY my husband and I looked off to the side of us away from the fire works event and saw strangely moving lights darting around then quickly darted off and disappeared. We spoke to a friend a year later that was at the same event and she said YES!! I saw that too! We discussed how strange the movement was that no machine we know of could move that way or that quickly.
