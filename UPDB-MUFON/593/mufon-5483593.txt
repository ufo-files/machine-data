{cak} Bright circular orb that flew over a car I was driving. Also witnessed by passenger, definitely not plane/helicopter

I was driving north on Dutton Road in Sudbury, MA when a bright, large circular orb flew over the car I was in very low and very fast before disappearing from view. Only lasted a few seconds but it was so large, fast, bright, low and circular that it was unlike any known human-made craft I had ever seen. Upon first seeing it myself and my friend in the passenger seat both agreed it was a UFO immediately, very excited and amazed at the event. The road I was traveling on was a somewhat major road in this small town with many trees so we only had a quick glimpse of the craft as it flew directly overhead.
