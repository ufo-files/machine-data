{nd} Observed landed craft and they could read our minds and let us know their thoughts, then saw it zoom back out to space

Driving in the country with two friends clearly saw the UFO landed in a pasture near a small pool. Colored lights circled around the base of the craft in a random pattern. It was a dull dark silver with a window all around the center but could not see through it like really dark sunglasses. We could feel their minds;they were surprised as this was not a busy road. We felt almost hypnotized and very scared. As soon as they saw us the craft lifted slightly about 6 inches off the grass and the lights started going faster as the speed of the craft kept pace with our car. It was under a long row of trees and kept pace with us as fast as we went.It reached the last few trees and rose instantly to above the trees and paced us to the end of the treeline, told us not to tell anyone,said goodbye and shot up into the sky instantly. All of us humans felt groggy and I am sure we lost at least an hour. We agreed to never speak of it and only a few know.
