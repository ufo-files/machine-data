fast silent round flying object

I was lying on pool deck just looking up the sky enjoying the nice weather I noticed a plane flying N-NE,then just looking straight up a fast moving round object came into view. It was moving in a W-NW direction. It seemed to be at a higher alttitude than the jet and moved in a straight path, it took about 4 seconds to be out of sight. So if you lay on your back and track your finger in a straight line and count to 4, thats how fast this was moving. There wasn't any noise or contrails or wings as I know them.
