{rjl} Transparent UFO with golden outline

A buddy and I were riding bikes in a wooded area near my friends home. We were most likely in second or third grade. The trails opened up to a long procession of city power lines. Not far above the lines my friend and I both witnessed a circular outline of something very large. However we could see through the object. It moved very slowly from our left to right and made no sound. Maybe a hum but that may have been the power lines. It was a bright, golden circle with a second circle in the center of it. We both were horrified and rode about a mile back to his house where our parents were visiting each other. They immediately separated us and had us draw and describe what we had seen. I never saw his drawing but both parents say we drew the exact same thing. My parents always claimed it was a reflection of some type. To this day I do not believe it was a reflection, because it was a crystal clear, warm day.
