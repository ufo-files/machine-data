I was walking my dog when I noticed what i thought was an an airplane with orange light. moving in a straight line north to south I didn't think anything about it and dismissed it. Then I looked up again and saw another one. Then I thought airplane's aren't orange, maybe it's a helicopter, but I didn't hear anything. Then I saw another, I ran inside and called my husband. As he comes outside we see another. This one stops behind our house, hovers for a few seconds then the lights go off and it disappers.
