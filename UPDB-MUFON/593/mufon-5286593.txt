Very bright, star like, steady light. No visible strobe lights. No noise.

Seen in the SW from my back door at around 02:30. A very bright, star like, light which remained constant. It moved, slowly (under 100 mph) from the SW Easterly direction to the SE. No noise was heard from it and it appeared to pass under a commercial aircraft which was at high altitude and moving from North to South. I could faintly hear the commercial aircraft and could see the strobes on it. I could see no strobes of any kind on the UFO. 
Unfortunately I was unable to get a photo.
