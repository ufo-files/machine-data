{dr}4 glowing orange orbs hover then ascend at rapid pace

On Halloween, 2011 while trick or treating with my wife, 11 year old daughter and her friend, we observed about 2 blocks from our home, 4 orange like circular orbs about a mile in the air to the northwest. We all watched intently as the orbs hovered for several minutes in a diamond pattern. After about 10 minutes, the orbs ascended at a tremendous rate into space and out of site.
