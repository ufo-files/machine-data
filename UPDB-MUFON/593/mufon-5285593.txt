Orbs only visible on camera

My motion security system alerted me. When I looked I saw a white orb next to my porch. When I opened the door I wasn't able to see anything with the naked eye. I could feel a strong electrical charge that made all my hair stand on end. I watched the orb bounce around on the cameras for several minutes. I then went to a dark room and took a photo with my cell phone, that's when I saw on my camera several large purple orbs about 500 yards across a corn field. I took a number of pictures. I have a large number of videos of the orb under different conditions. I live in the country, no light pollution and it was a moonless night. 
I'm unable to upload the videos from my security camera to this report.
