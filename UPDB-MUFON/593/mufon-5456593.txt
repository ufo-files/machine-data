Standing outside with the dog looking at the stars when I saw a line of lights blinking that appeared as high as the stars. I watched several fly above me when I realized I was seeing something I could not explain. I thought I was seeing things and wahed my eyes with my fingers to see if it was my eyes. When the were still there I counted an additional 50 lights which zigzagged across the sky from the SW to the NE disappearing over Mt Lemmon. I did not tell anyone for an=bout a week, but I know what I saw.
