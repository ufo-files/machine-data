{cak} A formation of about 30 - 40 bright yellowish star-like objects moving silently heading NE at night. Then came about 12 stragglers further apart. After long while burned out.

I was taking my dog for a late night walk. It was after 11:30 p.m. and dark. Suddenly I noticed a formation of 30 - 40 lights silently moving all at the same speed across my path about the height of planes. From where I was looking they appeared the same size as the stars but much closer and more yellow (and the stars were not moving). I could see more were coming. I knew this was not normal and looked around for someone to share this experience with. Unfortunately, I did not have my cell phone with me. I saw a man pulling up in his car but as I was about to go over, another man in his twenties came out of his house and I called out to him to look in the sky asking what it was. He had no idea and ran in to get his dad. They both came out and the younger man began filming on his camera. By now there were not as many as when I first saw them. They were spread further apart but there were still more coming. As we watched them head NE they appeared to burn out or disappear. Finally, the last one came all alone and went. I thought the last ones were moving a little faster than the first ones I had seen. They were all moving in the same direction silently. The guy in his car that I had first seen asked what they were so he had seen them too. We introduced ourselves and said goodbye. As I walked further along the street, a lady and man came out of their house, and I could tell they had seen it too. The lady told me they had also recorded the incident on her phone and said that is was a crazy experience. I am writing this as soon as I got home.
