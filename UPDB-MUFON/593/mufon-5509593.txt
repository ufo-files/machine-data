{kt}Unknown Object During Fireworks 2013

July 6th, 2013 during the annual local fireworks display I noticed a yellowish glowing object coming in from the West of the fireworks display, which was do North of my position. I had thought is was some firework being shot off from that area although I hadnt seen any other fireworks during the fireworks display. Thats most likely the reason it caught my attention in the first place. It seemed to be descending, then it seemed to move parallel to the deck (ground). It appeared to come out from nowhere and it disappeared into thin air, like it did when it appeared. I didn't say anything about it at the time, but upon reviewing the video clips of the fireworks I was surprise to have actually captured it for a brief moment when it disappeared.
