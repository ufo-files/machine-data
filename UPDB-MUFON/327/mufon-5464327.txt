{rjl} Lights moving in a linear formation over the land.

I was taking some night shots with my new camera on the Blue Ridge Pkwy in Virginia. I was too busy messing with the camera to notice anything going on in the sky. After I got home to look at the photos, I realized there were strange lights in the sky. In two pictures, taken roughly a minute apart from each other, 6 lights appear to be hovering in the left side of the shot. In the second picture, they seem to be leaving, with only 3 lights visible.
Initially I thought it was some lens artifact or aberration. But the camera shifted between shots and the lights remain in the same general position, relative to the land, leading me to believe I captured UFOs in the distance.
