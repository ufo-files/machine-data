{dr}Triangle shaped object that disappeared

I was walking my dog on the 700 block of Sand Piper St in Salem Oregon when I heard a propeller engine noise. I looked up but didn't see an airplane. My attention was then drawn to an object that was passing overhead. It was white in color and appeared to be a solid object. I was traveling slightly faster than an airliner. As I watched I realized the object was triangular shaped. I watched it for maybe 15 seconds when it suddenly just disappeared. At first I thought it had passed into a cloud but it didn't disappear like it had. It just vanished. I looked further along its path to see if it reappeared but it didn't.
