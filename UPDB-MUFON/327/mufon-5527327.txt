Green Flash shooting downward from sky

I was sitting in the passenger seat of my fiancee's car. We were literally 10 feet from pulling into our driveway when a green light from the drivers side caught the corner of my eye. It resembled a "glow stick" or fireworks which was my first thaught as to what it may be. The only problem is that it was falling downward on an angle, at a very fast speed and started from very high up. I turned my head and moved my neck to see around my fiancee's head and out her window, but the object was gone. My fiancee immediatly asked "you seen that too?" I said yes.  She, however, seen a differnt color, a discription she was so firm on that it almost sparked an argument, because I am sure the object was green.(is that common in sightings?) Before an arguemnt happend though, we turned the car around and drove up the road a little to the point where we both agreed the object may have touched gound. Of course there was nothing there and we just went home for the night.  Though I have held an intrest in UFO from youth, my fiancee has not and imeadiatly dismissed any supernatural origins. I only come forward now after watching an episode of the series "UFO's over earth" in which MUFON investigates a "flap" in bucks county, something I was completly unaware of until watching this TV show. I figure any info at all may help you guys
