{jpg} Glowing orb that emitted waves of light and changed direction

I was outside smoking a cigarette. It was roughly 11:45-12:00 Midnight throughout the duration of the event. I chose to turn off the back light and view the stars as it was a mostly clear night. For some reason, this particular light, that looked very much like a star, stood out to me. I fixated on it and, after a short time, I witnessed it move. In the following 10-15 minutes, I watched it move several times, not following any flight pattern that a satellite or plane would. Twice, I saw a faint emission of color leave the light, flowing like an aurora but pale in color. I also saw the light quickly and faintly change colors twice. While it was white in appearance 95% of the time, it did twice change to red, then purple. To be certain it was moving, I stabilized myself and used a tree branch for reference, which would be accurate as there was no wind to sway the branches. On numerous occasions I watched it dip behind the tree branches and subsequently be obscured from my sight. I attempted to take a photo and/or video, but was unable to do so.
