A string of bright white light that hovered for a few minutes, then began bobbing up and down while also moving in a circular motion, forward and back

Second night in a row observing these lights; first night was 2 of them, approx. 1/4 mile apart but at same altitude.
This report is on the sighting last night, when only 1 string of lights observed. Open farmer's field, with no buildings or light standards in area. Lights appeared about 2:30 am; I watched them bob around and emit a beam for approx an hour and fifteen minutes before I went back to bed.
First night was starry, but lights were not anywhere close to stars...very close to ground. 
Last night was starless and it was the only bright thing in the sky...still close to the ground, however in comparison to where I would have seen stars.
