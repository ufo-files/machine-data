{tjd} small black disk in the distance on a clear Alaska day

I was picking berries with my mom in late September on an abnormally clear day. The weather was so unusually beautiful that I decided to take some landscape photos. I did not notice the object until a friend pointed it out weeks later when we were debating which parts of Alaska had the best views. This area where the object was over is the middle of nowhere. The town of Sutton where we were is already on the edge of the Alaskan wilderness. The object was beyond the Matanuska river where there is literally nothing.

I saw no planes or helicopters that day, there were no sounds. We thought it might have been a parachute but it looks nothing like that and no one in their right mind would parachute into the wilderness out there.

Because I took 2 pictures of a very large frame within a minute of each other there was no way for a plane to come into the very middle of my fame in such a short time unless it was an SR-71. I suppose it could have came from over the mountains and just into view of the camera when the second photo was taken but we did notice anything when we were there. We stood outside just taking in the view for a few more minutes.

As an aside my parents who live in Sutton have been noticing a "woo woo woo woo" sound over the town as of the last couple of weeks and I noticed this as well. I took a video but when I listened to the video I could not hear the sound - perhaps you have audio equipment to isolate the sound. It was really strange and my parents indicated this was not the first time. It kind of sounds like a plane but not really

This sound was several weeks later after the sighting.
