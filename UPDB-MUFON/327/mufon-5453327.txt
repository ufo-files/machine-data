UFO sighting off of Pacific HWY 1

I was hanging out with a couple of friends before going into the military. We were at the cliffs off of Pacific HWY 1, between Santa Monica and Malibu. It was night time, it between 2130hrs and 2230hrs. I was staring at the stars in the sky over the ocean, thinking about the military and than it caught my attention. One star out of the rest just shot upwards and then stopped, it went down, than to one side, to the middle, and to the other side. I thought it was a satellite or something and than it zigzagged, it’s the only way to put it. I told my friends and they laughed thinking it was a satellite. I looked back it was moving erratically, than it had moved down, and it zigzagged again really fast. Finally it just shot off at a fast rate of speed. I had served in the Air Force for 15 yrs and I have seen aircraft fly. However I have never seen an aircraft move like that, not now, not then.
