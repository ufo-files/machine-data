{dr}observed black object stationary in clear blue sky over I-70 . Moved west slowly.Seemed to create heat waves around it. then vanished.

Driving to work between 7:30 and 8:00 on the Saturday after thanksgiving 2014. I noticed a black object in the sky over the road in front of me. At first glance, I thought it was a hot air balloon but then I knew it wasn't because it was 18 degrees, much too cold for that. As I got closer to the object, it looked more like the thing the astronauts used to land back on earth after going to the moon or the machines the people of India have ancient pictures of. It didn't seem to be moving. Its color was the darkest black you could imagine. The sky was clear with no clouds and no planes in sight. As I was coming up to exit 27, I stopped on the ramp. The car in front of me stopped also. The object started moving northwest and it seemed to be creating heat waves around it and seemed to be even darker black now. I decided to drive up the hill in front of me to get a better view of the object. It took me 15 seconds or so to get thru the parking lot and go behind the buildings. When I stopped, I got out of my truck. The object was gone. From my vantage point I could see for miles in the direction the object was moving but there was no sign of the object. The total time that elapsed from the first sight to losing sight of the object was around 5 minutes.
