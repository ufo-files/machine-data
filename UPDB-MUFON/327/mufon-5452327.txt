After viewing a Southwest airplane flying I noticed two round-like objects in the sky moving fast. They were lower than the airplane and moving South (airplane moving west). One object was slightly ahead of the other. Then I noticed another two objects behind the first two flying in same pattern. Moving too quick and fluid to be a bird and not big enough to be a plane. They also appeared to reflect the sunlight. 
Moffet Air Force base in close by so possibly a drone? But the shape and fluid nature of the objects made me uncertain.
