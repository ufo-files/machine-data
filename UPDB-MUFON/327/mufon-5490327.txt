Infrared lights, only seen through NVG's

I was a soldier in Afghanistan, and during my shifts on the base and very often out in the mountains during a snow storm these lights would appear and it was like they were scanning the mountains and would move faster than any object mankind has to the next mountain and do the same. Could only be seen through infrared. I called up on a recorded satellite radio to ask if we were being resupplied and they said no everything was grounded due to whether. Then within 4 miles 5 lights only seen through infrared appeared on the ground in perfect spacing and was moving like a convoy. Just when I thought it was friendly's i called and asked if we had ground supply and they said all units have stopped operating due to weather, then the lights moved in reverse and dissappeared. I watched a documentary and some of the things were simular to what I saw. I have always told people about it, but they think I'm just being silly. I'm telling you, no country has an something that can move so smooth nearly riding up and down mountains and moving to the next one like that.
