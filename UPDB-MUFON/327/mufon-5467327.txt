{jpg} Long slimy vertical cloud. No other clouds any where. Didn't move for 20 minutes. Have picture and witness

Myself and grandson going to work on a house near Jerome, Idaho. Both of is spotted this weird cloud. Tall skinny serial cloud. A cloud like a pole made of cloud material. Angle at3: 30. I took a picture with my phone. I was flabbergasted. I had never seen a cloud like that in my life. With no factory , fires, or no other clouds anywhere 
Strange. It took a very long time to move away. Didn't loose shape . It was seemingly stationary for a long time.
