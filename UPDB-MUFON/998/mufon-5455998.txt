{cak} Saw a line of lights

My husband and I came out to our front porch in the dark, clear evening to smoke a cig. Our home faces Longs Peak and the Rocky Mountain range. 
From the Boulder area we noticed a line of lights that are not usually in the night sky. It appeared the lights came in threes, a perfect line, appeared as if floating at a NE angle, but perfect line, not like being blown by wind. Immediately upon seeing them our whole back side became tingling. It continued for about 6-8 minutes we saw about 18, they all disappeared when they were about power line level. 
I took a video but can’t see the lights on the video, poor camera and too far away.
I hope they are the good guys!
