A single white light flew in front of a commercial airliner, circled, continued to lead, turned hard right and ascended quickly to northwest then vanished

I was in my driveway having a cigarette and looking at the stars and noticed that an airplane was coming in from the east and coming directly over (which is normal because I live about 2 miles from an airport) but thought it looked akward. Instead of just the red flashing light it also looked like it had a white light on the front. As it came closer it seemed like a single cylindrical-shaped aircraft (because of the altitude I could only see the lights) and also seemed to rotate counter-clockwise but passed under a few stars which I could see between the two lights, showing me that it was actually two aircraft ( one distinctly being an airplane). As they went directly above me I watched the single white light circle the plane 1 1/2 times then quickly darted in front again, lead for a few seconds, then make a hard right turn and accelerate up and to the northwest before disappearing. I also noticed that the faint sound of the jet was all that I heard, not anything unusual like I would have expected, just the sound of a single aircraft. The object moved swiftly and fluidly and did not flash. I am very excited to have seen this as this is the fourth time in my life that I have seen a ufo and I hope to see more! Also the airplane did not land here but kept going in the general direction towards seattle from Lewiston.
