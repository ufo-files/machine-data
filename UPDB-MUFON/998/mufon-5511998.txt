I noticed that they were directly aross from each other and both moving at same speed clockwise. They did this for some time. I said to neighbor what do you think this is. Another neighbor standing behind me said its a UFO! I called for my wife to come out. As I was watching them rotate trying to hear anything. There was no noise. All at once they stop rotating or moving at all. Then about 10 sec. went by and they both started moving to the SW from us and also speading apart from each other. Then I saw a third object same kind of light come into view. I was fixed on this and yelled at my wife to come out again. Then all at once a white orb looking object flew over my head coming from northeast to southeast direction. This could of been a meteor maybe, because after it went over very fast going towards where three other objects were it disappered. My wife ran back into house very scared. I observed objects for about a total of about 15 to 20 min., until they got so far from sight they were hard to see. Oh I had called Unisourse Elec. and reported power outage just before I saw all this. They told me they would call back when they got power back.
