{rjl} Fast moving blue light

In February 2014, just outside of Camp Lejeune military base in North Carolina, I noticed a blue light in the sky that appeared to be moving. I tried to photograph the object to see what it was, but still have no idea. I took a few with the moon in the photograph to show I wasnt moving my phone and to show the size.
