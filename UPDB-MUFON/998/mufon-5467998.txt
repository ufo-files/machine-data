While sitting in my sister's backyard on the evening of Saturday, June 9, 2018, I witnessed two glowing orange orbs slowly rise from behind the trees down near the river. These orbs slowly rose to about 50 feet above the ground, hovered briefly, and then disappeared into the cloud base. about a half an hour later, two more appeared from the same location and followed the same flight path. No sound was heard by any of the objects. This is about the 6th or 7th time I have seen these mysterious orbs since 2013, and have reported past sighting to MUFON a few years ago. My sister and brother in-law are also witnesses to this recent incident. Both agree that the objects are out of the ordinary. I have a YouTube video of the incident and will send link.
