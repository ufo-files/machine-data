I was driving with my windows down when I witnessed a fast black object going across the sky

I was driving with my grandmother to go get my grandfather from dialysis when I saw a very dark object that looked like a black bullet shaped fuselage with two small wings out the side. This object made no sound and must have been about 600-800 feet up. It was going 1.5 to 2x the speed of any normal aircraft that I see. It was gone across the horizon in about 40 seconds going southwest.
