Disappearing and Reappearing orb RETURNS!

I first saw this orb on the night of the 16th Sept 2011. It returned tonigh, the night of the 19th! I saw it when I happened to glance out my window, and it was in a completely different spot as the night of the 16th. It was now right in view out of my windows! I watched it until it disappeared to make sure it was not a plane or star. Just as the other night, it disappered starting with the outside of the circle and going in. I called my Husband out and just as I got him out it came back! We looked at it through binoculars and it disappeared once again. It did not come back a third time like the last one and it also never changed to red. It stayed orange. This happened at approximately 9:30pm and lasted for about 10 minutes in total.
