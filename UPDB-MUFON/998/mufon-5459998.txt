3-pointed object (star like lights) moved extremely fast, paused and bright lights would illuminate triangular shape - then go out, and repeat

Observed a 3-pointed object (star like lights made up the triangular shape of the moving object). This object was moving much faster than an airplane - did not have marker lights like a plane (e.g. no flashing lights on wings). There were six us in a backyard, all adults, that saw this object move in the night sky - like 3 stars connected and moving together. The object would pause for 30 seconds or so and VERY bright blue/white light that appeared to be casting from the bottom of the object and illuminated the triangular shape. It was as if it was casting a beam down towards the earth from cloudless night sky! The bright light would go out after 30 seconds or so and then continue on flight path (with slight but sharp turns/movement) heading in a general direction of South to North - and repeat the stop/pause - light cast (beam) and move. We watched this object move, stop, light up, and move, stop, light up again for about 5 mins! All six of us couldn't believe what we were seeing and knew it was NOT a plane. We watched until it was out of sight. Definitely an unknown flying object!
