{rjl} redish orange ball appeared in the sky.

I was driving headed south when I was a redish orange ball appear in they sky, it did not come from anywhere, but just showed up. It rose into the air and sat there for aprox. 45 seconds to a minute. Roughly 3 minutes later it reappeared in the sky and shot upwards, and stopped again. Shortly there after another one appeared below the first one. The lower one shot to the left, and then to the right, both subsequently disappeared.
