back yard to see where it was going. It made no sound at all. As it came over my house 
it stopped right over it. Then, the orange light got smaller in size and I could now see the shape 
of a triangle. What I was now looking at, was a triangular shaped object, with a 
small orange light in it's centre. I thought to myself, this is a UFO! After a few seconds, 
the object started to turn on itself slowly, than, it moved slightly to the right, and stopped 
for a few seconds, always turning on itself. Than, always turning on itself, the object came back to 
it's initial position for a few seconds, and than pursued it's initial trajectory. I than
ran trough my apartment to see it on the other side, but I did not see it, it was gone and I crushed my 
cigarette. So, the duration of my observation, was the time of smoking a cigarette.Than, I went inside, 
woke up my wife, and told her what I had seen! I told her this should be in the papers tomorrow,
after all, we are in Montreal, surely I'm not the only one that saw this thing1.But nothing."
