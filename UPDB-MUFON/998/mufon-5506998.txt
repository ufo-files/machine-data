We 3 persons watched three very fast moving lights with aprupt change into several directions over long distances, playing tactical games for minimum four hours

Weve been on holidays at the Black Sea and we all(3 persons) saw three shimmering lights up in the sky moving very fast and playing tactical games like two built a trap for the third. One light was brighter than two others. They were able to change abrupt the direction and were also able to stand till for a long time. They were able to fly "zig-zag" and were hunting each other for several hours. After they disappeared short before dawning. All this happened around the 11th of September 2013. First it looked like stars but nothing-nor plane, nor satelite can move and change directions so fast like this. We were amazed what weve seen and for sure we will remember this for the whole life.
