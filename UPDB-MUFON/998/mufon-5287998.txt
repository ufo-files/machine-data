Saw a white teardrop shaped object in the sky as I was on the way to pick my daughter up from school.

As I was driving out in the country to pick my child up from school, I stopped at a stop sign and noticed a white, almost cloud-like, teardrop shaped white object in the sky. It was a low-traffic road so I sat for at least 2 minutes and stared at the object. There was no discernible movement at all. Maybe it was just a weather ballon or something that will be obvious to others, but I had never seen anything like this in the sky before. There was a lighter spot in center of shape that looked like the eye of a needle. I had time to take 4 photos before a car came up behind me and I needed to continue on to my destination. It took about 10 minutes or so to pick my child up from school. I drove by the same place I had initially seen the shape on the way home and the shape was no longer there. Today was a clear sunny day.
