{cak} red orange flame moving silently. as it passed overhead flame went out and object became white oval shape.

I was under flight path of runway 31r of jfk airport. planes fly very low and i spend a lot of time observing them using website flightradar24.com to identify airlines and types of planes flying over. when i hear a plane approaching i go to the porch to see it. I was walking past the door to porch and a light caught my eye because it was a reddish orange color and appeared to flicker. At first i thought it might be fireworks but it kept getting closer. just about the time that it flew silently overhead the flame went out revealing a white oval shaped object. It continued moving at the same speed as it moved out of my view being blocked by the roof of the house. A friend of mine was with me at the time. I called her outside when i first noticed the light and she was just as amazed as i was. I couldnt stop thinking about the sighting all night trying to figure out what it was that we saw.
