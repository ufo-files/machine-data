Many, many crafts flying across the sky

Walking from my house to my truck to go to work, 3:38 am. Looked to the sky as usual to see if cloudy or clear. There were, streaking across the sky, a LOT of bright , large crafts . They were as bright and as large as the North Star which was very visible this morning. They were in single file flying from west to east. By the time I looked up I could tell that many had already passed, but as I stood there in awe, I counted over 30 flying over me. Could not tell how high, but they were higher that aircraft and lower than satellites. Speed was much faster than an aircraft would be. I flew 15 years in the Army as a Chinook flight engineer. I have many flight hours in many conditions. I turned on the video on my cell phone but didn't know how to set the lighting so I just held it and tried to focus on the crafts hoping something would show up. It didn't . At least I don't think so. I need to get home after work and have my techie kids try to see if there is anything on the video. I have NEVER seen anything like this before. It just blew me away. 
What were they? How do I find out if anybody else saw these?
