I went out to have a smoke, like I do on a daily and nightly basis.  The weather was amazing today (Aug. 29, 2020. 11:45pm) I love the fall and cooler weather, today was the first day like that of the season here in the twin cities MN area! I was slowly strolling and looking up at the stars, it was completely clear, quiet, no air traffic, I was just enjoying the night. I continued looking at the stars as I passed a large pine tree. I looked up into the sky behind the tree and saw a perfect sphere/circle, bright! brighter then anything else in the sky.  I waited for it to blink or flash or move (like a plane or satellite or something), but nothing, it stayed completely still, silent, and brighter then anything I've ever seen in the sky besides lighting. Aftet maybe 5-10 seconds it just vanished, like someone had hit the off button or shut off the lights, nothing....it was there then as I blinked it was gone.  I waited searching the sky, trying to find a logical explanation, that is how I was raised and that is what I have learned in my collegiate experience and my military career. After 10 minutes I went back inside and immediately shared what I had seen with my wife.  I would like to point out that the weather was perfect, no clouds, no storms, completely still and peaceful evening.... This is my first, I guess "experience " with something like this that I could not explain with logic and deductive reasoning.
