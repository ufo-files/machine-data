I saw 2 bright lights hovering then descending.

I witnessed 2 bright white lights W in the sky. They appeared one after another but left at the same time. The first light was directly in front of me but at least 10 miles away. The second light of the same color and size, appeared to the diagonal-up-left of the original light. I didn't even notice it appear. They both hovered in place for about 4-5 minutes and then started moving. The original light moved perfectly straight down towards the ground but I couldn't see if it had landed. The second light moved towards the first in perfect straight lines, then quickly vanished. I unfortunately did not have my camera when it occurred.
