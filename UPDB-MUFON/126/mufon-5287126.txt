Unusually low flying object with pulsating green and red lights

Honestly do not know what we have seen. Object appeared out the balcony doors while watching television. Thought it was a plane flying unusually low just over the treetops and roughly parallel to the autobahn only 1 km away. The formation lights were unusually bright and apparently sideways, green on left, white in middle, red on right, with object flying to our right in a northern direction. The green and red lights were not blinking as would an airplane but instead pulsating very slowly in alternation in two second intervals. We came out onto the balcony for a better look and there was no noise at all, either from the autobahn or from the object. From this distance, it looked like the object was gliding about 120m off the ground at a speed of about 100kph because the distance it covered in about 65s between appearing on the SW horizon and disappearing behind the tree line on the north is about 2.5km. Too slow for a plane, too silent for a helicopter, but too fast for an airship, and too far and too large for a drone, because from here the very bright lights looked about 2m apart. Radar control planes only fly during the day here, and there were no reports of police activity or fire in the area. We have not ruled out a military drone, but the object immediately gave us a very unsettled feeling and it left us with too many questions unanswered.
