A paper bag on fire.

I was in my backyard with the dogs listening to my neighbors argue. My girlfriend was getting out of her car,when i looked over my right shoulder and saw what I thought was an ember floating about 50 feet away from me. Then I realized it was farther away from me and not a piece of floating paper bag or something. I started yelling at my girl to run over and look at it. She saw it also. At first it looked like it was floating then it moved in a straight line, stopped for a half second and proceed to move away from us going east towards Bethlehem. It looked like fire on top, but no distinct flames. When it moved away from us, I could vaguely see 3 prongs..appendages ...whatevers coming out from the bottom . It got farther away and went behind a tree thats about 80 feet tall. I cant say exactly how far away it was or how big. I didn't have shoes on, otherwise I would have ran down my alley to see it maybe a little longer. I ran inside my house to get my shoes. When I came out, my girlfriend had already walked out front of the house to see if she could see it still. It was gone by then. I NEVER believed in UFOs. I don't have any explanation for what I saw tonight. I also posted it on Facebook hoping someone else had seen it. Im just glad my girlfriend saw it too.
