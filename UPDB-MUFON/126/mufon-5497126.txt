Viewed two orange lights travelling slowly together eastward then separated and headed east and southeast

My sighting took place the night of December 25, 2014 at approximately 9:00pm. The night was clear and about -5C. There was no other traffic on the road when I was there.

The sighting took place as I was approaching my condo in Turner Valley, AB on Imperial Drive and about 1 kilometer east of the house.
I was driving west about 1 kilometer from a cross street which has the orange vapor street lights. I noticed two lights somewhat higher in the sky which I assumed were other street lights. But then I noticed they were moving in an eastward direction towards me. They were of a similar colour as the street lights which made me think initially that they were other street lights.
I pulled over beside the road and watched them approach. It was obvious by then that there was two of them fairly close together. As they came almost overhead one turned and headed in a southeast direction while the other one maintained an easterly direction. The lights began fading and after approximately 30 seconds both disappeared completely.

As it was very dark I wasn1t able to identify any details about the objects other than the lights. As they passed overhead I listened out the car window but did not hear a sound.
