The area all around me is private property, a few houses, but mostly timber farms and pasture for cows to graze. I wish I could give more information, but this is the best I can do. 

This isn't the first time I have seen something weird in the sky since I have been living in the area. I did drive over to area where I believed it was hovering, but no such luck being able to find a landing zone.
