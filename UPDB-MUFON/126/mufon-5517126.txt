{dr}Round Orange Sphere moving North North West...then dimmed and disappeared.

I was out sky watching alone with 10x50 binoculars mounted on a tripod. At 10:14 pm I spot an object almost straight above me. It was moving North North West in direction. It had an orange glow. It was at about 9 to 10 thousand feet in altitude(AGL). It was silent moving at a moderate speed. As I watched it with binoculars it then dimmed a little and disappeared almost instantly. I spotted it in the sky near the star Vega. It disappeared just a little ways north of the star Vega.
I have seen many such objects like this, some have even made 90 degree turns. It is too bad more people arent more observant with what is going on in our skies.
