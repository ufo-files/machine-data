{cak} Glowing objects in the sky

I live in Southgate, Mich. From there, off to the 

west, I observed a bright, stationary object approx. 

10-20 degrees off the horizon. Observing through 

binoculars did not help me to determine a shape, but 

I was able to determine a red glow around the bottom 

of it.

 As this object was hovering in the air, 4 other 

crafts ascended from the ground into sky one after 

the other over a span of 15 mins. These crafts were 

glowing orange and rose to an unknown height where 

the orange glow then ceased from shining.

 After this occurred, original bright object slowly disappeared.

July 29, 2013 9:30p.m.
