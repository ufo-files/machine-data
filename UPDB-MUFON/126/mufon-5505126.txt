If that was a plane, I'm a purple monkey!

I was in my bed watching the night sky through the skylights, then that thing flew by! It looked like some sort of a military aircraft but it had no lights, it looked like it was made of metal, copper, it wasn't emitting light but reflected it. Flew in a straight line fast but not too fast so I was able to get some details. Its surface was patterned, like those battle spaceships you see in movies. To me the craft seemed to be small, like it would carry a maximum of two individuals. I often see planes passing by at night, I hear them, I see their lights, but that thing I saw, I don't know what it was but that was weird!
