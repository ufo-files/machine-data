{cak} JUST AFTER THE BLIZZARD OF 1978 I SAW A LARGE SPHERE SHAPED CRAFT TRAVELING WITH MY CAR NORTH ON HIGHWAY 93

FILED WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, AT MUFON HQ, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER #18809, ON 12/14/2016.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

JUST DAYS AFTER THE BLIZZARD OF 1978, MY FRIEND AND I LEFT TOWN TO GO TO LAKE WINNIPESAUKEE. WE WERE TRAVELING NORTH ON HIGHWAY 93 JUST OUT OF MANCHESTER. WE NOTICED A LARGE SPHERE SHAPED CRAFT TRAVELING WITH OUR CAR NORTH ON HIGHWAY 93. IT SEEMED TO BE FOLLOWING THE ROAD AND KEEPING PACE WITH US. IT WAS AS CLOSE AS 50 FEET FROM OUR CAR AND LOW TO THE GROUND. IT WAS ABOUT 8 FEET TALL. IT HAD TO ASCEND OVER THE TREES AND BACK DOWN AGAIN TO FOLLOW THE ROAD. THE CRAFT WAS 50 TO 60 FEET LARGE. IT WAS A SPHERE ON THE TOP AND BOTTOM WITH A BELT OR PANEL OF WINDOWS AROUND THE MIDDLE, LIKE IT HAD BEEN CUT IN HALF AND THE BELT/PANEL OF WINDOWS PUT IN THE MIDDLE.
THE CRAFT WAS ROTATING WHILE IT WAS MOVING. THE LIGHT FROM THE WINDOWS WAS VERY SMOOTH AND UNWAVERING. IT ALSO HAD A RED LIGHT ON THE BOTTOM. I WANTED TO STOP AND TAKE A PICTURE BUT MY FRIEND WAS SCARED AND ASKED ME NOT TO. THE CRAFT KEPT PACE WITH US FOR SEVERAL MILES AND THEN IT TURNED EAST, TOWARD THE OCEAN, AND SHOT AWAY. WITHIN A SECOND ALL WE COULD SEE WAS A RED DOT.
WE SUFFERED NO ILL EFFECTS OR MISSING TIME FROM THIS ENCOUNTER.
