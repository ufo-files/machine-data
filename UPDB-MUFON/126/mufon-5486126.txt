{nd} Orb with various speeds and direction disappeared by Polaris star

It was approximately 9:05pm. My boyfriend and i were out on our front porch smoking. We searched the sky for the moon like we normally do but we didnt see it. I noticed to the west of us was an airplane descending at an unusually steep angle. My boyfriend then mentioned that he hoped it would crash because he wanted to see something exciting. I then faced north and found the little dipper. I moved my eyes northeast and found what i thought was the big dipper. While staring at the star formation, one of the stars moved fast going east. We knew it wasnt a plane because of the speed at the altitude and the quick direction change. It disappeared then reappeared and headed northeast again. Seconds later it faded away. We waited approx 5 minutes looking for more movement. Shortly after, i noticed a helicopter not far from my house headed west. I had a weird feeling when the helicopter passed but i couldnt explain it. We immediately downloaded a constellation app on our android phone and discovered the big star the orb disappeared next to was Polaris.
