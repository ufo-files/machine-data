{rjl} Rose above western horizon, traveled to north, then dissapeared

My wife was out on our 2nd story patio when she saw several orange orbs rising from over a tree which blocks our view to the western horizon, traveled to the north maybe several hundred feet to the north, relatively slowly, and then vanish one by one. My wife quickly called myself and a friend out to the patio where I also witnessed these lights. There were at least seven of these orbs with more still rising from behind the tree when they began disappearing as in the brightness of the lights dimmed until there was nothing to see. I immediately thought they were UFOs as I have never seen anything like this in my life. My wife and I are still in awe after witnessing what we just saw.
