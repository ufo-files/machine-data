Strange lights in distance

I could not sleep so I went downstairs. I looked out the window as I walked down the hall and I noticed a strange pulsating blue and red lights. I wondered if it were a star or satellite so I stopped and stared. The lights were in an arc shape and they hovered. I watched for a long time because I could not believe what I was seeing. I kept trying to rule out possible answers other than UFO. After a while the lights moved quickly in a zig zag pattern and disappeared. I could not longer see the lights because of trees.
