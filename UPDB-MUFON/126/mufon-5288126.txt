10 foot diameter mirrored perfect sphere

We were flying from Ft Lauderdale FL to White Plain NY in a Challenger 850. I was the captain but was the pilot monitoring from the right seat while the other pilot flew, so I handle the radios. We were about abeam Cape Canaveral and ATC was routing us around various thunderstorms along the coast. We were in the clear with numerous cumulonimbus clouds scattered around us as we climbed through 16,000 feet.
I spotted a flash of reflected light ahead of us and checked TCAS for traffic, but there wasn't any. I called out to the pilot flying, "We have traffic ahead, not on TCAS." We both watched as the objected approached and then shot past at our altitude. It was a perfect sphere, and highly reflective, like a completely spherical mirror. Neither of us could determine the size. Our best guess was maybe in the vicinity of a 10 foot diameter. It approached and shot by the right side of the aircraft, my side. It was close, too close, well within 50 feet of us. No wake turbulence either. I thought it was possibly a balloon, which I've encountered numerous times in the past. But, unlike a balloon, the closure rate was insane. Usually in cruise we fly around 500mph, but in the climb we were doing maybe 350mph. So a floating balloon passing us at 350mph is pretty fast. When we pass other air traffic going in the opposite direction, either 1000 feet above or below us and at a similar speed, we have a closure rate of just over 1000mph. This was much faster. Maybe three times faster than that. That's why we threw out the balloon theory. Was it an alien spacecraft? No idea. All we do know is that we have no idea what the hell it was, but we both saw the same exact thing.
