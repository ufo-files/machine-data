ball of light slowly moving diagnaly upwards.

There was an oval shaped green/blue light with an orange tail headed upwards diagonaly. It only lasted about 10 seconds and then just seemed to vanish once it got higher up. The lady I was with saw it aswell and my boyfriends father (who was on the other side of town) saw the exact same thing. At first I thought it may be a meteorite but all the ones I have ever seen have looked as if they were "falling" this did not "fall" this was slowly moving up past the clouds in a diagonal direction and vanished.
