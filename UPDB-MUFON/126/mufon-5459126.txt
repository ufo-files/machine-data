At around 5:45 am this morning, Monday 7/29/19, I pulled into my works parking lot as I do every day and seen a bright light above a tree line to the south of my location that I have never seen before. I watched it for 4-5 minutes then decided to record it on my iphone. As I watched it, it split and became what looked like 2 lights. I stopped recording and watched for another minute or two, then it started to descend so I started recording again, at that time the lights were changing and pulsating and getting much farther apart as you can see in the 2nd video and then descended below the tree line and out of sight. The tree line is 2-3 miles south of where I was parked. my exact location at the time was, 43*25'23"N 95*8'47"W which is Spirit Lake, IA. area. The objects were appx at 172*S and 2-3 miles from my location.
