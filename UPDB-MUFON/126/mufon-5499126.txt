{rjl} Giant long object with box window like lights along the whole object. Hovering for mins.

Riding in a car with two other witnesses . We turned out of my drive way and all notice a giant plane shaped object floating above the tree line. We were at a lost of words for the few minutes which felt like forever. Frightened we backed into the drive way and the object then vanished without us noticing any movement, almost like it disappeared.
