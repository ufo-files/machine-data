{jpg} bright silver, saucer shaped

I was waiting for the bus. so I sat down on the bench and was cloud watching(we have great clouds here in and around Cincinnati) and saw the silver saucer shape, at first I wasn't sure what I was looking at then it moved quickly up down and then shot off to the right and was gone I looked around at the others waiting for the same bus and they were all looking down the street for the bus I almost said " hay! did u guys see that!! and then realized I better not say anything and so that is it... that is what happened and I never saw another one. however when I got some pictures from travels in the Baja there was a big gray spot on one of them I kept it, although my husband says it is a flock of birds...and I did not see anything in the sky when I took the picture...I would have noticed a flock of birds...
