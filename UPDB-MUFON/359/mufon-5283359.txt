Oval Tumbling Object

I first observed the object while at dinner at Bonefish Macs in Lighthouse Point. I said to my wife, what the heck is that? I observed an oval object traveling slowly east to west. The object appeared to rotate vertically. While rotating it would reflect the setting sun and there were two large lights on either end of the object. It continued upon its path but began moving in a north westerly direction until we lost sight of it due to trees. It appeared to maintain the same altitude during our observation.
