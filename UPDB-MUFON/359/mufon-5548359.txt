Intense blue/white light SSE sky at 45 degrees moving toward me then turned East

At approximately 9:30 I went to the kitchen to refill my glass of water. An intense bright star like light caught my attention through the window. It was a clear night and the full moon was not yet visible although the sky was getting light to the east. The light was really bright and looked blue/white. It appeared to be about 3 times the size of any stars. I opened the door to get a better look and decided that it was to big and too bright to be a star. The light appeared to be moving toward me at an angle. I went back inside to get my binoculars and called for my wife to come see it. When we got back outside the light had turned and was moving east. It very quickly disappeared. I did not get to see it through the binoculars as it was going too fast. My wife did get to see it for a bit before it went out of sight. I believe this may be the same as was reported in Texas this same date. The times are about the same and the position and direction of travel also. If it is the same then whatever it was must have been very far away and very high. I am in north central Arkansas on the Missouri border.
