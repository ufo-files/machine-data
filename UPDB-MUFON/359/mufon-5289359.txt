Orange glowing orb moving slowly and silently

I and my partner observed an orange glowing orb. It moved from the NW to the western sky where it vanished. The glow was unwavering and it was totally silent it moved very smoothly at a steady pace. We observed it for 3-4 minutes, until it vanished. My partner and myself then came indoors (we were watching from our backdoor into our garden) my partner went to another room. I went into the kitchen and then saw it again from the kitchen window. It was in the NW sky where I first saw it. I went outside to look again and then I rushed in to get my phone to try and video it , but when I returned it had gone. I waited a few minutes but it didn't reappear.
