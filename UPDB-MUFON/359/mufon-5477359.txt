The witness reported: Three times during three straight weeks, people at my cabin near Grantsburg, WI saw round glowing objects. The first sighting was by workers at my cabin. The moon was out. They saw 3 UFOs, stopping and going to the right, then stopping and going to the left. This was over a span of 30 minutes. I told my lady friend about them. We went out to the cabin; at 11:00 pm we were sitting by the fire outside. We saw 4 objects, not way up, close enough to see that they were round, for 15 minutes. They were of a yellowish-gray tint. The next week, we saw the same UFOs again; this time there were ten witnesses. They left to go to bed, while my son and I stayed up and sat around the fire. One came over and hovered above us much lower than the others. It was circular and had a smaller circle in the middle of the bottom. It took off fast. The next week I called the Wisconsin DNR office in Grantsburg. He said 1We are getting these reports all the time, from people in the area. We tried to find out what they are and we can1t find out anything.1 In all of these sightings, the objects came from the NW and left to the SE.
