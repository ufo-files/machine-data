triangular formation of orange lights in sky

I was outside having a few drinks for new years eve with 6 other ppl when i saw huge bright orange lights coming in from the East in a straight line. There were 3 of them then they changed formation to a triangle, they did this a couple of time and were traveling quite fast in reference to stationary land marks but were able to observe them for quite some time. At first i thought it was flares but there were no other characteristics to suggest this, once they started to make formations i immediately dissmissed this explantion. The lights made no sound and blinked a couple of times to each other like they were going to just fade out but the lights came back on and still kept a perfect triangle formation the whole time then they just dissapeared towards the SW direction. While we were trying to see if we could still see them One more light exactly the same travelling by itself in the same direction as the others came into veiw and it did the same thing but it was travelling much faster as to try and catch up with the others then it just dissapeared as well. All of us were just blown away by the experience. We were trying to get some footage of the event but the trees were in the way of a good shot.
