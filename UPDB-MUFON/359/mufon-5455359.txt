{cak} I was looking at Orion's Belt when I saw some lights fly by and then disappear. This happened 2 separate nights. The lights were not all together.

The first sighting of one star-like object moving across the sky occurred on February 14, 2020 at around 7:25 pm. I was in my hot tub on that night looking up at Orion's Belt when all of a sudden I see a star-like object move in a straight line from Orion's Belt south for about 12 seconds and then disappear. I ran into the house to see if it was the ISS, but it wasn't because the ISS was on the other side of the world. So last night, February 17, 2020, I went out to the hot tub again and started watching Orion's Belt in the sky. At around 7:22 pm, another light appeared at the top of the Orion Nebula, near Bellatrix and moved south slowly just like the other night. It then disappeared. Then a couple of seconds later, over near Bellatrix, two star-like objects appeared about 2 inches apart as I watched them move south and then disappear one by one. This happened two more times with two objects moving the same way and then disappeared. I ran into the house and pulled up Stellarium to look but could not find anything moving. I'm going out tonight to see if they come back. I will bring my phone to videotape it if it appears again.
