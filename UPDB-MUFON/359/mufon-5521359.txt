Bright blue light rotating from a fixed spot, creating a half circle of light. When rotation complete would drop straight down and stop and repeat total of 3 times before I lost sight.

Approximately 8:30 pm on 1/16/2012, I was walking my normal route near a neighborhood mixed with houses and industrial/commercial buildings. As I rounded a corner I saw a bright blue light, I thought it was a helicopter at first, but it proceeded to make a tight circle, as it did the single blue light would make a "half circle" appearance (I assume the light was on the other side). As soon as the blue light completed its circle it made a sudden drop straight down and then stop. It did the rotation and drop two more times before I lost sight of it behind trees. I observed this for about 5-7 minutes. I could not see the outline of the craft or hear any noise, I also couldnt judge how large it was or how far away it could have been. I was looking almost due west from the Cypress, CA area towards Long Beach, CA. I was probably less than a mile from the Los Alamitos Joint Forces Training Center, Los Alamitos, CA.
