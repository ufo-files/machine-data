Driving home after visiting hospitalized father. Alone approx 11 pmHighway ran alongside Ohio River I looked to my left and saw 2 objects flying alongside my car. They were very low over the water about 20 to 30 ft. I couldn't determine exact shape  Appeared rectangular with red light on front Heard no noise. Instantly they turned at approx 125 degree angle and disappeared over the horizon (a distance of 20 miles in a matter of 2 seconds I din't know how long they had been alongside me but when I turned and saw them they turned and were gone in an instant. I have never reported this to authorities but did tell family members
