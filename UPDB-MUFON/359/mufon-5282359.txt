Large number of strange lights in sky

My son and his friend were playing outside around 9pm when he came in our home to report strange lights in the sky. He claimed to have seen 2 triangular shaped objects. My husband, his 3 friends and myself went out to investigate. All 7 of us looked up at the sky in our backyard to find a very large number of lights in the sky, perhaps close to a hundred or more, littered about in no pattern and all moving very fast from East to West in direction. They seemed to be at various altitudes, some moving in very close proximity to others. Because of the sheer number, speed and close proximity to one another we don’t Believe these to be planes. We all Have said this is easily the strangest sight we have ever seen in our lives. While the 2 children were certain to have seen triangular shapes, none of the adults saw that, just the moving lights. I called the news and local police department and there were no other reports made at that time for this event that we witnessed. We would love to be able to find out what we saw.
