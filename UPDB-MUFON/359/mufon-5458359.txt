{jpg} Round bright white light with red center in eastern sky hovered at an elevation of 45 degrees

Family members observed a bright red and white light in the sky as it hovered for about 10 minutes. The light moved once slightly to the NE. Observers said its elevation was from 25 to 45 degrees. Witnesses also did not agree on the size of the light, varied between 1/2 size of full moon to basketball.
