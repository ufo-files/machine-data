Two red balls of glittering light chasing each other in the pouring rain.

I was moved to look out my large kitchen window and saw two red balls of uneven, slightly flashing balls of light in Newquay Cornwall where I often have sightings. They looked like they were on a mission but not moving as fast as I've seen other unidentified craft move, almost like they were playing, as the first one slightly changed direction and then the other followed suit. I stuck my head against the window so they might have seen me looking at them go by in my 3rd floor flat. I felt really elated afterwards as you do! Maybe they changed direction when they saw me but they then disappeared behind the house and didn't accept my offer of milk and cookies. I'm not joking...I'm a UFO/EBE enthusiast and have a BSc Psychology degree. Maybe they knew my birthday was coming up-awesome!
