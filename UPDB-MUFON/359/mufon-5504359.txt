{cak} Two red spheres in sky - beam of red light shoots from one to the other then they disappear

I was sleeping in bed and felt something on me (think it was a bug or a hair - startled me as my nose suddenly felt something on it) so I woke up. I looked around the room and found nothing and thought I probably spooked myself. I looked out the window and noticed two red dots. They were tiny because they were really, really far away. They were just sitting there doing nothing then I saw a beam go from one toward the other. It was the same color as the two red dots. I look out my window a lot so I know there's nothing like this in that part of the sky. It's usually just black on a clear night like this. It lasted maybe 45 seconds then they vanished - just blinked out of existence. I'd say in the next 10 minutes or so there was a plane that crossed the sky. This is the second time I've seen red dots in the sky in my area. This time though they were in a different direction and a lot farther away. It was at 4:30 in the morning. I know I was wide awake as I went to the bathroom afterwards.
