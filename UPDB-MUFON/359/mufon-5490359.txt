{cak} witnessed while observing the moon

at 4:55 a.m.of August 10 2015 i was standing on my porch observing the moon. suddenly to the east of the moon about one and a half diameters of the moon, from the moon there suddenly appeared a very bright white point of light. i was amazed as i had been looking directly at that area and the point of light seemed to "blink on" at a very great distance from me. after a few seconds the light diverged into four seperate lights of less intensity with the color and brightness of moderate starlight and at once began to move in a irregular diamond shape formation to the south east at a tremendous speed across the sky in a straight line and decending somewhat. as the lights moved the formation changed to a irregular line and changed from a straight line to arc and began to ascend. the lights seemed to seperate from a line to a irregular diamond shape again and appeared to be moving away from me the light then faded out of view. there was no sound or vapor trail there was enough moonlight that i would have been able to see a vaportrail if there was one, or four!!  it was my firm impression from the inital sighting of the four seperate lights that it was controlled flight. i was rather pleasantly amazed that i was witnessing a UFO event!!
