It was around 830pm at night Iwas working at a variety store in Belfair wa. 3 men came in dressed in clothes from the 1940s clothes ; there looks were perfect,no neck hair no 5 oclock shadow just perfect, talked perfect english asked if I was closing I said in about a half an hour,asked if it was all right to look around, I said sure, thay went up one isle and down the the other, one person would ask what is this used for,the other would say,this is a oil lamp, used for primitive lighting, the other would ask what are these,the other would answer,these are called a knife,fork,and spoon,used to consume foods, I could hear them all through the store asking questions,when thay dident know what a bath towel was I knew what was going on, all three came back up to me,looked at me,turned walked to the door,stoped,turned,and said GOD BE WITH YOU, . I told my family what happened,my brother said GOD BE WITH YOU is the long version of good by. I have had other contacts sense i was around 3 or 4 years old.
