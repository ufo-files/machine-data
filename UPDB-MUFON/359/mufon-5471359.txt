in April 1972 my mother, brother, and my self were sitting in the living room when an burnt orange sphere enter through the living room window. I was standing in the center of the living room when the object went around and up and down me about three or four times and then exit through the front door.my mother did not see the orb but my brother did. in which at that point he believe me from when I told him about the craft I has seen in early February 1972.
