{rjl} Triangle Hovered above house

A triangle shaped craft with no sound hovered above house.

Flight path was west to east direction no more than 1,000 feet above the terrain. moving approximately 25 mph. There was 3 primary lights below the craft that were red-orange, with a red strobe on the bottom. The back has two additional red strobes on it with two fins. This was not a normal aircraft, or anything I've seen before.

I witnessed this approximately 2310 Eastern Time. While on my balcony, I then proceeded to the front to take an additional 3 pictures.

I lost sight of this craft once it passed over my neighborhood.
