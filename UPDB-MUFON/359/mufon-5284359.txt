I saw this on two separate occasions about 3 hours apart in around about the same area. It seemed to be several lights or orbs. It changed shape often and then would disappear. When it reappeared it would be several yards away from where it had disappeared from. Several other people were pulled over looking up into the sky. It at first looked to be a large banner or balloons, but kept disappearing and reassembling in a circular formation or a v formation. OThere are several hospitals and one airport nearby, but this was not leaving any trails like the planes were or making any noise like the helicopters were. It was a clear day, no clouds, sun shining very bright.
