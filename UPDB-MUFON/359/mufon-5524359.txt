we were on our deck looking west just after sunset...about 8:15 when two very bright lights of an orange color appeared in the sky. Five of us exclaimed. I ran for camera. Witnesses said the lights rose for a time and then headed together to the NW. I was able to get one light on film for just a second. Nothing special except the magnitude can be judged as the other stars in the sky do not show up. It was pehaps 3 or 4 times brighter than Jupiter is tonight. We have been at this location now just a few months. Rural with little aritraffic I have seen several satellites, etc. This didn't fit any of those. I was a witness to lights in Phonix many years ago, these were similar in some ways..the brilliance and the apparent drift or fading after about 5 or so minutes. We could not tell if they went "out" or went into distance. Thre was no trail or smoke we could see. No sounds. No guess on distance. I reviewed the film, not so hot - no tripod and I was late getting back to porch. You can have it if need be, but would just frustrate web viewers.
