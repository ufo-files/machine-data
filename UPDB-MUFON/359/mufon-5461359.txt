{cak} Bright white light that flashed red rotating lights, then dimmed and brightened then dimmed out.

This sighting occurred on Monday, April 1, 2019, around 8:16 PM on Route One in Madison, CT.

As I was driving, I saw a bright white light in the sky and initially thought it was a street lamp in the distance. As I got closer, I realized that the light was too high up in the sky to be a street lamp.

I continued to watch it, while driving. I thought maybe it was an airplane or even a helicopter. However, it was too stationary to be either one. It could have been about airplane altitude.

I then noticed that it began to flash rotating red lights. I continued to watch it. As I got closer to an area where there were stores, I wondered if anyone else was seeing this. 

The next thing it did was to dim and then brighten again. Finally, it just dimmed out and I couldn’t see it anymore.

I didn’t feel afraid looking at this object. Has anyone else seen anything like this?
