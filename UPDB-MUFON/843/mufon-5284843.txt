Observed white/yellow glowing oblong object flying at a high speed then changing direction perpendicularly and disappearing.

While driving east on I 44 I saw an object flying above in the opposite direction and at a super fast speed. I describe it as “zooming”. It was an oblong, glowing “tic tac” shape. Definitely not just a light, but an object. It zoomed on a straight path then very suddenly changed direction perpendicularly and vanished. It was so unusual my initial reaction was “WTF is THAT!?”. It was…unsettling. Definitely not a normal aircraft or a star, etc. it was an object that glowed and was zooming. It honestly seemed as if it had just entered the atmosphere and then exited. It lasted for about 4 seconds tops. I’ve never seen anything like it and can’t explain it. Maybe 30 seconds to a minute afterward, I saw another identically shaped object flying at same altitude, speed, and direction, but it was NOT glowing and I only saw it for a split second. Literally a blink. It seemed to be in shadow or behind clouds, but it was for sure moving and was the same shape. I’ve never been one to explore or be interested in these types of things, but it was all so weird. I dictated a note to SIRI to remember where I was and what time it was, to check later if anyone else witnessed it. I was traveling East on I-44 through Joplin, MO, on October 11, 2021, at 5:40am.
