{jpg} Good sized dark egg shape hovering over foothills.

Was on our deck just before dusk in the burland meadows section of bailey Colorado. Saw this good sized egg shaped object hovering over the foothills looking north east. I watched this object for about 3 to 5 minutes to see if it'd move which it didn't. I had this weird, erie, creepy feeling like I never felt before that it was watching me. I went in to get binoculars to see if I could see it better. When i returned outdide in less than a minute it was gone.
