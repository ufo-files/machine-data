bright orange light 3 nights in row same spot completely vanishes

We live in Niagara Falls, Ontario and see planes passing all day and night long. We live right by the heli-pad, so we are very familiar with what's up in the sky. Every night we sit on our porch and drink our coffees when I get home from work during the week. For the 4th time this week, in nearly the exact same spot each time, and almost the exact same time, my husband and I have witnessed a bright orange light that shows up and moves really fast and gets really bright as it's moving, then as suddenly as ot gets bright, it dims and completely disappears. Last night we witnessed it twice, 10 minutes after it disappeared the first time, it reappeared in the exact same spot it had disappeared or dimmed out completely. Then it started to move again, brightened extremely bright and then dimmed and disappeared again. There was clouds to the north and south but this light appeared in a section that had absolutely no clouds. Tonight shortly after 11pm we saw it again. There was absolutely no blinking lights like planes and helicopters use. This was a solid white light that brightened into a white-orange glow and then dimmed out completely. There was a helicopter in the area and it landed about 10 min earlier just off Stanley and I just watched it leave right by my house with blinking lights - this thing was not a helicopter. It moved really fast and completely disappears every time. We watch to see if it reappears somewhere but it's just gone as fast as it appears.
