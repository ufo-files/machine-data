UFO i spotted while living in sweden.

I was reversing my van into a loading bay to drop off bread. I was just about to switch the van off when i spotted blue lights coming out of the trees just over the road. Thinking it was some christmas lights or something i got my cigs and lighter out. Just as im lighting the ciggy this blue light starts getting stronger and moving towards me. watching this thing from underneath made me think it may have been human made. It looked like it had pipework on the underside. strange humming like pulsating, the air felt charged and there was a slight heat on the breeze. considering this is sweden in the middle of winter it was odd.
