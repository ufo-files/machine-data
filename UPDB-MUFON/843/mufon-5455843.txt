Clear v in shape. Bright as magnesium when lit

Clear summers day in June 2017 am. I witnessed at first what I thought was a bright star in the sky whilst cutting my lawn. I noticed that this star has dropped lower in my horizon over a period of 4 minutes. I panicked thinking it must be an asteroid. It flew dirrectly from east to west over me incredibly high in the sky did not make a sound v in shape as bright as when you light magnesium. I honestly feel this object was not in our air space but far higher.
