{rjl} Got out of car, heard a noise, looked up, saw three dots in a triangle form. Went straight up.

Got out of my car at a little after midnight. I heard a strange noise and looked around me in every direction. I then realized it was above me. I looked up (through the tree line) and saw three golden lights in a triangle formation. After I looked up, they rose directly up and out of the sight, the sound dispersed accordingly. I was very freaked out.
