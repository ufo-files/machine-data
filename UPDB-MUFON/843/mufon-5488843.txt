rusty red cigar shaped 5 windows drifting at very low speed from my right to left

i was laying with my head on the back garden path facing to the left of my house when i saw to my right a wingless rusty red cigar shape.it had 5 circular windows and it was moving very slowly and silently from my right to my left in around ten minutes before it dissapeared behind my neighbours house i wasnt allowed to walk off around the streets at the time.the port holes were opaque black with a bit of movement inside couldnt see in them they were too far away to distinguish what was inside but definately movement i was aged between 5 and 7 years old at the time and so made mental notes as to what i was seeing because id never seen something like that before and somehow knew even at that age it would be important to remember what it looked like.. it happened between 1983-1985 sorry i cant be anymore exact
