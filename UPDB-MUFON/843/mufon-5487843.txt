{rjl} Triangular shaped craft with 3 red lights underneath

his morning I was driving North on Route 29 towards Columbia. I was past the ICC. I saw a metallic silver object and thought it was too big to be a balloon. I kept looking at it, it did not make any noise. It was a triangular shape but if it turned, it looked like a different shape. There were three big red lights on the bottom of it. I tried to look at it if I could, but the stoplight turned green, and I had to keep driving. It was not being chased; it was just moving southbound slowly. It moved very smoothly. Enough for me to have been looking at it during the red stoplight and not lost it from sight. It was a clear morning, so I know it was not anything else. And I know what a plane shape looks like, but this did not have any wings jutting out or a tail. It was just a smooth metallic triangle with lights on the bottom. I was not afraid, felt calm, intrigued when looking at the object. I was interested to see if it would disappear or if I had noticed it looked like a plane or helicopter but the entire time it stayed the same triangular shape with the three red lights continuously on.
