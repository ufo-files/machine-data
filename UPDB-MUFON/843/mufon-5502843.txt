{nd} Snake like segmented object, Loud sounds then shaped clouds N one approached

I was standing out side of my garage looking into the sky when I noticed an extremely dark cloud under the light gray cloud cover. It was shaped like a snake and was segmented proportionally. It's head was shaped like Marvin the Martians craft (only description iI could think of). It's movement was that of a snake but in the sky. After it had left my sight I heard a sound far to the west that chilled me to the bone. It sounded like the jake brake of a semi truck but way louder and slower, getting lower in tone until I could only feel it. After this I looked above my neighbors house and saw 2 clouds hovering there (they seemed a distance of 50-75 feet at first) the one on the left was shaped like a generic dinosaur skeleton but with a singular horn on its head, and it was staring into the face of an alien shaped cloud that was staring right back. After I had started at them for a minute or so the alien shaped one started coming straight at me (this was when I realized the clouds were only 25 feet or so away) I ran straight back into my garage and the cloud was maybe 7 feet away in my neighbors yard. At first the clouds were carved as if someone had gone up there and had done it by hand. Seeingthe craftsmanship of it gave me goosebumps (Expecially the prehistoric skeleton looking one how I could see through the spots where there was no bone). When the alien shaped cloud descended it got way smaller and lost its shape. After I had layed in bed I kept hearing movement in my house (too much to be the cats) and got strange sensations in random parts of my body. I have felt very paranoid since the incident.
