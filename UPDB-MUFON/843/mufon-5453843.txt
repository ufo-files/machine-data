{cak} Consistent trail of lights similar to satellites over Phoenix, AZ _ 4-15-20

Consistent trail of lights right above me, coming from SW and traveling NE. Lights would travel seemingly same path every 10 seconds from each other. They seemed like satellites. They were a bit brighter than some of the dimmer satellites I have seen. I have never seen satellites following each other either.

I watched for a few minutes, to see about a dozen following each other in the manner described above. I then went inside and told my mother who came out, and we watched about another dozen of them continue to follow each other, every 10 seconds (or less), all of them taking the same path, SW to NE.

I went back inside and then came out several hours later, at around 9:45pm. I did not see them anymore.
