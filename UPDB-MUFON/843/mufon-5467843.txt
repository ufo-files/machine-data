{rjl} Pulsating wandering lights attract planes before shooting straight up. Happens all the time.

I was at a bandmates house to practice, waiting outside with him and his son for the band. We spotted wandering lights in the sky, like stars but unwavering. They would randomly flash extremely bright in the sky, as if the light grew way brighter. Within ten minutes, three planes were visible in the sky pursuing the objects. One of the planes changed course drastically after a light flashed and shot straight up at extreme speed past his flight path into the sky. There was no sound during the entire event. The bandmates and in-laws both arrived and witnessed the lights. There were at least five different wandering lights that each of us confirmed, some at the same time, some fading away and others shooting straight up and vanishing. Apparently this happens so often in the area that his family are just used to it. My mind was blown. I am a very logical atheist and sworn skeptic but I can't explain what I saw.
