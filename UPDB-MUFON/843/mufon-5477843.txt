Just got home from work on the phone with my parents, looked up in the sky (as it's normal for me to look at the stars at night) I look up at the SW sky (not far from the Big Dipper) I see a strange star-like object high in the sky, it appeared low enough to not be a satellite, but too high for a plane. "sounds like an airplane!" yes I know... but this is where it gets strange...

I called for my roommate to come look,just before he came out of the house, the light disappeared... and then BAM! A short beam of light appeared. It was MUCH bigger than the actual object I witnessed. Then both the beam of light and the UFO disappeared completely. That fact alone, tells me it wasn't an airplane. Airplanes do not disappear in thin air. 

I posted the experience on my Facebook that same night as I wanted to document the crazy UFO I had just witnessed.
