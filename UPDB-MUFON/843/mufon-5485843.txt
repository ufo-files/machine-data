Little Brother and I shared a room when we were younger. Little brother saw it descending from the sky.he said Arlo look at this. I got up and we were looking out the window. It made no noise, was semi bright with no sheen. It hovered there above the hill. (where no is a larger cell tower) we watched it for about thirty min. No movement at all. it finally moved slow and smoothly to the east until we saw it disappear over the other hill and tree line.
