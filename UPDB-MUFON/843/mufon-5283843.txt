Siting over West Orange New Jersey

I was walking my dog at 6:30p.m. in West Orange, NJ when I looked up to see the night sky and saw a long, tubular craft with lights (about 20) that moved toward one another and then away from each other. There was a light in the front and one in the back and those were not moving. All the lights were white. It was moving slowly and headed in the of Montclair. There was no noise nor were any lights flashing.
