{jpg} Me and my mother living alone in an apartment building in Cleveland Ohio this thing happened to me

My mother and I we're living on 3rd floor of an apartment building in a major u.s. city our apartment was on the back side there was an alley below and then the frat house to a major university around 9 p.m. dark outside my mother is washing the dishes I walked from the kitchen into the living room there's a big window on the right I wasn't looking at the window but as I went by something caught my eye. I turned and faced the window. There's a big huge round white light or orb just sitting out there even with the window probably 30-40 foot out between our building and the Frat houses right above the alley. The first couple of seconds that I looked at it my mind is racing trying to figure out what it is because even though I'm 5 I've never seen anything like that, after looking at it for a couple of seconds a strange feeling came over me hard to explain but I could feel that this thing was alive and it was looking back at me, as that thought registered in my brain immediately here it comes straight towards the window straight towards me it didn't come slow it came fast straight up bam right against the window I mean it didn't make a noise but it was there at the window and I'm probably standing three foot from the window and it's right against it. I had no time to think of anything except is the window going to stop this thing or is it going to come on through. Suddenly I had a feeling at my feet like maybe a warm feeling or a feeling that I was being x-rayed that's the only way I know to describe it maybe like a warm feeling but it traveled up my legs slowly slowly for my feet up my legs through my waist through my stomach my chest my shoulders to my head when it got to head bam the thing was inside my head and I was in there with it but I was separate from it. This reporting site keeps asking me to describe my feelings, how does a 5 year old have any feelings I'm just trying to survive this thing , I don't even know what it is and it's in my head next thing I know it leaves me more or less in the present and starts going back through my brain downloading everything I've ever done all my experiences but it's doing it fast like Fast Forwarding a video but so fast you can't see any of the pictures. I could feel that it was in a hurry to do this, that it was excited and it was enjoying it, it went backwards a long ways and then I was aware that it has stopped and it was coming back forward doing the same thing going through every experience ever thought I'd ever had but it didn't do it slow it went fast until it was back to the present where it had left me. immediately it came out of me and I was out and I was able to see now it was at the top of the window before it was at the bottom now it's at the top. At that point I spoke to it mentally not verbally I said just leave you can see I'm not a threat just leave just leave it stayed there a few more seconds looking down at me and then it went up staying against the building after it went above the window I couldn't see it anymore.I couldn't move I was paralyzed I look down at my feet and told them to turn slowly I was able to turn and walk toward the kitchen where my mother is washing dishes her back was to me, 
I walked up to her and I said Mommy she didn't acknowledge me she just kept washing dishes I said Mommy Mommy then I noticed I didn't hear anything that's when I realized my vocal cords weren't even working I couldn't speak I reached up grabbed her by the arm we went in the living room and set down five or 6 minutes before I could speak. She called the Air Force the man on the phone said how old is he 5 years old oh he probably saw a car headlight the next day life went on and I've had to deal with this thing for 50 years I still don't know why it happened to me. I was a child and I didn't know how to handle it I didn't understand why it happened to me and no one else I thought there was something wrong with me why did not happen to anybody else I tried to block it out and pretend it didn't happen but I knew the memory was always there.
