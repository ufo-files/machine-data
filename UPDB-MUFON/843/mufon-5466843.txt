{rjl} I saw strange lights behaving in strange ways

I look up at about 55* in the Western sky and see a bright light which I first thought was a helicopter but it made no sound and then it started immediately dimming/getting further SW then disappearing suddenly. As I am stunned I get up try to see if it went behind the tree in front of our porch, I then noticed two stars and all of a sudden a smaller dimmer light came out from behind (?)and under and then to the right of the brighter star and then changed direction started moving like a dim satellite moving up (eastward). It made no sound. 
Then at 4:27 am I saw what at first looked like a plane then looked off and it was pulsating Orange and then whole thing was lighting up like pulsating and flying southeast. It made no sound.
