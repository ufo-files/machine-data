{rjl} First like plane on fire then saucer right overhead

New year eve I let my precocious 10 year old daughter stay up to bring in the new year. We had a window in the house that was about 20 ft. My daughter spotted what looked like a plane on fire in the distance with what looked like it was dropping flame from it. The house across the street had burned down the day before. As we watched it until we lost sight as it moved overhead of the house we ran to the front of the house to see if we could follow it. When we ran out of the front door it was hovering right over our heads except now it was a huge saucer blacking out the night sky. It made little noise a whirring sound. It was so close and had red and yellow lights on the bottom.we were so happy and excited never felt scared or threatened.it stayed for what seemed like 15minutes. After a bit it rose up straight still only making a whirring sound then instantly dissapeared in the sky. When we went back inside 2 hours had passed it was now 2am. Neither my daughter nor I ever felt anything but excited.
