Clear photographic evidence of a red/orange missile shape

At 2;10 in the afternoon, i accidentally photographed a uap while taking a picture of an 18th century cemetery located on the outskirt of my town. At exactly 2:13 the same day, i capture what seems to be a descending "whining" sound followed by a short pulsating noise (2 bursts) which could be compared to the sound made by the blades of a helicopter on the recorder of my cell phone. The sounds are faint and near impossible to make out, but definitely did not belong. There was absolutely nothing in the air or on the ground which could have made that sound. No plane or any flying object nearby, or in the distance. I also captured a clear evp while recording.
