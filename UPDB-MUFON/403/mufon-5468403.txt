{rjl} Bell Shaped Object with Spot Light - Ghost like figures also recorded.

Bell shaped object (approximately 1' to 2' in length) appeared out of no where in the middle of the night and lit up the side of our home. This was recorded by our Ring security camera and time-stamped around 4:48AM EST. Attached are seven (7) video files detailing this hour-long activity. The unknown object would suddenly move around the exterior of our home and then vanish. This repeated itself for an hour! In the final videos, the object can be seen changing from a "solid" object to a translucent object. In the final recorded segment (5:45AM), ghost like figures can clearly be seen and "heard" tampering with our security camera. In the very end, you can visually see the ghost like objects leaving (especially when the film is slowed down or freeze framed).
