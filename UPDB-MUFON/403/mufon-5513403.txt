Walking my dog, noticed a bright orange flickering object moving from the south towards my direction. Object was low and moving slowly in a straight line. Then noticed a second identical object moving behind the first. This object was also moving at the same slow speed, in the same path as the first. Both objects were bright orange, with a flickering, "fireball" shape. Both objects moved at the same speed and altitude, then turned towards the north west. Objects made no sound. 

At first I thought it was the spotlight of a helicopter, since I first observed it from a distance, through the treetops. As it moved closer and into open sky, I realized it was an orange orb type object. I could not figure out what I was observing. Ive never seen anything that looks or moves like this in the sky. Then a second identical object appeared in the distance and followed the same path. The objects moved slowly and in a straight line that eventually turned toward the north west. I watched the first one start to fade and I wanted to get my cell phone to film it, but couldnt take my eyes off it. Then I started to feel nervous and headed in the house. I didn't watch the second one fade or disappear.
