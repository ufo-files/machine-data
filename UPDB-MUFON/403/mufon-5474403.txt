{cak} White lights changing direction then a bright rectangle light flashed on then off, craft then went back to pulsating flashing light.

On the clear night of August 13 2017 I was sitting in my front yard watching for the Persieds meteor shower. At 11:35 a bright flash of light caught my sight in my peripheral vision. As I looked to it direction, NNW, I noticed a light that changed in intensity as it came on and would then go out for 10-30 seconds each time. At first there seemed to be two seperate flashing lights but they were following an erratic positions in the sky. Staying in one place then moving forward and back then sideways. The overall pattern was like two L's hooked together. I observed these light for what seemed like about 10 minutes. At about 5 minutes in to observing the Lights, all of a sudden a large sized rectangular light bluish/white flashed in the area I was watching and went out. It only lasted a couple seconds. Then went back to a single intermittent flashing white light. . The lights were very slow moving and erratic in its movements but after the lighted rectangular light flashed on and off it slowly moved straight east and then just disappeared. This incident still has me upset and unsettled. When I saw the large rectangular light flash, I almost ran inside my house because something was so weird about it and I couldn't tell for sure but looked like there was a dark black craft when the rectangle flashed but I could not make out a shape. I can still see that lighted rectangular light when I close my eyes. I will never forget that sight. Ever.
