{rjl} Triangular with white blinking lights at its points and red solid light in center bottom

I saw two of them last night, December 21st, about 7:15 flying together, over Myrtle Beach, SC, not far from the Myrtle Beach International Airport. They appeared to be about a quarter mile up, and about the size of a commercial jet. They both were triangular in shape and had white , blinking lights on each point, a red light in the center.  They moved at a slow pace and made no noise such as a glider would. I viewed them for a full two minutes as they flew south to north directly over where I was standing. Initially when I spotted them I thought they were military planes that we see so often flying in this area, but as they got closer I realized this was not the case. As I watched and realized they were flying too slow to be a typical jet, then noticed there was no noise coming from them, I became more and more fascinated and curious! And then, they were gone. At that moment I thought WHY didn't I record what I was seeing??! Too late! I can't be the only one who saw this, they were close, big and slow! Perhaps someone else spotted them and took a video.
