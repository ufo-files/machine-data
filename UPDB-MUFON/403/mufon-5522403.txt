Light moved abruptly without any sound

I had went out to get my grandmother when we noticed this bright light in the sky. It is foggy tonight, so a bright light in the sky was very noticable. We watched as the light moved across the sky, and then back again. I went inside and told my father what I had seen, and talked him into coming outside to see this object. When we went back outside, it had moved yet again, to almost completely over our house. We tried taking some photos of the object, but it looks like the photos are not very good.
