{jpg} I was taking fixed sequential photos of a setting Venus when the object appeared in all of the photos hovering, slowly ascending, rapidly descending hovering and morphing

I did not personally see the object, I was paying attention to my stopwatch and remote shutter for my Canon 5d on tripod with a 75-300mm 1:4-5.6 lens which I was using to take sequential photos of a very bright setting Venus. I didn't see the object until I reviewed the 57 images when I returned home which is approximately 1 mile west from the observation site. The object appears in all 57 images. The object is first seen hovering at approx. 25 degrees high looking west then slowly ascending to approx. 60 degrees high looking just south of west and hovering there a few frames then with the attached photo it flashed down before hovering and what seems to be 'morphing'at appox. 17 degrees high looking west to the left of Venus.
