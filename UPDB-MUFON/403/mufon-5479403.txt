{jpg} Light red in color, semi-crescent ribbon shape no flashing lights.

I was on the patio smoking a cigar at 12:05am facing South when I noticed a faint red
crescent shaped object moving from East to West. At first thought I was looking at cigar
smoke but cigar smoke isn't red and I had a (3mph) wind blowing from the West.

I was searching the night sky for satellites.

Cigar smoke

Semi- crescent object, it reminded of a ribbon being blown around by the wind. It was
moving on a horizontal plane from East to West at about 20 degrees above the horizon.

I was very surprised at first , I didn't have any idea what it was or what it could be.

It moved horizontal to the west until out of sight.
