White orb or object passing in front of clouds and changing direction.

My wife and I were in our hammocks, just enjoying the weather and swinging a bit. The sun behind the clouds was making for nice scenery. I thought that I would take a quick video and did not notice anything as I was recording for the short time I did. Upon reviewing the video, I noticed something move quickly through the area I filmed. I slowed the video down and examined it frame by frame the best I could. You can follow the object from the right side through the clouds as it changes direction. I don't think that it is a reflection. I thought originally that it was one and that the movement was from me swinging but I believe it is moving differently from my swinging. Not sure if it is authentic or just something accidental.
