(GS) Black Triangle

Stepped out on to the front porch to get a glimpse of all the stars that night, very clear night, the stars looked amazing, as I looked up I noticed a jet flying over going east, as I turn to look towards the south at the stars an object was coming in my direction from the southwest in the sky, as it got closer I could see it was triangle in shape, black, with round bright white lights on all three corners about the size of a eraser tip on a pencil with a round red one in the center of the triangle, the red light was a little bit smaller than the white ones, all on the underneath of the triangle, the lights did not blink or flash, just steady, there was no noise. The object traveled from the southwest to the northeast in a straight line pattern, the triangle object intersected with the jet with the jet being above it, looking at this, the triangle object was 3X bigger then the jet, could be that the jet was much higher up then the object, watched it till it was out of sight, told my husband about it when he came home from work, he said it was probably a stealth fighter, so I looked up "stealth fighter at night" on the internet and looked at some video clips, I can say NO it was not a stealth fighter.
