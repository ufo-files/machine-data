Sitting on couch looking out window silent black triangle type of craft zips through the sky and makes a hard turn all while being completely silent.

Sitting on my couch watching TV one afternoon with a window facing down into Benedict Canyon on my right side. All of a sudden in the sky out of a window a smooth - sleek black triangle flies into the sky and makes a turn from going east to south-east down into the canyon. Object must have been at most 300 feet in the air. Seemed possibly as large as a helicopter. Made completely zero sound.
