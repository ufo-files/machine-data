{tjd} Straight line of star like lights, perhaps 100, traveling NW to SE dissappearing in the SE

While outside at 10:11 Pm my husband and I were observing the Moon and what we decided was Venus sitting large and bright just to the right of the moon over our barn. When we noticed a line of star like lights, perhaps 100, maybe more, moving in a line from NW to SE where they disappeared in the southern sky but not at the horizon. The line was not exactly straight, but lights moved one at a time. Sometimes equidistant from each other, sometimes a little bunched up. They appeared from our vantage point to be high up and moving at a high rate of speed.
