{cak} Fast moving blue/white ball of light ascending up at a curve

The evening of the event it was around sundown there was still some day light left but not much. you could make out a star or two probably planets. the temperature was around 75 or 80 degrees so I had all of my windows open airing out the house. I was watching television when my attention was turned to the increasing amount of noise coming from the wildlife out side, birds, insects, dogs, and cats were absolutely going crazy. I muted the television and said to my self "what the hell is going on" so I walked out my front door which faces North and as suddenly as the noise started it had stopped I glanced up to see a fast moving blue/white shimmering object accelerate up and away from the horizon on a curved path to me it was LEAVING our atmosphere. I have seen hundreds of meteors enter our atmosphere and I am 100% this was not a meteor, nor was it an aircraft of this planet.
