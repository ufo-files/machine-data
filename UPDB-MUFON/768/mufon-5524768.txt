Triangle, 3 large round connected red lights formed the bottom

At approx 0126-0155 7 of us witnessed this shape above the louisville metro area. it flew silently and straight from east to west, stopped, then made came back east-south east as we stood watching it. The object then went straight up until out of visibility. This was a clear night. During this event, 2 UPS planes came in below this thing. Someone had to have reported this. The 7 that witnessed this work for the government, we were at work leaving the area when I looked up and basically stared in disbelief. There were no other lights, no sound. One of the guys tried to photograph this on his i-phone, it never showed up.
