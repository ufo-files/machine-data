{nd} orange fireball flys over 3

Myself and 2 of my neighbors were having a bonfire when I witnessed a orange globe of light flying towards us. It flew over our heads with no sound a second one flew right behind it in the same path. Both objects were moving straight and than made a sharp curve to the west. The objects than stopped in place and began hovering.The lead object was in the bottom right as the follower top left. The lead began swaying or moving left and right and faded out the remaining object hovered for about another fifteen seconds and than faded out itself.
