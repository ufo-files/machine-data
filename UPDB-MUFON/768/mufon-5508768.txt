{cak} Looked like it was over the City of Paso Robles,ca Hoovering then bouncing back and forth . It came straight toward me went backwards and took off up word out of site fast , real fast . Was turning different shades of color, blue, orange and yellow.

Both my mother and I were going to Santa Barbara this morning, so I woke up at 4:00 a.m. As I passed by the kitchen window, I looked out and saw an orb. It was hovering about approx. 7 miles away over a little City of Paso Robles, ca. I stood there and watched for awhile, I lost track of time. My mother walked into the kitchen and I told her to look. Both her and I saw the most beautiful colors of Blue, Orange and yellow it was an orb hovering. It would move left and then right real fast, then came towards us I felt it knew we were watching it... Then all of a sudden it moved backward and went upward real fast. I could not belive the speed, then it was gone. I will never forget this experience. I didn't ever talk about it to anyone. It was back in the year of 1969 and you didn't talk about things like that then. My mother has now passed on...
