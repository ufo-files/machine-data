I was driving early in the morning on my way to work. It was just after 6am. I take the same path everyday, as I like to get coffee at a certain place. Directly in my line of sight, in the still dark sky, I saw a bright neon green light form in the sky. It appeared out of no where and went quickly downward until it vanished on the horizon. The crazy part was that it slowed down once it got closer to the ground. So that’s what makes me think that it was a ufo. I’ve personally never seen anything green in the sky before and I’ve never seen human technology appear, go super fast and then slow down like this object did. It was almost like it wanted me to see it, because I was already looking at the sky where it appeared. I later looked it up and discovered that humans have been seeing green ufos for centuries. I’ll never forget it.
