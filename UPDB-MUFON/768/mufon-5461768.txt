This happened during the 1960's (I estimate about 1966). I was about 11 years old, playing outside at my family's farm, which was about 18 miles north of Pasco, and directly east of Hanford Nuclear reservation. We could look west, out over the Columbia River, and see Hanford.  I believe it was mid-afternoon, during the summer months, based upon my recollection. I do not remember if there was a noise, but something (perhaps a reflection) made me look into the sky. The sky had few clouds, if any. The sky was very blue. I saw an object, very shiny, like stainless steel or chrome, hovering some distance away. I stared at it for a few (maybe 5) seconds and then it streaked away (to the east) very fast - unbelievably fast. the object appeared perfectly smooth and rounded, like a fat cigar shape, and I did not see any protrusions or colors other than "shiny like chrome". It is possible there was a sound, but I can not say with certainty.
