{rjl} The light/orb flew over my house at about 1000 feet elevation. There were five witnesses.

Me and my family were in the back yard of my house when someone pointed out a round shaped object that was coming from the east at what looked like about 1000 feet elevation. There is a lot of flight traffic I see over the years but those are very obvious because light are flashing under the aircraft and the shape of the plane is obvious. This round object made no noise, was orange colored, kept the same flight path and disappeared at a high rate of speed. One witness ran out front to see the final flight path which I did not get on tape from my cell phone but I recorded most of the object flying over my house. I was shocked at first but I immediately took out my cell phone and started to film the object. There were five witnesses to the flying object and we all were surprised and basically could not figure out what it was in the sky.
