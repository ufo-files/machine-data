Glowing disc-like object in the sky; larger than a star.

Husband and I were going to go for an evening walk when I spotted a hovering, glowing white object in the sky. It glowed, but not in the same way a star glows; plus, it was very bright and about an hour too early for stars to start appearing in the sky. It moved very slowly; we were able to watch it for about 30 minutes before it went behind clouds. Viewing it through binoculars, you could see it was egg/oval like. Sometimes disc-like. I zoomed in to take photos with my camera, and zooming in on these you could make out strange light formations on the object. These light formations appeared the same in every photo I took. I've included one photo, one zoomed photo, and one video.

Regarding its size: at one point, a jet airplane flew across the sky and it appeared to be about HALF the size of the object. This jet airplane was very high in the sky, as we couldn't hear its engines. It flew in front of some lower clouds, the same set of clouds the object eventually disappeared behind, indicating that the airplane was closer to us. So this object, while far away, must have been quite large!
