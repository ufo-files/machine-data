{jpg} caught picture on trail cam, then batteries were sucked dead within 19 seconds.

around 10 a.m. on the morning of 10-25-2015 my spouse saw "someone" walk outside of our bedroom window. I got up,and went outside to check, I found no-one, but found a very large indention in the ground in the shape of a large foot print, where the grass had been pressed into the ground and the ground had been pressed down about 1/2 inch. I showed it to her but she told me she did not think it could be a track. Later that evening I noticed my trail cam at the end of my drive way was blinking red indicating it had low/dead batteries. I thought that was strange as I had just changed them the day before. they normally last up to two weeks on a charge. so i retrieved the camera, and replaced the battery and took the memory card out of it to look at it. At 6:16 a.m. it showed a very weird set of lights that seemed to be moving at a high speed hovering above the ground, going past the camera, it got one shot of the object, then one shot of the light as it receded. Then 19 seconds after the object passed the batteries in the camera went dead. This is not normal...I am not sure what went on here, or what the camera captured. I am not claiming aliens, but I honestly have no explanation for what the picture is, or how the camera batteries were drained, and who or what was outside my house this morning that left a 20" footprint.
