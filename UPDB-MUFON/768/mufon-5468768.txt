Spouse and I witnessed Ufo 200-300 in length with pulsating and random lights flashing moving over 100 mph moving east over South Mountain Golf Course estimate 500 ft AGL. Dusk, no fog or clouds, witnessed from 600 ft of elevation from home deck. After 5 seconds of flight vehicle tilted and cloaked. Cloaking system was not instantaneous. No sign of vehicle after it cloaked. As it was cloaking you could begin to see the transparent background of the Wasatch Mountains, blurry then clear. No possibility this was man made, the lights of the craft are almost indescribable, the pulsating pattern was rapid and very odd. The black light coming from the rear of the craft was consistent and pulsating.
