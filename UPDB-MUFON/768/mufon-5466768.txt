{jpg} Saw odd object, possible triangle shape, hovering on a path , zero sound, came back around a second time then continued toward a path behind trees

I was having a cigarette outside, lounging, talking to friends in a chatroom on my mobile. I noticed the object as it was fairly close, oddly shaped, no sound whatsoever and flew low and seemingly aimless. Definitely not a plane and too large to be a drone. The object slowly hovered toward the treetops but came back around a second time and I ran up for a better look as I've never seen anything like this before. I felt awed and excited, telling my friends, and tried twice to record a video but was too late. The object eventually returned to its first path and I lost sight of it as it went behind the trees.
