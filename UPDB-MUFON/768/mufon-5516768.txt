{dr}Solid bright red light making S shaped movement

I was in my car at 9th and West Ave sitting at the light about mid block. I noticed between the telephone/electrical wires and the corner of Luigis Restuarant a red light that was wavering slowly back and forth and slowly going up and down to a lesser degree than it was going back and forth.

The light turned green and I waited about 3/4th of a light cycle to turn left. I entered the McDonalds which is on the corner of 9th and West and proceeded to the far side of the parking lot. I parked my car and saw that the red light was still in the sky at approximately the same position it was when I observed it at the light. I watched it for about a minute before it occured to me that I have a camera on my phone. 

I opened my car door and I took one picture of it. Then I remembered that I also have a movie mode on my camera on my phone. I looked down at the phone to switch it to movie mode and looked back up it was gone. I looked away for less than 5 seconds. I do not know what happened to it.

The picture is grainy, but it does show the red light and the only reference point is a light pole from the parking lot which was in front of my car in the foreground.
