Light and Object discovered in photo

I stepped out onto my Eastern deck approximately 7:15 pm on January 29, 2021 to photograph the rising moon for a friend. I spent several minutes listening to the night sounds and looking to the sky since it was a clear night. Not noticing anything unusual, I took the shot and came in. Later in the evening I pulled up the picture to send to my friend and noticed the bright light above and to the right of the moon. Since I had not seen it when outside I was stunned that I had missed it. I do not have adequate equipment but cropped it out and blew it up and was shocked that it seems to resemble a machine with four rounded forms at what I took as the bottom. I have attached the pictures for your review.
