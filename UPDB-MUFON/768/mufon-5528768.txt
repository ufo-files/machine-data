Small circle bright light in the sky at dusk moved closer then expanded in size and shunk and expanded again

I was driving home from work on US1 and was thinking about my move to Mt Shasta and thinking how the times were changing and had the thought about a UFO then looked up in the sky and saw a bright light that was small very far in the distance. I thought, could that be a UFO, then this object came closer and became larger. As I was stopped behind the cars in front of me I stared at the object and thought what if the light began to expand and then the object grew 4 times its original size then went back to being a speck, then I passed under some trees and when I was back out it was gone.
