Cloud like object with sparkles

Witnessed from our driveway in Brentwood. Shortly after World War II planes flew overhead.
We saw something so weird about an hour ago. At first it looked like a white garbage bag floating in the sky then I thought balloon but the shape was off and it had a definite trajectory. I got the binoculars and up close it looked like a cloud with sparkles it didnt have a definite shape then slowly drifted off. I thought maybe a disguised drone but the edges were wispy and see through like a cloud the center more dense where the sparkles emanated. My boyfriend is an experienced pilot and has no explanation either.
