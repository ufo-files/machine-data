{cak} Large solid orange light unlike anything ive seen before. Appeared to leave atmosphere.

I don't have much info on the exact date as it has been several months. The sighting date i've given is probably off by a month or so before or after but I just found out about MUFUON literally minutes ago (watching UFO Files) but I remember the experience in detail so I will tell it exactly as it happened.

My girlfriend and I were on our way home after work at around 8:30 to 9:30 p.m., we were driving up Johnston st. in Lafayette, LA. when I saw the object high in the sky. It was a very large glowing orange light, larger than the lights on any aircraft i've ever seen in the sky, roughly the size of a penny to my eye. I immediately said aloud "What the f*** is that?"
The object was moving in the opposite direction of us so I lost sight of it as it was now over the roof of my vehicle, not close, very far and very high in the sky. I could not determine the shape of the object but judging by the size of the light it had to have been enormous.
I took the very next left turn and pulled into a parking lot and my girlfriend and I got out of our vehicle, maybe a total of 20 seconds after initial sighting of the object. It was now heading in the complete opposite direction having apparently made a very sharp shift in direction, not a U-turn but more like a V-turn. We watched as the object now appeared to be sitting still but it quickly became apparent that it was actually heading straight out of the atmosphere. I have never witnessed an aircraft leave the earth's atmosphere but I we both watched it fade outward. Again, it did NOT disappear over the horizon, it disappeared into the sky.
About an hour after witnessing, I was at home when I decided to search around on Google for any reports on large orange lights over the sky in Lafayette. I bookmarked this site.. I described what I had seen in an email and sent it to the address given on the site but never got a reply.
