Glowing Orbs

Overlooking the Mt. Vesuvius from the coast of Sorrento, I spotted two glowing orbs, the first one was white and reasonably bright, it followed a steady path then disappeared behind the vesuvius. The second one appeared shortly after the first one was gone, it was emitting a slighlty dimmer red glow, it followed the same path of the white one, and disappeared behind the Mt. Vesuvius as well.
