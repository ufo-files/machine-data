Triangular shape, unload and goes away

I am not sure that this can help you in anyway but I have been holding this for decades now. In 1973 or 74, in a summer evening, I saw a triangularshaped item going thru the clouds. It had white lights that clearly defined the object. Then, it stopped and stayed immobile for a few seconds. It was just below the clouds line and I was looking at it with binoculars. Then, a serie of small bright objects seemed to be coming out of the object from below. I could not say how many but there was a lot, at least 12 to 15, if no more. Once they were all out of the "mothership" (?), the lights of the triangular object went from bright white to red. The object started to move slowly thru the clouds. I could easily follow it untill it zoomed very fast out of sight. The smaller objects that came our from it had all moved in different directions and this, VERY fast. If this can help you, GOOD. If not, ho well.... I undestand, this was long ago. Since then, it does happens to me to have "lost time" every now and then.
