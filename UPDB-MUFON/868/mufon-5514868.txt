(GS) Son and friends heard loud rumbling noise and seen Blueish white light speed horizontally across the skies and ascended quickly in an L shape pattern and disappeared in high altitude within those 2 secs.

Received a call from my son while I was at work and was informed that he might have seen a UFO and indicated that a few of his friends had also seen it and mentioned it on facebook. We reside in Daly City, CA. My son informed me that about 1950hr. on 10-16-2012, he had heard a loud rumbling noise that drew his attention to look out his window. When he looked up, he had seen a blueish white light streaking above residential homes on clear night skies as the rumbling noise increased. He couldnt make out the shape of the object but thought it looked a flat object. After it passed over a few dozen homes, it made an abrupt vertical L shape turn straight up into the clear night skies and disappeared in about 2 secs. at very high speeds and high elevations. Wonder if anyone has seen this and reported this?
