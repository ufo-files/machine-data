Two bright spots (not Jupiter and Venus)

After work and on the way home I saw two stationary points of light next to each other in the sky. Both had gloss, although one was more intense than the other. Initially I thought it was a plane or the rendez vous of Jupiter and Venus planets but not found because the lights gradually disappeared. The light's were spoted (Between East and North)from observation point 38130'22.40''N 8158'17.74''O near Arr1bida - Picheleiros
