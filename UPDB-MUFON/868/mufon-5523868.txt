V-shaped with 3 distinct lights

I was at St. Helen's Church in Newbury, Ohio on 9/24/2011 playing bingo. At approximately 8 pm I went outside with a group of people for a cigarette break. A lady that was standing next to me noticed 3 lights in the sky over the lake across the street that formed a perfect V-shape. We thought at first that it might be a tower, but realized the lights were not blinking. We watched this for about a minute and then it just faded out!
