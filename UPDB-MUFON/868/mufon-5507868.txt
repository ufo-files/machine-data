{rjl} West to East object, no sound, no wings, no navigational lights.

At 6:53 p.m. 13 Sept. 2013 I witnessed a silver cylindrical object fly from West to East North of my house on the North side of Cannon Falls. This object appeared to be a jet at 3 to 4 thousand feet. Made no noise (calm evening). Looked closer for wings. There were none. No navigation lights. Stared at object for over 10 seconds as it rode as if on a rail through the atmosphere.
