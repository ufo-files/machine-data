{rjl} Large Triangle shaped vessel appeared over my head while driving in I-25. It just appeared out of thin air.

I was coming home from a international soccer game between USA and Trinidad & Tobago at Dicks Sporting good stadium in Denver. There was an accident so I was late coming home. I was driving on I-25 through the tech center by the Belleview exit. Above me appeared a large triangular vessel with round dull lights in each corner. Two arms lit up brightly 3 lights on each, moving and scanning the area. Hovered for 20 seconds and zipped away faster than I have ever seen something move. It disappeared to the west. I know what I had seen that night was beyond any technology I am aware of. I am not obsessed with ufos or anything like that, however in a universe with infinite possibilities, I did not question that what I had seen was not of this world.
