Bright star like object hovering over the mountain for a few minutes. Lights faded then moved west and dissapeared.

Another witness and i were outside smoking when we had noticed the air had become completely still. Very strange for being on top of a mountain. The smoke wouldnt move. Strange noises in the forest out back but this wasnt bothersome. We thought nothing of it. I looked over at the other witness when i saw this star like thing floating in the sky. We talked about what was happening and described back and forth what eachother was seeing. I had made a phone call to a friend durring this time. There was no interferance. The light faded and all of a sudden moved west. Its lights dim began blinking in colors of airplane lights. Almost like a camoflauge. it continued straight until dissapearing.
