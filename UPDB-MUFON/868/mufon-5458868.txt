{cak} Triangular, black unknown object that went in a straight line, no change in elevation. Stationary rotation of the object (triangle) as it flew in air

I don’t remember my exact age or the exact date it was about eight years ago, but one afternoon I was in my backyard a saw a black, triangular object in the sky. It was flying from the NW to the SE. The object itself was rotating while flying through the air. It maintained the same speed from when I first saw it until it was out of sight. There were no sudden moments of the object or sudden change of elevation.
