Glowing different colored lights

I was taking pictures with my cell phone of the Hunter's Moon that night over the Chesapeake Bay and 4 glowing objects appeared on the left bottom side of my picture frame, They just hovered in the same location but I couldn't hear any noise coming from them, which I thought was very strange because the bay water was still that night and sound really travels over the water from where I was at. One of the object made a sharp right handturn and sped off to the south east, was gone for approx. a minute and then returned to the other three, which were still hovering in the same spot. Joint Base Andrews AFB had launched fighter jets, which I heard coming from the northwest, behind me and when I turned back to see them and then turned back to the glowing objects they were gone, without a sound.
