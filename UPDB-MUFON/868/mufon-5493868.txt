Hovering fireball-like orb.

I was driving home from work after my shift ended at just after midnight. Where I live there are no street lights and it was a cloudy night, no stars or moon visible. As I reached the top of the hill, less then a mile from home a light caught my eye and I looked out of my car window. To my right there is a hill, with Scots Pine trees on it. Right above the trees, a good few feet above them an orb of fire suddenly appeared. It did not move and just seemed to pulsate a few times before it vanished just as quickly as it had appeared. If it had not been so cloudy I would have thought it was a meteor, but it also did not move across the sky as you would expect it to. I was so surprised that I nearly drove off the road. I stopped and waited to see if it would appear again but I saw nothing more. It had just gone.
