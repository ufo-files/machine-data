Fast Moving Orange Object

[Items moved/CMS/nd] For some reason I looked to the southwest and I saw an orange dot with no contrail moving very fast and disappear. It reappeared a second later and disappeared, again. I see large airliners all the time in which they emit contrails, especially late in the evening. This had no emissions and what was eerie, it appeared and disappeared. I never saw the object again. It was like it vanished. It was a clear beautiful evening. I look at everything in a scientific way, but this is something that I can not explain.
