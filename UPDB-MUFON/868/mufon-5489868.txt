{jpg} orb that morhped into a long tubular and twisted shape with multi colors and costantly changing shape

this report only allows details for 1 siting. I had 3 of the same thing in 2 different places just a couple days apart. 1st siting was in New Mexico. A friend and I were camping. Looking up at the stars and the milky way. We both saw this point of light that was acting strange,(moving without changing position, changing colors and blinking. So, I picked up my camera (canon 70d with 500mm lens and tripod with remote) To see if I could see it better and I was totally shocked. Then I started to take what shots I could and then I zoomed in my screen on my camera and was absolutely stunned, speechless. I continued to shoot as much as I could. I am a very level headed and rational person. Getting excited doesn't take me off my game. 1st, the object started out as an orb of bright light. Then gradually a tubular shaped thing started to protrude from the orb until it was very, very long and twisted and curly with many, many different colors (rainbow like). It also continuously kept changing shape the entire time. Then it would disappear fast than you can blink. After a while it would come back and do the same thing. Also, There was several of these in the sky in different places and different altitudes. Each was accompanied by others off in the distance. My friend was with me at the New Mexico siting. Another friend was with me at the Colorado siting. I have about 80 shots and they are very, very crisp considering the distance involved. I prefer not to attach my photos until they are copyrighted. I would be willing to bring them to you on my laptop wherever you are.
