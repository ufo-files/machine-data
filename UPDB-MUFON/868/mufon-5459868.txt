Spotted 1 large glowing orb over the Gulf Of Mexico roughly 5 miles SE of Biloxi, MS. Orb split into 3 slightly smaller orbs. The glowing orbs resembled a fireball, with a bright red glow that seemed to flicker. As the orb split into 3, they went into a triangle formation and seemed to rotate clockwise, continuously changing position. Almost as if one would take the lead for a minute or 2. I was with a few friends at the beach. We had just finished dinner. We all saw it and were in complete disbelief. Some other civilians on the beach freaked out when they saw it. A few people ran to their cars and one lady began to cry. It was astonishing. Near the end of the 5 minute sighting, the 3 orbs were now over land and heading NW over Kessler AFB. That’s when the 3 orbs came back and joined together to form the 1 bigger orb and rapidly faded as if dissapearing into space. Shortly after, 2 helicopters were seen circling the area. They looked like Blackhawks, but we didn’t have any helicopters at either Kessler AFB or at the naval station in Gulfport. This was the main event that opened my eyes to the fact that we are not alone. I was in so much awe that I didn’t even reach for my phone. I just watched in amazement. I just discovered MUFON tonight. This is why I’m finally sharing my story.
