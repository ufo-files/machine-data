ll-08-2014 UFOS OVER COON VALLEY, WI

Tonight 11-08-2014 at about 5:41 PM I was coming home from deer hunting when I had a strange Experience. I was near the coon valley Post Office when I noticed a strange light in the sky. I watched it as it move to the SE at a slow speed, and then moved upward got smaller and disappeared. Where it went I do not know. Next a second light appeared to the West of the first UFO it made some strange moves, was very bright and very strange. Like the first it had a fairly bright center with an orange glow around it. I photographed this UFO, and the photo below is of this second of the two UFOs. Both look similar. I detected no sound; however the traffic sound on Highway 14 was loud, and made it hard to hear. I hope others seen these two UFOs too.  Both UFOs (never together went from West to South East in erratic patters of flight. Each took about 40 to 50 seconds or so from first appearance to their slowly ascending and apparent disappearance. There was an overcast sky, and they were below the clouds; however, they may have disappeared by flying through the clouds and becoming no longer visible as a result. The distance they traveled is at best a guess, and that would be around 1 to 1 1 miles. I do not know if anyone else noticed these two objects. The photo is a blow up of the image I took of the 2nd object. My camera was at perhaps 50X optical, and I cropped the photo to see if any details would appear. The photo appears as strange as the object appeared to my eyes. If it was terrestrial my best guess is a helicopter; however they did not appear to be helicopters or aircraft since no flashing lights.
