VERY LARGE RED AND GREEN PULSATING LIGHTS /SLOW MOVING

Monday Feb 7 at aprox 7:30opm we were eating dinner, my 13 year old saw a very large green light out the window and asked what it was..so I went in the back yard and saw a giant green light and I think at least 2 large red lights flashing, I didn't see a shape, it looked like a cluster of pulsating lights slow moving west. My daughter came outand says she thought it had a outline of oval and or boomerag shape. We both tried to take a picture and video with our cell phones and we couldnt, it wouldnt come out on the camera. I tried so many times for like 15 min., I didnot hear any noise from the object, but my daughter thought it was like a swooshing water sound. It moved slowly towards the coast near Malibu it looked like and then it seemed to go over a hill and then there was a flashing red light that stayed there all night. I have looked outside last night and noticed that the small flashing red light on the hill is always there, must be like a coast guard light. We have seen some white lights on January 24, 2011 up here in a triangular shape, but that seems to be very common on these sites. This was very different, it was HUGE...something I have never seen before.
