{tjd} Series of star-like lights moving west to east in roughly the same direction before dispersing into a wider field of directions after several seconds

The four of us witnessed several small lights moving west to east in roughly the same direction before suddenly dispersing and disappearing. This took place over only a few seconds. There was no sound and the sudden fluctuation to the flight path seem to eliminate the possibility of helicopters or planes. The lights were not moving in an orbital flight path, especially the sudden shift in path shortly before vanishing. This would eliminate the ISS or orbiting satellites. The lights were uniform and did not shift, flicker or change size, eliminating the possibility of it being birds with light reflecting on their undersides. There was no contrail or flashes emanating from behind the objects either. That, along with their sudden change in direction also would seem to eliminate meteor activity.
