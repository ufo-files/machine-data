Two orbs hovering and shining brightly then moving upward simultaneously, one orb nearby began moving too but not with the other two.

I was getting ready to go to sleep at about 12:30am and my cat was bothering me for a few minutes prior. I decided to get up and close the curtain in my room so that the sun wouldn't wake me up early in the morning when I saw two very bright stars shining in the sky out my window. I first felt calm because I usually love looking at the stars, then something felt off and I realised the brightness of them was odd. After that, they all of a sudden both began moving upwards in the sky together in perfect synchronization and a very quick, instant start. They went up until they began fading and I lost sight of them almost directly above me. I looked around the sky and saw another orb, similar brightness, moving along the sky at the same speed but it also faded away after a short time. I quickly went to other side of the house (facing north) to see if I could find them maybe reappear over the town but I couldn't find anything but the regular stars that are there.
