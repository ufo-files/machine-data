{tjd} Extremely bright light in vertical decent like a meteor but no smoke trail

On Saturday June 22 at approximately 8am local time, I had stopped for gas while traveling west on Interstate 70 through Kansas. The sky was overcast with cloud bases estimated to be 2000 -2500 feet. I was stopped (approximately at the 17 mile marker facing east, on the phone looking east when I saw a flash of light (first interpreted as a meteor) dart from the clouds in a near vertical path and disappear into the ground. It traveled from the cloud base to the ground in roughly 1.5 - 2 seconds but did not make a sound. It did not leave a smoke trail or project any characteristics that solid material would be expected to display. I instantly commented to my friends on the phone what I had just seen referring at first to it being a meteor but then questioned the remark recounting that it displayed no characteristics of a meteor and made no sound of impact.
