'bottom end' of huge boxy craft silently ascends into cloud

I was watching a 3/4 moon and 2 oddly uniform clouds which parted leaving a space of pure black sky. At the instant the clouds parted, I saw a brief glimps 1-2 seconds of a UFO. It was rising silently from lower to upper dloud. It was very large and boxy. the Nose of craft hidden behind upper cloud as the 2 clouds parted. the rear end of craft was visible until it ascended into upper cloud. 

the craft: size of small apartment buidling, color of charcoal or slate gray, dull. 
The corners of the squarish bottom end of UFO (which I saw as it ascended into upper cloud) were lit with 4 to 6 bright red lights. The movement was silent, slow, ascending almost floating, upward and to my right. Seen from 3rd floor of my open window (no screen). Sky was oddly black (moon was bright and clearly visible during sighting. Only other celestial object was a single planet? to the left of rising moon.) The view was all seen just above the tall-tree-covered ravine outside my window, but nothing I saw here was obstructed by trees. It was all above the treeline. 2 odd clouds (as I call them) appeared very odd, flat, and gray against the black sky. No color variation at all in the '2' clouds. 
After the huge UFO 'entered' the upper cloud, the lower cloud continued to descend, and the upper cloud remained for some time. I watched for 5 more minutes to see nothing else other than constant shifting of misty formations in sky, I got up to tell my family.
