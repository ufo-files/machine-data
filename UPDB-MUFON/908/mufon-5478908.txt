{dr}2 flying objects moving what appears to be in unison disappearing into clouds

Noted 2 disc like objects flying together in perfectly straight line eventually disappearing into cloud cover. I did not see them reappear.
I noted no sound, however they were far enough away that sound may have been present. I estimate that they were at lower cloud level altitude. They moved somewhat quickly and were not affected by wind by the eye. The view of the houses in the video gives some perspective to the height and speed of these objects.
