I was standing in my driveway leaning up against my car looking at the clouds over waycross when out all of a sudden I saw a bright white and red light appear. At first I thought it was a helicopter but when I saw that they didn't have the flashing lights or nor did it make a sound. (in this part of Georgia when its 9:30pm you can hear a car drive by on the freeway from 5 miles away) So when I did not hear a single sound I was starting to wonder what it was. When it made a sudden direction change I knew this was not a helicopter or an airplane. I watched in amazement as the object appeared to gain altitude and proceeded to get my grandmother to witness the event with me. We stood outside on our back deck as we watched the lights get smaller intel the object stopped and hovered in the sky for about 8-10 seconds be for the red and white light started to flash randomly for about 3 seconds be for they stopped and the object just disappeared.
