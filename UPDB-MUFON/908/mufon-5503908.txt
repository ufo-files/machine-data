{kt}Went outside before sunrise & saw objects. Additional objects showed up in photo.

Walked outside with my dogs and saw objects, knew it was not stars. No stars, sun, nor moon was in the sky. Did not know what object was and got camera. Only notices all the other objects, i.e., the multiple orbs and straight line of red and white lights on ground. Telephone pole was barely seen to the left of each photo. This was not my first sighting, but first time I was able to get camera and take photos of the objects. I lost sight of the object(s) about 3 - 3 1/2 hours after I first noticed the objects.
