Two objects disappear

While looking out my bathroom window I saw two lighted objects just south of my home. Appeared to be no more the 2000' AGL heading from south to north. 
There were two objects. The lead object was bright orange. I mean super bright orange. The trail object was also orange but only a quarter as bright. The trail object appeared to be about a few hundred yards behind the lead object. After observing both of them for about 10-15 seconds the trail object just disappeared. It was almost like it turned off it's lights. The lead object continued to head north but after about 10 seconds it too simply disappeared. I thought that was odd since FAA rules dictate pilots cannot simply turn off their anti collision lights while operating at night. This was at 8:30pm. Sunset for Sept 7 was 5:50pm.
