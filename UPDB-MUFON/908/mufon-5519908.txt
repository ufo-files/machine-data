4 Orange lights in dog-leg formation, followed by 5th orange light, over Austin, Texas. Faded into twilight sky.

Im in Austin, Texas saw 4 orange lights in a dog-legged formation, seemingly moving slowly, towards my position, yet covering several degrees of distance (I could not read the depth of my perception for lack of reference points in the clear twilight sky). Moving from east to west, at first I thought they we military helicopters heading north to Ft. Hood, Texas as I have seen many times before, but there was no sound and then a 5th orange light appeared where the first 4 appeared just as the first 4 started to dim and fade into nothingness, before my eyes. then the 5th orange light followed the same path and faded in the same area of the sky, as the first 4 orange lights.

I have watched planes and star gazed from my deck for many years and this was 1-of-a-kind, bizarre and unexplainable visual. 

This occurred at exactly 8:17pm CDT (Central Daylight Time), March 24, 2012, (Saturday night) at Lat 30N and Long97W

I cannot find any refrence to these orange lights online or local news, except weatherwatch.com.nz, but this is almost the exact opposable Latitude and Longitude and almost the same time.
