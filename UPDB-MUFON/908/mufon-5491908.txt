{dr}Glowing ball of fire

On the 4th of July I was outside with my family, my daughter, mom, and step dad. We were enjoying the fireworks in the sky when I noticed a Glowing ball of fire, yellow/orange/pink colors, appeared from nothing. I alerted my family to come see this strange thing in the sky and they were all amazed. It flew slowly east to west three times, each time disappearing to nothing. All my family members observed this. A few times we saw the same object flash and disappear in different locations close to where we observed it. I had goose bumps all up my arms and was in prayer as my family was also. My daughter and I took video with two different phones, one an apple 6 and the other an android One Plus One.
