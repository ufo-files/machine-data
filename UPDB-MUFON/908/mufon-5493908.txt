{rl} Two cigar shaped ufo during daylight near Gulf of Mexico

We were going to Steinhatchee this morning and as I was watching a cloud I saw a cigar shape, seemed whitish blue heading for the cloud, before it reached the cloud another cigar shaped whitish blue ufo came out of the cloud and headed toward the Gulf. The first one then vanished into the cloud. I watched the second one for a few moments and then it vanished also. They both seemed about the same altitude which was cloud height.
