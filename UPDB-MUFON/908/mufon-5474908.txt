{rjl} Bright yellow dot was in the center of the big Dipper not moving, went dark about 10 times

I woke up around 3am and went outside for a smoke. I saw a bright yellow light in the center of the Big Dipper in the northern sky. It went dark about 10 times over a 5 minutes, not quite flashing. The yellow ball slowly moved from West to East, then it disappeared. I went inside to get my phone to take a picture of it but the yellow ball didn't show up on it. I checked NASA's website to see if it was ISS. It wasn't, but 2 days later, ISS did fly over in the same location as the yellow dot but ISS was a clear light that moved at a fast rate from West to East.
