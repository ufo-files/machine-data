Unexplained circlular object with lights seen over Newport coast by a crowd of people on a night cruise on 11/08/08.

Me and my boyfriend were on a yaught in Newport Beach, CA the evening of 11/08/08. We made our way out towards the deck,and began meeting new people, making "small talk". I was expressing my interest in UFO/USO pheonomenon, and began to share some of the stories I had heard about with a small group of people. Moments later, a man near by pointed and shouted, "what's that, in the sky?" At least 15-20 people who were outside on the deck looked in the direction of the man's finger. There are scattered, blinking lights in the distance. They were airplanes in the sky from the airport that was located just a few miles away. But, between the blinking-airplane lights was a circular object about 10,000 feet away. Initially I thoguht it was another airplane. But this object was stationary and not blinking. The doughnut-shaped object had 9-12 white lights surrounding the outter edges, and appeared to be a little smaller than the size of a blimp. The object was stationary for a moments at a time and would make sharp, sudden movements vertically and horizontally, traveling from 500-5,000 feet in distance. It appeared to reach as low as 500 feet from the ground. The object would also change degree of the craft itself, making a "ring" of lights visible periodically. It traveled a distant radius of about 5,000 feet for around 4-6 minutes. Myself, and the crowd of skeptics on the deck witnessing this event were confused and anxious for anyone who could answer the daughnting question of what that was. I reached for my digital camera and started recording the unexplained pheonomenon. Moving through the harbor, the craft became less visible and was shortly consumed by the lanscape.
