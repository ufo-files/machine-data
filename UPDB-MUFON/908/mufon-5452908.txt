Large red-orange orb

At 8:40pm I was walking from my parking spot to my condo. As I came around the building I saw a very bright red-orange light moving slowly in the east traveling north to south. It was moving similar in both speed and altitude as a plane on approach to Lambert International, but the airport is the other direction and I never see them travel this vector nor anything creating a ball of light this size and color. I watched for about 90 seconds as it moved steadily along and then flickered twice and completely disappeared. It was still light enough to be able to see the fuselage of a small plane farther south, so I have no idea where this went.
