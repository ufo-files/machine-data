{nd} UFO over Park City, shot across the sky in ways that modern technology can not do

I drive a bus in Park City. I was sitting at a mandatory stop in Deer Valley at the snow Park Lodge. I usually will get out and wait for my time point to leave, so i was standing and leaning against the wall facing down Deer Valley when a light ascended over the hill. It was flashing red, white, blue and at first i thought it was a helicopter, since Life Flight is a constant thing to see here. I love to watch and listen to helicopters fly, that is why i noticed it. but then it shot across the skyline, stopped on a dime, shot up, stopped, and then shot again at an angle towards Salt Lake City where i lost sight of it as it went out of sight. The sighting lasted no more than 30 seconds, but i do know what i saw. There is no way we have technology capable of that. I have seen other strange aeronautical things around this area.
