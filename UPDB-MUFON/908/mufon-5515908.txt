{dr}Bright light moving from east to west, stopped over Lake Erie, hovered and headed straight up.

On the evening of July 3, 2012, we were outside waiting for fireworks to begin over Lake Erie in Vemilion Township, Mitiwanga Park. Our party saw a bright light that was streaking from the east towards where we were watching, it suddenly stopped and hovered, it then changed directions and went straight into the sky.
We all saw it - family members and friends - and talked about it immediately and for days later.
My husband called the Sheriffs office but they did not have reports of the sighting.
I might add that my husband and I are former newspaper reporters and experienced observers. We both saw exactly the same thing, as did the others around us.
It was not a plane or a star, we have never seen a sight like it.
