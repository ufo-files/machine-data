Two lights moving in tandem across the sky

We observed two star like objects, first near another star/planet in the sky, forming a triangle when we first saw it. At first we thought they were satellites but we have never seen two of them together traveling In the same direction. Then the two lights started moving southeast together at a consistent distance from each other and stayed in sync until we could not see them behind the mountains/trees around our campsite. There was a faint hum, nothing like the jets we heard all day and night but otherwise it was eerily quiet while they were moving across the sky. The lights were too far apart to be from one jet airliner, as we saw a variety of those all night and they did not have any other FAA lights on the them.
