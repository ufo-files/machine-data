{nd} Noticed a small grey metallic object that hovered for several minutes in rural Northwest San Antonio

I was traveling on Highway 46 West, just 3 miles west of Higway 281. I was traveling toward the city of Boerne,TX around 5:15 pm on 12/23/13. As I was driving, I noticed an object out of the corner of my eye. The object didn't move and appeared to hover quite a distance in altitude. Around the same time, I noticed a commercial jetliner traveling near the stationary object. I noticed blinking lights and exhaust from the jetliner, while no lighting or exhaust was noticed on the stationary object. As dusk was approaching and the sun was setting in the horizon, the sunlight reflected off of the object. I tried to take a picture of the object with my professional camera, but I had no memory card inside. I then decided to take pictures of the object using a camera phone. I took several pictures of the object, before my sight line of the object was hampered by trees and the road going from a hilly terrain to those of lowlands. I noticed an object in roughly the same direction before, but a few miles to the east of Highway 46, traveling west. The object hovered as well, but was VERY reflective, due to the time of d1y(around 1pm) on a cloudless, sunny day. I had at first attributed this object to be a military plane of some sort, due to its proximity to nearby Camp Bullis and Camp Stanley military outposts. Now, after viewing the second object , Im not quite sure. Both incidents left me nervous. I couldnt believe that an object like that was displayed in bright daylight. Later that night after showing pictures of the object with a friend that lives in the area, I asked him if he had noticed any strange ph1nomena in the skies? He replied that he has never seen anything in the skies, but has heard several military maneuvers nearby at the aforementioned Camp Bullis.
