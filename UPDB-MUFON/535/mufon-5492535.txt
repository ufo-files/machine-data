{rjl} Hovering craft that was stationary for 10 minutes then took off in a flash

My mother and I were driving down the road when I pointed out hovering lights in the distance. We pulled over to the side of the road at watched the craft hover like a helicopter but with no sound. We observed multicolor lights blinking off and on in a steady pattern. After about 10 minutes the craft took off in a NE direction and upwards faster than any craft I had ever seen.
