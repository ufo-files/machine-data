{dr}Slowly Hovering Random flashing Object Changed Shape/ slowly turning in sky then fade away as i walked closer.

Walking down Bienville, at about Chartres going south towards the river when i noticed an unusual glittering, randomly blinking/ shining object about the size of an asprin pill hovering at about what looked like over the river, 1 mile away. Being instantly was suspicious of the object as it did not have the traditional navigation or anti-collision lights and was just hovering there moving slowly in a kinda of random, unusual way. 

I stood taking pictures from less than 1 miles as the object hovered above the river just walked stumbling down the Bienville towards the river stopping to take a few pictures, but was unable to get a clearer shot. The object looked as tho it was getting smaller as i was getting closer to the river. As i crossed Decatur St and walked up onto the levee i lost sight of the object & was unable to locate the object again. 

I asked some people who had been sitting on the levee the whole time if they saw it and they said they did not and couldnt believe the pictures i got.
