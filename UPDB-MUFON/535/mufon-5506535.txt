{kt}big bright orange balls of light that appeared, split, and then faded.

I was sitting in my house watching tv when I happened to glance out the window and saw two big bright orange balls of light in the sky. They never moved but then one ball faded into nothing and then the other. My mother was watching tv with me and a short while later said she said one ball in a different direction still not moving but then it split into three balls in a line and by the time I saw them they split into five in a line. Then from right to left they each started to fade. About 10 to 15 minutes later we both saw two more orange balls a little ways away from the five in a line. they did the same thing. After each sighting I ran outside but heard and saw nothing. Eventually they started appearing randomly farther and farther away to the South still. A few other people I know said they had seen the same thing on either the same night or the night before.
