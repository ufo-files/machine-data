I rolled over in my bed, and I saw a very beautiful object outside.

After retiring for the night and going to bed I rolled over after 5 seconds and I saw outside my upstaires apartment bedroom window a very beautiful object. It was traveling at 1 mph towards the preschool right over my carports about 15 feet away. It looked like an outline of a concoction of boxes. There were small Christmas tree type lights in every corner blinking randomly. The center of it had clear stained glass with a cross in the center. The center showed that there was light inside. Because it was dark in my apartment, and outside I could only see the outline of it. I mean I could not tell how thick it was. It really was very beautiful. I somehow got the feeling that the object was on auto-pilot, and that there were two or three beings arguing inside. I viewed the object on my elbow in my bed. Thank you.
