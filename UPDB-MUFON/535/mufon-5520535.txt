Took a photo using Nikon D90 of a very low vapor trail about 100 feet above the house.

I was in the living room on the computer and my dog wanted to go to the backyard for a restroom break. When I went outside I looked up and saw a very low white cloudy vapor trail about 100 feet high above the house on a clear night. I didn't hear any jet or airplane sounds. I found it strange that a vapor trail was so low and I always thought this can only happen at very high altitudes. So I went inside and grabbed my camera. I wanted to capture the vapor trail but when I reviewed the picture, there was no vapor trail but instead a blue orb hovering above my house about 500 ft. This orb appeared to be glowing blue inside and blue white outside with rough edges, like a pulsating orb. I took several more pictures and it seem to have moved to left upper quadrant of the photo. I took a couple more shots and the object disappeared.
