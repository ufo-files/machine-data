{dr}2 glowing red orbs spotted in overland park is July 4

@ 9:55 pm cdt my family and I were returning home from a fireworks show. As we turned on the street we noticed a bright red light pulsating in the sky. We quickly pulled into the drive and got out of our car to watch the craft go over head. It was a bright red orb that glowed making it look somewhat fuzzy. The craft flew by not making a sound going from south to north. We watched it for a min or two and it was gone. I then noticed a second light coming from the south again. It was the same type of craft on the same flight path. We tried to film the second with an iPhone. It was a poor quality video, but we do have it.
