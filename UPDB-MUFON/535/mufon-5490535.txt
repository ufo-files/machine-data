{nd} I was driving down the road when I saw these lights in the sky and then they disappeared.

I was driving down the road on the passenger seat. All of a sudden I look out the windshield and my father (driving) and I see lights. There were about 7 bright orangish yellow lights in the sky. They looked like about the size of a baseball in the sky. The lights were loosely clustered. There many other planes in the sky that night. All of a sudden, the loose cluster of lights got closer and then just disappeared.
