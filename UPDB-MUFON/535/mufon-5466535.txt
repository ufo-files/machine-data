{cak} Looked up from the bed of a truck on the road saw a yellowish star bigger/brighter than mars, stared, and it darted behind a thin contrail and disappeared.

I was riding in the back of a truck on some backroads with a friend, I was looking for meteors and saw a bright yellowish white star where it shouldn't have been. It sat still for a few seconds, and then darted off to the right behind what I guess was a contrail, just a long thin line of clouds. I watched for the rest of the ride and never saw it come out. My jaw dropped the moment it disappeared, I almost couldn't believe it. Then my friend commented on how nice the sky was and I laughed hysterically for a good minute without telling him what I saw. Almost forgot by the time I got home.
