{cak} white cigar craft spins hovering

I was driving home from work heading West on highway 72. As I approached CR 735 to make a right turn I noticed a hyper white object in the sky. I pulled my car over and got out to observe it. The sky was almost perfectly clear with a few light sparse clouds. The object stood out. I stood perfectly still and observed a cylindrical object with rounded corners (tic tac shape) spinning slowly. It widen as the sides faced me and dimmed as the edges faced me. I moved so that the object was sitting directly above a power pole. This let me see that it was perfectly still while spinning on its horizontal axis. It remained locked on its position for over 3 minutes then slowly descended while moving north. It remained spinning without ever slowing or speeding up. It continued to descend until it dipped below the tree line and I lost sight of it. I took 3 photos but none show the object as it was hard to see the screen to aim the shot while the sun was shining on it. The object seemed to spin at about 50-60 rpm which is why the pictures may not have captured it as it was turning and not very visible as the edges faced me.
