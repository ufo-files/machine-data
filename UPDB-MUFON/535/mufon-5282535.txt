Saw an unusually bright object outside the window and opened the front door to film

I woke up and saw what appeared to be an unusually bright light in the sky, so I opened the front door and filmed the object. It appeared to be hovering initially, and then it moved. When filming, I had my hand pressed next to the door so the phone was stationary. I only moved it up once to follow it after it started moving, and toward the end I zoomed out and then back in fully for perspective; a couple small stars can be seen near it.
