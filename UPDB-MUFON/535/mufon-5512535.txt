(GS) red/orange lights resembled chinese lanterns about 9 of them ascending in a geometrical shape almost like a triangle then disappeared. They moved slowly almost like hovering.

It was 9pm and I was driving when I saw the first couple of lights. I stopped my car and called my husband to go see it on the balcony. I then saw more red/orange lights that looked like chinese lanterns,or stars, but bigger. They were moving slowly almost like hovering. They were ascending on a Northeast direction.They were all formed in a geometrical shape like a triangle. It lasted about 4 to 5 minutes and then they disappeared. My husband saw the same thing from the balcony.
