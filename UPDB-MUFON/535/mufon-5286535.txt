Unknown object that looked like a telephone pole.

On Thursday February 25, 2021 at 3:39 pm I was driving South on Willard Ave near Garfield St in Newington, CT when I saw what looked like a telephone pole flying West to East in a straight line. As I was watching it, it briefly glowed brightly in the mid section from the sun's reflection and faded back to the dark gray / telephone pole appearance before disappearing. The surface looked like a mirror or a highly polished chrome surface when it reflected the sun.
Sighting only lasted 15 to 20 seconds.
