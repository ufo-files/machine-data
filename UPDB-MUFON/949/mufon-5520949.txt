White Star Light Over Ripon, WI

Was going out at around 5:36am to start my car before work and looked up into the sky to see stars as well as a much brighter object moving slowly directly above me. The object looked about 5 times bigger than a star and was moving east. I walked along my house as it moved trying to look for any flashing lights, etc. and concluded there where no flashing lights of any kind only a bright star like light. The object did seem to wobble from side to side as it moved instead of a straight point to point movement. I felt in awe because of military experience I have seen many planes, helicopters, and this was definitely none of them. There was absolutely no sound of any kind. The total experience lasted a total of 5 minutes as it kept moving till it was completely out of sight. The object did move slowly as a plane would move but was to low and to bright to be a plane considering there was no noise or flashing lights. Did not grab a camera since I would not have had enough time to put the battery in and start shooting video before it was gone, though I did have a cellphone I didn't not take a picture since the quality when have been beyond poor, I did however note the time.
