{nd} Object traveled @ slow speed, made u-turn into cloud cover, lost visual, no sound, w/in few minutes military fighter headed towards object direction

I was in my backyard, with my dogs for their potty break,and checking the sky for possible signs of rain. I saw an object that caught my eye. At first glance, I figured it was a commercial airplane. I noticed it was traveling at a slow speed and there was no sound, and no apparent appendages (wings ect.)It was silver/grey in color. There was no light relection from the sun making it glow or shine. It was a typical disc-like shaped object, flat and long. No other aircraft were visible from my vantage point when I first spotted the anomoly. Within a few minutes, a commercial plane did fly over, heading north making the usual noise one hears with all aircraft and I could distinguish that it was a Southwest Airlines plane,the colors, wings and tail were very visible. The other object then made a U-turn. The disc like object was traveling north in the western sky at a slower speed than the commercial aircraft and the u-turn was not abrupt, just a semi-sharp rounded turn, now heading in a southerly direction and disappearing into a cloud bank. After the SW Airplane was out of sight, I could no longer hear the SW jet engines, but I should have heard some engine noise from whatever was still traveling in the clouds above. I tried to see if the object had emerged from the cloud cover, but was unable to re-locate it. Within the next minute or two, a military aircraft leaving a long contrail, making considerable noise despite its height was headed extremely fast in the southerly direction I last witnessed the object moving in. I have excellent eye sight, and I could make out on the military plane its wings,and small size, comparable to a fighter-type plane. 

Due to trees in my neighborhood, I lost sight of the object after it made the U-turn, even though I went to the front yard to try and get a better perspective. The sky was cloudless from my southerly vantage point in my front yard. I also quickly lost sight of the military plane when the contrail ended. The whole episode lasted approximately 5-8 minutes. I have not told anyone what I saw, inclusive of my room-mate, and my daughter, my two closest confidants.

Did anyone else in the Dallas/Metroplex area report anything similar?
