Large star sized object seen, pulsating lights, hovering, floating, then went on a path

2 star sized objects emitting pulsating red and white light sometimes green also were floating/hovering west of me at about 130am and around 40 degrees off the horizon. They kind of floated zig zag back and forth in the sky then would smoothly glide southbound. Watched this for an hour or so. Animals in the area off in the distance very active, birds chirping, coyotes howling, dogs barking, another goat or something crying noise. The night felt calm, no wind. The objects would get brighter at times with their pulsating lights. Eventually disappeared in the distance.
