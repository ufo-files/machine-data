{nd} slow moving yellow light at 90 yards height, changed course to me, shot up in flash changed to saucer shaped disc with dome then landed in gravel pit

at 4:20 pm nov. 20 2014 I was waiting out in drive at my reclusive home for wife to come home from work. Yellow light traveling west, slowly and low, then changed course to south towards me, still slow. Object came to within 50 yards of me, 75 yards high then wife's car hit gravel road to our home, light shot up at tremendous speed, flashing like a strobe, climbed to approx. 1,500 feet in less than one and 1/2 second. Went black, turned light out. Called to wife to come look at it (she did) we both spotted it. Then in the shape of saucer, the disc drifted down in a leaf like pattern, falling slowly. We watched it for about 3 minutes doing this, it was a dull silver saucer with a black dome, approx. 25 feet around. It drifted towards ground behind tree at a gravel pit behind me home. Appeared to land as it was no more than twenty feet above ground when we last saw it. We guessed from position that it landed in Willow Lake (a large pond) at the gravel pit, an area we know well. Wife and I and 2 other people went back to the pond to look. Water in pond was steaming even though the temperature was about 34 degrees and night now falling. Have drawings of event. Contact me
