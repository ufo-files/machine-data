{dr}two objects seen; one with a bright light the other with a low light

First seen by a friend at approx 0450 in Ventura County. Two objects hovering in the sky towards the east vertical to each other. Friends distance to me was approx 2 miles apart horizontally. The bottom object seemed closer and had a very bright light and the top object had a faint light. Friend then drove approx 10 miles towards the objects which did not move any closer or change their distance to each other. I stayed in a stationary position until the objects disappeared as the sun came over the horizon. Possibly hovering over LA county or farther east.
