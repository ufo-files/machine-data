{cak} round orange light

We were attending a special event at the Union Cemetery in Kansas City on Saturday night and while we were waiting for our tour to start I looked up at the moon then over at Mars. I said to my two friends standing there with me that Mars looked really orange. Then aa we were looking at Mars a huge round light blinked on and in 4 seconds it went out. Just as if someone turned on and off a light switch. There was no way to determine the distance of this light but it was way out in space. It was right next to Mars and it had to have been very large. I was glad my friends saw it because they do not believe in UFO's or aliens. Now they are not sure what they saw. There was about 15 other people standing around so if they looked up when we did, they would have seen the same thing. I am hoping someone from Mufon might have seen that light so they can offer some suggestions as to what it might have been. It was strange. I couldn't stop thinking about it all through the tour that night. This happened so fast and unexpectedly that there was no way to get a picture or video of it. I hope someone somewhere did because I would like to know what we saw.
