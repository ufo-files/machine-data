{dr}Swaying Orb Moving South-East in Midvale, Utah

At about 7:30 tonight, I was walking up the outdoor stairs to my front door, and I looked East and saw a sphere with a small line on the top over the 4-story apartment building that's on the property next door. The sphere was navy blue, and the line was white. The white line had a mark sort of like an hourglass.

The object was swaying back and forth, and it was moving in a straight line toward the South-East. It looked like it was slowing down and speeding up.

At first glance I thought it was a plane, but after a few seconds, I saw that it was much closer and moving much differently than an airplane. When I realized that it wasn't an airplane, I was frightened and I came into the house. It was about 20 seconds between the time I first saw the object and the time I went into the house.
