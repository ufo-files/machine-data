{jpg} Observed a lights in the sky, no sound, flying separately then 4 in formation like a diamond then more in single file behind. We have a video of this.

We were in Jersey City NJ when we observed a light in the sky. As the light passed over us, we started to notice more flying above us. They were flying from southwest toward northeast, toward the downtown area. There were sixteen all together, but in the middle there were four in formation. Several flew toward the east while the remaining after the formation ascended into the sky and out of sight. They were not moving as fast as a helicopter because a helicopter had passed during and after the sighting. They also made no noise. It is very unusual for helicopters to be out at that time because the weather was rainy and they do not usually fly at night in this area.
