(GS) Balls of light

Talking on the phone while sitting in my car in my driveway, I notice a light in the south sky and just assumed it was an airplane, a rather bright one. Not paying much mind and several minutes later I noticed that the airplane was still there in the same spot. Not taking my eyes off of it I scrambled around behind my seat with my free hand to get my camera. With my phone wedged between my shoulder and my ear I started video recording the light and this video is what unfolded.
I did not want to miss a second of footage, but yet it seems as though there is some missing time as the sky goes from daylight to dark. I do remember the light coming very close to me and getting extremely large and being distracted by a bunny behind me. The light getting close and large is when I stopped recording and being distracted by the bunny is when I started recording again. The time in between is when it went from daylight to dark.
