{cak} Multiple orbs and two strobing discs in one night

We were driving home at about 6:30 pm Jan 6th 2016. Got to our street and first spotted one large yellow orb over Lake St. Clair flying west towards us. Pulled over to see what it was doing, but then it made an abrupt turn towards the south and disappeared. Turned around and noticed two more large wide glowing (almost like yellow flames) in the NNW hovering and then dropping down slowly to just above the tree line, seemed about a mile away. Getting closer they appeared saucer shaped (wider than tall but with no discernible dome) and were strobeing red & white horizontally. They stayed fairly low and started slowly moving southwest (crossing the street we were driving on [this is when we got the best view]) but we couldn't catch up to them. They were getting smaller and farther away towards the SW. Two more orbs appeared in the original NNW direction but were faint and star like. Decided to turn back because we were tired and we saw a flashing orb (it almost looked like it was rotating) approaching from the south sitting at a traffic light. This is when i told my passenger to please take my phone and get a picture, but she didn't know how to zoom in. I poked my head over to watch it. It was an icy blue metallic color and round and it passed almost directly overhead. It flashed a few times and was very eerie looking and then my passenger saw it momentarily zip extremely fast across to another point in the sky. We couldn't track it anymore as it had disappeared out of our view from the car once we started moving. We saw two more approaching white orbs on the way back in the east over the lake. I only have the one picture of the blue things approach, and it really looks nothing like what we saw with our eyes. It's the little dot way up in the sky directly over the bus stop in the pic. Extremely disappointing that this is all we could get but i'm going to submit it anyway.
