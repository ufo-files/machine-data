{cak} 2 objects one smaller than the other

Camping east of Saint Johns, AZ on private land less than a mile from the NM border. We had been looking at the ISS, Venus, Mars, and shooting stars. When one of the campers said what is that and pointed to the SW sky. I observed two objects a few miles off, one being about 5x larger than Mars and one about the size of Venus. Both had a constant white light under them with no flashing lights the larger appeared to have a small light on top of it . Climbing at about a 45 degree angle 2-3x faster than the ISS crossing the sky. Both heading north with about a 10 degree difference in direction from one another. I told the other campers they just saw some serious government classified stuff right there. As they climbed out of our atmosphere til they became as small as the stars and faded out. 5 seconds after I lost sight one of the craft became visible again and changed from a northern direction to a eastern direction. 20 seconds after the incident I pointed out all of the planes (about 5) I could see just to show them that's not what they saw. They all agreed it wasn't a plane. As well as pointed out that Mars was the largest thing in the sky other than the moon and what we saw started out larger than Mars. I also pointed out it was a lot faster than the ISS we had seen a few time over the weekend.
