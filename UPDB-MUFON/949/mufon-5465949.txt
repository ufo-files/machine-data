{cak} Light in the sky, then missing time.

I was recently divorced and started drinking heavily. The alcohol makes me question everything, but this particular event was different than drunkenness. Went through rehab and am sober now. I started seeing lights in the woods I was living near. I started seeing figures that were made of light. I started hearing voices psychically. The dreams became profoundly amazing and sometimes disturbing. One night I was in the woods in my car listening to music out alone as I did many times. I got sleepy around 11pm and drove my car back up to the house. I remember turning off the car and then got a creepy feeling, like I was being watched. It literally felt like 3 seconds. The next thing I can remember I am trying to put my key back into the ignition, but it will not fit. I felt sick and extreemly confused. I looked up to see the sun shining and the birds were singing. I was no longer in my own car. I was sitting in the neighbor's car about 100 yards away. The door to my car was still open. I am missing around 8 hours of memory! I got very confused the next day because it was Thursday and I could not remember Wednesday. I concluded that I could not have been gone for 32 hours, because the family would have noticed the car door standing open. I am considering hypnosis just to see if it was an alcohol induced blackout, or something more. I am an alcoholic, so I know what blackout feels like. This was different. Also woke up with a red sore lump on the back of my hand. And advice would be appreciated. TY
