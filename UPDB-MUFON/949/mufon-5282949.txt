Large bright stationary light.

I was headed home on i-65 north as I passed gardendale I noticed a bright object low in the sky that never moved or changed directions. I have been living here all my life never seen it before and haven't seen it since. The same night James Spann the local meteorologist had alot of reports of people seeing a "fireball", so I know there was other witnesses and many of the comments online matched what I seen and it wasn't a fireball, the object I seen was not moving so I believe there was two different things going on that night.
