{dr}Observed a fireball slowly descending until it exploded about 25 feet above the ground/river

I was outside around 9:15 pm last night trying to film in the dark when my dogs started barking and alerted me to what appeared to be a rather slowly descending fireball. It was at a distance of about 1 mile from where I was standing and descended into the beginning of the Gunnison Gorge area in the Western Slope region of Colorado. When it got to about 25 feet above the river it exploded, sending out 2 or 3 streaks of glitter which also then exploded. On the video you can see the light descending above the 5 stationary street lights from the electric companys service building. Then the 1st flash is visible to the right of those lights, followed by 2 more flashes. Thought the explosions were rather odd for a fireball.
Unfortunately Im recording with my $59 camera from Newegg so its not very clear since it was quite dark last night but the skies were very clear (as usual) and starry. You can see the light descending at an angle above the 5 street lights. It vanishes to the right of those lights, then theres a bright flash, followed by 2 more flashes.
The region it fell into is the upper end of the Black Canyon on the Gunnison River and is river/desert surrounded on both sides by steep cliffs. It lit-up the area as it descended, but not enough for the camera to pick it up.
