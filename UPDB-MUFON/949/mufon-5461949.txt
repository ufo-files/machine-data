{cak} 7 different objects in the sky.

I was taking out garbage when I saw 3 brightly colored yellow lights in the sky moving together from the southeast. For whatever reason I ran inside to get a phone and I began recording. I was stunned and nervous which is why the footage is vertical. After awhile I lost track of the 3rd one and only focused on two of them in the sky. These are the one's located in the photograph at the very top. The 2nd to the top at one point was bright red, it looked like a fireball in the sky. I only took these 3 very short videos as I felt that I looked ridiculous to others as there were literally people walking their dogs going by. I figured this was just something military and was content with that logical conclusion that I came to in my head. It wasn't till I showed people the videos in an attempt to make fun of myself for thinking I had actually caught something legitimate that they pointed several different anomalies that occured in the footage. I did not see these things while taking the video or even afterwards when I looked at the footage and photograph briefly. In the photo once I blew it up onto my computer there's a total of 6 objects in the sky two of which were apparently very close to me that look metallic and round in shape, I did not observe these while taking the photo nor any of the other objects besides the two at the very top. I also did not see the movement of one of these objects which this would be the 7th that zooms in and out coming next to one of these objects at around 10-12 seconds in the video.
