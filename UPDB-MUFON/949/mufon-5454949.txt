First like a white balloon and after like a plane without wings!

I was having my morning coffee after starting my working today when I listened to a helicopter passing by around 50 m high over our University (Universidade do Vale do Itajaí). After spotting the helicopter, I noticed something like a helium balloon around 100 m above the helicopter. First I thought it was too big for a balloon after it appeared like a parachute so I thought it was part of a weather balloon. Suddenly it moved and I saw it had a white color and looked like a zeppelin or a plane without wings. No sound!!!! The sky was totally blue without any clouds! When I looked sideways searching for another witness it made a curve heading to the shoe and in an eye blink, it accelerated and disappeared. I pointed my smartphone to it but I could only get the helicopter in the frame, I could look at it but it didn't show up in the phone!!! Very strange and unusual, it made me think why people are confused about reporting a UFO since it could be described as tic-tac, cigar-shaped, zeppelin, missile, a plane without wings but no one of these can really describe what I saw. I always thought I would see something glowing in the night sky or a metallic disc in the air. Today helicopter traffic over the city has been also unusual, after the event I saw 4 helicopters passing by, 1 was grey from the navy!
Dr. Rick Schwarz
