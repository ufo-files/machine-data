{nd} 2 bright Red objects peculiar movement low in sky on clear night

From back deck on clear night observed 2 bright red objects treetop height about 3 finger widths apart. The object in the right stayed stationary while the object on the left began horizontally moving away from the one on the right. Approximately 5 minutes later right one began to follow same path as the other but at much higher speed. Lost sight behind trees or disappeared.
