hovering lights in the sky

We went out of the lodge to look at the stars and one of our team noticed a weird light. It was hovering in the distance and seemed to be flashing white, green and red. It was moving, small zigzags, squares shapes and quickly desending and ascending. The light flashed randomly red and green and illuminating the nearby wispy clouds. It was in the cloud line. We watched it appear to have a thin flash down to earth and one of the party said they saw the ground way beneath light up. Then after 20 mins of so we scanned the rest of the sky and were amazed to see a second one, also flashing red and green and white and behaving similarly. Both lights got softer and dimmer as the time went on. After about 60 mins we went inside, then popped out at 12:30 and one was still there and bright again. We tried to take videos with our phones but they werent picking it up at all.
