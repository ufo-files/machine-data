I first contacted Mr. Joe Palermo by e-mail about this two days ago to ask him if he had ever came any information about this object. He returned my e-mail back and suggested me to make this report and this is it.

I woke up before sun rise on or about Oct 26, 2016 I opened my back door and looked out, then I looked up in the sky and could not believe what I was seeing. I payed attention to it because it was slowley flapping it's manta shapped wings up and down. There were no helecopters or planes in the sky, and there was no sound that this object made. It was comming from the NW heading SE. 

It moved like a manta ray in the sea and was somewhat shaped like one. The head and back end were ovel shaped and with the wings flapping up and down, it reminded me of one swiming through air. I noticed it because it was the color of a thin white cloud in a sea darkish blue in color. Shortly the sun rose slowly and when the first of the day hit on it's right side I could the object lit with sun light that seemed to be passing through it.  

I got the impression that this object was alive, and there was no time to call my neighbor or get my binockulars. When I first saw this object, I thought of how lucky I was to be up at that time to see it. As it passed over the house, I went to the front to get a longer look but because of the big tree in the neighbors yard front yard I didn't see it again..
