{cak} Huge fire like lights in a line.

I heard a rumbling that sounded like a helicopter while inside my kitchen. I went out on the back porch to see if it was a helicopter and how close? When standing on the back porch I realized the noise was from a military jet. I decided to watch for a little while. I saw 3 or 4 airplanes in different parts of the sky looking to the East. All of the sudden I saw one huge light come on and then another then the third and they were spread across in a line at a 45 degree angle. They were a fire color and after the 3 lit up there was a blue light that came one for the last 2 or 3 seconds then they all went out. Each light must have been half a mile apart. I think they were 10 miles away or more. I think these 4 lights were on the same craft. All around I saw some smaller star like lights that looked like stars that appeared about 4 seconds after and vanished in a sequence. There were 6 or 7 of the small ones that I saw.
