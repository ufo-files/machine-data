{jpg} three craft flying in formation one behind the other .. 2 lead craft where roughly saucer shaped blurry dark in color dark grey or black . the trailing craft was cube shaped same coloration. i would guess they about 20 feet apart.

We were making plans out side of work to go fishing.. when my friend pointed to the sky and said what is that.... I turned thinking i would see a hawk or something.. but instead we watched 3 craft fly very slowly over the building .. like i said the lead craft were a dark grey color they seemed to have a blurry quality to them.. The craft trailing them was cube shape craft also of a very dark color black or grey.. I thought ufo of some kind my buddy thought perhaps a box kite .. The flight pattern was direct straight line and did make sense cause of the slow speed they traveled.. my feelings where amazed almost speechless. These craft never broke formation and we lost them over wooded area on the oposite side of the road.. i would guess they were they were car size roughly and altitude no more than a few hundred feet.
