Single bright light about 1000 to 1500ft from the ground and no sound.

I was sitting outside having my morning coffee as usual. As it was getting light the only things you could see in the sky were the few remaining brightest stars and a quarter moon to the east and Jupiter to the southwest. By this time it is 5:30 in the morning. As I was sitting facing west I saw a bright light coming toward me over the roof tops and through the trees that I see to my west. To my direct west, on the northern side of the Port of Stockton, is I5 and the main channel to the Port of Stockton. I stood up to get a better view before it cleared the trees. It was a steady single bright light and there was NO sound. It was approximately 1000 to 1500 ft straight up. I truly expected it to be a plane but, with no sound I don't believe it was a plane. It also did not appear to have wings and it definitely did not have wing lights. No red or green only the single bright light. When I viewed it through the binoculars the light appeared to be on the bottom glowing forward. The light was so bright I could not make out the shape and when I started viewing with the binoculars it increased in speed and altitude. I see a lot of things at night but I never expected to see anything this early in the morning.
