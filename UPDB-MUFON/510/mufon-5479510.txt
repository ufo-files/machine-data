{rjl} Saw something plane-like yet it seemed to have cylinders attached and going like a high-flying jet and then could not be seen again.

I was at the corner of Lillian and 61st St. in Pensacola and noticed in the clear blue sky in the NW a plane-like craft with no jet trail following it for about 10 seconds and then it seemed to just vanish. I did not have my binoculars or camera with me. I looked again to see if I could see it again as it was moving from west to east, thinking the sunlight was making it extra visible as I have seen before, but to no avail. I tried to get the one I was with if they saw it and could not see it. It was moving like a high-flying jet but no obvious trail with it and then no more. Was there any man-made craft flying at that time and direction? That's the first unidentified flying object I seen in the daytime in a long time and that's coming from a seasoned sky-gazer for years!
