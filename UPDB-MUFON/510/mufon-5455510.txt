{tjd} Large Pulsating Hot Red Orange Round Hovering Sphere...

My husband, my mother and I were sitting on the deck of an ocean front condo, fourth floor, at about 9:00 PM at night. We were talking when my husband initially saw the UFO come into his line of sight. He says that it moved from the right to the left coming from behind the line of sight of the building and it stopped.... began pulsating. He expected for it to explain itself, in other words, he thought it would move in such a fashion that it would identify itself as a normal aircraft or a flare or a Chinese lantern but it did NOT. It remained where it was and at that point my husband drew our attention to what he was seeing. I was instantly taken aback. My observation was that it was HUGE. There was a full moon or close to a full moon that night. Maybe the harvest moon. I am not sure about that but I'm sure that can be researched. All night we were able to observe airplanes coming in to what I think would be to land at the Myrtle Beach airport. What I observed was huge in comparison to the airplanes. It looked HOT...red hot...pulsating. The red color on the exterior went to more of an orange color and then a dark colored center. Darker than the hot red/orange pulsating color. The moon was illuminating the clouds above the ocean. The UFO was just to the right side of a cloud that could only be seen at that time of night because of the moon or I can't help but question if the UFO was illuminating the cloud as well. Personally, my observation of the UFO was maybe a minute and then it just disappeared. No sound...no trail...nothing. My husband's observation was for a longer period of time and his description was it moved horizontally right to left, stopped began pulsating.
