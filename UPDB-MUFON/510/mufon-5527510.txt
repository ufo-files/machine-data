3 RED LIGHTS THAT LOOKED LIKE TRIANGLE HOVERING THEN SHOT OFF VERY QUICK

My wife and I was driving down hardy road when I glanced up and seen 3 red lights that was too close to eachother to be 3 planes with the red indicator lights. Im a recreational pilot myself and would know if that was the indicator lights people talk about. This was a ufo not a doubt in my mind. My wife and I have took pictures of the event on our cell phones and got really good pictures of it. As soon as I got my recorder out it blasted off way to quick to be a normal plane even a fighter jet. This was an amazing site. It didn't even leave a flare when it took off. THIS WAS GREAT TO SEE. IM GLAD I GOT TO WITNESS THE EVENT.
