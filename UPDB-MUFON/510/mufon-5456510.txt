{cak} Three bright lights that appeared independently at amazing speed, hovered and then left at amazing speed again

I was fishing late night for walleyes and trolling from west to east. Very calm clear night...stars were out and the occasional blinking lights from a passing plane. What looked like a falling star in the east sky, falling that fast, all of a sudden stopped and remained stationary in the east sky. It caught my eye right away as it was bigger and brighter and came in so fast...and stopped. I watched it for maybe 15-30 seconds when another one came in and stopped above and to the right of the first one. Then another one came in and stopped above and to the left. Now it had my complete attention. The three lights hovered there for about a minute or so...and then the first light zipped to the north and disappeared at an amazing speed...like pointing your finger straight out and then quickly pointing to the left...it moved that fast! There’s nothing on this planet that would come even close to that speed! I then looked back at the other two lights...the second light then zipped to the north following the first light. Then the third light zipped to the north also. All three lights were gone. The lights never blinked or where colored like a plane...they also did not move even remotely close to how slow our modern aircraft fly. There is no doubt in my mind that these were from somewhere else. They look and act a lot like what I recently read about regarding the lights over England in the 1950s.
