{cak} E2 of 3: Apr1979, silver, cigar shaped airplane like object without wings, traveling slowly to the west.

EVENT 2 of 3 by Karen Donaldson, Apr1979 - Witness (A) and her 12 year old daughter were driving east along M88 toward Central Lake, MI at about 2pm.  To the north on the driver1s side of the vehicle, witness (A) observed a reflective, cigar shaped, silver object like a jet without wings just above a barn at about a 25 degree inclination angle. She stopped the car and observed the object traveling to the west at a very slow pace. The observation lasted for about 5 minutes when the object suddenly disappeared. It was a clear sunny afternoon.
