{dr}sighting of four unknown glowing objects on new years eve

Driving home from a sober dance on new years eve I saw in the sky four objects that were basically yellow/orangelights. One light was alone to the left of three others. As I looked back and forth from my car window the three objects got together in a straight line. I reached my house and ran in to get the dog to take out . It took me no more than a couple of minutes but when I looked up in the sky it was clouded over with rows of rippled clouds? I had never before seen anything like this and it seemed very suspicious that the clouds appeared all of a sudden. There was no wind to cause such 
a fast cover.
