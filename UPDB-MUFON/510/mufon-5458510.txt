{tjd} Observed no less than 15 unknown satellites at one time

After putting away my yard tools I decided to sit out on the patio and enjoy a warm summer evening listening to the radio and looking at the stars. This is something that I do quite regularly in all seasons. As soon as I sat down and looked up I saw what I thought was a passing satellite at least a magnitude of -2 maybe brighter. Then I saw another and another and realized there were no less than 15 of these objects. I was facing east, it was cloudy to the north, but clear to the south where as far as I could see these objects were passing over. They were spaced well apart. I can not say if they were evenly apart or in formation but all appeared to be moving at the same speed.
 I live just ten miles south of Detroit so there is much light pollution. I tried to get a video and photo but my phone isn't good enough to get anything clear. I did however open my Heavens Above app and took a screen shot that showed seven unknown satellites on the screen.
 I ran inside to get my wife and daughter who came out and also witnessed about ten more still passing over.
