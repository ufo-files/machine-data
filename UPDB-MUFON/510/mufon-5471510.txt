observed ufo while flying in circuit for landing yvr. paralleled my track and altitude on downwind slightly forward and low of my left wing tip. observed by myself and instructor.

At the time of this incident I was flying in a landing pattern at YVR at night. I noticed this object off the left wing from the red nav light reflection and saw it was blocking city light below in my vision. It paralleled my speed and track for approximately 10-15 seconds. My aircraft was the only one in the landing pattern at that time and I had no idea what it could have been. This lasted for a duration as mentioned already, and this object disappeared as I turned to land. All previous and later touch and go landings were uneventful. The weather was clear and I was flying a RH circuit on runway 08 at 1000 ft.   My instructor and I both acknowledged this event as it happened and did look at each other in disbelief. It was shrugged off and we never spoke of it again. It bothered me because we were supposed to be all alone in the circuit at that time. That event has stuck with me for many years and I felt talking about it now may shed more light on what that was flying beside me.
