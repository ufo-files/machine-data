Elongated Triangle shape w/4-5 exterior lights, Black/darkened. Silent

Came home about 7:45 pm, 10/29/21, and it is/was a very clear sky. After walking out of the garage I was wondering if I could see the Aurora as we are supposed to maybe be able to.

Instead, I saw, going from NNE to SSW a black/darkened elongated triangle shape with 4-5 small lights on it. It was definitely solid as there are a lot of stars out and it was blocking them as it traveled. Not a bit of noise and it was going quite rapidly if it was close and I don't know if it was higher as I could not tell the actual size. It did *seem* to be good sized.
I watched this thing for about 10 or so seconds and not once did it slow or speed up. It did NOT seem like anything I have ever watched in the sky before. I was freaking out, saying, "Oh my god!" over and over even as is disappeared in the distance.
