Dark stationary object in western sky

The object was dark, appeared to be shaped like the end of a sharpened pencil, and appeared motionless. I was driving west when object was spotted at about a 65 degree angle, appeared to be somewhat distant, and remained in same position as I drove west toward it for approximately 15 minutes. I pulled over near Ralston, Iowa when darkness approached and took 10 consecutive photos using a Canon 80 D with a 100-400mm lens at ISO 800, f/5.6, 1/90 sec. I watched until it was too dark to observe it any longer. I returned to the same location the following day around noon to look for any structure or tower but found nothing that could have been mistaken for the object. The two photos attached include a color shot entirely uncropped or edited in any way, and a cropped shot in black and white to help with seeing the shape more clearly.
