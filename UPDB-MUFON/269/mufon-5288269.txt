UFO sighted directly next to lightening bolt

The object was not sighted in real time- there was a thunderstorm with frequent lightening happening that evening and the witnesses were taking a video of the storm. It was only later that an object was spotted directly beside a bolt of lightening. It exists for only one frame. This is why there is no info on the direction or motion of the object. The object appears to be between the witnesses and the lightening bolt, and appears to be reflecting the light from the bolt.
