UFO in picture when shuttle landing

Taking pictures of the shuttle landing at KSC as the shuttle came in from the south about to land. The shuttle is just seconds from landing and about to drop the landing gear. Picture taken from launch complex 39 looking west as the shuttle glided in. One of the pictures had an object that appears to be above and just west of the shuttle. The object was not seen while viewing the shuttle, only when examining the picture. The object only appeared in one of the photos taken. We did not see any chase aircraft.
