{nd} Solid but slightly pulsating orange yellow glow; teardrop shape; no sound; constant speed; varying directions

Approaching stop sign and noticed what I first thought was a Chinese lantern above the church. Watched it with my daughter and realized it did not flicker, did not make a sound, was not erratic in trajectory. Color was glowing slightly. Could not see any other lights or solid parts of the craft. Teardrop shaped and it moved slowly but steadily from NE to SW. I tried to take a video but by the time I got my phone ready, it was near a streetlight. I was not frightened but I felt very unsettled after. Lost sight of the object when we turned the car around to try to follow it (it was moving the opposite direction from where we were going).
