{cak} White oval with metallic sheen, shadowed underneath, size of a mini-van.

My son Keelan was driving, I sat in passenger seat.We stopped at a red light at the intersection, us facing W, me on passenger side glancing N. I noticed objects in sky, drew his attention. He glanced but was paying attention to traffic light. 

Perhaps golf-ball held at arm's length would cover one of them. Did not test this, it's estimated. They were fairly close.

Silent, white ovals moving W to E, smoothly. Rear one gained altitude and sped to close gap, then matched leader's speed to fly in tandem, one behind the other. 200 to 300 feet above ground, speed under 100 mph, (estimated), No exhaust, wings, or propellors. 

I reported this to MUFON at the time, but waived off follow-up interview. On the evening of Thursday 6 June 2019, after a presentation at Valley NE Public Library, my son Keelan showed this image to Dr. Jack Casher, who suggested I show it to Dave Pares at MUFON. Hence this report. Dr. Casher's essential comment was, "Wow."
