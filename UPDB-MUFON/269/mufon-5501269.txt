{nd} Three Yellow Lights in perfect triangle pattern appeared stationary for about 3 min. then just appeared to fade then disappeared.

On July 5th at approx. 10:30pm I was in Southfield MI traveling in my auto on Northbound Lahser Rd. between 11 mile Rd. and 12 mile rd. where I witness three bright yellow lights through my front windshield at approx. 60 Deg. from horizon. These three lights were in a perfect triangle formation, which was very odd to me. I slowed down looking for a spot to pull over to observe. These lights didn't appear to be moving or extremely slow. As I was pulling off road the lights just appeared to fade out till they disappeared. I got out of auto and couldn't find them again.
