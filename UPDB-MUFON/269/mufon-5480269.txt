{cak} Metallic sphere hovers then shoots out of the atmosphere

I was driving through a neighborhood on my way to the pharmacy. It was a clear sky with few clouds and a brilliant sunset. I saw a sparkle in front of me and slightly to the right at about 60 degrees above the horizon. I first thought it was a plane catching the sunlight, but I then realized that there were no wings! The object was actually sphere-like and it was hovering. I slowed down to pull over so I could get my smart phone out to take a photo. As soon as I stopped the car, the object took off straight up and out of the atmosphere! It left no contrail. It had just vanished. As I drove a little further toward my destination, I noticed a small twin engine plane off to my left but that is a usual sight in this area. This metallic sphere reminded me of the object my wife and I witnessed on our way home from work earlier this year on March 1, 2016, as I reported to MUFON in case# 74951.
