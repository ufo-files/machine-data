Orange to Red ball of light moving above North east Lincoln NE

I went out side in my back yard to smoke and was looking up at the stars when I seen this bright red and orange light moving fairly fast from east to west. At first I thought it was a satielite but then once I figured out the direction it was moving I realized I never seen one move from east to west, there was also upper level clouds out that night and the light was below the cloud level. I would guess speed around 500 to 600 mph. There was no sound coming from the light at all. The last I seen the object was still traveing west before I lost sight over it over my house.
