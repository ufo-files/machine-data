(gs) 3 people witnessed bright orange orb-like object moving above then dipping into tree line

its was 7/6/2012 at apx. 2300 hrs. when myself and 2 others were sitting on our front porch hanging out and talking when i noticed out of the corner of my eye a orange light. i immediately stood up, pointed and exclaimed "what the f**k is that!?" the other two turned and saw the object, we all ran to the furthest part of the porch to get a better view and observed the object clearly for at least 30 seconds before it moved into the tree line out of our view. at first we had no idea what it could be, it was hovering over an area further away and appeared to as is it were the moon hanging over the apt. bldg. across the street. the object was large, orb like, and bright red-orange, and looked as if it had a flame-like pulsing, flickering light being emitted from its center. it hovered for a few seconds before heading what appeared to be east then a quick directional change heading north. the object looked as if it were moving through water as its altitude was ever changing in a wave like motion. after about 45 seconds the object disappeared completely from sight, the buildings and tree line blocking the view. for the following 45-60 mins we discussed what it possibly could be coming to the conclusion that it was unexplainable.
