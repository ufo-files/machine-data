Bright orange/yellow starlike object moving high across the sky

As we were standing in our front yard we witnessed a bright orangish object that moved high across the sky from East to West. While the object appeared as if it was almost on fire, there were no visible flames. There was no noise and it was definitely not an airplane or a flare. The whole event lasted roughly a minute and a half until the object faded out before reaching the horizon. The movement was constant, with no change in direction.
