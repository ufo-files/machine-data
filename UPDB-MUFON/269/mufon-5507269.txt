{cak} Bright as venus

Me and my cousin walk at night and watch the stars just to see shooting stars and how bright the sky is. At 7:45 pm we looked to the east and their was a light as bright a venus. The light was moving at least 10x or more the speed of any aircraft in the sky. We watched it go from east to north east very fast change direction once and go back the same direction. The object was not a plane of any kind and it was not a meteor it had no tail and it was very bright.
