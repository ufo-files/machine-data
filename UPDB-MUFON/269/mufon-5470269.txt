{cak} red-eye flight from LA to KC, pulsating lights one end blue to other end red, hovered at least while over pacific

Mother and I were on a red eye flight from LA to KC. Plane took off, cabin lights out, we had our window shade open. We saw a huge cigar shaped ship lingering by the airplane. We discussed it in whispers, wondered what to do. It had pulsating lights along the bottom changing colors from one end to the other red to blue. We were afraid, no, petrified. I remember praying and crying. We held each other's hands and oddly fell asleep.
