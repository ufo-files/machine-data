Ufo spotted when I was taking pictures of my boyfriend and it appeared out of no where above his head

My boyfriend and I were walking through Niagara Falls State Park and I wanted to take a cool picture of him so I had him stand near the water and I took multiple pictures. I didn’t realize the ufo until after I took the pictures but in each picture you see it was not there then all of a sudden you see if creeping from behind the clouds. If you pay attention to above his head you see it move and get closer to the ground. One of the craziest experiences of my life we were so excited and freaking out.
