RED ORB IN CLOUDS

Was driving home along I5- from Paradise CA... During storm... Looked up in sky and was noticing a bright orb in the clouds. It was during the supermoon. It was basically motionless. I captured it on video and it seemed to drop a sparkly object into the clouds.
