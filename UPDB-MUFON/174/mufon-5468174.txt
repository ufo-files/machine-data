{td} Saw what we thought was Satellite. Flashed my flash light at it. 3 seconds later we were blasted with a beam.

Hard to really come to grips with this. Myself (male 40), my neighbor (male 34) and neghbors two kids male 12 and female 7. At approximately 1020 in Cedaredge Colorado, we were outside looking at the stars and planets. Neighbor suggested that you could see satellites and the ISs zip by if you were lucky. Not a few minute after, and we spot a "Satellite" (could have been anything. It wasn't flashing , moved along at a good clip. And was fairly bright.) In jest, I pointed my flashlight at the "satellite" as it headed towards the horizon. 
Not three seconds after I shined my flashlight at the light in the sky, what seemed to be a flash from a camera came from the same direction and lit up all of us and our immediate surroundings. 
 It made that children uneasy and scared.  And myself and my adult friend were just stunned. 

Occam's razor says that there was meteor 3 seconds after I flashed the flashlight. Can shooting stars flash at you? Does that question even make sense. Seriously, what the hell just happened to us. We are in some sort of a mental shock/ haze??
