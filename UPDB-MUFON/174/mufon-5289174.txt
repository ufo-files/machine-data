My name is [name removed], I now live in Florida, I was borne on 25 June 1941, so I’m 78 years old.
I grew up in Los Angeles California, in the Echo Park area. I don’t exactly remember the time frame,
but I think it was between 1950 and 1955 that my best friend and I observed a real UFO about 200 feet away from us for about ten minutes. 
I can still visualize that experience like it was yesterday. My friend lived in a house that was near a paved road that was elevated and both sides of the road were empty steep dry grass that we would slide down on cardboard boxes like a toboggan. 
We were climbing back up to slide down again and we seen this large silver, shiny, smooth; with a nice rounded nose; looked like it was about five foot above the road surface; no sound; moving very slow and as we climbed up, the entire object was exposed. It was so close that I could have hit it with a baseball! 
It had no windows, no markings, no rivets, nothing. It was cylindrical and was about the length of one railway train compartment but smaller in diameter. The back end was completely flat like it was just chopped off. It looked like one big shiny piece of stainless steel! It stopped moving (like it was
watching us) and we slid down the dry grass again and started back up again. It started to move again and we waved at it to say goodbye! From our position it made a half right turn, aimed its nose slightly upward, and then zoomed up into the sky (very clear day, no clouds) it only took about two seconds, when it turned into a speck and disappeared.
I document this in hopes that it may validated someone’s experience that may have seen it around the same time frame as I did?
