Bright light

I was out on my front porch around 2:30 in the am someking a cigerette before returning to bed and i was looking up star gazeing admireing all the stars that were visible from my front porch when i noticed that 1 of these stars was moveing in a straight line heading from west to east and it had speed to it. after i lost sight of it i came back inside and went to my back porch to see if i could pick it up again and i did. it was heading in a steady direction to the east.
