Star signals

Clear nightsky.
A small star, with no apparent reason as there were thousands like that one, attracts my attention.
As soon as I focus this small star, it starts moving, quite slowly (from my perspective) hovering with no apparentpath, roughly in a couple of circles.
As soon as I realize it is moving, the star, with a sudden acceleration, moves along a straight trajectory at incredible speed (close to comet-like..let's say half speed). However, while suddenly accelerating (I want to stress out: as soon as I am conscient it is moving, therefore weird phenomenon), it flashes.
3 times.
In 3 different points along the trajectory.
Like a signal.
And then vanishes into space.

This is the second event. In June 2021, in Italy (Baratti), I witnessed the exact same thing, but with 1 flash only.
