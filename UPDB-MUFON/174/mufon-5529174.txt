pulsating black/red aircraft flew over treeline and over street from right to left

I was walking back from 7-eleven with my friend Janel. On the way back, just past the intersection of Barton Skyway and S Lamar, an unknown, silent craft, there was a spherical object with a pulsating red glow from the interior passed us. It was directly in front of us. It came over the treeline, from right to left, it was approximately 40/50 feet in the air and was not very large. It was travelling rather quickly, so the entire episode lasted about 1 minute. I was in a state of shock as I looked over to my friend, looked back up and then looked back at my friend. I commented, "do you see that?" i'm like, "that's not a plane or helicopter". I stayed dumbfounded/in awe for about 20-30 seconds. My friend snapped me out of it and was like, "film it". I am upset with myself as I had a camera phone, but as I fumbled to get the camera/video function turned on, the craft was about 1/4 mile away. It was flying in the general direction of the airport. The craft did not seem very large, almost like some type of orb, between 5-10 feet in diameter, but i'm horrible with depth perception. All I can say it was fairly low to the ground for an aircraft and did not seem very large--but the pulsating red glow was its most salient aspect.
