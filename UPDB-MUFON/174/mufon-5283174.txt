Triangular shaped Large UFO or Many smaller orbs forming a triangle shape and flying in formation.

Witnessed a large craft of many smaller crafts flying in formation over the night sky on January 7th, 2022. Around 10pm at night in the crisp, cold clear sky was what appeared out of no where but wasn't super bright was a large triangular shaped object approx 80k ft in altitude.It's estimated at about 1 or 2 km wide. I saw for about 30 seconds or less this large illuminescent triangular shaped craft flying in a straight line going a few thousand Km/hr judging by how far and large and fast this object was moving. I screamed for my wife to get outside but by the time she got out she was looking in the wrong part of the sky and missed it, as it faded into view and faded out of view. If you didn't have your eye on it in the beginning it would have been hard to spot while fading back out of view. 

Truly shocking and amazing sight to see, I have been a ufo enthusiast and never thought I would get to see something like this with my own eyes. Still very unbelievable but I swear with my own life, I know what I saw and it was no man made aircraft. Nothing human built could be that big flying that high going that fast.
