Driving North at 4:40am 3-10-22. A long narrow object with bright colored lights at the tip was descending and firing circular shaped objects.

On 3-10-22 traveling North at 4:40am in the Turner, Oregon area near big open fields. Suddenly to my left appeared a long narrow white object with bright colors at the tip, red yellow, orange and light blue. It was descending downward, at the angle it was travelling it would've hit the ground. It was almost treetop height when it started firing white circular shaped objects very quickly, maybe 10 or more. At that point it descended behind a row of tall trees lining the road, maybe 100 yards or less long. Once I got past the trees where it was big open fields, I Saw nothing more. It was pitch black no other cars around and no other lights from anything. The flying object may have been 500 yards from the road? I heard no sounds, and saw nothing after that.
