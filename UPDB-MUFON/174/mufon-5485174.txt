Orb of light

My girlfriend and I were stargazing in an open field. At first we thought an airplane was approaching. When it got closer we heard no sound or blinking lights at all. It was going straight from west to east along the horizon and then it started zigzaging. Our jaws dropped in disbelief. As I attempted to grab my phone to record it the shape dimmed out. A few seconds later it reappeared further far away in the sky and was resuming a vertical fly this time and again it dimmed out.
