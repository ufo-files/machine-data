{rjl} I saw 16 objects single file

I was walking my dog at 5;30 a.m. I noticed two white lights going slowly from west to east. The objects were two or three times the size of a satellite. Then I saw another descending (I guess into the atmosphere as there was a very faint white haze the object looked like it descended through) and then another descended in like manner until there were 16 moving west to southeast single file. They were all moving fairly slowly and were completely silent.
