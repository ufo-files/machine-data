A glowing balloon flickering and moving upwards in.

A friend and i were standing at cottesloe beach, western australia and my friend spotted what appeared to be a glowing balloon in the sky (if i held my arm outstretched it would be as big as my thumbnail). It was flickering and moving upwards in the sky, getting smaller and smaller and then disappeared.

As we were leaving a party we assumed it actually was a balloon. But that couldn't really explain the flickering and 'disappearance'. And that was until we realised that the balloons at this party weren't helium filled either.

Very strange as we are both not really into "ufo's" as such. We both couldn't really explain it and went through the typical rational lines of thinking. Thought it may be useful here...
