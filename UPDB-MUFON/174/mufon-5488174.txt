{cak} Bright light in SE, stationary, then moved toward us, before directly above us three lights in triangle shape appear, hovered dircpectly above then moved MW, silent.

My sister and I were sitting in a swing facing south east, around dusk sun had just set, still good light. A bright light appeared SE, a jet or helicopter, it appeared stationary for several minutes then began to move toward us(my sister and myself) from about 1/2 to 1 mile away. Before it got directly above us, 3 reddish pink lights appeared in a triangle shape around horizontally the bright white light. When got almost directly above us the red lights began to gyrate or orbit randomly around the bright light staying in triangle pattern. When directly above us a green light appeared above the bright light but with the triangle of red lights. It hovered silently above us about 3 to 4 minutes, then moved silently to NW red lights orbiting randomly, all movement from beginning to end in straight line. While it happened we asked each other if we could believe what we were seeing. Wowed us. My sister does not like to talk about it in front of people. I tell it as described to many, I saw a UFO.
