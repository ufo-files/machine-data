Possible UFO in Bangkok, Thailand

My friend who is a photographer in Bangkok, Thailand took two consecutive shots of this image while trying out his Nikon D4 camera. The second shot was taken at Speed Shutter 1/125 at f13, Lens 16-35 f4G shoot 16mm. The location was a quick access from Ploenchit Expressway at 9:46am on October 16, 2013. Fast moving object from first to the second shot was in a split second because the continuous shot rate of the Nikon D4 = 10 frame per second. Therefore, shooting two frames so the frame is 1/10 of a second.
