{rjl} Bright UFO caught on Ring HD Home Security Camera

Bright, non-pulsating UFO caught live on Ring home security camera footage at approximately 9:37 PM. I ran outside into our backyard to view object after seeing it live on camera. It moved West to East and made no sound. It’s flight path doesn’t match known airline traffic here in Kansas City/Independence area.

The UFO looked similar to a bright comet traveling at high speed. It maintained a steady altitude, but appeared to move upward at a steep angle in the northeastern sky. The object moved a good distance in only a few frames of video footage. There were no blinking navigation lights or noise generated by the UFO. It was not any known aircraft that I’ve seen before. I am a former private pilot familiar with civilian and military aircraft.
