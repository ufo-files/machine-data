Bright white light slowed down then sped up.

About 10:00 at night we were looking at the stars, had observed how bright Venus was on the horizon. We saw a bright object that looked like a white light moving from north to south directly over head. Pretty sure it wasnt an aircraft because it was a consistant glowing bright white light no blinking or flashing. Moving too slow to be a meteor. It was moving in a straight line but appeared to slow down and speed up at times. We see alot of satellites and planes and this didn't look like anything we have seen before.
