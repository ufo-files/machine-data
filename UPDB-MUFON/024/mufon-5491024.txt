{rjl} Viewed 3 Orange lights passing over with no sound

It all started as I was returning home from work. It was 11:30 pm and I was coming down my street toward my house. I looked up to my left ( NE) and saw 3 Orange lights. I occasionally view the stars through my telescope and realized these being Orange and not where stars usually are !I pulled out my cell phone and started videoing. I do live close to a flight path of the airport but it's far to the east. I have also worked for 3 different airlines and know how to identify aircraft. I found this odd because of where they were. I thought they may have been Helicopters so I hurried to my driveway and got out to observe. These lights were moving slowly from the NE to NW making no sound what so ever. As I watched the first one passed behind a tree across the street then reappeared. The first one then slowly faded out, then the next faded out then the third did the same. This was a clear night with a half moon. I was amazed and ran into the house and woke my wife up to tell her what I had just seen and showed her the video. It is not a great video, it's turned sideways and you can't see the movement due to me panning back and forth, but I know what I saw and experienced and I'll never forget this encounter !
