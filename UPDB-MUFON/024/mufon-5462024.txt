{jpg} four orbs moving across Mullett Lake to my location on dock

My wife and saw four orbs at midnight on a cloudless night, four orange orbs about twenty feet in diameter floating four feet above the water moving along the shore coming in our direction. We were setting on an elevated deck overlooking the lake when we say these things coming toward us, so we hurried down to the dock to see better. They continued toward us while we were on the dock and they stopped about fifteen feet from us and stayed there for about five or six minutes, silently. I even waved at them, and said something. They did not emit any bright light, only within them were they bright. Then, three of them baked off and shot across the lake (three miles in one second) and the third stayed in place for another few seconds then returned across the lake slowly and after traveling about a mile, it shot off from sight. I tried to take a picture using a Sony camera, but it did not work, Even though the camera worked before and after the event.
