Sitting on my back porch enjoying the evening. Just a large black thing appeared in the direction I was looking (Westerly). Possibly a black, large balloon? then a blimp that became kind of "wavy;?" When I got my binoculars, I couldnt locate it; then found it very high up in the sky; moving in a straight direction, while at times rotating while appearing to stop, with "things" sticking out of object, looking somewhat like a bug with legs, then again without anything sticking out. By then it had moved in a direction (SE) disappearing behind the leaves of my large trees. I called my friend to search her skies and they saw nothing. My feelings during this was that it looked quite a bit like the "flying people/witches" Ive seen on internet reports of South America. Never seen anything like it. No sound. Very strange.
