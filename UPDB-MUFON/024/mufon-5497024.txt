{dr}Orange glowing orb that turned into chevron shaped 6 white lights then disappeared

Driving through residential area called Meadow Pointe subdivision around 8:35 PM when I saw an orange/red glowing object overhead towards the east. Made me ask "what the heck is that?". Appeared to be circular but without defined edges and was very close, maybe 500 - 600 feet away. It appeared to be about the size of a golf ball to my eyes, but given its height must have been larger than this if seen up close. Was convinced this was something unusual, so I turned my car around and went back to see it again and same object was a little further away and higher up. Took my eyes off of it because I was driving, and when I looked back up towards where last seen I saw a chevron shaped set of 6 white lights, and, I think, a red light, too. This object appeared high in the sky and was about an inch or so long in my visual field. This appeared to be higher than when I had last seen the orange globe but in the same direction of it. The lights were there, really high up, and then were gone, like they disappeared. Didn't see them move off rapidly, fade away or anything. They just disappeared. Night was cloudless but there was high humidity. Moon had large ring around it but otherwise the sky was clear. Seeing the object left me wondering what the heck it was. I really can't explain it. I was excited to be something so unusual and perplexed about what it was.
