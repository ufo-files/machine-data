{nd} flashed back when we flashed a camera

My husband and I went outside on our back deck. He flashed the camera once in the direction over the house and I was looking in the opposite direction over the trees and I saw "it" flash back. He flashed the camera again in the same direction over the house and he also saw a flash back and then it dimmed and started moving and then disappeared. Same kind of light we've seen in the past. No known visible satellites or flares in area at that time.
