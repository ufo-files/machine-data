Observed craft rise up, cleared tree line, flew off to the Ne

Briefly, I noticed something I thought was an odd shaped, half deflated Mylar balloon or a piece of plastic blowing up from the drive I had just come down. I was facing north, it was too my left in front of me. I turned to observe it and realized it was rising up on the opposite side of the tree line from me. That would have to be within a 1/2 mile due to geography. The object was triangular in shape like a > sign. I also saw the reason it looked weird and “fluttery/flickering” was that patterns of the clouds and sky were moving across its skin. It rose in a hesitating way which is why I thought it might be in the trees or possibly someone’s drone. There was no sound except starlings. I pulled out my phone to record it but hit the wrong key as I was trying to watch it. As it cleared the tree tops I could see it clearly. The grey clouds and pinkish blue sunrise images stopped moving across the screen and it became cloud white with a bit of a shine to it. It then began accelerating to the NE, at which point I had my phone recording. I didn’t think I caught it but when I reviewed the image later I saw the tiny white shape shoot between the clouds on the same NE trajectory.
