travelling sw, turned north hovered, light diminished and brightened at 10 sec intervals, headed nw

Enjoying a cooling night sky when what I thought was just another plane came into view from the southeast. I had my binoculars out and took a look and didn't see any blinking lights on the vehicle thinking that I had the wrong angle to see them. I kept my eye on the object. That's when it slowed down to a crawl and headed north before stopping. At that point it just hung in the sky dimming and then blazing with a white light in approx 10 second intervals after about 15 min of this it headed northeast and repeated this behavior until it disappeared from view. I should mention that as it traveled northeast the dimming blazing pattern continued. There were no normal flashing lights on this object at anytime that I observed it. This is not the first time that I've seen curious and questionable objects in this neck of the woods. I tried filming it but unfortunately my digital camcorder wasn't sensitive enough to capture the image.
