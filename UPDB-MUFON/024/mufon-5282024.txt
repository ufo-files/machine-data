Small sphere

During the time period, I was residing in the northeast quadrant of Amarillo, TX, and while outdoors in the rear yard area during the hours of dusk, was engaged in jumping/bouncing on our trampoline, while facing northward. While thinking about retiring back indoors with the approaching darkness, my attention was caught by the near-proximity sounds behind me, of the engine noises from a light, prop-driven aircraft that was revving and stalling far more frequently than normal, as if the pilot was attempting/executing an excessive amount of maneuverings. The commotion made me stop bouncing on the trampoline and turn around, so I could locate the aircraft and observe whatever antics were being performed. I visually located the aircraft, and was surprised as it barely cleared the top of the Levi Strauss plant to the general SW, and sort of stunned to observe that it was being "shadowed/dogged" in close proximity by a weird, somewhat small, dimly-glowing, spherical/globular object. The object maintained a steady, close distance behind the light aircraft, and mimicked its every move, appearing of a quite dim, reddish-orange, radiant glow that looked similar to diffused light through a lampshade. When closest, the object appeared to be approx. 1/4-1/3 mile southward, on a W-to-E trajectory that varied in altitude depending whether skimming over the tops of buildings, power lines, and or treetops, and appeared to be approx. 12'-15' in diameter. Observation of the scene lasted until sighting was lost towards the general SE, from view being blocked by neighboring housing row, and lasted for a duration less that 1 minute. Thinking to myself, that the scene looked like simple harassing/tormenting upon the pilot to me, I wished the pilot a silent "good luck", and promptly got myself back indoors. Sketch/map available.
