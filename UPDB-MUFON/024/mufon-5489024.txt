I was sitting on my deck at the rear of my house that faces east. I stood to adjust the deck umbrella when I noticed a bright red light moving toward my location at a low altitude from the southeast. The light reminded me of a road flare, slightly glowing. I watched it pass by at a seemingly slow speed, slowly turning toward the northeast in an arc gaining altitude, eventually fading from sight. The sighting lasted about a minute. The size seemed to me about the size of a golf ball if it was held at arm’s length. The object appeared to be a fireball or emitting flame and making no sound.
