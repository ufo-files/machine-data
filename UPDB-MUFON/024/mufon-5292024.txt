large orange orb. disappeared then reappeared and zipped across the sky

I was outside waiting for the pizza man to arrive when the large orange orb came into my sight. It was moving towards the South, but it wasn't moving fast. It was not blinking either. The pizza man arrived and I showed it to him and he thought it was strange. Then I looked back up and I watched it just disappear. I went into the house to grab my phone to call my mother and when I got back outside to the sidewalk where I was before and I watched it reappear, however it was about a mile away to the west and then it zipped across the sky in a straight line to the point it was when I first saw it when the pizza man arrived and it just sat there for about 45 seconds then I watched it disappear again. I waited for a while to see if it would reappear, but it didn't. I'm still watching the sky tonight to see if it comes back. If it does, I will report again.
