{cak} Slow moving solid greenish light moving west

Traveling North on HWY 63 between the Paris Road exit and Oakland gravel road around 8:30 pm on 2/15/2017 I noticed a star like object over the Prathersville Missouri area. The solid star like object was moving slowly or hovering. At first I thought it might be a helicopter with its search light on due to the Boone County Jail near by, that might be looking for an escapee. The light remained solid unlike a helicopter's search light that will wane due to the searching on the ground. I observed the object for 5 minutes traveling west at about 10-20 MPH in the sky on my way home. At times my view was obstructed by trees and buildings on the 5 minute ride home. Once home I walked out side to my west facing back yard to continue to observe the object with binoculars this time. The object looked like a bright white and greenish horizontal tear drop. My wife also look through the binoculars and confirmed what I was seeing. The object was still traveling slowly west at the 10-20 MPH and looked to be just north of the Columbia Ready Mix quarry area. My house sits at around 770 FT on a hill that overlooks west across Columbia Mo. I watched in the Binoculars for about 5 minutes then took some video of the object traveling west. I went back inside for 15 - 20 minutes to take care of some personal things then returned to the back yard to see if the object was still there. It was, but lower in the horizon. I stated recording again and the object disappeared.
