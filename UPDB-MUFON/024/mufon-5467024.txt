3 lights in triangular pattern observed

I had been sat in my rear garden watching planes and satellites prior to me and my son spending the night in our tent,At approximately 12 .35am,I had a last look at the stars to see if I could see any more satellites,as I put my binoculars down I suddenly saw 3 lights in a triangular pattern go from east to west,the object was silent and quite in distinct even through the binoculars as I said I had been watching planes and satellites for over an hour there were no flashing lights on the object .The object carried on in a straight line and disappeared behind a tree by the time I had got to the front of our house there was nothing visible
