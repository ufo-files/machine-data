{cak} UFO seen in Palmdale, CA.

We were driving up our hill at night and we weren't paying particular attention to the sky but saw what looked like a bright star hovering and then within 10 seconds of staring at it, it shot to the left out of sight. It was not a shooting star. I've seen plenty of shooting stars and meteor showers. This thing looked like a star but could also be mistaken for a plane. It must have been very bright. It stood there and hovered and then shot to the left out of sight. Nothing I have ever seen can do that. Me and my fiance both saw it and I'm only reporting it with the hope that someone else out there may have seen it. Also, there was a object that clearly looked like a normal commercial plane that was flying casually around the area but still somewhat far from it. Or at least from where I was looking. I really hope someone else out there saw it.
