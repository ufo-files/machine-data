{nd} Black sphere flew from South to North

My son (2 years old), daughter (4 years old), daughter (11 years old, and mother were in the back yard, as we were going to launch a model rocket in a few minutes. They yelled for me to come outside. My son had spotted the black sphere directly above the house, above the scattered cloud cover at the time. It appeared much larger than an airplane, made no noise, and seemed to flash once or twice, but it likely was the reflection of the sun.

As soon as we witnessed it, none of us though it was a plane - we see many planes daily as we are in the flight path of Midway airport.

We watched the object move from directly overhead, due North (not northwest/northeast but directly South), towards downtown Chicago, and lost track of the object after around 1 minute as there were a few trees nearby in the way. I did not have time to get my phone to take any video or stills.
