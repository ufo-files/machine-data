Flying like playing hide and seek between clouds

Driving on a route to Edinburgh, Scotland. Looking out of the front window, I observed two boomerang-shaped objects flying in an unusual way between clouds, as if they would play hide and seek. They stopped and accelerated speed in a way, which no aircraft could do, but they did it quite "elegant". I stopped my car and grabbed my camera, but there was no sign of them afterwards. The session took about 3 minutes. I was quite sure to see unusual activities at these moments.
