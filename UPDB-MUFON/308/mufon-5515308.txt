{nd} My wife and i witnessed 2 pulsating strobin orbs that changed directions for over an hour

My wife and I were sitting on the back porch while I.smoked a cigar. My wife told me to look at the star which was right in front of us to the east. I told her it wasnt a star because it was strobing and moving. I assumed it was an airplane until it ascended then descended then moved south slightly. We started to consider the fact that it might be a UFO. The object appeared to be getting smaller as if it was moving farther northeast.about 20 minutes into the sighting a bright white light about the same size shot out of it and dissapeared within roughly 2 seconds.the entire time the object flashed and strobed blue, green, red, and white. About an hour and 15 min. Into the sighting we noticed a second object to the east just above the treeline that was bright red/orange. It was very bright at first then faded as it traveled south it was also strobing but only red/orange. We lost sight of this second orb after about 3 minutes. It returned about ten minutes later in the exact spot we saw it before and did the exact same thing as before and we didn't see it again. We lost sight of the first object after it went behind trees to the northeast that are close to our house that blocked our view. My wife and I strongly believe these were ufos. My mother also witnessed the two objects but retreated inside because she was "creeped out".
