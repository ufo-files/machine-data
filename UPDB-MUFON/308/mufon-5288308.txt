Saw two flashes of light then a boomerang shaped object right over my house and back yard approx 100-150 feet up.

At approximately 9:30 on March 19 2021 my 11year old granddaughter and I were soaking in the hot tub. I witnessed two flashes of light approximately 100 feet apart one after the other. I then told her to watch the skies where I saw the lights. About one minute later she started jumping up and down and pointing right above us over our 75 foot pine tree. It was dark so it took me about 10 seconds to find it after it cleared the tree. It was approx 50 feet above the tree. Boomerang shaped. 30-40 foot wing span. 20 feet in length. Moving very slowly with no sound SW to NE. My granddaughter came in and drew a sketch immediately after. It might be important to note the evening before this occurred my fiancé, granddaughter, myself and several neighbors heard a giant BOOM in the neighborhood.
