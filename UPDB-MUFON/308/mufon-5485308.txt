{nd} Invisible aircraft temporarily uncloaking

I was driving to law school one morning, as I'm waiting at a stop light, a giant aircraft flies from the right to left of my field of vision. It was a large aircraft that seemed to be invisible at first, but for a split second became invisible, only to vanish once more. A blueish glow surrounded the whitish, see through looking air craft. It was triangular shaped. After witnessing, I turned off music and kept repeating "Oh my God." It immediately struck me as something coming out of Star Trek, it also had a metallic feel about the exterior because of the reflection created by the blue light surrounding the aircraft just before it vanished. Possible theories: 

1) Invisible aircraft became uninvisible for a fleeting moment. 
2) It was a UFO that was travelling at such high speeds it momentarily stopped and then went back to hyper-speed of some sort, to appear as if it'd disappeared. 
3) Angel. What I saw was triangular/cone shaped. Because I couldn't describe what I had seen, I had also wondered if I thought I saw an angel flying at a super high speed for a second. Often when super fast speeds occur, sonic boom occurs and trails the flying object. The object was triangular and could have had a sonic boom as well.
