{jpg} Comet Like Object Observed Near Plano, IL

This is the second time that i have observed what looks like a green shooting star coming into the atmosphere at high speed in the Aurora/Plano (Kane County) area. I saw a brief flash of green light enter the atmosphere, and then all of a sudden disappear in midair. I do have a dashcam in my vehicle but it is of low quality so I didn't bother checking it. I saw a plane or blinking light of some sort in the general area where the light stopped in midair and disappeared. This could have been a plane or a helicopter but I found it odd that this small blinking light would appear at the end of this comet like orb that I briefly saw.
