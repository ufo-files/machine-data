{rjl} Football Fans

I was standing behind the bleachers at my high school during a Friday night game with a friend. I don't remember the exact moment I saw it but just seeing this orange glowing ball hovering directly over the high school. I stood there shocked and fixed on as was she. It did not make any noise. As her and I were watching it, we were cracking jokes that they must be wondering what is going on under the lights (referring to the football game). Several people walked past and we pointed it out to them. Many just blew it off as a helicopter just in total denial of what we were seeing. My friend eventually moved on while I stayed there fixed on it. After about 10 minutes it started to move slowly in a northwesterly direction,then went into a hyperdrive and disappeared.
