{td} Saw it - spoke to it - it moved - I said hello - it blinked white. like Dr. Greer

I have had many, many encounters over the years. Currently, there is a bright light in the sky after dark. Some nights it is there - not all nights. I ask it if it is real. The light then moves side to side and/or up and down. I say hello - it flashes a white light. I speak to it to find out if it is man made or other - like Dr. Greer does in his gatherings. I have been feeling a hand on me (I always thought it was a pet). I would feel a hand rest on my arm or leg. Like Gramma would put a gentle hand on you. I invite it to come visit with me - IF - if it can come to me in a form that won't scare me. 
 When the light is not showing in the sky - I've had two abductions. Both times I was afraid, and was being hurt. 
 I know the craft is there when my dog goes to the door, and then refuses to go outside. She's the reason I looked up in the first place. 
 I live by a military base - so I assumed it was special ops. There is also a air route in the area, so planes are seen and heard. Planes make noise - the light sits in the sky - no sounds. I've seen planes go right past it and - nothing. If they are multi-dimensional, they must be cloaked to the plane's captain. 
  I stand in my door or out on the grass and watch it. It stays for hours - so I usually do the same things. Ask if it is real, say hello, ask for help with world peace, ask for a visit in a form that won't scare me. I wait a while, then go back inside. 
 This sighting is one of many for me. The abduction experience is life long, as well.
