a green light was observed headed SSW. when overhead it abruptly changed direction north and into the clouds.

i was on a friends property camping, at the time of the sighting i was walking through his cornfield at about 1 am. i was admiring the night sky since it was a very clear July night. i saw a green light coming from the north but i didn't think anything of it because the Lifeflight helicopter fly's from Cleveland to Columbus all the time and it has a standard blinking green light on its tail. this light was pulsating slightly but that was it, there was no noise. by the time i realized it wasn't the Lifeflight helicopter it very suddenly changed direction. i remember remarking that it changed direction like the ball in pong. it went up into the night sky and disappeared, me being used to wierd stuf just said "ok, cool" and continued on with my night.
