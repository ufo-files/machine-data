{nd} Two stationary metal crafts in broad daylight

My husband and I were going home (Bridgton) on rt 302 heading North. We were driving past 'Prism Works' when my husband noticed them and told me to look up in the sky. It was still very light out, blue skies and barely a strand of clouds in the sky. The two crafts were far away and we were unable to tell the shape of them (possibly spherical), but they were the size of a bb (from a bb-gun)held at arms length. The two objects did not move, and did not disappear behind clouds (which tells me they were below cloud level). They looked as if they were blinking, because the sun was reflecting off of the metal surface.One of the crafts sat higher than the other, and they were close (3 inches apart from my point of view). Due to our moving vehicle we lost sight of them behind the trees.
