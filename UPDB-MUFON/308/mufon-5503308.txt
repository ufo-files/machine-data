{rjl} 2 glowing red orbs met a 3rd glowing red orb

Appx 2020 hrs lying on couch in den. Two glowing red orbs appeared in my window facing west. Orbs slightly pulsating and next to each horizontally traveling southerly from north west. After they disappeared behind my neighbors roof line, I jumped up and told my wife. I grabbed my cellphone and went out front door and wife followed. We were both in driveway and saw the two orbs meet up with a third red orb which was stationary in line with my street. The two appeared to go above the third then travel either upwards or westerly, one at a time. Each of the two slowly faded out. Then the third traveled in the same direction of the first pair and faded slowly. It was dusk so there was still some faint sunlight on the horizon. Lasted appx 3-4 mins. Unfortunately I didn't hit the record button on my cellphone correctly and have no video.
