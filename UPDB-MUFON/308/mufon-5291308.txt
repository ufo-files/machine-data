Looks like cloaking device malfunctioned

I am a nurse & was driving to work towards San Fernando Valley from Santa Clarita, CA. At intersection of The Old Road ( it goes along the 5 fwy) & Balboa Blve, as I was about to turn right (to go north) when I looked across the 5 fwy & saw a blimp-like craft above the electrical generating area & Tilson Sewage Treatment Plant. 

The blimp-like craft was without fins or a cupola (it wasn’t a blimp).

The thing was outligned in black & I could see through it. It was not tethered but just silently hovered. It just hovered there, & there was no one on the road near me to see the vehicle. 

I watched it for about 4 mins (totally forgot I had my phone & could have taken pics) but made my right turn going south on Balboa Blvd because I had to get to work. While I was surprised at the sighting.

It was not upsetting. Living in Los Angeles & working as a nurse, I have see other unusual unexplained events. This was just another. 

Either a UFO had problems with its cloaking device or the US gov is farther along in its stealth technology than it wants to admit. A most unusual sighting, but, hey its LA.
