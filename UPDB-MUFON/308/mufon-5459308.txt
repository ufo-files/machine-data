{cak} 3 lights larger than stars

This happened a long time ago but I’ve always remembered it and have told the stores anytime ufos come up. I was laying in a boat on lake Powell to sleep, looking up at the stars I noticed 1 larger star moving and making sharp turns and starting and stoping. Then two more came in close, they started moving in all different directions around each other. Then all of a sudden they all shot off in different directions.
