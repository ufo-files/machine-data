We were camping out on Rt 66 in New Mexico , awoke to see 3 discs hovering

My dad, mom, sisters and I were on our way to CA, slept overnight off route 66, in New Mexico. we awoke and my dad said look up. There were 3 discs hovering above us. Very shiny metallic. Made a slight whirring noise. I was 12 yrs old at the time. We watched and the 3 darted first in one direction and then another at terrific speed. They were comletely out of sight in seconds. My dad thougth they were some new plane we had developed. We had never heard of UFOs at that time. In later years, my dad refused to talk about the sighting anymore as everyone he told said he was crazy. My momm and dad are now deceased.
