{rjl} object moved across my vision from south to north and disappeared in a burst of blueish light

I was on the balcony looking south, I thought the object was a helicopter. There was no noise. The object was going north. What I saw was an object that was red in front yellow in the middle and red at the end. The object had a fuzzy outline. The object traveled in a straight line and rose slightly. Suddenly there was a burst of intense blue light, which was very large and roundish. The front of the object entered the blue light and seemed to thin a little in front and disappeared in a burst of intense light. There was no noise. 
  After seeing this, I ran inside to tell my family. My daughter came out on the patio and I came down stairs to meet her. We both looked up and saw a repeat of what I had seen a few minutes ago. We were on the lower level of the house and could not see what happened to the second object. I feel I have no words to adequately describe what we saw.
