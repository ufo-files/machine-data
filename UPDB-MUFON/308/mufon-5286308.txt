I witnessed a blue orb moving erratically over the ocean for a few minutes. It was after midnight, dark outside except for the light of the moon, and the blue orb was not visible to the naked eye, but I could see it when I looked through the camera of my Galaxy A21 smart phone. My wife and I watched through my camera phone from our 16th floor balcony for a minute or so, then I took a few pictures. After watching with curiosity for another minute, I took a 47s video, which showed the orb moving in different directions and elevations in a somewhat random motion. Toward the end of the video, the orb can be seen quickly shooting up above the horizon and making a sharp, 135 degree turn back down and to its left before it disappeared from sight.
