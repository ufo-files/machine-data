I witnessed a Gold Color Star Like Object

I was living in Fowler,Ca. living in the country I was outside on Jan 6, 2013 around 9PM in front of my home. When I looked up at the stars I saw a very large bright gold color star like object coming and descending down towards me. I was very afraid I ran to hide behind something in my front yard as I watched this object silently come down and looked get bigger. I really thought it was going to hit the top of my house. As it was coming down it came down approx. just over the top of my house from the West turned left in the direction of the grapevines and glided right over the top of them just feet above them. gliding and steadily heading NE until I couldn't see it anymore. It looked just like a star. Since that time I witnessed 3 other star like objects but they were white. One white one came over my roof and over my front yard at around 30 feet high. Made no sound and softly glided over my yard and turned and vanished leaving a sparkling trail behind it. I couldn't believe it. Amazing. I submitted a hand drawing of what I saw that night. Thank you.
