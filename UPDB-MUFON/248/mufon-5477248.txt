Girlfriend and I went outside for a cigarette. I was looking south when I noticed what first to be a helicopter. I then realized it was too close and I could not hear the typical rotor sound and it did not have blinking safety lights. It was completely still and silent then moved from right to left and stopped. The movement was exact. Did not ramp up in speed nor did it slow to a stop. Hover, move, stop. 3 times. Then a short decent, stop. Another decent, stop then a slow decent below tree line. the object was dome or bell shape with solid red lights at corner edges from top to bottom with a whitish center vertical shaft of light blinking random. There is a large gated baseball park in the direction. We got in the car and rushed to the park. It was closed and only a city worker in his truck. He reported no one was in the park and he did not see anything. Super clear night. No wind. I have never seen anything like this. We both agree it just could not be a remote controlled device as the movement was so exact without a ramp up or down in speed. From my back yard to park is 1/8 mile with some homes and a small orange grove between my property and park. I would estimate the object to be 10 to 15 ft tall and 6 to 8 feet wide. Altitude approximately 300 ft. Speed 25 mph when moving. The decent much slower but absolute horizontal to vertical movement 90 degrees. Right to left (East) then descent to park area.
