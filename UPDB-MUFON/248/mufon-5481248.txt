{cak} an object in the sky traveling west to east

Seating in my back yard looking up at the blue sky watching the vapor trail of a commercial jet traveling from the West to the East. Then from out of the blue appeared this object following the jet it appeared to come along side of the jet how close I do not know. Then it flew straight ahead of the jet passing it at a tremendous rate of speed going East at the same elevation and it was gone from my sight in a matter of seconds. This is the first time in my 85 years on this earth that I have ever seen a UFO.
