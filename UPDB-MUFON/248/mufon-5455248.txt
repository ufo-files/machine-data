{rjl} Chevron shaped formation of about 20 objects passed directly over our location, travelling about 100 mph, 150 ft high, no sound.

February 16, 2020 at about 8:15 pm, my girlfriend and I saw a fleet of 20 objects in a V-formation pass silently over her house in Waxahachie, TX. Girlfriend's hearing is better than mine and she heard a faint "whoosh" in the air when it passed by. I didn't hear anything, but I got a good look at them. The objects were identical. Each object was about 10 ft long by 4 ft wide, shaped like an elongated arrowhead, with a V-shaped indentation in the back. There was a dark line going straight down the middle from front to back of each object. The formation was about 100 or 200 feet up and had a faint, light, greenish glow. Girlfriend noticed that the formation wavered similar to a flag in the wind but in a vertical direction. The formation was travelling northeast, about 100 mph. We had our phones in our hands but we were so stunned by what we saw, we didn't take any pictures. By the time it registered that this was something odd, it was gone. It was flying so low and fast that we could only see it for about 6 or 7 seconds and then it was gone over the horizon.
