{nd} Seen s.w. Of Holbrook az. 8 days later directly above me on Rt.377 dry lake

I was driving home from work worked couple of hrs. O.T. It was about 2 am. I was almost to Holbrook when I noticed 3 lights in a triangle shape between Holbrook and cholla power plant. I thought I've never noticed that before. Soon as I thought that it just vanished.i go what. It was as if they knew Ihad spotted them. 8 days latter I was going into work at papermill on Rt. 377 it was around midnight in the dry lake area when I noticed the same 3 lights in a triangle shape directly above me I thought to myself those looks just like the lights I seen by Holbrook and then they vanished.as if they knew hey had been spotted . I would say the two times I had seen this craft it was way well over 300 ft. Across . It is they were watching the cholla power plant the first time and the papermill the second time. And didn't want to be noticed?
