{dr}Very Very Bright lights over the car at night, and another time at day light cuz the sun was still high.

The first time was at night. I was going fishing with my dad. We stop at Kettleman City because he was getting sleep. He stop in a parking lot and he got in the back seat and lay down. I was in the front seat and then a very very bright light came on and the heat of the light. That's was it. I don't know how long it last it. Because my dad got up and said he was ready to go. Another time I was working at night and I was about 20 miles south of Corcoran I look up looking north and when I look up I saw this round shining thing it last about 10minutes and then it took off so fast.
