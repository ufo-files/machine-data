{tjd} Above bright lite changed to colors dissapeared

Was heading to work on my normal route around the 4:45-4:50 am. I have gone thru this route since 2011. I know a plane when I see one. Or a KFO "Known Flying Object". As I'm driving I notice this huge bright light high up in the sky but huge. Very bright. Didnt seem to be moving. I had to record it, which I did. The video doesn't do it justice. Right before my eyes it changed from bright white light to just 2 blinking lights small. Not bright anymore and it disappeared. I was blown away. Couldn't believe what I had just seen. I was shocked. Didnt feel scared or weird just shocked. It was around a minute or so that I was able to witness this. My video is like 14 seconds. Enough to show the bright light and it changing colors. My first reaction was it's a low plane. That scared me because I thought is it coming down. That worried me because I have never ever ever seen a plane around that area at that time . Tucson Az airport is close. I know where the planes usually turn to. It was too big if an object for it to be a plane. Too bright too circular.
