{dr}Approximately 12 balls of orange lights moved in an eastern direction in Ontario county on County Line Road around 10 pm on June 15th, 2013.

Two f us were driving south on County Line road around 10 pm on Saturday night, June 15th. We saw an orange ball of fire moving swiftly through the sky in an eastern direction. Behind it, in varying numbers (from 5 or so to 1) followed in same flight pattern. There was no noise, no change of direction. I taped the event on my iPhone. A woman and a man pulled next to our car and asked what were the objects in the sky, and we said we didn't know. (However, we did see two of these identical objects about three years ago in the sky in Brighton, NY, on Monroe Avenue, ) We observed the objects for about 5 minutes. There were about 12 of them, moving at the same pace, When the last one flew over, we tried to follow them in the car, but we couldnt find any of them.
