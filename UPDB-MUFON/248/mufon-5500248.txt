I observed a trinagle shaped object with a red light in the center and a yellow light in each angle.

I was in my car with my parents and an aunt when we saw a stationary strange light in the sky. I proceeded to park my car and started to observe that light and I realized it was a trinagle shaped object with a red light in the center and a yellow light in each angle. After some minutes without moving, the tirangular object took a straight-line path. The object emitted a sound just the same as a commercial airplane. The object was really close to me, so I decided to took some pictures with my cellphone. IT IS NOT THE FIRST TIME WE SAW OBJECTS LIKE THAT IN THAT ADRESS.
