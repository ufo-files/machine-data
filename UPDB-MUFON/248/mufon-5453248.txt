Walked outside at approximately 2240 and noticed a very bright light (Star like) in the Northwest sky just above the tree line that appeared to hover. I Did not see any blinking lights that would indicate it was a plane or helicopter. At times it appeared like it may have been descending or coming closer but at a slow pace. I called my family outside to witness the incident. My son and I both took pictures and video of the incident. We watched it for approximately 5 minutes. We all walked in and I pearled our the door a few minutes later and could still see the light. I waited about 5 more minutes returned outside and it was no longer in the sky.
