{cak} 2 lights flying in formation and 1 light following

While driving my personal vehicle, I turn north bound on Germantown Parkway from Memphis Arlington road. The road has a slight incline. Just above the horizon in the center of my windshield a noticed two orange lights. My first thought was that they were stars. I noticed they were getting closer and brighter. My second thought was that it was an aircraft. But the lights were orange. The lights turned left (west bound). I pulled over into the first parking I could and jumped out of my car. I pulled out my cell phone to take a picture. By this time the two lights had turned South bound and were rapidly increasing speed. While I was trying to take a picture I noticed a third light west bound. I took a picture which did not turn out very well. The blurry lights on the rights side of the picture are the two lights flying in formation/together. The light on the left side of the picture is the third light. By the time I switched my cam are to video, the first two lights were out of sight. The video shows the third light. I stopped the video and did a quick scan in the sky for other lights. As I tried to start another video, the third light turned south bound and quickly excellerated. I got back into my car and drove south bound and only saw the light for a few seconds before it was out of sight. This is my first sighting. I'm a retired police officer with over 26 years of service. I'm currently a commercial pilot with an Airline Transport Pilot rating. I have never seen anything remotely close to what I saw flying in the sky.
