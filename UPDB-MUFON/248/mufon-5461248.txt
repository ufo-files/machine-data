{rjl} Thought I was watching an airplane going east. It stopped, then went backwards. Then saw brighter , bigger light south. Stationary. Went north

Total of 3 flashing lights. Flashes faster than an airplane. Red and white flashes. First saw a small one flashing to the south going east. It stopped about 5 seconds then back tracked. Then saw a larger brighter flashing light to the south. Hovered, then went north just a little faster than a plane. then I saw a third small flashing light to the south going west then angled s.w.. The whole thing was only about 2 minutes. It was just before a thunder storm. Was lightning off and on to the far south.
