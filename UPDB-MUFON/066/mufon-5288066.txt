Two shape shifting chrome UFO

A chrome square shape balloon type object drew my attention, drifting SW. That's when I notice behind it (south)a stationary chrome pill shaped object hovering vertically. It went up slowly then dropped suddenly and made an immediate turn westward disappearing quickly. Reviewing the video I noticed I captured 2 flying objects flying together.
