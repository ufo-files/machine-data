{jpg} Myself. My brother and daughter. Witnessed 4 distinct and separate craft 3 different times within a 2 hour time frame

It was around 6:30pm on June 4th 2018. My brother and I were sitting in the backyard talking. I noticed something flying over my house. I asked my brother if he thought it was maybe a drone or a Mylar balloon. We both quickly realized it was neither. It was spherical in shape and extraordinarily reflective. We watched for a minute or 2 as it headed due east. At a constant speed. As it was in the eastern sky a bright light just appeared behind the object. It "bounced" around in the sky then disappeared. Then it would reappear in a different location. And repeat until both objects were out of sight. 
Then at around 10 after 7 or so. My brother and I were still in the backyard. I looked up over my house to see a brightly lit cigar shaped object heading southeast. My brother even managed to grab his cell phone and get footage of this second object. We witnessed this object for only maybe a minute or 2.
During this second sighting I was yelling for my daughter to come out and see this with us. She didn't get out fast enough. However, as my brother and I were describing this last event. I happened to point out a actual airplane in the north sky heading west. Once it crossed our path we saw a black top hat shaped object right behind it. The object headed northeast, upward very quickly. Then simply vanished.
