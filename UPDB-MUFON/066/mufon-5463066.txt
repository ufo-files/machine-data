{jpg} At least 30 objects, came in 5 or so, then in pairs! Startling!

Was on way to being dropped off in East Boulder. In car my driver and I witnessed about 5 objects moving fast and revolving around each other. We both thought they were lanterns. I know 5 others witnessed the event. The objects moved fast and low above treetop level, first 5 and then in pairs. The objects would slow, hover, one would start an ascent and dim, other would take its place and do the same. Seemed like a movie clip over and over. There were slight differences in the relative motion between objects on each path. Event lasted approximately 35 minutes.
