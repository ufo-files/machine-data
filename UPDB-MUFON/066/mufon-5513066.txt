(GS) Saw 3 red/orange lights while driving home from work

was on my way home from work at approx 12:40 am and on the phone with my Brother. I was on Big Beaver rd. heading East, and was about to turn right onto Rochester road when I saw this red/orange light flickering and moving West. It was about to cross my path and was maybe 50 feet above me beneath the low lying cloud cover. I pulled into the parking lot and stopped to watch it as I thought it was a Chinese lantern or something like that. I realized that it was not a lantern and was red light moving without sound, and it was not windy outside. Then a second light came into view, running parallel to the ground and following this light. Thats when I got off the phone, got out of my car, and attempted to use my iphone to record. Once I got out of the car, the lights started moving farther away and higher in the sky. A third light came into view and then all three formed a triangular pattern. They stayed in that position for a bit, and then disappeared one by one. My video was shaky as I got back into my car and tried to pull past the large lights in the parking lot so that I would not have any light pollution in the video. All I can say was it was extremely strange and I have never seen anything like that before in my life. Did anyone else witness this? I know there were other drivers at that intersection at that time.
