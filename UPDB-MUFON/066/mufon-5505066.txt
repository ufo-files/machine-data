{kt}It looked like a shooting star it was very high.

Approximately 8:50 pm, ... I was walking out the door to go smoke. I looked up at the clouds and saw what I can only describe as a shooting star but traveling north east in a very straight line. It was not descending nor ascending, it was fast. Then it appeared to stop dead, and hover almost. I thought it had disappeared, and that's when and a very bright like almost nitrous type boost came out from what I can only assume was the rear end. Like you see in rockets. Only smaller. It shot up and east immediately afterwards and disappeared completely.
