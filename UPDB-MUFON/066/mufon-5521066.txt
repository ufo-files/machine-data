It hovered over the Lake for well over an hour (when we observed it) It had colored light spurting or pulsating in red, pink, blue. It was dark and it hovered either over the mountains or the water. It was difficult to determine.

We were sitting on the terrace overlooking Lake Chapala and the mountains on the other side. It was dark, and when we first saw it we thought it was a plane that seemed to be standing still. We continued to watch it for over 20 minutes and began to realize that it could not have been a plane because it did not change. It was hovering. I got the telescope and saw the bright lights. It did not ascend or descend. It moved slightly and just hovered. After watching it for about one hour and 45 minutes, it seemed to become dimmer and move farther south away from us.
