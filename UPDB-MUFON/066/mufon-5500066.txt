{rjl} blue/green cone shaped object with white tail

I am reporting this for my granddaughter (age 20) and friend observed a cone/shaped light/object while driving west on the 210 freeway, yesterday, August 17, 2014, at approx 7:30pm/dusk. There was a cone shaped lights/object, blue-green on the bottom of the cone and white on the pointed end of the cone. As they looked toward the mountains on their right it was located 3/4 of the way up the mountain and descending quickly, in 3-5 seconds it went out of sight.
