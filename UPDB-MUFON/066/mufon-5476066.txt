{nd} While driving I took multiple photos of Sundog cloud. When I reviewed the photos the following day, there was a bright pink dome shaped object with light radiating from it's center, moving in several frames.

I was driving from Susanville, California, heading North on California Highway 89, when I saw the brightest Sundog I had ever seen. The colors in the clouds were vivid and lacked the pastel colors I was used to seeing. The main cloud struck me as odd, so I grabbed my phone and just started taking as many photos as possible while driving. I did not notice the object until the next day while reviewing my photos. The pink, dome shaped craft appears clearly in several frames and appears to be moving toward the strange cloud I had been photographing. The object had a translucent pink aura and a very bright internal light eminating from it's center. Upon closer inspection, I believe, it can be seen approaching the cloud from the Northwest in the first few frames, and then it hovers and seems to move around the cloud somewhat, then heads out in a Northeastern direction in the final frame.
