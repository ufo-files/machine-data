{cak} Spinning bright white lights with blue, red, green and white lights blinking

Leaving the house at 5:45 to go to a meeting in Downtown Austin, noticed a bright light spinning and flashing through the trees to my left. Stood there for a few seconds trying to wrap my head around what I was seeing. We filmed the light for about 2 min and once we got calmed down figured out that we should move to the back yard where we have a view over the Lake (Travis) We made our way there and saw at least 6 spinning bright lights blinking red, green, some blue and red light. The objects bounced up and down, not just a little, but several hundred feet at least if not more. Seemingly, no pattern, but random jerks up and down and 45 degree angles as well. I had my camera on and thought I was shooting a video, but was so excited I didn't press the start button even though i saw the images through the camera. Really bummed about that.
We watched the objects dart around for about 6 additional min. and then had to leave. They were still in the sky, fading in and out of view and bright when we left.
