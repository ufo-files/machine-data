One night in 2014, I was outside my house with my brother. I remember talking about the subject of UFOs and my brother says “can you imagine we see one now”. And we laugh, but then he shuts up and tells me to look at the sky. That night it was clear, not many stars were seen but these two were very bright. They were 2 stars that were together but with a small separation when one of them begins to move in circles around the other then after about 15 seconds the other begins to move and also and out of nowhere the first one makes an instantaneous acceleration and disappears to her right side and then the second follows her. The phenomenon lasted about 1 minute. We spent more than 2 hours watching the sky but we didn't see anything else.
