Witnessed bright light in sky. It traveled north and turned and went south.

I was putting my dogs out and witnessed a bright light moving across the sky, No blinking lights just a low hum. I went to get my camera and when I came out it appeared to be gone. I aimed my camera in the direction I saw it to take a picture just in case. As I was taking the picture I said to myself show yourself so I can take a picture. As I looked through the view finder to take the picture the object appeared from the north. After I took the picture and looked up it was gone again.
