{rjl} One white non-blinking object, about 2 hours later 2 fainf red objects of same appearance as each other

At about 7:30 PM a dull grey plane with at least 5 lights and two wing tip blinking lights (5 blinking) kept circling above Vegas, it was even near over me twice, it was quiet but could hear it. I figured it was looking for UFOs possibly. It circled till a few minutes after 8:53 PM which is when a white object at cloud level appeared headed towards Flamingo Rd from Tropicana Rd. I timed it from a power line cable to a wall edge, took 33 seconds in the gap, which visually looked like an 8 foot gap. It didn't blink and the plane was going in it's direction momentarily. Minutes earlier a cop helicopter violated my privacy rights by spotlighting me as it passed over.

At 10:43 PM heading away from the Strip towards Boulder Hwy. at cloud level was a dull faint red light travelling quickly like the white object. To my surprise seconds later another just like it travelled behind it, their speed seemed to slow and they faded out in about 50 seconds, they may have travelled upwards. All 3 UFOs appeaded small.
