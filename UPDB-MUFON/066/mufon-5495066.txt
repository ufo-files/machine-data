It was a nice sunny Florida day and I was sitting on my porch and looking at the sky, no clouds at all, and all of the sudden a small cloud appeared and then grew in size in a round formation vertical to the ground and from the center a hole opened and an object burst from the center in a flash of light and then the cloud and object vanished. No sound, nothing came from the area. It favored the pictures you see when an aircraft breaks the sound barrier but there was no sound what so ever. I have watched the space shuttle as it re-entered the atmosphere and heard the boom associated with it and also heard the booms when the air force flies of the eastern coast but there was nothing with this event. Just a bright flash and then silence. I really didn't know what to think at first but the more I reflected on what I saw the more out of ordinary it seemed. I could not figure out how a cloud could just appear in an other wise beautiful blue sky especially vertical to the ground. There is a lot of different opinions on UFO's and I respect most of them but I also believe we are not alone and history has shown over and over we have been influenced by other than human intervention.
