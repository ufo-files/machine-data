Slow moving orang/red light moving NW to SE then stopping above glowing sharp(triangle) before asending up and away

My wife and I were camping in Brazos Bend State Park in December 2010. I was sitting next to the campfire and just gazing up in the sky because of the beauty. I noticed a slow moving red/orange light moving NW to SE. During the evening I observed a constant jet flight pattern moving NE to SW so I was caught by this light moving the opposite way. As I watched it moving towards us I could see it was not high flying like the jets. I called my wife out from the camping shelter to take a look at the light. I just seemed different, out of place and was completely silent. I estimate it was about a 1/2 - a mile away but it was just a guess. As we were observing it the light just seemed to stop and grow sharp in focus then fuzzy. I grabbed my binoculars and that was when I saw that it was triangle shaped with its lights. Two on the bottom and one on top. We saw it for about 10 minutes when all of a sudden it just started to ascend up into the sky and slightly away, where it just faded out of observation. We went back to the campfire and talked about what we had just seen. My wife went back into the shelter while I kept the fire going. About 10 minutes later another object with light came from the same NW to SE pattern but the light was more reddish. It followed the same path, coming to the estimated 1/2 to one mile distance and did the same thing as it got very sharp and then fuzzy. After about 10 minutes it also ascended up and slightly away until it was not observable. I used my binoculars again when I saw it and the element was the same, two light on bottom and one on top. We were really excited as we thought we saw something odd. About 20 minutes later another object/light appeared again moving NW to SE but ther lights this time were back to the orange/reddish. It stopped again about 1/2 mile to one mile away. This time the duration was short, maybe 5 minutes but then when it ascended straight up in the sky it was almost instantly out of sight. There were no more after that and I am now still awed by what we saw.
