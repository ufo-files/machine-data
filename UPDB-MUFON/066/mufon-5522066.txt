Green light ball shot across the sky toward ground

It was Wednesday night and I was laying in bed at 10 oclock when I noticed at the corner of my eye a bright green light in the sky out of my bedroom window. A couple weeks prior I saw a similar light and thought that maybe it was a green shooting star. When I saw the same type of green light in the sky again it immediatly gave me chills and I yelled out to my wife that the green light was back.

It shot across the sky and went toward the ground. It lasted about 3 seconds.
