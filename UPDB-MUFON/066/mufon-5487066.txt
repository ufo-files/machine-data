{cak} Looked up and saw a hovering disc

I was walking my dog and happened to glance skyward and saw the disc overheard just hovering. I took a short video as I had to chase after my dog. When I tried to get a second video the object disappeared like turning out the lights and blending in with the night sky. While the object was visible it hovered and appeared to be spinning as it glowed to varying degrees while blending in with the night sky. I was shocked at what I saw and unsure of what it was. Very interesting to say the least.
