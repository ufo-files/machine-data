White sphere star-like object.

Very clear night looking at the stars and saw what looked just like a star way up in the sky. I was looking straight above when I looked a bit off to my left and saw an object that I would have thought was a star if it wasn't moving. This was so high up Ive never seen anything like it. White and bright light and round like a star but no glare or flickering like stars do. It was moving at a high speed first going sw and then making a half circle to eventually go straight north. It would block out light from the stars when it went under them. I can't gauge how fast it was moving since it was up so high. It covered nearly half of the visable sky I could see in 40 secs so that's fast. I know it was a UFO because the way it change directions. It seemed to fly jaged just a little bit but was mostly flying straight. Never saw anything like it before.
