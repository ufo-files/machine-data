{dr}Odd diffused light comes at plane

At about 10pm I was looking out my window of my (top floor 5th floor) apartment building, facing South, with a good wide view of the sky, despite some trees. Directly above me-and-building, moves this white glowing but diffused light larger than a star. It is coming from North-West going South East. About as fast as a sorta low small plane, I guess. An aircraft (plane) is coming from East and going West, at the same moment. (About 8 inches at arms length, up from my horizon, I think.) When the object/light is about 1 half inch (at arms length) from the planes nose, it vanishes.
