2 black craft

Was looking out pir south facing picture window. It looks out towards the James river which is beyond the trees several miles away. We live in a very rural area. These 2 black objects glided out from behind the trees. I thought they were Canadian geese but there was no flapping of wings. They flew perfectly straight one slightly below and behind the other. Absolutely no sound. We get planes and helicopters here a lot becuase of the river and because rhw pipeline has a facility nearby. They check the river and right-of-way. These craft were a bit plane like but very thin and all black. No exhaust or vapor trail. I have no idea of speed or distance. I'm not good at approximating. I cldnt make out any detail except they were almost bird or plane like. They were not fighter jets. No sound at all. I cldnt get my binoculars in time to get a better look. They were too quick. I suppose they could have been black prop planes but they were so quiet and I didn't see any propellers. They seemed bigger than that and much more streamlined
