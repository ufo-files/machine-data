Large green object plunges from sky, turns red then makes impact with ground behind hillside. Explosion could be seen

We were traveling through Dixon on I-80 west traveling southwest. We are approching pitt school road exit when my wife noticed a green object roughly the size of a dime in the sky from her passenger side window ( window at the time was facing Northwest), plummit towards the ground. She gasped and pointed. I looked over and was able to watching for about 2 seconds as it fell. 2 decond into me viewing it, it quickly fully turned red and went behind the mountian range. We then saw a burst of orangey light from an explosion. Looking at the map as soon as we got home it made contact between Winters and Lake Berryessa about where SR 128 cuts through the hills. There was no time to snap a picture.
