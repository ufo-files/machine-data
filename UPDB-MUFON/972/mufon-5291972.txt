60ft ball of orange light descended over the M1

I’m not 100% sure of the date as this happened 26 years ago but I think Sunday the 25th of September 1994 must be pretty close. 
I lived in a village called hathern just outside of Loughborough and I was seeing a girl in the next village called shepshed I didn’t have a car and there were no buses that ran between the two village’s so I would walk the 4 miles twice a day to see her I did this walk for just over a year. 
I used to like this walk as there was a big hill in between the two villages that I had to climb and I would always stop at the top and look down over Loughborough and shepshed a couple of times that year I had sat there and watched the police chase joyrides
Anyway that night I had left my girlfriends at 22:30 so it would have been around midnight or just after that I got to the hill on shepshed road as I got to the top of the hill as normal I started to look down over Loughborough to see if there were any police chases going on but Loughborough was quiet so I looked back down the hill to shepshed when I saw an orange ball of light travelling north to south along the Side of the M1 motorway At the same altitude as I was on the hill so maybe 100-150 meters and descending as it went towards shepshed I remember laughing to myself thinking wow someone is going to be in trouble in the morning as Initially thought It must be a military jet that had taken off from East Midlands airport and the orange light was the afterburner but before I had finished the thought I realised that there was no noise at all not a sound, so I started to run to the top of the hill where there was a gate that gave me a clear view of the valley I was 20 years old and in very good condition so to run the 15 or so feet to the gate only took me 2 or 3 seconds but by this time the ball of light had traveled a mile to the junction of the M1 and Loughborough and was now at treetop height maybe 100-150ft below me the light then followed The road towards Loughborough The A512 Ashby road it wasn’t over the but maybe 100 meters away from the road to the south going over the fields still at tree top height where it went behind a petrol station as it went behind petrol station I could workout it was the same size as the Front of the building which made it 50-60 ft in diameter. 
I watched the light continue along the road towards Loughborough until it reached the edge of the town where the buildings started just before it reached the first building It shot off at a 75 degree angle at unbelievable speed this thing never turned or accelerate it was an instantaneous directional, height and speed Change I don’t see how anything can move like that the GeForce alone should have ripped it apart. 
The whole sighting lasted approximately 15 seconds and the light traveled just over 3 miles that’s over 760 miles an hour without a sound
