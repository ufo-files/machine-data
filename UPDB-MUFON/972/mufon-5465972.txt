{cak} Saw something in large tree. It appeared to have 4 lights, 2 red and 2 white. Saw orange ashes fallling from the tree.

On this date at approximately 11 pm, I was at American Legion Post 159 in St. Clairsville, Ohio watching college football. There is an exterior deck at this location, and I went out on the deck to smoke a cigarette. It was raining at the time, and I took an umbrella out with me to the deck. As soon as I exited the door to enter the deck, I noticed in a large tree in a neighbor's yard, at about the 4:30 o'clock position, a square looking, lighted object in said tree that appeared to have four lights, 2 red and 2 white that appeared to be from the object in the branches of the tree. There were orange ashes that appeared to be falling from the branches of the tree. There was no lightning or thunder around at the time. I did not have a cell phone to take a picture, but I did yell into the Legion to get someone to come to the deck to confirm what I was seeing. A lady named Christie, who works as a bartender at the location, came out and observed the apparent orange ashes falling from the tree.
