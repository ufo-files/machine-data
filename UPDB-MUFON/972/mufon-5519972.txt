strange bright yellow light, long, diagonal line with a head shaped top and possible wing, slowly moving

My wife observed this object/event from the third floor of her college Art department at Orange Coast College in the city of Costa Mesa, she was just on the outside stairway talking on the phone with me and describing a weird bright object in the sky, diagonal shaped, she snapped two photos, one zoomed and one regular with her iphone and sent it to me. Both of us could not figure out what it was. She said it moved very slowly but overall stayed in the same position during the 5 to 10 minutes of her observation. Not sure if it disappeared by the time she stopped looking at it. To me it looks like a spirit with wings hovering and leaning forward, observed from its side.
