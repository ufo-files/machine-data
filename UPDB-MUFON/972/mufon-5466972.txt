{cak} I was awake lying in bed. Looking out my bedroom window I saw this craft moving N to S across the sky.

I usually wake up around 1:00 am to 2:30 am every morning. I am 69 yrs old and don't sleep for long stretches anymore. 
On the morning of July 24th I was laying in bed, wide awake, looking out my bedroom window. I have a great view of the sky. Pine trees to the left and the right and a low row of trees on the bottom frame the window. I was staring out at the sky when this craft rolled into frame from the left side, North. My view of the craft was a complete triangle. I say it rolled into view because it slowly turned in a clockwise direction while slowly moving South in a straight line motion. It looked to me like a Pool rack triangle on it's side. There was a wide red glowing band that ran around the perimeter of the craft. There also was a red circle at each corner. The red glow was very bright and slowly pulsed as it turned.
The red light clearly showed the outline of this object. I was fixated on the light trying to set this picture in my mind. The body of the object could have been anywhere from light grey to black. The light defined the edges but obscured the color of the body. I viewed the craft for about 5 to 6 seconds total.
After this experience I got up and made a quick sketch. Later I drew it up on a cad system. 
I don't really believe that this craft was trying to be stealthy in any shape or form. It was so clear and so defined I believe it could have been seen from miles away. I don't feel the need to have someone justify this siting but it would be nice to know someone else had this great experience. Thank you
