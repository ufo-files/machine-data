It was a bright cold unusal mild non snowy night. Looking into the western sky at what I thought was a star, and out from behind came another star and it did a "smily" or half circle and then went right back into or behind the big white star. It was so very high up that all it looked like was bright stars. Though stars do not move like that, nor do comets. It lasted 1 to 2 minutes. I stood and watched it for a while till I got cold and went inside.
