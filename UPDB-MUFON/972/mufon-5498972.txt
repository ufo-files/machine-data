{cak} Extremely Bright Light was close then sped away

I was having a camp fire with my family in Williamson, NY. My brother in law first noticed an extremely bright white light through the trees 100 yds away east of us. The light was blinding and we could not make out the shape. When we first noticed it we thought maybe it was a helicopter search light but there was no sound and the object moved in ways I have never seen. The object started from behind the trees then moved out into a clearing before excellerating at great speed up in the air and out of view.It started northeast in the tree line slowly moved south as it came out of the trees then shot up and away in the east. We were dumb founded and could not believe what we saw. We lost sight of the object after it climbed so much altitude it was no longer visible.
