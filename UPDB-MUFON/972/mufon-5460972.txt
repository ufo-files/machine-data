{tjd} two objects in air with no wings....one on left on a hill. Other one to the right about 300 yards.

AT approximately 12:15 p.m. Dusty Trammel, a ranch supervisor went to check on some cattle that are grazing on Bald Hill in Yavapai Country 1/2 mile east from the junction of Cherry Hill turn-off and I-17 (9 miles south of Camp Verde, Arizona). Dusty didn't see the objects until he returned to his office where his supervisor showed him the photograph he had taken.
The objects are at great distance from the photographer and they get pixelated.when enlarged. Interestingly. , a severe thunderstorm loaded with pea size hail was approaching the area within minutes which continued down into the town of Camp Verde to the north about 7 miles. The objects appear to be about 100 feet in the air. The right object is barely visible to the right. 
A "radar map of Arizona" clearly shows that area was completely covered with intense storm clouds. No one has returned to the site to see if the objects landed. It is unknown whether the objects were seen by others. Dusty did not see the objects. He photographed the grass for record keeping. 
Objects can be seen by zooming in. The vastly approaching storm is to the right moving toward the objects.
