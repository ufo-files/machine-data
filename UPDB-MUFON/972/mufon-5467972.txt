{cak} Dancing orbs spotted for a few minutes then disappeared

I should have submitted this years ago, but only thought to do this now so.. it must be the right time. When I was about 8 or 9 years old, I was standing next to the balcony glass door in our apartment. I wasn't out on the apartment, door was closed, just staring outside. As I looked up into the sky, I saw 4 glowing orbs, like little stars, moving in a dance-like pattern. Imagine four points in the shape of a compass (N, S, E, W). They would move to the center towards each other, then out to the first position again. They did this for about two minutes. I was transfixed. I tried to see if maybe it was someone shining a light into the sky but I could see an origin point on the ground. I turned to call my mom over and see, but when I turned back to open the balcony and get a better look, they were gone.
