driving and object appeared

Driving along a major highway (QEW Highway) 3:45 am going to work. The lights appeared between Lake Ontario and the highway just in front of my car. 4 or 5 very bright lights flashing almost blinding when I saw them. They were in a row. I made a mental note to calm down and make sure I was seeing what it was I was seeing. I tried to see the reflection of the object behind the lights but the lights were too bright so I waited to see what I could when the lights were out. It look like projections sticking out and at the end of each projection there was a light. I couldn't see what the projections were attached to at the far end. It was too black. I didn't know what it was but I know I was extremely nervous. I watched the object continue on it's path between the highway and the lake from my driver side view mirror. It was too low to be seen in the rear view mirror. The lights disappeared when it went around a slight bend in the road.
