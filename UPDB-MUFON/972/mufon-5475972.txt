{nd} Triangle shaped with radiant light, no sound moved south.

Was out with wife and daughter star watching when wife noticed above our heads a triangle shaped lighted object moving over head. The object moved slow over head with glowing lights south and was massive. You could see clearly the shape and size as it moved slow south. Wife had her phone but because of the amazing breath taking size she didn't take a picture. The triangle object moved slow as we all watched it and all the sudden it was gone.after the sighting I was completely besides myself and ran inside to get my phone to see if I could see it again to take pictures however nothing showed up.
