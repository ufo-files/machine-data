Large object rectangular shape with bright colors and rapid movements

At approximately 2:00 AM I woke and looked out my bedroom window to the northwest as I was looking for the Big Dipper and the North Star. At this time at night it is low on the horizon. As I looked for the North Star I saw on object below the position of the North Star that was changing colors from pink to purple and red. It was moving slowly in a tumbling motion and had bright white lights at the base. I watched it for several minutes and it suddenly moved very quickly to the left. Repeated the brilliant lights and then moved suddenly up. The lights continued and I watched it for a few more minutes and it just faded out, although it looked like it moved North before I lost sight of it. I have seen many aircraft in this area most of them headed south to Toronto. I know the forward white lights and red and green lights and the blinking strobes on a commercial aircraft. I have also laid on my beach at night and have occasionally seen a satellite travel from west to east. This usually lasts about 3 minutes from horizon to horizon. What I saw was neither of these. This was a brilliant and beautiful object moving with non ballistic motion. I would have tried to capture it on camera but it was to far away for an IPhone 7.
