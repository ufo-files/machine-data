{cak} A bright light in the middle of a craft that I could not see the rest of (it was nighttime) with four dim lights on the corners.

The UFO seemed to be either a low flying 3ft by 3ft thing with a bright light in the middle, or a high flying thing that was much larger.
The first time I saw it (Monday, Jan 12 I think), came from the southeast side of the house and flew by the house towards the north, then when it was about 15 degrees above the horizon it turned to the left and went that way. I dismissed it that night because I see lots of aircraft and satellites fly over/by my house. I did notice that it looked closer and bigger, though.
The second time I saw it (Thursday, Jan 16), it flew directly over my house from the northeast. I got curious and ran to the other side of the house (the southwest) as soon as it passed outside of my sight. Sure enough, it came over the house. It was gliding, and I could see four very very dim lights at the corners. It took it around a minute to pass over the house.
Both sightings were at about 10:45 pm at night, and I watched it until it disappeared behind houses/trees or went out of my eyesight.
