Star Like Above Atmosphere

At approximately 9:30 PM MST, I took my dog out to the backyard to use the bathroom. It is a VERY clear night. So many stars visible. I was told by my mom that the Aurora Borealis would be visible from my location tonight, so, I was searching the sky. I saw what looked like a star that seemed to be moving. It seemed to be moving towards ORIONS BELT. I kept my eye on this star-like object. Once it PASSED Orions Belt, I knew that it was moving. I know it wasn't a plane as it was too small and too high up. I checked my satellite map and the ISS map to make sure I wasn't seeing either. I wasn't. The map didn't show what I was seeing. It moved faster than a plane, but, slower than a shooting star or comet. Once it passed Orions belt, it stopped for a good 3 seconds and started to move again. Right after it started to move, it disappeared. Gone. Thats when I knew I saw something that wasn't ordinary.
