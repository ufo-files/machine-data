{cak} My wife and I saw about 20-25 lights moving in a direct line in the night sky outside our house.

I walked out of my garage at 7:00 pm and walked toward the neighbor's to see two deer in the front yard. As I walked back, I look up into the southwest sky and saw what I thought were stars lines up in a straight line.  Then I noticed they were moving slowly. They appeared about the brightness of a bright star. They were spaced evenly and more kept coming. I counted 12 initially. I ran into the house and told my wife, and we both went to the back porch to watch. They moved in a northeasterly direction, from the southwest sky (240 degrees) at about 30 degrees toward the northwest sky (300 degrees) at about a 45-50 degree angle then disappeared one by one, once they reached a certain point. They kept coming, about 20-25 of them, we lost count. It lasted from 7:03 to 7:08 pm mountain time. It was amazing.  I am not sure if they were satellites, or ufos, but they moved in a direct line, spaced evenly apart. I tried taking pictures, but could not locate the object in the dark, in the viewfinder.
