star like objects came into O formation and rotated in a circle then zipped off

Summer of 1970; Camp Tamaracouta, Laurentian mountains Quebec Canada.
A friend and I were sleeping outside on an elevated platform looking up at the stars sometime after midnight when we saw what appeared to be stars go into a O formation. They started rotating in a circle for a couple of minuets before zipping off one at a time in the same direction.The formation appeared to be just smaller than a quarter as it rotated and appeared to look like the other stars in the sky. My friend, (MA) whom I did not mention a second witness as I do not have his permission, looked at each other after the event and asked "Did you just see what I saw?" For some unexplained reason I don't recall talking to him or anyone else about it until many years later. I realize this imagery is very similar to the Orion movie production company graphics, interesting coincidence?? Anyway I was wondering if anyone has seen anything like this?
