{kt}3, bright, yellow/orange, fireball-like objects observed hovering in the night sky, lining up and then taking off into the sky, then a 4th object followed behind, 4th object was the brightest, biggest, and closest

My mother, 2 little sisters, and I were sitting in the car outside a Mexican bakery in downtown LA. We were getting ready to drive off when my mom spotted some unusual, objects in the sky. She freaked out and told us to look. My sisters and I looked up through the windshield of the car and noticed a bright, yellow-orange, fireball-like object in the sky lining up slowly. Then a second, round, large, fireball-like object came and stopped in the sky, hoovering. Then a third came along. They hoovered together for a few seconds and then all three took off into the sky at the same time. A second or two later, a fourth, round, fireball-like object came soaring through the sky. By this time, my mother and I were outside of our car freaking out trying to take video on our phones. This fourth flying object really scared us because it was the biggest, brightest, and closest object of them all. Thus, it was easier to see. It looked like a huge yellow burning star...it looked shapless and very bright. Luckily, we were able to capture some footage. We were fortunate enough to capture a good video of the last flying object. Which was the brightest and in the video it is the easiest to see. Needless to say, I was in complete shock and utter disbelief of what we had all witnessed together. I wanted to believe it was an airplane but it clearly was not. Airplanes in our area have white, yellow, and red flashing lights. These objects we saw did not.
