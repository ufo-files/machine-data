Silent Black Triangle

Conditions:	Crystal clear sky, no wind, no clouds at all
Being an avid stargazer and amateur backyard (telescope) astronomer, I am (fairly) familiar with the night sky, which (hopefully) might lend some credibility to my assumption that this was a UAP.
In lieu of the fantastically clear conditions that night, I was in the process of setting up my telescope. Had I not looked up at that specific moment, I would have missed it altogether!
Gazing towards south, it almost immediately occurred to me, that a large section of the sky was completely void of stars. Transfixed and unable to make sense of it, I realised the “blacked out” section of the sky was due to an enormous triangle shaped ‘object’.
Observed specifics:
•	The triangle was completely black, no apparent ‘nuts and bolts’ and ‘seamless’ as if die-cast
•	It’s composition was – what I could best describe as – what I imagine matte liquid mercury might look like
•	That said, it seemed to intermittently become translucent, allowing the stars to ‘shine through’.
•	It seemed to glide effortlessly at a constant velocity, trajectory and altitude
•	It was completely silent, absolutely no sound, no visible signs of propulsion and no emissions
•	As I am unable to give a good guestimate of size or altitude, I can only state that when directly above me, it completely blacked out the visible sky.
•	It then proceeded to disappear above the tree-line in my garden in a northerly direction

It bares worth mentioning, that a very similar description of this UAP was submitted by an eyewitness located about 50 km north of my position (the direction the object was moving in when I lost sight of it) on the same date and around the same time . Below link to witness report:
