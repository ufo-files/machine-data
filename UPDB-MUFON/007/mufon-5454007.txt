{jpg} Lights moving from NW to NE

Lights moving in a straight line from the NW to the NE moving in equidistant manner. Over 30 lights in one series that seemed to fade out after moving over head. There was a about a minute break in between the two series of lights. This was witnessed by 2 adults and two children. The second set of lights seemed to originate a little further from the NW . They all were equidistant, very high, almost thought they were satellites , no sound moved about the same speed. White light, spheres. I took video but nothing came out. I took photos and this is the best one. The two white dots that are equidistant were part of one of the series of at least 20 or more lights.
