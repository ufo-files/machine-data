Ball of light.

My friends and I were sitting outside when I saw something out of the corner of my eye. We all looked up and saw a ball of light moving slowly north. It disappeared after about 10 seconds. 2 minutes later it appeared again in the same spot and continued moving north and then disappeared again after about 5 seconds.
