Two Orb objects Red/Amber

So on a nice calm night i stood out on the front deck and looked into the sky and i noticed a Very huge amber colored orb object moving. I ran inside to find my camera to tempt to record (But failed because the video was set at black and white mode and also very fuzzy). Not long after i saw a huge red orb object lower altitude then the amber orb. They were flying together and going at the same pace. I then decided to try and follow the objects and when i got the the next block. I did not see these objects any longer (it was as if they disappeared. I had my camera handy and set to a better mode just in-case i would have seen them again. I then walked to the park in the direction they were headed. waited for 10 mins and nothing was visible. So i stayed outside for about an hour and decided to come inside.
