{dr}A large, dark triangular object apperared in front of me.

I was driving south on Craycroft Rd in Tucson near Inverton Rd toward the south on a brilliant, sunny day in May. it was around 2:00 PM when I was at a stoplight and saw this giant object in the sky ahead of me, and another driver saw it too. It was larger than anything I've ever seen. The driver next to me and I crossed the intersection and pulled over to watch this huge object float over the southern horizon. We shook our heads but stayed watching it. It seemed to hover with no movement. It stayed in place for at least one minute, and then dashed, and I mean DASHED faster than anything I could imagine toward the southwestern sky. This big object was at least larger than a couple football fields, dark gray in color and had no markings on it. It was triangular in shape. I think it made some shadows on the area below it, but I wasn't near there. It was that large. I stayed in my vehicle, but got out to see more when it took off in a flash. It was like in a sci-fi movie. It just moved to the southwest all of a sudden in a very quick movement, so quickly that all I could see was a trail of light. The person in the car pulled next to me left quickly, and I wish I was able to talk about this event. There were certainly several witnesses as per this event, so I'm hoping to hear more of their accounts.
