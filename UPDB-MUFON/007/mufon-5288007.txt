Sighted during army exercises in Canada

Whilst I was serving in the British army in 1985 I was sent to Calgary Canada 🇨🇦 
I was in a elite guards infantry regiment and had been assigned to take out supply’s to the men from my company 
I was driving a Land Rover that broke down on military Rd that took you out to the training areas ... it was called the rattle snake 
I called in that I broke down and waited for recovery 
After an hour I noticed a object moving over my position at a slow speed it was not a helicopter of any aeroplane that I had ever seen 
It was tic shape very big and was glowing a violet to purple colour 
I Watched it for 10/15 mins then it went up straight at a rapid rate 
No smoke or vapour trail
I never reported it to my superiors and have only ever told my wife quite recently
