{rjl} Blue light above cloud for 4 seconds and disappeared

At swimming pool with my wife at night, both noticed in the corner of the eyes a bright blue light above (north east) night sky around 11:59pm on 19-August-2015. It attracted our attention immediately (we did not have time to tell each other to look up) it was obviously visible as we both starred for about 3 secs and this light faded away. It happened quickly, we looked at each other - what was that? It seemed like a blue light behind a cloud (haze/mist around it) that brightened up and quickly faded out. There was no motion, no airplane in the area at that time. Hoping to find out if someone else observed the same event.
