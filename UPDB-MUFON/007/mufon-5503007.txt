Cigar shaped extremely fast and big, no noise and appeared metallic,reflective

I saw a cigar shaped UFO in Bath, Somerset, Which appeared to be hovering next to a church steeple for about 2 minutes as I was watching it in broad daylight at the back of a busy train station 2 people stopped and watched it with me. As we watched it ascended straight up extremely fast and stopped then plummeted out of view behind the train station that was just in-front of us. It then ascended again and stopped and hovered exactly where it was when I first spotted it. It then disappeared. As I was standing there watching where it was with my camera-phone ready another 2 people asked me why I was looking at the sky,it appeared again in its first original spot. it ascended again then went back to same spot again. Then it shot to the right extremely fast and stopped and hovered perfectly still. it then flew off in away from my point of view and disappeared in about 2 seconds.
