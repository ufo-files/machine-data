Returning to home on mountain pass road. Saw 2 flashes, sequential, longated shapes. Very bright, moving.

My husband and I were returning home from Taos to Angel Fire, NM on hwy 64. Remote, mountain pass. I was looking offf to the right, it was very clear, no clouds. I saw one flash then another to the right of the first flash. They both had an oblong shape. My husband saw this out of the corner of his eye...he was driving. It was as if one object was traveling at great speed and flashed twice or two separate objects that each flashed (flash as in maybe lightning though it was completely clear). Had no idea what it was (I was looking out the window at the stars...amazing star-watching sky in the mountains here). Since they were flashes I lost sight after the second flash.
