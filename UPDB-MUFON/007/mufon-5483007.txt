{nd} Light traveling low and slow then got brighter, accelerated and disappeared

My girlfriend and I had just left from visiting my parents and we were heading W on Case blvd when I noticed a light coming from the SE, heading in our direction. The light looked like it was from a plane, except that it was flying very low and moving very, very slow. We thought I was a small private plane, since there were a few planes in the sky, but it made no sound and it suddenly made a fast, sort of S-turn and then continued heading in our direction. The light looked like a flame, like if we were looking into a fireplace place, but it was mostly round. That's when I began to film it with my cell phone. As I was filming it coming towards us it slowed down even more, almost to a complete stop. Then the light became so bright that we can actually notice other colors in the glow as it went directly over us and over some homes and trees. I decided to follow it so I continued on Case blvd and then turned NW onto rt 202. We didn't see it immediately but after about 10 seconds it crossed over rt 202 heading NW and it had accelerated significantly. We tried to film it again but it just disappeared like if someone had turndown off a light switch.
