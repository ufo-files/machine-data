{dr}glowing refrigerator size object moving across snow covered corn field crossed road 40 ft from car into wooded area

Driving home from a friends in Waukesha,Wisconsin to Oconomowoc, WI traveling on Hwy 18 near the city of Dousman in my Ford Explorer around 11:50 PM. Noticed light in field moving toward the road I was driving on. The light appeared to be constant luminiscent. As it got closer I was able to tell it was hovering and appeared to be about the size of a refrigerator. I slowed my vehicle before coming to close to the object. My lights on the dash began to flicker along with lights of the vehicle. The object continued in a straight path across the road and into a wooded area. I lost track of the glowing object when it entered the wooded area. I called my parents very concerned about what I had just seen. I arrived home about 15 minutes later.
