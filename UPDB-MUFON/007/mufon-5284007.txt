Round gray sphere flying east to west. No sound. Approximately 6 feet in diameter.

I heard a small plane flying over and happened to glance up at it. As I was looking at it an object to the east flying at a very low altitude. Maybe 2 to 3 hundred feet high. It appeared to be about 6 feet in diameter and was gray metallic in appearance. No sound at all. No windows or wings or motors. Just flying across the sky at maybe 100 miles an hour. It was a bright sunny day and there was a reflection on this object from the sun. No special movement or lights of any kind.
