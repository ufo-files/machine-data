{cak} Driving South Rt 28 observed Bright light slowly falling- had time to take partial video

Leaving Chantilly around 6:20pm driving south on RT 28 towards I66 around dusk. 
I am familiar with aircraft that regularly fly north in that area landing at Dulles airport 10mi behind me to the North.
This caught my eye since it was extremely bright white & no red or green blinking lights, also not coming towards me, therefore did not seem to be a plane.
It seemed like a very slow falling meteor. I had time to observe the oddity, grab my phone, and video aprox 6 seconds of the sight (while driving)
I first observed it about 45 deg above horizon & it "fell" slowly away or downward (hard to determine) to aprox 20deg and disappeared before visibly being obscured by clouds or trees or mountains or horizon. Sky was clear.
Very odd to me
