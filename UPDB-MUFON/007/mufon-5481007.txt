I was taking a evening drive and decided to drive out to a boat dock west of Warsaw, Mo.
When I parked my car at the empty boat dock, I got out and walked to the waters edge to see if any boats in the water.
As I looked to my right heading south, I saw the large bright, white orb
slowly heading my way north following the river path.
I immediately went back to my car and drove away.
The orb made no noise and it lit up all the water below it. I had a thought that it seemed to be playing hide and seek.
