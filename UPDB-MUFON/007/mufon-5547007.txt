Blue ball of light falling from sky and zig-zagging.

I was driving to work and right when I was in front of the dairy farm on Sparta Avenue, almost to the city of Sparta in Kent County, Michigan I saw a very bright light out the corner of my eye and I looked up out of my windshield. I saw a bright blue light fall fast then slow down and zigzag as it fell. I saw it for about 7-10 seconds until I couldn't see it anymore because trees were blocking my view. I immediately looked at the time on my car stereo after observing this light and it was 5:37am (EST). It possibly could have been a meteorite, but I don't know if they zigzag and behave like this did. It was easy to tell it was very near, I would say it hit the ground somewhere around the Pamida store or around M-37.
