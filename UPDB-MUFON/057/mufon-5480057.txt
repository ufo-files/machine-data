{jpg} Miltary helicopter with lights folling 2 cigar shaped objects without lights.

I was inside my house when I heard a helicopter outside. We don't often get helicopters in this area, so I dicided to take a look. It was dark out but, due to the cloud coverage, I was able to see two dark cigar shaped objects without lights, being followed by the helicopter that I had heard.We live fairly close to dugway proving grounds AKA the new Roswell, but I have never seen ANYTHING like this before. The objects travelled in a southwest direction. I followed them until they passed out of veiw over the houses to the south.
