{cak} Flat bottomed disc that emitted l light.

At about 10:15 p.m. on July 22nd of this year I went outside to see if my friend's car was gone and discovered that it was. As I turned to my left I suddenly observed a flat-bottomed disc with no central cupola. It had a metallic luster to it, but it was bathed in a glowing distortion of some sort. It was about the size of two small buses, but it's precise height could not be determined as there was nothing to provide me with any scale or reference.

I was facing due west when I saw this object that appeared to be a solid object of some sort. As I live in a gated community here in the desert, there was no one outside at all, on the street in front of my house or anywhere on the block. The sighting only last a few second, but it was enough for me to think that this wasn't a conventional aircraft as it had no wings, tail, etc., and made no sound whatsoever. Also, this saucer shaped object did not fly away, it simply winked out the way a lightbulb does when you turn it off. This may sound rather odd, but when this object just turned off, I had the strangest feeling that it did so because it knew I could visually observe it. 

I should point out that several months earlier while taking out the garbage late one Wednesday night I saw a rather odd light hanging in the sky. The light was very bright and demonstrated anomalous motion in ways that modern fixed-wing or helicopters could not perform. I watched it for about two to three minutes, at which point it's luminosity dramatically increased as did it's apparently size. Following that, the light in question seemed to emit a small luminous discharge from the lower left region of the object.
