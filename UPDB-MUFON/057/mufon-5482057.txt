{jpg} Retangular/elongated object, well lite flew over sited area near Shawangunk Fire Department on Albany Post Road (also: county route 7near New Paltz NY

While driving on County Route 7 (Albany Post Road)south then west near the Shawangunk Fire House and small restaurant I witnessed a rectangular / elongated well lit object fly in a westerly direction. It was several meters in front of me while I was driving my car. The object was flying in a relatively straight course. As I rounded a bend in the road I saw the back of the object as it eventually flew over trees on the right side of the road and became obscured. The object was heading west toward the Shawangunk Mountain range.  The object was flying just above utility poles and I could see the silhouette of the telephone pole top against the bright side of the object that was facing me. The object did not produce any sound.
