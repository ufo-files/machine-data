{nd} big black object with one red light above truck moved slow

my dad was driving at night in desert in california and i was sitting up with him in passenger seat with radio going to keep him awake... i was looking at stars and they began to disappear and the radio went fuzzy sounding. i told my dad it got darker and the stars were dissappearing and i saw a red light tiny light and a big black edge of something.. i asked dad if i could use the spot light on the door and see what it was, he said no. i watched out the window at it. it went by slowly and quietly. the radio came back from fuzzy sound to clear. my dad use to deliver to all kinds of places at night and daytime for special products division. i usually hid in the sleeper area when he delivered, like he told me to do. happened in 1974 or 1975.
