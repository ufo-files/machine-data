{cak} Large disk craft with one central light and blue/ white lights flashed sequentially around craft, flew from SW to NW .

At about 2:30 am, a disk like craft was seen flying in a straight line from SE to NW at about 30 to 40 mph. The craft was round in shape, a dull silver color and had a large central light on the bottom. Around the outer edge of the craft, were white and blue lights that flashed on and off sequentially. The craft made no noise and and did not change direction. No pictures were taken.
