Five white orbs flying in formation in a straight line at a speed faster than an airliner departing the airport

I hesitated to report this sighting as a formation of UAP, but since I found that someone had seen and reported orbs over Naples Beach, a short distance south of my sighting location, just two weeks prior, I decided to go ahead. On 15 January 2022 at 2:15pm ET, I was sitting in a chair at [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/cms/LLL], watching aircraft departing from the Fort Myers International Airport (RSW), when I saw a formation of five white orbs moving from northeast to southeast. The formation consisted of three orbs on top with two more underneath them. At first, I thought it was only a flock of white birds. I often see flocks of white birds flying overhead, and they can sometimes be mistaken for UFOs. But several things made me doubt that these were birds. First, either they were quite large and very far away, or were smaller and close to my location.  If the latter were true, I should have easily been able to identify them as birds. Second, the orbs did not change formation as they flew, nor were there any indications that they needed wings to assist remaining in the air. I never saw any occasional flapping of wings or change of direction, either by an individual orb or by the formation. They were moving more rapidly than the airliners I see taking off or approaching RSW and were flying against the wind. As my view was about to become obscured by the tree under which I was sitting, I got out of my chair for a better look, but at that point, the entire formation simply vanished and I never saw any of the orbs again. I checked my phone app Flightradar24, but it indicated no aircraft in the vicinity other than the airliner I had just observed taking off.
