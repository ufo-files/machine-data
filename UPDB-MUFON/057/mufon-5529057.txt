Aligned like a roadway. Few crafts were low and plainly visible to naked eye

As I pulled into my driveway this evening i just happenned to look up which i never do anymore as there is nothing to look at. Until tonight. I have lived in Canada for 18 years now and have never seen a sight like this. At this very time outside of my home you can see approx 50 objects of the likes i have never seen. 3 of the objects are low enough to give definition of its shape and and the rest are high. Normally you would say balloon or aircraft. However, folks i have never seen an aircraft or balloon that can emit these kinds of lights. They are defiantly out there moving around yet clearly in the formation of a road and they are encompassing an spherical area so that they can clearly be seen.  Well take note i have seen it and it is exciting yet unnerving to say the least
