{cak} triangle of three lights

Saw triangle of three lights while I was facing south. It was heading in a north west direction. I only saw the object for about three seconds before the lights dimmed. I did not witness any body to the aircraft, it blended in with the night sky. The object appeared to be about as large as a full moon. The lights were dim, and were yellowish orange like mercury lights. I was listening to music at the time, and when I saw the object I immediately stopped. I have seen odd things in the skies in the past, but nothing that I would definitively classify as a UFO. This was absolutely a UFO, and it is rather unnerving.
