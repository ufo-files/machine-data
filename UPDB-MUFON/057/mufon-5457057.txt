{cak} Multiple (8-12) orbs of light moving in a triangular formation over Brooklyn.

Multiple (8-12) white glowing orbs passed over the Clinton Hill neighborhood of Brooklyn around 9pm on 11/15/19. The group of orbs, that were in a angular formation, passed over an apartment building cruising at about 20-30,000 feet. I observed the group for about a minute and a half before the formation entered a group of clouds and vanished. They moved seemingly completely silent across the sky. I should also mention that they were heading S.E. in the direction of local commercial aircraft. I only had my cellphone with me and thus was not able to photograph or record in the time allotted during the event.
