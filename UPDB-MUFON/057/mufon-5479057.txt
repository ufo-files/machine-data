possible abduction

Approximately 7 to 10 years ago I had what I considered, at the time, to be a vivid dream. In the dream I knew somehow that I was in the presence of aliens, I recall that I was on my back, suspended somehow. I do not recall seeing any beings, the atmosphere was dark, comfortable and I had no sense of fear. I was told and shown that I had a clear crystal at the base of my skull, 1 you no longer need this1 I heard; the crystal was removed and I woke up in my bed. I awoke feeling slightly confused, feel the back of my neck/head with my hand wondering what happened. It all felt so real.
I continue consider the possibility that this event was real but have no proof other than my experience.
