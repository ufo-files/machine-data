{nd} 1 red light, meets a second red light, they merge and shoot off in another direction.

Im home for the summer from college and my temporary home is in our camper out in the front yard. Last night, 7/27/12, at about 3 in the morning I woke up when I heard a loud car go by. I lay awake for a moment and was looking out the window of the van facing East. It was at that moment that a small red light came into my field of view from behind the pitch of my house. It shot to the left about an inch (relative to my distance), paused a moment, disappeared, then reappeared almost instantly another inch or so to the left except joined by a second identical red light. The right most light then flashed as it merged into the light to the left and they both surged a brighter red as they sped away in the direction they came from (most likely south) and disappeared behind the pitch of my roof again. I did not see them emerge from the other side. The whole event lasted about 4 or 5 seconds.

I don't know if this is relevant, but I had dreamed I had seen a UFO for the first time 2 days prior to this sighting.
