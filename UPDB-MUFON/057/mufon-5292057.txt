Saturday 8/15/2020 at about 7 am, i was looking out the upstairs bathroom window like I do every morning to see if the bunnies are out in the yard-They live under our shed. I can see 6 yards over the fences of our neighbors and the birds in our yard, and squirrels, so I was watching all of this for maybe 10 minutes when I saw what I thought was a small plane crossing not too far above the trees-we have tall trees throughout our neighborhood which dates back to houses being built in 1963. The plane wasn't making any sound and was moving very slowly. I couldn't see any wings though I was looking at the side of it, and no tail, no cockpit or propeller. It was shaped like a very long oval (flat on top & bottom & perfectly curved on both ends. The color was s slightly shimmering silvery white. It looked like there were 3 door or windows outlined in black. I could only watch it for about 4 seconds before I could no longer see it because of the tall trees. And there were no drone type legs.
