{jpg} Blue Green flash w/ a directional burst and trail left behind

Time and Date: 20:55 on December 4th 2018
[location moved to FI Section/CMS/jpg]

I was driving home and through my windshield I saw a vibrant burst of blue/green. The burst looked directional and was only visible for a quick second. I could see a streak of what looked to be clouds that trailed a good distance and ended at the exact point of where the burst occurred. The clouds seemed bigger and thicker than most "chem trails," and it was not in a perfectly straight line... the streak had a very slight "S" shape to it. the cloud trail also seemed a bit low for it to have been caused by a comet or meteor passing through our atmosphere.

I immediately sent a message to my friends letting them know what I saw, and that I though it may be a sighting. There were 2 other cars on the road with me and the car in front of me hit there breaks as the burst/flash occurred, but continued to drive. I pulled my phone out to record the "cloud" trail, but cannot see anything except black, as it was night time and I was continuing to drive.

The burst happened in an instant, but the clouds seemed to linger for quite some time.
