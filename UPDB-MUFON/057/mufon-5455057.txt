{tjd} A saucer shape craft landed about 39 to 40 ft away from me

In June 1975, I and a few friends witnessed a metallic, saucer-shaped craft fly over us, approximately 40 feet above us. The bottom of the craft looked like grey molten-lava concrete. It went over is at a slow pace and landed less than a half mile from us. We ran up to this craft which was on the ground on 3 metallic legs. We observed it for 15 seconds and 2 Cohoes Police officers pulled up to the scene with their lights flashing. Thet got out and observed this craft with us for approximately another 15 seconds before the craft let out flames from the bottom if the craft and it slowly ascended in the air, still moving at a very slow pace. It went south east of us and when it was out of sight the police officers told us to go home. The next day I went to school and told other friends what I saw and I told them I would show them where it landed. When we got to the landing sight there was 2 men in dark sunglasses and dark fedora type hats with long black coats on. I observed that the landing sight was cordoned off and a man in a white full hazmat suit was in the middle of the cordoned area digging in the grass. The 1 man in black told us to go play somewhere else and I said I want to show my friends where the craft landed and he said "get out of here before I throw your ass in jail rugrat" My friends and I walked away and I looked back at these men and they were staring at me. I went home an hour later and my mother said 2 men were there asking if she had a son jerry and she said yes and they told to not let me in that area again and that what saw was a helicopter. In 1975 I was 10 years old but I know that what myself, my friends and the 2 police officers saw was a ufo.
