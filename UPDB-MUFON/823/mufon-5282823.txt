Thought it was a satellite at first

Single light moving slowly from south to north. Thought it was a satellite, but it was a bit larger than a typical satellite - Mars or Jupiter size. 

It stopped. When it started again, it traveled same speed (slow) but was moving from west to east, then stopped again.

Two pinpricks of light show out of the object, one went east, the other went west. Then a beam of light flashed to the SE. It stayed in the same spot for the remainder of the time I watched. I was out there less than 10 minutes, more than 5 minutes. I can say that I have never seen a satellite shoot things out of itself, and I've seen a lot of satellites. I suppose it could have been the ISS, but I haven't seen it do that, either.

The beam of light that flashed after the two items shot out reminded me of the light in a dark hallway when a door is cracked open a little. It was fast - if I had blinked, I wouldn't have seen it..
