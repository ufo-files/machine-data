{cak} Bright Orange Lights in Formation

Over the Deep Creek area of Chesapeake this evening, I witness about 9 BRIGHT ORANGE lights moving in formation very slowly. There was absolutely no sound. Seriously, it was if the lights were going around something very large and it was very low to the ground...I would say about 1000 feet.
It was a cloudy evening though so the viability was not as good as i had hoped to get really good pictures. All I got were the lights.
