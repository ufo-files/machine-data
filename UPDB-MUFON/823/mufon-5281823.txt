Perfectly circular 'cloud' with center moving independently of the other clouds

Third sighting of same and very similar 'cloud 'object. This time the circle was moving up the beach. The 'cloud' in the center of the object was moving in perfect position to the rest of the circle not once shifting its position from directly in the center of the 'circle' . They were certainly moving as one unit all in same direction again not the exact direction of all the clouds. (circle and center cloud).
