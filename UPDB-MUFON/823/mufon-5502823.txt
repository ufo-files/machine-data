I WAS VISITING MY FATHER IN CAGUAS P.R. FOR ABOUT ONE MONTH. WHEN I WAS OBSERVING NUMEROUS FLYING OBJECT'S THEY WOULD LAND IN WHAT IS CALL THE 3 MOUNTAINS. THE FIRST MOUNTAIN PEOPLE LIVED THERE, IN THE SECOND MOUNTAIN THERE WERE SCATTER PEOPLE RESIDING THERE,HOWEVER THE THIRD MOUNTAIN NO ONE LIVED THERE.NO EVIDENCE OF LIGHTS WERE EVER SEEN THERE.MY BROTHER AND I WOULD STAY UP ALL NIGHT OBSERVING DIFFERENT KINDS OF FLYING OBJECTS HOVERING IN A V FORMATION AND THEN SCATTER AS THEY LANDED. NO LANDING STRIP TO accommodate THESE CRAFT AND IMAGINE THE TALLEST MOUNTAIN IN P.R. WHERE NO ONE LIVED THERE AND THERE WHERE NO LIGHTS. THE ONLY THING THERE WAS WILD LIFE WHICH ENGULFED THE WHOLE MOUNTAIN. YET WE OBSERVED THESE LIGHT LANDING AND THEN MOVE AROUND IN THE GROUND REALLY WERID. ONE NIGHT ALL THIS CAME TO A REALITY, WE WERE INSIDE THE COMPLEX, EACH ONE IN THER OWN ROOMS WHEN WE HEAR SCREACHING AND SCREAMING AS IF SOMEONE WAS ABOUT TO DIED. THIS IS WHAT MY FAMILY OF 5 AND MYSELF HEARD IN SPANISH "THE WORLD IS ENDING GOD!" AS WE ALL RAN TO THE TERRACE WE OBSERVE THEM RUNNING FROM SOMETHING. THERE WERE COUNTLESS PEOPLE SOME FELL YET I NOTICE THEY WERE ALL LOOKING UP. THAT'S WHEN I WAS STICKEN TO AMAZEMENT. YES IT WAS A UFO IT WAS A DICS SHAPE CRAFT WITH A END THAT LOOK LIKE A POINT. THERE WAS NO NOISE IT DIDNT WAVER FROM SIDE TO SIDE IT WAS PERFECTLY STILL IT WAS OBSERVING US. IT WAS SO CLOSE TO US I THOUGHT IT MAY LAND AT ANY MOMENT.THIS OBJECT WAS LIKE MERCURYS SUBSTANCE THERE WAS NO DIVISON OR BOLTS IT APPEARED TO BE LIKE MERCURY, PERFECTLY MOLDED. IT MOVED FOM ONE POINT TO ANOTHER IN A SPEED LIKE NOTHING WE HAVE EVER SEEN. IF ANY MAN FLYING ANY CRAFT KNOWN TO MAN WOULD DO THIS HIS EYES WOULD COME OUT OF HIS SOCKET,GRAVITY WOULD NOT PERMIT THIS. IT'S IMPOSSIBLE!.I CLIMBED ON A LEDGE OF A BANNERSTER INORDER NOT TO REMOVE MY EYES FROM IT, NOT REALIZING I WAS STANDING ON A LEDGE OF A SIX STORY BUILDING, ONLY HOLDING TO A CORNER OF THE WALL THEN I POINTED AT MY CHEST AND THEN POINTED TO THE CRAFT REAPTILY. WHAT I WAS INDICTING WAS TAKE ME WITH YOU,WHAT WAS I THINKING...LOL. THERE IS A HOSPITAL CALLED REGIONAL HOSPITAL THERE MUST BE ABOUT 3,000 EMPLOYEES THAT WORK THERE, WELL MOST OF THEM WITNESS THIS EVENT SHORTLY BEFORE AND AFTER THEY CAME OUT TO OBSERVE THIS UFO.
