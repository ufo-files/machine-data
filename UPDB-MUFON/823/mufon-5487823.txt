{rjl} Parshall Divide Incident

My wife and I were taking a scenic path and decided to pull over and take some pictures of the rock formations in the area. I looked back at my van and asked my wife to take a picture of our van, because in my mind something didn't seem right but without fully understanding what was not right with how our van was looking. The area we were visiting, my kids and I have experienced entities before while doing some rock-hounding. My wife had fear during the incident, and returning a few weeks later she was even more uneasy. The pictures included show something is "parked" over my van - I have submitted a picture of my van in our driveway to show the trim is actually solid. A few days later, I also noticed this design in the dust on my dashboard of the passenger side. The lines are too narrow for any fingers in my family, and nothing has that same design like a purse, etc. I do some alternative energy research, and see cosmic energy collector or schematic in this design, but am not sure what it really means. These photos were taken with a Windows 8 LG phone. Upon my own zooming in - there are blinking pixels or dots, there is a figure in the front of my van, another outside the door. Also in the side Windows are two white lines like I am parked next to a guard rail or possible contrails - but the sky in this direction from our van is cloudless and blue (personal shots can verify this). My area of Colorado has a lot of black-op type operations.
