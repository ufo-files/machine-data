Red Orange Balls Looked Like They Were On Fire

While filling car at gas station on Broadview Rd., north of Brookpark Rd a spherical red orange object passed overhead. The gas station manager saw it, a pilot and another witness. The object was coming from the north and heading southwest. A few minutes later a second object passed overhead. They looked like they were on fire.
