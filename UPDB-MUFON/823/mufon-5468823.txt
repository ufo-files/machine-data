I awoke paralysed with a bright light and saw 3 small grays in my room.

I had went to sleep as normal and at some point during the night I woke up with a bright light shining through the window to the left of me and as I tried to move I relized I couldn't move anything except my head. I turned my head to the right and in the doorway I seen 3 small figures about 3 to 4 foot tall with what looked like gray skin and large black eyes coming towards me and as I could not move I was getting very scared and I heard a voice in my head saying it's ok don't be afraid and one of beings lifted his hand and the bright light shone into the window again and they were gone and it took a couple of minutes before I could move again. At first I thought it was a dream but after reading other people's accounts I started to question it as it is very similar to other abduction cases.
