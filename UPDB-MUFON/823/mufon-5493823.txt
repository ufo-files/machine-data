{rjl} Triangular UFO above eastern Kentucky on 5/19/2015

My family and I had just pulled into our parking lot between 10:30 and 11 p.m. As we were sitting in the vehicle, I heard a sound like an aircraft passing by. I got out of the vehicle, looked up to the sky and seen a triangular shaped UFO. I am a huge believer in UFO's and extraterrestrials, so as soon as I seen it, I got very excited and told me boyfriend to hurry and look at it too. Upon seeing it, I immediately thought that it was an alien space ship. It was amazing! Since it was night time, I couldn't see the craft itself, so it must have been black. I seen round, white lights on the corners of the craft, that is how I could tell what the shape was. There was one light on each corner, so three altogether. There was also a shape in the middle of the craft that was red or orange in color and from what I could tell, it looked like it was round with a straight line coming from it. It moved like an ordinary aircraft, moving average speed in a straight line as it traveled. It moved from South to North and I couldn't see it anymore because it was out of view. I must say, I was like a kid in a candy store. Haha
