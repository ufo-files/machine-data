yellow/orange ball of light traveling around thunder storm heading north to north east around a thunder storm 5 of us age 38-24-21-15-10 were looking at the lightning of a passing storm in the east of our house when i pointed out there was a bright yellow orange ball of light around it travling at a decent speed, looked like it might go through storm or go above and i said i'll get my binos it took me less then a min since i keep them near the door on my telescope, there Celestron SkyMaster 15x70 i got them locked on to the object and described it to them as it looks like a star but not really its still moving its not blinking its going to go through the storm nope i lost it, it just vanished. not the first odd think i'v seen since moving to this area i do live in the flight path of the tricities airport and have looked at many air craft including airforce one when it came in july of 09 with obama didn't look like anything iv seen as of yet.
