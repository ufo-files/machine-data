Searching for video on quake and noticed objects

I was surfing online and saw the news for the quake in Costa Rica and began to try and find video because I have always wanted to go there to live. I thought, if they need help down there from a tsunami I can go there now and never come back. Fortunately, there was no tsunami, but there was this video from SkyNews out of Britian. The funny thing is, is that there are other videos posted on youtube from the same news company, but this part was edited out. Why would it be on their site and all of the other sources have it edited out? It was a live broadcast. It is certainly not a bird or a plane.
