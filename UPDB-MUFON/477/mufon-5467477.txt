The lights appeared in the direction stated, dancing around each other on a straight flightpath.

I was sat in my garden star gazing when 2 dull lights appeared to the NW, they were a dull Rose/Gold colour with what seemed to be an halo effect around them and for the few seconds that I observed them they were skirting/dancing around each other as if in a playful sort of way but in no particular sequence and travelled on a straight flightpath. I had observed several satellites throughout the evening and a meteor but these lights were a different colour/larger and travelling at a vast speed. I was excited and shocked that I was seeing the same kind of lights for the second time in 9 months.
