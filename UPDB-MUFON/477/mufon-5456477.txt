{cak} Red/Green light at Cincinnati Reds game.

I noticed an interesting red light during the Reds baseball game. I figured it was just a red light on a telephone tower but I kept thinking its too high and too far to be. After seeing it stationary for a couple of innings, I decided to take a video to compare the height and see if there was a cell tower during the daytime. When I started to take a video and zoomed in on the red light, it went out, then blinked green 3 times, and then there was no light the rest of the game. Again, I could see a red stationary light for at least an hour.
