Bright solid light, no flashing lights, changed direction

Location is just east of Toronto Scarborough, (near Scarborough Bluffers park) near the shpres of ake Ontario. Looking up at the night sky, as i often do, light was seen travelling north toward me from over the lake. Solid bright white light. At first looked like a satellite. Maybe the space station, which ive seen before. No flashing lights, no change of shape, no erratic movements detected. As light neared me, it started to change direction and head toward the east, out of sight. That is where i felt it was strange as i dont think satellites, or the space station, change direction.
