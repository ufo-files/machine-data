{nd} Rotating tv antennas and this transmission came through speakers instead of tv sound.

It was the afternoon and I was at home. I wanted to watch some tv before work. I only have an antenna that receives basic digital channels on my tv, and I could not find the remote so I was standing at the tv manually changing the channels to find something to watch. I stopped on channel 13, because it was coming in the best. There was a little interference, so I adjusted the antenna a little bit, which caused the sound to stop coming in clearly. It was no longer the sound from what was airing on tv, but something weird and different I could not make out. I put my ear a little closer and thought it might be worth recording so I pulled my cell phone out and set it to record sound. At first I had the phone up to the tv speaker, but it did not sound loud enough so I raised the volume on my cell phone, and turned on the surround sound speakers at a higher volume. I continued to record it and wondered if it was the sound from the tv just being distorted, or something else (alien sounding). It was like "Predators" were talking. I use this comparison because it did not sound human, and its the closest sounding example I could think of. I kept recording for a minute or two, and compared the sound to what I was seeing on the tv screen. They did not coincide with one another at all. I decided to change the channel to see if it would change, and it did once I got to channel 10. I went back to channel 13 and the sound was coming in perfectly clear from what was airing on tv. I was no longer hearing what I recorded. Very weird, and interesting. I feel I may have caught some transmission between an unidentified being.
