white and red light, flies like a jellyfish swims

Seemed to fly similar to how a jellyfish swims: up and down, hovered, circled. My friend and I stood in the parking lot of my apartment complex watching it for about 15 minutes or so. It had a broad white light that faded in and out (I think because of changing directions) and a constant red light.

When I first saw it, I noticed that it flew like nothing Ive ever seen before (given, I don't have much experience with flying objects). I pointed it out to my friend and we watched it. We tried to take videos with our iphones, but were unable to zoom so its really hard to see on the camera what it looked like.

Eventually we couldnt see it anymore through the trees and other buildings, it seemed to be flying away from us.
