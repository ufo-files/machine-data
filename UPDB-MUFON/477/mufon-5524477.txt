orange/red small circular light would pulse to bigger round orange light

I was attending the drag races at Cordova Ill. I happened to look up and directly overhead I observed a orange/red small circular light that would pulse to a bigger round orange light. I watched it move at a speed I would compare to a slow moving helicopter. I kept trying to find other identifying lights, but saw none. It traveled south with a continuous pulse, turned slightly to the west and then south again. Not much farther it pulsed bright and disappeared. I pointed it out to person by me and they just said UFO. I did make an attempt at a photo and wasn't going to report until I saw the Ill video posted for Aug 17. It looked similar to what I observed. I included the picture as taken, not zoomed or enhanced. Its just a small orange dot, but you can zoom in on it.
