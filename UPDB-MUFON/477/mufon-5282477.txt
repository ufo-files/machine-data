UFO DISC IN BELTSVILLE, MARYLAND

The sky was clear the night I was driving down route one coming from Laurel, MD. I was at the red light by the Inter-county Connector when I looked up towards the sky. I saw an airplane off to the left and then saw this beam of light that was rotating moving along the sky. The light turned green, so I proceeded to drive but I kept looking up. The UFO in the sky then came to a sudden stop. Just stopped in midair, lights were bright and rotating around in a circle. I went to get my phone and turn on the video. Just then it took off so fast that if you blinked you would have never saw it take off. It went in a straight line and vanished!
