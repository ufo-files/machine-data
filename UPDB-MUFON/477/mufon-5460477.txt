{tjd} Cylindrical object hovered then drifted into cloud and did not come out the other side

I was working in my home office late in the afternoon when there was a gush of wind hitting the trees in my yard. I looked up out of the window expecting to see a popup rain cloud. Instead, there were blue skies and white puffy clouds floating by. However, a hovering, cylindrical object then caught my eye. I first thought it was a slow passing plane but there were no wings! Just a cylindrical shape similar to a plane's fuselage or a rocket but round on both ends. It was a solid silver surface and was reflecting the sun. The object then flew west into the nearest cloud (which was surrounded by blue sky). I stared at the cloud expecting the object to reappear on the other side. I was hoping I could then see the wings and dismiss the object as a plane or take a pic of the object with my smartphone. Surprisingly, the object never re-appeared from the cloud! I assumed that whatever it was, it was using the cloud as a cover as the cloud drifted toward the east.
