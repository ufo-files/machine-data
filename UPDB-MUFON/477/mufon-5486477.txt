{cak} 12/18/15 at 7:45pm SW Fl saw red orb made no sound and had no flashing lights came out of the tree line, stopped abuptly for 1-2 mins and abuptly took off towards SW. Went behind trees and disappeared out of the sky.

I was outside on my stilt house deck with my dog at 7:45pm on 12/18/15. Noticed red non-flashing orb with no noise at all coming out of the trees. Changed position suddenly - came around the tree line and stopped abruptly right in front of me in the sky. For about a minute or two I stared at it trying to figure out what it was. I ran into the house and got my significant other as I could not believe what I was seeing. He also witnessed the orb and as we were watching it, it suddenly started moving again going southwest behind a group of trees and disappeared from the sky. This orb was a bright red orb making no sound at all and completely stopped and than took off - it changed direction and made some interesting turns - this was not a balloon, plane or helicopter! I wish I would have tried to take a picture of this. This morning we goggled SW Florida sightings and see that others have recently taken pictures on the Gulf coast of orbs that look very similar to what we saw last evening.
