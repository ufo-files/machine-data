Glowing Orb in Sky with Inner Circle

While driving this evening my son's girlfriend, Althea, and I saw a bright glowing light in the sky. It was pulsating and hovering. We went to get gas and saw it disappeared. I was curious so I left and went in the direction we saw it last. It reappeared and was hovering. After watching it for a couple minutes, I drove to a different spot where I thought it was. It seemed to be moving slowly away from us. We finally found it hovering over a hillside and took pictures. Our videos both got staticky after recording it and we felt we may want to not pursue it any more after observing it for 10 minutes. We took pictures, drove down the road and turned around and when we came back to the spot we last saw it, it was gone.
