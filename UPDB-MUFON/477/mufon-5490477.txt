{nd} Seen two glowing and pulsating round objects low to ground. Went and got someone to witness and seen same things.

I was headed home and was about to take a right down a street before the cemetery and noticed very odd object very low and a firery like appearance. I continued on down center street in place of turning down Hall or Hamlin street. Upon the intersection near First Niagra bank I noticed a second object. It seemed to get brighter and go up and down. It was kind of scary so I called someone to witness this. After picking said person up and returning she was also seeing what I seen. Now only one it was very bright and descended and seemed to dim out and disappear.
