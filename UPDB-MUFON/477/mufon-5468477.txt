I THEN NOTICED THE TRIANGLE CLOUD START TO SPIN, CHANGE DIRECTION AND THEN CHANGE SHAPE! I GRABBED MY CELL PHONE TO TRY TO TAKE PICTURES AND DOCUMENT THIS OBJECT. THE OBJECT HAD SHARP PERFECT EDGES AND WAS HOVERING OVER THE CITY'S SKY SCRAPERS.
