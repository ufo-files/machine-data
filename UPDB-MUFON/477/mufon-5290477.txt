Observed a burning green object in the sky in a straight line flying southbound on I-85 in NC/SC Border

My wife and I were driving down I-85 with our young son in the back of the car. We were on our way to Georgia for family. Around the border of NC and SC (we aren't exactly sure where we were in the car), we saw a burning green object over our heads moving extremely fast heading Southbound. It looked to us like a meteor. It had a green tail, and the burning glow was green.

Immediately afterwards we saw an object flying North back over our heads at incredible speeds. It was night time, so all we saw was the lights. It had red and orange lights - distinctly. It originated where we last saw the green object and moved, by our estimation, from that point (probably around a mile down at least from us), back over our head moving North in less then 3 seconds - completely reversing course.

The speed was most shocking, that and it seemed to originate from where the burning green object seemed to disappear (finish entry?). We obviously did not have time for any videos or photos, as we were driving, and it all happened in less than 5 seconds.
