2 fireballs moving west to east overhead

I walked outside facing west to get something out of my car and saw two "fireballs" at almost eye level moving west to east overhead very slowly with no audible sound. I ran inside to tell my dad and came outside to find them directly overhead. I could not tell if it was a ball or disk. From underneath it was definitely lit up by an orange almost flickering light. It almost seemed like it was a round kitelike object being lit underneath by a fire. They continued to float eastward and veered towards eachother and separated. As they floated out of site I noticed a third light sitting further south that didn't seem to be moving but had the same flickering appearance. It slowly floated away. My cell doesn't have video but I got one awful picture. These things completely confused me and I still don't know what I saw but it wasnt a jet or a helicopter. Still have a rush
