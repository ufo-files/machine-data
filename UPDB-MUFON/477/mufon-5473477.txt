A wave of about 60-80 lights that seemed to be traveling in formation.

Last night (in Kitchener Ontario) we were looking up at the stars in our back yard. I did see a shooting star earlier but then around 11:15 pm I saw what I will call a wave of lights traveling together. I said to my wofe beside me, "There's something!"

They might have been shaped like a large W (they were very dim and barely perceptible, but they were definitely there) and in terms of scale they took up as much sky as a small constellation does (from our perspective).

They were very dim, as I said, but I had a second to check my perception, and they were still flying in the same direction for that second or two. They looked like a fleet of lights very high up, traveling in formation, or a very large ship (VERY LARGE, would have had to have been) very high up in the atmosphere.

They traveled toward the north-north-west and faded slowly into obscurity. 

They seemed to be very high up in the atmosphere, at the height of airplane travel but perhaps ascending higher.
