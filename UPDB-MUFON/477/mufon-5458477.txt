{rjl} orange star-like objects

It was approx. 6:45pm, sunny, no clouds. I noticed a small "star-sized" object moving south to north overhead. At first I thought it was a very high flying aircraft, but after a minute or two I realized that was not the case. I then noticed a smaller, slightly dimmer "star" catching up to it and the two objects then seemed to become one. I then looked further to the north and saw three more identical objects moving in different directions. At this point I knew I was witnessing something really odd and moved to a point in my driveway where my neighbor could see me. After she saw me looking up, I asked her to also look up, and she too saw what I was seeing. The three objects to the north then moved in such a manner that they formed a triangle, while the original object moved toward them. The objects then seemed to move randomly, in different directions slowly. About this time a high flying commercial aircraft flew past emitting a contrail. It was impossible to accurately estimate the altitude of the unknowns or their speed but they seemed higher and slower moving than the commercial jet. The objects then disappeared from my view, weather this was due to them climbing higher, or some other reason is unknown, but they didn't just "zoom off". The entire incident had a duration of ten to twenty minutes. Sorry, I don't have a camera, so no images. I would be curious if anyone else also witnessed this. I don't know what these objects were, but they were NOT weather balloons, Chinese lanterns, the sun refracting off of ice crystals, or swamp gas.
