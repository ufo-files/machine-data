Then it is seen dissapering behind the solar panels of the Space Station. There is several( smudges or spots) on the camera lens that is visable. The object moves independant of the cameras movement.It is hard to determine the distance between the object and the I.S.S because there is only black of space and cloud cover on the earth in the for ground.I have attempted to make a video of this recording but my only means of doing so is with my cell.I have told several people about this at which they laughed, until I showed them the recording and every one that has seen it said that there is some thing out side the ISS window.
