2 Reddish/Orange Circular Orbs hovering then took off

I was driving on 294 Northbound about 3 miles south of Ohare Oasis and seen 2 reddish/orange circular orbs hovering about 500 ft altitude to the left of me as I approached them. I immediately pulled over on the left side shoulder of expressway to get a better look at the objects. They were literally just on the other side of the expressway. They were perfect circles and they were like no other light source I've encountered before. The objects didn't emit any type of glare whatsoever, and it was a dull light, but clearly visible is the only way I can explain it. They hovered in the same spot for about a minute then took off at truely incredible speeds going west. It was an awe inspiring experience!!! I also didn't see any craft they were attached to, but they stayed same distance apart from each other as they took off, making it seem like they were connected to each other. Hopefully others had seen these orbs too.
