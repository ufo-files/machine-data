Triangle with 8 yellow lights

I was sitting in my front lawn when I saw a few lights in the sky. I dismissed it as a plane, but then it began to hover. It hovered for 2 minutes then zipped off at an incredible speed. It was very confusing at the time, I'm still not sure if I believe what happened.
