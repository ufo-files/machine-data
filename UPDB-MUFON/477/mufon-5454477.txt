{rjl} Line of stationary lights turning on and off

Looking northeast from my front patio (east of South Mountain). Observed at 5:18 am on March 25, 2020 as I walked out of my house because my dog needed to go potty.
Straight stationary line of bright starry lights (6+) in northeast night sky ten miles away (?) in direction of Stanley, NM that turned on & off, then each light disappeared beginning with the lights in the middle of the line of lights followed by lights at either end of line of lights...clear night...observed the Big Dipper in Western night sky and jet overhead heading west toward Sandia Mtns...watched for approximately 2 minutes until lights faded...but phenomenon was already occurring when I came out of my house.
