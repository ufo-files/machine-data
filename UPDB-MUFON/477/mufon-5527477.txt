Two rapidly moving objects in tandem seperated by quarter of a mile.

During our evening walk, we (three individuals) noticed two rapidly moving objects traveling overhead at about 20,000 to 25,000 feet. Both objects were brightly lit and traveling in tandem by a distance of about a quarter mile. No sound was present and no coloured or blinking lights. We frequently see aircraft and these two objects were not airplanes, nor were they satellites.
