Strange lights with varied flight path

Last night, I saw 2 lights. I was laying in bed, looking out my window. At first I thought it was fireworks, but they weren’t fading. They fell sort of down and to the left. Paused for awhile, then went down to the right. My heart started pounding because I couldn’t figure out what I was looking at! I videoed for a minute and ran to get my husband, by the time I got back they were gone.
