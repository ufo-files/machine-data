Fast moving orbs

I was in hythe on the beach looking out to the sea it was pitch black. Dungeness is a shingle spit coming out far into the sea with a nuclear power station. I could see above the area the power station is in 3 bright orbs going left right up down very fast then stopping motionless then the orbs would go into one orb, with out changing size then 6-8 orbs would come out and fly around independently then all go into one orb again at most there was 12 orbs in the sky. They could move very very fast and stop immediately or go in a reverse direction instantly. In the end there were 3 orbs that went straight up and disappeared I lasted between 20-30 minutes. They were no planes in the sky and even if there were they could not be anything man made. They could move unlike anything in our world and all fit or multiply from one spot the same size of one orb.
