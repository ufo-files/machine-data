{cak} Weird, Ominous & Surprising.

I was hanging out with friends, six of us in total. We were discussing religion & i just happened to look up & i seen a brownish-orange, cigar-shaped UFO with windows on the side of it. You can see light inside the object & it gave off a funny haze/glow that was a neon green in color. The object was moving in a straight path but was panning left to right like a dial on a meter. It had no FAA lights. I was surprised & confused at the same time. All 6 of us lost sight of the object when it passed over a building. When i first saw it i thought it was a blimp as well as the others. We realized that no blimps fly that high & blimps make a humming noise. This object was silent. Its glow was also something that made all of us think it wasnt a blimp. I have spoken to Lillian Waters about this sighting in the KGRA chat room & was encouraged to file this report.
