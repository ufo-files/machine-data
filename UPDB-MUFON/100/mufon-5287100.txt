UFO of Pembrey Country Park

I was sat out side with me friend when he said can you see them light over there by pembrey range. I turned to look and there was a bright white orb keeping in the sky. After about three minutes another white orb smaller than the other seemed to come out of the other orb. This orb then slowly started to move forward then desended below the treeline. After about a minute it then reappiered over the treeline. The orb smaller orb then went towards the bigger orb slowly and seemed to go back into the first one. The first orb then started to move in a straite line heading toward Llanelli/Swansea. After it seemed to head over Burry Port it vanished all together. There was no sound coming from the ord's, no smoke trail or trails. It stayed bright while it moved. I know that this was not an aircraft as the firing range was closed and there where no aircrafts near by at all.
