{rjl} Apparition appeared in photo when viewed on cell phone

We were in Dallas going to the medical center. We stopped to ask for directions as I decided to take a picture of the sign to log into my ablum.I snapped the picture and put my cell phone away. Later that night I went to view the pictures I'd taken that day when I noticed on that particular shot a face peering at me. I was shocked because there was nothing on the lawn when I snapped the picture, it scared me when I saw the facial features. I took the picture to my family to show them and we all saw the face in the corner of the picture. We were all amazed and thoughts began to run through our heads. Needless to say I did not sleep that night, fearful of whatelse I might see in the dark. I am still wondering how and why it seemed to be right at the car window looking at me. I have since viewed another face on this picture also, just above the apparitions head.
