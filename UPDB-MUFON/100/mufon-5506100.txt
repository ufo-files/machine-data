{rjl} Star like light moved North, then flashed lights, then disappeared

My fiance and I were walking out to the car, facing South around 6:45PM on December 7th. Something moving very quickly in the sky caught my eye. It was about the size of a large star or planet, moving quickly from the SE toward us. It was moving so fast I assumed it had to be an airplane, but it just wasnt close enough and it didn't have light like an airplane. As it moved toward us (North), it flashed green, red, and white. Then it got noticeably brighter, then it disappeared completely. We both stood in the yard, awestruck. We would have thought it was a meteor or something, but it was moving very much like an aircraft, and not nearly as quickly as any meteor I have ever seen.
