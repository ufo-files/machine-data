{rjl} Star-like light on a straight flight path

A star-like craft appeared out of nowhere while I was stargazing. Headed 44 degrees to the NE. The altitude was much higher than normal planes that might be headed to or from the nearby airports. I watched it intently and after a minute or two it just disappeared. There was no cloud cover that night and the stars all around were readily visible, so I don’t know how it would just appear and disappear. Also the flight speed appeared to be similar to that of a normal aircraft at a much lower altitude so it had to be going pretty fast. At times there are National Guard jets that go to the local airport, but if he was planning on landing at the airport he should have had a much lower altitude and been preparing for his landing. We don’t get a lot of aircraft around here that just fly over us going to other destinations.
