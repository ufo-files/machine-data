{rjl} Orb

I was standing outside my house while looking up at the sky and I see in the northeastern sky what seemed to be the flying disco ball (this is what I call it because of the colors red, yellow, green, blue, and white) which are the colors it puts off while it's hovering and changing directions. I decided to use the zoom in + app that I downloaded from google play store to see if I could zoom in on it but instead, I saw the triangle formation. After taking a picture of that, I went inside and came back out 45 minutes later and tried again to video the "disco ball". It was far up in the sky but was in our atmosphere; I pressed record and zoomed in and that's when I saw the orbs. It was 2 white orbs that were side by side then appeared 3 red orbs in a triangle formation that were pulsating near the previous 2.
