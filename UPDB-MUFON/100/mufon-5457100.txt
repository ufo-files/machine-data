{cak} Approx. 20 white lights, approx. 1 mile long traveling in a straight line equal distance apart

I was swimming at about 7:00 PM and happened to look up into the night sky at the EXACT same location I saw a rotating orange and yellow carousel shaped UFO several years ago. There I saw about 20 white lights in a straight line traveling east, so perfectly spaced I thought it was a banner. But there was no "lead" plane pulling an almost 1 mile long banner! There were 2 brighter lights right behind the others so I thought those must be airplanes trying to catch up with the UFO's. But as one by one the smaller lights disappeared when reaching a specific area in the sky, so did the the last 2 brighter UFO's, obviously not airplanes. It is comical how my brain tries to come up with an explanation before I realize I'm witnessing UFO's, then they disappear!
