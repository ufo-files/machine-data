UFO's flying over Sherwood Park toward Camrose

As I have videoed ufo's in the past and had about 7 close encounters I have my combination scope, night vision and camera set up and ready to go. It is winter in Alberta so the skies are usually overcast. Saturday morning I got up for a glass of water at about 3AM and looked outside. The skies were crystal clear. I turned on my equipment and caught 2 orbs passing over Sherwood Park headed for the town of Camrose. In the fisrt video the ufo glows very bright as if to say, "I see you watching me."
