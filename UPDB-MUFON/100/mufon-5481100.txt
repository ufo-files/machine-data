3 Orange lights in a triangle formation

I was on my way to my friends house as we were going to have a few drinks and as I cycled there I stopped to put on my lights. There was a plane in the distance which was pretty far away as usual with its flashing lights but closer there were 3 orange ish lights that seemed to continue moving without stopping, also they didnt seem to hold a static triangle formation as if they were 1 object so there must have been multiple. I originally thought it must of been some sort of plane but there were 3 of them all with the same orange light. I wasnt really that bothered at the time because I just wanted to have fun, now I come to think of it somebody near me has seen the same very recently. They/It didnt do much apart from move across the sky then I continued on to my friends house which is how I ended up losing it, I tried looking for it when I got to my friends house as it was not so far away but I couldnt seem to find it.
