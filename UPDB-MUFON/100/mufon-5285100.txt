Huge Black Triangle spotted low while driving at night

I was driving across the causeway bridge at about 7 pm . The bridge is about 25 miles long coming from the north shore Mandeville area and headed south toward new Orleans . I was almost to the end and from my right which was the west a large black Triangle seemed to slowly hover or glide in a straight line right in front of my vehicle. It had 2 white lights and one red . the red was at the all of the lights were on the corners .I was with 2 others who saw it first as I was driving and was focused on the road .It slowly spun around .it made no sound and looked as if it were the size of a baseball field . It was thick and it had lines on the bottom like vents . I could only see it for a moment as it passes us and when the lights would pulse . It was not flat at all it almost looked like it could have been open like it had a floor and roof same size triangles and on corners were heald together by columns .. But when it passed I looked to the left ( east ) and I could see more geometric shape to one side of it . Super low ,glided effortlessly ,spun slowly and traveled over a bridge where many people were driving over a lake .
