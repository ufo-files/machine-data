{dr}Red-Orange Orb Similar to Park Ridge Sighting #49239

My partner was outside on the balcony smoking and called me outside to look at an object. It was at 10:55 PM - I remember looking at my clock. The object was bright and reddish orange in color. It came directly overhead at a 90 degree angle, coming from the west going east in a straight line (the balcony faces east). The object was completely silent and unlike typical traffic departing from OHare (no strobes, no sound).

The object was moving under the clouds and then ascended into a cloud. It was no longer visible. 

The sighting lasted about 10 seconds, but it was of note because it was so different from the usual commercial traffic in the area. I would guess that the speed was about the same as a twin engine aircraft, but not slow like a Chinese latern. From our vantage point, it seemed smaller than a commercial jet but bigger than a microwave. It was bigger in appearance than a star but smaller than an aspirin. As it gently ascended, Id almost swear it had a Saturn-like appearance on the right and left sides (probably just an atmospheric illusion). The object was very bright when visible.

I don't know if this is the same object reported in case #49239, but it is similar, though it happened later in the evening.
