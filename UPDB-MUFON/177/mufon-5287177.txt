Burning sphere

Saw a bright orange light while riding on my motorcycle heading east. It is pretty normal to see small single engine aircraft in this area and I always enjoy watching them. 

However, I noticed there were no navigation lights. After 30 seconds or so the object was closer and clearly orange with flames, like the flare on an oil rig but flying. At this point I pulled over and started to record as best as I could with my cellphone. I watched the object with my eyes rather than my phone to get a better look at it. 

As the object moved north at a constant velocity and altitude the flames just kind of "turned off" and what was left looked like a grey metal sphere that was reflecting the horizon light and was silhouetted against the sky. This detail does not show up on the video. I tried to take several pictures instead as the object continued on its path, but the resolution does not show much more than a blur.

The best description of what it looked like would be like a pellet from a shotgun shell. Grey/metallic and perfectly spherical.

The object moved further north passing in front of some clouds, at which point I could not really see it anymore with my eyes.
