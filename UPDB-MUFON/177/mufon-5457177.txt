{cak} Triangle in the sky.

It was firework night here November the 5th, looking South at the rear of the house. Looked up spotted 3 lights in a Triangle, slightly turning, no cloud. Stayed for maybe 8 seconds then faded. Looked large. The Triangle was to perfect to be stars, and they faded and couldn't be seen again.
