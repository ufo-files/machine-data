{cak} Star began moving

I watched a stationary star while looking for shooting stars. A star that was not moving for at least 30 seconds started moving to the left. I thought it was an airplane without flashing lights until it changed direction suddenly from North to NW. To quick for a plane. It moved slower than a plane too. It continued moving for minutes until it was between 2 stars then I knew it was not anything normal because it held position. Just hovered for about 2 minutes. While hovering it dimmed out completely twice. It always came back just like it dimmed out. Then it changed course yet again slowly moving downward. It took over a minute to fall beneath the tree line and would dim out multiple times and reappear exactly where it dimmed out and continue dropping. It dimmed out for good beneath the tree line and never came back. I watched that area of sky for a few minutes but it was gone. Then it was weird because a shooting star shot away from me lower on the horizon than normal. I'm not sure the shooting star was this UFO but may have been. It was NOT a helicopter as it was WAY too high up. No noise was ever made. This is unexplainable even to my fiance who also witnessed this. She does not believe in aliens but admits what we saw was spectacular.
