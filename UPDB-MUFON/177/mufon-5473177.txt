{cak} Two Lights flying unison

Myself and my Daughter, was in our outdoor hotub. We normally look for satellites and constellations. When we noticed Two Star-like lights begin to move. They were directly above our heads, not moving. I was looking right at the lights, thinking they were out of place relative to other constellations, when they started to move. They were moving to the North at a fairly high rate of speed. as they were moving away one of the lights got extremely bright. My daughter said "might be wonder Woman's invisible airplane".(she is nine) My wife came out and watched as it flew away. the lights moved in perfect unison. smooth flight, no bobbles, wobbles, or strange movement. Watched light for approx 1 min.
