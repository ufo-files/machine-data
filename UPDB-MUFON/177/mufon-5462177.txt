Washing dishes in front of kitchen window of condo (3rd fl.) looking SW at around 6PM (EST). Situated near Trudeau International Airport. Planes always in the air around condo. Landing lights of one was in view. I looked at it and then saw an object to the left of the plane that I couldn't clearly make out. I got my binoculars (10x50) and looked. It looked like a black capital letter A with the pont leaning slightly to the left. The portion above the horizontal bar was also solid black and the legs of the letter A were filled in like the 2 bottom arms of a star (Star like on a US warplane). It was floating at a 10 o'clock direction and I lost site of it after about 4 minutes when it went behind a another condo. It seemed just to float. It did not change shape or attitude during the time observed.
Being in the condo I could not hear anything. I went out to the balcony and observed it in the binoculars (no sound) but did not stay long (-8 C.)
