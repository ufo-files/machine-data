I was up before sunrise hunting, and at first light, a geometric pattern of what I thought were lights (star-like in appearance), caught my eye. They moved slowly in unison and tight (but random) formation from west to east (towards the rising sun), without sound, contrails, etc. As the sun rose, I realized that they were not lit, but illuminated and visible by the angle of the rising sun. I lost sight of them as daylight came on.
