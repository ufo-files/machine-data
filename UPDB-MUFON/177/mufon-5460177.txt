{cak} Metallic Humming Bird

This happened back in the mid 90’s. At the time I was 16 years old. It was summer and every weekend I would mow the lawn for my parents. We had a small backyard that was bordered by 4 other yards. All of these yards were separated by a chest high chain link fence. I had just finished mowing the lawn, as I was starting to move some of the lawn furniture back to their place. To my the south I could hear this metallic flapping noise coming from a few houses down. It was loud and it drew my attention right away. When I saw the source of the sound. I could see what I thought was a bird flying 15-20 feet off the ground. It was heading straight towards me. 
As it flew over me and our back yard, it abruptly turned to the east. Almost in a 90 degree turn. All the while it was making this loud mechanical noise. During the few seconds it was directly over me and it was making the sharp turn. It banked over and I caught a very clear look of it. First it was what I thought a very large hummingbird, 12inch wingspan at least. Secondly the feathers or skin was a reflective bronze color. I remember it caught the light and it reflected back like a metallic object. The nimbleness of the way it changed direction was also unusual. When it finished its turn it flew away from me and toward my other neighbors home and disappeared through the trees. Initially I thought that it was remote controlled airplane. But it wasn’t. It was flapping it wings, it navigated though trees and houses and it’s speed and agility was unique. I really don’t know what I saw. But see it and hear I did.
