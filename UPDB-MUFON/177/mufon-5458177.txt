{cak} Hovering white round object (maybe an orb?)

Noticed an object hovering high above just northeast of Peterson AFB very near the Colorado Springs airport’s air traffic flight path. Too far to identify shape or details. At first I thought perhaps it was a white plastic bag that may have been swept up high or a balloon. Then it occurred to me that if it were, at that height it wouldn’t be visible. After about a minute or two of hovering it started to move southwest towards Cheyenne mountain. It was below the high clouds and the sky was mostly clear and sunny. The white object then appeared black as it continued its trek SW. Maybe the sun reflected off of it making it appear white? Or, maybe it was just a white orb? Drone? Maybe too high up to be a drone. It was at least to me, an unidentified flying object. There was no sound, no contrails, and no other aircraft. I filmed a few seconds of it with my iPhone.
