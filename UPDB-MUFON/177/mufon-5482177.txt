{rjl} Thought it was a plane till it moved... like a dragon fly. Covered 10 ft in a blink. Angle turns

It was Saturday evening when my wife and i decided to go out to dinner" outback". We walked down Utica ave in brooklyn about a block to stop at the bank on church ave and Utica ave. Afterwards as we were standing on Utica and linden my wife says.." is that plane standing still?" as i look ready to explain why it appears as if it's not moving i realize it is not. As i looked for signs like a tail rotors.. Wings...i don't see any. Then all of a sudden this object moved in ways I have never seen at that speed or how it would change directions without turning... first it hovered still no movement... then it went left about 7-8 ft only to come back to the right stop....it made what appeared to be a letter C then directly to a broken Z ...as i fumbled to activate my video camera it shot straight out going south bound at speeds far faster then anything I've ever seen... It made no sound.... It was like it went from zero to 1000.... It just vanished. Thank god my wife saw what i saw.. it was unbelievable.
