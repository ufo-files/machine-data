Starlike light moved across the sky. Made several flashes and changed direction and flashed some more.

Starlike light moved across the sky to the East of the Adelaide northern beach area, traveling from North to South. It had a similar illumination and speed to a satellite. It then flashed quite brightly while moving then flashed again less brightly, flashed again brightly. It appeared to turn 1801 and travel back from the direction it came from flashing a couple more times. It then made one almighty bright flash like a giant flashgun, much much brighter than any star, and disappeared.
