erractically flashing colored lights traveling rapididly in straight and zig zag lines

I was out putting up the waterhose and looked up at the crescent moon and noticed these colored lights traveling from the soutwest to the northeast. I watched thinking it a jet when I realized that the lights were flashing erractically and were multicolored. I continued to watch and also noticed that it was not always traveling in a straight line, but at times would zig zag and then go straight and it repeated this behavior until it just vanished before my eyes! As it passed over me I expected to hear a sound indicated I was mistaking a jet for something else, but no sound was ever heard.
