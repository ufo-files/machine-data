saw star like object move slowly increase speed and turn north

I was standing in my driveway when I looked up at the stars. I was looking east at a group of stars above the top of the tree in front of the house when one of the stars began to move west. At first, it moved very slowly and it began to move side to side in a jumping type motion. As it did this It began to turn north in an erratic fashion. I yelled for my wife to come see. She ran up to me and we both saw it quickly speed up and go past the mountains incredibly fast. I was too stunned to take a photo or video.
