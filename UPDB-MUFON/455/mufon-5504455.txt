{nd} We witnessed a humanoid like figure which appeared transparent and moved toward us.

Transparent human like entity seen 30-50 ft from us. A clear outline of the figure could be seen from the head to the mid chest. The figure appeared transparent as light shinning on the bushes directly behind the figure from an adjacent house porch light could be seen. The figure moved toward my friend and I. As I began to step toward the figure, I heard the foot steps of my friend running away. It was at this point tha it occurred to me that moving toward the figure might not be a good idea. I then ran after my friend. We have not seen anything like it again. I am submitting this report because I want to know if anyone has seen anything like this before.
