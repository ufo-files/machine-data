We saw what we thought was one craft, a large v, fly over our house in a northern direction. It had no sound seemed to be going at slower pace and then it broke apart into 5 smaller triangle crafts each with a border of white lights. They continued north just no longer in v formation. If it were planes or helicopters we would have heard it. After they had passed out of sight , I noticed heat lighting to the north possibly because of the crafts.
