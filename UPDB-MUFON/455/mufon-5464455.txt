{cak} Sky glowing red for approximately 15 seconds then several flashes of white light with no sound

Travelling east to work i noticed the sky to the NE light up with a reddish orange glow. The sky had been dark black but i immediately noticed the sky light up and could see a few clouds with a reddish orange sky. The sky glowed reddish orange for approximately 15 seconds but it was obvious that the light was being emitted by a source that i could not see because of the mountain. Suddenly the light went out and the sky returned to black and within seconds i saw what appeared to be a flash of lightning. I continued to see the flash of white light every 10 to 15 seconds with there being approximately 5-7 flashes total. The last two flashes i witnessed i was stopped and standing outside my car and there was no sound. It was 35 degrees F and i am positive it was not heat lightning.
