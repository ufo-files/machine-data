We saw several ufos at the beach in Puerto Rico

Dec 23 while at the beach with two friends we noticed a white orb manifest out above the ocean. We all though it was weird. All white orb. It then seemed to be coming towards us. From where it was to where we were it took about 5 seconds to get to us. Then the white orb went completely dark and then just lit up in all red lights going up. When that happened we could see the shape of a black oval object. From there it seemed to know we were watching it and it out on. Light show for us as it hovered over the ocean. We were in awe. My buddy said man I wished we would’ve recorded it. At that instant another orb appeared. This time I recorded it. Again it came towards us and at that point I said it knows we’re watching it and the object blinked twice. In the video you can see the object stop and just hover over the water
