{rjl} Battle of Mars

Dec.5 2015 around 5 am est south of Boston Mass. I was working, I went outside for a break. I was looking up(like I always do)in the area of Jupiter, and saw a [dim star} flash of white light in my field of view. I moved my eyes over to where the flash was and saw another flash. Now I'm looking right at them.In the area of 7 o'clock of Mars I saw 4 more flashes,(not timed, 5-15 seconds between flashes} all stationary. I held my arm out, pointing finger extended, the flashes were fingertip to 1st knuckle away from 7 o'clock of Mars. Then the flashes changed color to (not sure)and moved closer to Mars 3-5 o'clock of Mars and I saw 3 more flashes. Again not timed,1st flash 5 o'clock, fingernail distance away from Mars,2nd,3 o'clock closer to Mars, 3rd flash almost over Mars, maybe another flash after that, not sure, I had to blink. Then I had to go back to work.
 I've spent a lot of time looking up over the last 20-30 yrs., I've seen many interesting things. This was not a helicopter. These flashes were not in earth's atmosphere or orbit, they were VERY VERY far away. This is not a joke or a hoax, and not my imagination. I couldn't find anything on line that could confirm this. I was hoping maybe your organization could find a telescope that might have been watching Mars that evening. If yes, please send me a video of this event. I WANT TO SEE THAT AGAIN, because I think I saw just the end of this event   thank you for your time
