Bright vivid pulsing multicolored object

It was me and my daughter in the car, driving Northbound I-5 through a darkened quarter between Ferndale and the Border when we noticed a bright multicolored light in the sky. Its Structure almost appeared as a screen, but also appeared as a cluster of 4 orbs but maintained formation. Light and color passed across these orbs or screen without ever blinking off. It emitted colors of red,orange, bright blue, white, and green. Sometimes more than one color appearing at once. We watched it get closer in the sky to us, almost hovering at the same altitude while moving across the sky. Never seen anything like this and we have a lot of aircraft flying around patrolling the border. This was NOT a Canadian/US border patrol or a plane. Only thing similar Ive ever seen to it was on UFOs Best Evidence Ever Caught. There has been a lot of weird lights around her lately... Dont know what to think about a lot of them.
