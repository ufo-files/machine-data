{jpg} Rocking UFO

I was with my mother, my little brother and her friend at the time grocery shopping. They had finished putting the groceries in the car when we heard people in the parking lot talking loudly and the sound of worry. My mom and her friend started saying "What is that!" over and over when I looked towards the windshield from the back seat I saw a very bright light rocking back and fourth like in a small U shape. I have never seen anything like that in my b entire life at that point. I remembered being extremely scared. My mom drove off very fast and I tried to hide in the backseat as much as I could. My brother saw it too and remembers that night and says to this day it was in the local newspaper.
