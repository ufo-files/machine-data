I was traveling east on a gravel road in southern Colorado. I notice what i thought was a cop car really moving the thin strip of light that was US 280. The next second it was hovering behind a farm house over some equipment. I passed the house and now the pulsating bright blue light is about 200 yards away. I think this must be an airport. The the next moment it was tracking me at about 50 MPH. It was about 150 feet away for a while, maybe 90 seconds, keeping pace. Then it flashed a three points just twice, about a 6 foot triangle in vertical plane, and the next blink it was 40 to 50 feet away. Now I went from curious to scared. forgot to get the phone out to take a pic. 20-30 seconds elapsed a then it was just gone.
