Very reflective stationary object

At 1800 EDT my husband and I observed a very reflective object through a thin layer of clouds Northeast of our position. It was absolutely stationary. It was bright enough to see easily with the naked eye. My husband got the binoculars while I kept an eye on it. I found the object again with the binoculars and watched the object for several minutes. It never moved. I did not have to move my arms at all with the binoculars. The object was gum drop shaped, silver with 2 circular areas in the bottom third that were extremely reflective and unwavering. Object was then concealed by cloud cover which passed in about 10 minutes at which point the object was no longer there.
