I saw through my window in the early evening a large(fast traveling) oval shape flying

I was sitting on the couch in our den last night watching TV. Right behind my TV is a large window without curtains etc; we live in the country. My wife was sitting to the left in the recliner. The night sky, that I saw through the window was dark; it was approximately 8:45pm. The object I saw was extremely bright and very large. It appeared to be solid,almost circular, more like a oval shape. It was traveling extremely fast. I only saw it for about 3 seconds; but definitely long enough to realize it was not a star, it was not a meteor(no trail). It disappeared in the clouds almost instantly; with no signs of light behind the clouds.

After I saw the object, of course I was telling my wife while it happened; she was not sitting in the view of the object and did not see it. I went outside immediately to my back porch to see if there was anything else in the sky that was unusual. I did not see anything. About ten minutes later I did notice several airplanes in the sky that appeared to be flying same altitude, etc...more than likely..nothing. What I did find weird! I tried to call my brothers to tell them of my experience, and I had no signal on my cell phone; although my wife did. I have Verizon service and see has T-mobile..I called Verizon; using her phone and I was told there was outage in numerous areas in North Carolina..Might have been a coincidence! Then again, maybe it wasnt!
