In the eastern sky from my outside deck in East Rutherford, NJ, my fiance and I observed 2 bluish glowing orb like spheres moving across the night sky from left to right. Our deck looks eastward at the New York city skyline. One orb was circling the other the entire time. After 20 seconds a plane came into view and the orbs must have been in clear view of the plane's pilot. The flight in our view lasted just less than one minute. This is the second UFO sighting in the last couple years from the same vantage point. This time the orbs were farther away.
