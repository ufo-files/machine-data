I was watching this strange object in the sky looked like a blimp it disappeared.

I was at the Lake Alliance in Potterville/Charlotte Mi. I was in the parking lot of the disc golf and my boyfriend was talking to these people I was in the car and i remember looking at the sky and it started doing all this weird pulsating patterns. I thought i was tripping out or had low blood sugar or my contacts needed to be washed. I looked at the man my boyfriend was talking to and next to his head i seen an object in the sky. It was very strange i went thru in my mind all the things that it could be. It was not an airplane it stayed in one spot and shifted in kind of like a square pattern, i wanted to show the people i was with but i didn't want to take my eyes off of it and when i finally did it disappeard.
