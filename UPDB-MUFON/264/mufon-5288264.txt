150’ diameter fireball silent moving across lake then stopped and became transparent

At approx 3am a large fireball approx 150-200’ in length- and at least 60’ tall traversed across the lake and came to a stop. It was silent and there was no wake from it. It went black. I got the binoculars and shut off all the house lights. 30 sec. Suddenly there was a large round white light. Thru the binoculars I could see that the vessel was still there but was now outlined in pale bluish green lights outlining the entire structure. It was at least 4 stories high with spires of various heights rising above the main structure. It looked transparent. The large white light moved away from the vessel- best guess estimate 40’. Was there about 10-15
Minutes then moved back to the outlined vessel and was extinguished as The b/g lights changed and the vessel took on a red/yellow color.- less transparent. Two shafts of red/yellow light extended up into the sky from one of the spires. The shafts of light had dashes in them, appearing as though they were moving skyward. This took about 7-10 minutes. Then the fireball appearance returned and the vessel moved back towards where I was standing and then veered off to my right behind the tree line. It covered a mile in about 3 minutes. I was unable to see any lights or glow thru the trees or reflected from the sky so I am assuming it ascended or disappeared. There was never any sound or any wake -not even a ripple from its movement.
Unfortunately the phone photo does not show the intensity of yellow red fireball light.
