{td} Glowing orb near Denver

I was travelling North on I-25 from Castle Pines to Northglenn. I noticed 2 glowing orbs in the sky North East of my position (approx. Orchard Road Exit). One of the orbs had a reddish hue the other was glowing white and had an oscillating flicker. These 2 objects were the only 2 objects visible. No other stars/planets were visible at that time. That struck me as strange so I continued watching the orbs. There was a car accident on I-25 northbound just south of Denver and I ended up stuck in traffic due to the highway closing for cleanup. As I was watching the 2 objects the oscillating white object ascended at a high rate of speed and disappeared.
