{cak} Appeared suddenly from nowhere

In 1965 I was an elementary school student in a rural community. During recess, a huge object materialized directly above us. Playground monitors hurriedly moved students inside. We were shepherded so quickly 1 girl ran into a closed door hitting her nose hard enough to cause nosebleed. I KNOW I did not dream this yet cant find ANY info ANYWHERE. I'm 64 now. I had numerous experiences of accompanied free flight at the friendly invitation of an indescribable entity until adolescence. Each time we went further into space. I could literally turn to see the Earth the size of a golf ball! The last time, I got scared when I saw how far from Earth we had flown and was given the option to continue or return. When I chose return, the entity reminded me it would be "painful". Physical effect much like a horrifying roller coaster. I'm RH Negative. My eyes mutate color. I have remitting-relapsing MS.
