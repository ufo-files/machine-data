{cak} Orb of light seen in southwest sky in Coral Springs Florida.

I went outside to have a cigarette and as I always do I observe the air traffic of Ft. Lauderdale exec., Ft. Lauderdale/Hollywood Intl. and further to the south Miami Intl. I immediately noticed three separate aircraft, one travelling west, one headed east, and the third one moving northwest. The fourth object was stationary and blue/green in color. My first thought was is that is a blimp. But was immediately confused when the object/orb of light rose quickly and changed from blue/green to magenta/redish in color. I kept telling myself its not real its only something other people see, or something you see in a movie. I kept observing for a couple of more mins, and then ran inside to tell my wife and children to come see this strange light in the sky. They all came outside to see what I was seeing. They all were calm and were trying to give it a rational explanation. But I can tell you as a lifelong observer of aircraft this is not something I have ever witnessed before.Being a professional photographer I ran to get my camera and tripod, set up and began to make a few frames. Attached is just a few out of the total of 33 I took before the object/orb decended behind a building.
