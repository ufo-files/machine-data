Cigar shaped craft flying northeast

Locking door as I left for work. I look up my driveway and see this strange row of lights in the sky. I realize it’s moving. There is absolutely no sound. No birds chirping at all. No sound emitted from the craft. It is cigar shaped with no wings and dark, heading North east at an extremely slow speed. No blinking lights like a passenger jet or plane. As well, it’s longer than a passenger jet. Down the side are bright headlights, like you’d see on a car.
