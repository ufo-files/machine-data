{cak} Series of lights Hovering towards the southwest

Myself and my sister both observed, at first, only 2 pulsating lights heading northwest to southwest. about 30 seconds after the first two, we observed 4 more lights, all in a straight line path. shortly thereafter, we observed 2 more lights following behind the other 4, only these 2 changed position with one another. The continued southwest, moving very slowly before disappearing suddenly two at a time.
