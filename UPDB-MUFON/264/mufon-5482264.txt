{cak} Object observed from I-5 swiftly flying south

Object in the sky observed by myself from vehicle (Oregon, I-5 south just north of Salem) where I was a passenger. The object in question (which I thought at first to be a plane) was moving quite quickly south. I soon realized it was not a plane as I noted it was traveling much faster than one would, and there was no contrail observed. It maintained height above the low clouds, and at one point seemed to flash; although, it appeared more a reflection than a flash. I looked down at my phone to note the time and when I looked up it was gone.
