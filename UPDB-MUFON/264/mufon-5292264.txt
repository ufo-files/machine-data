2 light orbs, paralyzed, levitated, clicking noise

Winter night, log cabin in the woods outside Birchwood, WI. I was alone, laying in bed , heard clicking music and multiple voices. Tried to get up to check radio but was paralyzed. Tried to get up and was paralyzed. Again I used my minds inner strength to break away, falling to floor.
