{tjd} Bright green light appeared, descended quickly and then dissappeared

While sitting stationary in my patrol car in the parking lot of the Woodbridge Elementary School in Greenwood, Delaware, I was facing east so that I could observe both north and southbound lanes of traffic of US Route 13. I was looking at my phone to read a message that had come in and had happened to glance up to observe traffic. I was sitting with both my front drivers and passengers windows open because it was uncomfortably warm in my car. 

As I looked up I noticed a bright green light appear and then watched as it rapidly descended towards the tree line across the northbound side of the highway. I at first thought that it might be fireworks, as I had seen some fired off in that area a couple weeks prior. However, as I watched it rapidly fall and then disappear, I realized that there were no other lights around it, that it had not made any noise, and that it appeared as it was falling towards the ground, not as it ascended, which is how fireworks normally appear. 

The duration of the sighting was no more than 2 seconds. I looked up, saw the bright green light appear, shoot down towards the ground, and then disappear just as fast as it had appeared.
