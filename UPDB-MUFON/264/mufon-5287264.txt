Large white light passed over moving vehicle

While driving home, a large white light passed over me just offset to the right of my vehicle. It was traveling in the same direction as my vehicle. I was traveling at approx. 60 mph and the object was traveling at least twice the speed of my vehicle. It appeared to be 100-200 feet away from my vehicle at its closest discernible distance. The light did not seem to be projected onto the ground and it shut off very abruptly once it got out in front of me. There were no other lights associated with the craft and it seemingly disappeared once the light ceased. My cell phone was not functioning properly before and after the incident. My radio was turned off so I was not able to determine any kind of RF interference. Vehicle continued to operate properly.
