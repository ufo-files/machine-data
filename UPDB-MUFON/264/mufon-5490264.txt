My boyfriend and I were out at what was to be the best time to see the meteor shower. Being a photographer I have A Canon 60d and was using a lens 50mm f1.4 and am able to clearly capture stars. as it was approaching 4 and we were getting ready to go in I was looking at the many stars that appear as pairs. I have always found them out of place so admiring them was not uncommon. As all of a sudden the pair of stars I was looking at one of the just look like it took off. I was so shocked I couldn't speak I hit record on my camera which was already facing in that direction because the meteors were supposed to be coming from the north east. I have actually seen with my boyfriend on multiple occasions objects moving seamlessly across the sky, only this time I saw the beginning it was a star, it was stationary for a very long time I have had that duo of stars in past night pictures I have taken but this time I had my camera ready. and filmed this object move clear across the night sky until it vanished. We're in still in shock. And I would like to add that I did not enhance this video in any way.
