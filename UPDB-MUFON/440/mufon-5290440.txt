Multiple objects travelling faster than satelites.

Multiple objects traveling faster than satellites over a 31 minutes period. Directions were W-E, NW-SE, N-S, S-N traveling at incredible speeds, normal satellites in our area travel S-N. The glowing fast movers seemed to be at the same elevation as the satellites. The local airport is 20 km away and most aircraft fly W-E at 20 degrees looking from my backyard to the North. There was some air traffic but due to Covid no where near as much. The fast movers my wife and i watched were 3-4 minutes apart, West to East & South to North mainly but moving incredibly fast compared to the Satellites (I would say 8-10 times faster). They started around 9:05pm and stopped at 9:40pm, 14 in all. We have lived here over 3 years, we love to sit and watch the stars and count satellites at night, we have never seen these fast movers before.
