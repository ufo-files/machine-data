On Jekyll Island, Georgia, on August 9th 2013 around 8:30pm I witnessed 2 orangish/white lights hover over ocean then disappear one by one from outside towards center.

On Friday, August 9th 2013 , between 8pm and 9pm , (Best estimate is 8:30pm as it was right around sunset) my family and I were walking back from the beach to our hotel room on Jekyll Island , Georgia. My nephews mother stopped me as we were walking and asked what is that in the sky? I glanced up real quick and saw a couple of lights close to eachother& parallel & I said its a helicopter and kept walking. She then said, "but its not moving".My mom, her and I then stared up at it. Not only was it not moving, lights were not flashing & it made no noise. I saw 2 circular lights hover. They were larger than any star and glowed/pulsed a orangish/white light. One by one the lights dissappeared from the outside towards the center. There may have been a third light that had disappeared because my mom thought she saw 3 at first. It all happened very quick,within 30 seconds or so.
