{tjd} Red/orange pulsating stationary light until it accelerated and changed direction and light pattern

I live in York Haven, Pennsylvania. The location of my house is about 3 miles south of TMI near the Susquehanna river. I smoke and went outside on my back porch. I see many strange things in the sky since I moved here in 2014. 
Shortly after lighting my cigarette, I notice an orb-like light hovering in the night sky at my 11 o'clock position. It wss less than 2000 ft off the ground. I watched it for a good 30 minutes. It pulsated a red--orange glow and it remained pretty steady in the same place the whole time. It made no noise. I ran inside and grabbed my phone to record what I wss seeing.
This is the crazy part.
As soon as I hit record, it appeared that it knew I was recording and started to move eastward and as I focused in, as you can see in the video, it appears to accelerate very quickly and ascend while changing its light pattern to a white and red alternating flash. As if were trying to mimic FAA lights.
Harrisburg airport and Harrisburg International airport are close to my house(one alittle north and one west of TMI) and I see many, many aircraft in our area
However, this was no plane. Maybe military? Fort Indiantown Gap is north of Harrisburg and I do see military aircraft all the time, day and night. I was hesitant to report this until this past Friday night, July 12, 2019. I apologize that it took a year to file this.
