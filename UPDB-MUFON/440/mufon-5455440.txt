just gliding around over tree tops near pond it had lights blinking around it one after another not all at same time there was an older man with me fishing who drove me to pond I ran over to him in other side of pond and told him what I saw by then we could not see it anymore so the pond was st the end of a cow pasture this was farm land not many people living there so we had to walk about 300 yards up a hill thru pasture to the truck we were leaving anyway right before I saw it sd we started down the country rd I saw it again hovering over a tobacco field we stopped the truck then the man with me who was a friend of family saw it to he had since long ago passed away now when we stopped to look at it I got out of truck and was about 150 yards from it that’s when I saw one side of it
