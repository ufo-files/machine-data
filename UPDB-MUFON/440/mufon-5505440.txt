{cak} red fire-like balls traveling the same direction one after another, sometimes 2 or 3 at a time

i went to take the trash out around 10pm on jan.13,2014. walking back up the driveway i looked up at the sky and saw 3 red balls traveling at the same speed in the same direction, from south-east to north. so i watched to see if they were planes. they were not. they didn't blink, they were flying at a slower, steady pace at a lower distance. they disappeared and then 2 came from the same place and started traveling the same direction as the first 3. i went inside and told my husband and we went out into out backyard to see if there were more. there were, but now single balls were traveling. as soon as one would disappear another would appear. it was also strange to see a low flying, relatively quiet jet slowly flew over coming from the east and heading west. then all activity subsided. i think it was a governmental plane checking out the scene. my feeling about the sighting was amazed. i always watch ufo shows on tv and have always been intrigued by the possibility of there being ufos. never thought id get to witness them myself.
