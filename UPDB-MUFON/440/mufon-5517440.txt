{ck} Burning light that hovered over city then dissapated.

It was the 4th of July in Lees Summit, MO and we saw a burning ball of light that almost looked like one of those Chinese lanterns. It hovered and came right to us, within 100 feet then took off in the distance and disappeared. We got into Independence, MO and saw them all over the sky about 5 of them. We stopped at a stop sign and one was hovering over a house and again it was the same thing we seen in Lees Summit, it was a burning ball of fire it looked like with a slight tail behind it. We then drove towards our house and seen yet another in the distance and decided to follow it out of town. As we followed it, it kept getting higher and higher in the sky and started to pulsate light and then disappeared. We then went home and stood out on our back deck with binoculars and seen that the object definatly had a tail, was a burning ball of fire almost, and it looked to have a "eye" that was robotic that was looking all around! We watched about 5 more within the hour go west of our house and get higher in the sky, headed north and they would start pulsating and finally burn out high in the sky.
