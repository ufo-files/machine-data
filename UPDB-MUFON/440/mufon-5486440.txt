I was out ufo hunting in Manhattan, with my phone camera. I had a feeling that if I walked around in East Harlem where I live that I might see something. I live on 117th and Lex so I walked south on 3rd Ave first. I saw several fast moving strange lights, but I couldn't capture them on camera. They were too fast. Then at 109th I walked over a block to Lexington Ave. there was a strange whiteness in the clouds and this was at night. I tried to take a photo of it, and the photo data on my camera says the flash was off, but there was a big flash of light when I took the picture, which came out looking like a black saucer that I have seen in daylight before, but did not have a camera handy.i saw it once in Colorado, and had two really good daytime sightings in New York. This photo really surprised me, and it still don't know what it is.
