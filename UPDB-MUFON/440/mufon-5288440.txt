Hovered over my cottage dock

Came out of my cottage for a piss and saw the object above
my dock. Called my witness outside. Went down to the dock

About 75 feet long and looked like the body of a 747 plane.
After 8 min it moved across the lake and before it
hit the high hills it vanished.
