{cak} Large bright pulsating red light that moved close to my car changed direction & shot off into the night

As we were driving home at night, through the countryside from Dryden to Groton Ny, a very large glowing red light was above the treetops. We decided to go in its direction. We couldnt believe its size. Turned onto another road to get closer to it. It got closer/larger. We drove closer & it began to pulsate and move. We sat silent, opened windows & heard nothing. We watched as it changed brightness enough to notice. Neither the huge light or our car moved..like it knew we were watching it. It began to get smaller as if to asend up but it began to move to our left/NE then back to us..we drove a little bit and then it came back towards us. Scared, we stopped the car and watched. It got larger again and then pulsate more. Then it shot straight up then off to the east/south & disappeared completely. We were so alarmed & had so much adrenaline that we forgot to take our phones out. We came home and told our families. We didn't know who to tell. Tonight I was watching History channel explaining how we can report sightings. I'm so happy to be able to share my experience with the right people.
