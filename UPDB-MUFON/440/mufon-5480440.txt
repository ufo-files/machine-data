{nd} Noticed object-flashed strobe flashlight at it-that's when it began the color light show lasting 10mins

Stepped out in backyard at dusk and looked to the east/northeast toward St Louis City less than 10 miles away when I first noticed unusually bright and larger than normal light just hovering. So I grabbed My strobe flashlight & laser pointer-as soon as I flashed the object it began putting on a color light show & that's when I grabbed My iphone to record video. During analysis of video by zooming in and taking screen grabs I noticed incredible colors & icons changing in split seconds. Towards end of video there is a smaller color changing craft approaching the larger original craft-then they took off to the north in an instant. This isn't the first time I have witnessed these lights in the same general direction.
