{tjd} What looked like a distant star began to move up and down and sideways, very smooth and slow

Going outside with my dog around 9:00 pm 4/10/2019, I stopped to get my eyes acclimatized to the night sky, and began seeing stars and the moon. Out of the corner of my eye, a small or distant star, began moving. I watched it for about 25 minutes moving slowly and smoothly up and down, backwards and sideways. It dimmed for a time, but the light got brighter again. I've never seen anything like this! I was awestruck.
