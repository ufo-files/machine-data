{cak} My girlfriend and I witnessed a streetlight size bright white light from a mile away suddenly appearing and descending (faster than object falling) straight down and disappearing before hitting ground, and without a tail trail...

My girlfriend and I were driving West on Little Blue Parkway towards where highway 40 and highway 470 intersect in Independence, MO at approximately 11:50pm on Wed. 11/14/18... maybe a mile away at the most. We both witnessed an extremely bright white round light the size of a streetlight (but a mile away) suddenly appear while descending straight down from less than a mile off the ground, and then disappear again before hitting the ground. It was shooting straight down faster than anything that would be just falling, all within ~3 seconds. Since it was just a big bright light shooting straight down at a 90 degree angle, and without any sort of tail trail of fire/smoke, or the shape of any object that would be merely falling slower (on fire), we both instantly ruled-out a jumbo shooting star, or a descending meteor/commit, or an airplane/helicopter on fire... as they would all be falling/shooting at an angle and descending somewhat sideways until either hitting the ground or disappearing into the sky. This light flashed on big and bright just as fast as it flashed back off again. I quickly recapped and analyzed the entire event with her while it was all still fresh in our minds, as I have personally witnessed two other sightings in Overland Park, KS and reported both of them here on MUFON, as well as on NUFORC...
