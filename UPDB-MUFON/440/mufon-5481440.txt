{cak} really bright lights hovering then just disappeared

Driving south on M 1 highway in Gladstone, Missouri with my niece.We both noticed real bright lights in the sky southwest of us. We were talking about how it was just hovering with no blinking lights like airplanes or helicopters have.It just sat in one spot. We were watching it for maybe two minutes joking that it might be a ufo then it just disappeared. It never moved and there were no clouds at all in the sky. It just DISAPPEARED! We were shocked and couldn't believe what we had just seen.
