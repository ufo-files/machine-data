{rjl} Orb appears every single night lately. Looking at over the pacific ocean from oceanside or san clemente. Resembles large white star but it often moves

Others and myself see this orb almost every single night in the same spot in the sky. Looking out over the ocean from beach in Oceanside ca, you can see it up in the night sky. Looks like a huge star but glows much brighter. Usually returns to same spot every night. Have witnessed balls of light detach from object and then re group with object. No idea what it could be. No sound emitted. Sometimes will change path direction with no previous motion in that direction. baffled. Stays in sky till about 8:45-9:00 pm every night. West direction over pacific ocean.
