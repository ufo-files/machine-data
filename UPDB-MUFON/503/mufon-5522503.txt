witnesses bright blue flashing ufo on hwy 166 in central ca

My wife and i were on our way to san simion ca, for a one week vacation. We left los angeles about 430 am, at about 6am, we started making our way west on hwy 166 in central ca, it was still very dark, and hwy 166 is a very desolate country road, i noticed up ahead about half a mile, a flashing brilliant blue light, i thought it was just.
