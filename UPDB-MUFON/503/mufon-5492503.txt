{rjl} Very erratic blinking and arc type light that shot off object at one point.

I came home from my daughters dance recital. I saw a skunk in my yard so I went outside to see it. I like to look at the stars as I sometimes see shooting stars. This was different. Weird blinking and what looked like sparks shooting off of it. It followed a linear path at a steady speed what seemed slower then an airplane but way higher. I have never seen anything that I can compare with.
