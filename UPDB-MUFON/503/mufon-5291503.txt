5 Orbs Flying in a Boomerang Formation

It was about 13 months since my prior sighting, never given a second thought to what I had seen the 4th of July a year before. This time, it was 11 pm, I don't remember the exact day in August 2012. I had taken my dogs out the front of my townhouse, which faces west/west of south. I had noticed 5 red/red-orange orbs again. My dogs continued to mull around and do their business. As I was watching the orbs, they seem to fly or hover as one object as there was nothing but black wedge, similar to a B2. There was no way to tell how big the shape was or how high as there wasn't anything in the sky to give it a frame of reference. It was just eerily floating from the NW to the SW. In this instance, I just went back in the house as I was a bit freaked out by the fly by. No sound, no type of discharge to give off signs of traditional man made propulsion.
