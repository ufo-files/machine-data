{jpg} 3 bright round orbs in triangle formation slowly moving from my left to my right for 30 seconds until I lost sight behind trees.

I walked into my backyard in Palos Verdes that overlooked the ocean as I did all the time. I noticed a group of 3 bright lights in a triangle formation slowly floating in the distance. My home sits at 1,500 feet above sea level and this object was probably close to 1,000 feet above me. I closely watched this trio fly from left to right until I lost sight of them behind some trees. I also witnessed the same 3 orbs when I was 6 or 7 years old. Also in palos verdes, also during the day and lasting around 30 seconds. I haven't seen them since. Hope this helps. James
