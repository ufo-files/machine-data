UFO over I20 in Arlington, TX

I was on my home from 24 Hour Fitness off Cooper street and took interstate 20 home, as I was approaching the exit I noticed a bright white light in the sky but it looked kind of low and I just assumed it was an airplane. I really did not think anything of it until I observed this light then immediatley turn red, then green and blue. As I was coming up to the light on Park Springs I observed the object fly over me where I notice that it was not an airplane and was more cigar shaped. I then turned around to try and follow the object but by the time that happened which was a matter of seconds, the object was already gone.
