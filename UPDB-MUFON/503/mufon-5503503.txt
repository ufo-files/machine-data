Weird craft hovers for over 10 minutes

I was looking at the sky as i always do, when i look the other side, i saw what i think look like a triangular or delta shapped craft.

It kept still for 6 minutes and then It took off in slow speed. 
Took photos. 
Dont know what It was, but defenitely it' was not an usual plane.
