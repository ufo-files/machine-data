{cak} white pulsating light

I had gone out on the porch to let the dogs do their business when I looked up and noticed the light moving from my right to left. I watched it and thought it was an airplane when it moved across in front of me. The object was probably 5 to 6 thousand feet up and moving at what I thought was an average speed, nothing really attention getting. All of a sudden a yellow portal opened up in front of it and it went through and it closed behind it. I have thought about this for 3 years and wonder if anyone else has ever seen anything like this. I am not crazy!!!!
