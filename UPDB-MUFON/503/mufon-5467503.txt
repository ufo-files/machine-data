{dr}Bright light impossibly far away moving at an impossible speed.

I walked out my families house and looked up at the stars, then a bright light shot from the northeast to the southwest. I thought it to be a comet because of its speed but then realized it didn't have a trail or tail. In less about a second it crossed the whole sky and was out of sight. I've saw meteor showers and shooting stars and this thing had a higher speed. It was very high up, maybe out of our atmosphere.
