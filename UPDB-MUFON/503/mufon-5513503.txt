{nd} Amber colored light

My wife and I were driving west on I-10, when we saw an amber colored light moving in the sky, along the right side of the interstate. (The object would be moving north to south.) As my wife drove, I saw the object first; it looked like a helicopter. However, there were no other lights on the object, it made no jet/prop sounds, and it moved slowly. The color of the object was unlike any helicopter or plane Ive ever seen... it seemed glow like a vacuum tube, or a very old light bulb. 
When we noticed it, the object seemed to be following the road. It seemed to slow and stop in the sky a few times, but, it moved in an unbroken, unwavering path. It didn't shoot across the sky, or make any odd movements. I kept an eye on the light, and watched it as it moved further toward the south side of town. I noticed a few things; at a distance, the light couldve been mistaken for a star, but, it was moving in front of cloud cover. Also, an airplane flew near the general area of the object, which made the difference all the more apparent in comparison. The airplane had flashing green/white/red lights, while the object was an unblinking solid amber light. I lost sight of it after it had moved further south of the city.
