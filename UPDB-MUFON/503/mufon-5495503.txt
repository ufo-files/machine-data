{nd} Large triangle floated right overhead and took video

As I locked up after work, I notice a white light to the west with a red pulsating below it. Took my phone to start recording video. It had a larger white light on the front(it was shaped like a triangle with the corners not connected) and 3 white lights in a triangle formation with a red pulsating light in the center. My phone picked up white light at first but as it was overhead only the red light can be seen. I don't understand because it was so clearly seen with the naked eye. It was very big. It floated right over west to east in a straight line without any noise.
