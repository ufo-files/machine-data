Brightest light I have ever seen white/ ice blue.

we were driving home on the freeway i'5 from Tacoma wash. going north at 1:30 in the am,march 21st sunday morning about 6 miles from the south center mall exit, my husband was driving and over to the left over the trees about 10 miles away we noticed falling from the sky vary vary fast shooting straight down a vary bright ice blue/white maybe the size of a volkswagen car u.f.o.We were both screaming oh my god do you see that!! then it was gone, my daughter was in the back seat and are screaming woke her, she didn't see it,i called my mom and uncle & told them what we saw, we were talking about it all the way home, when we got to exit 7 just before the Newcastle exit, at 1:45 we saw on the side of the freeway going south lined up about 5 army trucks 1 lane was shut down and a army helicopter hovering over head, we were so excited, and nervous, and we will never forget it.
