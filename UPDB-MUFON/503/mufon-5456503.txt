{cak} My best friend and I were out looking at stars.

Myself and Brittany went outside with her dogs when we let them out and around the same time, she and I noticed something that was a little brighter and seemed to be moving. So I asked her “am I crazy or did I just see that move?” She confirmed she had thought it moved too. We are both look up at the sky kind of humans. We continued to watch it and the object continued to move in different direction. We had been watching it for maybe 10 minutes before we started filming. When we started filming, we noticed all the different colors and the strange movement of the object. We couldn’t get over how awesome it was. The feeling of being a little kid again with big smiles on our faces, that no one could wipe away, no matter how hard they tried. We never lost sight of the object. We live in Ohio and it was an 18° December night here, we got too cold to stay outside. We also saw what seemed to be the same object the very next night. That one stayed around equally as long. This has been one of the most amazing experiences of our lives.
