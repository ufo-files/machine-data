A ball of light thought to be a star moves erratically and then disappears into itself.

Never told anyone this due to the fear of being ridiculed but in 2010, me and my girlfriend at the time witnessed a UFO. we had just grabbed food and stopped in a parking lot to eat. She mentioned her step dad saying mars was supposed to be visible that evening. We see what we think is mars and after a few moments it moves, as if it knew we were staring at it. The reddish orange ball starts to move to right at a high speed and then randomly changed direction to the left and upwards. It then comes to a complete stop and seems to implode or disappear into itself as if it was never there. The most amazing thing ive ever seen.
