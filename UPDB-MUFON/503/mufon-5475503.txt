{nd} Stationary sphere

I was driving from clermont Indiana to Brownsburg doing a delivery service app called doordash and I noticed a black disc in the sky. At first I thought it was a hot air balloon, but it never moved an inch for about 15-20minutes. I turned off of 136 heading to my home, observed what I thought was a hot air balloon still hadn't moved. I decided to drive closer to the object and stopped at a church parking lot, where I took a picture and recorded a short video. I got another delivery and had to drive to Avon Indiana, by the time I came back it was gone.
