Bright green orb in the sky in the middle of the day

I was driving down the road with 2 friends who were arguing with each other. frustrated with their conversation turned to look out the drivers side window to my surprise I see an unbelievable sight. a massive green orb in the sky at first I couldn't believe my eyes. I thought it was a balloon until I realized that it was easy a mile up in the sky and stationary. Then the orb began moving slight to the right and down slightly then makes the same maneuver but to the left side. After these maneuvers the orb flashed away leaving only a green tracer in the bright sunny blue sky
