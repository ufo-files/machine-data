{rjl} Black shaped Triangle about 25-30 feet long with 3 unblinking red lights.

In the summer of 2007 when I was 26 years old myself and a friend were sitting out by my pool at about 2am, we were talking and relaxing when suddenly there came a large black object from out of the woods directly behind my house. The object made a strange humming type of noise, it seemed to have taken off in the woods and started to climb steadily above the tree line until we both observed the object at a height of about 60 feet or so above my house. The object was moving much slower than a plane would fly. Perhaps only about 10 miles an hour in speed. It was a large black triangle, it seemed to me to be about as long as my swimming pool -32 feet long, under the aircraft I could clearly see 3 red lights as clear as day. The object was observable in the air for only about 40 seconds. It simply came out of the woods behind my house slowly flew over my house at a height of perhaps 60 feet made a turn and seemed to the back towards the woods.
