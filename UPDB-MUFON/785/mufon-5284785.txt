Unidentified black triangle discovered while reviewing photos

I am 31 years old, from Wilmington, N.C.
Occupation: Airport Operations Supervisor & Aircraft Rescue Firefighter.

As an Airport Operations Supervisor a HUGE part of my job is ensuring that our FAA part 139 certificated Airport (runways,taxiways,lighting,signage,navigational aids,etc) is always in compliance with the standards set by the FAA…around the clock. 
I work 3 overnight shifts and 2 morning shifts each week. Fast forward to the sighting..On Thursday October 14th at exactly 3:47am while I was inspecting the work my team had completed the day before on the primary windsock (we installed new led lights and replaced the actual “orange sock” . I took 3 photos (within 10secs). Later on while reviewing the photos I noticed what I consider to be a triangular shaped ufo/uap. I immediately felt a cold chill slowly roll up from the base of my spine all the way up to the top of my head. This is not the first time I have witnessed strange and unexplained objects in the sky BUT this is the first time I’ve managed to capture a photo of something without even realizing it in the moment. I will post all 3 photos that I took that morning between 3:47-3:48a, I’ll include the time stamps. 
There was absolutely no commercial or general aviation air traffic in the area at the time. (The first commercial departure would occur @ 5:19a) 
There was no sound , zero radio traffic over the CTAF. (The radio Frequency used by all area air traffic, including airport vehicles operating on the airfield) 
I can’t explain it.
