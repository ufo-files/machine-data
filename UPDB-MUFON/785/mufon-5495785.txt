{rjl} Unusually clear water for mid Atlantic, sunset pictures, found it after later analyzing photos

I was in the navy and we were on a deployment. It was probably late 2006. We were heading across the Atlantic Ocean, from Norfolk, towards Spain. One evening the seas were unusually calm. The water was like looking at glass in all directions. The sunset was surreal, so i decided to take some pictures. I took three pictures before being called back inside. Fast forward almost ten years and now im in college taking a photography class. While going through my pictures for an upcoming assignment I accidentally hit the zoom button and before my eyes was a real flying saucer! In the pictures the UFO is flying in a straight line and the saucer is clearly visible. In the second shot the UFO itself is missing but it's trail remains. Perhaps the UFO was moving further away; or was using some kind of camouflage. The second trail is pretty far from the first one, meaning this object was probably moving at a really high speed. In my final picture the object has dropped significant altitude. Its much closer to the horizon and the saucer is still not visible. The interesting thing about this particular picture is that the trail that was previously following the UFO is not pointed back in the opposite direction. I have always been very skeptical of things i cannot see or touch in person. But this evidence has left me with a much different taste in my mouth. I also have no experience or expertise at manipulating photos.
