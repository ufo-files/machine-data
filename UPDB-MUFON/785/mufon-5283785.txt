Huge oval-shaped UFO

Driving in a westerly direction, a huge object (100-300’) above and ahead of me moving very slowly in a northwesterly direction. It was oval shaped, silver-gray fuselage with a thick black trim that was elongated in the same shape of the craft, set in the center. I couldn’t discern any windows on the starboard side which faced me. It was flying so low and very close to the power lines along the ridge. My sighting lasted less than five seconds as it flew behind the ridge.
