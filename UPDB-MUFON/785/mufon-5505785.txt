{rjl} Fireballs sparked in the high sky fell to earth with long tails

I was outside in my courtyard when I heard people saying whats that? I looked up into the sky and saw one singular fireball falling to earth and I thought it was a asteroid, shooting start, it had a large tail and was completely on fire without a doubt. A helicopter was in view right above the fireball and continued on a path until it was towards the San Diego harbor. One more fireball sparked in the sky and then another. these three fireballs were falling and then stopped hovered and one changed direction and flew in a straight line between the other two as the video shows. I was very nervous and my phone was shaking as I recorded the event. One of the fireballs made it to the horizon and disappeared then re-appeared and disappeard again. In the mean time another fireball appeared to the north of the original event and eventually moved its way east towards the others. I called a friend who works for a news station and he told me they said it was most likely skydivers with pyrotechnics on their shoes for the Holiday Bowl. However, in my opinion none of what I saw looked anything like it might plausibly be skydivers with pyrotechnics. Ive asked others if they heard anything and no one has. The video speaks for itself, sorry for the shakiness.
