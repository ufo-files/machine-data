CE1 on Saubee Lake - 2 Fishermen see strange objec

This is a summary of the Field Investigators report:In the summer of 1989 the witnesses were fishing at a small lake called SaubeeLake in Eaton County. Between the time period of 11:00 p.m. to 12:30 p.m.during the last week of July, as stated by one witness they both saw between twoand three craft (depending on who told the account), many lights, and a jet. All of which was over Saubee Lake. The lake was small in size with houseslocated along the shoreline at the main point of entrance to the lake. The 21 y/o witness stated that at the time of the sighting he looked at thewindows on the craft. He said inside the craft there was a coloration of gray. During this interview an appointment was made with the second witness for aninterview. On November 19, 1990 I met with the second witness for about 30minutes. Some differences were noted between the two accounts. This secondwitness hesitated in giving me indepth information. The 21 y/o witness describes the object as, "very large looked like a giantmorel mushroom, also a bright white flashing in the center of object for approx.10 sec. The first object was approx. 60-80 ft. wide. It moved up and downjust floating over the tree top. Made no sound, color was bright pinkish toreddish orange. Second object was a cone shape much smaller than the first oneand had a cone shaped window in the center with a very bright white light comingfrom inside. The first object disappeared in the trees after 20 minutes ofwatching it. The second object went to the same spot the first one went to. Motor wouldnt start on boat, they avoid lights including my flashlight when Ishined it at them."The second witness simply states, in reply to the Form 1 Questions, "I was atthe Lake Fishing and my friend told me to look." I saw a, "red dot in the sky",and "I didn't pay much attention to it. It looked like a triangle shape, itstayed still. It moved fast."
