Two star-like objects appeared near full moon in the night sky.

I, along with four friends, were at the Palatka (Florida) Golf Club having dinner, when we saw two star-like objects near the full moon. The five of us witnessed this event. We all took pictures and videos with our cell phone cameras. The objects darted about the sky for about an hour. The objects made movements unlike airplanes or helicopters or balloons or flares. There was no noise. None of us had ever seen anything like this before. The same two objects were also seen, at the same time, by my family members living 20 miles to the west.
