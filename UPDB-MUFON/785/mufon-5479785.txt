{nd} Sudden Bright Light Appeared in Photo

My wife and I had walked through Central Park around dusk, the sky was cobalt blue and fading. We were up on the Cedar Hill area looking towards Fifth Avenue. I am a serious amateur photographer, and know about reflection of light etc. I used my flash to light up my wife, and balance the fading light. I noticed some dust illuminated...often orb-like but are pollens etc..I pointed my camera about 30 degrees UP towards the buildings to get a photo with the 1st Quarter Moon which was about 90 degrees up and just over the buildings. I normally use a telephoto lens, with specific settings to lower the shine of the moon, but I used a wide angle lens 24mm at F8 1/80th and wasn't too worried about it being overly bright. (I did use my flash, but the inverse square law would negate any issue as it drops off to 1/100 the power after only 10 feet). I took a few in the exact spot, and took a peek. I suddenly saw this BRIGHT light in the top left area! It was not there 1 second prior, nor after. (There were no airplanes or helicopters or drones, and even then, they flash a red warning beacon, not as bright as the moon. It was not a meteor as there would have been more in the photos. I cannot explain what this is! In the photo, you see a cloud and if you look close, 2 orb like reflections, and 1 small one near the Bright White light. I have included a photo I took to show the same area w/o the light.
