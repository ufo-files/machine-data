{rj} 4 of us when partying down on the shore of the Hudson river encountered a search vehicke looking for something in the water of the Hudson River.

Well.. Here we go. Summer 1978 we 4 went to the Highland shore between the Poughkeepsie bridge and the old rail bridge. Just relaxing and having some beers. Looking down river we all noticed something like a helicopter traveling back and forth with lights from shore to shore; while continuing north past the Poughkeepsie Bridge. It was common for searching suicides with a helicopter since the Bridge was a common place for jumpers. As the vehicle came closer we all noticed there was no sound coming from the vehicle. It came directly above us in its search. Then continued north in it's quest till it was out of sight. It was moving at a very slow rate and we all got a good look at it. Oval about 25 - 30 feet in length; about 20 feet in width. The lights were too bright to stare directly up at them but as it went back away from us heading due east we could see them as recessed and the bottom of the vehicle was kinda shiny silver. My take is it was looking for something and it wasn't us. All of us agreed we would be mocked if we said anything so we kept it a secret. James and Firp are deceased and Dave is now missing so here I am saying this alone.
