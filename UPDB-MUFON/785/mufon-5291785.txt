Owl Outside my WIndow Staring at Me

I woke up afraid from a sometimes recurring dream. I have no recollection of anything other than waking up from this "dream" (no prior dream state). What wakes me up is my fear. My dream is very vivid and very short. In my dream I look outside my bedroom window and noticed that there is a large whitish owl staring at me through the stems of the hydrangea plants in the garden outside my window. I look through the stems of the flowers at this large whitish grayish owl and there is an incredibly white and very bright light shining down on the owl. I at first thought that I left the outside floodlight on that lights up my bedroom window side of the house, but realize that it is never that bright. I wake up bewildered and afraid. I've had this "dream" several times throughout my life (once in Austria in 1978), once in Norway in 1985, and Tuesday night in PIcayune, Mississippi
