UFO activity near Johnston,R.I.

I was in [item moved/CMS/nd] traveling south on I295 at appox 5:00am 3/27/12. I saw fairly low in the sky what looked like white squigly shaped clouds in a north to south direction and relatively short in length. Plus they seemed to be brighter than a cloud should be. Suddenly I saw a dull white light heading south next to the existing clouds, and it was leaving a trail that was identical to the other clouds and went for the same length as the others. The light disappeared and showed up next to that cloud and repeated making another squigly shaped cloud and together there was five of these clouds when the activity stopped and I continued traveling towards Hartford,Ct on Rte. 6. I observed this for appox. 15 minutes.
