THEY APPEARED AS LITTLE OLD MAN WHEN I WAS 7. THEN SHOWED ME A SICK BODY DOWN IN THE BED. THEY TOLD ME THIS INCARNATION WOULD BE ROUGH AND GAVE ME AN OPTION, SAYING THEY WOULD PROTECT ME FROM ALL ILLS. IF I WOULD NOT DESPAIR AND COMMIT SUICIDE, AND LIVE THE LIFE, I WOULD ATTAIN A GREAT STATUS. WHAT THAT IS/WAS, I HAVE NO IDEA, BUT I OPTED TO STAY IN THE BODY. I PUT UP WITH MY PARENTS DRINKING, FIGHTING, VARIOUS ILLNESSES-SCARLET FEVER, ETC. AT DEATHS DOORSTEP WITHIN LEUKEMIA, BUT THEY HAD PROMISED ME HELP, AND I HAD FULL FAITH IN THEM.I HAD MY ONLY SON AT AGE 40 AND SUFFERED RENAL FAILURE A YEAR AFTER. THEY SAW ME THRU THIS WITH 6 LITHO-TRIPSIES, TWO EPISODES OF PNEUMONIA, ETC. IN 79 WHILE LIVING HERE WITH MOTHER, THEY SPOKE INTO MY 3RD EYE:" AWAKE! AWAKE! WE ARE MAD AT YOU!" I TOOK THIS TO MEAN I NEEDED TO TAKE A SPECIAL MATE TO PASS ON A VERY SPECIAL GENETIC CODE, ONE PASSED ON FROM MY FATHER TO ME BY "THE ALIENS", BUT IT FRIGHTENED HIM TO NEAR DEATH! I DID LEAVE, FOUND MY MATE, AND WE HAD OUR SON! BEFORE I LEFT ARK I WAS VISITED BY THEM...WITHOUT SEEING THEM, BUT HEARING ODD SYNTHENIC SOUNDS, WAS PARALYZED, LIFTED FROM MY BED, TAKEN IN FULL BODY UP THRU THE ROOF AND THROUGH A SPECTRUM OF BLACK WITH INCREDIBLY FAST SPEED THRU WHAT I CAN ONLY DESCRIBE AS STARS. THEN, A BLACKOUT! SOME "PROTECTOR", AT MY LEFT...I BEGGED TO RETURN. HE (HE?) RE-TURNED ME, AND I COULD FEEL MY HEAD TOUCH MY PILLOW. WERE THEY STRENGHTENING ME FOR MY JOURNEY FROM ARK TO PA ? I TRUSTED THEM IMPLICITLY! IN 92 I HAD THE CHANCE TO SEE FOUR OF THEM COMING THRU MY WINDOW! I SLEPT MIDROOM WHILE HUSBAND SLEPT FRONT ROOM! I STILL HAVE THIS 11x12 INCH RENDERING. I AM SORRY TO HAVE SKIPPED AROUND SO. THESE ARE NOT BAD EXPERIENCES BORNE OF HORROR! I BEG FOR YOU TO KNOW THIS!!
I AM A WOMAN OF 65, AND THINK OF NOTHING OTHER THAN KINDNESS FROM MY "LITTLE OLD MAN". AFTER ALL, THEY CAME TO ME AT AGE 7! IF IT WERE ALL A DREAM, WOULD I HAVE RECALLED IT ALL THESE DECADES? HOW MANY AMOUNG YOU CAN HAVE VIVID RECALL OF A DREAM THAT VISITED YOU AT AGE SEVEN? MY BEST TO ALL! PROTECT THE PLANET,TAKE CARE OF THE YOUNG, AND PLANT TREES!!!!P.S. MY ONLY SKETCH 92 "STRANGERS IN THE NIGHT" WILL NOT REPRODUCE. THIS IS NOT A JOKE.
