Like a meteor but not like a meteor.

Probably just a meteor. I only report this because I have never seen a meteor described w/ these characteristics on any science or astronomy Show. I was about 9 yrs old in 1976, Summer, about 9 PM sunset. While playing outside, I happened to look North towards the Goodview MN airport. Goodview is a suburb of Winona MN. I saw a very small, bright white fireball descend at a shallow angle near the horizon. It was not a straight line but had a slight curve downward. It started below the edge of the bluff-line (500 ft.). 1/2 way thru the decent, a bright red and bright blue light split off the larger white object. The red and blue light/object went almost straight down at about an 80 degree angle. The entire episode last about 1 second. I did not see these objects hit the ground. They just faded out. I have read hundreds of "bright light" UFO reports. None matches this except one of the Wiinona MN UFO reports about a white light emitting other colored lights. I was a skywatcher as a child and had seen thousands of meteors during meteor showers. I never saw anything like this before or since. All 3 lights had a short tail that faded out in less than 1 second. I will never forget the blue and red colors. As perfect a blue and red as I have ever seen.
