My August 18th,2009 UFO Notes. [removed/CMS/fgc] witnessed. 

"Weather was clear at sunset with unlimited visibility. Temp. 64F and 75% humidity at 2200hrs. Our home is located directly on Lake Michigan, in Stevensville, Michigan, 55 miles east of Chicago.

Three oblong shaped objects were travelling separately from north to south, estimated at 1 to 1 1/2 miles from shore and dime sized, with no running lights, no sound, at varied speed. There was a 1 minute interval between each object all following the same flight path. After travelling across our property and over Lake Michigan, they angled west toward Chicago, climbed, then quickly disappeared. 15 minutes after the initial appearance of the three objects, the identical sequence of events occured with 3 more objects.

The objects were oblong, like a bongo shaped drum, with what appeared to be an open or recessed end. The colors were a glowing blend of golden orange, red and yellow."

Our neighbor to the south, a commercial airline pilot with years of flying experience saw a similar event 2 months earlier, with only 4 objects flying in formation at varying speeds. Another neighbor also saw "something" in the sky that night.
