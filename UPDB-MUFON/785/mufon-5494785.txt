spotted from deck, watched for aprox 3 min

Wife was out on the deck for a smoke, called me to witness the lights. Moving slowly south to north east and slowly descending and ascending as it moved along. Very big in size (maybe the size of a double garage) very intense bright white lights, 4 across, seemed to shoot out like a small search light occasionally. Lights would then change to a brilliant red color that were in a triangle shape pointing upwards (maybe 5 or 6 red lights) When this happened the white lights went out or were not visible. Quite Friday night at 11 pm, no noise from object. Didn't know what it was but my reaction was to go get the camera, of course camera didn't work, we had recently downloaded some personal pics and accidentally put the card in protect mode. Grabbed the i pad and managed 1 pic but by that time it was almost out of site and I couldn't zoom in with the new Ipad. Object moved out of sight behind buildings and trees. Seemed to be over a ring road around the outskirts of the city. Wondering if this was witnessed by anyone else.
