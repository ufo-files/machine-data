(GS) 1973 UFO Over Decker Power Plant - Austin, Texas

This is the 2nd UFO that I have seen in my life time. It was in June - July 1973 and it was hovering over the Decker Lake Power Plant near Austin, Texas. What a sight seeing it going zig zag across the sky then stopping for about 5 minutes to recharge then off it went at a blink of an eye it was gone. My wife Nancy witness it with me too and my son Steve at six months was in my arms... Went to get a camera and it was gone. Hovered over the now Walter E. Long Power Plant east of Austin.
