glowing lights above the Sea of Cortez coastline 8 in straight line then disappeared one at a time after a few minutes

December 8th 2012. Five of us were sitting outside on a deck with a great view up the hill from the beach in Puerto Penasco Mexico around 7pm. The sun had gone down but 8 red orange glowing objects appeared above the coastline in the west a few thousand feet up it seemed.They were similar to stars but had no blinking lights and were a orange red color none of us had ever seen in the sky. They were in a straight line formation then disappeared one at a time in a matter of minutes.
