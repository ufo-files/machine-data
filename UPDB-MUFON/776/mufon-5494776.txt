Diamond star descended over field behind house.

In the late 60's or early 70's I witnessed an object decending straight down over a field containing cows. it was evening time but good moon light to see clearly. The cows seem to know about this ufo because they all ran together across the field and tried to hide under the hedge way. there was NO noise from the ufo. The cows were very, very scared. It was upsetting to see such strange behavour from cows witch at that time would have been laid down quietly for evening. if you want to ask me about anything else please e-mail me as i do not want my family to know i have spoken to you as they do not believe me. but i remember it as if it was this morning very scary.
