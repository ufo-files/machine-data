on friday nov 4 6:15pm 2011, very clear evening,was distracted by strange swooshing sound.a triangular object,abought the size of a pickup truck passed over head.the corners of the object were squared off.it was silver mettalic in color,and no wing or exhaust were visible.it had one white light on, in bottom of craft. there were three other circular items, one in each corner of bottom of craft. all of these circular items, inclding the center light were mounted flush with bottom of craft. as craft continued on its sw course it turned on very large lights which i believe were coming from each corner of craft.a large red light in front, a green and blue light in back. a sister and my brother were in field several hundred yards south of me also experienced the loud swooshing and seen the object from the distance.my brother had just retired from military and could not identify object nor sound. several years ago i had an encounter with a huge black triangular object in this same spot.it made no sound at all,and had remained motionless for several minutes above me just above tree tops.when it moved off it was on the same course but moving opposite direction.
