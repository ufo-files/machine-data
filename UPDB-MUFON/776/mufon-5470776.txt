{jpg} Orange light passed overhead disappearing before I lost sight. Bright and orange star sized passed just after ISS had passed

The object appeared in view about 20:50 just to the left of the moon in the SW sky. Object was stationary at first and was same brightness as the ISS which had passed overhead about 10 - 15 minutes before. It was orange in color and began to move across the sky from the SW to the NE going directly overhead. I observed the movement in a straight line path for approximately 2-3 minutes until it flickered and disappeared completely. The space station had passed in a similar path but was visible for a longer period.
