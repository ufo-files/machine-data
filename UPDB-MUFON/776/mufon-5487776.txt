Noticed bright orange light in sky to NE. Looked up again and saw disk shaped craft with three revolving lights on bottom of craft.

While hunting in Quebec, I went out late at night to ..... While standing there I noticed a large bright orange object (too large for a star)to the NE. It was so bright it made an orange reflection on the still lake water. As I finished my business. I looked up in that direction again and it was gone, but looking out straight ahead to some islands about a quarter mile away (NNE), I saw a large orange disk object. As it was high enough in the sky I saw three rotating circular lights revolving on the underside of the craft. It then went out considerably farther until it was just a very tiny orange dot. One other hunter saw it just before me while it was still in the NE, but returned to hunt cabin.
