A large isosceles trianglar ufo the size of a football pitch with a dim round purple light in each corner passed silently overhead.

My friend and I were sitting in my garden looking at the stars as we had both seen a small lit object travelling very quickly. I was on a sun lounger looking straight up and within around fifteen minutes saw a large isosceles triangular shape pass silently overhead. I could really only see the three dim purple round lights implying the shape. They were a little fuzzy with radial segments and perhaps yellowish round centres. I think it was four or five hundred feet high and it was swift. I am used to Hercules regularly overhead and this was much quicker. The thought of three geese initially flashed through my head then three craft in ridiculously exact formation and finally one object. It flew in a straight line. During the sighting I pointed and said look, and Tom said he could see it. It was like a sci/fin movie come to life. I am so glad there were 2 of us and not just myself. Object went out of sight after around 7 seconds. It had not reached the horizon but quickly faded into the distance.
