(GS) Group of stars that move

For 2 months now I have noticed a group of what to be 4-6 stars above my house that move. They appear to move like bugs on the water in various directions. They do seem to have a limited range though and don't stray from it. They also look like stars but do not twinkle, and almost appear to have an aura around them. This happens every night when it gets dark and lasts all night. On Tuesday nights I have noticed that one of them, appears to move over my neighbors home to the East. I have seen planes and satellites, and thats not what these are.
