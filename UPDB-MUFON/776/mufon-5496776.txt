{cak} 7 orbs moving N then hovering ascending and heading mostly W / NW

I was at home, I called a friend and had just stepped outside for a smoke, when I noticed a orb moving north, i hung up pulled out my phone and started taking pictures. I was so focused on the one that I failed to notice the other two following behind the first in a line formation. when they got a little bit closer to overhead of my house they formed a almost triangular formation and started to ascend. as they were doing this they started to dim and become almost opaque one headed E, another W and the third just kept ascending almost looked like it started to head west as I lost visual. Called my friend back and 2 followed the same path, again hung up took video and picture both headed east after ascending. called my friend yet again and 2 more showed up, as they were almost directly over my house my mother came home I asked her what they were and she couldn't tell either. As i was filling this out my mother spotted yet another one. total count is 8 as of right now. I will be going outside to watch for more. I have more photos and video upon request. I knew these were not airplanes or helicopters on first sight as they sky around my house is rather lousy with them. During the sightings I was just in complete awe, there was only calm thoughts in my mind, it was rather beautiful.
