Orange lights flying about NOT AIRPLANES

One large light move up from horizon and sit stable in air. Several orange lights move up from horizon and take different paths. Some in formation hover and drop back down . Some in formation move in changing paths and than move off into space. More come over horizon hang around than move off into space or back down under horizon. After about 20 minuets the main white light sunk behind horizon. I was recording these events from the front door of my house on a cell phone. I was taking out the garbage when I noticed the lights in the sky. These were not airplanes. I live near a flight path and more than once a commercial airliner flew thru the shot confirming these were not airplanes.
