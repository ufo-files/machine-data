Arrow shaped craft spilling out lava type material on ground.

Went to make a security round. Turned left corner and immediately raised my left arm, to shield my eyes to the unexpected bright, vibrant light. I viewed a arrowhead shaped craft spewing out white, yellow and red lava type material from the right side of the fuselage. First 2 seconds of seeing craft. I could not move. Felt someone watching and puzzled why, I knew, it knew, i knew. Approx 15-20 mins (of weird time) went by but then, time went to normal. hardest thing to explain is, from the very first time, I saw the craft, to when, it disappeared. It honestly felt like only 6 sec of time had only transpired. Only when I finally returned to the guard house. I was able to realized approx 35 to 45 mins had passed, from when I first exited the guard house at approx 0208.
