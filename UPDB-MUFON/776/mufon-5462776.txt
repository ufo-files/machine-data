{tjd} Craft

Observing the unusual heading of a jet, for the area NW-SE sitting at my kitchen table next to my glass deck slider. I live approximately one air mile east of a Lockheed-Martin facility. I notice a stationary disc-like, metal object near the contrail. Most air traffic is SW-NE, heading toward DIA. I have noticed military traffic, especially helicopters at cross patterns with the normal traffic. The object is reflecting the late sunlight. I was a bit shaken when as it occurred to me the object was stationary and not leaving a contrail at that altitude. The jet is probably a mile ahead (SE) of the object at this point. I can hear the jet, but no sound is coming from the object. The object then slowly floats over the jet's contrail and as I reach inside a cabinet for my binoculars, it disappears into a cirrus formation. The object is roughly 50% larger than the jet. Type of jet, unknown. My initial thought, the object was a propeller craft, but there was no sound, no wings and no contrail as it maneuvered over the jet's contrail. The object, on the SE of the contrail, at walking pace went over the contrail, remaining partially visible on either side, then it walked into the cirrus clouds and I lost it. This is my second UFO sighting. Because of a Hangar 1 episode, I'll submit a report about my first sighting.
