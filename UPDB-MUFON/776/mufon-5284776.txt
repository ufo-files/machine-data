Hovering, at about 500'. Large craft about 250' x 500' rectangle, about 40' tall.

As I was driving north on I-394 from Chicago Heights at about 4:00 PM, I started to observe something just above the horizon, east of the expressway, several miles ahead. As I got closer to the I-394/I-294 interchange (about 2 miles before the interchange), I saw with greater detail, a large rectangular shape (much larger than a barge), dark in color. It was hovering over I-294 approximately 1000' east from I-394. It was directly over the east & west bound traffic. It seemed to be about 500' above ground level. There was no apparent movement. I wish I would have stopped to try to see more details, but I was in traffic. The object was approximately 250' wide, 500' long and about 40' tall. Its length was parallel with the expressway (I-294).
