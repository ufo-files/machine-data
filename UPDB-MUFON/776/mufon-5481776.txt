{dr}Possible sighting

I was driving on a dangerous road, too dangerous to stop and take any pictures. I noticed it when the car in front of me almost came to a complete stop as it came into our view above the trees. At first I thought it was a plane, then a blimp, but the shape was so clear. It was - as best I can describe it - a trapezoid shaped object, similar to a triangle, glowing and white, with 3 huge lights underneath it pointed towards where my car was headed. It wasn't moving when I saw it at first, but as soon as I passed it I could no longer see it, but my significant other - who I immediately dialed - said it was above him approx. 12 miles away. I just wanted to see it again to really understand and get a better look at it - I'm not sure if my eyes were playing tricks on me, if it was just a blimp or plane, but it left a few people confused.
