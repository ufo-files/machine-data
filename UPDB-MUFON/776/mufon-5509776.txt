{dr}This was a bright orange light moving steady through the sky, as low as a small plane would fly.

I was with friends watching several personal fireworks around a lake. I was looking around to see if there were any more fireworks. It was then that I noticed a bright orange, low, steady light moving through the sky. I noticed it over the south side of the lake, moving northeast. As it came closer to me it faded almost instantly, off to the northeast. I was feeling since I first saw this, that it was a UFO who had come to observe the fireworks. It was huge and bright, unlike your typical flashing airplane light, and too large and low for a satellite, in my opinion. I was amazed that I was seeing this. The object merely faded into space. It seemed as if I should have kept seeing it, since it was so huge and bright. I mentioned it to my friend that did not see it. She agreed it was likely a UFO. I am amazed that there are similar reports of this in other states.
