Witnessed red glowing lights doing weird things at night above the ocean.

While on vacation in Myrtle Beach, SC, from June 20th thru June 26th 2020, i could walk on on my balcony on the 6th floor, every night and witness a glowing, red light appear out of nowhere, out over the ocean, one red light would turn into multiple red lights, and they would do wierd formations and then disappear..Minutes later it would appear in another location, doing the same cool looking formations. One time they even appeared to shoot straight up out of the ocean, almost like a flair, but they never flared. I saw this phenomenon for 5 nights out of the 7 nights that i was there, and the sky was too cloudy to see for those 2 nights. There was never a plane or helicopter or drone, or ship in the night sky out over the ocean when, I witnessed these strange red lights, and a 70 year old man and his wife watched this along with me one night on the beach. We were in total awe of whatever it was that we saw.. I am still in total awe of what i consider to be a once in a lifetime event, to be able to see. I most certainly cannot explain what it was that i witnessed, it did not appear to be man made at all..I witnessed these strange events, i feel like i have a million dollar to show and tell, and i feel like the world needs to hear it..
