{rjl} filmed while elk hunting

I was hunting elk in September in Uintah County between Vernal and Roosevelt, I noticed a light that didn't move like an airplane. I watched for over an hour with binnoculars and then through a Swarovski spotter, dropped and ascended what appeared to be hundreds of feet in seconds. Moved from West to East and covered an area I estimate to be 9 miles from the distance I was at. Finally went out of site into the North East. I have video, if you want to see it please contact me.
