{rjl} A yellowish gold light traveling fast and high north to south then stop and immediate reversal no turning

It was October about 10pm i stepped out of the side exit to my parents house where i lived at the time. I lit a cigarette and wss just watching the sky to the north as i often would. Growing up in Vienna Ohio i know very well how planes look in the night sky i know how they look and move because there is an air force reserve base and civilian airport i in the town. Nothing seemed off at first the object was high to the north heading straight South. In fact i looked away for a moment and looked back (just to give you an idea of how normal the whole thing was) but then the object just stopped dead for a moment and at that point i was very interested this object was to high to be a helicopter and the light never blinked and then it just reversed moving now South to North in the exact track it had approached. I cannot stress this enough the object did NOT make any kind of turn it didn't slow down it didn't do any of the things you'd expect from an airplane. It just stopped and then reversed in air.i was shook and confused i watched it head north until i could no longer see it over the pine tees i then finished my cigarette and went inside where i decided to keep it to myself only ever telling a few close friends for fear of ridicule.
