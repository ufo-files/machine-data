{dr}All white, reflective, moving about jet speed, no wings, no sound, no contrails

I was entering a restaurant in Wexford, PA on Sunday October 26th at 5:55PM. A white object caught my eye and at first I thought it was a jet airliner. It was moving from north to south in a straight line. It was a cloudless clear 60 degree afternoon. As it flew over my head I noticed that it was a bright white color, not the typical dull airline, and appeared to have no wings. It also didn't have a thin clean cigar shape like a jet. It was bulkier and seemed to come to more of a point. Then I realized that it didn't seem to make any sound. Even jets at high altitude can usually be heard. I also realized that it didn't leave any contrails. It appeared to be flying at an elevation around 30,000 to 40,000 ft at a speed around 300 to 500 mph. Jets at that altitude leave contrails. At least I think they do. I took a picture and video with my phone but it's sketchy at best. I looked away for a min and it was gone. I couldn't find it again. Although I'm 42 years old and have excellent long distance eyesight, I was probably just seeing things, but I thought I'd report it anyway in case someone else did too. You never know. It flew at jet height and at jet speed. But that seemed to be all it had in common with a jet.
