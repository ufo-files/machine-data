Bright light going down to earth, Nasca

Nasca,south east of the Nasca Lines

Looking through one window the full moonlight in the horizon over the earth, I saw a light with the shape of star or a ball with small spikes, but the spikes did not twinkle like a star. 

The object was very bright, much brighter than a star and the Moon, but it was like the light was not going beyond it1s contour, as if it was an unchanging shape captured in one image.

The object was going from sky to earth for almost 3 seconds until it dissappeared far in the horizon.

When it was high in the sky it was bigger, and smaller close to the earth, so I think it did not descended vertically, but diagonally, as if it was going to the Nasca Lines or the sea.
