String of 15 to 20 straight row

Lights first appeared in the sw at 50 to 70 degree off the horizon. I was facing north. I saw 15 to 20 objects in a string. The objects were white but not exceptionally brilliant, maybe like a distant star. They moved much quicker than a plane or even a missile. They had a slight upward trajectory and were visible for about 20 seconds. At a certain area of space maybe 30 to 40 000 foot they began disappearing one after the other. I thought maybe a meteor but these objects were stayed exactly spaced and moved as one. I am hoping someone else may have seen this.I have watched the sky for many years,but this was not easy to believe. But it still would be nice to have confirmation of the incident. Thanks
