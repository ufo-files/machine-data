Glowing orb flying fast in a straight line overhead.

Approximately at 6:05 pm in San Diego looking up at our palm trees and pine tree in the back yard I saw a white glowing orb. First appeared coming from behind the palm tree canopy (west) approximately 90 degree elevation heading east. I saw it for 3.5 to 4 seconds before it disappeared into the white of the horizon about 15 degree elevation. Size was about like 1/2 a pencil eraser at arms length a bit oblong in shape. The white glow masked any outline of a craft. There was no sound and the landing jets to my right would not drown out normal noises of even birds. By itself this is not an exciting report but my first. Hope others will have seen it also and report it Multiple reports are better than one. At first I tried to figure if it was a bird, but the path was to straight, fast and no change in color or orientation. A high flying plane was also ruled out due to the shape, the glow and no noise.
