{td} Looking at stars last night one seemed to explode in white then yellow flash, emitting orb or beam.

Standing on a small hillside in Arvada Colorado Sunday night around 10pm: It had rained most of the day and the sky was finally clear for stargazing. After 5-10 minutes of looking at the starry sky a bright flash occured SSW of me approximately 60 degrees above horizon. The flash started bright white and faded to a semispherical yellow/white crescent; with a thin spiraling contrail or rocket tail like emmanation shooting away and gaining altitude resebling the smoke trail of a firework: traveling SSW and rapidly gaining altitude untill out of sight. The event was naked eye visible with no sound.
