Slow moving 'fireball' witnessed from Wolfs Lodge Bay to Mica Bay CDA around July 12, 2000 appox 3:15am.

Was at LIT, leadership in training, at Camp Lutherhaven at CDA Idaho in the summer of 2000. I was having trouble sleeping on the beach with the group during a team building exercise and was awakened by a brightness thinking it was morning. Looking to the east, thinking it was the sun over the lake, I saw what looked like a meteorite moving across the sky, but it was much larger than any I have observed in my life. I guesstimate, thinking back comparing to the tops of trees, buildings it passed over and knowing the bay, around 75-100 feet in diameter. The object moved slowly and crossed my field of view over the course of what seemed about ten minutes at a height I estimate around 500-1200 feet. I estimate this due to me having been doing model rocketry for a few years prior and know approx apogee. (Later, going for engineering I am able to verify this distance re-visiting the camp as an adult). 
The object disappears over the east post Mica Bay. My watch at the time said around 2:15AM. Object made no noise at which point struck me as the most odd as I was also into military jets and could identify them at that time let alone the at most stall velocity it was moving at if it was an aircraft. 
The sun rose at ~4am. No one else saw it as far as I know. 

Short, simple but curious.
