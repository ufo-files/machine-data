I saw 2 clusters of lights hovering in the sky on Christmas Eve

Entered by FI # 31522 - I was on my way home from a Christmas visit when I stopped at the stoplight on 14th street and looked to the West toward 15th street up the hill, and I saw 2 clusters of lights. One was larger than the other and seemed to be closer to me, the other was to the right and followed behind. I watched it for 3 minutes to see if it would move or flash. but it just stayed there. I continued my drive and lost sight of the lights as I approached my home.
