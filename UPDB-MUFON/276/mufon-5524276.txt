Mass of blu-green small varied size atom shapes rotating very fast making loud humming sound.

On the morning of 10/22/82 7:30 a.m. I awoke to a real sighting of an unknown object which was over my floor in front of my bed to the left. It was aprox. 10"w x 18"h cyclinder shaped, rotating its mass of small round, different sized shapes at an extremely fast speed in a clock wise direction, colored in blues and greens, creating a loud humming sound. This was a starting experience to come in contact with while I was awake and watching it from my bed in total amazement. I almost could not believe this was happening, before my very eyes and ears. I was looking at something that was spinning with centrifugal force that I could not explain. After viewing it for some time I gave myself a reality check and pinched myself. After I realized it was real, beautiful and bizzare I did not know what it was there for or what to do so I walked along the right wall into the next room, the bathroom and I could still hear the humming loud sound it was making so I came out went into bed and watched some more untill it dissapeared. It did not scare me and it left me with tranquillity and joy. I actually believed immediately afterwards it came to heal me. Aprox. 5 years later I was in a health food store and picked up a book called METAPHYSICAL MEDITATIONS and by coincidence the first page I came to was page 35 titled "Tune in with the Cosmic Sound" and read about Aum for the first time in my life. Then I realized what I saw was the cosmic force of the universe creating the aum sound which attuned my brain wave activity; which allowed me to feel hope, security, love, and a connection to God which I still have today.
