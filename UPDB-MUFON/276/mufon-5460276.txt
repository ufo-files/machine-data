{cak} Black Triangle

Via another social media web site I and another individual have been witness to this HUGE Black Triangle that flies inbound from the Spartanburg, SC region towards Columbia SC at random and onward towards the Atlantic Ocean in the region of the Cherry Point Marine Station in NC. Both runways at Cherry Point are 3+ miles long. When its flies near Columbia it never keeps the exact flight path and it can vary + / - 10 miles either direction to the North or South or directly over Columbia. The engines seems to sound like a roar as you can hear them in the video. I took the video of it flying under the heavy clouds on Friday, June 7, 2019 at 5:20AM over Monticello, SC [approximate] The video was made using a handheld Samsung Galaxy Android phone at 4X magnification and the video does not capture the enormous size of this thing as I was able to see the triangular shape by the white lights on each of the three tips wearing glasses as I have 20 /70 vision. The sound is defining and it’s always in a decent mode. I also checked on [Removed link] and its flight path does not appear on their web site for that day nor historically. The other contact suggested that it’s not landing at Cherry Point but flying into the Ocean. I have no idea but due to its size I also do not believe that this object can land on land.
