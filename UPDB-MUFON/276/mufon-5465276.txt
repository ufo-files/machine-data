{tjd} Emitted reflection, changed altitude, and position.

Was looking up at the sky, and noticed what I originally thought to be a balloon as it flipped and emitted a reflection from one side as it was floatung upwards. I found it odd because of the altitude it was at (about the same altitude you would see airliners take). It made no sound. As I watched it, I seen it disipate, then noticed what appeared to be a star as it was at the altitude you would see at night if star gazing. 

I then noticed it dipped back down and have a cylynder shape moving from SW to NE then disapear again.(Did this once). After it disappeared I seen it again at the same altitude as if it were a star again.  I took pictures and noticed in the pictures that it had changed position relative to the sun. I sat and watched it for several minutes before losing sight.
