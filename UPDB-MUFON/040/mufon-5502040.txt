Heading home on Hwy 99W, driving towards Amity, saw bright yellow light with light clear lights on edges

I was heading home from work Friday morning around 12:am. I just went over a little bridge and up the hill, when I saw a bright yellow pulsating light with little white lights on the outside edges. I slowed down, turned off my radio and open the passenger window to get a better view. I thought that it may have been a tractor in the field, but I didn't hear an engine sound. It was heading south at a slow pace, it picked up speed when I slowed to watch it. The yellow light was very bright as I had to squint to look at it. As I rounded the bend in the road, it disappeared. I don't know where it went and I didn't see any lights in my rear-view mirror. I thought to myself, that was pretty weird. I keep driving home and would go back to look when it was daylight, to see if there was a tractor marks or field lights.
