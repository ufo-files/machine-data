My mother and i were sitting on the porch. Across from the beachbwhile on vacation at St George island Fl..i was on the phone and right in front of our beach house over the ocean appeared a round orange light, stayed there for about 20 sec then went off it repeated that about 5 times for about 30 min i had went in to get my phone,and about 30-45 min later the orange light came on..i ran infront of the driveway to get a better view and by the time i had got there 8 more lights apeared in a staircase pattern 2 sets of 4 lights..i went to record it and took a pic instead..hurried and switched it to record by this time the lights were going out one at a time..i stopped recording looked at the direction my mother was sitting. And about 100 ft + down the coast line..the lights were on in the same pattern i put my phone up to record it again..and all the lights went out at once...there was a four story house across the road from us and the highest light looked about 15 ft above the house and the lowest light were around the 2nd floor..i was talking to my mother about the sighting when a man and 2 boys came across the road from the beach..he said did you see that? I said yes we did..he said ..well im 1navy and all i can say is there were no planes and no boats on or over the ocean !
