[dr]It was a bright blinking sphere. It pulsated and then disappeared, after it disappeared another one appeared.

I was on the way home from an event at about 9:48. I was on the highway driving home when I noticed a bright reddish-yellow sphere to the right of my car right above the treetops. The object was probably about a hundred feet away. When I first saw the object, I thought that it was a light from a helicopter. I was led to this assumption because the light seemed to blink and pulsate. I continued to watch the light and while I was watching it pulsated one more time and then disappeared. About 20 seconds after it disappeared a new one "formed". I then happened to look out of my window again and noticed a large triangle formed out of what looked like three small orbs. The three orbs seemed to be turning in sync which leads me to believe that they were all a part of one "mother-ship" I guess you could call it. This was the first time I have ever seen something like this.
