I was driving home at around 12:15am on January 1st. Saw around 8 - 10 reddish/orange orbs flying in a path coming from the North to South over New Port Richey, Florida. Then the objects turned toward the west and flew out of sight due to cloud cover. At first, I thought they were airplanes or helicopters. But then I pulled the car over to the side of the road to take a better look. There was no airplane or jet sound. There was no helicopter sound. To the best of my knowledge, these objects were not anything I've ever seen before.
