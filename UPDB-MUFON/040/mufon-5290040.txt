3 UFO's flying in an insect-like trajectory

I witnessed two lights in the clear night sky, flying in a straight trajectory overhead, but weaving erratically, in and out, up and down from one another, like an insect (think of a bee) would fly, being closely followed by what appeared to be a "normal" aircraft, which appeared to be following or chasing the two UFO's, flying in a straight trajectory. 

Approximately 2-5 minutes later, another single light, again flying in a straight trajectory overhead, same direction as the first two UFO's, erratically weaving up and down, in and out like an insect, being closely followed or chased by what appeared to be two "normal aircrafts", flying in a straight trajectory. The three UFO's and the three "normal" aircrafts were all lit by a single white light.
