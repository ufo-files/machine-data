Viewed from airplane window, two separate elongated black lines moving through the clouds, close to each other. One either turned or changed shape.

Flight LS733 from Edinburgh to Kos on 31 July, 2019.

Flight was four hours. Took off at 16.30 UK time. At 18.38 UK time I looked through the window to the left of the plane and saw 2 elongated, thin black lines moving through the top of the highest level of cloud in the sky, at least 2 miles away. Probably more.
As I saw it, two children of at least 12 years of age behind me started commenting on it also, saying things like "There's no way that's a plane."
My wife also watched them.
They were both at the same level and close to each other. The one on the left appeared to have a very faint pale yellow light, unblinking, towards it's right hand side.
The one on the right didn't appear to have any light. They were possibly moving slowly through the cloud or were maybe stationary. Hard to tell as we were in a moving vehicle too.
Then the one on the right appeared to either rotate to display a different side to it or it changed shape completely, as it slowly stopped appearing elongated and thin, and started to become a solid black block.
At this moment, our plane did a sudden left turn of roughly 90 degrees, so the view of these objects moved towards the front of the plane and eventually out of my sight. 
I thought it was strange that the pilots chose to make this manouvre at that exact same time. It was either coincidental or the pilot spotted them too, and moved the plane to allow himself a better view of what was happening.
Both lines appeared too long to me, to be a military or commercial plane, and they were very close to one another, almost moving in unison until the one on the right did it's shape shifting thing.
I can supply sketches of the event if required.
