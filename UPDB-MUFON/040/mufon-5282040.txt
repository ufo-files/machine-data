At 9 pm, on 1-75 by CVG airport, 2 circular objects, with white circle of bright pulsating light passed by.

On 2/26/22, driving north on I-75 after I-275 2 objects passed me going SW along 1-75 S at a high speed, possibly 150-200+mph, much faster than a car can go. One was following the other toward CVG airport. They had a very bright ring of rotating, pulsating light on edges and black in interior, slightly translucent. I saw something inside which I believed must be equipment flying the drone. Later found out there were no drones like this. Elevation was about 150 ft and they were about 100 ft to my left. I called the Kenton Co Police and Kentucky State Police but they do not fly any drones. I don't know if the elevation in degrees is correct.
