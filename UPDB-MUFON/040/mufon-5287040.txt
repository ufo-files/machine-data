Enormous disc shaped, multi storied craft with windows and multiple lights hovered over a farmhouse in a quiet village.

It was over 50 years ago (1975) and never reported. A friend and I were walking home through our village around 9pm. We crossed the road at a crossroads where to our left was a farmhouse. Over the top of the farmhouse hovering quite close to the roof top was a huge craft. It is difficult particularly after so many years, to describe accurately the size but it appeared absolutely enormous I would say much wider than a 747 possibly a football pitch size and round. It appeared to have many levels of multiple storeys with windows of lights all around and through it. The shape was distinct and disc like however it also appeared to be translucent, you could see the dark sky through it. It made no noise and did not cast any beams. My friend and I both saw this craft and stood watching it for a few minutes. We decided to run home and fetch a camera which took no longer than 5 minutes as we were close to home. When we ran back to take a photo the craft had disappeared. I can still visualize the event after all these years with great clarity.
