{nd} Several Blinking lights dotting the sky and a bright yellow orb.. Definitely not a plane.

2 friends and I were sitting on the deck at the side of my home. Looking up at the stars we noticed 3 starlike objects hovering but seeminly moving from a certain angle.. They glowed brightly unlike many of the other stars covered by clouds. They were distinct both in their brightness and in the fact that they seemed to be constantly glimmering which never typically happens for stars of that brightness and size. We began wondering if it was possible that they were UFOs. We began searching for the moon and after running around the village for a few minutes we finally found it. We walked back to the deck and upon coming to the chairs we sat at originally, we looked to the south and saw a bright light. It was distinct unlike all the others as it was not glimmering. It was bright and strange. It moved at a rate faster then ive ever seen any jet move in my lifetime. Plus jets don't have large yellow lights on them. The plane turned at a sudden 120 degree turning angle. It flew into clouds and the light suddenly simply disappeared. In the Southeast not very far from where the UFO mysteriously disappeared, a red flashing light shown indicating that a flying object was there, but it was as red as an ambulance siren. It simply hovered in the area. Before we stopped to watch what happened to it we went inside and began discussing the matter. we began searching for a video or story to show the similar turn of the UFO that had the bright yellow light on it and found a video that seemed very close. Not saying that the video is a hoax or not but it was very similar to that turn just at a different angle.
