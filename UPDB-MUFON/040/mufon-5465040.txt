{cak} high altitude wingless craft that moved to south appeared to turn and morph into glowing sphere that looked like a planet coming toward me, laser streak came from north captured my attention

We were outside standing by our pool when I looked up into the clear blue sky almost directly above us at a high altitude and observed a wingless aircraft, a roughly rectangular shape moving north to south at first appearing to be moving at the speed of a regular passenger plane, but then I realized it was moving faster as it was off to the south before I knew it and it seemed to turn and what looked like a massive headlight beam pointed toward our direction that changed into a glowing sphere that looked like a planet. I remember seeing in the southern direction what was looking like a shooting star in the daytime. Then a laser streaked across the sky from the north to the south which grabbed my attention then it was all over. When I first saw it I tried to point it out to my wife who could not track it. I described all of this while it was happening, but she saw nothing. I even posted a question on an online app asking if anyone else observed this. And asked a question about the international space station, as I was wondering if that was a possible answer to what I had seen. My feelings about the whole thing was utter amazement. As I told of the sighting at dinner later in the evening everyone got a good laugh, apparently not buying into what I had seen.

Not sure I should (But I did) count my wife as a witness as she never saw a thing, but she could hear my description as it was happening.

The whole experience took about 20 seconds, but I doubt I will forget it.
