{jpg} Glowing disc over NW Chicago Suburb

On august 24 about 9:30 I took my dog out for a walk. Looked back toward our house and saw a flickering light. Kind of looked like a star but I knew it was not. So I ran in and got my external zoom lens for my iPhone and shot some videos. Pretty amazing g videos for an iPhone at night. Looked like an orb to the bare eye, but when I zoomed in the camera made my jaw drop. There was a glowing saucer shaped ship. Hovering but still moving slightly side to side and up and down. It was amazing.
