10-20 red-orange objects ascending then fading into one general area of the northern sky.

Standing on my back porch, I looked toward the northern sky and noticed red-orange objects beginning to ascend from behind the tree lines. At first, the objects appeared to be some sort of fireworks; however, the cluster began to include several of the same objects, all ascending to a particular area of the sky - just slightly north-west from where they appeared. None of the objects fell toward the ground, instead, they all ascended and subsequently faded into the perfectly clear night sky. There was no sound coming from the objects.
