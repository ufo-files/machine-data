Hemisphere Hovering the bus stop

Concerns: UFO sighting of November 1975

Dear Madam, dear Sir,

My mother made in November 1975 in Pully (SWITZERLAND, VD) an observation I would like to share. I am his son.

She was born on October 13, 1941. She gladly stand at your disposal for an interview if you wish.

Here is the observation : Date: November 1975.Th exact day is not known. At 19:00, it was already dark, she left her home to go to work by bus (Cloakroom at the Municipal Theatre of Lausanne). She descends a sloping avenue (Av. Rennier) to get to the bus stop at Place de la Clerg1re.
The Lake Geneva is facing her. Going down this avenue, she saw in the sky in the direction of Geneva (southwest) a very bright spot, like a star, but with a much higher light intensity. She continued to go down and she realized that this spot has gained in size and intensity. Intrigued, she tried to follow him, but the houses and trees obscured her vision. Arriving in the square, she observed, vertically a hemisphere shaped object, still, about thirty meters above the ground. The object made no sound. Its dome looked metallic, while the underside of the object is flat and diffuse a very harsh light, cold, blue white, like "fluorescent tube". She estimated the diameter of the object to 4-5 m. There were no cars or passersby. Then after a while she estimated at 30 seconds (hard to say), the object accelerated to a meteoric speed noiselessly toward the northeast. She went up the Avenue to see the object flying away, in vain.
