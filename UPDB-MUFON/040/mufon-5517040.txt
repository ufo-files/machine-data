On 7/21/12 I was in my yard at about 11:30 pm walking my dog before I went to bed.I just happened to look up and noticed what a crystal clear night it was and how bright the stars were. I tried to find the big dipper when I noticed what I thought was a star only brighter than the others.
I then thought it must be the north star. I observed this for about 20 seconds and this light suddenly got brighter and then seemed to ascend very quickly getting smaller but not diminishing in brightness. This lasted about for 5 seconds until I could no longer see it. I was confused as to what I saw and thought to wake my wife but it was already gone, I then thoght mybe it would come back so I returned the dog to the house and steped outside for awhile but saw nothing else.
