Line of 10 or 12 lights over blue ridge mountain range

Driving Eastward along SC 11, as I passed over the lake keowee bridge, I looked off northward to the Ridgeline of the blue ridge mountains, approximately 6 miles to the north maybe 9 miles. Moving from west to east seemingly a long the ridgetops was a long line of approximately 1 dozen lights. They weren't in a perfect line, but a generalized line, as if convoying in a sloppy unregimented manner. These crafts did not behave and the typical way aircraft behave. For one thing, what's a string of 12 crafts doing a conga line in the sky over the ridgelines at 11:30 p.m. doing in the sky? I've had two other sightings nearby this location in the past year. Admittedly, this siding is not very compelling, but again water 12 lights spaced out over 2 miles following a Ridgeline about 500 ft above the Ridgeline doing in the sky at 11:30 at night in the middle of nowhere?
