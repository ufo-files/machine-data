(GS) 3 lights going over my apartment flying directly above me very low to the ground.

Around 12:30 i was standing outside, walking around my apartments, when all of a sudden i see lights flashing around in the distance in the sky. I stood there watching it for 15-20 minute thinking to myself, come on prove to that me youre real aliens. Thats when i saw something with 3 lights on it flying towards me. My first instinct was to run, but i stood there and watched it. I honestly thought i was about to die right there. As i was standing there, this object flew right above my head and made no noise until it was directly above me. It made the craziest roaring, low-toned humming sound. It flew off towards the north, i raced inside to grab my girlfriend before it left but it was too late. The other object was still there in the sky. Im hoping what i experienced happened to someone else. This was honestly one of the most intense moments in my life.
