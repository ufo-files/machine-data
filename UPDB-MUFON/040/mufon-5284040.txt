A white light to the north, low in the horizon changes shape and pulsates upon closer examination.

On Nov. 23, 2021 at approx. 1830 hours, (mountain time). From my yard, I observed a white star like light to the north of me.. After retrieving my 1080P video camera, I zoomed in to see more detail. The shape appeared to hover. I heard no sound. I estimated the shape to be approx. 11 miles north of me. The shape of the light changed from a circle to a baseball diamond shape to a diamond and square shapes. Inside the main shape, I noted a black round smaller shape which seemed to move in a circular motion within the main shape. Around the smaller black round shape were what a appeared to be spiral lights within the main shape. The object then moved southwest of me and remained to the point of me getting too cold to hold the camera steady.
