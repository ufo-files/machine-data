UAV/UFO light over North Denver area

Looking out a second story window noticed a bright light rise vertically and quickly into the sky. I estimate the light was 15-20 miles NE of my position. Against the dark night sky, it was a very bright, white light, and so I thought it was a plane incoming to Denver International Airport DIA) which is about twenty miles East of my location. 

The bright light seemed to glow and change color, it was not a blinking light as seen on airplanes. As I watched the light, it traveled South in a straight line but suddenly it reversed course in the opposite direction! At that point I knew it was not an airplane. I thought, a helicopter. As I watched the erratic movements, interspersed with stationary periods, I concluded it was not a helicopter either. The steady light emitted from the object was not characteristic of lights seen on planes or helicopters. I watched for about five minutes then got a pair of 10x50 binoculars. 

Using binoculars, I watched the object as the light alternated smoothly between bright white, red and occasionally blue. The object glowed; the lights were not blinking. At times it glowed red for several seconds but most of time it was bright white. The large object appeared spherical in shape with random black lines on its surface, see my rough drawing. 

I watched the object for about fifteen minutes and most of the time it appeared to stay in the same location, but the color of light continued to alternate mostly between red and white. At some point I noticed another bright light approaching from the East. After observing the second light I determined it was a plane leaving DIA. As the plane traveled in a NW direction towards the UAV/UFO light, the UAV/UFO light descended and disappeared.
