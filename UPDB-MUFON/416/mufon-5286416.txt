Orb moving strangely in and out of clouds

As I was being picked up to go to work one morning, we were driving down a steep hill away from my apartment where the skyline was in Clearview. They weren’t very many clouds but I noticed this like object moving strangely in and out of a cloud. It would come down a little moved to the right then move back up into the cloud and then come out at another place not very far away from where it went in. Then it just never came back out of the clouds. Except for the shape, color and how it was moving I would’ve just written the south. And for several years I just ignored it. But now I’m hearing other people tell similar stories and I’m wondering if what I saw wasn’t what I’m trying to believe it wasn’t.
