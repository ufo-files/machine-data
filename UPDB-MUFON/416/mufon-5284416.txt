Star Shaped Object In Sky (with video)

Back Story: I enjoy astronomy. I have a habit of always looking up at the sky both daytime and nighttime. I was outside at around 1:20 am. While watching a few YT vidoes, I would take a few seconds to look up at the sky and admire the stars..
. 
My encounter happened around 1:55 am. I looked at the sky and seen this. On my phone it appears to look like a circle. It wasn't. It was shaped like a 🌟 star. A literal star but appeared to have a softer look. 
.
It got my attention because I hadn't seen anything like it nor seen it during the time I've been outside. I wondered if it could've been a tall (very tall) light post. Then I wondered if it was a planet close to the earth. I even wondered if it was a star because it mimicked the flicker of the stars. 
.
I wasn't able to make sense of what I was seeing. So I had one job and two options. The job was to pull out my phone and the options were to record this or use an astronomy app to find out if it could identify what I was looking at. So, I chose to record. 
.
I was so focused on getting a great clip that I zoomed out when I wanted to zoom in. As soon as I went to zoom in on the object. I hear a car approaching. I moved out the way and faced my phone down. It took a few seconds to pick it back up because 1. It moved out of view. 2. I was looking for it.
.
I was not scared but curios and excited.
