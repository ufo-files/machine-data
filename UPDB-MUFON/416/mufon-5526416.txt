flashing light over Chicago

I was looking out my window when I saw this object. In the past, when I see something strange, it goes away quickly. However, this particular time the object hovered back and forth for about 20 minutes. It was in the sky for about 10 minutes before I started filming. Prior to filming, the object blinked red and orange. While I was filming, the object was only blinking two red lights. Once, I thought the object was out of view I stopped filming. Unfortunately I did not get on tape something very strange that I saw. I live in the fly zone of o-hare airport. So there are planes landing and taking off that fly by my condo. After I had turned off the camera, the object got close to an airplane. When it was close to the airplane the lights on the objected widened. This was a pretty strange occurrence.
