classical UFO round-up manuver

Just coming home and saw 6 bright objects converging together in the sky east by north east on an approximate path over the Delaware river. There were two other objects approaching from the South in route to join the first group. At that time two of the objects in the first group of six began to oscillate and move erratically at which point I immediately recognized that they were signaling to the other craft to leave the area, and I knew instinctively that these same two were about to disappear and they did. As the two from the south joined the remaining four of the first group of six two more disappeared and the were four left continuing due north two more of the group disappeared and the two remaining continued on course northward until they left from the line of sight.
