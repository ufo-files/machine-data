huge hovered then shot up through clouds on wet windy night

whilst driving home my partner and i noticed a large red light with an orange glowing center. we stopped and i got out of the car to take a pic. by the time the camera started the object shot straight up through the clouds. we could still see the lights through the clouds. it wasnt a lantern as the winds were high and it was raining, the object was stationary till it shot off. we initially shrugged this off but since seeing it we have had a few worrying experiences and feel we need to talk to someone about what is happening now.
