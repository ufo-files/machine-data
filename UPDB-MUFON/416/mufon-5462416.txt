{rjl} Dim orange orb formation, no noise.

I was standing looking out at the clear sky and i noticed very faint orange orbs heading over above me towards the mountains (heading north east), they were flying in a triangular pattern, no sound, one of them made a squiggly motion right and left in a distorted path... they then sped up all at the same time, as they re configured into a more linear formation and became more dim until I could barely make them out. I could tell they were just about 80 feet above the mountain peaks. i lost sight as they went over the north east mountains. It just wasn't a maneuver i could see any military craft making in aware of (squiggly motion) and accelerated VERY fast, not to the point they zipped out of view, fast enough to probably injure anyone in a human craft. they were probably 20' in diameter. it was very weird, i felt like i wasn't meant to see it. I wish i would have thought to record it, but it didn't even cross my mind in the moment.
