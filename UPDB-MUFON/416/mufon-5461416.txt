A bright light - short distance

A bright light that traveled a short distance and then went behind a cloud.

Was moving slowly.

I was riding as passenger in vehicle travel southeast on the State Hoghway 16 (NorthWestern Motorway)

I was located approximately by Rosebank road - the light was in front and to the left of me. It was high up - and went behind a cloud. I suspect it may have been sunlight reflecting off a plane - BUT it just did not look like a plane shape at all. There was no contrail - and that is not a usual flight path for planes.
