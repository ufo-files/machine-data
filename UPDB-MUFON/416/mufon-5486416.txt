{rjl} Saw a strange light in the sky while walking with my son.

While walking around the block with my son I noticed a strange light.  It looked like an afterburner to a jet but was silent and had no visible sign of any craft. Elongated fireball would be a good description but was a very consistent orange.  It was the brightest object I the night sky and travelled fault quick. Much quicker than any jet I've seen but slower than a shooting star. Maybe a missile test? Not many military bases nearby though. Strange enough to report.  Never saw anything like it in my life.
