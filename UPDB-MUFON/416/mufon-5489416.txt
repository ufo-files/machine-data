Star shaped followed me when I stopped it stopped then went vertical a high speed and out of sight

When I was walking home from primary school I noticed the object just above the power lines about 20 feet above me it was a dull silver 5 pointed star shape on its flat at first I thought it was a balloon but it seemed to be on the wrong orientation for a helium ballon and much too large as it I could see the power lines in front of it. As I stopped to look at it it stopped. I walked on watching it as I walked, it kept pace with me. I stopped again and it stopped. I lifted a stone from the ground and fired it at the object but missed. The object then went vertical upwards faster than I had ever seen anything move before and was out of sight in about 2 seconds. I was about 10 years old at the time and have never seen anything like this since, but this always disturbed me not knowing what this was. Since that day I have had an interest in UFOs but have never mentioned this incident to anyone except close friends until now.
