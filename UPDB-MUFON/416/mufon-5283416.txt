Stars, but under the clouds

I was driving north on Mountain View hwy, from about 7800 s to 201 in Salt Lake City.
I was what looked like 5 bright white lights north of me, north of SLC (possibly close to Bountiful UT).
They looked just like stars in the sky, but under the clouds and at 3pm. If this had happened at night on a clear sky, I wouldn't have noticed them because they looked exactly like stars.
I watched them while I drove (appx 10 minutes) and they did not appear to move at all in that time. 
I wanted to drive toward them because this looked very curious, but my wife was with me and she was afraid, so we continued on our way.
