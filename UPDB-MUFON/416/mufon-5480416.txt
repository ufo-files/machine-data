{nd} Multiple objects with pulsating white light that transforms into a read orb. Objects moved independent and randomly of each other and at times were in sync. Clear night no obstruction.

My son who had been deer hunting called me at approximately 8:45 PM central time and alerted me that he had seen strange lights on the evening of 8/4/2016 and was seeing them again. His hunting location is approximately 20 miles east of my residence. I thought I would step out to see if there was anything visible from my house and indeed I immediately saw what he was describing to me on the phone. I called my wife to come outside and grabbed my binoculars. We all witnessed the lights described above for approximately 1 hour.
