{dr}cylindrical ufo over Kentucky

My husband, toddler grandson, and I went outside to see some new blooms on our flowers. I always watch the sky and happened to spot a cylindrical, wingless object coming from the southwest. I pointed the object out to my husband (a skeptic), and he asked, "What is that!" I told him that it was the same type of ufo that I had seen a few years ago. The object appeared to be a silver-white color, emitted no sound or trail, and had a dark spot in the middle which could have been a bay of windows. The object seemed to be traveling too high to be a blimp. It then turned eastward toward Lunken Airport. I really would like to know if there was a blimp in the sky today, and did Lunken see anything on its radar. I have only a feeling of self-satisfaction for being given the opportunity to view a possible ufo. Also, I am happy that my husband also witnessed this sighting. We lost sight of the object as it continued toward the east.
