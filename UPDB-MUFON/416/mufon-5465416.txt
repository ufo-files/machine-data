{jpg} Star-like object hovers and moves over river & farms for several hours.

This object has been seen several times in the area at the sighting location. It is star-like, but moves; it appears for an hour, and up to three hours, at night. It is bright. From the video you can see some structure. A pulse came from it on this night too. It seems to hang over the Flint River, and over the nearby farms. I first noticed it outside at night, and have gone back to the same location and sometimes it is there. I didn't know what it was.
