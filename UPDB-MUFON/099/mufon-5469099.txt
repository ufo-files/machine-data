Pulsating light spiting and gathering smaller orbs.

It was warm summer evening sun just went down but was still bright enough, as sky was without clouds and stars clearly visible. For of us played in the back garden and then we noticed strange star in the sky compared to other stars it was 20 times larger and changed colors more interesting was we noticed a smaller objects size as a small star shooting out of the main object an in curved trajectory in 2 seconds disappearing out of view ... and then returning back from slightly different direction (about 5 minutes lower if using clock arrow as example) smaller objects were during acceleration where one color and on return other color as well can be seen speed difference on launch acceleration disappearance - appearance deceleration and docking. On same points it was possible to see 2-3 objects leaving main body and others returning. We observed main object in the sky hovering in the same spot for a view hours with the orbs jumping out- disappearing and appearing returning back to main object every few minutes. Smaller object disappeared in the sky about 2 foots from the main object (from our perspective on the ground) and appeared again just below or above never in the same spot in a few seconds.

We had no cameras or other recorders to record a siting.

Before i went to sleep i still could see lights in the sky.
