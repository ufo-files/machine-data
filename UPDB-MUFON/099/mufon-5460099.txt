{rjl} 3 objects in sky

I witnessed 3 bright objects in the sky, one north, one northwest and one west. I observed them with a telescope, they appeared 3 dimensional, nearly transparent with rings or panels all around the sides. 1 object had a circle of lights around the top and bottom, clear in color, did not blink. I was able to observe all 3 objects for around an hour before cloud cover obscured them. I heard no noise, did not see anything emit from the objects.
