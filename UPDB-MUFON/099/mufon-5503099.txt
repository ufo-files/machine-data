{kt}Cigar shaped amber light SW of Casa Grande, AZ

My husband and I were driving Southbound on Arizona Highway 387 (North Pinal Avenue) in Casa Grande when we observed a amber cigar shaped light to the Southwest of us at 45 degree's above the horizon. It was 21:30 on a clear evening. The light was very bright and there was a smaller white light next to it. It was there for just a few seconds and then immediately disappeared. Use to seeing many aircraft in the area as they approach Phoenix Sky Harbor but the size, shape and color were unlike any other aircraft we have ever seen in the area before.
