I went outside on July 17,2020 at around 9;15pm to say goodnight to my sister and niece. as they were leaving in their car I looked up and saw something flying by. The space station is the bright white dot in the middle. The object is in the top right corner of the picture. I do not know if it is a reflection or anything else. It looks like it has lights around the outside and it looks transparent and disc like. Just thought I would share it. Maybe it is nothing, but it is very odd to me. Sorry if I am wasting your time. I did not notice it with the naked eye, since I was watching the space station. The space station was coming in the WSW direction. The picture was taken at exactly 9:18 pm.
