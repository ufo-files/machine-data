Hanging under moon

On 12/12/11 at 7:30 am I was driving North on Waverly Road in Lansing. I looked over at the moon which was still fully visible in the Western sky. It looked like there was a craft hanging under the moon. I did believe it to be an airplane, but what was strange was that the craft was fully lit up.  Airplanes aren't typically lit in entirety. As I continued to watch it, it began moving and was, perception wise, at a level that was higher than the moon. I noticed a flashing light, but again the entire craft was very bright and I was having getting a good look at it and was also was driving. I continued to watch for it, but I lost visibility of it. There was no contrail, and it was much lower than a jet would have been traveling. When I arrived home, I got out of my car and noticed a jet in the sky that was traveling south and it clearly had a contrail. The jet was much higher than the object I saw and being a jet, it was barely visible in front of the contrail.
