Large bright perfect square fell through cloud cover, have 8-9 pictures with 757 for size reference in several pictures

This is from the email I sent picture to friends: The attached pictures were taken at approximately 4:30 to 4:45 pm, looking west from Robert St (south side of St Paul MN where it meets the 494 bypass. These are raw pictures taken with a Cannon Power Shot A1100IS camera with a 12.1 mega pixel capacity and a 4 X Optical Zoom(4X IS lens,)(6.2-24.8mm/1:2.7-5.6)

I first took notice of this object as we were travelling North on Robert St when I had my wife, who was driving, pull over onto a side road so I could get a better look at this event. She told me she had her digital camera in the car and I grabbed it and took a set of pictures that showed the various stages of progression of this event.

Being a trained military AO and having worked within the aviation industry for over 25 years, I immediately realized this was something way out of the ordinary. I know aircraft, can identify most US and foreign aircraft all the way back to the 20s, I know jets, rockets, props, rotorcraft, blimps and hot air balloons. 

In pictures 667 and 670 you should use your computer zooming as it shows the relative size of the "thing" in question to a 757 type airliner. I was approximately 4-5 miles from MSP and this was further out, I estimate approximately 6 miles.

You will see this cloud, or what ever it is, showing a path through the adjacent clouds, its shape approximately square. Please note that the sun was setting behind it so the lines through the cloud cover is definitely not a shadow, as a shadow would only be seen if the sun were on this side of it.

Also, when you zoom in you will notice 5 clouds that are approximately the same shape and situated below and around the area of descent.

I viewed this for over 15 minutes, and did not, unfortunately, witness it actually progressing through the clouds. The entire time my wife and I viewed it it stayed relatively in place, eventually dissipating but still showing the residual of its obvious descent.

If any of you have a clue as to what this is, was, or could be, I would be most interested. I do not possess a program that can do more than enlarge the pictures. Perhaps if someone out there has something that can do a detailed pixilation it would be more helpful.
