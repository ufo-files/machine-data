It was a orange yellow light that made jerking motions then took of super fast and vannished

I work at a Walmart in the Lakeside/El Cajon CA area and one night while I was working outside around 10-11 pm I looked up to the northern sky and I saw a yellowish orange light slowly moving across the sky. I new that it wasnt a plane because I work by an airport and I can easily tell the airplanes and helicopters that flood the night sky because of their blinking lights and at this time of night there usually are no planes in the sky. Also I could tell that this object was really high in the sky because at first I mistook it for a satalight in orbit passing by, but then suddenly I new that it wasnt a satalight because it stopped in mid-air. Then it slowly moved in a downward sothern motion and then in a upward northern motion and it then started moving all around. After it did all of those motions in a time span of around 5 minutes it seemed to either take off really fast or it just dissapeared. After that I didn't think much of it because it was close to the end of my shift and I was tired and wanting to return home, but then about a month later another person who I work with who lives in El Cajon said that he saw a light stop in mid-air and do motions very simmilar to the one I saw. It shocked me because I didn't tell anyone about what I saw and I almost forgot about even seeing it. I was always a skeptick about UFO sightings and I thought that people who claimed to see UFOs were just flat out liers, but now I don't know what to think.
