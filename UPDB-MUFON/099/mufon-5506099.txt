{rjl} a silver football lile object in the sky hovering under the clouds

It was many years ago and I didn't think of sharing my brief story with the world until my mother brought it up to me. 
I was young, around the age of 9 and I was at my home in Oregon City, that is now my grandmothers home. I was standing in the front of the house in the street. I remember this as though it happened just yesterday. 
The clouds were pure white, and it was about 3pm
There were no objects to be seen in the sky, except for one.I looked at it for a few moments. Trying to figure out what it was. It was a silver football shaped object. It stood stationary in the sky. And ill never forget what I was thinking to my self, too young to understand the idea of another life form. I was thinking, was this a flying plane was someone playing with a toy? I kept looking, and it remained stationary. Not moving, perfectly silver no rough edges, just sitting there under the clouds. I was afraid to take my eyes away thinking it would start moving and I would follow it. It did not move, I looked go the other side of the sky and when I looked back it was gone. 
The image is burned in my head and its a memory ill never forget, despite all the other memories that have escaped my mind over the years. 
I told my mom about it that same night, we never talked about it again until I asked her if she remebers what I told her I saw.
She said yes of course she does, I asked her if she thinks we are alone in the Universe, and she said "No." For my mom to say that ad a god fearing women, put chills to my spine.
