{cak} Balls of light

Driving home from work i noticed what i thought was a shooting star. I then saw another one and kept my eyes on the sky as much as i could as i was driving. my trip was a bout 30 minutes up a mountain then down the mountain into my town i live in . the entire way i saw these lights dance around above the clouds and sometime below the clouds. I turned into a pull off where i had a good view of them when i saw another man watching them to. He told me he had been following the lights from a different town an entire county away.
