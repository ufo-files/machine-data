Warp like incidents

I was at our hunting lease located about 3 miles north east of the Laguna Atascosa Wildlife Refuge. It was at or about 10 pm. It was a clear night no clouds and I started to look at the big Dipper who was very bright and easily distinguishable in the night sky, at the very bottom of the Dipper I observed a very bright flash of light that lasted about two seconds, shortly after I saw a white sphere travel into the same area I saw the flash of light and it disappeared in same area. I have seen satellites and you can track a satellite but this one just disappeared. I had seen a flash of light one time before this sighting it was around December during hunting season and around the same time as this one. The closest I can describe these incidents would be like when a ship goes into warp. Like the Enterprise in Star Trek. I could not get any video footage due to how fast these incidents happen but the brightness was intense.
