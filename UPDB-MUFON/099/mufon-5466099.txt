{jpg} 20-25 orange orbs observed

Shortly before Thanksgiving 2013 I and two companions were driving home from work in northeastern Vermont. The time was approximately 5:30 pm and the sky was completely dark. As we approached my house we noticed several bright objects in the northern sky. Within thirty seconds we arrived at my house and excited the car. We could now see 20-25 orange colored orbs in the sky. They emitted no noise. They possessed no defined features. The objects appeared to be in a loose formation; they appeared to stay the same distance from one another and to drift slowly. In size I would say they were slightly larger than the largest star or planet. The sky was partially cloudy that evening and the objects drifted in and out of the clouds. I would estimate the ceiling that night st 3000-5000 feet. The clouds afforded me depth perception and I could tell the objects were not out in space. After 3-5 minutes if observation, the objects disappeared. They did not fly off, they vanished. I recall thinking at the time that what I had just witnessed was outside if my ability to identify. I am convinced these objects were not planes, satellites, stars, flares, etc. I have waited several years to report this but I will never forget what I saw and am convinced the objects were not oif this world. It has changed my thinking from accepting the possibility of UFOs to knowing they exist. I am curious as to whether other similar sightings were made around this time and location.
