Sitting at stop light at intersection of 776 and US 41 in Venice, FL. Noticed extremely large rectangular shaped object almost directly overhead with green lights surrounding the entire craft. It was a UFO without a doubt.It hovered for about 1 to 2 mins before a quick exit toward the north. I along with my passenger just stared at the object and said 'do you see that?'. We observed it for about a minute, then it quickly disappeared to the north without any notice, noise or sound. I was stunned. the lights reminded me of a green neon sign that I saw in a business window that could have been for O' Douls beer. It disappeared instantaneously.
