{tjd} Very brightly lit cloud like that morfed into a ring

Thought I heard someone talking outside? I stepped outside my shop and saw no one. I did notice a stationary very bright light due West of my home that was brighter and bigger than any object I had ever seen in the night sky. I am about 2 miles inland from the Oregon coast and I have a Florence address but my residence is actually in Dunes City. 

I am a long term private pilot and have done considerable night flying. This object was extremely bright and VERY large. As I watched the Mood continued it's track to the west but the object seemed to be in geosynchronous orbit and stayed in the same position

I used my Kyocera cell to take still pictures. I took several that were free-standing and blurred so I leaned against my shop and got 4 more that turned out pretty good. It was cold and I had no coat so I just came in. I looked out in a few minutes. and it was gone. The next day I opened the pictures on my computer and enlarged the photo and found out the anomaly was a ring!

I would describe the object as a dimensional portal or jump gate?
