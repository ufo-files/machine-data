{cak} Oscillating orange light. Came straight at us from the west. Vectored and ascended.

We had stopped home to pick up a laptop I was working on for a bartender. As I got back in the car, and turned west, we saw what looked like a hot air balloon on fire. There was fire, and the object was oscillating right-to-left. It came straight at us, then vectored sharply without banking to the northeast. Stopped and ascended, disappearing completely. Whole thing took about 45 seconds.
