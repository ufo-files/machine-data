{rjl} Observed flying craft traveling from west to east. Traveled slowly and seemed to hover for a bit, and then proceeded away.

About 10:10 EST on 7/30,2019. I witnessed in the western sky a slowly moving object. At first, I thought it to be a helicopter but it had 3 bright white lights that looks like a helicopter search light. Woke my wife up to see, she was less impressed. I rushed down to my deck and it flew right over my head. It appeared to be black in color and in the shape of a triangle. on the 3 corners were a bright white light like a search light. in the middle was a red light that seemed to randomly blinked. I watch as it traveled East. As it went off in the distance ,looking at it, it seemed to hover for 15-20 seconds because as it traveled away, the space between it and the top of the trees stagnated and then it went off out of site. In all I say it lasted 2 minutes. It moved at the speed of a slow flying helicopter, but certainly wasn't that because it didn't look or sound. I made a sound but wasn't a jet sound. It did make a noise, but I don't know how to describe it. For disclosure , I was a HAWK Missile Radar Repairer in the Marine Corps in the late 80's. I've been to Nellis AFB on operations and have seen many different types of aircraft. Also would like to disclose that I am very interested in this subject and have prepared for the day that I saw one that I would pay attention to detail so that I could recount the details.
