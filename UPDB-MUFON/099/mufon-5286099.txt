Strange blinking and reflecting object, changed direction and height

Location: 93095 Hagelstadt, Germany. GPS: 48o53'41.65'' North, 12o13'17.15'' East
Observation time: August 15, 2021, 11 - 11:15 local.

Report: On August 15 at about 11:00 hrs I suddenly noticed a strong flash from our house terrace in western direction (estimated 280 degrees). This repeated at intervals of seconds, with no exact frequency detectable. I estimated the distance in the range 10 - 20 km and the altitude in the range between 5 and 10 km. For the first 1- 2 minutes it looked like there was air turbulence around the location of the flash. The flashing object then continued to move at the same altitude in a general southerly direction within the observation cone that I attach on a Google Earth map. The flashing and flare did not subside, but I thought I could tell that the "frequency" was decreasing slightly. When the object almost reached the southern edge of the observation cone (estimated at 250o) I got my 8x binoculars and could see that it was an solid object. One side flashed up in the sunlight, the other side showed a brown-black color (maybe burned?). I could not tell if anything was hanging below this object. I estimated the size of the object to be in the range of a single-family house, (a good 10 meters in diameter for a sphere).
The object drifted now with same height further in general direction west-west south . After about 2 minutes it changed course and came back almost on the same path, but now it rose significantly within a few seconds (about 15), I estimate about 3 km. The flashing and blinking decreased more and more after that and I lost the object out of sight.
Attached I have the map and a picture, which I succeeded to take with about 2.5 times magnification with my mobile phone right at the beginning of the observation.

I don´t know if the object was a weatherballoon or not. I t was strange.
