ginormous balls/orbs/fire balls of light

Myself and my fiancé were driving north on route 3 in Kingston heading towards Pembroke ma. After we passed under the overpass of exit 9, we noticed over the Kingston Budweiser plant were huge balls/orbs of fire/light off so far in the distance, that from our judgement the orbs of fire light we observed were definitely above the ocean which is not less than a mile away. We captured it on video while driving on the highway. We got off the next available exit 10 and rushed to the direction of the ocean so we could get to a clearing to observe closer what we both witnessed. We reached a conservation field in duxbury Ma, by the time we got there one of the 3 orbs started to fade away and then immediately the 2nd then the 3rd. They all disappeared in a matter of seconds. There were 2 other vehicles parked at this conservation land parking lot that had stopped because they witnessed the same orbs of light that we did. After the rods disappeared, my fiancés camera started picking up a very strange orb that seemed to be evading his camera (we also have footage of). It was nothing I’ve ever seen before or since.
