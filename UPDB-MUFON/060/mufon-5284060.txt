One craft with five lights in an obtuse angle or boomerang shape.

Drove home from San Bernardino through the 241. Felt very uneasy as that toll road going through the hills was very dark and completely void of other cars for about ten miles towards the end, near rancho Santa Margarita. At the end of the 241, I drove into Dana point (which is much more populated and lit up) i parked, my friend and i got out of the car and headed toward the house and we both saw the spacecraft fly past us. I got the impression that we may have been followed through the isolated canyon area until going back into town and becoming too much of an exposure risk to actually act upon.
