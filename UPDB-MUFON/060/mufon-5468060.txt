DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE UFO EVENT:
MY WIFE, STEPSON AND MYSELF DROVE TO A CAMPING SITE WE LIKE OFF AZ ROUTE 93, THE MAIN HIGHWAY BETWEEN LAS VEGAS AND PHOENIX. THIS CAMPING SITE IS JUST BELOW WIKIUP APPROX 10 MILES. SHORTLY AFTER GETTING THERE A NUMBER OF YOUNG ADULTS SHOWED UP AND WERE MAKING A NUISANCE OF THEMSELVES. WE DECIDED TO LEAVE. IT WAS ABOUT 9:00 PM AND DARK. WE TRAVELED SOUTH ON ROUTE 93 TO ROUTE 96 WE TRAVELED UNTIL WE FOUND A HIGHWAY MAINTENANCE SITE NEAR A BRIDGE. WE PULLED BACK ON THE BLACK TOP AND PARKED OVERLOOKING A SMALL STREAM AND A RANCH ON THE OTHER SIDE WITH A COUPLE OF HERDS OF CATTLE ON IT. I ALMOST GOT TO SLEEP WHEN I NOTICED A LOW FLYING OBJECT MOVING TOWARDS US SLOWLY. AS I WATCHED I WOKE MY WIFE AND WE BOTH WATCHED THIS THING COMING CLOSER. AS IT APPROACHED WE NOTICED A STRANGE BEAM THAT TURNED ON AND OFF, SIDE TO SIDE AS THOUGH THEY WERE LOOKING FOR SOMETHING. ALSO WE NOTICED THE CATTLE MOOING LOUDER AND LOUDER. I HAD A CAM RECORDER IN MY CAB AND ATTEMPTED TO GET IT OUT TO TAKE PICTURES. BUT BEFORE I COULD GET IT GOING THE UFO RETRACED IT MOVEMENTS MOVING SOUTH AGAIN. AS IT MOVED WE NOTICED THAT CLOUDS FORMED THAT ABSORBED THE UFO MOVING SOUTH. WE SAW NOTHING MORE AND WE WERE SO SCARED THAT WE DECIDED TO DRIVE BACK HOME EVEN THOUGH WE WERE VERY TIRED.
