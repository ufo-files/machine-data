{nd} Woke up to super bright light out my window.

I woke to a bright light at 2am. Super moon bright. I thought it was the moon or a light from a property behind my home but it wasn't the moon and there are no lights behind my house. Just large trees. I could see it through the trees and then it was more clear. I got nervous. Woke my husband and the light dimmed. My husband saw the dimmed light but assured me I was ok. I laid back down and the light was so bright I could see all of my trees (as bright as a baseball feild light) I jumped up and grabbed my son from his room and laid him next to me. Closed the door and the light dimmed. I stared at it for a few minutes then it was gone and complete darkness outside. Couldn't see a thing. My dog started to growl uncontrollably and my husband took her to walk the house until the dog was more comfortable. We all laid back down and fell asleep. Wish I took video this went in for 20 minutes or longer.
