{cak} Blue light that quickly changed direction and speed.

I was walking my girlfriend to her car and as soon as we stepped outside we saw a bright blue light in the north/ north west part of the sky. At first we thoughy it was a star or a helicopter but we soon realized that is was neither. The Arial movments it was making was unlike any modern craft known. It would move up then down then side to side and diagonally. We watched it for a good 2 min before I rushed inside and got my brother so we could have more witnesses. We all watched it for another 5min at that point it blinked off and my mother came out. Then it came back and then was pulsing soon after it pulsed it zoomed off. I was shaking from the adrenaline. And we all felt scared and confused.
