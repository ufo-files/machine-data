5 red/orange lights over Canfield, Ohio

Last night, my wife, our friends, and I were in the back yard at 10:15 pm. We noticed a bright red/orange light on the horizon, which passed overhead in a SW to NE direction. As the first one was passing over, I noticed a second one coming from the same direction. I retrieved my binoculars for a better view. It was a dark, clear sky, and I could see no details - wings, rotors, etc. There was also no sound. A total of 4 - 5 identical objects passed over in the same direction. There is likely a very basic explanation for this, but I thought it best to report. A brief video of the event is attached.
