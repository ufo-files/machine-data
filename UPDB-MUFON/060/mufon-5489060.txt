{cak} High speed straight ball of bright white without tail traveling west to east. Thought it was crashing airplane or space junk and think it was going as fast as a landing space shuttle.

My adult daughter and I were driving east on Hwy 80 in Brandon, MS and we both saw a white ball speeding east in the dark sky at about 8:30 PM. It appeared to be on a long trajectory to hit earth at some distance away and was lost to the horizon in seconds. There was no apparent tail but felt that it could have been a crashing airplane or something from space. The only other thing that I have ever seen traveling at this speed was a returning space shuttle in the daytime which was trailing smoke. The local news media did not report anyone seeing what we saw. I am unable to speculate on the altitude or actual size of the object however from my point of vision the ball was about the size of an aspirin or M&M candy. I did not report what I saw except this report
