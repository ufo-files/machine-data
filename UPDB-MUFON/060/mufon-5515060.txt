{cak} Came in from the south and continued north. Round, dull, glow.

Me and my girlfriend were fixing to sit outside our home ( backyard) to hangout on a cloudy night. She was trying to make a phone call and her phone would not work. So I went in to get my phone. As soon as I walked into the house I could hear her mumbling my name over and over. I ran out and saw she was looking up. As I looked up I saw a round, dull glow, flying towards the north. As I looked at it, it felt like my eyes were being pulled away from it. So I stopped looking as I was going to lose sight anyways cause of the house. I then turned to my girlfriend, and asked her if she was okay? 
When my girlfriend was looking at it and mumbling my name. She said it did something to her eyes, like it didn't want her to focus on it. But she had no fear. I am used to these events and I don't fear much. But when I could not focus on it I felt something.
