I saw 5 lights flashing in sequence in western sky ont once, but twice

FILLED OUT FROM A PAPER REPORT SENT IN TO MUFON HQ, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR ON SEPTEMBER 3, 2020. HAD TO MAKE UP DATE FOR REPORT, WITNESS SAID OCT 2005
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Qct. 2005 on Bible Hill in Ryegate, VT, three of us were burning firewook brush. We had a large fire. I turned to my right facing west and saw 5 large lights flashing in sequence in the dark sky. The lights appeared to be from 60 to 70 feet above ground, and about 20 to 30 ft across. They flashed for 5 to 10 minutes, then went out. One friend had to go home. Soon after he left, the lights reappeared. They flashed in sequence for another 5 minutes, then dissapeared for good. 
It was my thought that they were signaling us to exstinguesh the fire. But they were certainly trying to tell us something. Due to past experience, I had no doubt that we were witnessing an alien craft. I was watching the light sequence only. My friend said he thought the craft was a triangle shape. I was at once curious, thrilled, and awe struck at the size, procimity of the ship, and the brazen way they had approached us. I can never forget it, and while the three of us witnesses are still alive, I wanted to report this incenent.
