{jpg} Oval shaped aircraft with triangle upper body. 3 white lights very quiet

At around 615pm, I was on my way home from work when I noticed bright lights in the trees coming from an easterly direction. I kept watching and assumed it was a helicopter but as it got closer I realized the shape didn't make sense and it had too many lights on it. I stopped in the road and turned down my music to try to hear it so it would help me identify it. The craft passed directly over my car probably 200 ft above the treeline. It looked like it might have been a helicopter body or oval shape with triangle upper attachment. Each end of the triangle had a white light. It also had flashing lights that resembled formation lights. The craft was moving slower than an airplane or helicopter and faster than a hot air balloon. But the sound was unlike anything I have heard from an aircraft. The best description would have to be air gushing from an air compressor nozzle.
