{cak} Apparent cylindrical object with orange glow and orange trail in flight

My 8 yr old noticed it first while walking our dog. She asked "Daddy, what's that?" My 7 yr old can be heard on the video asking questions about it. My phone camera kept going in and out of focus, which is unfortunate, likely the result of my 7 yr old tugging on me, and me trying to balance the phone while holding the leash with the dog. As a result, there are only some seconds in the video where the object is fully in focus. The object didn't appear and disappear as in the video, it was visible the entire time it was in our field of view and was on a steady trajectory. The object made no audible sound. An airplane at that elevation and apparent distance might have been heard. There was another plane in the area, not sure in which direction, I could only hear its sound, which is captured in the video, and it sounded like it was relatively close to us, so it couldn't have been the object we were observing in the distance. The object appeared cylindrical to the naked eye, with a distinct orange glow and orange trail behind it. It could have been reflecting the sunset, as it was observed to the East of us. It was flying neither too fast, nor too slow and appeared to not ascend or descend significantly.
