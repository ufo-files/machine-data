I was flying my drone (A parrot bebop 2) shooting video at 1080p 30 frames per second, just ascending to do a 7 minute flight, it wasnt until i reviewed the video that I saw the object fly in and out of exactly one frame at an incredible rate of speed.
At first I didnt notice it cause the speed seems to almost make it invisible. then, for some reason, something caught my eye. 
I played it back several times, then i slowed down the video using imovie. If i have the frame rate speed correct that means it moved in 0.04 seconds .
I heard no sound, and at its trajectory it would have blown apart the trees or made some sort of sound and would have hit the ground of the hill it was heading to.
It was a quite, clear day, and there was hardly any traffic so it was practically silent. At the rate its going if it was man made, or a meteor i would have heard something, but i didnt.
