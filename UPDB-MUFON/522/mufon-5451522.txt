Ball of light crossing over my head.

After work at about 2300 hrs a ball of light traveled in a SW to NE direction over my land. It moved on a path with slight and random changes in direction. It made no noise. A few times it appeared to either go behind clouds or disappear only to return. There were no significant clouds this night. I took a video. There was a commercial jet to the north traveling W to E. The object was moving towards that plane.  This object was not on my flight tracking app.
