{cak} Two bright lights that appeared oval and interacting with each other.

I witnessed two bright lights that looked either oval or disc like hovering above a ridge line. The coordinates from Google maps are: 39.138771 / -78.485454. I saw this along with two other people at around 8:20pm. The lights glowed, brightened, and then dimmed and lasted for about two to three minutes before shooting straight up to a few thousand feet. The objects then made a sharp almost 90 degree turn to the North. There was no sound and these were not drones or any other known aircraft. As for the size they looked to be maybe 50 1 100 feet long. We did find some debris that looked burnt but was not able to determine if the debris were related to the event.
