{jd} I saw a large, stationary, hovering object with lights above Butler Blvd

I was driving home around 11:00pm going West on Butler Blvd. At 11:07 I noticed lights in the sky ahead of me to the left of the highway just above the tree line. At first, I logically thought it was an airplane, but as I got closer I realized that it was not moving at all, and it was also extremely low for how big of an object it was. My next thought was that it was a helicopter of some sort, but as I got even closer, I could not see any type of propellers on it, and there was no noise coming from it. When I got the closest, I was almost underneath it. I could see that it was triangular in shape, and had a big bright, almost white light facing the direction i had been coming from, and some red and blue lights on the corners of the triangle with a small light on the back 'corner'. The bottom was a glowing orange/red-ish color, but I'm not sure if it was a reflection of the city lights or if it was glowing itself. I watched it (not moving) on Butler Blvd for a good 5 minutes, while driving, before I exited on Southside Blvd South. I could still see it to my left while I was on Southside, and pulled into FSCJ Deerwood campus to stop and watch it. By the time I pulled around to the front of the campus where I would have been able to see it perfectly, it was gone. This was 10 minutes after I had initially seen the object. Although I dont think I could give an exact estimation of its size in feet, it was very big and obvious in the sky. At arms length, its apparent size was roughly softball sized. I would be shocked if I were the only one paying any attention to it, but I am very curious to know if anyone else saw it.
