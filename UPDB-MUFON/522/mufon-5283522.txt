3 balls of light in Triangle formation

Live in SE Michigan but work in Perrysburg OH. This morning approx 6:40 am I happened to look up while sitting in my truck before I clocked in. My parking space faces east and west. I back in my truck. So I’m facing west. I happened to look out my driver side window thinking I saw a light in the sky move. Didn’t see anything initially so I looked at my phone. Then I happen to roll down my window and looked up 60 or 70 degrees approx. I’m awful at guessing which. Saw a triangle shaped formation of 3 small white/orange lights moving slowly headed north. For maybe 5 seconds they slowly faded and disappeared. Did not look like 1 object rather all individual. They all faded at the same time. I just stared at it squinting as they dissolved trying to see if they reappeared.
