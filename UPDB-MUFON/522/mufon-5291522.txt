Shiny silver colored star shaped object with orange lights on appendages flew just north of house at slow rate of speed then disappeared

While gathering vegetables in the garden, I spotted a shiny silver colored star shaped object with orange lights on the appendages flying just north of the house at a slow rate of speed going from NW to NE. There was no sound at all. The object appeared to be about one mile to one and a half miles up. There was no cloud cover and the sun was shining. As it moved past, I kept the object in sight and watched it turn a gradual turn slightly to the North and just vanish. Not 30 seconds after it vanished a black jet flew near the house going from NE to NW at about the same altitude. This occurred between 9:45 AM and 10:15 AM EDT on October 14, 2020. There were no other witnesses on the ground. There had been some single engine planes flying around earlier to the South of the house. These were propeller driven and made noise. I could not see any type of engine on the object. Nor were there any visible signs of a means of propulsion to the naked eye. No contrails.
