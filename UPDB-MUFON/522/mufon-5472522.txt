{jpg} triangle like with lights on every point

I was driving to a location, that I I was staying in Loganville and went to the Quiktrip that was a few miles away, I was driving on a road and turned the curve and observed a triangle floating over a house as I approached a stop sign, for some reason I turned to the side of the road and turned my lights off and became filled with dread seeing this object,I could not stop staring at the object which seemed dull like a smooth rock and there were white lights on every point of the object and I stared at it for about 20 seconds and it shot up into the sky very fast and unnaturally and the light in the house turned on and I turned my lights back on and sped home, I remember feeling dread and having nightmares through out the night and a few weeks after my sighting.
