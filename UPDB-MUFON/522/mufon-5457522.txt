{cak} UAP sighting and disappearance.

On the evening of Tuesday October 15,2019 while on break at work,I observed a shimmering light which caught my eye SE of where I was. Where I work is on a hill on the edge of town,the object appeared to be about 3-5 miles away. The light appeared to be translucent and at one point the object appeared to have a tail(s) beneath it,making it resemble a jellyfish due to the distance I was from it.This lasted for about 10-15 seconds and then it disappeared right to left as if it went behind a curtain.
