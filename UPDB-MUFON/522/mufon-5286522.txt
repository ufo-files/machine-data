UFO IN THE SKY

ACCORDING TO THE WITNESS, IT WAS 1 AM, AND THERE WERE NO CLOUDS IN THE SKY. HE WENT TO CLOSE THE WINDOW WHEN HE SAW THE UFO UP IN THE SKY. IT DIDN'T KEEP STILL. IT MOVED A LITTLE BIT TO ALL DIRECTIONS. HE COULD SEE THAT THERE WERE SOME RED FLASHES THAT MOVED FROM THE MAIN LIGHT (DRAWING ATTACHED). HE TRIED TO PHOTOGRAPH WITH HIS CELL PHONE AND ALSO WITH A MANUAL CAMERA, AND GOT IT (EVIDENCES ATTACHED), BUT THE RED BEAMS DID NOT APPEAR. HE STAYED FOR ALMOST 30 MINUTES WATCHING THE UFO, WHEN HE DECIDED TO GO TO BED.
