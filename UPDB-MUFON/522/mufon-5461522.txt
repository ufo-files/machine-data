Triangular pattern of 4round lights

I was looking up at the sky to see if any stars where visible although it was a cloudy night when I noticed what i first thought was an aeroplane , as I watched it became clear that there was in fact a triangular pattern of bluefish coloured lights with a fourth light in the centre . It flew across the sky for approximately 8 to 10 seconds then disappeared for sight . It was after about 6 seconds that I realised there was no sound from the object although it appeared to be quite close .
