April 15 2021
Mid afternoon. Bright sun, clear sky. Object judged to be 500m to 1000m away. Initial altitude judged to be approx 600 meters above ground. Size estimated at approx 2 to 3 meters in length.

Object resembled a tooth paste box flying in a vertical position. Object dark on top 1/3, evenly spaced flashing on surface of middle 1/3, possible whitish color bottom 1/3.

Possible object may have been rotating along central axis and flashing may have been result of sun reflecting off center 1/3 surface. 

Object initially moving sw to ne from Vancouver BC toward North Shore mountains. Object decending on shallow angle of perhaps 5 to 10 degrees. Object continued decending and disappeared behind treeline at base of mountain. There seemed little doubt that the object was under some sort of control.

Approx 3 min later, object re appeared climbing above tree line and began to ascend up the side of the mountain. Object maintained same speed throughout and continued to emit evenly spaced flashing even at ground level which suggested flashing was being generated within the object and was not from the sun reflecting off its surface.

Object continued ascending all the way to the crest of the mountain and continued beyond. It climbed through the single cloud above mountain top, re appeared above it and then continued climbing until it disappeared from view.
