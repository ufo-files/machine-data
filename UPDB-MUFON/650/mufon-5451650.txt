Brilliant lights

I was driving in Bellevue, headed north on 36th street. I was close to the area where Old 36th Street meets the main road and I looked up in the sky. I was towards the highest point of the road around 1:00-2:00 pm. I always enjoy looking at the sky in this area because there are no buildings or anything in the way. I saw at least 30 white “lights” in a vertical formation that varied in width. They were exceptionally brilliant. In about 3 seconds, they vanished. Nothing visible, no clouds to hide behind. I kept looking, was going to pull over, and then boom, there they were again. Hard to describe how brilliant they were. I saw them the second time, and then about two seconds later they were gone. No sign. No trace. No birds in the sky. I realize this is a "lights in the sky" report, but it was not anything typical in my observation.
