I was coming from a friend's house and saw a bunch fire trucks blocking the street. I looked up and saw a solid dull blood red light hoovering just about the tree lines. The light were coming up Market street to intersect Baltimore street. The light paused above the intersect point of Market and Baltimore for a few seconds and shot vertically up until you could not see the light. I ask my friend............Did you just see that?
