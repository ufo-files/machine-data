{td} On Aug. 21, 2017, while photographing the Solar Eclipse, I have an unexplained object in 2 photos.

On Aug. 21, 2017 at around 11am, I was taking pictures of the Total Solar Eclipse. The camera, a Sony DSC-300 (35X Optical Zoom)was on normal setting for the first photo, then I zoomed in a little for the second photo, back to normal for the third and back in for the forth and back to normal for the rest of the photos. After I was done, I downloaded the pictures onto my computer, it was only later when I went into the pictures that I noticed the object in two of the photos (when I zoomed in close). I didn't know what it was, however I do know it isn't a weather balloon or swamp gas. I can only speculate that when the sun was coming out of the Eclipse is when I lost it.
