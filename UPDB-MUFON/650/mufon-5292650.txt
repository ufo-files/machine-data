One stationary and one moving object

The witness, Mrs. Magdolna Szabo of Parry Sound, Ontario initially saw one bright stationary object in location as described above. 

Within a minute or so, another, smaller object separated from the stationary object. It started descending at an angle downward to the left, (8 o'clock pos.). It did this at a fixed speed. About halfway to the treetop line the object changed its descent and moved vertically down. It disappeared and went below the tree line. After a few minutes (witness is uncertain about the duration), the object rose again and moved vertically up at the speed at which it descended. At a certain height it angled up and to the right toward the stationary object. 

It then merged with the stationary object. 

Within a minute or so, the larger stationary object moved slowly off to the right of the field. It then disappeared behind a tree line to the right. 

Note: The witness is a senior citizen, who was able to describe the event to me (Andrew Ernyes), but she would not have been able to manage the on-line form-filling and other computer related activities on her own. This is the reason why I, her brother is doing this for her. However, if she needs to be contacted, she is quite willing to agree to do that.
