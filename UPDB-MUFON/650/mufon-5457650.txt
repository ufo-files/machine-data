Very abnormal sky happenings and UAPs

I noticed a difference in light color coming from the sun, as I couldn't look directly at the sun I decided to film using my mobile phone. I made a lot of videos, during a 2 hour period of the sky and clouds. During filming I observed several strange phenomenons happening. Afterwards I went through the footage, too which I was gobsmacked with what I was seeing. Strange flashes of light, the sky would change in an instant in-front of me, flying objects which I cant explain!! I have tried to show to friends and family but they don't believe what they are witnessing. I have slowed the videos down and adjusted brightness, contrast and saturation, but it is still actual footage I have recorded and witnessed.
