Observed round glowing objects in sky

Frank came in my room Tuesday night, May 21,2012 to tell me he saw some red glowing objects in the sky. The one out front was high near the stars and glowing a bright red glow and changing directions up and down and at times the red glow became dimmer, then bright again. We also saw two red glowing objects above the hill behind our home. One was sitting on top of the hill, then out of sight, then back above the hill again, then back on top of the hill, then out of sight. The other one stayed higher above the power lines. don't know how long they stayed around, I went back to bed after watching them for about 20 minutes.Not sure what they were, just red glowing objects moving around in the sky.Event happened May 21, 2012 at 10:30 pm when we first noticed it.
