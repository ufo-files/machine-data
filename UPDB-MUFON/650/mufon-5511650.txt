{cak} brother and i saw a light in sky that did not blink

My brother and i were out in backyard smoking when he noticed a bright light in the sky coming from the south that he thought was a slow shooting star. We both watched come from the south, then come over our heads then head north. Slowed down a little then sped up a little bit. No blinking lights at all. The light stayed the same even after it went well to the north. You could see the light for miles from both directions. Was weird no blinking, no different colored lights. Did not change directions though. It did change speeds but continued to fly straight. There was no noise at all from the object.
