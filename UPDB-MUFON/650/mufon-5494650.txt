{rjl} I saw a light that looked like a star completely illuminate to the size of a basketball and then dim out and disappear.

My husband was driving me to work. We were going southbound on Sunrise Avenue toward Rancho Cordova from Carmichael California. I kept looking out my window (toward the West). The sky was pretty and you could see several stars despite the city lights. We crossed the bridge over the American River (this part of the road is the darkest). I saw a star that seemed to really be blinking all kinds of colors. When we were over the bridge, I looked away for a moment. I looked back and the "star" that I had been looking at was gone. I looked at my husband and was telling him what I was seeing. I looked at the sky again and there was another "star" that looked like the size of a pin dot. It suddenly became illuminated, the light was so big and bright that it looked as big as a basketball. It then looked like someone turned a dimmer switch, because the objects light slowly faded until nothing was visible. It looked like it was very high up in the sky. The whole event (with the light going from tiny, to large, to disappearing) lasted only for seconds.
