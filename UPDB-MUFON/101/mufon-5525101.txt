I was sitting on the sidewalk and I notice a red light, that I soon found to be a huge hovering black disc with red lights.

I was sitting on the sidewalk staring off into the stars, when suddenly out of the corner of my eye I saw a red light appear out of nowhere. I took my eyes off of the stars and stared at the object that had caught my attention (this was during nighttime by the way). It was a huge, black hovering disc spinning with a dome atop of the spinning disc and had a big red light in the center of the disc with three smaller ones on each side of the big red light. It hovered for about 10 seconds or less and flew EXTREMELY quickly down from the sky and disappeared into nothingness.
