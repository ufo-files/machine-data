{cak} Observed thru binoculars a fast moving cigar shaped object

I was in my back yard bird watching. An aircraft from a nearby airport
was approaching my location. It was traveling from West to East at a fairly low altitude. I looked at the plane with my binoculars to see it up close when into my field of no obstructions. The object simply vanished.
The plane that I had been observing had by this time passed on. There was no interaction between the airplane and the object. The object was too high in altitude. The plane was much lower and flying in the opposite direction. The time of the observation of the object was about 6 seconds, then it vanished.

My reaction was astonishment at the speed at which the object flew and the fact that it simply vanished so suddenly. Frankly it was creepy to realize what I had seen and what the object had done. The observation was admittedly short, but, what I saw is accurately described.
I am familiar with various aircraft and this was definitely no airplane and was not a bird.
