Small white orb appearing very briefly in security camera recording.

This case report has been filed by Field Investigator re: an additional security camera recording submitted by the witness in Case No. 112536. This video recording shows a very small white object moving very rapidly through a section of the viewing field near the camera. The object is in view for only a fraction of a second. The FI does not believe this object is related to the object appearing in the video recording in the original report and is, therefore, being submitted as a separate report.
