Jets! US military

Sorry for waiting so long. I was reluctant to say anything at all, because it really shook me up. I haven't been the same since. What I saw were 2 military jets (not sure of the model), January 10, 2019, 1:35 PM, Hartsville, SC, in the backyard of a house on Peachtree St., Flying from the left side of the photos to the right in the southern sky, I saw some sort of vortex form just a few feet ahead of the jets. From my point of view, the vortex appeared to be white in color and slightly concave, like a parenthese - ) . The vortex maintained a uniform distance in front of the jets for a few seconds. I reached for my mobile phone to get a photo, and the jets vanished into the vortex. The jets and vortex were completely gone, not a trace. The contrails stopped abruptly, and the very faint sound of the jets' engines ceased. The first photo was taken within 5 seconds of the disappearance. 

There were lots of jets in the sky on that particular day. There may have been an airshow nearby, not sure though.
