Night explosion near Kokomo Indiana

Want to let you know about the Night explosion Kokomo, Indiana as NOT a airplane. The reason I say is that I worked in the Command Post on Grissom Air Reserve Base and I received the call from the Howard County Sheriff Dept. When the deputy called he got reports of a huge explosion South West of Kokomo. I told the Deputy it may have been a F-16 out of Ft Wayne Air Guard unit, but the Deputy there was fire ball it maybe concerned maybe there was a aircraft was lost. I called the Ft Wayne unit and the controller there said all fighters have been down for hours and are all accounted for. Then I called Indy ATC and asked one of the air controllers if they had any explosions or missing any aircraft in the area of Kokomo and they said no. I called the ATC controllers on Grissom and asked them the same questions and they also said no all Grissom Tankers are down and no missing from Grissom to Chicago ATC. There is a training area west of Grissom but nothing was in the area. The next day the local papers had a F-16 on the front saying it was a fighter which was not true it was to quick. I also monitored first responder radio Freq's they said they found something then all freq's were switched to another freq that no one can monitor. I am retired from the Air Force now and the story that was told was wrong .

Jerry Humphrey
