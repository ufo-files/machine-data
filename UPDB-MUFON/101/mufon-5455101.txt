Heard my dogs acting up so went outside on my porch to see if something was out there.Did not see anything but could not calm the dogs.Started hearing a low hum or whir noise so kept looking in the direction it was coming from and the dogs were barking.Then noticed a light coming over the trees .Kept watching and was amazed as it came over my head it just stopped as if it knew i was watching it.Allof a sudden a blue light lit up my whole yard and myself.I held my arms up in front of me looking at them blue and was thinking wow I look like a Smurf.I kinda felt that it expected me to run inside but not me .It started moving very slowly off in the direction of Columbia S.C.I watched until it was out of sight. Then in 2016 or 2017 i once again saw something.Looking out my kitchen window i noticed what appeared to be a very large star.Then it moved so i went out to see what it was.It was 3 of them just zipping around in the sky.They would go together and look like a big ball of light then zipp off in different directions.I call them my dancing lights.Have seen them several times got a couple pics of them on my cell phone and sent to my sister and neice.
