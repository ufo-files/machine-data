Retangular object with a dark center both (arms) sides lit up

There was a forest fire in our area on Saturday, which was still out of control at the time I went to bed at 11pm. So Sunday morning at approximately 5am, I went outside in my back yard to see if I could still see the smoke from the fires. While I was standing outside an object drifted over head, moving from southwest to northeast. It was rectangle shapped with a black center area, extending out from the black center were two lit up (arms) or sides. It made no sound at all. It drifted over my head and kept drifting in a north east direction. I observed it for around 20 seconds and ran for my camera in the house. By the time I got back outside the object was gone. I thought it looked like a close up view of a satellite, but satellites are not that close to the ground.
