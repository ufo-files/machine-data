{nd} starlike balls in sky

In Anaheim, CA on November 24th 2012 at around 9:45pm I witnessed a starlike ball in the sky move around. It maintained a constant light and looked exactly like the adjacent stars. I then called my wife outside to see what I saw and after I showed her the area I was looking she then said, look there are four moving stars. We both witnessed about 4-6 moving lights changing direction, hold a pattern and then leave heading in a south west direction.
