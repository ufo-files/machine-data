{rjl} explosion and then white chem trail descending downward and west over golden gate

driving home in eastbay hills, saw an enormous looking puff of white like something had exploded and then a downward chem trail going west over the golden gate bridge. it looked like a vessel of some kind exploded high in the air and came falling down towards the west over the bridge out toward the Pacific ocean. it was like a rocket had been descending to earth, but with a large white/greyish mass high above the tailings which were white and it seemed to be moving downward and not entirely straight. although there are some winds about 20 plus miles an hour right now. it made me excited to see it and couldn't understand what the heck it might be or what caused it.
