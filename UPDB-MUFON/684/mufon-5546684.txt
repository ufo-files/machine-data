Egg shaped object that circled in and out of clouds for hours

Some friends and I were camping in my parents back yard (the address in I gave above) when I was about 13 and at around 2 AM in the morning we saw an egg shaped object in the sky. We watched it for hours, it would make circles in the sky going in and out of clouds and then disappear for a few minutes. Then it would reappear in the same spot is disappeared from. I am not sure why we didn't wake my parents up but none of us were scared of it. The object almost seemed like it was watching us. After a while it just disappeared and never reappeared. We really didn't know what to think about it. Then I went to the library a few days later and started looking at books about UFO sightings and found a picture of an egg shaped UFO that looked a lot like the one we saw and I knew then what it was.
