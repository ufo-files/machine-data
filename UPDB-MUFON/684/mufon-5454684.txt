{cak} Dream or reality about 2 sightings?

My 2 stories may not be valid or appropriate here as I feel they were both dreams. After having read that someone else's experience felt like a dream, however, I've wondered about mine. So here it is:

I'm standing outside the front of the house in the middle of the night. I see an oval-shaped UFO come across the sky and stop to the right of the house and above the tree tops. There is a window across the middle of the UFO. I can clearly see a few American military men standing next to a few aliens. I can see the shoulder boards on the men, but I can't see the aliens faces, just their figures. It is very clear to me in the dream that our U.S. military and the aliens (or as I prefer to call them now our "star ancestors" as some Native Americans do) are working together in a very cooperative manner. That's it -

then, a few months later, I have a second dream: I see a whitish, oval-shaped flying object hovering above the house at tree top level again in the night. What is abundantly clear to me is that I'm being "allowed" to see this (just like the first dream). It moves around to the other side of the house and lets me observe it there. Then, it moves slowly by once more and finally, takes off in a flash.

Thank you for reading.
