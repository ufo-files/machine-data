In the early spring of 2002 I woke up in the middle of the night, my room was filled with light, I walked over to my open window and looked out into my backyard, the entire night sky was filled by what I thought was the moon, I could see the texture of the moon, the Crater’s, it was the most beautiful sight I had seen in my life, I felt calm and at peace, I would like to mention I am facing west and the moon is always to the south of me and the moon I’m witnessing is 100x larger than the typical moon you see at night, I was certain this was the moon I was staring at though, I don’t know how long i stared at the moon but I don’t remember walking away from the window, I woke up from my bed the next day only to discover that the day is not what I think it is, I went to bed on a Saturday night and I woke up on Monday, I was so confused and unable to explain it until years later when I heard of abductees experiencing lost time.
