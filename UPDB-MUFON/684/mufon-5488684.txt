White object in sky like a star but brighter. One with pattern on side.

I just left work in Bristol travelling home at about 0410 when I saw a bright light in the sky to our right at first I thought it was a police helicopter hovering looking for someone, as we got closer to the end of the bypass to go on to the M4 (west) we noticed it was still on our right like it was matching our speed. It was just there in the sky I started to think it was the North star which would make sense, any way we left the M4 and joined the M5 north and again this bright white light was still on our right when it should of been in front of us if it was the north star. We just forgot about it until we got back to our home town, anyway one of the guys wanted to go through the drive through to get some food when I noticed this light again and thought I would take a couple of photos of this bright star thingy while I looked at the photo we noticed two other lights that we couldn't see so I took another picture and again there it was but this time it had a kind of a triangle shape with vertical lines on which remind me of vents. I felt kind of scared because the day before I saw a Phil schyder seminar(on the internet) and said I wish I could see something like this and get some kind if proof then BOOM! The day later this happens like "it" knew what I was thinking and it made me feel scared of what I might if got myself in to so I thought I would fill in a report and just forget about it. I went home told the misses who said I should report to you guys because you know more about it than me and could possibly been seen and reported by someone else.
