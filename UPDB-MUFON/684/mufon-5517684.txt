{ck} Small dark craft, appeared to slightly wobble...two round things on back propelled it slowly. No sound or lights. Low flying.

I sat outside on my front porch to have a cigarette around 1 am and soon noticed an object appear from behind the trees and home across the street. I quickly stood up to examine it, as its flight pattern/characteristics were mechanical-like. It was traveling diagonally away from the home, so I observed the rear of the craft. It seemed to wobble and teeter a bit and traveled slowly above the powerlines and trees. It had two round propellers on either side of its rear. I watched it continue on a straight course, till it was no longer in my range. It was approximately 3 feet wide and had a dark coloring. The "propellers" had a lighter color...more of a yellow/gold. It travelled around 15mph. The "wobbling" flight was most unusual and I made sure to observe it carefully to confirm it wasnt bird of some sort.
