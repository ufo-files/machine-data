2 brilliant blue-green orbs (dots) coordinated flight

Was on a conference call with the CEO of another company. I was standing in my office, which has tinted windows, while talking. I noticed 2 pinpoint orbs (for lack of a better term) that were as brilliant as a star at night, but this occurred around 1:15pm. They shone brilliantly against the azure Texas sky. They caught me by such surprise that I lost my train of thought. I tried to get my camera app opened while talking to this CEO, but they went behind a couple of live oak branches as they moved NW. They were either really, really small, or very high. The color was brilliant blue-green. Like a plasma level of brilliance.
