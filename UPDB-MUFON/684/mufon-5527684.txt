I was traveling on I-480 west bound to I-77 north bound. I noted a dark object flying towards me. It look like a black aspirin floating slowly turning right to I-480. It bank right on it's fly-bye. I was riding my motorcycle while this event ocurred. The top and bottom of the object had cast iron look to it. the side wall radius appeared to be dark plastic with no visible stems to it. The top surface had a three pronged antenna that rotated counter clock wise. My estimated size of the object to be about 10-15 feet in diameter. The height of the object about 3 feet. I'm guessing the object was a probe or a drone by it's size. You have to understand I was riding my motorcycle in the opposite direction and not  see where it vanish into the sky. I was curious that no one saw this object or reported it to any of the news media. I felt a deep longing to see again. I was not afraid of the object but why did it appeared to me?
