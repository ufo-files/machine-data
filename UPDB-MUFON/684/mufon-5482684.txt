Hovering pulsating orb that headed off slowly in northeast direction. Very high, clear day but at least at cloud height

Kids were playing basketball in the front of the house. They noticed a shinny object around 5:15 in the east, called us to come look, but we didn't. Around 10'mins later, they said the object is still there, so I headed to observe. The object had moved slightly to the north since they had first observed it. It was shinny, looked to be pulsing or rotating as it got brighter than fainter in a short cycle. It was pretty still for 10'minutes or so, called my wife out to look. After another 5 minutes or is, it started moving faster In a straight line to the nort east where it went behind our house and we lost sight of it. Total observed time was about 45 mins.
