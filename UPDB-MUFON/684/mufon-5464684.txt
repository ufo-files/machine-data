{jpg} 3 Lights discovered in photograph after the event.

My wife and I were on holiday and visited the 2018 Arizona State Fair where we took some pictures on an Apple IPhone.
Some time later, after the holiday, I transfered the photos from the iphone to a memory stick and from there to my laptop PC.
When I reviewed the photographs on my PC I noticed 3 lights in the sky in two of the photos, which neither my wife or I remember seeing at the time the photos were taken.
From what I can see in the two pictures, 3 orbs or sources of light seem to move from right to left in two pictures both of which were taken in the same minute.
Although we were in a location with many bright lights at ground level and just above, I dont believe the light sources in the two pictures are reflective light, because of their position in both photos.
The images are original and have not been altered or enhanced in any way.
