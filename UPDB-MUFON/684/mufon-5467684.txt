A glowing light in the forest

It was early in the morning as I always observed out of my bedroom window curious about lights I would often see on the ocean. Often would be crewships and boats. I was looking when something caught my eye in the forest. A glow of a light, a flashlight maybe? I lived next to a middle school that I used to go to called Cape Lazo. No longer a middle school but the forest behind it has a creepy feeling to it. The middle school was a road away from me and the sighting happened at the end of the school grounds. 

I thought someone was walking in the forest, usually people with their dogs would walk in the forest. It was odd in behaviour as it bobbed along the tree-line. I was not prepared for the moment it met the street. A tall slender with a big head being wearing a silverish suite came out of the bush and it levitated. It made no movements with its arms or legs. Just moving around under the streetlight. Eventually it went back into the forest at a different entry point. I was over 400 feet away.

After the encounter I was afraid as it changed my view on what I knew. I did not know if the entity knew I was observing it as it didn't make any indication it knew. Nothing that strange has ever happened to me ever again. 

To this day I cannot explain what I saw better than what I've described. I've searched everywhere to find more information on what I saw but I cannot find anything. I've gone back to the forest to explore and found nothing. Only feeling watched at times.
