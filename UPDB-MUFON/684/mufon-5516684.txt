{dr}three lights flying in perfect triangular formation

Driving home from McDonalds in Othello at around 8 p.m. and saw three lights flying over the mountains in a perfect triangular formation. I thought it was odd, I pulled over and noticed they werent moving. I texted some friends in the area to make sure I wasnt seeing things, and they confirmed they saw it too. I continued driving home and noticed them moving relative to my speed, but they never seemed to get any closer, like a rainbow. Once arriving home a half hour later, I took my dog for a walk and noticed the lights still there. I continued walking my dog, normally, and looked up and the lights were gone.
