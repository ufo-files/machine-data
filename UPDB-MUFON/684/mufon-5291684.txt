Round object that changed colors

I got out of my car at my house and saw a red-orange ball in the sky moving slowly from north to south, fairly low in the sky. It appeared a little bigger than a planet and brighter. I watched it for a few moments, grabbed my cell phone and shot video for a few moments, and then watched with just my eyes as it slowed down and seemed to stall and then vanished. I ran inside where I could celebrate without arousing my neighbors and looked out the window. I witnessed the same thing again. Same appearance and disappeared in the same spot. When I slowed down the video and enlarged the images, I could see it morph and change colors.
