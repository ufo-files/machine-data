20 - 30 lights in a uniform line, incredibly far away.

This is my first time seeing something I know is beyond human. Four of my dear friends and I were about to leave the clubhouse off the C of E church in Stourbridge. We were talking when one of the group mentioned lights in the sky. We all looked and saw 20-50 uniformed white lights travelling East East South at a high speed, they were evenly shaped and spaced except for the last few lights that were spaced further apart, the very last one being very dim. We all tried to get a picture but only I succeeded as my phone has a 4K camera. The picture I managed to get is attached, to see the lights clearly you will have to have a bright screen, or change the picture's brightness. This is, to put it lightly, mindblowing. We watched the lights for roughly 8 minutes before they moved out of view. There are too many lights covering too much sky to be a plane, the lights never dimmed or blinked, meaning it wasnt helicopters, there was no occlusion or blurring meaning it wasnt natural light and the sky was completely clear so it wasnt light refraction from clouds.
