{nd} a swarm like '52 over Washington DC, only higher and faster moving...

My coworker and I were headed north on ST RT 62 north out of Hillsboro, Ohio around 7pm. It was mid-December, 2008?...gets dark real early...My friend was driving the box truck, I was on passenger side. looked up and noticed out of the upper windshield a swarm of bright white lights, 15 to 25 or so, flying in a ragged horizontal line formation NW to SE... Flying at a high rate of speed. We watched them go by for probably 15 to 20 seconds. Some of them would bounce up to another craft, then fall back into a loose swarm "formation". They looked to have some altitude, 10 to 15K?, not sure. But they were moving fast, and definitely controlled by intelligence. We lost sight as they disappeared over the horizon, heading SE...My buddy was a ufo skeptic, till that nite. I've always believed, tho this sighting reinforced my knowledge...now I know...very exciting.
