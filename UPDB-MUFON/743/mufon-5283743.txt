Strange triangle in sky then disappears

My Daughter and I were camping near our local Dam, It was around 7:40pm as we were leaving at 8pm.
We were packing up when my daughter pointed to a "star" in the sky getting brighter, I looked around and saw a blueish white light way brighter than a star and bigger than any air plane lights. 
It was visible through the clouds and in about 2 minutes of descending it broke through the bottom of the clouds and for about 1 minute it hovered there, there was an audible electric hum and a deep bass sound coming from it, its lights then dimmed to almost turned off they were very dim at this point.

after a minute or two the humming sound from the object got progressively louder then boom, a really loud bass sound came from it and it took off above the clouds and disappeared.

I have no idea what me and my daughter witnessed nor have ever seen anything like it, I'm not a believer in the extra terrestrial but that definitely got me wanting to know more about what i witnessed. There is no way that was a helicopter or any plane as it was very visibly a triangle shaped object about the size of a large bus with blue white lights that dimmed. No flashing lights or anything and the sound was very strange almost ethereal.
I did not have my phone on me at the time as it was in my car as we were packing otherwise i would have gotten a good video but the event only lasted about 5-8 minutes if that and I was not expecting to witness something like that.
