(GS) bright light, changing colors, moving all directions

this is the third time in 2 months on a saturday night that I have seen these lights, first I noticed a bright light that appeared to be moving back and forth in the sky, then up and down, so I grabbed my camera and filmed it and others moving in all directions, it kept moving up and down and left and right, leaving trails and changing colors, red, yellow, and green, my camera would not zoom in on the object, but I got good video and some pictures, and a witness 
2nd time I did not have a camera but I did have 2 witnesses, as we watched the light go up and down and back and forth, it blinked on and off and traveled in many directions, it changed colors often and after about 10 minutes it blinked off and in few seconds it came back on and turned off and made no noise, it would go from tree level to about 5000 feet in the air and would swoop down towards the tree line and back up then disappear and reappear in a different part of the sky, then it blinked off like someone turned a light switch off
3rd sighting, I was looking at the stars and noticed a bright light in the sky and noticed it was changing colors, my camera had dead batteries and I could not film it, I do have 2 witnesses, we watched it change colors very fast, it has red, green, white, yellow lights that appear to be circling the edge of the object, so we grabbed binoculars and it appears to be round like a plate, or two plates inverted on each other with the lights moving around the seam of the two plates, the lights were all colors and it appeared to be flipping over and over and standing on edge, we watched it move and get lower and lower on the horizon and suddenly it just turned off and was gone
