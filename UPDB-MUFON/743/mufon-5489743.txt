In or near the city of Brawley, CA a lightening storm was raging. There was no thunder. The clouds were bright red and orange when the lightening flashed. Above the clouds I observed a glowing ball that would appear and disappear from sight. This went on for about an hour and then the ball of light suddenly shot skyward and disappeared.
