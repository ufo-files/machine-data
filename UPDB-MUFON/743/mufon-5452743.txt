Wobbling star-like white light, low altitude

5 witnesses watched an unwavering white/yellow light fly 40 miles south to north. No noise whatsoever. Brighter and bigger than any star or satellite. At first appeared to fly in straight-line, but was definiteively wobbling or subtly changing speed and direction as it flew. Approximate altitute was 2-3k feet.
