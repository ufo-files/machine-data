{dr}There were about 4 lights in the distance hovering and flying around each other for about 3 minutes.

We were parked off the East of Dicks Sporting Goods Park. Fireworks were seen around the city. Someone in our group pointed out the lights. The more we looked at them, the more we knew they were not fireworks or planes and helicopters. The lights were hovering and flying around each other for about three minutes. They were disappearing and reappearing randomly. The lights looked to be hovering over The Rocky Mountain Arsenal area. Several people in our group filmed the lights with their camera phones. The lights faded away one by one. Other people who were there at the Park were tweeting about it and pictures/videos were posted on Instagram. When we got home, our cell phones would not work. We felt shocked and surprised.
