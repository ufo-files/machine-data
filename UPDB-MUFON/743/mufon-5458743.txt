{rjl} UFO near Dulles Flight Path not far from Mount Weather

My daughter and I were returning home at 9 p.m. on December 21st 2016 when we observed a series of lights in the sky over signal knob Mountain near Strasburg, Virginia. We went inside the house into the backyard so we could see the object better. My wife's parents were in town visiting for Christmas holiday. You could see a dark V shaped object with a series of perfectly spaced lights.  At first there were three lights on and then a fourth light came on and eventually there were seven lights in all. This thing was very close to the Dulles Airport flight path from the West. We watched the object slowly turn to the South and within a few minutes it was out of sight.  Approximately 10 to 15 minutes later we saw several military jets come from the Northeast at a very high rate of speed. We assumed the Jets were on an intercept path but we could no longer see the object. Tried to film the object with my cell phone but the quality was so poor you really couldn't see anything of value other than lights.  I posted the image on my Facebook page for several days but it was eventually deleted.
