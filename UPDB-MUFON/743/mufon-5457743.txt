{rjl} It had a discoidal shape. Silver blue.

I was walking on the Wilson Ave bridge. At that high point I could see a blue silver colored discoidal ship, it was parked in the highest part of the tree and behind the Macdonalds restaurant. Cars were passing on Route 59, but on Main Street, there was no one. I made no noise and it was there for about two minutes, it disappeared in milliseconds. Six months later, that tree dried up and 1 month later it was cut by the town administration. I have put a photo with the location and an old photo (of another sighting) of its resemblance.
