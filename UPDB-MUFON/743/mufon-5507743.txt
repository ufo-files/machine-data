{kt}Triangle shaped craft observed for about 5 minutes.

A friend and I were driving down the ocean road towards my beach house when we saw a large triangle snapped craft formed out of three lights over the power lines hovering over the ocean. We pulled over and watched it for a couple of minutes before the lights suddenly turned off. We then continued driving down the road and didn't see it again.
