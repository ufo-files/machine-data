Lights prominent on top and to one side, silent, low to ground, flat surface on bottom

Was driving down Ball rd in Anaheim and saw a weirdly blinking helicopter.... after watching it for 2 minutes at a red light, noticed it was clearly NOT a helicopter by the weird flashes, above the flying unit and to one side, a "circle" of red lights would flash... then on the other side there would be singular orange and green lights that would flash. There were other helicopters in the area - and they did not compare. As we drove down Ball, the flying thing nearly passed over our car, we rolled down the window and it was SILENT - no noise, and the bottom of the craft was a matte black and flat - with what looked like a seam down the middle. It was hard to tell the shape but it looked triangular. Have never seen anything like it.
