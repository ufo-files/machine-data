Tic Tac Shaped UFO, white in color. Sighted on Highway 191, Sighting lasted approx 3 min

I was driving north on highway 191, north bound towards the town of Ganado on the Navajo Reservation. I drive a mail route that starts from Chambers, AZ and I was near the town of Klagetoh, about 15 from Ganado. As I was driving, I noticed a reflection from the sun in the sky. I wrote it off as a helicopter at first, because of the angle of the object in relation to the sun but as I got closer to the object, I noticed that the resemblance was inaccurate. The object looked identical to a Tic Tac, White and smooth looking. Moving slowly to the west as I was heading north. I started losing sight of the object as I was driving past it, and I also checked my watch as I was driving past the object as well. After checking my watch for a brief moment, the object disappeared from the clear, blue, cloudless sky. I was stunned by the fact that it happened on a normal day, in basically the middle of rural part of AZ. One vehicle was in front of me but it seemed the occupant was none the wiser.
