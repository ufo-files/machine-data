I was walking my dog at 1:25 am on Friday , May 8th and I believe that I witnessed a UFO in flight . When I looked up in the air I thought it was just a star at first but it started to flash green , it kind of glimmered as a light when it hits a diamond . At that point started paying attention to it because I thought that it might be an jet having trouble with an engine or something , but it was making no noise . Then it looked to make a flying maneuver to the SE , but now I think of it , it could have been gaining altitude . That only took about 2 seconds , then it took off to the NE faster then anything that I have ever seen before in my life . My brain was telling me that maybe it was a shooting star ? But I have seen shooting stars before and it really didn't resemble that , plus I have never seen a shooting star change directions . And I didn't see a tail on it like you would from a comet . When you see something like this for the first time in person , you start to second guess yourself . I wish I would have taken my cell phone out and filmed it , but once it started moving it only lasted a few seconds . Not 100% sure that what I saw was a UFO this early morning , but after going through every other option and trying to think everything through , I am now 75% sure that it couldn't have been anything else .
