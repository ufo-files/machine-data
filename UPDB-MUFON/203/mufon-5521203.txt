My son and I were driving down to the store when he said "whoah dad look at that"...I looked briefly as I was driving, but got a better look at it when we stopped at the light, at first I thought it was a really low flying plane. It was no airplane. I pulled over and we observed it for 3 mins.+. It was floating down in a circular pattern, occasionally darting straight up 50 feet then back down, then back into circular pattern before dissapearing behing tree line. We drove down PCH toward Doheny Beach hoping to pick up viewing it, but lost it. It was too big from the distance we saw it at to be a remote control plane or helicopter. The lights stayed bright white--no colored lights or flashing/blinking. My son was really curious as was I about what it couldve been.
