Like stated I just went out to smoke at five in the morning as I take my wife to work every morning upon doing so on the morning of November 27 I saw an anomaly that looked like a good-sized low hovering bright star, upon further review it turned out to be something else as my photographs have revealed, it has appeared three of the last four days, this morning was cloudy and that may have had a bearing on it. When it appears it just hovers, it looks to me to be about 5 to 10,000 feet up, it comes in slowly and just hovers above the tree line a couple of hundred feet north west of my house, it makes no noise whatsoever.
