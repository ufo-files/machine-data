{dr}Black Trinagle flew in a rectangular formation just under the Full Moon

Saturday, 26 June 2010 at just after midnight walking my dog, I was coming around the far Western edge of the track. My dog became nervous. I looked up and notice a massive Black Triangle making a rectangular pattern just under the full Moon. It stopped a few times & hovered. I was in mental shock at what I was seeing. It made zero noise and turned perfect 90 degrees w/o slowing down. This went on for about 10 minutes and then it suddenly vanished. I went home and told no one not even my wife or my best friends for over a year.
