{rjl} Round object slowly moving across the night sky with pulsating violet/purple lights with sudden change in direction

I had gone on a hike with my dog in the Blue Ridge mountains and decided to take a break in somewhat of a clearing. It was our annual midnight hike, so I had thought nothing of being out this late. It was probably around 2:15/30 when I saw what I thought was a plane. It was slowly moving across the sky. I didn't have a second thought until I noticed the lights on it were an odd purple/violet color and seemed to be pulsating instead of blinking like airplanes normally do. My dog started to walk around nervously in a circle and barked up at the sky. I looked down to calm my dog, however, when I looked back up it seemed as though the object had stopped moving. It suddenly changed the direction it had been moving, a faster pace as well. I watched it for a good while until it had passed over the tree line and away from my view. I tried to hike along a bit longer to see if I could find it, but I didn't see it at all the rest of the night. However, a helicopter had flown by in the direction I had last seen the object head.
