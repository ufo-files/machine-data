Steady moving light high in sky then stopped.

I was on vacation in Niagara Falls Canada. I was in a crowd of people waiting for the falls to be illuminated. It was getting dark with scattered high clouds. I noticed the light high in the sky steadily moving from the American side of the falls to right over my head where it stopped. There were no strobe lights associated with this light. At the same time I clearly saw a plane high in the sky and could clearly see the strobe lights and knew it was a plane. Once the light stopped moving I watched it until the clouds moved in, maybe 5 minutes. I used the compass on my phone and it showed I was at 43deg 5' 16" N and 79deg 4' 11" w.
