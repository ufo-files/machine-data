{rjl} noticed unusual bright moving light, it wasn't

On the evening of dec.24th at approx. 11:10 pm I had just arrived home after driving some friends to their house. As I pulled up to my residence, I noticed an extremely unusual bright light that looked like a "mini-sun" the usually clouded sky's were clear and could clearly see the stars in the background. This object "re-acted" to my horn honking and my calling my wife outside, At least I felt that way because it changed its direction and "floated" in my direction, I never let my vision lose sight of the object, my wife came outside with the camera and I was able to take a picture of the object, its kinda grainy, its the orange "blip" on the lower right corner of the picture with my neighbors houses across the street in the back ground. The object headed in the direction of the Oakland International Airport, I know the flight controllers had to see it because if they didn't they need to be tested, there is no way this object was man made, I used to work at the airport and I know flight characteristics of "all" known man made jets. This object flew right over me, its center was bright blue, the outer edges were like orange-yellow, it was the most beautiful thing I've ever seen in my life because it was alive. It Cruised. I'm used to seeing lots of "traffic" in the skies of Oakland and it can be said sometimes there are some colorful lights on some of the planes coming and going, like I said, this "object" wasn't one of ours. Swamp gas and Venus ? Been there done that. I know what I saw, my wife seen it too, and I snapped a picture.
