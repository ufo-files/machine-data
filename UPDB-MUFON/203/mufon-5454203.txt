{tjd} Looked like a rocket launch

Ok, this is NOT an April fools joke. On 04/01/2020 at 03:45 the sky was crystal clear, looking south/ SW (about 210 degrees) from our house we saw a reddish orange glow just above the tree tops. At first I thought it was the planet mars but realized it was moving upwards in the sky. Very slow at first then noticeably faster. My wife and I watched for at least 2 minutes then it disappeared and you could hear the noise like a rocket after. I know Wallops Island, VA has launches that can occasionally be seen from here. I have been searching the internet and can't find anything about a launch or any reports. Very strange.
