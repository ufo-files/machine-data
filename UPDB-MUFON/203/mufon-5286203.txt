UFO sighting in Tunisia

We were aprox. 8 people on a roof top, it was at night and the sky was clear and full of stars, me and Irene, was admireing its beauty. I saw a object I firt thought must be a satelite, becaus it was much bigger than the stars and much brighter and it didnt twinkle. Then in a blink of an eye, it shoot off in a stright path and in 1 sec. or two it was on the otherside of the horizon. There it stayed for a few seconds, before it shoot off again to almost were it was earlier, there it stoped again, before it shoot of stright up, it kind of glowed up a bit, and the poff it disapeared out of sight. Out of us 8 we were only 2 that saw it. We both work on airplanes as cabin crew. The speed that this object was traveling at was something out of this world, Not from here. We both will never forget what we saw.
