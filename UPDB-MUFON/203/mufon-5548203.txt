Black sphere that did not move

I was on my deck of my house on July 26th. reading a book when I thought I saw a baloon in the sky. I looked back 10 min. later and realized the balloon hadn't move even though the wind was pretty good. I grabbed my camera and shot this footage looking almost straight up from my deck. The zoom on my camera can only be seen on the viewer on the back of the camera and due to the light I could not really see if I was getting it in the viewfinder or not. I swear it happened....
