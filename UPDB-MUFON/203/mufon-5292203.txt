Light pointed east, then pointed down, then ascended at extremely fast speed

2/14/2017 at approximately 6:30 am while driving west-bound on the 210 freeway approaching the Pasadena Rose Bowl. I saw a light that I though was a helicopter looking at morning traffic. Light was pointing east where it looked very bright. As I got closes the light began to point down, creating a cone shape light. As i got closes the light accelerated and unprecedented speed straight up. It passed the the high clouds almost instantly then disappeared.
