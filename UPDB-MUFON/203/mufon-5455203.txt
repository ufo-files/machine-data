{rjl} Domed saucer had lights around its circumference. I

Upon returning home from dinner with my sister and parents, as we exited our car, we noticed a disc shaped object hovering over our local grocery store, which was about 2 blocks away. As we stared at it, it proceeded to come towards us and stopped at the end of our street. No sound. There was a single row of lights around it’s circumference, with a small section of no lights. It hovered for about 15-20 seconds, then returned to its original location. After hovering there for about 10-15 seconds, it sped off at a high rate of speed to the East and disappeared. We went in the house, where I proceeded to call O’Hare Airport, as we were pretty close to it, as well as a local military base ( I don’t recall which one). I was told they didn’t have anything reported. It bothered me for a while that no one seemed to care. I can still see the ufo in my mind like it was yesterday.
