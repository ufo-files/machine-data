I was letting our dogs out to go to the bathroom before bed. About 9:15-9:30 pm. I noticed a bright star that seemed like it was hovering. Light movement. As I watched it it was clear to me it was moving right and left and going up and later down. I could see colored lights in green and blue and red that would flash then disappear.  It was far enough away that I thought it was originally a bright bright star. I went up to my sons room and we watched it. He has bad eyes so he couldn't tell that it was moving. I showed my wife it from our second story bedroom. It was too high to be a drone. She could tell it was moving too up then right then hovering ect. It was there in the sky until we went to bed over an hour later. Still hovering with some clear movement.
