Seen a group of ufos in basically straight line moving eastward then disappear one by one.

On the night of may the 6th, 2021..at 10:40 p.m. .i witnessed in my driveway looking north westward towards garden of the gods, up in the sky a basically straight line of 6-10 blinking lights that moved in an eastward trajectory and moving perhaps slightly slower than a usual airplane...then suddenly disappear one by one, one after the other. The very odd thing was that there was noobstruction to my view of this. None, zero, zilch, nada. No trees no clouds , nothing.. They simply vanished.
