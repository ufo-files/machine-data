Found on photo not seen until viewing photos

We took a day trip to Nigara Falls on the 22 June 2016. We did the usual tourist thing just shaped away photos on a tablet. Looking at photos first the black Grey dots look to be in the same place so just thought of a speck on lens. However just return from cousin house showing him photos and he asked about the black shape by the flag pole. It loops like a vapour tail behind a dark object. Looking back at photos the dot has got different shades and I cannot explain the one by the flag pole.
