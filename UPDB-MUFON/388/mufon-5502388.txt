On I-85 driving, black object in sky thought it was turkey vulture, black orb disappeared after I passed under it.

I was driving to work on I-85 about 0625 when I noticed a black object in the sky over my lane of traffic. At first I thought it was a turkey vulture flying along the freeway looking for road kill. As I drove closer to the object it was clear the object was not a bird. I could tell it was black in color and round so I thought it was a balloon floating but it did not move. It was at this moment I realized that it was a black sphere or orb was just hovering over my lane of traffic about 300 feet off the ground and 1/4 mile away. The object just hovered and made no sound as I drove under it I felt nauseous for a moment and I looked back in my mirrors I could not see the object in the sky. I exited the freeway and crossed over the bridge I slowed down and looked for the object but could see it any longer. I have never experienced anything like this, it left me excited and confused at the same time. Has anyone seen the same thing?
