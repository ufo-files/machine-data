Fast, bright white/silver object.

Out walking dog. Heard plane. Looked up. Saw the plane then noticed this other object. It was high in the sky - was a clear sky. It was moving south to north at a faster speed than the airplane. It had no flashing lights. Was solid. Bright white. Then it shot off due west really fast. There was a change in the atmosphere - like a kind of time shifting feeling. A wierd woosh like it was warping. No sound. Couldn't be sure but I think there was a flash/beam as well.
