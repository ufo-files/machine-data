Star like object moving across sky

I saw what looked like 2 very large stars which caught my attention. I realised one of them was moving. It was glowing silvery all over. No flashing or coloured lights. Too low to be a satelite or space station. It appeared to have like a swallows tail at the back of it. There was no sound at all. It just suddenly faded away. I thought maybe went into cloud but there was no clouds! My son tried to video it but it didnt work.
