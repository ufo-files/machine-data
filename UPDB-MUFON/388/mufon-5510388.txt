(GS) Large glowing green orb slowly fell towards earth. It left a green trail behind it.

I was out smoking a cigarette around 8 pm and I saw a large glowing green orb slowly falling towards earth. It was lower than the moon and it dissipated before it touched the ground. It had no sound that I could hear, but it looked like it was miles away. It has a glowing tail, like a comet tail, behind it. It took about 6 seconds for it to fall and dissapate.
