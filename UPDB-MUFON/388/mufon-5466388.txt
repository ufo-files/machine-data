{rjl} Three large bright lights in triangle formation moved across the sky

My daughter and I were stargazing, since it was the night before the peak of the meteor shower. There was a storm to the north and lots of lightning flashing, but was not close enough to hear thunder. Three large bright lights in a perfect triangle formation, much larger than stars or satellites, appeared and moved across the sky. The glow of the lights seemed different. We watched it move from south to north at a constant speed that lasted about 45 seconds. Right before this came into view, we saw what we thought were two satellites moving in the same direction, one right behind the other. I have stargazed a lot in my life and have never seen anything that I thought was a UFO. I was super excited! I have been watching to see if anyone posted such a sighting near me, but haven't found anything. Since it was a meteor shower weekend, I hope someone else witnessed the same thing!
