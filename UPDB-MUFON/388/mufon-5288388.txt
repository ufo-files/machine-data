Moving orb

We were walking from my house to his and after we turned the corner we saw an orb slowly moving over the houses on the left side of the street and it kind of looked like it was coming towards us and keeping its altitude coming down the hill then moved over the street and hovered for a little bit then shot off into the sky at about a 60 degree angle and disappeared.
