{dr}2 black snake-shaped objects, spinning slowly in a spiral, ascending out of sight toward southeast

I was sitting outside on my back deck, looking up toward the sky. I watched 4 very high planes go by, with no sound, and emitting trails. 2 of them went from north to southeast. 2 went west to east. I thought that was very strange. Just a couple of minutes later I saw a black solid snake-like object over my trees to the East. It was spinning very slowly to make a spiral-like shape. Appeared to be close, maybe 100 ft from me. It was making it's way very slowly toward the southeast. My wife was inside the house. I wanted a second witness so I yelled at her to come outside. During the couple of seconds that I looked away to get my wife's attention, the object had gone from maybe 100 feet away to extremely high in the sky, maybe 500 feet. It took my wife and 4-year-old daughter about 5 seconds to make it to the deck. By the time they made it outside, a second object had appeared near the first object. The second object was exactly like the first object. The 2 objects slowly ascended out of site toward the southeast. All 3 of us watched the objects until they were out of site. During the sighting, a 5th plane went by, from east to west, very high in the sky, with no sound.
