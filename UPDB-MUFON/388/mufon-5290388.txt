6 craft travelling in 2 formations of 3.

Observed first formation of 3 triangular craft in the night sky heading from north to south at approx 7pm. Formation was one in front and other two flanking to rear. Formation was loose as each craft seemed to ascend, decend and slow down/speed up at will just around the cloud base which I estimated at around 1500 feet. Speed was approximately 60-70 kts and made no noise with 3 intense red/orange globes of light at each corner and one smaller one in centre. The body of the craft seemed to reflect a deep red which illuminated the cloud around them red. Changed direction in unison several times before ascending above cloud base and out of sight to the south.

Second formation observed a minute later after I moved my position. They were to the West, travelling toward the East about some 2 miles distant at a much lower altitude of around 500ft from top of distant hill. Same formation as before however, this time they all slowly and silently decended to below visible tree line on top of hill at about 1.5 mile distant and out of sight. Speed this time around 20-30 kts. Observed for several minutes until they slowly reappeared and quickly ascended out of sight above cloud base to the East.

Have often tought it may have been the same 3 craft on 2 different approaches but the speed they were ovserved at would have made it astonishing for them to reapear so quickly in a completely different flight path so far away in less than a minute?
