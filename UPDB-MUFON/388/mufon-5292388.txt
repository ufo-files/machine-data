Stars, satellites or meteors don't move like that

I was doing my usual star gazing.  meteor shower this weekend so I expected to see some things. and I did catch a few shooting stars..., what I thought was first a star, then a meteorite, then a satellite as it started wobbling in its motion,  then realizing it was very much something when it impossibly shot off in the opposite direction moments after it caught my eye.

The path the object took was much like that if you traced out a line in the shape of a bobby-pin.  starting from the wavy end.
