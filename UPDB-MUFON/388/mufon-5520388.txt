Started as fireball/comet broke to 3 orbs in formations

I took my dogs out in the backyard as have done countless times over 8 years here. As I was standing there looking at the night sky as normal, but this time I saw something different. I noticed what at first I thought was a very low shooting star. In the half second it took to realize this was no shooting star it broke from one solid object into 3 separate orbs. These orbs very quickly fell into formation of a perfect triangle for about one second before taking more of a wide V formation as it disappeared behind trees. The whole event did not take more than 5 seconds total. The object/objects never changed heading or speed during the entire time and was moving much faster than planes I have ever seen fly over this area, and being over the flight path of Lambert airport, and 3 smaller airports I have seen all type of plane fly over from commercial, single engine, to military jets. I have never seen anything that has even come close to what I had seen. The object started as a streak of blue-white light with a tail of matching light similar to a comet or shooting star. After it broke it seemed to have much less light, but still same color as it started. Wish I had anything record it with, but aint that the way!
