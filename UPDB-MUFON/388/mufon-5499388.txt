{nd} Orange glowing light hovered, then made an L shaped path then disappeared.

I was standing on my deck when I noticed a glowing/pulsating orange light in the NE sky about the altitude of an airplane, I called to my wife to come look at this "ufo" to play a joke on her thinking it was a satellite or such, when she came out onto the deck this aircraft stopped pulsing moved towards us briefly then made a 90 degree angle and took off into the sky disappearing. A movement that seemed very unnatural and very smooth. During our experience we both became captivated, and a dreadful feeling came over us which watered our eyes and was actually terrifying because without speaking we knew that what originated as a gag was actually was an unknown object, it took our breath away and caused our hearts to beat out of our chest. There was no clear definition to the craft just an orange sphere like pulsing light that became solid orange once the movement began. The whole experience lasted about 5 minutes. I served in the military, have experienced many things, but nothing like this, visually or emotionally.
