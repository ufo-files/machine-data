String of orange balls of light on NNE trajectory. MIssiles?

I was at home at about 10:10 pm. June 2, 2012 watching TV. My wife stepped onto the back porch and called me to come look at something weird. I didn't want to go, but she seemed alarmed/puzzled, so I did.

I saw an orange light traveling overhead on a what I believe to be a NNE trajectory. I assumed it was an aircraft, but the light was orange. It didn't blink like private aircraft beacons.

I thought it might be a helicopter beacon, but I couldnt hear it. Another one appeared over the hill following the same path. That didn't make sense, because it was a similar orange light which must have been visible in front of it like a landing light. But it wasnt landing. It followed the same path. Straight line until it disappeared perhaps behind clouds in about 90 seconds. More followed, a couple nearly parallel. I ran back into the house to take pictures with my cellphone, and then I took video. My wife did likewise. We speculated that it could be helicopters, then fighter jets from the Pittburgh Air Force base running afterburners. But it was no burst, and the lights only slightly flickered, not faded out, or were turned off. Each was visible for about 2 minutes, and there were at least 8 of them, perhaps more.

After the irregular series of them stopped, I continued speculating, and decided they most resembled what ICBMs in flight must look like.

But there was no news of multiple rocket launches. We cant believe nobody else near Pittsburgh noticed them.

We posted a video here, but have far more raw digital footage.
