Five bright lights traveling together.

My wife and I took our dogs and cats out for a walk down the street before begining our work day. As we turned and began heading back in a Northern direction I looked up and saw what I thought was a satelite. I said hey look a satal....and then saw a second one in the exact same flight path. I said wow theres two of them, then my wife and I stood and watched as a third, fourth and fifth appeared. All were in the same flight path. I would have thought satalite as I've seen many but never have I seen two right next to each other let alone five of them. They were bright for satelites but not enough that they attracted a lot of attention. Definatley not the standard dim hard to see without squinting satalites. They were all evenly spaced except the third and fourth or fourth and fifth, they were a little closer spaced than the rest. They crossed above the road we were walking on and as they reached the right side one by one the disappeared completely. When I went to work I told a coworker that I saw UFO's and he seemed surprised and said I saw some yesterday! He then told me how he had seen five lights and though no blinking he assumed they had to be helicopters since they appeared to be hovering. I was just stuck on the fact he said he had seen five since I hadn't yet told him what I had saw. I had my camera but doubted anything would show so I didn't even try to take a picture.
