{dr}Blinking white lights

I was getting ready for bed, and looked out the window at the stars before retiring. I saw blinking white lights near the stars, and watched for 20 minutes. These 2 objects gravitated horizontally and vertically and slowly navigated to the NW over the Pacific Ocean. I quit watching after 20 minutes and they were still there, never lost sight of them. I opened my window to hear if there was any noise, but there was none, just the sound of the ocean.
