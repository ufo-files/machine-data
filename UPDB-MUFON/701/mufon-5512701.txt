{cak} Very large, dark triangle, 3 lights (one at each angle). Moved from Oakland, CA toward San Leandro, CA (east/so. east) directly overhead.

I was standing in our street with my partner and neighbor, at approximately 6:20-30 p.m., Jan. 31, 2013 on our way to a concert. We were talking and for some reason I looked up, saw a very large, dark triangle, 3 lights (one at each angle), moving very silently. It moved from Oakland, CA toward San Leandro, CA (east/so. east) directly overhead. As I noticed what it was, I pointed it out to my friends and as I did, the lights went dark, and it was as if the craft "cloaked" and disappeared. The two people I was with were talking and by the time I could point it out, there was no sign of the lights, just darkness and then stars reappeared.
