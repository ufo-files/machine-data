It was a black triangle with three lights. It was seen from a plane window.

I was flying from London to Helsinki at night in a Finnair plane. Then I saw three bright lights. First I thought the were stars. But then I noticed that the lights outlined a black triangle. The triangle hovered and went different directions. Then the lights went out and I couldn't see the triangle anymore. The rest of the flight was normal.
