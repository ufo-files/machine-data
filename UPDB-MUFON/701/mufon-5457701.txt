{rjl} We saw 2 v- shaped flying objects

Around 7:40pm, we saw 2 v- shaped flying objects. By 9pm, looking north, we saw a twinkling light but it stayed still for about 5 minutes. It started going towards the house and stayed hovering. While it was hovering, there were 4 different flashes of light. The lights would zig zag from northeast to west. Then it went northeast, then finally over the house. I had to go to the back of the house to see it again. It was hovering next to a star. It proceeded to go northeast. Total time was about 55 minutes in the sky. We see them almost every day, different types, as long as the sky is clear.
