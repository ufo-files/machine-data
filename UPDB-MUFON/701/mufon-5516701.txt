{dr}Two large glowing red-orange orbs that looked like huge meteors

Took dog out around 10:45 PM. Saw what looked like a shooting star off in the distance. As it got closer, it looked more like a meteor. It appeared to be heading straight in my direction and appeared to be getting very close. 

A moment later, I saw another one directly behind the first, following the same path. The orange-red glow was too bright to look at for very long. I could not make out any kind of shape. I went inside to get my phone to get a video, but when I came back out, they were gone.
