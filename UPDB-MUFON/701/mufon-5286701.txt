Oval shaped, reflective object ascends over German city and flies away silently and horizontally

At 12:50 (Noon) I noticed an oval shaped, metallic object ascending diagonally into the air. While I thought it might be a balloon at first glance, its flight path looked extremely strange to me. The object was flying at exactly the same speed, no sound, no visible propulsion and there was no wind. I noticed, that it flew extremely steady, unlike a balloon which would flutter in the air. When the object flew behind trees, I rushed to the 10th floor of our building to shoot pictures and videos. Unfortunately, the oval shape is not visible on the pictures. However, the object was reflecting the sun, so that its flight path is visible on video. The pictures and videos are shot on an iPhone X (2020 Model).

After the object flew out of my view, I rushed back to the other side of the building, found some binoculars and waited for the object to appear on the other side. However, it never appeared and I could not find it in the sky. 

Further Info: There is a small airport nearby which also has a blimp, but it's east from my balcony, so the object did definitely not come from there. I can 100% exclude a plane, but not a balloon. However, I'm 90% sure it was no balloon.
