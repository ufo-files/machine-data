Fly by of 3 bright lights on 4.11.16

I was lying in bed facing a window which is 40cm wide. The sky was grey. As I lay looking out of the window I first saw 2 bright lights heading in a similar direction of the flight path of planes heading to Manchester Airport. As the 2 lights were passing a third light shot up behind them as if catching them up in a chidlike way. The 2 lights definitely were slowing to wait for it! They passed the window. My window has white organza as a curtain.
I jumped out of bed and ran to another window for a better look. They had gone. The lights didn't follow the flight path exactly, they were slightly off course but heading in a straight line. When I looked out of the second window, there was a double rainbow. I have a photo downloaded from Twitter from someone else who saw the rainbow. 

I first thought the lights were stars or maybe a plane as I see them so often.
