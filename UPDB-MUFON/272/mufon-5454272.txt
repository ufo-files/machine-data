A green shooting star like flash in front of me in a diagonal direction

I was driving south on Highway 19 just outside of Cassidy BC when I noticed a green shooting star anomaly that flashed in front of me in a diagonal direction. It lasted 10 seconds or less and disappeared before my eyes. It was probably a km above the ground - came from nowhere and was gone just as fast.
