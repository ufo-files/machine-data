Huge mothership like a city hovered above me.

Early on Boxing Morning about 6.30 am between 1995-2002 I was walking my dog near Bearsted golf club in Kent, England. It was a cold dusk morning, just becoming light, when I became aware of an object hovering directly above my head. Both my dog and myself stopped to look at it. The object was completely silent, about a mile above me and looked like a huge city just hanging there. It was bullet shaped, dark grey in appearance with a red pulsating light all around the rim. Underneath it seemed to be a huge swirling mass of white silvery stars that were constantly moving and changing position. It was awe inspiring to look at and must have been bigger than 20 football fields wide. It remained above me for about 12 seconds and then shot straight up and was gone in about a second. I have never spoken about it as at the time, no one believed me but it has remained with me ever since.
