Red spheres seen in sky

I reported this case last night when I spoke to a field investigator on report # 115047.

On 09/06/2018 at approx 415 am I woke up to use the bathroom.
While in the bathroom I looked out the window which faces east.
At this time of morning day break had not yet happened. The sky was still dark, and light was just starting to brighten the horizon. I viewed (1) red sphere on the horizon solid in color at the time start to blink almost like the red lights you would see on a tower or a water tank.
It blinked slowly, and then proceeded to split into (2) separate red spheres ( think of the red lights on a school bus) The original sphere would be on the right hand side as you viewed it with the new sphere on the left. Both spheres still red in color were solid in color as they hovered horizontally in the sky. I do not remember the time frame but both spheres went from both being solid in color to blinking together several times maybe 3 or 4. After blinking together they both blinked sequentially 3 or 4 times.
Both red spheres proceeded to rotate clock wise 4 or 5 times in a circle. ( like a reflector on a bike does when you ride it. After rotating several times they rotated counter-clockwise 3 or 4 times.
After the counter clockwise rotation they both returned to a horizontal position.
Both red spheres were solid in color during their rotation in both directions.
Now from my bathroom window north is to the left and east is directly straight out the window. Red sphere # 1 on the left was a solid red color and proceeded to move north. Red sphere # 2 was solid red in color and was moving east.
Sphere # 2 disappear in the air. Sphere # 1 was still heading north very slowly before it shot straight up into the sky.
There was no noise at this time of morning other than the birds starting to wake up.
There were no jets, and no helicopters in the sky. 

It should be noted that my house is 1 mile from the hydro electric plant on the Hudson River between Mechanicville and Stillwater.
The positioning of the objects that I saw was roughly in the vicinity of the dam and the hydro electric plant.
