Three lights crossing the nightsky in different directions.

My nephew called me tonight to tell me about lights in the nightsky. I went out my house, the nightsky was cloudy but I saw some lights that were moving from south to west spreading and crunching, creating clouds that surrounded them. When they reached de west at 40 degree above the western horizon, they turne towards east as following an ecuatorial orbit. Then I couldnt see them any longer.
