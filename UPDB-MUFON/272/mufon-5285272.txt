Orange lights circling like a carousel. Hundreds of sparkling lights in sky with a few white streaks here and there.

I was going on break. Two of my coworkers were sitting at a picnic table looking in awe toward the south as I came out. The one exclaimed, 'what was that! '. The other said 'I don't know'. As I approached them I asked what was going on. They didn't say exactly. I started to look at what they were going on about. I see the sky is filled with hundreds of flashing lights in the sky. The sky is filled with them. Like looking at glitter sparkle. And every once and a while there would be a white streak go across. Like a shooting star would. But they were everywhere. As I sat down at the table. Big orange lights appeared above the logistics building. From very dim to very bright. And it was slowly moving toward the East. It disappeared behind the pine trees and reappearing on the other side. All the while slowly spinning. Like a carousel. There was no sound what so ever of any aircraft. Eventually it disappeared behind the side of the building I work at.
