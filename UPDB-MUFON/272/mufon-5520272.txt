Saw two square like objects moving in unison over south shore blvd.

I was in my car making a turn when I noticed two very large objects in sky. It was about 1pm. Not in the shape of any known object like a plane or a bird. One was larger than the other and they were moving in a somewhat straight line and in unison. They seemed to change shape slightly as they moved but were still oddly square. Did not look like any bird or plane I have ever seen. I lost sight of them when I made my turn, I was on my way to work. other wise I would have followed them. Very strange, have never looked at anything in the sky before and not had an explanation.
