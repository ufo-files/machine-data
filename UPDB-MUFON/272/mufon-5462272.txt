{rjl} Slowly moving object observed between mountain peaks

I was out cutting cotten wood logs. This was my fourth trip out this day. It was a beautiful sunny day, clear skies, warm sunlight, maybe a small breeze. The sun was over the mountains which were south of me. There was another gentleman present cutting firewood. As I looked at him, I saw him gazing up towards the mountains, so I looked in the same direction. At first I only saw the sunlight above the mountains. In the moments it took my eyes to adjust, I saw a round, cylindrical, hot-dog shape between the ridges of the mountains from my point of view. It was an orangish-brown object which I estimated to be about 150-200 feet long and 40-50 feet high. I did not talk to the other wood cutter. I did not notice it the first three times I had gone out that day. It was slowly moving eastward. I researched the location on google maps and I estimate that I was about 4 miles away from the mountain ridges. It slowly moved out of sight behind one of the ridges. The total observed time was less than 20-30 minutes. The object did not change direction.
