I was outside in my car talking to my wife while I was taking a break from work. The location where I was parked is located on the far west side of Lawton, OK. Looking north from there, I could se the low lying Wichita Mountains in the wildlife refuge. As I was talking Inorth I saw a bright silver and reflective object appear and fly east for about six seconds before it disappeared. I told my wife that I think that I just saw a UFO. The Fort Sill Army base is in the area, but not in the direction where I saw the object. I am 41 years old, grew up in Lawton/Ft. Sill, and have never witnessed anything like that in my life.
