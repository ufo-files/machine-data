A very bright light appeared in the sky, descended, and then disappeared the exact moment I started recording.

I was sitting alone on a bench at the local cricket green on an October evening, and the sky was just beginning to get dark at sunset. To my right (so just slightly south of East) a really bright light appeared in the sky. My initial reaction was, "wow, that star is very bright considering how light the sky still is." I continued to look at it because it was sort of incredible and arresting due to how bright it was. I'm pretty sure that this light was completely stationary at first, but as I'm looking, I think to myself that it looks like it has started to ever so gradually move downward.

I look away from the light, thinking that I just need to adjust my eyes, but when I look back at it, I can tell that it definitely is moving downward. I feel like I watched it move downward very slowly for around a minute, all the while wondering what it is that I'm looking at. 

After about a minute, I think to myself, I should get my camera phone out and zoom in on it because I feel like I'm possibly seeing a UFO. This may sound strange, but I was hesitant to do this because I was considering at the time that if it is something unusual/paranormal, it would possibly disappear upon me getting my camera out. I have previously spoken to a MUFON investigator who told me of an experience once, whereby they saw a ball of light rise up and down from the ground, and that it disappeared when they pulled out a camera. If this was going to happen, I wanted to at least look at it for a while before it was gone.

So I got my camera phone out and did not press record, but I just zoomed in on it as far as I could to get a better look. All I could tell was that this was a bright white, glowing, circular light. It may have been a ball, but it was too far or abstract to determine that.

I watched it through my camera for around 10-15 seconds. At this point it had almost descended from an elevation of around 75 degrees from my eyes, to around 35 degrees, and I feared it would soon disappear behind the treeline in front of me, so, I decided to hit the record button.

The moment I pressed the record button, this thing instantly vanished from before my eyes. It was actually deeply confusing to me at first because I thought I'd somehow nervously moved the direction of my camera as I pressed record. I looked around the skyline with my eyes and it was nowhere to be seen. I only recorded the video for a few seconds and stopped it because now I felt like there was nothing to record. I then looked at the video I'd just captured, and sure enough, the bright light is right there for the first frame, and then just vanishes. At this point, I actually became quite alarmed and scared to be there alone. I couldn't believe that what I had considered and anticipated as a possible outcome, had actually happened before me.

I briskly walked, and I think I even started to jog out of fear, around the cricket green to the exit. A few days later after looking at this video over and over, I decided to put it into After Effects, stabilize it and crank up the contrast to see if I could make sense of the video clip. The light seems to disappear in a way that looks like it's wiggling, and it still faintly apparent for a second or two. It also flashes again for a single frame after around a second of it disappearing, but when it flashes, it has moved to the right and is lower down than where it was a second previous.

A few considerations I have: • this light appeared not too far away from the military airfield (Predannack Airfield) where I often see helicopter (Royal Navy Merlins I believe) training. This was not helicopter training, as that is impossible to miss because of the incredible sound that makes. I have considered that this was a flare, but I was sitting on that bench for a good 10-15 minutes before I saw this, and there was no sign or sound of anything flying up there. It was a beautiful tranquil evening. • I've also considered that this thing was disappearing behind very light and low cloud cover. What makes it strange for me is that it disappeared the very moment I pressed record, and also that on scrutiny of the video, it flashes after a second, and that the flash is not quite in the place that the light was, and that it has moved considerably for the time that it was not visible. • Thirdly, there have been other recent UFO reports down here on the local Village message board on Facebook, whereby numerous people witnessed a giant diamond shaped craft, 10-15x the size of a plane, disappearing and reappearing in different positions int he sky, and moving in the direction of this very village where I saw this. It was this recent sighting that prompted me to get on with reporting this to MUFON.
