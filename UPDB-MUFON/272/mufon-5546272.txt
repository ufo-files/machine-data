Flashing light.

So I was looking up at the sky hoping to see some stars, when all of a sudden I saw this strange flash in a triangular form. The light went from east to west and it only took about a minute before it was out of my sight. I knew it wasn't a plane cause a plane doesn't have 3 lights that are in a shape of a triangle. But I tried looking for it again, but it just disappeared for the night.
