{dr}2 observed by u.s. army air defence unit while on manuvers in N.M. desert (1971 July

in July 1971 we were on military manuvers in N.M. desert, west of Roswell. I was in an army air defence unit, stationed at Fort Bliss, Tx./
First object was spotted coming from the north and stoped. another object approached fron the west. For the next 5 hrs. the objects made eractic moves remaining in a close area.not knowing the altitude, the sizes were unknown. at daylight we were ordered to a location in the desert for chow. The whole unit was discussig the ufos. My unit had 500 plus soldiers, we were a vulcan/chaperal air defence unit. We were trained to recognize all foreigh and domestic air craft flying at that time. The ufos remained visible until the sun became too bright.
 This event will remain etched in my mind until I pass. Thanks for allowing me to share this experience.
