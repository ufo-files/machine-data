Helicopter type sounding machine chasing bright round light

We thought a large military helicopter must have flown over our house around 8:pm heading west from us. Around 9:00 it came by again, only I took my son out to see the "cool chopper". When we stepped out to see it, we quickly realized this was not helicopter. It was very thin, I referred to as "stick shaped" and very LONG. VERY BIG AND LONG. It hovered briefly and then changed directions. Then I saw a bright round circle light in front of this big "helicopter" thing. It appeared to be chasing the circle. The helicopter "thing" was black, had no flashing lights as a plane or helicopter would have during night flight. There was a smaller and brief red light circle. I feel as if it changed quickly? Something changed but I cant explain what. It was hard for my mind to comprehend what I was seeing. My son started crying and ran in the house. This was not a silent object. One of the two things made a noise that shook house. Assumed helicopter....but this was no helicopter and did not see any propellers. Flew lower than aircraft would. I asked neighbors if they saw, and they only heard but did not go out to look. They also said it shook their homes. I am positive it was chasing a round bright light that when I first saw I thought was a star. But stars are not that low and moving! My son thought the "helicopter" was shaped like a key.
