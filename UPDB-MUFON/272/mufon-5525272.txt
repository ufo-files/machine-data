Monbulk Sighting 31.7.2011

Witnessed by two people in Monbulk Victoria 6.20p.m 31/7/2011. Two star like objects travelling Westward from the South/East . Turn South in straight pattern and fly off in perfect alignment to each other . No noise ,photos,video taken directly after sighting revealed some objects that were not visible to naked eye . If you zoom in on the pictures you can observe two orbs . Not sure if these were the ufos seen as they had gone from eyesight this was taken 30 seconds after.
