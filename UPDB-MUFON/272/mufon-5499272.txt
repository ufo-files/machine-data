{cak} Humming sound, then silence, then silent lights in the sky

It happened be then 10:00 to 10:45 pm . There was a loud humming sound. It lasted approximately 5 to 8 second. We thought it was the garage opening, cause it sounded close. But it was unusual, unlike anything we ever heard before. It was so loud it caused alarm. So we immediately went outside. Several neighbors went outside to expressing concern and alarm. The dogs in the neighborhood were barking then they got very quiet. This is when we looked directly north of the home and saw in the direct path of my home and my neighbor's home some very low lights in the sky, amber in color and changing color with some reds. We watched it for about 1 minute. IT was stationary at this time, then it started to move to the west very slowly without sound. It was about 1/2 mile away at this time. Shortly after I went to the 7/11 directly north about half a mile from me. Asked the attendant did he hear any strange noise. He said yes, and all the power went out JUST in the 7/11. This would put the objects path directly over the 7/11 at the time of seeing the lights. The attendant said the computers all failed and nothing was working. After the lights dimmed the object could not be seen any longer. Heard a helicopter about 15 minutes after the object was seen. Did not see the helicopter. I found this strange cause in the beach community it is extremely quiet we do not get air traffic so late.
