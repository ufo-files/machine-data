{rjl} Bright oval shaped object, moving south to north, no blinking lights, no sound. Plane in area right after, could hear it and could see blinking lights.

I was star gazing as usual early morning before sunrise. I looked up and saw an oval shaped object flying from south to north at what seemed to be a pretty good rate of speed. I watched it for about 45 seconds before it disappeared into the clouds. This is actually the 4th time I have seen these type of objects. This one seemed to be either bigger or closer than the others. Just after seeing this unknown object a plane passed overhead. I could plainly see the blinking lights and hear its engines. The object I saw before had no blinking anything and I made sure to note that there was no sound. I suppose it could have been a plane that was so high I could not hear it. But in the night sky, from my experience, you can usually see flashing lights. Also, this object was all white and the light was bigger than the lights from an airplane. Just a white glowing oval object.
