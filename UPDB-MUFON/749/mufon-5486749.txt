I was standing outside (Westheimer Road 9400 block) when my apartment security guard said what is that and I turned around and saw what appeared to be a moving glowing ball of fire. I ran to my apartment and grabbed my cell phone and recorded this off my cell phone. I saw about seven of these what appeared to be balls of fire flying at a very low speed of about 6 to 10 miles an hour and an altitude of 500 feet off the ground from the southeast of Houston to the northwest direction one after the other about 30 to 40 seconds apart for a total of 5 then two more about five minutes later moving in the exact same direction. I only saw one object moving across the sky at a time. I was able to take several different videos and two pictures.
