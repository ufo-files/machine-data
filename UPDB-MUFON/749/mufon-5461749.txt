{rjl} Three Goose shaped objects in a triangle formation.

Three goose shaped objects. Gold in color. Could have been reflection off the high altitude objects from the sun. It was cold, crystal clear night. The objects appeared from the SE heading NW. The speed was amazing. Guessing 1500 mph. As they came across the sky to the north of me, they changed direction 20 degrees and continued due north about 7 seconds and then nothing. However, they should have still been visible in relation to speed/distance. I continued watching for a the objects, about 6 seconds, nothing; then from the South I spotted a meteor (I think) traveling NNE, just West of the objects disappearing northern path. When the meteor intersected the objects previous NW path it split into about 5 pieces and disappeared. Texted sister. Described the above. 6 hours later, our text history showed our conversation, but my description of the 3 gold or sunlight reflecting (Cross/Goose) shaped objects flying in a V formation was no longer on our smart phones. It kept me quiet for 2 years.???
