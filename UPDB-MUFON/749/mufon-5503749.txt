{dr}In sky following us then disappeared

My aunt and I were driving home after a late dinner and I noticed what I thought to be a very bright large star which I had never noticed before . I looked at it for a minute mesmerized by this new found sight then it moved quickly but erraticly in the sky then it started getting closer to the horizon following in the same direction as we were going. Then after a few minutes it disappeared from view as if it went invisible.
