Observed 2 groups of lights move silently over homes on our street

1. My husband and I were getting into bed when I looked out the window and noticed 2 groups of lights in the sky.

2. We observed them as they moved over the homes on our street towards ours.

3. It was clear to us it was something we had not seen before and thought they were ufos.

4. There were two groups of lights close to one another,we are not sure if it was 2 ufos or one with two groups of lights on it. Each group of lights had 5 white lights on the bottom and one smaller red light which appeared to be ontop of it. We observed them move 1/2 a mile at a a slow and steady rate of speed until they went over our house and out of site.

5. We watched intently and opened the window to hear any sound it might have made and there was none. We both felt we had seen a ufo and were excited.

6. It went over our house and out of sight.
