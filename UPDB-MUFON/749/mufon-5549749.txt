Saw V-Shaped Formation Of Crafts

Upon exiting my house, I paused to check the clarity of the sky and noticed a V-shaped formation of crafts traveling at moderate rate of speed headingsouthwest. We first thought they were a flock of geese, but they were traveling2-3 times faster than birds could fly, and were moving in an unvarying pattern.Our view tracked them through a 60 degree horizontal arc. I excitedly broughtthe object to the attention of my husband and 2 children. They were veryexcited to see this as well. My husband is reporting feeling unusually tiredtoday.
