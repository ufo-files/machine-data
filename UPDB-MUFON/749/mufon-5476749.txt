{dr}Looked above me to see basketball-sized metallic sphere flying at treeline next to a goose, both heading west

I was walking west on the High Line canal trail, just east of Broadway. For some reason I looked above me and slightly behind, where I saw a basketball-sized metallic sphere flying just above treeline and within about 4 feet of a canadian goose; both were heading west at about 35 mph, though their trajectories differed by about 5 degrees. At first I thought the object was an RC helicopter, but then I realized it had no propeller, no wings, no stabilizers, nothing--just a sphere. With this realization, I stared at it, and watched as it (and the goose) flew due west overhead (at that point following the trail) and across Broadway. There was traffic and I hoped that others saw what I was seeing. I lost sight of it as I watched it for another 15-30 seconds as it disappeared from my view.

To me, my reactions were more remarkable than the actual sighting. Initially I was disappointed, as I would have liked to see something more interesting than a metallic sphere that flew without changing course. That feeling only lasted a few seconds before the entire incident was "pushed" out of my thoughts; as if part of me didn't want to remember what had just happened--at all. I continued experiencing this "lacunar amnesia" for over a year before I could even tell my wife about the incident, and then only with great effort, and nearly two years to make this report.
