Object sighted from 2nd floor window looking west,had a row of blinking lights on lower edge ,watched for 15 min in clear sky

My wife got out of bed at 0230 & looked out the window noticing how bright the stars were when a horizontal oblong green object directly ahead caught her attention. Extending the arm with the finger & thumb spaced at 2 in. apart was the apparent length. The opaque green object was hovering stationary not far above the tree line which is 500 ft away. It also had a row of 5 near white lights in a horizontal row in the lower half of the craft that would blink in sequence, 1,2,3,4,5 than all out, 1 2 3 4 5 , all out, etc from left to right.The lights were large enough to see easily but more like marker lights than spot lights. After thinking this was highly unusual she returned to bed without noticing any change in movement or appearance.
