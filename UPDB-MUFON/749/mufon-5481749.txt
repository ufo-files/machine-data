{dr]50+ lights exactly the same relative size as aircraft from DIA in a

looking south from I-25 & hwy 52, I noticed a cluster of lights 50 or more, these were not in standard aircraft patterns and obviously too many in proximity to be conventional aircraft from DIA. this "cluster" was west of DIA and could have been over an area between my location & Denver, but not over the city. the lights were the same size as the aircraft I could see, and do see on a regular basis at this location, I also noted a helicopter passing behind me. after awhile I noticed the lights were moving in a set pattern and in groups of 3 were egressing from the area towards the west and at a high angle upwards.
I thought that surely somebody else saw this, and went back to the same spot nightly... after a couple days someone started setting off fireworks in about that same area of observation, the kind that produce large spheres of lights, I had to imagine somebody was covering.. they just needed to say I had seen only those fireworks!, but the difference between the two phenomena is rather stark. 
I noted the time and from 11pm to 12:20am the lights in 3s all left the scene... after you could see conventional planes with the green/white light combination moving on their expected usual vectors....
