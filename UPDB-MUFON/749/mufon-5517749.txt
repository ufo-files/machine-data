{nd} Red shimmering orb, pulsating, rising streamers, yellow solid lights

It wasnt there, then it was, maybe a mile or so away and maybe 1,000 feet up. Two small yellow lights with a big red shimmering ball of red above and centered. Kind of looked like it was on fire . It hovered for a minute or two, then went straight up for maybe a few hundred feet, stopped for a moment, then slowly moved away from me. It made no noise that I could hear. Wind was variable and was faster than the object appeared to move. Watched it head NNE at a static altitude then after about 5 minutes the lights disappeared.
The yellow lights were solid and had definite outlines, perfectly round with no spots, the red orb in the center above shimmered.

I thought for a second it was one of those lanterns people launch, but it was clear it wasnt after it started to move. The movements were very straight and were going in a direction different than the prevailing winds. And it was too steady a path.
