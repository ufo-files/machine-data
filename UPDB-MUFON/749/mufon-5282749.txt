6 orbs caught in my photographs

I took 4 photos of the Empire state building from 27th and Broadway on my way to a get together. It was very misty and the empire state building was glowing some nice reddish colors through the fog. The street car lights were bright so I took 4 pictures hoping one would come out. This morning I looked at the pictures and in the 2nd and 3rd photo, I could see 6 white orbs apparently moving in a NE direction over the Empire State building. They don’t appear in the first and last pictures but the middle 2 I took within seconds of each other and they are in similar but not in the same positions to each other or relative to buildings. I am submitting the 4 photos in the same order I took them, with zero editing. This is only a possible sighting as I don’t know what they were. But they look like what a lot of other sightings look like. the pictures were all taken within the same minute and I didn’t notice them until I looked at the pictures this morning.
