my husband and I went out side at 8pm, we talked for a couple of minutes. It was a wet, cloudy night. I looked up from where we were standing in our back yard and thats when I saw it. It was like a a massive ship with fake reflected clouds across the leading edge (over the length of a football field)of what ever this massive ship was. I believe that because there was so much moisiture in the air and so many lights, you could actually see the reflection of the leading edge of the ship(it clearly was meant to be invisible). I was so glad my husband was with me, he is a non believer and he was shocked and I think scared. It moved across the sky so quickly and the length was frighteningly big. It was gone out of sight in under 1.5 - 2 mins. I have never seen anything like it, it was like something out of a movie. It was right before Anzac Day. I reported this to an Australian/New Zealand site on the net...never heard anything back. This is my last attempt.
