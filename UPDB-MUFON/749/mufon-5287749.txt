cloaked hovering flying object oval shaped with eye in center and wings that rotated around the outside of the object

I was sleeping on the couch at a friend's house during the day. I woke up to a small flying object hovering in front of me. It was clearly visible at first. It was oval shaped about the size of a baseball. It had an eye in the center of the oval and it had wings that looked like cilia all around it while it was hovering. They were whirring around the object. Then it appeared to cloak itself and disappear. It was semi-transparent (blue/green silver).
