{dr}I was looking up in the sky at stars saw two lights close together one after the other, one smaller. It was clear and looked to be a jet but it moved incredibly fast and disappeared in a second like it went straight up. No sound, no trail, no flash.

It was about 10:05 PM monday night. I was in the backyard with my dog and saw how clear the sky was and how bright the stars were. I watched a jet flying east to west go off in the distance and heard its engines as it moved away. I looked straight up and right where I was looking I saw two white lights in a row, very close together, one slightly smaller than the one in front of it. They were clear and looked like a jets lights from a commercial jet. but it moved incredibly fast and disappeared in a second in mid sky. It looked like it went higher and faster and then just vanished. There was no trail like from a comet and no flash and no sound. it was going north to south. I said it looked like a jet although the lights never blinked but I saw them clearly and it didn't appear to be extremely high up, no higher than a commercial jet would be. But Ive never seen a jet move that fast and disappear that fast and seem to speed up. If I hadnt have looking right where it flew I wouldnt have seen it as it was gone in a second.
