{cak} 5 redish-pink glowing objects hoovering over Dayton during fireworks.

My young child and I were leaving the Moraine Ohio fireworks displays around 10:25p.m. I drove down Dryden Rd. until I reached Nicholas Rd to turn left. The traffic light was red so we stopped. I told my child to look out her window we were on the small bridge and we can see peoples fireworks. We saw a few fireworks going off when I noticed five balls of light that did not fade. I turned left when my traffic light turned green. I turned left and pulled off the road to the right on Nicholas Rd. The balls of reddish-pink light were still there. I watched the lights for several minutes. One off the objects kept moving back and forth between the other objects. Finally 3 of the objects took off and flew over to the right a short ways and then flew straight up and disappeared in the sky in like 2-3 seconds. At that time I got back in my car and drove home in excitement and called my friends and family to see if anyone else has seen what I had seen.
