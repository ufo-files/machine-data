White orb crossing the skies during the day

I took a 10-minute video of the sky during the day, using a Canon EOS 550D digital camera (T2i) and Canon 18-55 mm lens (in 20 mm) in video mode (1920x1080 pixels, with 30 frames per second). The width of the covered area of the sky is about 58 degrees by 34 degrees.
At about 8 minutes of recording, you can see a bright punctual object, about 0.15 degrees in diameter, crossing the skies from east to west, traveling around 32 degrees in 1.83 seconds (captured in 55 frames). The calculated angular velocity was 0.3209rad / second. If the object were at an altitude of 10 km (commercial aircraft), it would be at almost 12000 km per hour! The lens was focused on the infinite (using a perfect focus on the margins of the few clouds in the blue sky). Using the Stellarium program, with the satellites plugin enabled, there are no satellites visible at this speed (crossing 32 degrees in less than 2 seconds). Is it a UFO?
