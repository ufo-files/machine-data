Unknown objects in the sky.

I was cleaning the pool in my back yard for a bout 20-25 minutes the I saw an white object to the east but right above me in the noon position. My child came out to see if the pool is ready and noticed the object with me. I soon got my cell phone to record video and take pictures. The time of the first pictures was at 3:38 pm. After a couple of minutes my other children came out to go into the pool and I asked my oldest to get digital camera so I can get better look at 5hia object. I have a Panasonic DMC-FZ1000 which has a 400mm optical 1600mm combined zoom digital. I got 20 mp pictures and full HD video of the object throughout the encounter. The main white object was the biggest of all the objects. The other objects were small white or reflective like surface and move or shot around the sky. Another object look like a green balloon but that one I am not 100% sure. But the other object were not terrestrial or conventional. One small object went moving down the made a 90 degree turn up then turned again afterwards it disappeard and my camera lost focus. When that happened my child and I were the witnesses. My whole family came out and viewed the objects until about 5pm. And at about 6:20pm I was outside observing the main white object and after I took a glance at it, it was gone from the sky like it just disappeard. During that time my family (8 people) we witnesses to the event.
