Sitting in my truck, looked up at the right time.

Came out of my bank, got in my truck, after I put the key in the ignition I looked out the windshield in time to see a disk type UFO move between lower hanging clouds (very cloudy day). Looked for about five minutes but did not see it again. The disk was gold in color, flat on the bottom, straight sides at about thirty degrees tilt in and a thin spindle on top. The first time I saw a UFO I was ten years old, I have seen many more though the years since then, this is the first gold one. This is the first time I have reported a sighting.
