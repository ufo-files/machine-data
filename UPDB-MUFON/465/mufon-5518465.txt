Driving home 4am noticed conventional aircraft at low altitude suddenly being paced by red/orange orb that made fast erratic movement before vanishing.

At approx 04:00 hrs I was driving home from my fiances house in Suoth Park. As I sat awaiting a traffic light at the intersection of Maple Springs Dr and Brownsville Rd I observed an aircraft flying approx 1500 ft altitude heading northeast towards Allegheny County airport at approx 70-80 degree elevation. Suddenly a red/orange sphere approx half size of said aircraft appeared maybe several hundred feet below aircraft. The sphere object paced the aircraft momentarily then made a very fast Z like movement that left a firery like trail and suddenly just vanished. The aircraft did not change course or altitude or make any attempt at evasion though I suspect the pilot would have had to observed this object. My vehicle windows were up so I could not discern any noise from either craft. The entire sighting lasted less than 5 seconds. Coincidentally, my fianc1 himself wittnessed a similar Red orb/sphere at a lower altitude last summer about 1 mile away from my sighting. Also, coincidentally (maybe) he have been noticing an increase in Apache, Cobra, and even Kiowa helicopters in our area even though there is no Army airfield base anywhere near here. At least once or twice a week we have observed these helicopters always coming from and heading towards different directions.
