{cak} saw a ufo at night a bunch of us.

I was 10 years old. A bunch of us kids were playing tag in the field behind a friends house. It was dark at this time, around 10 pm. I noticed one of us was looking up towards the sky so I followed suit. Then all of us were staring at this thing. It a humongous boiling ball, it reminded me of the sun. It was slowly descending to the ground on the other side of the valley. As it got closer to the ground I saw the trees bent over like some tremendous force was blowing them around, practically touching the ground. The light from it was glowing yellow and lit up that whole side of the mountain. Right before it physically landed we all scattered, ran home like frightened rabbits. None of us ever talked about it. I tried to talk to my mother about it when I came busting into the house but she just yelled at me like I was bothering her. I thought about it for weeks and know in my heart that it was something not man made nor of this earth. Ever since then I believe.
