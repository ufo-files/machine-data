{rjl} It was a very bright star until it pulsates and vanishes right in front of me.

I was going to church for a bible study at St Vincents in Richboro and it was before twilight and I noticed a very bright star. I was thinking it might possibly be Venus and right while I was looking directly at it the thing pulsates once and vanishes without a trace. Gone. Like it had never been, while I was looking directly at it. I have no explanation and there were no clouds nearby. I saw something vanish while I was looking directly at it wondering if it might be Venus! It was freaky.
