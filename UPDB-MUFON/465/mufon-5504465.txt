I was sitting outside having a cigarette and talking my cell phone. I was just looking at the clouds and a orange light blinked on. It was bright and moving slowly. It was about the size of a golfball if my arm was stretched out. I ran in and woke up my husband and he came out and saw it as well. He agrees it was a ufo. It was beneith the low clouds and not radiating off them like the helicopter that flew south a few minutes earlier. I tried to film it with my phone but it didnt show in my viewfinder. Then after watching it for several second it blinked off. It had no sound and the lights did not change. I could not see shape because the lights were so bright.
