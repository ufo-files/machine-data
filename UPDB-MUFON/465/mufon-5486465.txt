{cak} Lime green bubble wrap texture at bottom, reflected off trees

I came onto the base btwn. 11 pm to midnight. coming in off liberty. I was walking, about 1/4 mile or so past the guard shack, into the outer road that goes into woods, comes out at marine barracks. All of a sudden there was a bright lime green light around me, I stopped walking, looked at my arms that were lite up, I turned my palms up, amazed I looked up and saw a very large, maybe 40-50 ft. What i thought was the bottom of a craft, it was reflecting off the top of large pine/fur trees it looked to me like big bubble wrap, lime green. I stared at it for what i thought was only a min. or two. then when I started to walk the rest of the way, I was standing at the opening to the barracks, the sun was just breaking the horizon, Apx. 5 hours missing time. I felt unsure of what just happened
