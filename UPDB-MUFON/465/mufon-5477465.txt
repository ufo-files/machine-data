{cak} Round stereo typical looking saucer. Individual lights all around. Made many directional changes at high speed and slow speeds.

I am a Coast Guard Veteran, now working on a Tugboat as a civilian.
I woke up this morning around 5:30am
It was still dark. I went outside on deck of the boat to drink my coffee and get some fresh air. Visibility was 100℅ without cloud cover. There was a low lying fog on the swamp. Location is Port Allen Louisiana. I looked up and noticed a object moving from the North to the South underneath Venus. I figured it to be a satellite at first. Then when it got to the South of Venus it stopped. It was much to high in the sky to be a maned craft. Over the next hr I watched it make several direction changes at extreme angles. Hover in a circular pattern and just float back and forth. We have a pair of binoculars onboard, so I used them to gain a better look. It was round and had circular lights that where around the rim of the craft. They where white lights. I couldn't tell the surface color. It looked dark from my point of view. It was definitely not a star. It was a flying craft. But nothing like I've ever seen in person. At 6:30 a airplane passed at a much lower altitude. Gave me an idea of how much higher it was. I had a half moon and Venus to give me fixed points of reference to observe the flight pattern of this object.
