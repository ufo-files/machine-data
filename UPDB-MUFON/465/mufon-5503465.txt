{cak} Silver cigar shaped object travelling at a very high rate of speed, much faster than conventional aircraft.

I was driving my vehicle on the way to the store. I had only traveled 2-3 blocks from my home when I noticed a large cigar shaped object in the sky traveling in a SE direction. The weather was clear with no cloud cover. The object appeared to be silver in color, and it was very high in the sky and appeared to be miles away from my location. What caught my attention was both the very high altitude and the speed of the object. It appeared to be much higher in altitude than a conventional aircraft, and it was traveling at a great speed, much faster than an aircraft appears to travel in the sky. I only observed the object for a few seconds before it disappeared from sight.
