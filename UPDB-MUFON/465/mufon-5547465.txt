Lights moving in a circle motion , changing colors

I was a cadet riding with the pickens county sherffs dept while i was a junor in high shool. We recevide a call about strange light that we responded to,when we arrive the deputy and i did observe a circlar shape object that contantly changed colors but made no sound. It was located over an open corn field . we drove very slowly and the craft started moving with us, it was 100 to 150 feet away from us. it was a clear night with a full moon, so we had a very good look at this object. this whent on for about 6 min and then suddenly, it jumped about 3 miles away and then, made an upward motion and vanished toword space. I went to call in and report what we had seen an the deputy told me not to because there was no way we could prove it. I was told by the deupty that they had receive many calls but was not allowed to report for fear of being fired.
