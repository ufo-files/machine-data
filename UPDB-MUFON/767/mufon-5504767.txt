I was walking kind of aimlessly around 8:10 pm, heard a loud buzzing behind me and though someone on a mophead or something was behind me. The thing was bright red and green and I'm not sure of it was completely square or not but was squarish and zoomed past my head way up in the sky going very quickly. I knew immediately that it was something strange and stopped and stared. It went one block down then took a sharp left , went about half a block, took another sharp left and came back to about three houses down from where I was standing. It tilted downwards as if it were looking at me and lights went off and I only saw it to be a dark color ( it was night) then hovered for about a full minute, then started descending and when it seemed like it got to the rooftop it just completely disappeared. I was very freaked out and immediately started googling pictures of ufos to see if I could find anything similar. This is the weirdest thing that has ever happened to me. My boyfriend came out while it was descending and hovering so he saw as well.
