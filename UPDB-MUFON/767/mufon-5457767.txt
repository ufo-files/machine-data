{tjd} Looked like a 60'+ orange bus that bent as it came around the sharp curve.

I was driving my father from Oregonhouse to Oroville on a country back road just after dusk and had just crossed a small one lane bridge. As we headed up a steep hill (approx. 60 yards long) with a sharp 90 degree left turn at the top a 'vehicle/object' came around the curve at a super high speed about 3' off the ground and no wheels. The outside was a glowing orange. It was lit up inside white & orange with windows. We saw 'figures' through windows that were almost human shaped, but with very long necks. Also, the vehicle seemed to bend as it came around the corner and past us. My father yelled, "What the hell as THAT?" I pulled off the road and we both were shaking. After we calmed down, I asked Dad to discribe that he thought he saw. We had both seen exactly the same thing. Unfortunately my Dad passed away about 2 months later at 92, so he cannot be contacted as the 2nd witness.  I never told anyone about this until earlier this year, and only to a relative who didn't think I was 'nuts'! I haven't traveled that road since!
