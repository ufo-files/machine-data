2 separate brightly lit fast moving objects one white trailed by one orange on a straight path passed overhead while sat outside a friends garden

While sat outside enjoying the evening with friends who had just gone inside to put thier children to bed.
Myself, wife and son observed two fast moving objects, one brilliantly white, the other bright orange, parallel in flight, the orange object slightly lower and smaller in size.
The sighting lasted apprximately 10 seconds max travelling directly overhead in a NE direction.
I have in the past worked nights driving about 20yrs so have been used to seeing plane lights, helicopter lights, used to the speed of travel.
This sighting did not resemble, anything i could identify, as a plane helicopter, the speed was way too fast, the sighting was over as quick as it had begun.
I have had a few memorable sightings working nights that span a few years, from verticle green lights, to a black triangle that passed over my truck.
I have always looked for the logical explanation, first in anything strange that i have seen, from reflections to the explainable, some things just defy all those statistics.
