I saw a light in the northern sky at about 60 degrees high that moved east then dropped extremely fast and disappeared.

I was letting our dog out around 11:00 pm on Thursday, October 14. I was standing on our North-facing back deck and happened to look up at the clear night sky. I saw several stars, but one of them appeared to be pulsating and slowly moving eastwards. I thought it might be a plane, but all of a sudden it shot towards the ground at the speed of a bullet and was gone in an instant. I know for certain that it was not an aircraft — I lived next to two major RAF bases in the UK and know how aircraft typically react. This anomaly was in no way suggestive of military or civilian aircraft might operate. Further, there was absolutely no sound associated with this sighting.
