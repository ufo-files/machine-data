Blue orb sighted in 1972-74 close to Camp Lejune.

Between 1972-74 me and approximately nine other kids were playing football in an open field. The weather that day was overcast. All of a sudden a blue orb appeared at the other end of the field above pine trees almost treetop level under 100'. It was moving from right to left at a steady altitude constant speed very slow as if observing us. It then started gaining altitude at approximately a 35 degree angle same speed and within the blink of an eye gone. This object had no sound. I am a retired Fire Controlman and know what I saw was not even close to the technology of the time and quite frankly was out of this world. At the time I was 10 or 12 years old.
