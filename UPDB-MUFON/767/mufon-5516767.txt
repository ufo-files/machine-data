{dr}Green Light in the Sky

Bright neon green spherical light, similar in light and color to a large firework, fell at meteoric speed leaving a slight trail. Like a meteorite but bigger than any I have seen and going straight down. I was most struck by the size and color. Never seen anything like it in 37 years. Seemed to just materialize out of the clouds and silently rush toward the ground. I don't think it was any type of fire.
