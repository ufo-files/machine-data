FILLED OUT WITH WITNESS, ON THE PHONE, AT MUFON HQ, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR  L. FLECHTNER 8/27/15
___________________________________________________________________________

IN THE SUMMER EVENING OF JULY, 1967, ABOUT 8:30 PM, I SAW THREE GLOWING, PULSATING, RED LIGHTS/BALLS GLOWING UP IN THE SKY. IT HAD COME DOWN AT AN ANGLE AND HOVERED UP TO 15 MINUTES. IT SEEMED TO BE 20' WIDE AND 10' TALL.THE WHOLE NEIGHBORHOOD OF MT HEALTHY, CAME OUT TO SEE IT. IT MADE NO SOUND, BUT WAS THERE LONG ENOUGH FOR PEOPLE TO CALL OTHER PEOPLE TO COME OUT AND SEE IT. AFTER 15 MINUTES IT JUST LEFT STRAIGHT UP IN THE AIR. IT COULD HAVE BEEN ONE OBJECT OR THREE SEPARATE OBJECTS.

THE SECOND SIGHTING WAS IN OCTOBER OF 1976, IN THE EVENING. I WAS TEACHING MY GIRLFRIEND TO DRIVE A STICK SHIFT CAR AND WE SAW THE FLYING SAUCER WITH LIGHTS MOVING AROUND THE CENTER OF IT, MOVE ACROSS THE SKY SLOWLY. WE FOLLOWED IT TO THE CHURCH WHERE BINGO WAS JUST LETTING OUT AND STOPPED THE CAR. ALL THE PEOPLE FROM BINGO SAW IT TOO. IT WAS LARGER THAN A 747 AND MADE NO SOUND.. THEN IT SEEMED TO EMIT SMALL LITTLE ORBS AS BIG AS VOLKSWAGENS. THE ORBS MOVED AROUND THE SKY, WENT BACK TO THE BIGGER SHIP AND IT SHOT STRAIGHT UP INTO THE SKY TOWARD THE WEST, AND WAS GONE. THE CHURCH WAS NEAR GALBRAITH AVE AND THE SIGHTING LASTED 10 MINUTES.
