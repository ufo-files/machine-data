{rjl} Dark Orb Spotted Above Waterway

The dramatic event happened in a matter of seconds. My friend and I were on a walk to a dock in our neighborhood. While taking pictures of the scenery, we heard a low hum. I snapped a picture of a tree, my friend and I looked at it to find a dark orb in the photo. Our stomachs dropped. We looked into the sky and felt heat on our faces (despite the 32 degree wind chill) the sunlight was reflecting off of it rapidly and with a flash it disappeared.
