{jpg} A flickering bright star like object just hovering towards the West of my location

I went outside to smoke a cigarette and was looking around and noticed a bright star like object blinking white, red and blue or green colors. I picked up my phone and started recording it as best I could then just watched it for about 10 more minutes before going back inside. I was starting to think it may have just been a strange star, until I had a feeling to go check again about 10 to 15 mins later and it was gone. I felt my heart racing when I first saw it and stared to record, but then became calm. I know darn well this isn't a star because stars do not dissapear and there are no clouds in sight anywhere.
