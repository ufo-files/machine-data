{rjl} cylindrical objject moving below the moon just after sunset

At approximately 2030 EST on the 14th of September, in early twilight, sitting in a lawn chair facing south, I was looking at the Moon. The sky was clear and the planets, except Venus, were not yet visible. Suddenly, I saw a fuselage shaped object about 3 degrees below the Moon, moving East to West at what appeared to be aircraft speed. It was dull light brown in color with indistinct lines along most of it's length, with vertical divisions, suggestive of louvers or windows. It was fuselage shaped in that the front and rear were cone like. I wouldn't describe it as cigar shaped because the ends were more pointed. At first I thought it was an aircraft, but I could see no wings or tail section. No vertical stabilizer at all. There were no lights at all, neither green, red, nor white. Nothing blinking as a aircraft might. It made no sound that I could discern. I blinked, glanced at the Moon, and then back to where the object should have been, but it was gone. In all it was visible for a short time, perhaps 5 seconds, and traveled 2 to 3 degrees across the sky, 4 or 5 lengths of itself. The object was clear and distinct, with a sharp outline, except for the lateral and vertical lines. It is tempting to describe this object as a saucer seen from the side and spinning, but it was not. My sighting seems fairly consistent with others of the cigar/fuselage shaped objects. However,my impression is that this object was not larger than 200 ft. in length.
