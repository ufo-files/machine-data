Cluster than a sequence of patterns

Approx. 8:20 pm, i was traveling north bound on the i15 cruise control at 70mph, between Barstow and Halloran Springs right before Baker. And to my left is saw a cluster of lights which i assumed was a low flying helicopter and didn't pay much attention, when i looked back to see what kind of helicopter, a split second the lights had diapered. The sky was still light a dusky pink orange, so there was no way i could not see the helicopter if it just turned off its lights. Before i could think where did it go? A triangle pattern of three lights appeared for about 3 seconds and flashed of at the same time, and then before i could absorb what just happened one bright light appeared and separated into 2 lights coming apart in a straight line. This pattern of 3 and 2 lights repeated and seemed to follow my drive for about 8mins the last pattern i saw was a straight line of lights that turned off one by one. NOT FLARES!! No SMOKE and No SOUND!! I wish I could have videoed the event but there was no great vantage point, and even thought i could see them clear as day, there was not enough light for my camera phone. Hopefully my drawing helps.
