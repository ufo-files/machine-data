This was a long time ago probably no earlier than 1967 no later than 1975 I was at my mothers house which is on the corner of 2 streets so the back of her house faces the side of the neighbors house where it is a narrow strip of land on a hill where nothing flying could safely land, plane or helicopter. That day my mom was washing dishes in the kitchen sink and said (name removed) I think there's something strange outside, I went in the kitchen and looked through the blinds and there was a capsule shaped object hovering just off the ground no landing gear no propellers it made no noise and there was no exhaust. The capsule had a window that went across from left to right and then it curved down back the other way. I saw two men that looked like 2 American men with sunglasses on and I saw a chair the seat and back very square a little out of place looking. Then it took off w/out noise or exhaust I tried to run outside and see it but it was gone. I wrote down the date Aug. 9, 2017 watching Ancient Aliens a man sitting on a tall stool or chair w/a desk behind him is talking the camera angle get wider and I see on the left side of the TV a capsule w/a window and a square chair like the one I saw at my moms house driving to my mothers end of June 2013 approx I saw a bright ball in the sky traveling very level from West to East Towards Wash D.C. near to New Hampshire Ave. Stopped car a tone point it looked like a circular saw blade on fire burning with teeth on the outside. (only other unusual sighting I have had)
