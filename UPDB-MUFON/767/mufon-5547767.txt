Neon Green light fell from the sky

On monday december 22 I was watching tv around 10-11 at night with my brother. Something caught my attention outside, so I took galnce at my field behind me. I saw a bright ball falling to the ground. I jumped up and told my brother to look outside. I thought someone shot a firework into our field.(And no there is no neighbors around just woods) The bright ball had a little bit of an arch to it but it disapeared and there were no signs of a ground impact. I didn't think much of it until december 26 12:16 when my friend was waiting for me outside to leave. He came back in, and described the exact same thing i saw a few days earlier. This was strange because my brother was the only one who knew what I had seen. My friend and I both agreed that the object came very close to the ground and was only a few hundred feet away from the house. It was only around for a few seconds before disappearing.(It was not a fire work!)
