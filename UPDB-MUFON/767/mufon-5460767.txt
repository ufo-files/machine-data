I first noticed the light on the east horizon and thought it was a radio tower or a star but then it blinked and I started to take a video. It moved very slow over us to the west. When it was closest it looked like a black triangle with hundreds of different color and different size lights. Ronald joked that it looked like a christmas tree. We filmed it until it got to the west horizon. As it flew away it turned into two lights then three and then finally one and it was gone. The whole time the lights would seem to change color. There was red orange
White blue green pink and purple.
