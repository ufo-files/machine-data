{nd} Observed green ball descending straight down from the sky. No pieces broke apart like you would see with shooting star or fireball.

I was driving Northbound on a county highway at about 7:47 pm. I was coming up on an intersection when I observed a bright green sphere descending straight down from the sky. I noticed that it wasn't breaking up or changing in intensity, like you would see with a meteor or fireball. I saw it for a short period of time, as my vision then became obscured by trees. From my vantage point it seemed like it was within the city limits. The object was penny sized and seemed to be at a distance of under one mile. I heard no sound, and it just seemed to be something other than a celestial object.
