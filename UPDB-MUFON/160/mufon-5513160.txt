{cak} Red & blue rectangular lights flashing above treetops.

As I was looking out my kitchen window towards Lake Michigan, I noticed two rectangular lights side by side blinking above the treetops. There was a red one on the left and a blue one on the right. They blinked quickly two or three times in five seconds or less and disappeared. The lights were stationary and from my perspective were about 100+ feet above the tree line, they definitely could have been out over the lake... not sure what I saw.
