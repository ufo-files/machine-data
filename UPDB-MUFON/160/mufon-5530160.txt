Three very bright star like objects moving slowly.

We were sitting on the front porch.I looked off in the distance and above the trees in the East I saw three very bright stars in a triangle pattern.There were'nt many stars out yet and I was thinking about my son starting astronomy class this week.I said to my husband "are those stars over there?" He commented how strange to see those three bright stars so low on the skyline in the East.The one star started to move towards the other.Then they were all moving slowly as if floating.We were all amazed and tried to take a video but the battery was dead.We would lose sight of them behind the trees and rooftops.I called my neighbor who lives closer to that direction.Her family went outside and they witnessed the same slow moving bright stars.They told me they witnessed one go directly above and over the back field and disappear.There was no sound related to thier sighting.We saw one bright star appear again and move slowly over the horizon.This went on for about 30 minutes then they were gone.
