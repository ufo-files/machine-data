{cak} Two large orange/blue objects travelling 30 seconds apart west to south in night sky. Slowly disappeared

My wife was having a cigarette in the back garden when she spotted a large orange/blue object in the sky travelling west to south. She shouted to me to come and have a look and i saw the object for a few seconds before it slowly disappeared. At that moment another orange/blue object appeared in the sky travelling west to south along the same trajectory as the first, i quickly opened the camera on the phone and took a picture. The second object then disappeared like the first object, it looked as if it was travelling south at speed as it disappeared. These were definitely not planes, helicopters or satellites, nor where they Chinese lanterns as they didn't flicker. The objects seemed to be very large in size but did not make any sound.
