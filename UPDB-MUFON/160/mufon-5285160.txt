Blue orb/light rise from field

While walking my dog saw a bright blue diamond shape light rise up from light house field. At first I thought it was a drone, it wasn't. It then shot up really high and then flew way out over the West Cliff then out over the ocean. It came back over lighthouse field and just hovered for about a minute, Then it took off towards downtown Santa Cruz, disappearing. Then it appeared again over Lighthouse field, and now I have my iphone camera trying to zoom in. It then goes across the field and hovers next to another bright white round light orb next to a 3 story condo next-door to lighthouse field. While Im zooming in with the camera, it take off again .
