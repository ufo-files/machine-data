USO. Fast moving, directional changes, in a lake, silent, no wake, bright, disappear and reappear.

I was in the arctic circle in norway backpacking and I noticed this bright fast moving light in an arctic lake below me from the mountain i was standing on. The lake was roughly 300 to 400 meters below me and a distance of about 1 to 2 kilometers away. I first noticed it when I thought i was looking at an iceberg but realized it was much more when i made s turns and 90 degree right angle turns on the dime, then it shot across the lake probably covering a distance of half a kilometer in 10 seconds giver or take. after it shot across the lake to the other side I began to film it where it continued to behave in a similar manner as described above, however less drastic and less turing. the video will show its turns and how it disappears and reappears also its apparent flashing sequences. 

I was very shocked by what i saw, I knew it was something that does not occur in these arctic lakes. Some of the other ppl i was with didn't seem to be as shocked about the event and they simply explained it with nonsense such as mating snakes which makes no sense at all. after watching it and then seeing the videos again all the ppl who saw it with me actually realized that what they had seen was supernatural to say the least. Overall I was creeped out mostly because I have spent allot of time in this region of the world and have never heard of anything in these waters except trout and icebergs. I lost sight of the object after it vanished for the last time. all of my descriptions can be reinforced in my video. I know that its not technically a UFO but I believe USOs are equally important in understanding and critiquing anomalys.
