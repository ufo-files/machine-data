{rjl} UFO flying above above a valley In Yosemite Nat'l Park

This is information and footage/screenshots from a friend of mine out west that was sent to me.

He was filming the landscape and making funny "blinking eye" motions over the phone camera for comedic effects. He actually did not notice that he captured a video of a UFO until later when he was doing a slow motion effect to his footage. He told me he couldn't see the object at all at normal speed. 

The object appears flying through the sky and slightly banking to its right extremely fast (even while in slow motion) and completely silent, we were both in the military together and agreed that as far as we knew, our aircraft technology hadn't gotten so good to be THAT fast AND SILENT at the same time.

As you can see from the footage it appears in upper left corner and disappears behind the mountains to the right.
