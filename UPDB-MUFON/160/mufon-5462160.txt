{tjd} white dot darting in and out of clouds

A woman I know at work noticed a white dot buzzing around the sky out the window. She took out her phone and recorded some footage. when you first view the footage, you can't see it. But after a few times of pause and play you WILL see it. 
She sent it to me. I took the footage and put it in some video editing software and painstakingly tracked it with a circle frame by frame. so now there are two clips. the files are fairly large.
I don't know what this is. It could be a bug. I think its too fast and erratic to be a drone. definitely not an airplane.
I thought after two years it should be sent to you.
