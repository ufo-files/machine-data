Huge bird-like object seen from flat facing north west over south Glasgow area. I have good knowledge of ornithology and this wasn't a bird.

The object glided effortlessly across the sky making occasional flaps of huge wings. I could make out a central body with wings either side. I am familiar with birds of prey and actually seen a large eagle-like bird being escorted by helicopter a couple of years ago. However, that looked like a bird, probably eagle, whereas this object was considerably larger and definately not an eagle. I'm hoping other people observed this and took photos/film from closer proximity.
