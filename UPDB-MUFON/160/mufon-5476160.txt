{jpg} Rounds, sphere like object that hovered S/SW of downtown Chicago

In the afternoon of May 25th, I observed a nearly stationary, black spherical object from my home balcony. The balcony faces south towards downtown Chicago, which is only about 1 mile away. The object appeared to be over the south loop area or just to the west of highway 90. I observed the object for about 10 minutes before it appeared to fade away without moving. I have included a video I shot from my iPhone as the object can be referenced just to the west of the Willis tower. With my iPhone I could zoom into the video and observe the object spinning/pulsing.
