{cak} 4 transparent white body with red outline object with red outlline , appear in straight line hover over us 1 at a time then head north and vanish

My wife and I arrived home from dinner around 10:30pm. She went inside and I remained outside in front of my house gathering my belonging from my car to bring inside. I noticed past the roof of my house 3 red/orange lights that looked unique, living on long island and in a flight path to LGA airport I see a lot of planes (commercial and private) as well as helicopters. I almost instantly ruled out the light that were heading directly towards my home, was a plane or helicopter. As the 3 identical objects approached in a single file, all proceeded to slow down when directly over me/my home. I had a great view of the objects, they appeared to descend to about 500-1000 ft as they slowly flew over me. They were white translucent like that illuminated a skeleton on a plane, with wings that were outlined in bright red. I was amazed, excited and very curious. Once they passed they seamed to transform back into the orange/red spheres. This could be from the distant from me they covered in seconds. I immediately called my wife and she came out to witness the 3 spheres head North; its was when I was telling my wife where I first saw them we turned around and a 4th object was heading in the same direction as the first three. The 4th object repeated the same pattern as the others including ascending and slowing when passing us. My wife witnessed the same thing, I had only witnessed moments earlier, however she saw an outline of a head with arms navigating the object and looking down at us (looked to her it as if it was flying a plane). The figure turned it1s head and looked down towards us. All 4 objects headed north (towards the long Island sound) and disappeared, instantaneously.
