{dr}Observed row of lit, square windows moving beyond the trees below our position.

While descending Corral Creek Road from the higher altitude of Parrett Mountain late one evening, my daughter and I observed a row of six to eight windows floating beyond trees, below our twisty descent. When we reached an open area and observed the craft moving over our car, we exited the car to look up but the craft had disappeared. It was very dark and no visible shape could be determined. All that we really could determine was the row of windows.
