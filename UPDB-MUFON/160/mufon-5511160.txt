{cak} Seen glowing light in sky slowly moving then stop and go in another direction.

Standing outside restaurant with wife at about 10:30 PM and seen light glowing in the sky, It was like an orange flame almost. It was moving slow in one direction then stopped and started moving in another direction then disappeared. We were looking at the stars because it was a clear night and noticed it was very bright compared to the stars. Thought it might have been a helicopter but up to high to be one and it was slowing down and then going faster it did that at least two times and then it turned west and going up at the same time then completely vanished !!
