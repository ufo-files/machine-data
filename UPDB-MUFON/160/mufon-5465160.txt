{rjl} reddish light gaining altitude, same color tail at first followed by wake arc of light in front of object

Located in South East Florida - Spotted reddish/orange light first noticed above the treeline (but many miles away) moving upward at a 25 degree trajectory. Briefly noticed a tail of the same color which made me think it was a comet or an asteroid. It kept ascending smoothly at a constant speed and then produced an arc of light/illuminated cloud in front of the object, also the same color. Went behind a cloud and never saw it emerge.
