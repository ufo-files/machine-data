As soon as I looked up, I saw over ten orbs passing through a few kilometer break in the clouds. They were heading from NNW to SSE over Toronto, Ont. I ran into my work and grabbed the first person I could, and ran back out to see the last six disappear behind the clouds. My workmate saw the last two orbs. We stayed outside for about 5- 10 min and saw nothing else before heading in. I came out 5 min later to see another orb following the same path just coming into sight. I banged on the window of my work, and two workmates came out to observe the last orb. I stayed out for about 30 min after that, and saw nothing else. There were planes in the air, and were easily distinguishable from the orbs. The orbs were much higher, and faster. I have seen many satalights, meteors, comets, planes, helicopters, balloons, birds, space station, and even what I believe to have been a supernova, and this was unlike anything I have seen before. Both my workmates are as baffled as I, as to what we saw.
