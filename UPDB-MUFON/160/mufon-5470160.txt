{rjl} curved boomerang with at least a dozen bright round lights rapidly traversed a clearing sky

we were walking the dog and noticed over a dozen stars slowly moving to the west. Some dimmed out then regained brightness while moving. After we brought the dog in the house I went back outside to see their progress and a white blur which I thought at first was a small cloud but the few clouds overhead were moving to the east. It was a rapidly moving silent blurred white object moving from the east to the west. As it went directly overhead the shape was a curved boomerang with at least a dozen round white lights underneath it. I have witnessed other sightings years ago so I wasn't shocked but surprised and excited. The object did not appear to fade away with distance but rather simply disappear after it was about at a 45 degree angle from my sight in the west.
