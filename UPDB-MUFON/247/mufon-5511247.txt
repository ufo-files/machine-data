{dr}Small pulsating lights

About 10:30 EDT was on our dark patio in Springfield, Ohio, having a smoke. Far out and very low in the southweat sky I saw what I thought was a bright star. Then noticed it was pulsating. Then thought it was an airplane. It wasnt moving anywhere. Then thought it might be a helicopter. I got my binoculars to get a better look. Object was pulsating redish/greenish/yellowish colors.
Thinking my eyes were playing tricks, I asked my wife to come look. (She is skeptical of everything) However.....She did see what I saw and neiher of us could explain it. I came back out about 15 minutes later. It was still there and had not moved. Came back out around 11PM. It was gone.
