White bright light changing size, with transforming shape and blue, green, yellow and red circling lights, with more objects coming out.

What was seen as a bright pulsing white light, looked like a star or planet, but it pulsated changing size and shape with circling red, green, blue, and yellow lights? Then would become fuzzy and small, almost to the point of going out completely, then would become bright again as it moved slowly towards the southwest, then became too far or too small to see. all of this was recorded on video and photos were taken of the objects. Several times during the sighting, white glowing objects came out of the original object left and right side, then going back in. As the object was fading away in the distance a red-lighted object shot up at approximately an 11 o'clock angle and streaked across the sky and in the blink of an eye was gone, traveling at the speed of light it seemed and was gone. The red object could not be photographed because of the high rate of speed.
