{nd} Glowing spheres

Observed four spherical rose peach colored objects at 11:10 pm while I was letting dog outside.they appeared to my south just over tree line bright with no sound no wings no rotors and to fast for balloons. They continued north for about two thousand feet gaining altitude then made a 90 degree turn to west and very rapidly gain altitude and extreme speed and faded away. I know this wasnt man made cause they were right over me when they made their direction change
