Unexpected New Years lights near Bonney Lake, WA

I was visiting family in Bonney Lake, WA from CA over the NYE holiday into 2021. About 10 min after midnight on New Year’s day I stepped outside to watch the panorama of fireworks going off in the surrounding area. I had a full 180 degree view from west to east. After a few minutes I saw something I couldn’t explain. At first I thought I saw fireworks off to the west, but as I focused my attention I realized that there were (at least) 4 bright Orange orb-looking objects, clustered quite closely together moving toward me and up at the same time. They began to spread apart from each other on no discernible path. Slowly but steadily moving about. I searched my brain for answers...not fireworks, not a plane, not a drone, not a light show from the ground. Then one by one their lights faded out into the clouds. It was surreal. The whole sighting lasted about 30-60 seconds. I waited for about 20 min to see if anything would return, but it never did. I kick myself for not having my phone with me to record it.
