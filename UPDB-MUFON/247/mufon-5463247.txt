{cak} Cigar shape object witnessed by 4; couldn't see in binoculars only with eyes!

[I saw this in southern sweet water county while working in the oil field about 10:15 am while taking a coffee brake. This is just one of three I saw in about a 6 or 8 month time period. I would like to tell you about the others seen by me my wife and our children and two friends. I this same time period in 1967. (This narrative is incorrect for this case, per Witness during Interview it belongs to Case 97572).]

Witness narrative from interview:
Driving south on Irish Canyon Road toward Colorado, west of Kinney Rim in Wyoming, when the four of us observed just a block away, a low-flying silver cigar. Had no features. Moving slowly, about 500 feet off ground, about 55 feet long, 20 feet in diameter, maybe 15 feet high. No windows, no sound, no tail. Both ends of the object were rounded. We got out of the truck and got the binoculars out; we steadied our arms on the hood of the truck. When Al and I looked through the binoculars, we could not see the object! We put the binoculars down, and we could see it with our eyes. We are both experienced with binoculars and cannot explain this. The object moved slowly along Kinney Rim. We saw this object in Hiawatha Oil Fields.
