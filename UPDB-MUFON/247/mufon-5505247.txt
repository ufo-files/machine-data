{rjl} 2 starlike objects that weirdly faded out.

On January 20,2014 at (1810hrs) 6:10 pm pacific time I went outside to smoke a cigarette.It was a clear night and I could see stars.I looked N.E. approximately and noticed 2 star like objects about 40 degrees above the horizon.When I go out to smoke I always watch the sky and have seen ISS/Spaceshuttle and shooting stars many times.Immediately when noticing these 2 objects I realized they shouldnt be there.They appeared to be outside earths atmosphere and seemed to be like any of the brightest stars.Maybe a little bigger.The objects were pretty close together.That is why I noticed them as they looked like stars and there arent 2 in the sky like that as close to each other.They did not move at all in any direction.As I watched them they suddenly seemed to be dimming although in a very unusual way that is very hard to describe.It just looked very weird.Not that they were just dimming but the immediate area around them was being affected also.I witnessed these for approximately 1 min. with the last 15 seconds being the weird dimming part.I hope someone else witnessed this as I have never seen anything like it.
