{rjl Two sets of seven star-like orbs evenly spaced with larger space between sets

I awoke at around 5:30 a.m. My bedroom facing North and as I looked out my window. At this time of the morning, I'm watching the usual air-traffic and looking for meteors, etc. Then at about 5:50 a.m., the air-traffic seem to stop, I didn't think much of it at the time and continued to look for meteors. I then notice star-like orbs at what I would consider to being at below orbital to above orbital paths. As I watched out my window, I started to count them. There were seven in a straight line. Using my fingers to guess the spacing between the objects, to be about 1 inch from my point of view. The orbs appeared to glow colors that seemed to fluctuate from white, with orange-yellow and occasional reds. I have never seen anything quite like this, hence the reason I was taking a unique interest. I think they were coming from about 290 deg. (west) with a heading approximately 30 deg. (north-northeast). They were moving at a rather slow pace. Slower than the space station and satellites I have seen before. Then using my fingers again I'm guessing the second set orbs were about 2 inches of space and seven more spaced about 1 inch apart. All moving slowly on the same path. This lasted about 30-40 minutes. At about 5:20 a.m. air-traffic was back. Planes come in every 5-10 minutes. No air-traffic during my observations. I thought this to be odd. As I thought about this, I think these were on the same path I saw the space station fly-by a few months back.
