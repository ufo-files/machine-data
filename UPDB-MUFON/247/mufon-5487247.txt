I was out in my back yard around 2315 on 11/11/15 (Chandler, AZ) watching the Taurid meteor shower. I notice a helicopter coming in from the east at a high rate of speed. As it got close to my house the helicopter turned toward the NW and at that point I notice a black object in the sky in front of the helicopter. The object had no lights and made no sound what so ever. I lost sight of the object and helicopter behind trees in the neighborhood. I was not sure what the object was and I still don't. Having grown up around Air Force Bases, I have seen airplanes and helicopters flying at night with out lights. This object was neither. It made no sound at all. I am not sure what it was I saw in the sky tonight. I am very unsettled right now. Any other out there see this or know what happened.
