{rjl} Group of 15-20 came up over the hills very purposefully, flew much like a flock of birds

I was parked in the parking lot of my husband's work waiting for him to get out. It was a little before 4:00pm on Sunday Nov. 19. I noticed what I thought at the time was a flock of white birds fly up over the hills, across the river very purposefully. It was when I noticed that I could very easily identify other birds flying in the same area as birds that I realized they were not in fact birds. That is when I started to take pictures. These things were following each other but not in any type of solid formation that I could tell. Much like a flock of sparrows or crows.
