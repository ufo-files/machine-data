Silent, hovering, featureless white egg-shaped object, : strange luminosity, appeared to maneuver out of sight in response to filming

This video was taken on top of the dam in Doan Brook, Cleveland Heights, OH at 6:34 pm on August 20, 2021.

About 20 seconds before this video begins, I noticed a smooth, white egg-shaped object a couple hundred feet above us, slightly to the northeast. It was clearly visible, silently hovering in mid-air without any propellers, wings, or any indication of conventional craft. It also appeared to be bright (which could have just been the sun reflecting off of the object). 

Nathan confirmed what I was seeing. I zoomed in on the object and started recording. Nathan watches it zig-zag and speed away. The video captures that last moment of the encounter. You can see the object make two abrupt turns (one of those turns is in frame) before disappearing off to the right.
