Inverted pie-shaped craft vanishes in sunny, bright Arizona skies

It was a bright, clear, sunny day in Tempe, Arizona. I left my apartment riding my bicycle directly eastward just after crossing the street to ride on the sidewalk. In the east sky about 40 degrees up I noticed a silver inverted pie shaped craft gliding across the sky for about 15 seconds, then I went under a tree which obscured my veiw for about 2 seconds. After I emerged from my blocked sight, the craft was completely gone. Disappeared. The object was about the size of the moon in the sky. I happened to notice this easily because I live in the flight path of Sky Harbor Airport in Phoenix. The craft was flying north to south, which I1ve never really seen before. The planes usually fly east to west. The craft might have been in Phoenix flying space going in the wrong direction
