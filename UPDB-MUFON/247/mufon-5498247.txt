{dr}Flashing white light near commercial airline flight path, that flashed on and off for a few seconds before disappearing

It was a little after 9:30 pm. I had just finished putting my kids to bed. I was in my Kitchen rinsing out a glass to pour a soda for my wife. I looked up and out of the Kitchen window that is directly behind the sink. I often do this to watch the commercial flights come in while I am doing dishes. As I looked up I first noticed a plane with its typical flashing lights pass by on the usual eastward flight path, coming in for a landing at the Ft. Lauderdale Intl Airport. I have seen this many, many times before. Then just as the plane went out of sight behind the trees, a light blinked on in the west and caught my eye. I watched it flash 3-4 more times, with about a one second interval, give or take few tenths, although it was definitely the same interval between each blink/flash. It was almost like a light on a plane or helicopter being controlled digitally. I even thought at first that it was a plane on the westward approach, which is further out in the distance than the eastward approach, but the light appeared to be in the middle somewhere, where no planes should be. It also seemed too high in elevation to be a helicopter. I watched that same point in the sky for at least 30 more seconds after the last blink before taking my eyes away. It never reappeared there or anywhere in sight. I immediately checked my phone and noted the time to be 9:41 eastern standard time. I then ran out to the back patio without even pouring the drink, to tell my wife what I had just witnessed. I know it's not much. I have no photo evidence or other witnesses to back me up, but I am sure of what I saw. Thank you for everything do. I hope to hear back.
