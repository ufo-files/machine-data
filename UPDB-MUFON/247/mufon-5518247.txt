bright light flys across sky

our shift change at work starts at 4:30 am, 5 of us are standing outside, around 4:45 am, I spotted an unusual light in upper western sky and it made its way to the south eastern horizon. A solid white light, bright, no tail, no blinking lights, 10 times faster than any jet I have ever seen, yet slower than any meteor ( and no tail). As it was coming towards us and was going away from us, the light nvr changed brightness or waivered. ( not a headlight on a plane~ if it was leaving)( no blinking lights)(extreme speed to cross the sky ), 5 witnesss.."we have never seen this king of thing before!" ~JS
We were in Springhill KS. and the object just faded away into distance  < extra info.
