On the night in question I was arriving home from work at my usual time (approx. 10:20 PM). Just as was getting out of my car I saw a bright, white light with four smaller, red lights below the white light. The sound was a soft whirring kind of sound but it seemed very muted, too soft, or quiet. for as large and close as the thing seemed to be. It was about 100 feet from me, about 25 feet above the ground, and was about the size of a large SUV. It was either round or oval shaped, it was hard to see beyond the bright lights. After about a minute, the sound be came slightly deeper and faster, and the thing just went straight up, not super fast, but it took maybe 5 seconds to go out of sight, straight up.
