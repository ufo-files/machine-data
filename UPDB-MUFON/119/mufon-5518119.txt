Massive Blue Green Pulse of Light in the Sky disrupted Power

Just after 730pm on 16th June 2012, I walked out my back yard as my dog was howling and I go to the 1st step and noticed an air plane flying from the south east heading toward the north west toward the airport. It travelled toward the horizon then there was a HUGE flash of light that filled the entire sky that was really strange. It was an unusual blue greeny color - almost started like a radiation outward of energy like an expanding light that went in all directions... i was shocked/awed by what i saw and my heart was racing... its like something from twilight zone or the movies... it was quick but BLOODY ENORMOUS! Whatever it was it cause the power in my house to go out and come back on... I have one witness to the affects it had on the power...(she was inside) i couldnt help but think immediately the idea of an EM pulse but It wasn't an explosion as there was no sound at all and no wind. It definately WAS NOT lightning - it expanded outwards... the plane was still visible in the distance... no idea what is was other than unidentified
