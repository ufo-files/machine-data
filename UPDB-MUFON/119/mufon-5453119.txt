Round disc shaped solid object in same position in sky each evening for 3 months. No movement or sound

Object hovers in western sky every night for over 2 months. It is round and disk shaped and well lighted. To the naked eye it appears to be a bright star or orbiting object remaining in the same position in the sky every night ; does not move or emit sound. At times it has been observed to emit downwards small glowing red balls that seem to burn out. Position does not match up with any known star or planet. After photographing and enlarging photo it appears as white disk shaped object. 

I am a retired airline pilot who has been flying for over 50 years and this is no craft or phenomenon that I am familiar with. I have many photos I would like to share with a trained investigator. Some are raw data and some have been enlarged. They are quite remarkable and warrant a thorough investigation. Thank you and feel free to contact me at any time.
