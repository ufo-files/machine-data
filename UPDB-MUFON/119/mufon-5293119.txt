Unusual craft in night sky with odd lighting and strobes.

I was looking for the NEOWise comet from my back porch. I noticed a bright light in the west sky just under the big dipper moving east at the time. The thing the struck me odd was the bright light just vanished. Then after a few seconds the object blinked a dimmer light as it moved through the sky. The object was high in the sky it would go dark but unlike a plane their were no fixed lights, it was completely dark. Then it would blink a few blinks every 9 to 11 seconds with no certain cadence. At times the bright light would appear and blink fast for a few seconds before it went dark again. It veered to the NE before I lost it behind the trees of my neighborhood. Thank you.
