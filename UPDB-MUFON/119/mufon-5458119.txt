Red-orange orb seen moving W to NW slowly. Orb went behind a tree and siappeared

Saturday, 22nd at 9:20 pm 2015, My mother and I witnessed a singular, orange orb that was easily 8-10 times larger than a star would be in the sky. We were looking North-West and the object was traveling North-East. It passed behind a large tree and then disappeared. The object was seen for at least 45-60 seconds, longer than a shooting star or object burning up in the atmosphere would last. It stayed at a horizontal trajectory and was moving at a slow but steady pace and made no sound. It was 1/2 way between the horizon and straight up. We ruled out airplanes because it did not have aircraft marker lights and it was not a balloon or drone because of the odd shape and fuzzy or distorted edges. I contacted National Defence to just send them a "hey, this is what we saw" as well as Chex News. There are plenty of the exact same objects on Youtube, but they are all in groups. I wonder what it was?!
