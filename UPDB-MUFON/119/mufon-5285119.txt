UFO hovering above Seymour, IN

As we were driving south into Seymour on Hwy 11 we looked to the southwest and noticed an object in the sky that appeared to be hovering and flashing multicolored lights. This is the 3rd or 4th time we have seen this same thing within the past month. We haven't seen it for a couple weeks though. We drove into town and tried to get closer so that we could record it. We drove west on Brown St behind the hospital and stopped near the ambulance building as there weren't any trees or lights to obscure the camera. We recorded the UFO for approximately 54 seconds. It appeared to be a metal cylindrical aircraft with flashing lights that changed colors. We went home and it was still in that same place when we entered our home.
