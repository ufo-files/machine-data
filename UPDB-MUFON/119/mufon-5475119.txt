Large circle with center light and light around rim, blocked out stars from view as it passed over

On the 27th of May 2017 i witnessed a large circular object moving left to right at Amiens range on CFB Meaford during a machine gun shoot. The object was very large and completely black except for that it had a bright white light in the center as well as a light that consistently spun around what appeared to be the rim of the object. Imagine looking at the face of a kitchen plate, that is the shape the object appeared to be. What convinces me that a physical object was there is that as it moved and passed infront of stars they would be gone from my sight until the object had passed over. I observed it for about 5 minutes and then it disappeared.
