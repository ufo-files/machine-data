{jpg} Triangular dark object with orange/yellowish lights on each corner

April 29, 2019 at approximately 9:15 pm
I was walking a short distance back to my house in Ventura when I looked up and saw a triangular (equilateral in shape) object flying directly above me, no more than 500ft above ground. There were three glowing lights at each point of the object that were constant and unwavering. The object was emitting absolutely no noise and moving faster than any plane possible could especially without emitting any noise. I’m still having a difficult time processing and making sense of exactly what I saw.
