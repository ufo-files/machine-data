WHILE VISITING A FRIEND I SAW SOME CHEM TRAILS AND TOOK PICTURES OF THEM. AFTER LOOKING AT THE PICTURES I SAW AN ANGEL LIKE CRAFT IN THEM.

AT THE SAME LOCATION I ALSO SAW "COP LIGHTS" IN THE SKY, THEY LOOKED LIKE TWO STARS BUT THEY MOVED INDEPENDENTLY OF EACH OTHER AND IN DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS.
THE LIGHTS WERE NOT LIKE PULSARS, FADING BRIGHTER TO DIMMER, BUT BRIGHT. THE COLORS WERE WHITE, RED AND GREEN IN A SEQUENCE WITH THE BLUE LIGHT COMING ON EVERY SO OFTEN. I WATCHED THEM EVERY NIGHT FOR TWO MONTHS, FROM ABOUT 9 PM TO DAWN. THEY APPEARED TO BE ABOUT I/2 MILE AWAY. THEY WERE HELICOPTER SIZE AND MADE NO NOISE. THEY HOVERED, AND ZIG-ZAGGED AROUND. THEY LOOKED LIKE A 7'CASKET WITH A TWO FOOT RING AROUND IT. THEY APPEARED TO BE ABOUT 3 FEET THICK. THEY WERE OBSERVED NEAR HOSIER NATIONAL FOREST. THIS IS A VERY RURAL AREA. 1/2 MILE FROM THE RIVER. THESE SIGHTINGS STARTED IN JUNE AND CONTINUE TO APPEAR.
