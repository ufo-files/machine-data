{jpg} Egg shaped object with no wings or rotors

I was traveling northbound on Hwy 27 at I4 at a slow roll due to traffic. I observed an egg shaped white object flying westbound above I4 cross over Hwy 27 at approximately 400 agl. The object did not have a tail or rotors. Once the object crossed over Hwy 27 it changed course to Northwest and accelerated at a high rate of speed. When it changed direction I could see it did not have any wings either. It only took it a few seconds to be out of eyeshot. I am an agency drone pilot that works this area and was working at the time. Clear day with broken clouds at 5,000 feet.
