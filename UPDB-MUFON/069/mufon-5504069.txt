I have seen alot of aircraft and this would have taken 100 airliners to span that amount of space at that altitude. it continued north out of site. I ran round to the other side of the house and watched it continue north.The shape of the grill stood out between the lights though it was the only real part that did not get blurred by the strange glowing lights. No pulsing or blinking occurred. No sounds I have 10 years military service and spent years on an aircraft carrier.I have seen alot of aircraft but never this sized which really was freaking amazing.
