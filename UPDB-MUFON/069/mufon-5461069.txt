About 130 am I wawas smoking and watching the sky as usual and noticed a bright star like object near Polaris. Assoon as I noticed it, i realized how slow it was movingand it almost immediately dimmed out to almost invisible. It appeared to have another very dim light following as if it was being towed. It was moving north to south. Minutes later I witnessed what looked to be a fireball that was led colored, like man made. Itwas very brief and moved south to north and just before dissipating, abruptly changed its direction to the west. I started to record after the first object but unfortunately it didnt show up in the video because it was too fast or not bright enough. I do have the video still.
