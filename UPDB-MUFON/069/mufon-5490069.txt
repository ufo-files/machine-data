{rjl} Saw light in space moving at speeds and turns not possible by human craft

Was on duty at night saw a light moving left to right at in the blink of an eye. I am talking at least 100 miles in less than 3 seconds. The ship did not turn but went straight up then, it turned again went back to the left at least 150 to 200 miles. This time it dropped in altitude in a flash. I was a SSG. in the US ARMY at the time and was told by officers on duty that night that I was not to talk about what was seen that night for threat of court martial. But now 30 years later what can they do to me.
