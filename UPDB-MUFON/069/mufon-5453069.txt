Four diamond-shaped white objects moving at high speed over Sherwood Park, Animated video by witness added by FI

Four white or light-grey colored diamond or square objects moving at an extremely high rate of speed. They were traveling in a diamond formation moving from the south to north-north-east. Their diamond formation rotated around one another.
At one point, the lead object performed a maneuver which it appeared to flip or barrel roll. The other objects adjusted course to follow, but they did not roll.

Due to the wind on this evening, it's unlikely there were small 'toy' drones flying, especially at the rate of speed these object were moving. 

The objects did not emit any sounds we could hear, nor did they have any lighting.
