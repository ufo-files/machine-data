{rjl} I saw upside down flying baseball cap, with two small bumps undercarriage.

On 4 June 2019, at approx 7:08 am CST, i was driving to work thru downtown okc, using highway I-235. It had been raining and the clouds were low. Apprximately halfway thru downtown (close to bricktown). For some unusualy reason, i glanced up at the clouds to my right, and high in the sky between two large rain clouds, i spotted what seemed like an "upside down, flying baseball cap".  It was generally darker in color than surrounding rain clouds. It has two protrusions at bottom (when I think of it now, i think of binoculars extension bump protruions) and slightly situated to the back.  I work for the airforce, and I could not explain a type of plane that fly that low in the rain environment. I thought maybe they are practicing for an air show which was to happen the upcoming weekend. The more i thought about what i had seen, the more i felt compelled to report an object whose shape i could not identify. I later went to the NOAA weather report and screen shot the rain clouds in the area at the time. Also, i went to flight tracker software and pics what kinds of aircraft were flying in area. There were several aircraft manuevering in the area, but at much higher elevations to get away from the storm clouds. this object i saw was going right thru/above them like it was not afraid of the weather. All Okies are very suspicious of rain clouds, and our pilots would also avoid them, cause we live in tornado alley. If i were to guess, the ufo looked like in the movie Dune, the Harkonnen flagship.
