{tjd} Multi-white lights on object that appears nightly. It has appeared to be shaped differently from time to time.

Approximately at 6:30 pm each night an object appears and hovers above the horizon at an extremely high altitude for long periods of time. While it descends to an approximately 5-7 miles up in the sky, it begins to take on a rotating hover. While looking at it through binoculars, it's exterior is covered with bright while lights. While the object is rotating the lights get brighter and then dimmer continuously. Even while driving in the direction of the object, it always remains to appear very far away. It continues to present itself with regularity. What appears to be red orbs occasionally appear at the perimeter of the object. The bight, multi-white lit object also seems to shine a broader, white beam down toward the earth.
