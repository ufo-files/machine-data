3 objects flying in a straight line, disappearing into cloud cover

Looking up, I and a family member observed 3 objects, each one a few minutes apart. One flew into a cloud bank, one flew under the bank, and one flew way over it. Two of them were round in appearance and silver, one was oblong and silver and much larger. I could not make out features since they were so high up, and all were brightly reflecting the sun.

There was also what I believe to be a plane flying across the flight path of the objects in between appearances. I say “believe to be” since I couldn’t make out any wings and it was as noiseless as the others.

Notable oddities: for about 2/3 of the sighting there was no noise at all, except distant cars. Strange for metro NYC. Also, and this is really weird, right before the sighting the family member with me was staring up at the sky and joked about seeing a ufo. They didn’t believe me when I initially said I saw something.
