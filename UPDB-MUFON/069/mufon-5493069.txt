{cak} 5 glowing fireballs like spread around above the horizon.

My family and I were heading north on I-5 in national city CA when my wife ask, what was that? I saw about five glowing balls in between orange and yellow. I saw them for only 3 sec because I was driving and the buildings and trees were getting in the way but I'm sure this were no helicopters like she thought and no stars. From what I saw it seem they were in a Stand still and spread out. They were at least 50 miles away and maybe half a mile away from each other. I wish I could have been in a place were I could have seen them better and observe them longer.
