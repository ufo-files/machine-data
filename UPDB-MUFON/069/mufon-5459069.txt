{rjl} 12-15 orange variable lights moving slowly N to S over a 15 minute period

From approximately 9:40-10pm EST 12-15 lights were seen by the two of us over a 10-minute period. Usually 2 at a time, but as many as 5 at once. They appeared as bright orange glowing point that would wax and wane in brightness, at times bright enough that they had a vague star-like pointed shape, much brighter than Mars at its brightest. All were moving in the same general direction from N to SW. After perhaps a minute each would slowly fade, turn to a dull white point, and disappear completely. The sky was clear and cloudless, several planes high up moving in different directions with blinking red lights. The objects appeared to be much closer than the planes. All lights appeared to be at the same distance from the ground, moving horizontally. No sound was heard at any time. 

The first two lights caught our eye due to their brilliance. We thought they were an approaching plane with lights at each of its wingtips, save for the fact that the lights were bright orange and randomly flickering. This large 'plane' began banking as one light moved higher and the other lower, then back, then twisting again until one was nearly on top relative to the other. Although they stayed at the same relative distance to each other subtle differences in speed and distance could be seen; one would move slower momentarily and veer away, then return to matching the speed and direction of the other, and so on to the point that it was obvious that they were separate objects. After these first two, others appeared and moved slowly across the sky in the same manner.

4 photographs and 2 videos were taken, the first 2:47 and the second 1:09 in duration.
