{cak} on-going light anomalies

For 2 years we've seen on-going, erratic lights on Indian Hills and Mt. Evans. They defy every attempt to debunk as headlights or night construction. They hit an all-time crescendo during the lunar eclipse. They've moved down from the highest peaks of Mt. Evans into the lower range of Indian Hills. They are easily visible from Hampden and Colorado Blvd. in SE Denver from sundown to sunrise. Perhaps they are there all day but not visible.
