{rjl} A formation of dull white objects over Natrona County, WY

I noticed what looked to be a group of geese flying in a V formation directly north of my position. The more I watched this group, 8-10 objects, the more I realized that they were'nt geese. This group flew in a westerly direction, and while the group were moving, the objects would "bounce" from one position to another, changing the shape of the formation from a V, to a triangle, to a somewhat cross shape, to an irregular circle. I observed the group for approximately a minute until it was out of sight. I took 2 pics of the group. At first I didn't think I caught the group, but when zooming in, you can see it.
