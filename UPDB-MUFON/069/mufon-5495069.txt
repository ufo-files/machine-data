{dr}Large green light flew across sky. Gone in a second.

I was driving home, and as I was driving, a large green circular object flew across the sky. It was gone in a second. It flew over me directly over me coming from the west, and went in a straight line east. Immediately after, I searched "green light" on Twitter, and a handful of other people said they saw the same exact thing. These witnesses were from PA, New Jersey, and Massachusetts.
