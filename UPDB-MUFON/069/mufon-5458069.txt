{cak} Watched a large, indiscernible object go directly over our head from north to south, then it abruptly turned ninety degrees to the west

My wife and I were in the backyard as we do almost every evening hoping to catch a shooting star. She thought she saw one then realized it was something oddly different and it was still moving. She alerted me and I picked it up as it moved almost directly over our heads. It moved from north of us to the south for several miles before abruptly turning due west. It traveled west for a brief moment then it was just gone. The object had no discernible edge but basically rectangle. sort of, kind of. It looked like it was maybe segmented at times in its movement but confusing at the same time. It appeared to shimmer and move in a wave like pattern at the same time, almost as if it was cloaked in some fashion. We don’t know how else to explain it.
