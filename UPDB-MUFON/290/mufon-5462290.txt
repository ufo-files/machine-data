{rjl} sudden very bright light in woods appears, then shoots straight up very very fast!

September of 1966; the country side North of Trenton, NJ 
along side the Delaware at Washington's Crossing State Park
 It was about 8:00 PM on a clear and moonless night as I drove towards the Delaware River past the Washington's Crossing State Park entrance. Suddenly, I was startled by a brilliant white oval shaped light (maybe 50' diameter & apparently on the ground)that lit up the woods. Next, all the cars that were in the park almost simultaneously started turning on their lights and pealing gravel - headed for the entrance as I drove past. (This was the local "Lover's Lane"). The light in the woods was 1000 times brighter than their headlights! I was nearing the stop sign and my attention was diverted to the panicked drivers near me. All of a sudden the countryside lit-up with a cold grey light. I could see for miles around from this light which seemed to shoot up at an incredible speed and then just wink out as it distanced itself from the ground. The speed was beyond anything I've ever seen. I pulled off onto the shoulder as several cars from the parking lot sped past. I next pulled up to one of these cars at the stop sign and rolled down my window to ask the couple inside what was going on? The guy answered excitedly "Did you see it...did you see it?" He continued "we were just sitting there in the dark when all of a sudden the woods lit up so bright! Whatever it was must have been there the whole time! It just took off straight up!" The couple just zoomed away at that point.
 I would love to hear from any of those fellow witnesses to share our thoughts!
