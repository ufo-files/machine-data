I saw bright orange lights ascending and eventually fading out.

I was watering the plants as I normally do in the afteroon, when I looked up at the sky to see the stars when I noticed that to the east of me there was a relatively bright cluster of yellow-orange lights ascending. At first they were close together but eventually branched out, but remained geared in the same direction. When one would get a certain height it would fade out, as did those after it. Not quite sure what to make of them honestly. Im used to seeing lights, but not in that direction. Normally they are towards the north by where Nellie AFB is, but we are all used to those here.
