Bright shiny object

On my way to drop my daughter off at school something bright in the sky to my left caught my eye. I looked up to see a bright, somewhat slow, moving object that some would think was a star, if it had been dark outside. Others may say a plane. I’ve never seen a plane that bright and possibly shiny that doesn’t make a sound or leave a trail in the daylight. We watched it move at a slower speed than an airplane then just disappear.
