Six starlike points moving in a straight line NW to SE

Gazed up to the eastern sky to see 6 starlike points of light moving slowly toward the SE sky. They were equally spaced and were in a straight line pattern. They were first seen just above the constellation Bootes. Upon moving SE they slowly faded out from view. I had them in view for about 3 minutes. In my opinion they were either 6 individual objects moving together equally spaced or were part of a very large object that was invisible. I have been observing the sky for most of my 71 years and have never seen anything like this. My education includes a B.A. degree in Astronomy.
