{cak} White object coming across nite sky while 5 helicopters observed its movement

Setting in living room approximately 11:00 pm, when heard helicopters hovering outside. I went outside and observed 5 helicopters in the sky, 3 of the helicopters very just hovering while the other two were moving around. These were not police helicopters because they had no spot light on as if looking for someone. The three that were hovering were very dim lighted, no flashing light at all while the other two had red blinking lights. The three were spaced out several hundred yards apart not moving. These copters were not real, real loud despite the fact that there were five of them. I waited to see what was going on because I never witnessed copters like this just hovering, while the other two seem to be looking for something in the sky not using spotlights. Then I seen a white light coming from the SE direction. This white light started coming toward the copters, the three idled copters seem to finally start to moved away from the object as it came by, one copter that was over my head that was hovering kind of started moving around the object as it was passing (I have this on video on my phone). The object continued to move, then copters backed off, it went over my house almost in the same flight pattern as the jets fly, but then made a u-turn and started heading back the same way it came except in a NE direction. Then it disappeared. By the. The five helicopters had left.
