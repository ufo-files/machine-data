I viewed a shiny silver cigar-shaped object floating slowly in the sky with no wings

At about 6 am I was taking my dog out and I observed a shiny silver cigar-shaped object low in the sky just above the trees. It seemed to be reflecting the sun. I found it strange that it was so low in the sky and appeared to have no wings and making no noise. I kept watching it in amazement and it seemed to be hovering and floating and not flying. It was moving slowly southeast. I live near a military base and police training facility and I've seen other unexplained aerial phenomena in this area that I have never reported.
