{cak} Black elliptical object in the East, flying North to Northeast

At approximately 2:30pm on 8/16/17, I was sitting in my vehicle and looking out of the sunroof at a cloud, when a black object crossed my site. It was flying East of St. Peters, from South to North. I followed it for several minutes as it flew what appeared to be just below the clouds and it never seemed to enter the cloud cover. Just before I lost sight of the object, it looked like it made a slight directional change to the Northeast. 

I was careful to observe that there were no visible wings or tails out of the object and it emitted no sound or trails.
