Green Fireball sighted over residential area of North Vancouver,BC

Sighting Location: North Vancouver - Latitude: 49.3196, Longitude: -123.068 4911 1911 1111 North, 12311 411 511 West
North Vancouver Altitude 105 m
344 ft 

Observation: I was standing on the south side of West 4th St almost at the corner(2blocks in) of Lonsdale Ave and West 4th North Vancouver,BC talking to a friend/colleague when I observed at exactly 8:57pm(20:57) PST a green object moving through the clouds heading in a North Westerly direction. As the object passed over my head, it appeared to be decending and/or losing altitude,rate of speed unknown.

First thought/impression was a flare then a unique meteor(see Wikipedia -green Fireballs)

Peripheral vision made me look up at the object.

Green ball of light,comet like movement with a trail behind it similar to a comet,consistent and maintained speed.

Object appeared to be moving in an specific flight path or trajectory similar to my close encounter sighting over house in Mississauga - Dec 31 2002 at 11:31pm.

My feelings and actions were obviously "What the hell is that" I tried to get my friend/colleague to look up but he thought I was playing some kind of joke on him.

I lost track of the object as it decended over an apartment building.
