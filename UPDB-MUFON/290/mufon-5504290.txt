{cak} Standing on roof top midtown

My friend had a apartment on the top floor of the building. That day a few friends were having a blue fish roast on the roof. There were no drugs involved and any beer was gone when I arrived. The building which is situated right next to 46 street park you can see west across the Hudson River and east toward the east side of manhattan. The 8 to ten people who were there were looking toward the west side of the roof and you can see planes lights landig and taking off from that vantage point. Some were saying the lights of the planes were ufos. I knew they weren't and walked toward the other side of the roof. I looked up toward the Empire State Building and above me was a huge triangle shaped ship UFO I don't know. I stared at it for ten seconds I think and then I had to turn Away because I felt someone on that UFO was looking at me. I yelled at the other people on the roof to look in the same direction but no one turned around. When I looked up Again it was gone. No noise just gone. Years later when I asked my friend About it he told me I never spoke about it. I can tell you I have been in law enforcement for nearly 29 years and I have heard stories from other officers who have had their own experiences. I'm not a fraud I know what I saw and that's my story.
