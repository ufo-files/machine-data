It was a nice summer night in 1991.My father, my friend and I were at the balcony and we were watching the stars. On the left side of the building where was our flat there was a hill about 300 metars far from us. We noticed that something bright and shiny was howering over the hill and few minutes ago it wasn't there. At first we thought it must be a big star. We all were surprised by its size. We were watching it about 2 minutes and at the moment when I pointed binoculars toward it,the object was suddenly accelerated unimaginable speed that we couldn't even seen it anymore. The object just disappeared from our sight. We all was so surprised with what we saw and my father was much more surprised when he checked the time. It seemed to us like everithyng happened in just 2 minutes but in fact it was one hour that we missed.The strangest thing was that we lost one hour of time. Because of that I think it hasn't been a military aircraft but something else, something not from this world.
I've also had two more srange encounters but decided to report this one becouse of the lost time.
