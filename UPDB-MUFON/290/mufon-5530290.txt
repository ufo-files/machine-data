Big bright light moving then stopping several times heading south east

Went outside the house with my two year old son at about 8 pm it was dark but the sun was still setting , I wanted to move the car across the street for the street sweeper in the morning and my son loves sitting shotgun when i have to move the cars so as i did a lap around the block i noticed a neighbor from down the street at the edge of her driveway looking up at something with binoculars, as i was driving i tried leaning forward and looking up but couldn't see what she was looking at. When I parked the car and got out with my son I looked up and there was a Big white light at first I thought it was a star but it was moving then my son was saying plane plane but it had no sound and didn't have the colored flashing lights. As I stood there and watched amazed it suddenly stopped for like 30 seconds then began moving again it did that two more times like it knew I was watching it. I felt nervous then excited and ran in the house to tell my father in law and without skipping a beat ran back out and it was gone. My wife thinks I am Crazy but I have never seen anything like that before in my life.
