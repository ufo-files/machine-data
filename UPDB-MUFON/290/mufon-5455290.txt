{rjl} Family saw UFO coming home from grocery shopping

Family was driving down Arlington Road in Brookville Ohio and saw a dark object no higher than the power lines that were on the east side of the road. My dad was driving and I was sitting in the back behind him. I asked him what that was(at that point everyone in the car was looking.) and I rolled my window down to listen to it. There wasn't any sound even though it was hovering. We stopped and when we did it flew across the road slightly behind us so the focus turned to the west side of the road and the open field. My dad sped off and to the stop sign on the corner of Arlington and Westbrook Road., He turned right(west) and we were able to still see it moving across the field until it came to a patch of woods and hovered above the woods and we saw it dropping blue and red lighted objects into the woods. In the meantime my dad was flying down Westbrook trying to get a better view and to try to get down to the road we could turn right on. As we approached the first road to the right the UFO took off and was gone in an instant. This event happened when I was 11 or 12 in 1973 or 1974
