{dr}UFO seen hovering over Chatanooga, TN in 1973

When I was almost 11 y/o family and I were traveling from Missouri to Florida in Aug of 1973. We were on the interstate coming down towards where the interstate loops around a kind of bowl/basin area that is Chattanooga. It was about dusk, kind of dark but not all the way dark yet. When we curved around a mountain and headed down we saw a round disc-shaped object with lights going around it hovering over that basin area. Other people were already there stopped along side of the road and were watching it. We stopped at the area that would have been the top (North side) of the basin. At first I thought it was one of those airplanes that used to have messages scrolling on them but when we stopped I saw that was not the case. The UFO was on the east side of the basin and moved silently towards the west side. It then moved back toward the east side. I remember it hovered on the east side for a while, but do not recall if that was before it moved to the west side or after. After about 10 minutes or more the UFO took off, kind of slowly but not as slow as it had be going over the basin, over the south mountains. We got back in our car and my mom drove on down around the basin. I kept an eye on the UFO and saw that it hovered over the mountain for a short time, but then bam, it took off in a westerly direction and up real fast and was gone. Shortly after that we stopped for the night because it was dark. We checked all the news stations to see if there were any reports of it since so many people saw it, but nothing was reported. I put below that there were 10 witnesses. I really don't remember how many people were stopped at the side of the road and then there would have been others traveling on the interstate that could have seen it and did not stop. There were four of us that saw it.
