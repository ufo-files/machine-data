Saw 5 glowing objects in the sky on Sunday September 4 at 8:50pm EST

It was 8:50pm Sunday September 4, 2011. While waiting for a friend I was meeting for dinner, I was looking up at the sky when I saw what I thought to be planes.....but 5 so close together. My first thought was....how can air traffic control be choreographing this cluster. Then I recognized the movement........the larger being still......a smaller object moving right slowly....then fast...then changing direction. the 3 smallest were close together....moving up and down right and left.....the medium sized one, at a still moment....vanished.....the 3 smaller ones vanished one by one....the larger became more brilliant...like it was ablaze.....then slowly disappeared. It was a remarkable experience. When my friend finally arrived, he commented that I was glowing and my eyes were sparkling. I had to share the excitement of what I saw. I was actually tingling all over. I waited to see the news the next day.....but nothing. I contacted a friend who led me to this sight. I would love to know what I saw. Next step is to learn how to work the video app on my phone.
