Spade shaped cut in half with black glow appeared in NW sky, left trail from SE to NW. Appeared then dissapeared.

On 6.6.21 at appx.1:15 am, my family and I were at Brietbeck Park in Oswego stargazing, as it was a clear night. We had seen several small lights traveling the sky that we were unable to identify. Two of our family saw a giant light trail, described by my father as "looking like a drawing of the tail of a comet" as they were looking at the light trail, I saw the craft. The craft was the shape of a spade or sideways heart. There was a line in darker somehow glowing black down the center with two dull, grey copper colored wings. It was massive, larger than a basketball, yet still very far away. I was able to see that there were markings of some sort on the wings.
