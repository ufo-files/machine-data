Bright flying light,

I was sitting on the Shore of Lake Ontario, on May 24th around 2;30am. I was looking out at the sky, and noticed a really bright light. At first I thought it was an airplane, but then noticed its flight pattern was not normal for a plane. 
The UFO, started flying normally, but then flew up and got dimmer as it got higher, made a route change to the South West, and vanished. As I was trying to figure out what happened I remembered a few cases in where the same flight pattern was noticed.
