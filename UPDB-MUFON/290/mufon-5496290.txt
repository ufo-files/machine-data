{dr}2 bright white orbs followed us. One appeared red orange streak plummeted to the ground.

My wife and I were coming home from McDonalds with dinner around 5:55 PM and my wife saw the bright white circle in the sky. We had seen one a couple weeks prior and it seemed to follow us home. There were 2 in the sky this time one followed us and hovered above the tree line and stayed there. My mother and stepson saw it this time too because I ran in the house to get my binoculars. My stepson noticed it was diamond shaped, his eyesight is better than mine. It was very bright but looked like a circle without the binoculars. Before we got home one of the bright white ones was on my left side, driver side of the car. My wife and I were watching and a reddish orange one appeared out of nowhere and appeared to crash to the ground at high speed. After we all watched the hovering one for around 30 minutes ish we went in and had dinner and watched a movie. We decided to go drive towards the one that crashed and the hovering white one was gone. We also couldn't trace exactly where the other one went down. It was exciting and scary at the same time. We will continue to watch for them. Unfortunately my camera was not charged but I will have it on stand by now.
