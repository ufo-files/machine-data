Boomerang or triangle shape UFO in Brazil 2001

Huge boomerang or triangle shape UFO, it was a summer night, I was in the pool looking to the skies, I saw it and tried to call my cousin to come with the camera, he didn't listen to me, I kept watching it till I had no sight anymore. It was flying over my grandma's house, SW direction, much slower and bigger than any airplane I've seen. Only after a few seconds I noticed that there was very little lights under that thing. I was scared, and didn't mention that to anyone till 10 years after when I left my religiosity.
