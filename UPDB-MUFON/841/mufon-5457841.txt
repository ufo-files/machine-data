{tjd} 2 'horse shoe' shaped crafts, 'floating' west to east, upside down 'u' shaped, black on one side, reflective silver on other and would alternate

Two Upside down 'U' shaped crafts, similar to a horse shoe shape. My husband, son, and I were sitting outside when we noticed, what we thought was a balloon at first, floating from the tree line. As it got closer we realized it was bigger then a balloon, about the size of an adult person. He thought it was someone in a glider suit. The object seemed to alternate from a black to reflective silver surface. It was a horizontal upside down 'U' and the 'legs' of the 'U' would sort of 'kick' changing from the black to silver, as if you were in a pool and pedaling slowly to stay afloat. We then noticed a 2nd one, following the same exact flight path. We live by 2 airports, usually a lot of air traffic, when objects flew through, we realized, there were no planes in the air in any direction which is unusual.
