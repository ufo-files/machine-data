Flashing lights over head

I was doing CE5 and have a contact event. I first seen a flash of light in the south east and then right above that and then another above me. I don’t know if there was 2 or it was the same one coming back but it appeared then a 4th time and flashed right above my location. It materialised, flashed, then disappeared again on all 4 flashes. I managed to record a video of 2 other flashes but missed the other two. It made no sound it was very high up and way brighter than any star in the sky - it was extremely bight when it flared up. It flashed every 2-3 mins I guess but not in any order. Just randomly. I took a 1080p video and a few pictures and I just added contrast and a bit of brightness.
