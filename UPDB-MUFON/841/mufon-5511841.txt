For 22 years I have not told a soul what I am about to write. I am a member of an intergalactic team whose job it is to rescue beings from different dangers. We wear what is called battlesuits which totally encompass our bodies. The suits are very technologically advanced drawing on technology from multiple races. They can fly turn invisible, move through solid objects, have shields and weapons, and can teleport via wormholes. They can translate any alien language. They upgrade the suits technology periodically. My first mission was in 1991. We had a planet where a comet crashed carrying a plague. We could not teleport and our suits would not protect us from this disease which was transmitted by touch.We had to rescue "children" from this plague region. The entire rest of my team died, I was the only survivor but I accomplished the mission. My second mission, the bad guy aliens were so slow you could walk around their shots. I went from the hardest mission to the easiest. I am responsible for saving 16 planets from destruction. In 2001 I was named leader of my team. We work off of a spaceship or from our heads up display inside our suit. In 1995 an alien assasin tried to kill me.I am telling all this because I want to talk about, why me. I have never smoke, drank, done drugs, cussed, and I go the speed limit. In other words, I can do no evil. I want you to realize the power of good. We only live a few short years. I was given a battlesuit based on my morals.I would not change my life.
