I was laying on sofa in front of big bay window with view of night sky. It was about 10:15 PM, when a huge square with unblinking lights at each of the four corners passed in front of my 3rd story window. The object was about 300 ft. off the ground. I researched drones but I didn't see any pics of drones that resembled the craft. Besides the object travelled in a very straight line. A drone might be able to fly like this??. The object was flying over the inlet, and was just left of a shipyard.
