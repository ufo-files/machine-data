I filmed 4 red orbs in 3D, passing over me in front of planes.

I was on my rooftop patio, watching fireworks and keeping my eye on the sky. Since my report last year, I constantly look up. The red orbs come back occasionally, sometimes during fireworks. I noticed the first orb, off in the distance to the south west. Bright red/orange, about golf ball size, solid non-flashing light. A different light, from the planes flying around and above it. I start filming. Dead silent. Steadily moving to my position. I notify my girlfriend. As it nears our position, it slows and turns east as though it were on rails. We both feel really happy that they've come back to visit. We feel validated from our sightings before, and kind of joyous. A landing plane passes over it's position (2 engine passenger jet). Both are in the shot for comparison. The orb heads east & disappears through the clouds. I stop filming. My girlfriend spots orb #2 headed from the same direction as #1. I begin to film again. Same flight path. Same characteristics. Same slowing and course change towards the east, when it reached my position. We waved to it and tried thinking happy thoughts. We both felt an electric charge. Goosebumps and the like. Orb #3 came from the same direction. Same story as the first 2. During it's turn, the light looked like it does when a laser gets shone in your eye (I assume that's exactly what happened). Orb #4, came from SSW. Under the path of landing planes. Once it reached our position, it veered east roboticly/on rails/perfectly. I noticed the air traffic was reduced to small planes, 2 of which moved rapidly from the south, to north east. I assumed that was a fighter interception. For 15 to 20 minutes after that, only small aircraft flew around the downtown core & along the lake shore. After that, the heavy aircraft began landing again.
