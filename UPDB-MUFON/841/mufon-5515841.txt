{dr}Flashing/ rotating object observed over Maple Grove MN on 8/3/12

Wife called me to the back yard at approx. 7:15 in a well illuminated evening sky (noclouds/airplanes.) She pointed to a brightly flashing light hovering perhaps 5-6,000 feet up and 2-3 miles away. Using binoculars I could see that it was rotating on two differant axis. During this rotation one view would be of two lights and another view of one big light.

When the two light sequence would appear I could make out a black fusalage attached to each light much like the tail of the old WWII fighter plane. The brightness of the lights obscure seeing the full shape or outline of the object, I looked for a balloon, tails, wings or anything to indicate it was aircraft or from a local source but saw nothing.

It just hung in one spot like a disco ball, not rising or falling and with no lateral movement. We watched it for about 5 minutes and I went to get my kids cheap telescope. I was while setting that upthe object disappeared from sight. I didn't see it exit as I was looking at the telescope.
