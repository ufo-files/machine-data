{tjd} Giant mother ship on the ground, 2 smaller discs overhead

My friend, [Item moved to FI Report {tjd}], and I were on our way to work at Interlake School in Medical Lake, Wa. We had our 3 children in the car with us. As we turned right off of W. Westbow Rd and headed toward Medical Lake we noticed a giant spacecraft sitting on the ground out in the field, it had multi colored lights around the dome. It appeared to be larger than a football field. We were so surprised that we actually stopped, backed up and just stared at it for what seemed like forever. Then we noticed two smaller discs hovering overhead and decided we had best get out of there. We drove rather fast,as the discs followed us, to try and get our children to the daycare and safety. As we took them into the daycare we told the ladies there about the craft and discs. They came outside with us and observed the discs hovering and moving back and forth overhead,waiting. These two smaller discs were looked like bright white circles. WE got back in the car and drove on to Interlake School with the discs pacing us. When we arrived, we ran into the building. We did not discuss this with anyone at work, we were too afraid to. Other workers started to discuss having seen this later in the week and also spoke of being contacted by the military, not treated kindly and told to keep quiet about it. Jerri and I never discussed it with anyone at work.  I don't remember the exact year as I was in my 20'sand am 70 now. I worked at Interlake School from April of 1973 until June of 1997. My best guess is it was sometime in 1974 to 1975 at around 6 to 6:30 in the morning. It was very dark out and cold, we wore our winter coats, I don't remember the month. I don't know if the daycare ladies ever spoke of it either. I wish I had physical evidence to give you, but there were no cell phones back then. I only put down 4 witnesses, but there were more from work. We were not abducted that I am aware of. I was more than happy to forego any free flying lessons or experiences with them. I actually was wondering if you had anything on file from around this time.
