Ufo was heading south at very fast speed then went directly up and out of sight.

I had just picked my daughter up from work in Melton and was heading north along Melton Hwy when my daughter and I both noticed a light zipping across the sky, we watched it as it headed south and then straight up and out of sight. It completely changed direction without any apparent change of speed. The entire event lasted less than 10 seconds.
