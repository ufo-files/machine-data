{dr}5 to 7 bright lights traveling at very high speed, not in formation.

I was sitting at the kitchen table and happened to look out the window
in the south east direction when my eye caught the bright obects. At first l thought they were a reflection from lights in the house, but then I saw they were moving from left to right. The first group were at different heights and not in any formation, followed closely by 2 or 3 in the same manner. They were all traveling at very high speed, as with a few seconds they were gone out of view. So much so that my wife did not have time to get to the window only a few steps away.
