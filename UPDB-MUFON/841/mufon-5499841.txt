{jd} Flew beside us in the air.

We were on our way to go out to eat. We turned onto the Main Street in town and my mother and I noticed a glowing orange orb flying beside us about 40 feet in the air. It fallowed us all the way though town. We stopped at a 4 way stop and it stopped and waited until started moving again. When we pulled into the parking lot at the restaurant the orb stopped then when I got out of the car the orb went dark. After it went dark I could still see a black orb hovering over the parking lot.
