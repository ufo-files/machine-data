{rjl} Round flashing object moving downwards in the sky

I was in North Highlands, California. I was arguing with my boyfriend at the time and he was in the garage while I was in his room. His room has a large window that has a perfect view of the entire front house. I was laying down on the couch which is placed in front of the window and I happened to glance up and saw an airplane. I enjoy looking up at the stars at night and watching airplanes fly by (there's an airport close so it's very common to see them). The second I looked down I noticed that there was all the sudden a very bright flashing light moving in the sky so I looked up and it was moving in a downwards direction. At first I thought that maybe it was a star but after watching it for more than 30 seconds it was clearly NOT. This object was round and had bright lights surrounding it. Im not sure if the flashing lights were under or around the object because it was so far away but to me it looked like it was under it. There were definitely multiple lights but in the video it looks like one. The object, again, was flying in a downwards path but it seemed to scatter side to side a few times before I lost sight of it behind a tree. I went on google and typed in "flashing light in the sky" and it took me to multiple websites about UFO sightings and found an article with the same type of image I saw/recorded. Today I came across your website and decided to share my experience with you guys. I hope this helps!! :)
