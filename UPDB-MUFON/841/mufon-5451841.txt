Stationary Brilliant 5 lights in sky

I was taking a walk on the beach with family. I saw 5 bright white/yellow lights in the sky for a second, and they suddenly disappeared. I alerted the group. A few seconds later a similar single white/yellow light appeared and then disappeared. It was visible for about a second. For a minute after, our group witnessed smaller faint white lights in the sky moving in erratic directions. We were looking out to sea (which is south from Ocean Isle Beach).
