A star appeared out of nowhere, and then divided into three stars which went west, south, and northeast until out of view.

We were just finishing up at work, in a gravel pit in the Miniota Valley, and decided to have a beer while sitting in the darkness looking at the stars.

Suddenly, a random bright star immediately appeared out of nowhere, and then split into three stars/lights of the same size, which went in three different directions, until out of view.
