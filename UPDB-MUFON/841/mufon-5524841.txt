Bright star light cylinder craft moved SW to NW changed directions to North then disappeared

The star entered the "portal" in the southwest. It became brighter as it moved across the sky in an easterly fashion. It changed speeds as well as movement up and down. As it started to slow for a bit I noticed another craft, an airplane perhaps, with flashing light coming from the north toward us. At that moment the star light craft slowed, turned left and proceeded to go in a northerly direction towards the "plane". However it moved rapidly outwards and disappeared while the "airplane" with flashing lights continued on its southerly path.
