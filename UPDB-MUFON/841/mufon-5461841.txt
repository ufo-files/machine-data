{rjl} Light above Anniston,AL

Im a truck driver i was heading home and it was 23:55. I was on i 20 eastbound one mile before the eastaboga exit. I saw what appeared to be a star in the north west towards anniston but brighter. Really bright. I have never seen anything so bright in the sky. It was moving like what appeared to be south then it started moving south west until it was directly overhead of me directly above the highway. I couldn't see it anymore and pulled over to try to look at it. When i stopped i could see it moving at a south western direction. I heard no sound form it. The object was a bright circular light moving slowly across the sky. Too slow to be any kind of plane. And too quiet to be any kind of helicopter. The light was so bright you couldn't make out the shape of the object making the light or what it could have been made of. But it was low to the ground in my opinion. To me it appeared to change altitude kinda randomly. One second it seemed it was as low as around 1000 feet or so and then it seemed to move up to twice that height. After about a minute of watching it while i was stopped it disappeared over the tops of trees. I watched it for about 2 minutes before i lost sight of it and stopped to continue watching.  The only possible explanation i could think of is ball lightning but i know thats also really rare. Buf it didn't look like electricity at all. It was just a bright light.
