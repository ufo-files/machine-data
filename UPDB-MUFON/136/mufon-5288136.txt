Traveling Lights

I was driving west out of Bluffton on highway 116. It was a clear night and there were visible stars and what I thought was Orions Belt was actually three objects in an uneven line that were pulsating slightly and moving from, maybe, south to north. I pulled off the road on a dark section of the highway and as I watched four more seemed to flicker into view. There were six in total traveling in a ragged line, slowing to almost stopping then moving again in unison. I don't know how high they were but they were higher than an airplane I saw traveling south. They pulsated light slowly. I called my nephew in Bluffton and he verified that he also saw the objects but counted 8. As we spoke they flickered out of sight. I tried to get some pictures but only 2 showed anything other than complete black. After about 10 minutes there wasn't any sign of them, they sort of disappeared. In the photos one object can be seen in the sky along with blue and white orbs near ground level to the right of my vehicle. They are in both photos and there definitely wasn't anything that I saw there with my naked eye.
