Large, silent V shaped object

I stepped outside to view the lunar eclipse just before it's totality, and saw a very faint, but large V shaped object moving steady southeast overhead.
It appeared grey, made no sound, and had no lights.

It appeared metallic and thick. It was very hard to see against the dark sky.
