{dr}I noticed an orange beam in the sky and then it continued past. After I got several peoples attention the light turned off and I could see a little black circle that seemed to rise (getting futher away/up) as well as continue to fly straight.

Right around sunset 745/815 I was on my back porch having a cigarrette and talking with my friend and boyfriend. I noticed an orange beam in the sky and got the 2 peoples attention that were with me. My boyfriend said it looked like a red faded color and then we all really paid attention. It was traveling away from the sun in my direction. It was not very high up maybe the length that a helicopter would travel looking for someone. As soon as it got above us the light turned off which allowed us to then see that it was a black object. But as the light turned off it seemed to go up or further away while still traveling straight. My friend looked away for a moment and was not able to see where it was after that becuase it became so small as it moved further away. There were no clouds in the sky so my boyfriend and I were able to continue to watch grow farther away until all of a sudden it just disappeared. I think it just finally was too far that I could no longer see it but my boyfriend who has better eye sight than me said it just disappeared. There was absolutely no sound of the craft at any point which was very odd also because when it had the orange light/beam in the beginning it was pretty close. So there should have been some noise. When the beam of light was on it made it impossible to see its shape or even color. It wasnt until the beam was off that we were all able to see the circular shape and its black color.
