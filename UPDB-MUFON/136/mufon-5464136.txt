Black soundless Triangle

Family was out laying in the grass looking up at the stars looking for satellites. All of us looked to the SE at the same time and all saw a dark black, light obsorbing triangle floating about 200' SE of us at an approx altitude of 450-500'. The object made no noise, and seemed to block out all back light and the stars. It hovered for approx 5-8minures while we observed it, it then quickly took off due south at an incredible rate of speed. No sound, no light.
