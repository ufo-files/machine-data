{rjl} 11 lights equal distance from one another moving south to north directly overhead.

I woke up early in the morning around 5:30am. I went outside, which is not abnormal for me, and I looked and started following a faint satellite in the clear pitch dark morning sky. I’ve been a sky watcher for years and have never seen what happened next. It appeared the sky was moving and when I looked directly overhead I saw bright lights appearing high in the sky. They were inside the faint light of the satellite I had been tracking. One by one they appeared, all in a line and equal distance from one another. There were 11 of them traveling from due South to due North. I followed them until they disappeared. I’ve never seen anything like it nor heard of anywhere like this before. 
I wanted to test it the next morning so I got up again at 5:30am and went outside. At first I didn’t see anything from the south so I walked down the street a bit and then it happened again. This time they came from Northwest and traveled Southeast. The difference this time is there were at least 30 I counted, all in a line and all equal distance from one another again. One light sped up and caught another one but otherwise they stayed in formation. I again watched them as they disappeared one by one to the Southeast.
