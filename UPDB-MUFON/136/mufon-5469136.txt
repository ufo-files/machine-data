Zig Zag in flight. Opaque bright white

I was returning to my car when I started my engine waiting for my wife to get her self organised before driving away. When the ignition has been turned on so do my dash cameras front and rear. Viofo A119S with polaroid lens fitted.

I first noticed the object once I got home from our short day trip and was reviewing my dash camera footage for unrelated reasons.

I was unsure what I was seeing when I happened to notice a white object zipping around in the footage virtually right from the start up of the camera.

The object appeared to come out of cloud cover then zip around then made its way straight up. I happen to also notice in the background another object from the centre of screen to the top left corner. If you look you will see it.

I was is shock and awe at the same time. I had seen one twelve years ago but never had any footage to back up my sighting.Now I do, and boy is it amazing.
The object moved out of frame. I had many sightings captured as I went over my footage throughout the day. Still finding much much more as I continue to sieve through my days footage. 

I have two lots of footage which I would like released but also have more and all from that same journey on the 13 of April 2018.
