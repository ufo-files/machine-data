Me and my friend saw a UFO fly over our cow field. We saw it when a flash went into the ski. When we first saw it we thought it was a another airplane but then it didn't make a noise. So then we got scared and hid behind a trailer. About 5 seconds after we saw about 3 to 5 airplanes zooming towards the craft. Shortly after we lost site of the craft and planes. Then we took off running to the house and told our parents. Then we looked out the window and saw more planes flying across the sky in the direction of the UFO.
