{rjl} Unidentifiable aircraft with blue, red, and white lights over Huntington Beach

I was standing in my driveway (Indianapolis and Newland) and saw a strange hovering aircraft with a bright blue light that changed to red and then white. It was a smaller aircraft but it was impossible to judge how far away it was. The aircraft made fast turns in directions as though it was a drone, but no regular aircraft could make such swift turns or move that way as though it was standing still, then moving slowly but quickly changing directions. Then it just slowly moved away and a tree was blocking my view, so I moved to see where it was but it suddenly disappeared.
