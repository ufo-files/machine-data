{cak} Ufo in front of the Super moon.

I was taking photos of the Super moon on 08/28/2015. I noticed a small black dot on one of the photos. I know it isn't dust or dirt on the lens, it is in only one of many photos of the moon I took that night. I also made sure there was no aircraft in the area when I was shooting. I originally passed it off and a random event, but then I read a report that someone else had photographed a pyramid shaped craft over the moon that very same night. I figured I would share this with you guys and see if it is anything to you. Please feel free to enlarge these photos, one is an original shot, and the other is a cropped image.
