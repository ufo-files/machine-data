{tjd} Changed from comet with tail to rectangular object.

Was standing in front of my house and saw what appeared to be a comet.( It was very clear no clouds in the sky). A large orange ball of light with a tail far to the west. It was going north to south. I started to go in the house and have everyone come out to look, but it stopped and the tail disappeared. It then went on a large arc from southwest to north to east of me. The whole time in a start stop or leapfrog type of movement. When it stopped again it had made a semi circle approximately 180 degrees. It stayed there for a few seconds and I could see the rectangular shape and two appendages on one end that stuck out just a little from the end. Also it appeared to be maybe white or lighter orange under the glowing orange light that encased the entire thing. Then it made a series of 90 degree turns as if making 3 sides of a square pretty much straight overhead. Then it was still for a bit more and finally in a split second it went straight up and turned into a tiny spot that disappeared. The whole thing lasted maybe 2,3 or 4.minutes.
