I saw a UFO over Staten Island NY

On a summer night in 1986 around 2: Am I was lay on the hood of my car outside my girl friends buildings window, a buddy was sleeping in my car. Suddenly and slowly a large black triangle shaped object came over the near by building it made no sound and had red and green lights on it. I watched it fly over the buildings and down the street. I ran to wake up my friend by the time he was awake and looking it was over the Varranzano Bridge he said "where I don't see it" I point to the Bridge and said "over there" as he said "do you mean those lights over the bridge?" Suddenly the object shot away to the left leaving a shadowy motion blur.
