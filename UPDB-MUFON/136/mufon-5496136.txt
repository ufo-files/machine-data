Today, 2/12/15, in Cabo San Lucas Mexico we were watching whales on a commercial whale watching tour. We were doing the tourist thing. We saw a beautiful halo shaped rainbow around the sun off to the east. I wanted a pic of the rainbow so I snapped one and then went back to pics of whales. 
Looking at my pics tonight I was surprised to see what was on one of the pics since my girlfriend said she had posted on her Facebook page the pic she had snapped of the same rainbow with her own phone. Several people were raving back in the states about the object they were seeing on the Facebook pic. She went back to the pic and zoomed in and showed me on her phone. I then looked at my pic on my phone and the very same object showed up. Except that there was a different lighting pattern on the object. I have 7 lights on my pic. She had 6 lights on hers and they were in a different position. Her pic was obviously not taken the exact same time mine was and it was obvious the object was moving. There is a specific/definitive pattern to the lights on this huge ball shaped object. She took 3 pics of the rainbow and all 3 of her pics showed the item was moving into the halo and then outside the halo. Therefore, we got 4 good pics of this weird happening. So, what is it guys.
