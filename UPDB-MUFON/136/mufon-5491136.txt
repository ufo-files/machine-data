{rjl} Approx 20 aircraft flying VERY high observed in the sky. Flying mostly in straight formation. Glowing halo with blinking lights. Could not make out the body of aircraft.

While walking my dog on the night of July 19th at approx. 21:20 in Brookhaven, Pa. I looked up to observe at least 20 aircraft? Military? with blinking lights and what looked like a halo of glowing light around each one. They were flying very high. There was another aircraft flying in the area that was at a much lower altitude. They were all headed in the same direction which I think was NE. Another neighbor also observed this with me. Eventually they disappeared from sight.
