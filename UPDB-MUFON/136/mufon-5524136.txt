Satr like object slowly ascends and disapears

while observing the moon tonight i noticed a starlike light under the moon and off to its south east direction slightly above the tree line. I was facing east in the direction of Lake Erie's western/southern bank. i observed a star like object that slowly began to ascend after a minute, and slowly ascended through a small wispy cloud and shortly after rising through the cloud the light disappeared. the sky was clear except for a few small whispy clouds. it appeared as though it left a trail as it ascended.
