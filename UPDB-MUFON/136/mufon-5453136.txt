large ball of light traveling in a straight path at an extremely high rate of speed

Driving South on highway 93 about 2 miles north of highway 72 in Jefferson County, CO observed to the West a large ball of light traveling at an extremely high rate of speed in a straight path to the south with no apparent sound. The Silhouette of the foothills could be observed clearly and the phenomenon was clearly below the tops of the foothills which would put it at an elevation at 500' or below and within 1 to 1.5 miles from our point on the road. An educated estimate of speed of the object would put it in the range of mach 15-20 or higher. It was like a ball of light traveling on a beam of light, there was a beam in front as well as behind. Considering the speed of travel it could have only appeared to have a beam in front, hard to say. It was mentioned ONCE that night on the local news. I want to know if anyone else reported it at the time of occurrence.
