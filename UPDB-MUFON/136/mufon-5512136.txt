(GS) Bright orange lights over Atlantic Ocean. A set of three, then to the right, a set of two more.

I took a break during work and was standing out behind the restaurant, looking over the marsh. Three orange lights appeared, then two more appeared to the right, maybe a mile or two. I watched and rechecked later on. About thirty minutes they were there, in the same area.

A year later, the exact same pattern, color, and number of lights, in the same area. A couple of coworkers saw it at the same time. The lights blinked out one at a time.

Many people have reported this same thing.
