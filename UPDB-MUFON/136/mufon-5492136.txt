{jpg} Two lights in sky fading in and out. Hovering.

I was out in my backyard smoking a cigarette when the lights caught my eye. They were similar in appearance to stars but only brighter and larger. There were two of them, a larger one with another smaller light above and to the left of them. As I watched them the larger one started to fade away, within about 4 seconds it had disappeared. That really caught my attention. Within 30 seconds it returned in the same way it left. The second, smaller light then followed suit. I immediately called to my dad to come look at it and I could tell that after watching for some time he was as shocked and confused as I was. We both took photos and video with our phones, but never really got a good picture of what we were seeing. It just showed up as a bright light. Maybe if someone can enhance them it will become clear. I kept trying to rationalize it but no explanations were coming to me. It went on like this for another hour. They would even sometimes disappear together, only to light up again. After it faded away for the last time I was left grasping for answers. That's when I came across this site, and the pictures from case 67409. They are EXACTLY the same formation as the one I just witnessed, but even better quality. I will never forget this.
