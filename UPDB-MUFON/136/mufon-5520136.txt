Very low no more than maybe 3-5000ft. above me moving slowly in a northerly direction

I was in my back yard on a very clear night taking my dog out before bed. I was gazing at the stars when I just happened to notice this triangular shaped object flying very slowly at a VERY low altitude. I thought it was just an airplane but it had dimmed lights that outlined the shape of the craft. As it passed I didn't hear anything as you would with a typical aircraft. It couldnt have been no more than a couple thousand feet above me. I stood in awe, wondering what it could be. It kept a northerly direction as I lost sight of it over the trees to the north of my house.
