A white sphere, or egg shaped object was photographed low in the south east that varied in brightness.

I noticed a white star-like object from my front yard about 30 degrees above the south eastern horizon.It appeared white and was brighter than Venus. The motion was uniform and towards the east. I ran inside to get my camera and found the object to be much fainter to the eye. I took 6 photos by pointing the camera in the general direction of the object. It appeared to brighten again as it approached the trees. In all I observed it for about 3 minutes.I found the variation in brightness interesting.
