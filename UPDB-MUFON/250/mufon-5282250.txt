Black sphere hovering benind plane, then circles around plane and flew away to the south.

I was pumping gas and was just staring off into the distance at a plane flying by. I was looking south and the plane was about 2 miles away flying East to West. While watching it I notices this black ball flying behind it, it then appeared to fly under it and then in front of it and circle the plane. I was looking at it thinking, I must be seeing something wrong because it felt unreal. Then I realized I should film it. I got my phone out of my truck and by the time I started filmed the ball was much higher than the plane and flying away into the upper left. I did not go a good job capturing it on camera, I was looking at it while filming and not looking through my phone screen. I managed to locate it after putting the video on my computer. At the very end of the video an object moves from left to right, I'm not sure that is the same object I originally seen, it appears it might be fluttering and is most likely a bird.
