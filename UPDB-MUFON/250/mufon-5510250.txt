3 prominent lights red-orange near ottawa flashed and hovered and moved then disappeared

I was driving North towards quebec when I noticed strange lights in the sky. There were what appeared to be 3-5 at first. I tried to get a better view and when I caught the lights again there were 3 and then down to one. I watched the one move around as what seemed to be a couple others pulsating or flashing like. The one eventually just disappeared as it was moving in a cloudless sky.

I lost sight due to them disappearing. My feelings are excitement..pure utter excitement. What I saw was not what we know of yet.
