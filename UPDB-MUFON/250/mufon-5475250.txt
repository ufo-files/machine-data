{cak} Glowing circular lights emanating with pulsating hum

My friend and I were both looking at the stars. We clearly saw objects in the sky moving. I was able to take a couple of pictures and videos then my phone died. My friend also mentioned she had taken pictures then her phone died and turned back on. Electronic interference was present in both phones. Her phone then turned on and said hello then her name how can we help you. Siri on her device was not turned on and disabled. It continued to say they were carbon based lifeforms and we were totally freaked out and went inside the home. While going into the home I felt like we were in slow motion walking. Almost as a dream. We continued to stare at each other in disbelief. After the phone interference we heard quite a loud humming sound emanating outside of the home. The humming was pulsating and almost sounded electric. At this point in time my other room mate came outside and we witnessed the craft hovering and pulsating light at about 3000 feet very close. We then talked about how crazy it was then went to bed. When going to bed we noticed the time was almost 6am for a time loss of almost 6 hours. After the encounter in the coming weeks I felt very strange. Had blood pressure issues, had sensations of being very hot, and a very fast heart rate. To this day I am still confounded by the events that took place. I find it hard to talk to other people about the experience and it was very scary.
