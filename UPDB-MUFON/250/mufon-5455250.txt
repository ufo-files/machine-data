{rjl} Saw 20 star-like objects moving in space in a straight line at a steady pace and an even distance from each other going W to E across northern sky

On 24 February 2020 at 5:45 EST I witnessed 20 star-like objects moving across the northern sky from east to west. They were star-like, resembling the usual satellite in size, brightness and speed, only there were twenty (20) of them flying one behind the other in a straight line, at even distance from each other, like pearls on a string. Only one of them was out of place, flying parallel slightly above and slightly ahead of another, but again at the same speed, and it had the same brightness. Elevation is hard to say, but it was approx. 60 degrees from the ground, and I'd call it 'in space'. I heard no sounds. It took approximately 5 minutes for the 20 objects to cross my field of vision.
