{cak} Two sets of lights were hovering or moving very slowly over the Interstate. They were too low to be a plane and were not blinking.

My fiance and I were driving on Interstate 35 in Duluth. While driving up the hill in West Duluth we noticed two sets of three lights hovering over the freeway or moving very slowly. They were probably 75-100 yards apart. The two outer lights were brighter and the one in the middle was slightly offset. Almost like a boomerang but more of a straight line. We watched them for about thirty seconds while driving and drove directly under one of them. Looking back we could still see them but kept moving due to being on the Interstate. When we got to the top of the hill we looked back and they were no longer there. They were very bright and probably only 100 to 500 feet above the ground. Maybe drones but very strange. I have never seen anything flying in that area.
