40 lights in perfect formation moving rapidly East

I stepped outside to smoke a cigarette and I looked up and noticed lights flying in perfect formation with perfect equal distance between them, making no sound. I said to myself "Oh, no." 

I thought they could have been high altitude planes but they just kept coming and coming. I got my phone out to take video but I don't think they are showing up as the clouds started to move in at that moment. On my commentary, I say "you can't see them on video". I could still see them with my eyes. 

I observed at least 20 before I got out my phone, and I would say 30 went past by the time I hit record, and another 20 or 30 went past before the clouds became too thick to see anything more. 

It occurred to me I might have been seeing Starlink satellites, but before I decided to make this report, I watched the videos from 2 different news sources and the Starlink look very tightly packed together, or else they have erratic spacing between them. These lights were in PERFECT formation with EXACT SAME distance between each. If I held my 2 fingers up the space would be one and a half inches to 2 inches. They were the size of stars or airplane lights at a high altitude, but there was no noise. Probably they were just those satellites, but it didn't look like the videos on the news stations, so I thought I better send it in.
