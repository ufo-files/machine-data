1 objects 4”x41/2” off ground stationary. Second object lumpy ball

One month ago I saw two objects in my backyard. Appx. 2:30 pm. Sunny, clear and no wind. I saw directly South of me an object hovering appx. 41/2’ off the ground. It was stationary. The object was appx. 41/2” by 4” square. I observed it for appx. 7 seconds. I concentrated on it to “burn” it into my memory. At the last second object #2, which was lumpy and about the size and shape of a golf ball, came flying over and then under the first object at a fast rate of speed. I looked away for a split second and when I looked back they were gone. Told my husband and he drew what I saw to scale. The square had three panels that appeared to have a slightly concave smooth surface.They were white and silent.
