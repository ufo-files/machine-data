{cak} repeated light sequence from my hand held laser pointer

back in july my daughter and I were star gazing on my porch in northern mi,..around 11pm on a clear night, i pointed my lazer {one flash} at cluster of stars in the lower west sky an received a slow flash back, repeated this several times and received slow flash of light like a star in sequence of my lazer...this went on for a couple of minutes then stopped...my daughter and I thought this was very strange.....Oct 11, warm clear night around 11pm grabbed my lazer pointer and flashed it once at same cluster of stars as in july and received a slow flash of light like a star on a dimmer switch,repeated this with 2 then 3 flashes from my hand lazer and received in sequence 2 then 3 slow flashes back,then object started to move and slow flash to the southwest and slowly faded away..i would like to point out this light was slow flashing in a stationary position for several minutes before it started to move and at one point I went into the house to see if I could video it on my notebook camera but that didn't work.i got the feeling that this thing reacted to my lazer pointer then decided to leave area.....I will follow up on this strange light on the next clear night...thankyou.....
