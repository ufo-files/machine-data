Glowing flying object decended and landed flashing a beam of fireball

This event happened on tuesday 8th September 2015 at Kaduna South in the city of Kaduna, Nigeria. The Unidentified object appeared in the town and several people observed it as it was hovering and glowing and people where gathering shouting Haleluyah and some where praying. They mistook the object to be virgin mary mother of christ. In a community like kaduna most people have never heard about UFO or extra- terrestial craft or beings and i guess the reason why they thought this phenomenon object is virgin mary. This video was posted on our church fellowship forum and when i viewed it i knew immediately this is not ordinary and i was convinced this is an unidentified object. I was told several people recorded the video with their mobile phone and i have decided to report this to MUFON so they can review this video and carry out further investigation.
