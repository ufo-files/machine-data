{tjd} Cone shaped with fire like lights. Saw something fall off, but no reports of findings

I observed a very long cone shaped object in the sky about 300 feet above me which came out if the south west. I tried to outdrive the object at first as my first instinct, but i knew my car would not make it out of the range of the crash if this object if it fell out of the sky. So i observed it as long as i could and i even tried to listen to the power sorce or what ever i could. There was no sound. After about 10 minutes the object stayed on course over lake superior in a northeasterly pattern i had to follow the road and the object was gone and agian i saw a flame/fire like exiting the back if the cone shaped object. The next day the paper stated it was a soviet satellite that crash lanned in Pennsylvania. Which I have concerns about the pattern of this sattilite and what I saw the night before.
