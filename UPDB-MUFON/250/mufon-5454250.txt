{tjd} Went out to search for dog, Object appeared overhead, I stared at being in window for about 15 minutes.

I was staying overnight at my friends house were awakened early, told by froiend's mother to go look for the dog, we went outside. It ws a Sunday, no traffic, no one. Bob went one way, I went the other way, walking down the center of the street, looked up and it was right there. A domed disk, about 10 tt above the street, I think I could have jumped up and touched it. It had a window in the front, about ten feet from me, a being with a large head, eyes seemed not human, don't remember any bliniking, ears or mouth, in a red suit, like a poncho without a hood, I could see him waiste up, it had arms, couldn't see the hands. It. stood there and looked at me. It looked at me, then looked over to it's right, at something else in the craft, then looked at me, then stared off to it's right. This repeated for 15 minutes. A new community, trees were all small, it raised up to housetop level, one block from my house, could see my house. As the object hovered, it rocked back and forth a little bit as it was moving. I rean after it, jumped fences, cut my hand, so I stopped. It went about a mile, then it went out ofsight over the tree line. After it left, I was in awe, walked back to bob's house, walked in the house, Mom and 3 older sisters were sitting smoking & drinking coffee. They asked "Where have you been?" Don't know if I ansered, but I went downstairs and went to bed. Because they asked me where I had been, it seems that I had been gone longer than I thought.. Not sure of time lapse. I've only told my children and a few others; they looked at me funny. They had let the dog out, he didn't come back, so they had sent us to look for it. I talkied to Bob 20 or 30 years later, and he didn't want to hear anything about it, swore at me! He was agitated! I heard he had brain cancer. I just had heart valve replaced.
