Saw a black globular blog shaped UFO flying overhead with daughter and wife

Was driving home from a friends place, I had 2 beers and 2 glasses of scotch. I was with my wife, son and daughter, my wife had nothing to drink. She was driving, we were almost home when she gasped and said "Oh, what's that?" she was looking up and to her right outside of the driver window, she continued to sound puzzled asking is that a drone, is that a plane, she opened her window, then my daughter opened her window and both were asking the same thing. 

From were I was I could not see I had to lean right over and when I did I saw what appeared to be a completely black almost spider shaped blob or globular type object. Flying low, flying from East to East or maybe even slightly South East. 

Yes it could have been a drone or a bunch of balloons however it did not appear that way at all. No flashing lights, no lights, no sound. As we continued ahead I noticed about a 2-3KM directly ahead (north) there appeared to be another similar object, completely black flying in the same direction from the same direction only I could not make out a shape as it was too far.

We eventually got home about seconds later and had lost both objects among the trees.
