White glowing object with planet/star like appearance in late dusk sky

Appeared to be a planet in the night sky with a Venus like appearance. My wife and I were going out to celebrate our anniversary. It was a full moon on September 20th 2021 so I decided to bring my green laser to point in the night sky since we planned to look at moon. I decided to show her the laser pointer before we got in the car. I randomly picked out a bright star in the sky to aim at with the laser and when I shot it we discovered we hit an object that looked exactly like a star sitting perfectly still in the sky. The object darted quickly to the north then began to move toward us appearing to keep its altitude the same. We got frighted and walked under a car port. The object got closer then changed direction and began heading NE toward the foothills.
