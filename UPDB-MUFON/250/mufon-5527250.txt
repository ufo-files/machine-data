Ufo with face looking down

After a long day, I was sitting in a patio chair on my back deck, trying to relax,looking up at the night sky. I noticed a very bright what I thought was a star, but was changing colors, red,blue,white,green, and moving slightly at first then making strange patterns in the sky. I went inside the house and grabbed my digital camera, walking back outside I took a few pictures, and then decided to take video for the time that my camera allowed. I took video for about 4 minutes, and then it disappeared. I downloaded the video and pictures to my home computer, and viewed them and was not impressed with the quality. Thinking no more about it till my home computer started to run slowly and purchasing a new one was the only option. I started to go through all the family video and photos and which one to save and put on my new computer, I came across the video/pictures I took last august. I put them on my computer and with the new media viewer installed looked at the photos and zoomed in and to my shock I saw what looks like a star in the center of a swirling mass. The next photo is the same thing but with what looks like the profile of a face sticking out of the star shaped object. I was spooked to say the least.
