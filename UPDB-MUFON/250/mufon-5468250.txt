Larger than a star shaped orange twinkly light appeared, moved around and grew in size slightly,then shot off in an unexplainable speed and watched get smaller in size.

Me and my boyfriend were on the hill, which is in the middle of nowhere, just chilling, when we both looked upto the sky there was a strange firey sun like object buzzing around in the sky. It had a twinkling look to it like a star would, it stayed doing this for about thirty seconds. We continued to watch it, then with in a click of your fingers it darts off behind where it previously was buzzing around to, we watched it decrease in size until it disappeared. The speed it left off with was unexplainable and the fastest moving thing I have seen in my life.
