{dr}Glowing ball of light almost like a rocket booster glow.

Outside taking out the trash. When I look up to the sky and see a red orb glowing like a solid rocket booster would do. It moved in a straight line across the sky in a SW direction. My sister and I watched it until it was out of sight. I noticed the object because it was unlike anything in the sky I have seen. I live near the airport so I know the difference between planes and other things in the sky. Like I said this thing was glowing red/orange like a rocket booster would in the night sky. I lost the object as it flew out of distance and then when I looked back to see if it was there again it was no where to be seen in the sky.
