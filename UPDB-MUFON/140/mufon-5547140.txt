2 sonic booms followed by strange lights

I was working at the University of Colorado Colorado Springs (UCCS). I was outside watching the classes change when i heard a loud explosion in the distance followed by the ricochet of the sound off the ground. So i looked up thinking i would catch a glimpse of a jet going supersonic. But then another happened right after it. I looked up along with the other students and saw two white lights flying from east to west. They were basically streaks in the sky only for a few seconds as they dissappeared over the mountain range. At first i thought it was just one of the air force bases doing some practice runs. But I was military for 6 years years and have never heard of the air force doing supersonic runs over a city.  It seemed weird to me that there were two in such short time followed by the light anomaly.
