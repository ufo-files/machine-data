String of spaced lights traveling north to south very high speed

Yorktown, VA 37.238754, -76.509. Looking to the west, at 2130 EDT observed well over 50 bright star-like lights very high moving at a high rate of speed from north to south. They were space further apart than the video short showing the launch of small satellites. Many were in direct line with the previous light, some were slightly out of line. No sound. Very high altitude. Very high speed. 

Maybe these were mini satellites...but it was very remarkable.

I've seen many military aircraft and watched satellites in the sky. This was quite different.
