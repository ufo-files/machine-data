{nd} glowing orange orbs traveling north to south then fading

Arriving home at approx 1930 and witnessed two glowing orange orbs traveling from north to south. Called roommate to observe. At this point the first two had traveled overhead and one had disappeared. We observed five more of the same objects cross over our heads. I commented maybe they were lanterns but my roomate pointed out they were not traveling with the wind. There was no sound. It was very odd.
