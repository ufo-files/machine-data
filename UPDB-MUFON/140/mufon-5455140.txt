{rjl} Red light observed coming in from miles off shore with light reflecting off cloud cover coming directly towards me at 5-10,000ft

Red light coming from SW toward Coronado Island from miles off shore, I could see the reflection of the red light on clouds as it moved occasionally being blocked by clouds. As it got even with the coast line I could see that the altitude was above 5000ft, I know this from extensive observation of all aircraft military and civilian, and I am ex-military. The flight path for commercial aircraft into San Diegos Lindbergh field is right behind my house, and I watch airplanes fly over every day and I know altitude and airspeed of airliners as well as military aircraft. As the solid red light got even with the coast line it made a 90 degree turn south at Mach speed for approximately 5 mi then another 90 and back out to sea, in 50 years of flying and observing planes, jets, drones,helicopters, Ospreys, and rockets, I’ve never seen anything make such a high G turn and then accelerate at such speed, I know that a human could not survive such a turn, just seeing it paralyzed me for minutes attempting to process what I’ve just witnessed, the Navy is based here the Seals training is here and the Top Gun school is here, I believe there are 2 or 3 nuclear powered aircraft carriers here now, relevant maybe, for me a life changing experience, and am glad to share my story with anyone.
