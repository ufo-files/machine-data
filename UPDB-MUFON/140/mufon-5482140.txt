{rjl} Something made me want to get a photo of object

Since first UFO sighting, I actively keep eyes on night sky. Sometimes objects first look like stars, but something is off about them, so knowing my camera can photograph more clearly what my eyes cannot see, I took a photo. Didn't know what I had captured until downloaded to computer. Recent Ancient Aliens episode discussed black satellites from the 1950s, and this looked similar to the craft they showed. I'm always excited when I get a unique photo. If I recall correctly it flew away.
