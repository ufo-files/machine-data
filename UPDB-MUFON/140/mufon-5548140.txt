I saw a cigar shaped UFO when I was 15 or 16

I was with my cousin John outside of our Nanny's house at 72 North Main street in Beacon Falls 06403, we had smoked a joint. It was some time during the summer at night. John had to go inside to go to bed to get up for work. I went to walk away when I was frozen in my tracks and looked up in the sky north of my nanny's house, there was a transparent cloud which glowed from the inside out with a cigar shaped UFO in it which just hovered, I felt as if the whole town stood still. I do not know how long this lasted but I know I was missing some time. I sent out a letter to my cousin yesterday to see if he saw it too. I was not afraid, I just went home, which was three houses north of my Nanny's house. Approximatly one year later there was an article with drawings of what I saw in the Naugatuck Daily News in Naugatuck Ct 06770 that a man there saw the same thing that I did.
