A bright red-orange ball was seen by two neighbors and I between 10:30 pm and midnight on July 4th 2015, just after the fireworks. The object was moving faster than a plane and in a straight line headed NW. We were able to follow the ball in the sky visually for a good two minutes by just moving our position a bit in the front yard... we all continued to make comments and follow it with our eyes before it just disappeared. It never dimmed or flashed, just vanished out of sight.
