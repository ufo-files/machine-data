{cak} THREE BLACK ORBS/CRAFT FLYING NORTH OVER EL MONTE, CA

I was in El Monte in my kitchen when I turned around and looked out the window at 6:37pm as I went to grab my cell phone.

I saw 3 black orbs (or craft) - two together and one off to left side of the two - whobbling and moving in a pattern toward the northwest (Location removed). The craft moved in a straight pattern while the one on the left seemed to swing back and forth or 'wobble' moving at the same speed.

I lost sight of the object when it left my view through the window and following after, I looked west and I noticed a white plane afterwards (after I lost sight of the crafts).
