IN 1989 AROUND OCTOBER OR NOVEMBER IN ARMOURDALE, KANSAS CITY, KS, AROUND 10 PM ON A FRIDAY NIGHT, I WAS OUTSIDE IN A YARD WAIT FOR MY FRIENDS, WHO WERE INSIDE THEIR HOME GETTING READY TO GO OUT TO A JAZZ BAR IN K.C. MO..AS I WAS WALKING AROUND THE YARD AND NEAR THE MAIN STREET SIDEWALK, I GLANCED UP AND SAW A RED, ORANGE AND SOME YELLOW COLOR GLOWING FLAMES. i WAS AWESTRUCK AND AS IT WAS FLOWING IN A LINEAR DIRECTION, I WAS TRYING TO GET SOME PEOPLES ATTENTION. THERE WERE A LOT OF CARS DRIVING BY, AND I TRIED TO LET THEM KNOW THAT THERE WAS SOMETHING IN THE SKY, BUT THEY JUST KEPT DRIVING. I LOOKED UP AGAIN AND SAW IT KEPT MOVING SOUTHWEST WARD AND STILL TRIED TO GET PEOPLE'S ATTENTION BUT TO NO AVAIL. when I LOOKED UP THE THIRD TIME, IT WAS GONE.
AS I WAS LOOKING AT IT, I WAS AWESTRUCK AND COULDN'T BELIEVE WHAT I SAW. TO ME, IT LOOKED LIKE A HUGE GLOWING RED-ORANGE FLAME WITH NO GEOMETRICAL STRUCTURE TO IT. iT WASN'T A FIRE IN THE SKY, BECAUSE FLAMES WOULD DISPERSE. BUT IT SEEMED DISCIPLINED MOVING A LINEAR DIRECTION. AFTER IT DISAPPEARED, I FELT DISAPPOINTED, BECAUSE I COULDN'T GET ANYONE TO LOOK UP AT WHAT I SAW.
