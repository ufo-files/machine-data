I was headed home traveling east on W. Town Plaza Blvd around 7:30pm on September 1, 2018. I was stopped at the traffic light of W. Town Plaza Blvd. and 434.  I looked up in the sky and I noticed what appeared to be a trail and I followed the horizontal trail visually in a slightly south eastern direction And saw a bright white light. It appeared to be hovering or moving very slowly and I noticed coming from my right towards the object but underneath it a large black plane. 
I continued through the traffic light, because it had changed, while in the process of parking my car I noticed the object was heading from the south east towards the north east.   I parked the car got out of my car and walked around to the front of the condominium complex where I saw the object directly east moving in a northern direction.  Coming from the north / left and heading east was a large black plane. 

I lost sight of the object.
