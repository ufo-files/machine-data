Large black V shaped ufo

Driving at 1030.PM on the night of 13 Aug 2013, South on route 2 towards Oregon, Il. Two of my friends, and myself saw a dark object low in the sky. It was a dark night as the moon was only a sliver was illuminated. The dark object stood out among the dark sky as it seemed to be darker than the already dark night sky. As we got closer to the unlit stationary object it seemed to suddenly turn on their lights. On the bottom of the object there appeared 5 large round white lights with smaller white lights between the larger. The leading and trailing edge of the object had pulsing orange and green/blue lights. After passing directly under the object I pulled over to get out of my car to look back at the object only for it to have either left the area or disappeared. But there was no noise to let me know that it had left.
