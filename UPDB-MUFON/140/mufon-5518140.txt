[Object description from the field investigator report/cms/tg]

OBJECT DESCRIPTION: The object was described as an ultra-bright white light, emitting rays with a bluish contrast to them. The object was first observed at about 45 degrees above the horizon, at an estimate of 2000 feet up, appearing just larger than the size of an asprin, traveling from the North to the South. When the object reached close to 45 degrees on the opposite horizon it suddenly changed direction and began heading East. The object then appeared to greatly increase in speed before it disappeared behind some mountains. The witnesses remained fixated on the sky for about 10 minutes when they noticed an airplane approaching from the South. They tried to compare the visual aspects of what they had just seen with those of the approaching plane but could not find any similarities between the two. The total elapsed time for the sighting of the unknown object was about two minutes, and was estimated to have traveled about 100 miles. No sound was heard coming from the witnessed object, but was heard from the later approaching airplane.
