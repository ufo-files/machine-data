2 pinkish orange vehicles passed overhead while driving in a convertible

Driving west on DryCreek approaching the stop sign at South Broadway in a 69 Camaro convertible, two tear drop to triangle shaped glowing pinkish orange vehicles passed overhead,at approximately 200 ft. traveling west at approximately 50 mph. We turned south onto South Broadway after stopping at the stop sign and continued watching until they were out of sight approximately 20 seconds later. I remember thinking to myself and wondering if any people in the homes that they went over saw them,too. My companion, the driver ,and I just looked at each other without speaking at all. WOW! I think that was in 1973, and I wonder to this day if there have ever been any other reports of vehicles fitting this description. During that same summer we also discovered a mutilated cow about 5 miles from there in a pasture while out shooting our .22’s
