{rjl} Object captured in picture of suns aurora

At approximately 1:00 pm I was attending an employment function at 7 Springs ski Resort in Champion Pennsylvania. As my group gathered around for a short meeting on the outside pool deck I noticed a huge aurora around the sun. Difficult to view for any longer than a glance as the sun was very bright and directly overhead. As the meeting ended I pointed out the anomilee to several people in the group.. 2 of us took pictures. My forwarded pic is taken from my Galaxy 7 Samsung smart phone. I pointed the phone straight above my head and pointed it towards the sun and blindly took this pic.Which I didn't see for hours later until I returned to my hotel room. I never intended to capture this object in the pic. 2 other people that took pics also have the white ball like center of the object in their pictures in various places. But none are brilliantly aglow as this pic seems to be. Also appears to have possible thrust emissions. This picture is unaltered and may be easily explainable. But, I have no idea what this object is.
