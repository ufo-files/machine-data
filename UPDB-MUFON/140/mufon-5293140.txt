UFO hovered in NE sky low enough to distinguish it's blinking light pattern, then moved slowly west then south, hovered, then moved east

Unloading my car at around 10:30pm 7/15/2020 when my 5yo tells me to look at that satellite in the NE sky. It was no satellite. Could really only see it's green blinking light circular pattern. It seemed very far away. We watched it slowly move NW where it seemed to hover for a few minutes, then it traveled south and hovered directly over my home where it became difficult to watch as my 5yo and I were starting to have neck pain from looking up. It was at it's lowest altitude when we first saw it and it gained altitude before it moved. My phone could not capture it with video and pictures as far as i can tell. If brightened, maybe! My 15yo also came out to see it when it was at it's lowest point. My 5yo and I watched for approximately 20 minutes.
