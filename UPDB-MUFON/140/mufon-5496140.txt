{nd} LARGE saucer shape still behide and above my mothers neighbors house, My wife was in the truck with me. I am looking for someone to put me under, and they me more.

We were coming home one night, when my wife asked me what that is in the sky. From my side of the truck I could not see it, but when I turned to the right and down a hill...there it was. A Large craft the size of a football field. I was near my mothers house so I drove down the hill to her house, and it was right in front of us, I ran in my mothers house to get her to see it. When I came back out it was gone...My wife said it just disappeared. Some people on the street also saw it.
