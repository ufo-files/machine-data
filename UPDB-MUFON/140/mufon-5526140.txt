Real bright object in sw sky.

My husband and I were driving around in our convertable about 9 pm Sunday June 12, 2011 when I happen to look up and saw this really bright round object in the sky. It got extremely bright and then it got real dim. It did this several times. At first I thought this was a falling star but it moved from west to east. I watched until it finally went out of sight. This was really strange. It was not an airplane or helicopter. It sounds like the persons in KY and CA saw what I saw.
