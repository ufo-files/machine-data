silvery angular object, car size rotating slowly & hovering silently in front of balcony - 10 min view from close range

See attached photos. Observed by me and my partner. Very bright (reflecting sunlight), difficult to distinguish details, as edges seemed blurred but also due to brightness. Appeared to be rotating through vertical axis at about 15 rpm, clockwise is viewed from above. Rotation not consistent, ie stopped and restarted. Hovered for about 2-3 minues directly in front of my 12 story balcony about 2-300m away and at similar elevation, perhaps 20 m higher. From the cars on the road below it seemed to be the size of a large car. Subsequently it began moving south at a slow pace (5-10 km/h) and after traversing about 400m it began moving sw towards the bay and ket on this track slowly ascending. I watched it until it was over the bay perhaps 3-5 km away. By this time it had ascended to perhaps 300m. Total sighting about 10 min
