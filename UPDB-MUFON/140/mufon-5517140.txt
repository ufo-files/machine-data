{dr}Bright never present lights in shape of an oblique triangle.

My sister came outside to walk the dog and came back in to get me saying she thinks there is a UFO outside. I put on my shoes, grab my phone and go outside. I look east where she pointed, and sure enough saw what she saw. Three lights not ever present in an oblique triangle shape. The far right light is less dim but pulses/blinks constantly. The bottom light is the brightest with a red light emission on top, white-ish in the middle, and blue coming from the bottom. The top light is constant without being super bright or blinking, but has the same light color characteristics. As I was trying to take pictures, she went inside to get the binoculars. With a closer look all we could see was a light aura around the two brighter lights. We have been outside for a half hour now and they have not lessened in brightness or shape or moved any direction.
