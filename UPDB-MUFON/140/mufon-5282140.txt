Three {stationary} objects, one transparent which was {oval in shape (blimp like), two objects were dark gray in color, triangular in shape, and fuzzy (almost cloud like).} - {djs}

Witnessed three objects about 150 to 200 feet above land while traveling eastbound on Interstate 94 about two to three miles outside of Detroit Metro Airport. One object was oval in shape (blimp like), transparent in color, blending in with the sky, however slightly lighter in color than the sky. The other two objects were dark gray in color, triangular in shape, and fuzzy (almost cloud like). The oval object was 6 to 7 times the size of the dark gray objects.
All three of the objects were visible for about ten seconds. The oval object seemed to be floating (stationary) adjacent to the dark gray objects which for lack of a better term, appeared to have energy. No lights were seen. One second prior to the objects disappearing, one of the two dark gray objects had a dark gray beam or tube shoot down to the ground, then bow and fragment toward the oval object. Immediately after that happened, all objects disappeared.
