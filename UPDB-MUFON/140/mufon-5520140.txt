Orange light transformed into two lights, then into three lights.

It was twilight. I was hiking in the foothills of Morrison, Colorado, when I saw three lights in the southeast sky hovering over the city of Denver. I ran to my truck, grabbed my camera, and by the time I started filming, only two lights remained: they were amber-colored, they glowed unlike commercial aircraft, and they stayed still. 

Then, one of them began to move: it split into two, raced across the sky underneath the other one, and then transformed into three objects. It went behind a hill and I lost sight of it. The whole event lasted about 20-25 minutes When contacted, I can provide you with a link to the video on youtube.
