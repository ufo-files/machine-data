Triangular UFO sighting.

I was traveling to work around 5:30-40ish am. I noticed what appeared to be a plane in the sky in the distance. But something was off about the light placement on this plane. I thought it was odd, but didn’t give it much thought after that. I proceeded to 291 South in Liberty MO. Shortly after I passed Liberty Parkway, I noticed the same light pattern from which I thought was a plane directly in front of my vehicle. The craft couldn’t have been anymore than 40-50ft off the ground flying to the west. I got a good look at the craft, and it was a triangular shaped craft. Very dark, almost black in color. It had red and green lights at the points of the craft. The craft made absolutely no noise at all. As I passed it, it continued west for about another two to three blocks. It then stopped right above this tree line that is right off of 291. That’s when the red and green lights shut off, and the craft had these circular lights that illuminated around the craft. I kept driving, in utter shock. I kept checking my rear view mirror until I learn over the hill, and lost site of it. There was at least five to six other vehicles on 291 with me, all headed south. So other people had to have seen the same thing I saw.
