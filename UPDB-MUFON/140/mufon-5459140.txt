{rjl} Bright light that seemed to phase out of one area and reappear a slight distance away.

My mother called me out to look at a light in the sky that she had seen driving home. She told me that it had been stationary and was white with red and green flashes. When I came out, I saw a single very bright white light still stationary in the sky. I took my phone out and zoomed in on it, beginning to record video as I did so. Through my phone, I could see a roughly round object. It was pulsating white light, but there were also shimmers of red and yellow. Several times, it appeared to fan out or expand and then disappear. It would then reappear a slight distance away. After watching for approximately 4-5 minutes, we noticed plane lights heading toward it from the south west and the object took off eastward then disappeared. We live approximately 10 minutes away from the Y-12 National Security Complex and Oak Ridge National Laboratories.
