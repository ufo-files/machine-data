{nd} gold-orange glowing orbs x 3 traveling W to SW. When one was nearly out of sight, the other appeared from the west and repeated

Gazing up at the sky looking for satellites. Standing on my deck looking south over the top of my roof. Large gold-orange orbs x 3 observed in the west traveling toward the SW. When the first orb was nearly out of sight in the SW another appeared in the west traveling what appeared to be the exact same path. As before, when nearly out of sight, a third appeared and traveled the same path. They took about 15 seconds each to move across my field of vision. I assume they were very high in elevation since they slowly diminished to a pinpoint in the SW. Cloudless night. No sound.
