{tjd} Shiny disk or cigar shaped object hovering and ejecting pod

I saw an object hovering above the apartments in my neighborhood that was shiny and disk shaped. it made a low soft hum and had a flashing light that would come and go. I decided to film it because it remained stationary for so long about 30 minutes. While filming in night vision, it emitted a rainbow colored aura when it pulsed. When filming in standard video, I could see some movement on the surface and possibly other objects around or emanating from the object. in one shot at 1:10 it appears to eject a pod type object toward the ground. This object was about a quarter of the size of the object or less and had a cone shape with a black dot on one end. I did not see the pod land or where it went but my camera picked up a pop and then a squeak noise. At that point I stopped observing as the object kept disappearing and reappearing as it pulsed.
