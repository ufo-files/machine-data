3 bright pulsating lights, color changing and shape morphing

I spotted 3 ufos hovering in the sky out my side of the window. I had the curtains closed and I got up to open them and I see this SUPER bright thing just in the center of the sky. My heart stops and I just stare at it. It didn’t move at first, then I started to look around and saw 2 more above it forming a triangle. I got the binoculars and saw that the bottom one was flashing all sorts of colors, red, green, blue and it was moving a bit - I looked at the other 2, one of them was def silver and blue and the other was green. The center most visible one was morphing and shape shifting from what I could see through my binoculars. At one point I swear I saw 2 very close to each other, almost like it was docking to each other. That only lasted for a minute or two, then it went back to a singular object 

I tried to get a photo through the binoculars and I got something I think. Also got a video of how bright it was in the sky. You can see in the center there is a pulsating bright white light. My phone did not pick up the changing colors like the naked eye/ binoculars saw. 2 planes flew around it so it def wasn’t a plane. 

It took about an hour for the lower light to make its way from east to south east in the sky. By that time, it was a little after 3am and i couldn’t see it anymore (blocked by trees) so I went to bed. Never physically saw them leave the sky. Fell immediately asleep and didn’t wake up until 10am the next morning.
