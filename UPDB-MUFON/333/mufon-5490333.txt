{cak} moving slowly, observed through binoculars, had some kind of force field around it

My wife and I were in the Florida room, sitting by the large picture window. It was around 7pm. The sky was clear and blue. I noticed military jet passing by and a passenger jet also. Then I noticed a 'dot' in the sky. I asked my wife if she could see it.. she said yes. I got my 10x zoom camera and took 2 pictures of it through a double pain window. Then I got my binoculars and was totally amazed at what I saw. It was round or oval.. had what looked like balloons or bubbles all around it that were rotating clockwise, changing colors with what looked like some sort of electrical field or force field around the object. This field would vary according to the bubble rotation and color. It finally went out of view to the south east. We've lived in this house for a short time and we are constantly seeing different ufo anomalies.
