(GS) Three white lights slowly descending behind building

On Friday November 9, 2012 my daughter was heading home from work and stopped at the local Redners Market in Phoenixville off route 23 sometime between 4:30 pm & 6:00 pm. 

As she parked the car she noticed three large white lights (size of baseballs from her distance) descending slowly from the sky. She watched them as they descended out of view behind Renders. 

She took a picture of the last white light she saw before she lost view it. She said she knows what airplane streaks look like and this wasnt it. They were moving together very slowly downwards towards the ground. 

She took a picture because the lights acted so strange & out of place. She couldnt wait to tell me about it and show me the picture!
