I went to a place near Porto, Portugal to see the Stars, its a very dark place where its really easy to see star clusters like Pleiades and to see the constellations. When we arrived I saw a strange star agglomerate, I took some time for my eyes to get adjusted to the darkness and what I saw something I cannot explain, they were hundreds of very small rounded lights, Yellow, following two blue bigger ones, they were moving all aligned and them changed the "formation" to a arrow, with the blue ones in front, and then to a full circle, they moved slowly and then they made a turn and they accelerated until disappear. I Tried to take a picture of the small ones but my mobile phone isn't good at dark photos, but i got a good picture of the blue ones when they were starting to take a turn.

I don't know what happened for sure, when I was driving home I started having doubts about what I saw or if it really happened...

The place has got no lights nearby... You are the first ones I ever Tell this story, I hope you can help me understand what happened to me...

Just for you to understand the magnitude... My friend Ran away because She thought it was an invasion...
