We were sleeping outside. It was 6am my friend touched me and said what is that? I looked up and tapped my brother and said it's a UFO. Then a black like door appeared on the side of the green sauser. Then one at a time a creamy looking Alien slide out and looked down at us. It was after the third one came out they were standing on the rim of craft for about a minute. Then in the same way and time they came out they went back in the black like door. Once the last Alien went in the black door it made a swish sound and door disappeared and the craft went up at an angle and disappeared. All the animals were silent until it went away. Then when gone all animals started to make the noises that the animals make.
