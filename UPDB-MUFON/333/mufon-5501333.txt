{nd} red light zig zagging by moon. then a second white ball of light streaking east followed by plane

At 9:18 PDT Twenty people on my street noticed a red glowing object by the moon.I along with the others watched through binoculars as it zig zagged and came to a complete stop.It hovered in that spot for what seemed about thirty min.Then it left in a straight path to the southwest until it was out of sight. The second sighting was approx. 20 mins later when a dull white glowing object streaked eastward toward the cascade mts. It was moving very fast. I was able to get an approx. speed,because it was followed by a muli-prop plane moving at a slower rate. the object appeared to be moving approx. 400 mph. I also noticed it didn't have any port and starboard lights like the plane and it was too low to be a satellite. It appeared to be the same elevation as the plane,approx.1000 ft.The plane wasn't that high as it had just taken off from a local airport so it was easy to gauge elevation.
