{jpg} Hovering Light beneath the clouds in Fort Myers

I took my dogs for their usual walk around the complex. While walking east I caught a glimpse of what appeared to be a flame lingering in the sky. It was yellow orange and had a halo around it. The entire light seemed flicker, but not did outright blink as do the individual lights on an airplane. My first thought was that it was a plane until I realized by stopping, that it was not moving. It just hovered there beneath the clouds, but close enough to the ground that it appeared like a lick of flame. I grabbed my cell phone and tried my best to record it but iPhones don’t like trying to focus on anything in the sky. I videoed for approximately 15 to 17 seconds with it zoomed in. Then I tried a second video of about the same length with the zoom out. During the second video, the light lost its halo and just burned bright orange. Then it just vanished. I have no clue what it was, why it made no sound, or how it simply hung in the air. I have included the videos with this report, though they don’t show the detail I had hoped.
