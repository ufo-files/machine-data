Female Sheep Found With Strange Burn Wounds

At approx 0215 hours on 22/02/15 in Shrewsbury I was awoke by the sound of a screeching animal. I look outside and I could see a sheep laying on the grass not too.far from the house. I went down to investigate and saw th st the sheep was dead. It looked like it had been lasered from the jaw down it's side to its rectum area. The wounds were a brown colour and clean cut. There was no blood but there was a strange smell of sulpher in the air. On closer inspection I saw that it's lungs and rectum had been removed surgically. I put my finger inside and pulled out some strange glue like substance that was a white and yellow colour. It smelt weird and there was approx a cup full of it. I called my friend the farmer who came and collected the dead sheep. He said a few of these things had happened in the area recently and he was scared. I told him that it was definitely aliens and that he should keep a check on this.
