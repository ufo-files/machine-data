I witnessed and recorded a metallic ball hovering then flying slowly

I was out walking my dog, late in the evening, when I noticed a metallic ball hovering over me, near the Air Force base I live next to. I quickly pull out my cell phone and begin recording. The ball slowly began to circle the base and I felt this was odd because the ball was traveling at a slow speed and was completely silent. Living next to the Air Force base, I am used to seeing plains and jets fly over me but they make noise and travel much quicker than this UAP. It looked more like a hot air ballon slowly flying through space. Except this was a ball, a metal ball.
