{rjl} Black triangle

I was sitting in the hot tub staring up at the sky (facing Southwest), looking at the stars, when suddenly I see a black triangle come right over the top of my head. It was almost undetectable, it was moving Southwest, it took me a second to realize that it was a completely black triangle. The only reason I saw it, is because the sky was brighter from the moonlight (almost full moon)I couldn't tell how high up it was, but from my perspective it was about 3/4" of an inch wide, in comparison to the roof of the house. No sound, it was moving not much faster than a plane. It had very dim, yellow/whitish small lights on each corner, you almost couldn't see them.Once it disappeared behind the roof, I jumped out of the hot tub to try and keep an eye on it, but I couldn't see it. It was at that point Katie (my wife)opened the back door. I was and still am in complete disbelief at what I saw, it wasn't a plane, or birds, with out any doubt. It was an absolute perfect triangle!
