Red sparkling like a flare round object. The same for the second but it was shaped like a four petaled daisy. Round center. Four petals.

There were two objects in the sky. First one in the east it was red and sparkling like a flare, changing direction randomly with incredible speed moving generally left to right across the sky. It was more or less a round shape. Then in the south across the sky from the first one a second object appeared. It was also red and sparkling like a flare but it was bigger. It was the shape of a flower with four petals around a central hub facing our direction with the petals folded in around the hub. It was moving right to left and as it moved towards the first object it slowly twisted to the right to face it and at the same time the four "petals" "opened to their full extent, unfurling like sails. The exact moment the petals were extended to their fullest the two objects intersected one another, the first object going into center of the second and then they moved together left to right as the petals rapidly closed over the first object and then they slowly disappeared. What I thought I saw was some kind of craft going through a portal maybe but I had an odd feeling that they were not so much a ship and a door but both living things.
The next day their was a massive blackout here. Maybe not connected but that is how I can remember the date
