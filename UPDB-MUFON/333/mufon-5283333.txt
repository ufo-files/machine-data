Took dog out, seen light over mountain and tower. Then it slowly moved away

About 8:45pm I took our dog out and noticed a light over Cape Mears mountain across the bay from us. There are some towers that are at the top. I thought it was a new light that might have been place on the new tower they just put up not too long ago due to it looking like it was right there. Looking around and waiting on the dog, I looked back up and seen it move to the right/West a little bit. As I watched it seemed to be moving very slowing. So I then ran into the house and grab the camera, ran out, and began taking photos. I rested against a deck post trying to stay steady, but most of the pics have a little blur to them. As I took pics I noticed it floated/hovered/traveled slightly up at first and towards the SWest then traveled more Westerly turning downward behind the mountain towards the ocean. In the pics, the two lights to the left are the towers. The light to the right is the object.
