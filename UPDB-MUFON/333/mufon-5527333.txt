Three yellowish-white circular lights in shape of triangle

I was southwest bound on Pearl Road and coming to a stop at the intersection of Pearl Road and Ridge Road on my way to Parma General Hospital when I noticed through the front windshield fast moving lights from the east/southeast moving west/northwest that seemed much too low to the ground for the speed at which they were moving. I live in this area and am quite familiar with what is usually in the sky. I saw three large white lights with a yellow hue that were perfectly circular flying at what seemed to be a high speed, but were, again, very low to the ground (200-300 feet?). I thought at first the object was a plane, but this was too low and too close to be a plane, plus there were no wings or any other discernible structure. I then thought helicopter, but I saw no blinking lights or colored lights of any kind, heard no noise when I rolled down the windows (though I did not think to roll down the windows until object was out of sight). The flight pattern was smooth and level, did not seem to be descending or ascending. I looked to see if the driver in front of me was also looking at these lights, but could not tell if the driver noticed them. Sighting ended when the object flew over houses & trees. The lights were not equidistant from one another either, the back two lights seemed to be a bit closer to one another and a bit farther away from front light on the triangle. My feelings included surprise, disbelief, then awe & wonder, then denial, then acceptance. It was eerie to see the lights so close to the ground, moving so quickly and being so close to me, especially in such a heavily populated area. Others must have seen this object. The sighting was short, however, so it was fairly easy to put it out of my mind and carry on with my day. Nevertheless, here I am writing a witness report about the sighting.
