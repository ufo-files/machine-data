{cak} fishing on lake when object appeared

I was fishing on lake serene in catawissa mo when a red and orange circular object was spotted over the lake. being familiar w/ drones and the like i knew this was not the case. there was a pontoon on the lake and the young daughter 6-7 yelled "look daddy what is it". It was hovering at a speed of maybe 1-2 mph in a straight line fashion till it was out of sight. This was not a drone & I immediately thought ufo. nothing I have ever seen. My friend with me also witnessed it and swears we had just seen a ufo. we could not identify it as anything man made. I myself was a bit freaked out, thinking I hope its friendly and at the same time just amazed. In my 50's not on drugs and concidered pretty intelligent this was nothing I would consider man made or in the normal scope. I am happy that it continued on in a peaceful manner. I never thought I would ever in this life be documenting something of this magnitude. I saw what I saw and am not afraid to share that
