{jpg} MUFON # 94689 In all probability high altitude balloon from Holloman AFB, NM.

I worked at Holloman AFB, NM for a 12 years as a civilian contract
Photographer/Cinematographer, and photographed the launch of a large number of high altitude balloons from launch at Holloman to recovery of the payload wherever it landed. At near 100,000 feet the package was released and floated down on a parachute to be recovered by Air Force personnel. 
Many balloons floated West and this is probably one of the balloons. We recovered the pay-loads near Deming several times when they drifted off the WSTF Range. The balloon was expendable.
The shape is exactly what you could see from the ground when the balloon Helium has expanded at high altitude, some reaching 300 feet across There would be a payload hanging from the balloon which might not be visible from he ground depending on it's size.
I think if you check with the Balloon Branch of the Air Force at Holloman AFB, you will find that there was probably a High Altitude launch within a day of the sighting. They are usually launched at night or early morning from Holloman when the wind is calm. The morning wind usually carried them West.
