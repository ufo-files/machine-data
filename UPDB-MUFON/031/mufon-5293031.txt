A very large golden cylinder with blue edges standing straight up and not moving. Much larger than any star, and just above the treetops.

In the early morning hours of Saturday July 18th I stepped out my front door and immediate saw a very large glowing cylinder in the sky to my east. It was casting a golden light, with faint blue around it's edges, and it was low in the sky but above the treetops.. It was standing on end. I observed it for more than 20 minutes and it didn't move. I attempted to take pictures of it, but they only show a white ball.  

It was not the comet as it was right straight east of me. It wasn't a balloon because it was still dark outside, and it never moved. I have no idea what I saw, so I am reporting it here.
