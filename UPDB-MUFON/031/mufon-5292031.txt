UFO with strange noise at the end

Sitting in my backyard when I observed a white circular shape rapidly starting across the sky. Pulled out my phone to record and got video of this unidentified object. After watching the video the next day I realized that near the end of the video there's a strange noise that seems to come from the object that I did not hear while I was recording. I was only able to hear it during a playback of the video.
