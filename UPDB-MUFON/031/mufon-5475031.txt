{cak} Two large balls of light appeared in southern sky. Disappeared after abiut 30 seconds

I was sitting on my back porch lookin at the southern sky about 1:30 am. Suddenly 2 large spheres of light appeared side by side. They hovered for about 30 seconds then disappeared. The one on the right (west) appeared to move slightly to the east. They caught my attention because, relative to the stars ans planets, they were so large and bright. Their appearance startled me and their sudden disappearance startled me even more.
