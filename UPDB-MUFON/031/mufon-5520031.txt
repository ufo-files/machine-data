On March 16, 2012 between 5:00 and 6:00 am I was retrieving my morning paper from the driveway and noticed a big bright circular white light above my neighbors roof. I thought it was strange because there was no sound but quickly dismissed it and closed my garage door. I then put the leash on my dog and went out back heading to the southern side of the house. There it was again only floating south instead of east on first sighting. I was looking at it when it skipped to the right real fast. I turned my head torward my dog and then back and it was gone. There was no sound at all and appeared to be fairly low to ground. I have no idea what it was, but the night was completely clear.
