Walking dog-partial clear sky (clouds rolling by-windy)- I always look up-as I love astronomy-I see what I think is a star (very bright, almost pulsating)- at about 45degrees facing east, Venus?-it had fuzzy edges (to me)- and rear facing "arm like appendages" on top. I let dog in and asked wife and daughter to get shoes on and come out to help me clarify what the heck I am seeing?-They see it also. I took cell phone video, with audio description of what we are "seeing". I ran in to get binoculars-this is where it gets weird-the craft is dinner plate shaped with "indented like pie plate" look to it. The "arms" I though I saw was actually the craft TURNING UP ON IT'S SIDE while hovering (total 51 minute of observation (intermittent because of clouds rolling by-very windy here tonight).Big Dipper on my left upper, Orion on my right rear upper, and near full Moon just up east in tree tops. At least 10 other jets, planes going by in different directions (we have airport 3 miles away-and major airports within one hour or three hours from here)-this thing defies ANYTHING I've ever seen anything do on a very windy night-up in the sky. We felt awe, and my wife was unnerved, my daughter a little of both, as myself. lost sight when stars now obscured by clouds.
