Weird object seen from my balcony

I was outside on my balcony around 4:30 in the morning when I saw a strange light in the sky. I looked closely and saw that it was stationary, I could see it was changing colors. It was white at first but I saw it change to blue, green, red, purple, etc. I couldn’t really get a photo of it, since it was nighttime and it was definitely a few miles away from me at least. I saw the figure zig zag up and down, side to side, any direction. It was circular and just kinda hovered there. This lasted over an hour, until the sun started to come up. I could no longer see any stars, but I could see the object, eventually I just couldn’t see it anymore.
