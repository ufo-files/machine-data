Saw bright lights directly overhead & had missing time

I was at one of my best friend's house for a backyard campfire & drinks. It was us and our husbands (six of us). All good friends. 
We hadn't been there long - us girls went into the house once to gather drinks before going to the fire - we're all sitting around the fire, laughing, chatting. All of a sudden, there are bright white large lights right over top of us...... NO SOUND. kind of up and to the right, like an arc, maybe 6-7 initially but then SUPER BRIGHT WHITE LIGHT RIGHT ON TOP OF US. 

My next memory is: I'm sitting in my seat, still at the fire, head is down, hands are palm up resting loose in my lap, the ground all around me is completely lit up, bright white. 

Then suddenly we're just all sitting around the fire, the lights are gone. I'm immediately questioning everyone -- what were those lights? Everyone is like out of it, quiet, doesn't know what I'm talking about, doesn't remember seeing lights..... I'm incredulous - what do you mean you didn't see those lights! They were RIGHT on top of us!! How could you not see them??
Kristi is the only person who remembers this night and the mysterious lights.... she said she saw some lifts but surmised it was a meteor that burned out right over top of us...... but that doesn't explain my memory of the ground completely bathed in light - if it was a meteor, it would have burned out too fast to light up the entire area like that.... and so this has been something I wonder about often. Also, as Kristi and I go inside the house and I'm excitedly talking about "what was those lights", we go inside to use the bathroom and see on her microwave clock in the kitchen that at least 45 minutes or more has passed by and we both remarked that's strange, time has down by, and we just got here! 

But since no one else recalls the lights, it's all dismissed and we continue our night with drinks by the fire. Super weird night. I've never experienced anything else like it.
