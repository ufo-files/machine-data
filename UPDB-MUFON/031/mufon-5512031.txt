{dr}Observed from car 5 lights in curve formation floating above us with no sound and disapeared into the clouds

My husband and I were heading east on hwy 20 when I spotted some lights in the sky that I had never seen before in that part of the sky. I brought them to my husband attention and he confirmed that they were indeed strange. We drove a little further and drove under the lights and pulled off the road to watch them. They looked liked stars that were lower in the sky than any stars could be and they were moving very slowly with no sound. They moved off to the the north and disappeared into the clouds.
