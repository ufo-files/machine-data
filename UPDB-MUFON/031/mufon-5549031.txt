EGGSHAPED ORB

WAS WITH A FRIEND WALKIN THE DOG BY LANGNES AIRFIELD, IT WAS A CLEAR SKY, NO MOON , NO WIND, WAS LOOKIN FOR SATELITES ND SHOOTING STARS WHEN WE SAW AN EGGSHAPED OBJECT CRUISING BY IN ABOUT 200 MPH. IT LOOKED LIKE BRUSHED ALUMINIUM , NO SOUND ND NO LIGHT CAME FROM IT. IT WAS BETWEEN 100-200 FEET AWAY ND ABOUT 3-6 FEET IN DIAMETER.. THE OBJECT FLEW IN A STRAIGHT LINE AND NOT MORE THN 60 FEET UP IN THE AIR, IT DISAPERED AFTER ABOUT 5 SEC. WE WAS STUNNED OF WAT WE HAD SEEN, IT WASNT LIKE ANYTHING WE HAD SEEN BEFORE
