{tjd} Zig-zagging, bouncing and zooming across the sky

Let the pups out at 6am today, looked up at the sky (was surprised it was actually clear for once) happy to see a few stars. As I was waiting on the pups I noticed a bright distant object (l thought it was a star) beginning to move. I assumed my eyes were playing tricks on me, but the object began to rapidly zig-zag back and forth, shooting across the sky in some kind of pattern. It also would rapidly descended, (looked like it was falling) but then shot up and across the sky. This went on for 16 - 18 minutes.
I had my coat (which contained my iPhone) and was able to get 4 videos and several photos. It was still pitch dark at 6AM, so I aimed my phone at the sky and just hit record. When I came inside I had to bump up the brightness ALL the way on my phone to see the videos clearly... but the thing is most certainly there zipping around! I ended up staying outside for almost 20 minutes watching it ping pong across the sky.
