Subject
Old sighting by myself as a child
Message
Around 1968 a friend and I were riding our bikes on an gravel road. We were stopped and just talking when out of the corner of my eye I saw a gray cigar shaped aircraft. I turned and looked and it was still there. I watched it slowly move until it disappeared behind the trees. My friend also saw it. It was approximately five miles from our position. Once it disappeared we never saw it again. It looked like a Zeppelin. Once we made it back home we were glued to the TV hoping to hear what it was. None of the two channels said anything about such a Zeppelin. At that point we were convinced we saw a UFO/UAP. This has been the only sighting I ever encountered. I consider myself as one who is accustomed to looking at the skies.
