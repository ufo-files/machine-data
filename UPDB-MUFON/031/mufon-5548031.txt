White Blimp

I was walking home from school as a kid with my friend. We noticed an object that seemed very high up that was blimp-shaped and white in color. I don't remember what made us look. It was the middle of the day and there wasn't a cloud in the sky. Also, there were a couple of what looked to be fighter jets following the object at a distance. This sound of the jets may have been why we looked up to begin with, but I really can't remember. We both watched for a minute or so and agreed we had seen a UFO. Neither of us made a big deal out of it, and to be honest I've never really grasped what I may have seen until now. To this day, my friend and I still agree about what we saw. I've never taken the time to officially report it, and I figured I would.
