{kt}Saw it, then it disappeared. Hour later doz f16 in sky.

Sitting on my deck steps a couple of weeks before thanksgiving, watching my husband rake leaves and watching the turkey buzzards gliding in the sky. Noticed a football shaped object right above the buzzards. Thinking to myself it was tilted in such a way it looked like a football and laughed. Then I looked to the right and looked at another group of buzzards for about 10 seconds. But I was suddenly curious about the object and wanted to see what shape it really was. It was gone! I jumped up and yelled at my husband I think something just crashed. Im standing there looking for signs of smoke. Nothing! Now my husband is looking at me like I am crazy. I am walking back an forth like a crazy person. Thinking if that wasnt a plane what was it? It looked like a football, with a very black area on the bottom, above that was what looked like the sun shining very brightly and the rest looked like stainless steel. I said to my husband it must have been a ufo. I was not prepared for the disbelief I was faced with. So now I call my daughter who lives next door a couple acres away and tell her. I am smacked with disbelief again. Wow! An hour later my daughter calls me and tells me to go outside and look at the sky. There were at least a dozen F-16 in the sky. The sky was crisscrossed with their tail smoke. Have never seen it look like that before and never seen it look like that since. Watched the newspaper and news for days. Not a mention of anything. Fort Bragg is about 20 mi from where I live. I don't like you mufon and only report this cause I promised Chris Bledsoe I would.
