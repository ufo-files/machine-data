UFO Hovering Over Park w/Strobe Light

I was driving up highway 101 in San Luis Obispo, California when my passenger and I saw a very bright light that flashed on for a fraction of a second, then off for about 6 seconds the whole time we saw it. As we got closer to the source of the light, I noticed that the light was coming from the top of an object that was hovering about level with the trees surrounding Cuesta Park field. The object looked like it was hovering directly over the south end of the field.
