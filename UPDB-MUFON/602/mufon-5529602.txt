bright strobe like lights red, green, and amber

We were driving on Cobb prky going NW. In the sky were what appeared to be six strobe lights. one was about a mile away from the others. Then you had 3 that made a triangular shape another a few feet away from the nose point of the triangle and then one just above tree level. They were strobing in red, green, and amber lights. They were stationary for a good 30 min. I was on my way to dinner. By the time we came back out from dinner about 45min later the lights were gone. I tried to take a picture with my camera phone but they were to far away.
