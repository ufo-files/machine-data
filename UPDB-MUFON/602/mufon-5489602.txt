I was photographing our full, August moon. Experimenting with various distances and exposures. I didn't see the object before, during, or after photographing the moon. I went outside to see what it might have been but nothing made sense. While I'm not believing I saw a UFO, I assume this would be the organization most likely to tell me what I'm seeing in the photo.
