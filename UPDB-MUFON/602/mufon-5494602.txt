{rjl} Metallic hamburger shaped ufo over cloudy skies.

I was at work waiting to punchout and the guys were playing football , I was following the football as it was thrown in the air and that's when I noticed it . At first I thought it was a metallic ballon but then I realized that it was traveling at a high rate of speed like a plane would . It was simply flying in a straight path , it had no wings and seemed to glide through the air but what was most peculiar was its shape . This may sound crazy but it appeared to have a metallic hamburger type of shape ! I had a clear view of it for a couple of seconds as it traveled in a patch of blue sky and then it was barely visible as it went into another cloud formation and disappeared . I asked my co-worker to look up in the direction of the object but to no avail it was gone ! My friend said he believed because he had witnessed a ufo as a teen . Also there were several planes that passed by in the same general are but none of them were flying above the clouds as the the ufo was .
