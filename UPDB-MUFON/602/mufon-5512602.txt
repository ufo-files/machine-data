(GS) I was driving when I saw two diagonal from each other with a third lower on the horizon. The low one disappeared then the two above hovered for a few minutes. One disappeared first, then the other.

I was driving down Clayton Road when I noticed weird, red lights in the sky. There was an airplane flying as well in the same area and these lights looked nothing like it. They were far too high in the sky to be helicopters. The event lasted for several minutes in which the three lights disappeared one after the other without about a minute or two in between each disappearance. They looked like red stars, thats the only way I can explain it. They were just hovering in the sky and then I watched them disappear. I saw people outside their houses looking up at the sky after this happened.
