Triangular shape accompanied by orb X2

Saw what looked like a large bank of electric lights at a distance, in the sky. Originally thought they might be parking lot lights from an auto dealership a couple miles off that were elevated on a hill and just “looked” like they were in the sky. After driving a bit further saw this was not nor could not be the case. Pulled over in a rest stop to get photos. Took 2 with an additional light visible in photos but not to the naked eye. What at first looked like a bank of electric lights from one angle, now was obviously triangular in shape. Lights eventually (couple of minutes) faded out. As I was pulling my car out of the parking spot another triangular bank of lights came into view coming from the north. Took 2 more photos. Another, second light appeared in photo that was not visible to the naked eye.
