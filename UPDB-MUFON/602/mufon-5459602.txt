Was working just west of SeaTac airport in Seattle. Happened to look up & this object that had no wings, blades, parachute... was hovering within the airport flight cone. After I got a few pics & a video it shot off in a NE heading almost too fast for my eyes to track. From start to finish was about 4 minutes. When it shot off it was instantly at breakaway velocity. -note: the plane taking off went between me & the unidentifiable craft.
