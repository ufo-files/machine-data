Saw a chevron formation of about 16-20 lights in S

At approximate 8:25 PM Central, 3-20-2004. I had just returned from the movies and I paused just outside my front door in SE Alabama for a minute or two to admire the clear starry night sky. I first noticed the UFO when it was directly overhead - it was a formation of about 16-20 lights, forming a slightly irregular outline of a chevron or boomerang, about twice as wide as a full moon, travelling north I think. It disappeared over the horizon in only about 5 seconds. I thought at first maybe it was a very low-flying ultralight, except it was travelling way too fast and it was completely silent, and I got the impression that it was at a very high altitude.
