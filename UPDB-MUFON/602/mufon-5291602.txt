Light went from south to North then stopped

I was jogging at a park and decided to stop and do some sit ups on a bench. I looked to my left side and noticed a bright light heading from south and moving north across the sky. The light suddenly stopped and started to slowly go up as the sun was coming up. Then it shot straight up at a fast speed and right out of our atmosphere. I watched the craft until it was completely gone.
