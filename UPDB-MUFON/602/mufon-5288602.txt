Playing golf with three strangers. Wind at over 20 mph. Took pictures and video of object above McCarran airport. Object did not move at all in wind

While playing golf at Bali Hai in Las Vegas with three strangers, I observed, took a photo and a video of a stationary object which was directly above McCarran airport. I watched it for a full minute before getting my phone out to record it. After I stopped recording, I glanced up and it was there. Then it just disappeared. My video shows that even on a clearly windy day, the object never moved.
