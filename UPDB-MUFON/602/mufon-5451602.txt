Spinning black cube(s)

30, May, 2020 just before 5PM, city of Van Nuys, Ca. Shows one black cube, spinning on 3 axis (tumbling, rotating, spinning) slowly. Rising from about 400ft up into the over cast sky to about 2000ft. There was a total of 7 to 9. They regrouped and separated, moving in different directions, moving against the wind. One one in the photo is about the size of a VW bug. It was lower than the arrivals flight path to Burbank airport. I could see them on the screen of my phone and this was the only photo that captured an object.
