Saw a large hovering disc then pixelated and disappeared

I was driving south and east on the Sam Houston tollway. I saw a plane about a mile in front of me and heading northeast toward IAH airport. To the left of the plane I saw a large silver disc hovering about 2,000 feet almost straight above me. I could see it out of my rolled down window. The bottom of the disc was at about a 45 degree angle to the ground.and looked like it was turning slightly. I could see it sort of had a dome top but I couldn’t see any windows also no writing on it. Completely silver metallic.
I was driving , looking at it and yelling OMG . I was scared! After about 60 seconds it looked like it pixelated out and disappeared. Then I really got freaked out.
It was so close that I could see it well . I would say it was 100-200 feet in diameter.I was so freaked out I didn’t tell my family for 2 weeks. It sounded so crazy. I’ve never seen anything like that before that or since.
