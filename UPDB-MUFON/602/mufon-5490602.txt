{nd} driving on why 528. saw helicopters. fighter jet following why turned to areas where helicopters were. silent object came from that area. It was so quiet I could hear crickets.

I was on my way to work on 8/3/15 when I saw helicopters off to my keft, about 6. A fighter jet flew by on my right, about 20 feet above the trees when it turned left and flew south to the helicopters.  I stopped my car to watch. I don't know why I did that. I saw another jet fly towards the helicopters.  About 30 seconds later an object approached from the south where the helicopters were. It headed north right above my head. It was rectangular and had 2 large lights on opposite ends in the front. It flew directly above my head and did not make a sound. It was dark not giving off light. It was about a foot above the tallest tree. It has a tiny lt on thee bottom. I was shaken. I've never seen anything like it or a craft move that quietly. It moved north and the trees blocked my view.
