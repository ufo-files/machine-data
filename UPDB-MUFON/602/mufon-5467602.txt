It had a bright white light

I was with my cousin driving home on the QEW (Queen Elizabeth Highway) I looked to my left and seen a bright white light hovering in the sky.I thought at first it was a helicopter until it started to get farther away at a rapid speed and the light was still bright, never seen a helicopter with a bright light on the back, than we drove under an overpass and it was gone! it just vanished out of nowhere..
