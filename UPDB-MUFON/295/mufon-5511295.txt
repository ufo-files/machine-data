{dr}flying craft

About 9:00 pm my husband called me and our children outside to observe 2 objects facing west and coming from south ascending north. They were bright red/orange and seemed to be blinking. The first one reached a certain height and seemed to just disappear. Then the next one disappeared then 3 more came from the same direction. All of them seemed to just disappear. My husband took a photo and it looks like a ball of fire in the photo. There were airplanes that were flying higher than the fireball looking objects. They were clearly different.
