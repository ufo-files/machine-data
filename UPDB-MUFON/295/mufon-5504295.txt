{cak} A blue-white light moving extremely fast, side to side with moments of hovering. Left in blink of an eye.

My father and I were driving to pick up prescriptions at a local pharmacy when out of nowhere a blue-white light suddenly appeared on the horizon. It was moving awkardly and hovering. We observed it as we went into the parking lot and parked the car. We walked a little bit closer to it, mostly so I could try and see if I could get a definitive shape of the object. It started to move closer to us so we decided to play it safe and went into the pharmacy to pick up the medication. When we left it wasn't there anymore. About ten minutes later we left a nearby Starbucks and I spotted it to our right which was eastward. It was hovering mostly then all of a sudden accelarated to blazing speeds, I would put it between 500-600 mph, and was gone in the blink of an eye. Right before it disappeared I got a little bit of a closer look and saw the light coming from the bottom of the craft and the craft had an oval/hat like shape to it. On the way home we saw multiple airplanes so we know for a fact it wasn't that. The object also emitted no sound, it was dead quiet. I'm very positive this is nothing that the common person knows. This object moved way too fast to be a conventional plane, made no noise which a helicopter would do and was visible both in the twilight and in the dark.
