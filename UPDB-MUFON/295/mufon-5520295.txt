White and grey disc object hovering in sky in the afternoon

I was riding in the car with my mom at around 4:30 pm. We had art club at school, so my mom had to come pick me up. A few minutes from home, I noticed in the sky a white/gray disc over the trees. I noticed because, well, you never see that stuff around here. I pointed it out to my mom, but she said "Yeah, ok" and didn't even try to look. She probably didn't believe me because I talk about this kind of stuff all the time. It looked like it was just hovering over the trees and as soon as I noticed it seemed to turn the direction we were driving and fly off suddenly. I couldnt see where it went because the trees were too tall and we were already home by then. When I first saw it, my heart raced, but I wasnt scared. I was actually excited because I love this kind of stuff, as I said before. Now Im happy because Ive always wanted to see a UFO.
