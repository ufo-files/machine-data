triangle shaped UFO

I was driving to work, it was 5:45Am and completely dark I was on a quiet street by the lake...I saw a cluster of bright white lights. I thought it was a helicopter. As I got closer I realized it was a triangular shaped object with white lights at the corners and a red blinking light in the back, The object was only maybe 200 feet in the air and just hovered there. It was completely silent, I rolled down my car windows as I approached where the object was. There was not a noise to be heard, no birds, bugs, the air wasnt moving. As I drove toward the object it began to turn and slowly began to drift away toward the lake. I felt nervous approaching the object because I had to drive under it to continue driving, I parked my car and just stared at the object while it moved away although I don't remember seeing it actually leave, I just saw that it was gone when I realized 17 minutes had passed. When I first saw the object my clock said 5:48am, when I realized the object was gone it was 6:05am. I felt dazed and distracted and couldnt place what had happened. I went into work and immediately began to draw what I had seen. I almost felt obsessed with having to get down what I had seen. I also felt the need to just stand outside like I thought it was coming back. I had to focus on how I was acting if others saw me, I wasnt acting crazy, Just dazed.
