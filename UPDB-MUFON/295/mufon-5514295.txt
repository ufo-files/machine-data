(GS) Orange object in east. Just hovering, not moving. Then it moved west.

I saw an orange orb glimmering in the east, not moving at approximately a penny in size from my view point. I thought it may have been a tower light until it slowly started to move west. As the object floated to the west, it lost its orange glow and appeared as a cylinder shaped craft. It went directly over my head, over my house towards the capital building in Lansing, MI. It was just above the tree tops. Ive seen many odd flying objects, but this is the first time Ive felt an overwhelming feeling of dread. Very ominous.
