{nd} Change color from a saturated orange back to white. Moved very slowly stopping twice. I reported when it happened on NUFORC but wanted it here too.

I went out of our house to get the dog and there was a saturated orange colored object just below cloud cover basically in front of the house. It moved very slowly from north to south. We are 1/10th of a mile from the ocean so it could have been moving south from Town Neck Beach in Sandwich. The strangest thing about it was about after 3 minutes it turned white for about 45 seconds, and then turned back to the deep orange. It's edges were clearly delineated, and I watched for the full 4.5 minutes until it disappeared into the cloud cover. I was thrilled, not scared at all. I did not sense any kind of harmful intent. In fact, I wondered if it might be some kind of alien drone as I did not get a sense of any living connection either--but that's completely subjective. I also have no idea of the size as it was below cloud cover during the time I was watching it and I don't know how low the clouds were that day. I feel very lucky to have seen this ship. And when I looked at NUFORC sighting logs, there had been two sightings previously, both a few days before, but not on the same day. One was in Barnstable and the other was in Sandwich, MA.
