Black triangle escorted by 2 helicopters in Utah

It was a day light, middle of the day, summer time.
Me and my wife was driving south on I-15 in Utah county, UT (around Orem-Provo area).
I looked east to the mountains and i saw a black triangle hovering escorted by two helicopters.
I was so excited and told my wife to watch it and she saw it just right before they disappeared behind the mountains hills.
The event was few seconds before they disappeared and unfortunately i didn't have time to record it.
I have 8 years of experience with aviation, i was drones technician and instructor in Israel, it didn't seem like anything i saw before.
