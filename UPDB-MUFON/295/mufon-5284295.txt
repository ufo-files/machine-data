Red and white orbs at night

It was July, and very hot this night.  For some unknown reason, I woke up (2:33am) and went straight for my bedroom window and couldn't believe i seen what I just seen.  A red and white ball of light came flying out from behind some trees across the street from me. The white ball, just went straight but the red ball of light, took a sharp curve, was a little above the height of the trees, came flying in between my house and my neighbors. There was absolutely no sound coming from either ball of light. And they were close enough that there is no way to mistake the identity of them. 
But what I don't understand is why I would wake up from a dead sleep, and walk over to my window? That is not normal for me.  And then to see 2 balls of light.
