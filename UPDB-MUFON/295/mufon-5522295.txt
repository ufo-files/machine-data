A triangle craft with a bright white light on each cornor and a pulsating red light in the center.

I was trying to get to sleep one night, staring out my bedroom window, when suddenly I saw a bright light shining through the trees. I didn't know what it could be, so I got up to see what it was. Suddenly, a triangle craft flew over the trees and my house. It was moving slowly, so I was able to watch it for about 2 minutes before it disappeared from sight. It was flying lower than most aircraft. It had a bright white light on each cornor and a pulsing (NOT blinking) red light in the center. It made no noise and didn't emit any exhaust. I don't know what it was, but my belief in the supernormal was comfirmed when I saw it.
