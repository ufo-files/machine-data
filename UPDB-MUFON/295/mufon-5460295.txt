At approximately 12:19am I let my dog out to go, well pee. I was sitting on my patio and the dog dropped to the ground and wouldn't come. I was sitting in a patio chair and I was looking at the stars and I noticed one dot of light that pulsed.... like on and off. I called my dog and it wouldn't come. He had his head flat on the ground. The light appeared to change altitude three times... Then it lit up and I could see boomerang shape in all white light. It then appeared to wink out, but could be seen for a few seconds as a dot of light going what appeared to be straight up. Dog won't go outside right now.
