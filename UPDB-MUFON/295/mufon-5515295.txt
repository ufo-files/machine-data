I was at work and had gone outside for a break. I was admiring Venus which was due east. I noticed a light a little to the south and when I turned to look I realized there were two lights, one right behind the other one. They were identical and a little less brighter than Venus. They looped around as they had been coming from the east and then they headed north at a leisurely pace. After about ten seconds they both disappeared. The lights just switched off at the same time. It was still quite dark, I couldnt see any crafts the lights could have belonged to. They emitted no noise at all. I stood out there for another ten minutes, but they didn't reappear.
