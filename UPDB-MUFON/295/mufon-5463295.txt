I was outside running my dogs in the morning. It was a very windy day so I was filming the wind to show my husband what he had missed while gone hunting in Montana. As I started to film, I heard a crash, which I think was just something being blown over.I turned my phone quickly to see what it was and suddenly heard leaves blowing too. I showed it to my husband a few days later when he got home, which was my first time watching as well. We saw a green glowing ball like object fly in from the North (just as I hear a crash) land to the left front of me then kind of roll out of site. A second or two later it bounces back in to sight coming from the South. Meanwhile the wind is Easterly. We zoomed in on a few still shots we made from the video and it was glowing and reflecting on the ground. When this light flew in the sky it got much darker. As I stated I never watched this until my husband got home. If I had watched this earlier I would of left and got a hotel!!
