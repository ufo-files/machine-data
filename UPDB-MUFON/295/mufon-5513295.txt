{dr}Friend was driving saw light in field, It came over road and hovered over us for a distance then shot off.

I was riding with a friend from Monett Mo., to Springfield Mo. on Hwy.60. We were about a mile or two south of Aurora Mo. Off to the east side of the Highway I saw a very bright light in a field. First I thought it must be a combine, because it was very large and it was so bright, also its location in a field. It moved slowly toward the road I continued to watch it because it seemed to be moving towards us and when it reached the edge of the field it was right with us and continued over us. It was so bright it was hard to see much detail, but you could tell it was oval shape with what looked like a dome top. I don't know if it had lights or if it was it that was glowing. The dome seemed to glow at a slightly different glow or color then did the oval body. It was very close to us, and stayed right with us for about 1/4 mile or more. It then continued on its NW direction leaving rapidly turning 90 degrees and going straight up and gone. Neither of us had said a word the entire time, I did not want to influence my friends memory with mine ,so I asked him what he had seen, It was exactly what I had seen. We decided not to tell any one so others would not have negative opinion of us. I did however tell my father about it, and he told me that was strange because just a couple weeks before he had read an article in the Springfield News Paper about a girl Who had a similar encounter in the same area as ours, but in her case her car lost all power headlights etc. until the UFO left then her power returned. It has been a long time since this happened and Im sure I have forgotten some things, but when something like this happens its to forget. m just guessing about the date, but it is close.
