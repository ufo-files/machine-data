{nd} The 6 -7 orange glowing UFOs formed the Big Dipper. They hovered and moved across the sky to ascend into the atmosphere.

My boyfriend and I both were witnesses to this UFO sighting in OFallon,MO in Patriots Landing subdivision on August 24, 2013. All 6 - 7 of the glowing orange "objects" seemed to be flying somewhat low. They formed the what looked exactly like the Big Dipper. That is what caught our eyes. I got some footage of the event, although it is not very good. The best footage only lasts about 20 seconds. I did a couple of not so great photos. Each UFO hovered and smoothly went across the sky. At some pre-determined point, (so it seemed), each one went up to the sky and vanished. It was like a fade-to-sky acension.
