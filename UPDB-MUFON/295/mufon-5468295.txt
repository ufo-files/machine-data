{td} US 12 Bertrand, Michigan

I was headed to Central Indiana on us.12 from Bertrand, Michigan, when I witnessed a round, bright, white light in the sky in front of me. The sky was a cloudy gray that evening (scattered storms). When I seen the light, I was almost frozen on it. It hovered above, and then I literally seen it move back in forth, and it retreated straight back into the clouds. I did not see it again. 

On the fourth picture, which Is cropped, you can see a second light to the lower right of the first one. The one in the middle is the one that I could see. I did not see the 2nd on until I had zoomed in. I circled both.
