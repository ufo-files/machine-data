{rjl} Totally black triangle shape with one strong light in the front as it's coming towards us flew overhead and flew past us clearly clearly saw the shape

Heading north one night around 10:30 p.m. from Santa Fe, NM going towards the Pueblo of pojoaque. Saw a light from the Northeast I could have sworn it was a helicopter searchlight but it stayed positioned right in front heading towards us as we're driving on the highway. As it came closer saw that it was a huge football length triangle black object with a powerful light in front, no other lights. came over me as I'm driving my car slowed down to take a look at it went in front as far as I could to see it pass over head around a hundred feet over and then kept on going towards the Southwest with no noise just floating in a straight line with no exhaust until it disappeared to my left. Classic black triangle object.
