Silver TIC TAC object reflecting sunlight moving slowly my right to my front, looked down and the looked up, it had disappeared in a clear sky.

Traveling south on Interstate 35 early in the morning just after sunrise near Devine, Texas, noticed a small silvery pill shaped object far away in the lower sky in front of me. It was moving slowly from my 1 to 2 o’clock on my right to almost in front of me. It was reflecting the sunrise behind me. It appeared to be light aircraft sized object some miles away. By apparent low altitude and slow speed I was thinking it  might be a crop duster or maybe small helicopter. It showed some evidence of surface detail but was too far away to discern what. As I was thinking I was starting to drive close enough to figure out what it was, what is strange about this is that as I looked down about 1or 2 seconds to take a bite of a breakfast taco , when I looked up it was gone. Totally. I thoroughly scanned the clear sky for some time and it was empty. I believed if it had been an aircraft or object that had turned away so as not to reflect sunlight It was dimensional enough that I would have still seen its tail end. And it was high enough that it could not have landed in the time I looked away. It is the strangeness of this quick disappearance that has led me to report this in the hopes that someone else saw it in a confirming report.
