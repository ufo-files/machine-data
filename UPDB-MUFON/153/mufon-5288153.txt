4 bright white round lights equally spaced apart, hovering over swamp, then flying slowly.

While in northern Minnesota during muzzleloader season back in 2005. Bright light observed over Huntersville WMA. Light remained in place for around 15 minutes over swamp land, which then flew directly over myself. Could not make out any shape since it was pitch black outside. Flying object was silent as it flew, was only slightly higher than the tree tops, and made a hard right bank to head north. There was an obvious twin propped airplane which was in the area, the pilot had to have seen it as well. Have not ever seen any other reports of this instance from the area. I can honestly say it was the strangest/creepiest thing I have ever seen.
