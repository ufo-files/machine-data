I was in my dorm room looking out at the window when I saw a weird object hovering about 700 feet away from me. It had really bright, white lights, and it looked like they were 3 lights in form of balls formed into a triangle. It seemed small at first. I thought it was an airplane but as time passed it did not move, just stood there floating in the air. I kept staring at it, trying to see if it moved. A few seconds passed and it did not move, so I called my roommate who was in the room next to mine. The exact minute she looked at it it started to move. My roommate thought it was a drone, but it looked too big to be one. I thought it was coming in my direction, but it turned right. When it turned, I took my phone to zoom in and it was like there were multiple little lights in form of tennis balls were in the UFO forming a certain shape, with a light flickering behind it. 
I was really curious about what it was because I see airplanes all the time here, but this was different. After the object turned right, it left my sight. As it left, it looked like it was bigger than when I first saw it.
