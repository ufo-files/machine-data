{rjl} Bright dot starlike in daylight

I have witnessed twice at about 1-4 pm plain daylight a floating/hovering object resembling a shiny star that appears to randomly emit or reflect sunlight and slowly move towards the east of the Franklin Mountains in El Paso TX. I have taken pictures of the object next to passing airplanes with a 40D Cannon camera and 135 mm zoom lens. I have searched the internet and have found other witnesses who have posted videos of the same object
