Teardrop/missile shaped craft moving fast

My mother, father, and daughter were driving on I69 (the Western Kentucky Parkway) going west at approximately 1:30pm on Friday afternoon 1/15/2021 when my mother, who just called me to tell me about it, saw a craft right when they were passing the Providence/Dawson Springs 1 Mile Exit Sign for Exit 92. She said she saw something in the sky to the southwest and moving fast. As it got closer, she saw it did not have any wings and was long like a missile but pointed in the back like a long teardrop, but missile shaped. It was traveling at a high rate of speed and about 100 feet above them. Solid, black. Mom pointed for dad to look at it while he was driving, but by the time he looked, it was already passing them, over their van, and heading south. Daughter was watching TV and saw nothing. Mom was afraid and had to call me after dad stopped at a store in Princeton, KY. We see UFOs all the time in Hanson, out in the country, on any given day, not just starry filled nights. New shape. I've never seen a teardrop shape. Just cigar shapes and orbs.
