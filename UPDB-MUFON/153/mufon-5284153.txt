2 white light spheres. One statioary and one moving from side to side

I was sitting on my terrace looking out over the water at night. I saw what I thought to be 2 helicopters with their spotlights turned towards me. One light stayed in the same position while the other light moved from side to side going toward the one light then moving away from it. They were on the same level plane while this was happening. I watched this for about 90 seconds before the clouds moved in and obstructed me view of them. The clouds were broken up and had sections of clear sky. When the clouds were in front of the lights blocking my vision, there was also another bank of clouds beyond the lights and they were dramatically lit up from the light spheres. To the point of turning the black clouds almost a glowing white. The clouds then moved in and that was the last I saw of the spheres.
