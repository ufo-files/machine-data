{jpg} Glowing orb approached living room window, went outside and observed orb hovering 25' above patio, orb slowly ascended then dissapeared

At 9:50pm eastern standard time myself and my mother and father were sitting in our living room watching television. My mother suddenly stated. "whats that?!" I looked out the window of our patio door and a bright orange/red orb shaped object was just outside and hovering some 25 feet in the air and had a yellow glowing aura around it. My father and I quickly went outside to observe the object. The orb hovered 25-30 feet above our heads as we watched it from our patio. It had approached the house from the east and traveled in a south-western path. I managed to video tape our observations from the point in time we had gone outside onto the patio. At this point my mother came outside as well. We watched the object for around 2 minutes, upon which point in time the object ascended straight up and suddenly vanished/dissapeared. The object was larger when first seen from inside the house, and upon walking outside it shrunk inside and also changed shape. It had a elongated shape when first viewed, but not with exact, sharp dimensions. Its shape looked as a stroke from a paint brush looks, and was surrounded by a glowing yellowish aura. When it shrank in size upon us going outside to view it, that is when it took the shape of a ball, sphere, orb. The object was viewed by myself, my mother and my father. No one was drinking or doing drugs.
