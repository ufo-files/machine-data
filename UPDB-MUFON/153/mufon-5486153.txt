black triangle. scalped edge. afternoon. no lights. silently glided overhead then gone.

I was living in Edmonton. In the city. It was a July afternoon around 4:30p. I was sitting on the stoop of my doorstep. I was reading the Internet on my smart phone. I just happened to look up and noticed a Solid Flat Black Triangle Craft just "Gliding" across the sky just over the tree top to my neighbor's house. It was about 4/5 story's high. It just slowly and specifically Silently, moved/glided through the air in a straight line. I was sittding off to the left of it, but practically underneath it. I never saw lights. I think due to the fact it was a beautiful sunny Sumner day. No clouds. The left side had a scalped edge (like a steak knife and a straight edge on the right). I am partially disabled and need a cane to walk, so I couldn't get up quickly enough to walk past the tree tops to continue to view it. And of course even with a cell phone in my hand, it was on the Internet news and I wouldn't have been able to click over to camera quick enough to take a photo. This sighting was extremely brief. But I saw what I saw and have tried to make sense of it since. Thank you. Suzi
