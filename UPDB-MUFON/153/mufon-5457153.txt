{cak} Viewed from my car hovering slowly over parkway entrance ramp.

I was just getting on to the Taconic State parkway, when I noticed a massive slow moving object flying above me. It was triangular boomerang shaped, with many windows, and I even remember wing lights that were flashing, one red, one blue, like normal plane lights. There was no sound. There were other cars getting on or off the highway but if anyone else noticed it they did not stop. I got the distinct odd feeling that this was... normal. I wanted to stop to investigate further but was worried about the safety of stopping on a curved highway entrance ramp as well as engaging whatever this thing might be... I didn't want to be captured... I wanted to go home. So I slowed down to view it but did nothing more, and kept driving home. I told my boyfriend about the incident but then practically forgot about it for almost over a year. The odd feeling of normalcy stated along with an erie desire to forget the whole incident...which is not like me since I do have an interest in UFO's. I'm fact it's because if that feeling I am making sure to report it, finally, a few years later. Apologies for the late report, I just figure better now than never.
