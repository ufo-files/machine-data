shimmering white/silver lights suddenly appeared in sky changing form rectangular to starlike 3 min approx disappeared

Our home is in the hills, homes have alot of acreage between them, my granddaughter noticed lights in sky through picture window in house over hills, not very high up in the sky, my daughter, grandson and I came to see what she was calling us for, we all observed rectangular/starlike objects which seemed detached from each other and connected at the same time, about 745am MT clear skies, very bright glowing white/silverish shimmering alternating appearance, rapid movement, stayed in one place and rapid movement of lights made it hard to get a fix on any more detail, lasted about 3 minutes, witnessed by 4 family members, instantly disappeared as we viewed, just poof and it was gone, I went outside about 10 minutes after disappearance and the air seemed electrically charged, fresh and had a lightness about it, noticed that clarity of landscape, colors and sounds of many birds seemed more vibrant and enhanced, lasted about one minute or so although the residual effect on me personally lasted throughout the day as far as feeling charged up, at first we felt a bit stunned by what we were seeing and we just viewed it in awe of how beautiful it looked as if time stood still, almost as if it was a normal occurence, what we observed was clear and beautiful, unprepared to video or take photo
