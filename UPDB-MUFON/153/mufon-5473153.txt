Lights near airport.

I just saw a fairly large light show UFO in Ottawa Ontario Canada. Maybe 100 yards from the Ottawa airport. I work at the airport and I am familiar with aircraft. At first it looked like search lights in the sky, I was driving. I pulled over and witnessed roughly 10 columns of large spread out light all moving towards a center, the light got smaller as they got closer to one another. Went on for a couple minutes. Couldn't see an object, just strange white lights. I tried to get a video but it was too dark. It was a good one.
