Strange green/orange light that changed direction and color

On March 20th, 2022, at approximately 2100 hrs, a large orange/green light appeared low in the sky near Goshen, OR. It slowly moved north towards south Eugene… Then hovered for 30 seconds… Then reversed flight path… Light turned blood red in color and disappeared behind Short Mountain landfill near I5. 

I checked with Hobby Field to see if it was one of their planes. Hobby Field is closed on Sundays at 1600 hrs. Incident was at 2100 hrs. 

I am a PhD, combat veteran, and commercial drone pilot. I have no explanation for what I saw.
