{jpg} Bright orange sphere hovering and performing corkscrew path

Husband and I were stargazing. I was pointing out the major stars in the sky when we both saw what almost appeared to be an orange star or planet just behind a tree in front of our home. Within the span of 2-3 minutes, the orangish-white light sphere moved from behind the treetops to center itself in a break between the trees. It then proceeded to perform a corkscrew type hovering pattern that was just barely discernible. The light sphere appeared to be rotating, and the object was so bright that it appeared to have small beams of light around it. At times, it would seem to vanish and reappear in a slightly different position.
