{nd} A bright stationary white light hovered. A 2nd red light passed by and left the area.

I happened to look out a window in my house and saw a very bright white light hovering in the southwestern sky. At first, I thought it was a helicopter, but I heard no sound. The light remained stationary and after about 15 minutes a red light approached from the south, passed near the white object, and proceeded northward until it left my field of view. The white object then proceeded in a northerly direction until I lost sight of it. I am hoping other people saw the event and will also file a report with MUFON.
