{nd} Flashing lights in loose formation, some switching places and some staying in precise proximity to one another flying northwest at a steady rate.

Driving home with wife around 10 PM eastern time, I saw objects that took up a big portion of the viewable sky and they were hovering and very bright, so they were noticeable. Individually they appeared to be almost like planes, but they were flying in close proximity to one another and kept formation. The light they emitted looked like they were almost like LED lights. They looked like a group of 6-10 objects flying in formation. They kept their distances to one another. They were blinking all the while - the light colors were purple, blue, red, and white. We were puzzled by the lights. They didn't look like anything we have ever seen, considering that in this area we normally see a lot of flying objects. We don't normally see objects that switch positions and cannot imagine seeing ones that do it so quickly while stopping on a dime as they do it either. We got out of the car to try to tail them until they were indistinguishable. Unfortunately, we didn't have a camera and we were not able to capture any documentation.
