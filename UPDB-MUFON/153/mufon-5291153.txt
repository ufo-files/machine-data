Four spheres moved close to my face and stopped appr. 5 inches from double glazing doors.

Looking out over the garden I observed four round balls entering the garden flying over the back gate. They stopped at the back of the garden in front of some pine trees between 5 or 6 foot of the ground. First they stood there for a few seconds in diamond formation then they started to circle around very fast in a oval formation. After a while, some forty or fifty second they stopped as of they observed me watching them and moved towards me in perfect formation closing in to each other while doing so. About 5 inches from the window they stopped and seem to observe me as I observed them. After 40 or 50 seconds they suddenly moved over the garden wall and out of sight.
