I saw the ufo appr. 9:00 PM in Ft. Pierce, Florida after eating dinner.

I was working at one of Florida Power and Lights Nuclear facilities on Hutchinson 
Island right off of A1A.
On a Friday early evening, I went home, changed and decided to go and drive to
Ft.Pierce to have dinner.
After dinner I stopped at a Gulf Station on US1 to pickup gas. There was commotion
at the bay next to me, the individual who were at the next bay were looking up toward, the sky I looked up there was a ufo directly overhead. 
The ufo was a circular object with a dull silver white glow. making abdrupt movements.

After filling up with gas I went over the Ft.Pierce Bridge and started driving home.
