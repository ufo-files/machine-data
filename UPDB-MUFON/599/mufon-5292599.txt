3 Bright Lights

Was driving down Trans Canada highway from Sault Ste. Marie to Parry Sound. About half way there I seen a bright light just hoovering near a tower looking thing, it was way in the distance, thought it was just a road light (which there is none) took forever to get up to it. Thought nothing of it, once I got closer I noticed it was 3 large very bright lights, on a triangle shaped dark thing in the sky. It was hoovering right next to the road on the east side. It moved over my car as I drove past, the radio went weird and there was a weird static feeling in the car and the air felt weird. I started to panic and drove super fast away tried calling my partner 3 times but the phone would not work. As I drove away it slowly moved to the west side of the road and seemed to follow me a bit. Then it just floated away.
