{dr}Orange light that changed directions and colors.

My wife and I were on our back deck after taking the dog outside. It was overcast and there were no stars or planes in the sky. The object was very bright and easily seen. This is the second time we have viewed this object within the last week. This time I had my phone. I have a pic and a 2 minute video of the object. The object was oval in shape. It was orange in color at first and then changed to red. The object came slowly from the east. It stopped and hovered for a few seconds. It then went to the left and right slightly. It proceeded to move backwards and headed in a northeastern direction until it was behind the trees and out of our view. When witnessed last week the object acted in the same manner. It came from the east and hovered only to move left and right slightly. Last time it headed back east but it faded out. It didn't go behind any trees or move to a distance not seen, it just disappeared. We were mesmerized and weren't quite sure what we had witnessed or what the object was.
