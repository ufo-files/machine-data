{dr}Silent Craft Approx 200-250ft altitude

Weather at location was approximately;

temperature 681-751
calm winds
low ceiling of around 200'-250'. 
(The ceiling height was estimated by comparison of the cloud ceiling to the 148' amatuer radio antenna tower at the noted location.)

A slowly strobing red light was first observed almost due north of the noted address. The object was estimated to be in the area of Kings Bay Naval Submarine Base moving slowly, approx 15 mph +/- towards the noted location. 

The strobing light would go dark, then slowly increase in brightness to its maximum then slowly decrease brightness until dark. This sequence would take approximately 6 to 8 seconds each time going dark to dark.

As the light came closer it was obvious that the there was an object with no defined shape moving towards the noted location. As it passed almost 901 overhead the object was completely silent, and was approximately 200'-250' above ground level. When the light would increase to full brightness the bottom of the object was illuminated which revealed a dark grey or black bottom which had a very, very slight concave curve. The edges of the object were not seen due to the cloud ceiling. However, the bottom hull of the object was clearly visible when illuminated by the red light. 

The hull of the object was almost flat with a slight convex appearance of the bottom. The illuminated area of the hull did not appear to be that of an airplane because the hull of an airplane would be cylinder or cigar shaped. The bottom of the object was illuminated in an equal 3601 pattern as would be consistent to a large, mostly flat surface and not tubular in any way.
