4-5 bright lights below airplane

I seem to be one of the few that has seen them from an airplane but down below the plane but above the clouds, I was at 35,000 feet and I estimate they were at 10,000 feet. I always look out the window -constantly. This one time I spotted some bright lights down below the plane. At first I thought it was a reflection on my glasses, then on the plane window, once I realized they were actually down there I watched them for about 45 seconds as we flew over. I estimate it was east of Atlanta and it was in the year 2000. When I got home I went on the internet and learned they were called Foo Fighters in WW2. They were stationary but going back and forth quickly. But taking turns. It was so weird. Now I am pretty sure they were UFO's and most likely aliens. Once you have seen them you don't care who believes you because you *know*.
