{jpg} Bright wobbling light moving from SW to NE

I stepped out on my back porch facing the south to smoke. I noticed the light almost immediately. It was very bright. Almost as bright as the moon, although much, much smaller. It was yellowish white in appearance and wobbled slightly. Fom where I was standing, it was just north of the Southeastern horizon. It moved slowly and my 11 year old son came outside about 30 seconds after I spotted it and we watched it together for approximately another 3 minutes, never slowing or speeding up. I'm not very good at angles and degrees but I would say it moved at a less than 10 degree angle to the Northeast. We lost sight of it when it went behind a tree we have on the southeast corner of our property.
