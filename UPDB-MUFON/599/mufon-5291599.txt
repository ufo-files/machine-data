Bright hovering light near 95 north

I was driving on 95 North between I think Edgewater and The Daytona exists, when I noticed this really bright light- I thought it was a plane. As I continued driving I noticed that the light hadn’t moved, and that I was just moving closer to it as I was driving. When I got parallel from it, it had no wings and no propellers and definitely did not resemble any drone I have seen. I took a few pictures of it, it was still there after I had passed it. I don’t know what happens to it after I lost sight of it. When you zoom on the pictures a little you can see the light clearly. I only know the exact time because the iPhone tells you what time you took a picture.
