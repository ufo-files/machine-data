{dr}BRIGH ORANGE LIGHT RIGHT ABOVE MOUNTAINS NEXT TO WINDMILLS ON INTERSTATE 8

MY HUSBAND AND I WERE DRIVING TO SAN DIEGO ON INTERSTATE 8 ON JULY 1ST, AROUND 12 MID NIGHT WE SAW A VERY BRIGHT ORANGE LIGHT THAT APPEARED, WE KNEW IT WAS DIFFERENT FROM AIRPLANES AND OTHER LIGHTS THAT WERE AROUND US B/C THE COLOR WAS SO BRIGHT ORANGE. ONE THING WAS WEIRD THE LIGHT KEPT DIMMING IN AND OUT, THIS WAS DIFFERENT FROM THE REST OF THE LIGHTS THAT WERE AROUND US AT THE TIME. THE WIND TURBINES HAVE LIGHTS AND THEIR COLOR AND BRIGHTNESS NOTHING TO DO WITH WHAT WE SAW. WE KEPT AN EYE ON IT FOR ABOUT 5 MINUTES, KEPT APPEARING AND DISAPPEARING. WE NOTICED THAT BEFORE IT VANISHED THE LIGHT SWIRLED AND IT WAS GONE. WE KEPT LOOKING AROUND US SINCE THERE WAS NOTHING BUT DESERT. WE COULDN'T FIND ANYTHING SIMILAR TO WHAT WE SAW. THIS HAPPENED ON INTERSTATE 8 RIGHT BEFORE YOU GET TO THE WIND TURBINES THIS THING WAS ON TOP OF THE MOUNTAIN IT SEEMED TOO LOW FOR AN AIRPLANE AND TOO DIFFERENT FROM THE REST OF THE WIND TURBINES.
