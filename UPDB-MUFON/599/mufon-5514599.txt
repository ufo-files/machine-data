{dr}Series of lights, merged together, changed direction

At the beginning of October, we were driving East down Oak Ridge Turnpike in Oak Ridge, TN and noticed three bright lights in the sky. They were in a diagonal line flying east along the same path we were on. We pulled the truck over to watch them - we watched the lights merge together into one larger light and then turn an immediate right (south, southeastern). We searched online to see if we could find anyone else with a similar sighting in the area around the same time and came across a video someone posted on youtube just recently that is nearly identical to what we saw.
