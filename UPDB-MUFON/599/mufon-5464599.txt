{jpg} Reflective object rotating and changing direction

When I saw the object I was walking out the door to go home from work with a fellow employee. I noticed the object by a flash of light, the sun reflecting off of it I think. The object seemed to have a reflective exterior and flat sides. The object appeared to rotate randomly. The object would fly in a straight path, stop and change directions abruptly. The object would become invisible to me then flash in appearance again. My first reaction was shock and shear curiosity. I couldn1t take my eyes off of it and I could luckily share the experience with a coworker and film the experience. After the experience, I watched the video over and over and I1m still talking about it today. I only lost sight of the object when I had to leave the premises.
