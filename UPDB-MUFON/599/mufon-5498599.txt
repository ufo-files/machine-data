{dr}I was on a flight Oct 7th from Chicago to LA on decent saw a silver ball it was close to the plane appeared stationary.

I was flying from Chicago to LA Oct 7th I was in the back of the plane looking out the window as we decended we passed a silver sphere. It was about 3 feet passed the wing and about 3 feet below the wing. It was 5:30 pm still daylight the object reflected the sunlight.It was about 4 feet around. I got a good look at it because I saw it coming and watched it as we passed it definitely not a balloon. I was pretty scared after I was it because it was so close to the wing. If we had hit it we would have crashed. I flew out a couple hours later but didn't see it.
