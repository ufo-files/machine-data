{tjd} Observed a straight line of Lights travleling towards the North East

Saturday night around 10pm we were sky watching on our back porch. We were facing north when somthing made me look over to my right, i looked up and there was a Line of lights traveling across the sky from the South East to the North East. There was 20 to 30 lights each evenly spaced apart but each light pulsating at a different pace and brightness. Also there was a faint blue beam/hue that would have made up the structure of the craft. This last less than 2 minutes.
