{rjl} I WAS OUTSIDE MY HOME LOOKING AT THE MOUNTAINS WHEN I SAW THIS BRIGHT WHITE OBJECT DIRECTLY OVERHEAD.

FILLED OUT AND FILED AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS, WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809 ON TUESDAY 9/19/17.

I WAS OUT SIDE ABOUT 10:30 TO 11:00 AM LOOKING AT THE MOUNTAINS.
I SAW THIS BRIGHT WHITE OBJECT STRAIGHT UP IN THE SKY OVER ME. I COULDN'T TELL IF IT WAS WHITE OR THE WHITE WAS LIGHT. IT WAS CYLINDER SHAPED AND HAD A DOME ON TOP OF IT. IT CAME STRAIGHT DOWN AS IT GOT CLOSER, I COULD SEE THAT THE DOME WAS ROTATING AND POSSIBLY HAD WINDOWS. IT WAS ABOUT 5 MILES AWAY AT THIS TIME AND IT STARTED DOING EVERY CONCEIVABLE ACROBATIC MOVE I COULD DESCRIBE.
IT DIDN'T SEEM A HUMAN COULD BE INSIDE OF IT. IT WAS AS BIG AS A 747, I USED TO WORK AT THE AIRPORT SO I AM FAMILIAR WITH AIRCRAFT AND THIS WAS NOT ONE I HAD SEEN BEFORE. IT MOVED STRAIGHT UP AND DOWN AND THEN STOPPED,THEN IT PUT ON A SHOW DOING THE ACROBATICS.
I FIRST SAW IT IN THE SOUTHEAST AND IT LEFT GOING NORTH. I WAVED AT IT. THIS WHOLE EVENT LASTED 10 MINUTES.
