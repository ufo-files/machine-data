{dr}Light emerges from behind mountain then hovers then reverses course

My two grown sons, along with their families and my wife were sitting outside on the deck of a vacation home looking west toward the Sangre de Christo mountains. It was about dusk and I noticed a bright light about the size of Venus in the night sky emerge from behind what I think was Mt. Horn. Even though it appeared about the same angular size of Venus, it was not as bright. I'm guessing that it was 1000 ft below the peak but it was hard to judge as this was about 15 miles from us. It was moving very slow to the north and then stopped and hovered about 1000 ft. north of the mountain. This was far enough to realize that it was not a vehicle on a road on the side of the mountain but something in the air. It was almost as if it was peeking out from behind the mountain and then went back behind the mountain again. It did this twice. All of us ( 6 adults and 1 teen and 3 pre-teens)saw it.
