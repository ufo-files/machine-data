Fireball like lights turning on then off over the Gulf Of Mexico

On the night of 11/3/2011,my wife came home stated that she had seen a ufo when driving home from her friends house. So I stepped outside to see if I could catch a glimpse of what she might have seen.My wife told me she saw it to the west so I wasn't out there for a minute and then I saw the following saw one, then two, then three and the fire like lights all blinked out at once. Then we saw three again much further to the west. They also blinked out at the same time. Then we saw one light appear,then two,three,four, five,six and seven until there were seven. The lights at first looked like flares but flares don't jump over in a straight line and then jump again. You could see the jump as it left a kind of trail. Then my wife went in side when we saw no more activity and,I stayed outside for a bit longer and saw white light slowly move across the sky under the moon moving south west direction I ran to get my wife and in 2 sec I ran to get her the object was gone.
