{jpg} Observed a sphere moving slowly in a straight path

I was drinking coffee on my porch swing when i saw motion in my right peripheral vision. I turned my head and saw a large sphere passing from west to east. The sphere appeared to be a ball of energy. It was very bright and intense. I expected to hear thunder or an explosion as it passed me, or to feel heat or wind. I experienced none of those things. The sphere continued moving at a steady pace until it entered the woods across the road from my property, to the east. The sphere just disappeared into the woods. There was no leaf or tree\brush movement, nor any indication that anything had passed into the woods. I walked the path where I saw the sphere move, expecting to see singe marks from the intense energy the sphere exhibited but saw nothing. My most profound reaction was surprise that something that appeared so intensely full of bright energy made no sound, gave off no heat, and moved so smoothly\determinedly on its own. The sphere simply moved into the woods as though by osmosis.
