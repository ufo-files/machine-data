Very bright white light zipping through the sky, abruptly changed direction then dissappeared. No clouds were in the sky that night. 

1. I was in sterling heights area, just turned down my street with my convertible top down.

2. When i laid my head back on the seat/head rest I saw a very bright white light.

3. At first I thought it was a shooting star, but noticed it was in our atmosphere and not in space.

4. The object was moving north east then suddenly changed to north west and about roughly 4 seconds later it disappeared without a trace. There were no clouds in the sky, no helicopters no planes. I did notice a lot of people in the area claiming they saw some lights in the sky that night as well.
