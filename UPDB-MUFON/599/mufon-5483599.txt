{cak} Triangle with 6 lights and smaller lights on side. Hover then path.

I was out on my front porch at approximately 9:30 pm. I looked to my left an observed 2 bright white/yellow lights about 20 degrees above the horizon. It caught my attention because these two lights were very bright. Typically if it is a helicopter I can hear the blades from the distance that it was at, but I heard nothing. So I watched some more. There are two towers, with red lights to the west and slightly north of my location. This is where the 2 lights lights were hovering. I watched the object move from one tower to the second tower and back to the first tower, while hovering. Still all I could see is two bright lights. So I was still thinking helicopter...But there was no sound. Then the object moved toward me, to the south west. As I was standing there it became a visible triangle with what I'm assuming are 6 white lights. One in front, middle, and rear of craft. The sides of the craft had smaller lights along the side. This object turned south and headed in a straight line. It was at this point I could hear a sound similar to a jet plane. I did not observe any blinking lights on the craft. I don't know if it could have been an f-35? I don't know of any other jet that hovers, other than a harrier. But what I observed last night looked exactly like the craft reported in St Louis recently. 2 large yellow/white lights that hovered the. Continued in straight path to reveal its shape, a triangle craft with 6 lights and smaller lights on the side. Was cool to see. I didn't go get my phone because I thought it was a helicopter. By the time I realized it was something different it was on its way out of sight. I watched as it disappeared behind the trees to my south west.
