{dr}Chritmas tree shape object

I have always been fascinated with stars as far back as I can remember. One summer night at about ten at night, I thought I would go out and look at the sky and I looked toward the south east I saw red lights in the woods. The unusual thing about these lights is they were twenty to thirty feet high. We are talking about as high as the tallest trees which were twenty to thirty feet tall. The lights took the shape of a Christmas tree, from the top to the bottom, all red lights. We are talking about hundreds of lights. By the time I went and got one of my brothers, the object was gone. The object was about three to four hundred yards away. It could not be mistaken for any thing else. There was no noise or any type of electrical wires to say that some-one had hooked any lights to the trees. It is a clearing in the woods and in the middle of no where. Maybe it doesn't mean anything, just thought I would share. If you are interested there are two more sighting and locations for all. THIS IS NOT A STORY OR A PRANK.
