{cak} Red large light in sky,

I was outside in the backyard last night, it still was light out, clear skies. I look up in the sky and there is this red glowing light hovering way up in the sky. About 3 seconds later it started bouncing up and down. All of a sudden it started moving forward towards me, stopped for a few seconds and then shot to the north at unimaginable speed.  No airplane, copper or anything manmade could produce the speed it was shooting through the sky.
