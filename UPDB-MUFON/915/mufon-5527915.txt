Two red orbs circled each other and one turned blue

My wife and myself were driving on road 100 toward the interstate, I noticed two red orbs circling each other. It looked as though the orbs were over road 68 in Pasco. One of the orbs lowered in elevation and turned blue. We kept driving and could not see the orbs.
