{nd} Three bright white lights in a line with obvious connecting structure

I was standing outside with two other friends. My one friend was smoking and the other and I were just standing around, hanging out with him. My buddy and I love to look at the sky, and I was looking up when I noticed these three big, bright white lights in a vertical line in the sky. I pointed to it, and said, "hey man, look at that." Initially I thought the object was an airplane, but airplanes do not have three bright lights in a vertical line. Another thing I immediately noticed was that there was no sound. Airplanes make a noise; this craft was silent. There were three really bright lights, obviously with a connecting structure. The UFO was flying in a straight line, silently. Initially it as really far off in the distance but it quickly approached. It was flying way lower than a usual airplane in our area would. My friends and I looked at the craft, and it just kept getting closer. I started to experience fear, and I think the others did, too. The object was getting way too close for comfort. So we all agreed to go inside. I was freaked out for the rest of the night.
