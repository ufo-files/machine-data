{jpg} Was talking to family members who were sitting in a van; I felt like I needed to turn around and when I did, I saw the black craft moving real slow, like 10-15 miles and hour, and about 3 telephone poles high. Black and no noise.

When I looked up, I saw three Caucasion men and one Caucasion lady dressed in tight fitting black suits. They looked down at me and returned to whatever they were doing. It seemed like they were learning how to fly the machine. It didn't bother me to see the ship. I did not get all excited. I just told the people in the van to look. By the time they got out of the van, the ship was not there anymore. It didn't seem to be anything special. When the craft went over the house, we didn't run around the building. It was not big deal.
