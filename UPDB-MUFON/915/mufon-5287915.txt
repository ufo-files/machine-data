Odd row of lights

I was outside stargazing at a rented cabin. I was using an app on my phone to help me remember my constellations. I saw a blurry orange blob to the west just a little north of due west. I tried to use my app to identify it but nothing was showing up in the app. As I tried to get a better look the blob turned into 4 or 5 distinct lights. As I watched a bit longer they seemed to be getting closer. I found myself a little creeped out by the experience as I was outside alone so I went inside. No idea what it was but I haven't seen anything like it before so figured I would report it.
