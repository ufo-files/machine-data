Silver or chrome circular object hovering, turned white then vanished. Reappeared appearing white, vanished again.

While south bound on NM state highway 61 at or about 0730 hours just south of San Juan, NM and north of Royal John Mine road, my wife and I saw a silver or chrome circular object in the southern sky above the hills.
It did not seem to move in any direction or elevation. It changed from silver/chrome to white then appeared to vanish. A few seconds later it appeared again and appeared whit in color. It appeared to be in the same place in the sky. It then vanished.
It could not have been more than two minutes from first sighting the object to when it was not seen by us any longer. 
I am unable to say how far away it was or how big it was. I don’t not believe it was close.
