{cak} unkown lights over Gulf of Mexico

Im an airline captain with 30 years flying experience. We were operating a flight from Miami to Nashville cruising at 36000 feet over Lakeland Florida. Suddenly I and my First Officer noticed about five very bright lights over the Gulf of Mexico between 50 and 100 miles to the west. They were very bright , uniform in distance, and were stationary. when one would extinguish on the right another would appear to the left. The entire event lasted less than one minute. We discussed the possibility of them being military flares but they maintained altitude and appeared to turn on and off as opposed to burning. It was definitely not another aircraft. I have seen many strange and amazing things during my career but nothing like this.
