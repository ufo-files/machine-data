{cak} Strange fast moving object on live morning news cast

On the morning of May 10th we awoke to heavy flooding in Corpus Christi so we turned on KRIS channel 6 news to get information as it was still dark. As the weather person was showing film of local flooding they went to a live shot of the city and sky that they called the Shoreline camera. Just then a lighted object came on the screen from the right side then changed direction in a 45 degree angle at an extremely high rate of speed. I contacted the station via email and asked them to check the video and maybe help identify what it was. I have not heard anything back. I do have the event on my DVR but I don’t know if there’s a way to send anyone else a copy.
