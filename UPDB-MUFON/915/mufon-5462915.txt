It was may 29 2018 early morning just before sunrise it was dark still the sky was crystal clear no clouds I was at work driving my work truck northbound on I-94 near white sox park about 4:30 am, I seen 3 bright lights looking like bright orbs about 2 to 3 thousand feet about downtown Chicago coming from the north I thought they were helicopters at first then they came within the vicinity of willis tower and in uniform made a sharp 90 degree turn east towards Lake Michigan to accurately describe its speed when they turned at my best guess would be hypersonic speeds like how superman takes off in a flash, I couldn't see them after they moved out towards Lake Michigan, buildings obscured my sight as to where they continued their flight I was shocked and stunned by what I seen and too my amazement nothing was reported on the news about. it frightened me I'm still shaken up about it.
