Very bright blue light

Mom was driving grandmom was in passenger seat I was sitting in the back. It was dark out. The interior of the car lit up with a blue light. Mom thought she was being pulled over by law enforcement. The object then hovered over the River for about 5 minutes, then in a blink of an eye it was out of site. Mom and grandmom were very upset and dad who was a commercial airline pilot told mom that planes don’t have blue lights. Thought about this incident my whole life.
