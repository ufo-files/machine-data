Brief round or egg shaped craft moving north-south no wings in clear blue sky disappeared without a trace in @5-10 seconds after sighting g

Walking in prospect park approx moon/12:30 on December 23 or 24 I enjoy observing airplanes in flight and landing patterns above nyc and prospect park landing at LaGuardia. While looking up noticed a a round or egg shaped white object moving very slowly (slower than typical at that altitude) north-south (not normal for that location in sky usually would be moving south-north to land) and expected to see wongs but after 5 seconds or so realized object had no wings and what I assumed would resolve into elongated fuselage was actually round or egg shaped white object w no wings at all. Sky was bright clear blue at that altitude w some clouds moving past at very low altitude. Upon observing this and realizing what I am seeing a low altitude cloud flew past in about another 5 seconds temporarily obscuring this object (which was much higher altitude) . After low clouds blew past (5 seconds or less, maybe 500-1000 ft altitude clouds) object was GONE without a trace. At speed it had been moving it should’ve appeared almost in same spot as before cloud but it was completely gone. Spent 20 minutes scanning sky in all directions for it to reappear or find it along alternate vector but it was gone. Sky was a bright clear blue in every direction above the low moving clouds so would have easily seen this if it was still there. Object was above was between 1000-10,000 ft . Estimated. Round or egg shaped w no wings or other lift bodies apparent.
