Red light in the sky immediately followed by military air craft

The light appeared in the mountains of Upstate New York in Bleeker. It moved across the sky, at first resembling a star but than began to stop, hover, and the proceed moving. Shortly after the sighting reports on my scanner indicated a police and military presence in the area. The light then disappeared and all was silent. Moments later the sky was filled with black helicopters followed by a strong police presence in the area.
