Two lights far away from me I saw it out of my window it was pretty far from me in the sky it took very long until it was gone. I was a kid and also recorded it.

Perhaps it was on the 31th of December or after New Year's Eve. I was a kid like 13-14 years old when I looked out of my window. It was at night and I noticed two big lights in the sky. I realized that this can't be stars they were way too bright and thick. I also thought it could be an airplane but it didn't move. I told my mom to come and see but she didn't believe me she thought they would be fireworks or stuff. I put out my camera and also recorded it. I watched the lights the entire time, I went into the kitchen for like 3 minutes and when I came back it was gone
