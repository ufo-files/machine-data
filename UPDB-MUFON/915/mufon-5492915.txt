I was directly under it when it beammed a light down to the ground

Myself and 2 friends were hanging out in nearby wooded area,sitting under a large tree.We suddenly noticed and felt something beyond description.A sort of light was above us...not directly at first,but it seemed to be slowing and descending to our location.First thought was a helicopter,but we knew almost right away it was not that.I can easily say that a powerful light was being emitted,and a very strong sort of sensation of electric static,making hairs stand up type.This was a color and type of light not seen by me before,and makes it hard to describe.Blueish white,and very intense.It made the surrounding area so crystal clear and vibrant.Then 2 beams of even brighter light shot down,making sweeping motion and I vividly remember the circles it cast where it traversed the uneven ground.It seemed as if time slowed down or something and a quiet eerie sensation built to a point of breaking.It caused a sudden flight reaction on all 3 of us.We jumped up and ran,and the speed and agility which we did was unlike anything ever.I ran across a very rocky creek bed,and the 2 others used a culvert pipe i believe as a crossing.We made it over and then up a slight bank and onto a side street area.Here we stood and stared,and the object hovered silently.As we stared in awe,panting and scared it suddenly went up at a intense speed and arced away to a pinpoint and vanished.You could tell it had traveled many miles in just a split second.It had left a corkscrew "mist trail" of exhaust I suppose.The trail stayed for quite some time.We ran back to my friends house,and hurriedly had his mom look at the green trail.She sort of acknowledged it but was unimpressed.I never understood that....anyways this is a very real sighting,and if you look into it further it can be verified by articles in paper.The kicker is this.The Navy mentioned missile test fired of the coast was the source.I can tell you for sure it was no missile.Santa Rosa Ca is far inland,and missiles do not hover over creek beds and shine lights.J also can say that others did see this object hovering that night,yet they had no idea it was directly above us.I found this out years later.I do not know the exact date,as for some reason we disagreed on it over the years,and with passing of time it got foggy.The 2 other witnesses are brothers and never really liked talking about it.Friends and schoolmates thought we were crazy.Articles were in fact in the Press Democrat,but I have had no luck finding them in basic searches.Perhaps because of the date discrepancy. I would like to explore this,and try to find answers and I think people of your experience and resources could be the only way.If you ever wish to speak in person,I think my description and detail would easily convince you a real UFO was there that night.No damn missile at all.Thanks.
