Sighted a glowing boomerang shape moving silently along the coast.

I live in North Naples Fl. very near Wiggins Pass on the southwest coast. On the early morning of September 20th,2009 at approximatly 1:30am my husband and I was taking a late night swim in our pool. The night was clear and we had been watching stars. I noticed a glowing v shape light moving south along the coast It surprised me so I told my husband to look. The light was a glow and in the shape of a boomerang or wide v shape nothing was flashing or changing, just a steady glow. We watched and followed it as far as we could see. There was no noise with it and it moved faster then any small plane. My husband and I are both pilots so we often watch plane traffic. When I first saw it I thought it could be a light reflecting from traffic but when we followed it we realized it had the v shape form. It caught us off guard but we were able to watch it for about 10 seconds until obstacles got in the way. WOW!
