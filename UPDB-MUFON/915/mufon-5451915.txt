Tic-tac over Oakland

Event occurred two years ago and I recently decided to file a report. Tic-tac UFO flying over Oakland was seen by me driving west across San Mateo bridge in California. At first I thought the object was a cloud. However, since it was a cloudless day it captured my attention. As I watched the object I noticed it was wobbling gently. At first, I thought it must be a balloon but it was stationary and wobbling which seemed odd. It was far too high to be a marketing prop, like balloons used by car dealers. It also looked more like a cloud and not a balloon. I captured a photo of the object with my iPhone. It was difficult to take the photo with my iPhone while driving. When I arrived at home I checked my iPhone and I was surprised that I captured the image. I was also surprised to see the object in the photo didn't exactly look like the tic-tac I saw. Perhaps it was my 54 year old eyes that smoothed out the image to make it appear like a tic-tac. I any case, I don't think it was a balloon, aircraft, or cloud but I'm not sure what to make of it. I have not seen anything like this since.
