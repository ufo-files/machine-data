Approximately a year and half ago 30 of us had witnessed a strange event that took place above are church on a Saturday. I would have to look into it the figure out a direct date. The objects appeared approximately at cloud height somewhat difficult to be sure from the lack of cloud cover. The objects were round with what appeared to be a small round sphere in the center. First I noticed one object then appeared several more, returning back to one object with no more than seven appearing at one time. I found your website while watching TV show on alien mysteries. I have to ask why the number seven? The objects were very difficult to see as if they were to blend in to the blue sky above. It took a great deal of focusing to be able to see the objects and of course the 30 of us talked about what they could possibly be the whole time.
