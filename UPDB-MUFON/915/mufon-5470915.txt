{rjl} 4 lights that emitted material from them

My wife and I saw four bright glowing lights that we though were airplanes from Martinsburg airbase or some other air planes. We observed the lights right above the trees in the direction of Slanesville. The lights were very bright and did not move or blink and dropped some sort of bright material from them. The lights pulsated from a yellow to white blue color. After the lights dropped material they remained in the area for another two minutes and then flew off at an extremely high rate of speed in the direction of Slanesville. On the 14th we drove up to the area where we though the lights were hovering above and found some metallic drops that look like soldering. The side of one tree had a thin burn mark with the material laying at the base of the tree. We did take a couple of soil samples and scrapped bark off some of the trees.
