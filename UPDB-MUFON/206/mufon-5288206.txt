8-10 balls of light appear and disappear over Los Angeles NW skies

I was sitting and relaxing in the back patio when I looked up in the sky and (from a distance) saw these white balls of light just appearing from the western skies. They weren’t planes because some of them were too close together and they were all at random distances. It seemed that they faded in, almost following each other but they direction changed slightly moving towards the NW and then just faded out into space. As soon as I noticed what was very strange to me I immediately called for my husband (who is a nurse and is facts first oriented) who sitting in the family room to witness what I was observing so he wouldn’t think I was crazy. He immediately came out and witnessed exactly what I wrote above. He couldn’t explain it either and also made the comment “ it’s like they just appear, fly across and then fade out.” Unfortunately I was too busy watching and didn’t record it in my phone but at least I had someone else see what I witnessed.
