out walking dogs at night, i look up & see a giant triangle dimly lit up from bottom by 2 lines connecting at a point

I was out walking my dogs the night of the 21st of Oct around 9:00 & as always, I'm looking up & I see a giant triangle moving fairly slowly from north to south in the florida sky. Silent, it caught my eye because there were 2 rows of steady non pulsating or flashing lights framing the outline of the craft meeting at point, very dim overall. It was about 500ft up under the clouds, larger than a football field; it stayed in my view for about 20 secs until I couldn't see the lights anymore. Just last month in the same area I saw something very high up in the atmosphere manuvering around like it "shouldn't have" but this was my first Unmistakable. It was huge & defiantly a triangle, on the side w/o lights I could sense a solidness. The thing was darker than the surrounding sky & then quietly was gone. This time I don't know what else you could say it was
