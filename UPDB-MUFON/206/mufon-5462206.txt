Went for a run and saw around 25 star like objects easterly direction. Not lanterns due to the movement

It was approx 10pm and performed my usually Sunday evening run. I started off the run and headed down Long Lane (south direction). It was a clear cold night (around 4 degrees Celsius. As I was running a group of 25+ objects all moving in an easterly direction caught my eye. I stopped as I knew these where not lanes as they where very close to together and some trailing behind. I then called my wife at home who observed the objects from the bedroom window of the house. She could clearly see them moving and close other and some moving around each other. A plane further up could be seen (with flashing lights) so it was clear this was not planes. After a short duration a few stopped and looked as they where rising and getting fainter. Some of the other objects kept going. After around 10mins none of the light where visible. It was not laterns as they looked pure white and the movement was not consistent with lanterns.

I tried to take a video on my phone but it was too dark and not possible.

I was shocked and expecting other in the neighborhood to report read about this in the news but nothing has come up so far. Wanting to know what we saw and if others also saw these objects.
