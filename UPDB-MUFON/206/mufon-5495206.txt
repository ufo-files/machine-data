{rjl} 3 glowing but dimmed lights in triangle form within 100 yards over head

I awoke one morning, around 5am, and went to my back deck to smoke a cigarette(we do not smoke inside). After a couple minutes of looking to the stars, I see 3 red lights. While they looked solid, they appeared as if looking through a stained glass window(blurry almost). The lights were in triangle formation, moving south at a fast speed. The object was moving fast enough that I was sure I would hear a noise, typical of any known aircraft, but no noise came. There did not appear to be a body to the craft, though common sense tells me there should have been one. I could only assume it may have been cloaked in some way, but that is only speculation. There are no roads behind my apartment, nor any other buildings close enough to cause any reflections. There were no clouds in the sky at all, nor any fog. I have been a believer in the unknown and/or unexplained, but I know to reason things out before coming to any conclusions or judgements. The object and lights moved too quickly for me to take any photos, and I do not care if anyone thinks I am crazy. I saw what I saw. I hope this little bit of information helps towards a full disclosure some day.
