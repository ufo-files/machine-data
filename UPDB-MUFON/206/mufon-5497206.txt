{rjl} Saw 2 bright lights in excesses of 2000 M.P.H.

Worked for Santa Barbara Police Dept. My partner and I were at Leadbetter Beach looking for a runaway child at 1A.M.. We observed 2 bright lights about 100 above the water apps. 200 feet apart. They came from the direction of Vandenberg A.F.B. in Lompoc Ca.. and flew down to Oxnard Ca. The miles by air around 90 miles. Observation time was less than 5 seconds. We knew we had seen something that was faster than anything we have.
