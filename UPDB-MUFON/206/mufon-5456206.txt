{jpg} Went outside and was visible through the trees, slowly rotated

Dad was outside having a smoke, called my husband outside, who in turn came and got me. Once outside there were what looked like three lights hovering in the sky through the trees. The centers were white and the outer glow pulsated between white, red, green, and blue. Seemed to slowly rotate, causing my dad to think they completely vanished, but they were still there. Didn't move from the spot for a solid five minutes, so there was no way it was a plane.
