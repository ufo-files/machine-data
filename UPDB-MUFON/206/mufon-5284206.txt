Saw a triangle shaped craft in the sky.

While driving in the country I came around a hill and saw a huge very bright light in front of me up in the air about 1500 feet agl. As I got closer I saw that it was actually three bright lights each at the end of the points of a triangle shaped craft. It was sideways so I was looking at the bottom of the craft. I got a very good look at it. It was moving West There were several cars on the road coming toward me in the other lane and one behind me.  There were no shoulders on the side of the road so I couldn't pull over right away. I watched it for a few minutes. I found my pocket camera, held it in one hand while driving with the other and pointed it the the general direct where it was and started shooting hoping to get something. I shot the pics through the windshield. I wish I would have taken some pics when it was closer but it was so amazing I just stared at it for a few minutes while driving. The pictures are blurry because of the camera movement. It was slowly moving West. When I finally pulled over it was almost to some clouds. I got some shots as it entered the clouds.
