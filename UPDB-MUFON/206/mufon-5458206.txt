Two large shadowy chevron/triangles with amber glow at each point--high in the atmosphere

Sky was clear with bright moon. Was watching for meteors. (Perseids shower) I had just seen a fiery meteor flash across the sky from east to west toward the north. About a minute after that, I saw two giant (I”m assuming giant because they seemed way higher up than normal aircraft) objects flying silently from northeast to southwest. One right behind the other. They seemed like black translucent/blurry shadowy chevron/triangles with a large dim amber glow at each point of the chevron. It seemed like they were behind a deep layer of clouds, causing the blur/glow, but the sky was completely clear last night. They blurred across the sky silently and gracefully. There were many planes in the air that night. Every single craft made a noise or left a trail. They also had blinking lights. These were much larger, seemed higher up than any plane and were completely silent. No sound, no trails. Lights were still the entire time, no blinking. It happened so fast, I was in awe...no time to get my camera.

NOTE: There was a power outage in Queen Anne yesterday morning, FWIW. I saw this around 10:30 PM.
