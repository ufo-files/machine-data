Large orange light hovered over houses. Remained stationary, then blinked and disappeared.

I got up to go to the bathroom while it was still very dark outside which is unusual for me. Upon stepping back into the hallway to return to bed, I immediately noticed bright orange light spilling out of my bedroom door into the hall. I remember saying, 1What the hell?1 out loud. As I stepped into my bedroom I noticed bright orange light coming through the window so intensely that it was making shadows in the folds of the curtains. I went to the window, looked out, and saw a very large round orange light at the top of the neighboring hill above the trees and houses, almost solid full-moon size, very sun-like. I turned to go get my glasses and it blinked out a couple of times and then went out completely. I returned to the window with my glasses on anyway but saw nothing. The orange light was gone. Nothing can really be concluded since I didn1t have my glasses on for near-sightedness and the 1object1 was half of a block away . . . but there is no such light on any home or building where it was. A couple of days later my sister, who lives 1-1/2 blocks away, immediately spoke of seeing an orange light on 3/17 upon entrance into my home for a visit. Her exact first words to us were, 1What was up with that orange light Monday?1 Oddly, the location where I believed the light to be when I saw it would be over a field immediately in front of my sister1s house.
