Cigar shaped UFO zipping over forrest preserve north of Chicago.

I was driving east to work on rt.137 Buckly Road north of Libertyville Illinois. It was about 7:00 am. There is a large decline in the road as it ramps down into the forrest preserve. As I came up to Milwaukee Road I saw this HUGH cigar shapped UFO. I only saw the front and then the back as it zipped by super fast heading north. As I got to the intersection of Milwaukee Avenue I looked north to see if I could see it again and it was gone. It was probibly in Wisconsin by then concidering how fast it was going. I saw it plain as day. It was like looking at a hubcap on a car as it drives by. I filed a report with the Libertyville police soon after. I recreated an image of what I saw that day. Remember I first saw the front and then I saw the rear, just like a hubcap on a fast moving car. I felt like I saw something very strange. I think that the location on top of the hill where I saw this thing would be a great place to set up some high speed camera equipment.
