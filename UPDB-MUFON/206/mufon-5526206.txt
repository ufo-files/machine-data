Triangular aircraft with three lights and a laser

At around 11pm on June 11th 2011 my mom and her boyfriend saw an "aircraft" in the shape of a triangle and it had a white light at each point. Periodically, a laser which was also white in color would be coming from said aircraft. During this sighting, two helicopters close together flew overhead and they were close to the ground and loud. The aircraft was flying exactly north as was the direction of the laser pointed in the northern direction. The object emitted the laser, it flew in the northern direction, and was often blocked by clouds as it was an overcast. I was right outside of our house, my mom was outside smoking when she and he noticed the aircraft. Upon first glance, they assumed it to be a star. My mom and her boyfriend were very shocked at the object and they were trying to get me to see it. We lost sight of the object as it moved behind the clouds.
