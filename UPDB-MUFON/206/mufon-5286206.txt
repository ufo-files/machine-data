saw bright light like a star during the day

I was outside in the heat of the sun in the Colorado mountains during the evening to photograph a large hawk that had landed on a close treetop. I was facing the direction of the sun so it was very bright, not a cloud in the sky. All of a sudden, I noticed a bright light that looked like a star a little to my left between the hawk and the sun which was furthest left. I stared at it for several seconds and it stayed fixed in the sky. I decided to run in and grab my digital camera because I didn't think it would show up on the phone camera. It took about 7-10 seconds to run in and back out and it was still there in the same spot as far as I could tell. I snapped the photo in the UFO's direction not even knowing if I captured it. I kept looking at it until it just disappeared (I don't totally remember it leaving). I didn't feel scared or anything strange, more of an exhilarated feeling. When I zoomed in on the photo later and saw what I captured, I wondered whether or not it was from our neighborhood! Pretty amazing to see.
