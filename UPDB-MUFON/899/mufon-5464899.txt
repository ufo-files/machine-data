{cak} Came to a stop and noticed a craft that looked like it was made of liquid Mercury. No openings or windows.

My brother and I were driving down the road and stopped at a red light. We then observed a silent UFO stationary at first right above the tree line. As we were noticing that it had no lines or windows, the light changed and as we moved forward the craft seemed to start forward as well. We turned up Rt 9 and the craft went straight. We then noticed quite a few helicopters surrounding the craft as we lost sight of it over the trees. That wasn't all though. My brother and I said we were going to tell the family we were going to visit which was about 15 mins away but We never spoke of it again for almost 15 years. I was in a motorcycle accident and remembered the sighting vividly. I brought it up to my brother who also remembered it as I did. We are still puzzled how we "forgot" to tell anyone for 15 years...
