Flashing Light over I-295

I was on my way home from dropping my girlfriend off near Williamsburg rd in Sandston, Va. As I was turning off Williamsburg Rd onto Drybrdige Rd I noticed a blinking light far away. I continued down this road towards Meadow Rd, I could still see the light blinking above the treetops. I took a right and headed down meadow to the bridge the goes over I-295. As I reached the overpass I stopped the truck on the side of it( stupid, I know ) and there was a pulsating white light over 295, it was bright enough to light up the surrounding clouds. I sat there long enough to note that It was pulsating at the same rate, it was a white sphere the whole thing would light up at even intervals. I mean this thing was BRIGHT white, pure white. At this time I was panicking and rode home to tell my mother, she wanted to go see it. About the time she was getting in my truck we heard the ROARING of jet engines at Richmond International Airport (the military base over there, they have f-22's etc etc ) I have never heard the jets take off so late at night before. As we made our way to the bridge you could hear the jets still firing up, one after another, there was atleast 3 to 4 that took off. Upon reaching the overpass the light was gone. My mother looked at me like I was crazy, but she DID hear the jets taking off.
