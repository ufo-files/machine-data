VERY bright object appeared to change course

During my early morning exercise I looked up between the trees at the night sky. I noticed a very bright object moving from northwest to southeast at a relatively slow speed. When I first noticed it I thought it definitely wasn't a satellite because it was as bright as Venus in its gibbous phase. At about 10 seconds after I noticed it there appeared to be a course change to the south. I continued watching for about 3 minutes until the object disappeared behind some trees. About 5 minutes after this sighting I noticed a dim object moving from west to east at a relatively fast speed. I figured this was a satellite because I've seen a similar object on the same trajectory at about the same speed before. I'm always watching the skies. I just wish that L.A. didn't have such light pollution.
