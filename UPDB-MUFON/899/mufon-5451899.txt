Several dozen lights traveling in a straight line

I was trying to locate Jupiter with my telescope around 3am when I noticed what I thought was a group of helicopters traveling through the sky. After counting more than 30 lights go by, I changed locations to get a better view of where they were coming from. I found a vantage where I could observe south and saw these lights, dim at first, come from seemingly nowhere and slow down and brighten as they followed one another in a northerly direction. They seemed to stabilize in speed and relative distance from one another. Some would speed up to catch up with the one on front of it. As I turned and looked north, I could see this line of lights go for what appeared to be miles. At this point I ran to our campsite and woke my ex wife and she also witnessed the same thing I had. The lights continued to appear for about another two minutes when they abruptly stopped. We watched them as they flew north and then began to discuss what we thought it may have been.
