(GS) Orange Lights playing follow the leader.

I was in my backyard which faces south at between the evening hours of 9:30-9:50pm, i had just sat down in chair to relax after work,when i noticed a very orange light traveling from the west to east at a speed which seemed to be a rate of a fighter jet in motion, the light was about 30/35 degrees above horizon at a distance of 1-2 miles, I lept out of chair to follow it, the craft had a grayish internal soft light emanating from within it then veered south easterly to disappear all this happened within a matter of seconds,not a sound was heard i,am standing there still looking up when 1 minute later the same type of light followed the path of previous craft, now i,am believing my eyes when again another light passed at same speed/path. 1 minute later another light passes same distance as other two, then a fourth passes, all this took 5-7 minutes total, its as the lights were playing follow the leader, they all vanished quickly. I have excellent hearing and sight,what i saw but did not hear makes me feel very special!
