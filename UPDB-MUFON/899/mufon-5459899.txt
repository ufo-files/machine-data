{tjd} Unusual Lights seen while taking trash to the curb

Unusual Lights seen while taking trash to the curb. As I took the city trash bins out to the curb, I saw this unusual bright light. It was so different that I just kept looking up at it. So, I yelled at my Patner Jeff, who was inside, to come out. We both stood there looking at it. Then I had the idea to get my camcorder and ran inside. I placed it on the trashcan lid to steady the camera and filmed it! I was so excited! You can hear me in the video. I cut it down to a few minutes for Facebook and Youtube. but it was a pretty awesome sight and I feel humbled to have captured it. It changed shape quite a few times.
