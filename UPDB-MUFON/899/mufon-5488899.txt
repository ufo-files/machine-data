{rjl} Horseshoe shaped light and sometime shooting out red or green light from it.

I was truck driving on I-74 east and got ramp to go hwy 63 south. While on the ramp, I saw red ball of light. While heading south, the red turned white, and got smaller in size. Then, noticed that there was more than one and they were horseshoe shaped. At times, they had red or green streaks shooting out of them! After a couple of hours and a few pictures, the sun came up and it was all over.
