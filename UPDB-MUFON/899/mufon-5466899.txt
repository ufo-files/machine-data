I stayed with my brother at my grandmother in the village of Kukhtichi (not far from the city of Uzda). I was 8 years old, my brother 13. In August, 1997. I returned with my brother and grandmother from the guests late at night. My brother and I are slightly ahead, my grandmother is behind us. Not far from the old grain storage was a crooked concrete fence.Right above the fence we saw two pulsating glowing spheres, in each of the spheres we saw two five-pointed stars. The light was diffused and pulsating. Spheres circled around each other. As soon as we approached them, they seemed to notice us, froze for 5-7 seconds, and sharply, at the speed of light, swung to the side of the skies, it was like teleportation.We saw nothing more, there were no traces on earth. It seemed to us that these two objects were studied something above the ground, but they did not think that they could see something.
