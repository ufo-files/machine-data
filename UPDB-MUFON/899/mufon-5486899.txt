{rjl} morphing light craft over Manhattan after a meditation

This very interesting light craft was captured on the west side of Manhattan after a CE5 indoor meditation followed by a visual sky watch. The light appeared stationary like a star, but much bigger, brighter, and closer. It had a disco color shape to it and as I zoomed in with my video camera, it appeared as it was morphing, almost as if it was a disco heart pumping. It turned into a donut with a black hole in the middle and I noticed that it was drifting slowly to the right based on how it was centered in my camera LCD. I have captured this light along with similar lights that have appeared after deep meditations. All videos are documented very well and are on my youtube channel.
