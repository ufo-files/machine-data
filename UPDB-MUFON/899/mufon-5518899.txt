Three Ords in vague triangular formation followed by a fourth a minute later. All four were bright red-orange and one after the other faded out traveling from west to east

I was on rear deck of home feeding a cat, As I stood up I looked at the planet Venus and thought how bright it was. I then saw three bright Orange-Red orbs traveling from west to east moving about the speed of a med-evact chopper as they flew over my house they faded out completely first the two trailing orbs then the leading one. About a minute later a fourth one traveling in the same direction appeared and as it approached the same location as the others it also faded away. The sky was clear and the moon had not come up at that time.When I first saw them I thought they were military choppers but there werent any strobe or flashing lights. After I realized what I was seeing I thought that now I have witnessed what Ive heard other people describe.I wasnt afraid but just facinated.
