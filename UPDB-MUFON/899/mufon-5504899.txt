I was driving from orangeburg,SC to Denmark,SC in April of 1992 not sure o f the day or that particular road.I think it was route 70 or 75 or 77 and it was about 7:00 pm to 7:30 pm and it was dusk in the horizon. I came to a slight hill going downward and I looked straight upward in the sky and notice a black oval shape object in the sky. The object looked like it was far from me a large distance but as I started down the hill coming to a four way road not sure of the crossing road.But there was a old white little building looked like a closed gas station on my right side.The black oval object seemed like it was getting closer and closer. Now, there was still light on the horizon and before I new it the black oval shape object was hovering over my car in perfect silence. I looked for other cars coming in front and in the rear but I was the only car on the road at that time which I considered very odd during that time of day. As, I slowed my car down to about 3 to 5 miles per hour I stuck my head out the car to get a real good look at this strange object. I notice that the object did not make a sound plus it had 6 rows of pulsating lights like a whitish,orange,red color.Then when I thought to myself at time this must be a UFO I lost some time because all I remember that I was driving looking back and the black object was still in my rear moving real slow to my left. Then when I got to my destination I realized that it was around 9:15 pm and trip should only taken 25 minutes and I cannot recall the time loss.
