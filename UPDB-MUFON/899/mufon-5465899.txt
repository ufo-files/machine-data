{cak} Flew due north nearly in an approach attitude to the SLC airport. Large, bright white pulsating light in the center of the bottom of the craft.

I was in the backyard with my wife. I happened to look up and saw this craft moving silently in a due north direction. It flew perfectly level and its course never wavered. A large, bright, circular white light pulsed in the bottom center of the craft, but there was no sound. We live on the edge of landing pattern for the Salt Lake City Airport, but no aircraft were visible at that moment. The craft continued in a northerly direction and was lost to sight. Note: I am a former military air traffic controller. I have never seen this type of air vehicle before. Amazing.
