{nd} Friend woke me. Watched it with binoculars for several minutes next to star. Extreme acceleration to second object and stopped on dime next to it

I was in army mechanized infantry. My friend [Item Moved/CMS/nd] woke up everyone and said he saw a UFO. I was only only in the M113 armored personnel carrier to get up and look. He said it zigzagged across the sky and pointed to it. I took my binoculars and looked at it. It was next to a star and was therefore easy to tell it was perfectly still. It blinked two lights just like what you would expect an airplain to do. [Item Moved/CMS/nd] was quite excited and insisted that it zigzagged impossibly across the sky. I was trying to figure out what it could be when it shot across about 60 degrees of sky in less than a half second and seemed to instantly stop next to another one. I am an engineer and know that the acceleration this thing displayed was not even close to possible unless it used technology far beyond anything commonly known. Now I understood why [Item Moved/CMS/nd] was so excited. After another few minutes, one tuned white and seemed to shoot off toward space and disappeared in a few seconds. We got a call to move and so that was it. We didn't tell anybody it or even talk about it which seems a bit strange to me in hindsight. I don't think it was something from our military. It was way too fast. It was over a military base. I am quite sure it was a real object even though I didn't see anything but lights. I don't know why it would have blinking lights like an airplane or why it zigzagged assuming [Item Moved/CMS/nd] was accurate and I don't think he lied. To me, my attitude was, wow they really do exist. I will tell people about it from time to time but I see a lot of skepticism which is kind of annoying but understandable I suppose.
