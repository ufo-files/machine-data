{nd} Started as 3 helicopters inline then 1 outran the other 2 then went thru 1 of them then flew faster then other 2 in a different direction

My friend and I were driving home in Parksville MD, and there were 3 objects in the sky flying in formation. They were moving slow like helicopters. Then the last object following behind and took off towards the other two then looked as if it flew into one of the other ones. It then changed direction on a dime then took off in a different direction much faster than the other two in the opposite direction then it disappeared.  While the other two helicopters kept going in the same direction towards Towson, MD. The objects headed east towards Essex area.  

This happened around 8:30 pm and 9 pm. The other two helicopters had running lights with the red and green taillights like a plane, yet this object only had a bright white light on the front with no landing lights. The object was moving faster than any plane I’ve ever seen and tuned left in a straight line not like a plane would do or a helicopter. Then it disappeared from the site.
