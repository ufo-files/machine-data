Mass ufo 's leaving a black rectangular cloud.

Myself and four other witnesses saw about 6 to 10 ufo's leaving a strange black rectangular cloud near Kapunda South Australia. The objects travelled east to west at great speed and appeared to be disc shaped and metallic grey in colour. All five witnesses photographed the objects from five different cameras and five different angles. Mufon can have the photos of this event if they like to share on their site. This event has left us still shaking !
