{kt}10 fourteen year old boys see green orb (Just saw on Hangar 1 that they're called. plasma orbs.

About 10 guys I went to Jr. High school with were riding our motorcycles on horse trails in Chicamauga Nat'l battlefield park in North Georgia near Chattanooga, Tm. We stopped next to a field to smoke a cigarette. I heard one of the boys say, "Hey, What's that? I turned around and saw a green orb next to the timeline. It moved back and forth left to right, then back right to left and up and down near the ground. We watched it for at least 10 min., then it started coming toward us. We got the hell out of there and didn't look back. We blamed it on swamp gas ( even though no swamps were around)
