UFO on fire

I was walking home from my shift at work, not a cloud in the sky. It was still summer so I knew I wasn’t seeing things. I seen a craft engulfed in reddish flames I couldn’t see what was on fire and it left a small streak behind it as it was flying straight. My mind was racing I thought I was witnessing a horrible event but to my dismay it quickly disappeared leaving a red lighting like streak beaming upwards out of the atmosphere, the beam had only lasted 5-10 seconds longer. I looked around to see if anyone else saw what I did but I was the only one. I still remember it like it was yesterday! I wish I took out my phone but it I was in shock and couldn’t stop watching it.
