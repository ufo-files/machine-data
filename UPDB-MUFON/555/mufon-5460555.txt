{tjd} My friend and I were catfishing late at night on the Scioto River and witnessed a triangular (three bright lights - one at each corner) craft.

The center area was dark like the night sky, but it was definitely up there a good ways, maybe a mile or so, and was still and quiet. It appeared for a few moments and up to a few minutes. It then disappeared. Very strange but I have read about similar sightings and thought it would be helpful to report this.
