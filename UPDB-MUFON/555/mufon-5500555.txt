{cak} Disc photographed by Bishop Peak that became a Project Blue Book case.

On July 7, 1967, a disc was photographed over San Luis Obispo, California. I first became aware of the photo on www.old-ufo-photos.com, and became interested because the caption read; "San Luis Obispo, CA July 7, 1967" and that was all. I searched for other instances of the same photograph, and found it on over 200 different websites, but never any more information about it. I even found out that it went to the Foreign Technology Desk at Wright-Patterson AFB, and was part of a Project Blue Book file (File # 11864). I have sent off FOIA requests to the National Security Agency, the Defense Intelligence Agency, and the Air Force, and have received the first round of replies (see attachments). I have also had the photographed by an independent source, Intelligence Analyst Robert Morningstar, and his photo enhancement is included in the attachments. According to Robert Morningstar, one of the objects that at first appeared to be a hair, in fact was a "tube-like" object, that has been seen before (see attachments).
