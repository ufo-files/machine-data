Around June -July. Large stars sighted in night sky

I was in my bedroom something made me inclined to look outside my bedroom window I believe I was asleep before the event took place and I woke up it was late at night anyway. 
I saw large star looking lights in the sky too big to be stars. I was impressed.
I had an urge take a photo but did not. I recalled waking up later in the night and looking outside and I looked at the stars and having urge to take a photo some time. I saw the a light blink and I believe the lights disappeared all together.
