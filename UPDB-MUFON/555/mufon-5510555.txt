{cak} observed lights moving from NW to SE with no sound

My wife and I were in our backyard and we noticed a group of lights moving from the northwest to the southeast. At first I thought it was a couple of helicopters but then other lights appeared. I believe it was 3-4 lights. As they crossed closely overhead we heard no sound and my wife ran into the house and got her cell phone to take a picutre and then a video. As she was doing this more lights came into view in a group of 3 lights. In all I believe there were 8-10 lights but it could have been more.In the last group of 2 two lights one appeared to be dropped lower to the ground as it moved to the southeast.
My feelings at the time were confused as I expected some sound to come from the lights as they passed overhead but none came. It was very still during the time this took place.
