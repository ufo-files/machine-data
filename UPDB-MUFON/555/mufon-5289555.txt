UFO sighting as I took photos of the sunrise above the treeline in the mountains.

As I went outside on the morning of February 15th, I was taking pictures of the sunrise. I took 3 pictures at one elevation and 1 more at a bit lower elevation. Since I was looking into the sun, I couldn't see the object. After reviewing the photos, I noticed an object to the left of the sun. At first I thought it was an optical illusion from the sun. Once I enlarged the photos, I soon realized there were details to this object. As I sent the pictures to a friend of mine, she later had the pictures inspected by her daughter, who is a retired police investigator. As she was inspecting the photo, she realized that I took the pictures in Live Mode. She pressed on the pictures to activate the Live Mode and saw the object literally flying into the view of the camera. She texted me to check on my end as well. Sure enough, the object flew into the view of my camera when I was taking the pictures. I have 3 pictures that were taken in Live Mode and 1 in still frame, each Live Mode photo clearly illustrates the object flying around with a faint noise that can be heard on my phone as the Live Mode is being played. In addition to the object, there was an aura surrounding the circular object. The attached pictures don't do the justice of the detail and movement as illustrated in the Live Mode of my camera on my phone.
