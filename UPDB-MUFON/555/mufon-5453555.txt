{rjl} Round brilliant light zigzagged up and down in clear night sky.

In the evening around 10:30 pm on July 11, 2019 I was moving items from my car into my new residence located in the countryside near Mineral Springs, NC. All of the sudden I noticed something strange in the clear night sky above me. An incredible brilliant round light zigzagged erractically up and down and sideways while moving forward. This was definitely no plane! It’s manuvers displayed a higher technology than any of our aircraft. It appeared at about 150 to 200 feet. Then, it just disappeared. A few minutes later a plane came heading toward the same area where the ufo had been spotted. It seemed as if the plane was tracking or following the object.
