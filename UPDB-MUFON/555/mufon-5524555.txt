round light green lights in the clouds making a straight line and into a circular pattern laste about 15 mins and disappeared when the cloud descipated

Got out of my car at midnight on August 27th 2011, and looked up at the sky. There were two light green circular lights in a mass of clouds. At first I thought they were spotlights or a search helicopter, but the more I watched, I realized that there was no sound and no trail from a light to the ground! My neighbor came home at the same time and looked up to see what I was watching. She also couldn't believe what she was seeing. They started at one end of the cloud (from the west), shot straight out and then one circled to the right and one to the left and then started all over. This all took place in the cloud. Once the cloud descipated they were gone. Went on for about 15 minutes. It was almost as if they were playing with each other! Talked to another girl on the 28th and she said she saw the same type of lights at 5 a.m. She lives within 6 or 7 miles from me except in the country. Talked to another man today 8/29 who said he sees them all the time in this part of the country in our town. Told me he quit telling people because they thought he was crazy. What are we seeing?
