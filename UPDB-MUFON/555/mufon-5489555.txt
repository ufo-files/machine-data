{dr}Driving down Route 79 and glanced to side. Saw huge hovering mass with bright lights coming in all directions.

I was driving down Route 79 and right after the bridge where you can exit for Neville Island, around those long curvy bends I turned my head because I saw bright lights. I had to double take because I thought it was just a hovering helicopter but when I looked again I saw a large black mass floating just above the tree line with huge lights all around the base and on the front of the structure. It was quiet, there was no noise and it just hovered there. I was unable to stop and observe it so I am unaware of how long it stayed there.
