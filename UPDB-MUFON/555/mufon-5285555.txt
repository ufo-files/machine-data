Light woke me up and seen ufo hovering above trees

I was sleeping, and a brilliantly bright light woke me up shining through/beside my blinds on my window. This was odd to me because I had two thick blinds on my window to blackout my room since my room was facing the direction the sun comes up. So I get up and look outside to see where the light is coming from, and that's when I see a circular disc shaped ufo with lights along the outside of it. The weirdest part was, I felt so tired, I just crawled back into bed like it wasn't a big deal.
