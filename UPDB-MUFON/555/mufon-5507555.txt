{nd} circular object stationary in night sky, went through series of bright purple, red, blue lights then descended into nothing

While walking my dog in Bordentown New Jersey at 9:33 PM Sept. 27, 2013, I witnessed a circular object in the night sky sitting stationary about 1/2 mile beyond the horizon. Bright purple lights were seen all around the object then red, blue, back to purple. This went on for about two minutes then all of a sudden it went completely dark descending to the ground. I noticed several airplanes above and beyond this object in the night star lit sky. I could not believe what I was seeing and the extent of vibrant colors this object emitted was extraordinary.
