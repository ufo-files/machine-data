Very fast moving LARGE object in the sky

I was out walking my dogs at 1940 EST on 10/5/2011. Shortly after taking them out, I noticed a very fast moving group of lights that came over the trees near my house. These lights appeared to be VERY high in the sky. I went into my home to grab a set of binoculars and returned outside to try to see more. The lights which were travelling in a long pattern for me to see had already travelled 15-20 degrees in the short period of time it took me to get my bino's. I was having a hard time seeing the object, mainly because of the speed it appeared to be travelling. From the time I took my bino's down to see where the lights were and put my bino's back to my eyes, it was further ahead in it's flight path. When I did manage to get a glimpse of it, it appeared to be VERY large, randomly flashing red and white lights in a crescent shape and had no noticable sound. About halfway through the total time that I saw the object, I noticed another set of lights come from over the trees, appearing to be trying to follow/catch the set of lights which just simply left it further away by the second. This second object had the noise of a jet of some sort and sequentually flashing lights like any other jet seen in the night time sky. The total time lasted about 1 minute, but surely appeared different in the skies that any other object I saw before.
